## Sequence 5: Ocean Songster

![Potion - Tyrant Sequence 5 - Ocean Songster](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/lord-of-the-mystery/images/3/37/Potion_-_Tyrant_Sequence_5_-_Ocean_Songster.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/200?cb=20260329183425)

Potion - Tyrant Sequence 5 - Ocean Songster

- Overview
- Formula
- Ritual
- Backlash
- Acting Method

### Potion Properties

- The liquid is dark, akin to the ocean before a storm, devoid of ripples or bubbles.[^6]
- The liquid is cold as it brings about a numbness that slides down their gullet and into their stomach. It then spread throughout his body cells at an unimaginable speed.[^6]

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> The translucent jellyfish contained azure-blue seawater, with occasional swirling vortices and flashes of silver lightning. It emitted a faint, ethereal song, as if carrying the distant voice of the elf predecessor within its mesmerizing depths.[^7] [^8]

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- Siren's throat.[^2]
- Crystal cluster from a Crystal Jellyfish.[^2]

Or

- **A [Ocean Songster](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tyrant_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_5:_Ocean_Songster)** [Beyonder Characteristic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristic "Beyonder Characteristic").

Supplementary Ingredients

- 500 milliliters of high-purity liquor.[^2]
- 5 strands of Siren hair.[^2]
- 5 tentacles of the Crystal Jellyfish.[^2]
- One tide-shell.[^2]
- One porous coral branch.[^2]

### Advancement Ritual

Take the potion in the belly of an [Obninsk](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Creatures_and_Plants#Obninsk "Creatures and Plants").[^8]

### Ritual Essence

— No data —

### Consumption Backlash

> In the instant they drank the potion, they would hear countless voices. They came from all life at sea.[^6]
> 
> They would feel that their heart was beating violently as it spewed blood outwards. Their spirituality and the sound waves begin to mold their voice and soul.
> 
> One may not help but open their mouth, letting out a loud sigh. Amidst the sigh, they would feel their [Spirit Body](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul#Spirit_Body "Soul") being ripped slightly. As the sound waves spread outwards, they first turn into mottled scales on their skin before pulling out long flesh tendrils that appear like flailing tentacles. The sound wave continues to spread outwards with their [Spirit Body](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul#Spirit_Body "Soul") fragments.

### Ritual Effect

> The voices they heard at the start of the backlash from all life at sea would be blocked by [Obninsk](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Creatures_and_Plants#Obninsk "Creatures and Plants") ’s body, leaving a relatively screened out version.[^6]
> 
> The sound waves continue to spread forward, until making contact with the sticky liquid within the [Obninsk](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Creatures_and_Plants#Obninsk "Creatures and Plants") ’s stomach, and they magically bounce back, infusing their body once again.
> 
> They, who were on the brink of losing control, instantly feels better as they may seize the opportunity. Beginning to sing loudly in a bid to vent the invisible sound waves that would blast their body apart.
> 
> The exact singing would depend on them. As the tunes spread outward, wave after wave, mixing with the numerous Spirit Body fragments before bouncing back on the [Obninsk](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Creatures_and_Plants#Obninsk "Creatures and Plants") ’s sticky stomach walls.
> 
> > In [Alger](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Alger_Wilson "Alger Wilson") ’s case it is a rough, messy, off-tune singing filled with a metallic quality.
> 
> In this process, they are like an ingredient being baked in sound waves as they are being molded into form.
> 
> Finally, they would regain control of their body, and grasp their spreading spirituality.

### Acting Method

— No data —

- Overview
- Formula
- Ritual
- Backlash
- Acting Method

### Potion Properties

> Water of thunder formed by intertwining flashes of multicolored lightning.[^2]

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> A "scepter" woven of lightning.[^2]

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- **A [Thunder God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tyrant_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_1:_Thunder_God)** [Beyonder Characteristic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristic "Beyonder Characteristic")

Supplementary Ingredients

- A fragment of lightning from the Astral World.[^2]
- One Depraved nature of a [Hanged Man](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hanged_Man_Pathway) Pathway Angel.[^2]
- One stone from the underground Nightmare Swamp (Born under the influence of the "Chaos Sea", widely present underground—yet approaching it means coming dangerously close to the Chaos Sea, so High Sequence powerhouses cannot personally retrieve it).[^2]

### Advancement Ritual

Find and harness lightning to disrupt at least three Sequence 2 Angels' advancement rituals to Sequence 1 (optimal if the angels belong to the [Hanged Man](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hanged_Man_Pathway), [White Tower](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/White_Tower_Pathway), [Visionary](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Visionary_Pathway), [Sun](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sun_Pathway), or Pathways).[^2]

- Interrupting a ritual to Sequence 0 will count as completing it in one go.
- Interrupting a ritual isn't limited to drinking the potion but any part of the ritual, such as blowing up the marionette town of a [Miracle Invoker](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fool_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_2:_Miracle_Invoker) that want to advance to [Attendant of Mysteries](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fool_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_1:_Attendant_of_Mysteries).[^12]
