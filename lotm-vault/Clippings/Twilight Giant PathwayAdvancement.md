## Sequence 3: Silver Knight

![Potion - Twilight Giant Sequence 3 - Silver Knight](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/lord-of-the-mystery/images/1/10/Potion_-_Twilight_Giant_Sequence_3_-_Silver_Knight.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/200?cb=20260330034551)

Potion - Twilight Giant Sequence 3 - Silver Knight

- Overview
- Formula
- Ritual
- Backlash
- Acting Method

### Potion Properties

> It looks like as if chunks of Mercury were scattered throughout the pale white liquid [^1]

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> A mass of silvery-white luster that resembled a heart-like miniature sun.[^7]

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- The essence of Aurmir's blood.[^1]
Or

- The Heart of a "King's Court Chaser" (a type of giant).[^1]
Or

- The heart of a "King's Court Watchman" (another type of giant).[^1]

Or

- **A [Silver Knight](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Twilight_Giant_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_3:_Silver_Knight)** [Beyonder Characteristic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristic "Beyonder Characteristic").

Supplementary Ingredients

- 300 milliliters of Water contaminated by the power of Death.[^1]
- One item worn or carried by a Sequence 3 or above from the (for armor-type items, only one armor plate is required).[^1]
- One piece of Amber with a trapped ancient insect inside.[^1]
- One piece of paper recording 100 or more kills of the oneself.[^1]

### Advancement Ritual

Arrange a complex altar and place the remains of six powerful creatures that were hunted by oneself in the correct order and receive the blessing of a God (Angel-level at minimum).[^7]

The six creatures shouldn't necessarily be monsters, six Demigod-level Beyonders would also suffice.[^8]

### Ritual Essence

As a Sequence 4 [Demon Hunter](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Twilight_Giant_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_4:_Demon_Hunter_\(Witcher\)), the hunting of every powerful creature was a battle of intense mental catharsis. It would leave a deep impression in their life.[^9]

With these powerful mental imprints, they are able to locate themself after consuming the potion, and not be lost in pain and madness.[^9]

This too is a type of [Anchor](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Anchor "Anchor"). As this wasn't the level of an [Angel](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Angel "Angel"), an [Anchor](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Anchor "Anchor") didn't necessarily need to be a believer. It could be replaced by other things, such as the clear marks in one's life that had meaning in mysticism.[^9]

### Consumption Backlash

> Without a sound, the [Demon Hunter](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Twilight_Giant_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_4:_Demon_Hunter_\(Witcher\)) ’s body swells up, turning into a grayish-blue giant with bluish-black veins covering them. They stand several meters tall, with black cracks on their forehead.[^9]
> 
> Every inch of this giant contained the indescribable power of mystery and terror. Apart from their head which still retained the appearance of a human (if they were human), the rest of their body was akin to a Mythical Creature that exerted a strange mental influence.
> 
> In the next second, the skull underlying their head seems to soften as it sinks inwards. With the black crack as its core, their head slowly squirms, as though it is forming a nebulous vortex.
> 
> Such pain would make them, who likely had killed many powerful creatures, unable to restrain themself. They would let out a series of howling cries that could make the minds of ordinary creatures split apart.

### Ritual Effect

> The remains of the six powerful living creatures that were situated in different spots floats up under the invisible power of the ritual, circling the mutated them, using some sort of mysterious connection to awaken some of the memories of this peerless Demon Hunter.[^9]
> 
> > For [Colin Iliad](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Colin_Iliad "Colin Iliad"), it was the experience of hunting “demons.” It was him painfully ending the [former Chief](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hawick "Hawick") ’s attack with his own hands. It was his life of defeating powerful monsters again and again.
> 
> These manifestations appeared like painting on an altar. Sometimes, they would be integrated into them, and sometimes they would be extracted from them, helping them define themself. They would maintain a certain level of clarity during the extreme pain and changes.
> 
> In the memories that had been awakened, they can slowly regain their self-awareness. They would feel their body again and grasp its changes.
> 
> Right on the heels of that, a layer of wings formed from illusory light extended out from behind them. They fuse with the surrounding “paintings” as they constantly shrink inwards. The surface of their body collapses into a silver armor that are firm and beautiful, but doesn't possess any weight to it.

## Sequence 2: Glory

![Potion - Twilight Giant Sequence 2 - Glory](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/lord-of-the-mystery/images/1/10/Potion_-_Twilight_Giant_Sequence_2_-_Glory.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/200?cb=20260330034604)

Potion - Twilight Giant Sequence 2 - Glory

- Overview
- Formula
- Ritual
- Backlash
- Acting Method

### Potion Properties

> A liquid formed from pure, holy, and profound light.[^1]

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> A pure square stone that radiates divine light.[^1]

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- The Crystallization of a Glorious Giant (e.g: The One Eye of [Bladel](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Bladel "Bladel")).[^1]
Or

- The [Crown of Aurmir](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Proof_of_Glory "Proof of Glory").[^1]

Or

- **A [Glory](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Twilight_Giant_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_2:_Glory)** [Beyonder Characteristic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristic "Beyonder Characteristic").

Supplementary Ingredients

- 300 milliliters of blood from the Glorious Giant.[^1]
- One item eroded by the power of [Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Darkness_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Darkness).[^1]
- One corpse buried for over a thousand years.[^1]
- One poem praising oneself (recited and passed down for at least 10 years).[^1]

### Advancement Ritual

Fight an [Angel](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Angel "Angel") or a Creature of equal power on your own (using Beyonder items is allowed).[^1]

Or

Kill enough Enemies or Blasphemers on behalf of the God who blessed you.[^1]

### Ritual Essence

— No data —

— No data —

### Acting Method

There are two directions to act as a Sequence 2: Glory similar to the ritual, although the acting method is not bound to the ritual done, but the direction one wishes to take.[^10]
- the Glorious: Gain glory for yourself
- the Glorifier: One who glorifies others

TRANSLATION NOTE

The [Sequence 2 Glory](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Twilight_Giant_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_2:_Glory) (*荣耀 者, Róngyào zhě, lit: The Glorious One*) has two rituals with different meanings: "the Glorious" (*有 荣耀 的人, Yǒu róngyào de rén, lit: The Person Who Has Glory*) from fighting a strong opponent to gain glory, and "the Glorifier" (*荣耀 授予者, Róngyào shòuyǔ zhě, lit: The One Who Grants Glory*) from granting glory to their God.[^11]
