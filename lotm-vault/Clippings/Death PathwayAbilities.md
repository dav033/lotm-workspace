## Sequence 9: Corpse Collector

**Description** [^2]

From this stage forward, these **Beyonders** will become increasingly afraid of **Sunlight** and **Purification**.

**Traits**

- **Physical Enhancement**: A **Corpse Collector**, upon drinking the potion, will not only gain enhanced physical attributes but will also develop a gloomy presence and a lower body temperature.
	- A **Corpse Collector** will gain resistance to the **Cold**, **Decay**, and **Corrosiveness** of cadaveric auras, making them resistant to such effects to a certain extent.
		- Their gloomy presence and lower body temperature will prevent a **Corpse Collector** from being attacked by **Undead** creatures and **Spirits**.
- **Spirituality**: Their **Spirituality** is slightly enhanced as a **Beyonder**, allowing them to use **Spirit Vision**.
	- **Spirit Vision**: A **Corpse Collector** can use **Spirit Vision** to see non-physical things, such as ghosts and specters. They could see the different parts of a [Soul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul"), deduce a person's health and emotions, and determine if something has a magical aura.
		- Due to their unique nature, a **Corpse Collector** is able to directly see spiritual bodies, including a portion of [Evil Spirits](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Evil_Spirits "Evil Spirits") and restless **Wraiths**, even without activating their **Spirit Vision**.

**New Abilities** [^3] [^4]

- **Knowledge** (**Undead**): **Corpse Collectors** will gain an abundant wealth of information pertaining to the specific characteristics and weaknesses of many **Undead** creatures.
	- This also includes just regular corpses which makes a **Corpse Collector** an expert on autopsy.

## Sequence 8: Gravedigger

**Traits** [^2] [^4]

- **Physical Enhancement**: They become physically stronger and more agile.
- **Spirituality**: They gain an increase in their **Spirituality**.
	- **Spirit Vision**: Their ability to use [Spirit Vision](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_Vision "Spirit Vision") is enhanced.

**New Abilities** [^2] [^4]

- **Spirit Communication**: They are able to communicate with nearby **Spirits**, allowing them to provide the **Beyonder** with help.
	- **Gravediggers** can command them to do simple actions such as tightly grasping onto a target to immobilize them.[^5]
- **Eye of Death**: By pressing down on their eyes, it will turn gray then colorless, allowing them to quickly determine the weaknesses of even unfamiliar **Undead** and **Spirit World** creatures through observation.[^6]
	- It's as though they enter the world of spirits and a kingdom of **Dead** souls, looking down at the enemy from a higher vantage point as they search for a “node.”
		- [Rampagers](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonders#Rampager_\(Losing_Control\) "Beyonders") — **Beyonders** who have lost control—count as **Dead** souls.

## Sequence 7: Spirit Medium

**Traits**

- **Physical Enhancements**: Compared to the previous two **Sequences**, the **Spirit Medium** does not gain significant improvements in physique or agility.
- **Spirituality**: A **Spirit Medium's Spirituality** has been greatly enhanced.
	- **Spiritual Perception**: Their **Spiritual Perception** becomes extremely strong.
		- They can sense the hidden **Spirits** around them even with their eyes closed.
		- **Spirit Vision**: Their **Spirit Vision** becomes more powerful.
- **Spirit Affinity**:<sup>⁂</sup> To the deceased and natural **Spirits** around them, the **Spirit Medium** is akin to that of a fellow **Spirit** and thus not dangerous to them.
	- If the **Spirit Medium** is friendly enough, those deceased and natural **Spirits** will even take the initiative to pass on information to them.

**New Abilities** [^2] [^4]

- **Spirit Channeling**: Their core ability is **Spirit Channeling**, allowing them to directly communicate with the natural **Spirits** and loitering dead souls in the real world.
	- With the advancement of each **Sequence**, the quantity and quality of natural **Spirits**, **Undead** and [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World") creatures they can control and order increases exponentially.
		- They can use different **Spirits** to actualize different kinds of effects, creating various kinds of supernatural phenomena in a rather multifaceted way.
		- Since they can directly communicate with natural **Spirits** and ghosts, they have informants everywhere that can alert them of any danger or information.[^12]
		- It can also be used on living creatures to directly communicate with their **Spirits** to obtain real information, but this method holds a lot of danger for them.
		- **Channeling** a living [Soul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul") is difficult due to that [Soul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul") possessing its own will; however, this could be faced by a **Spirit Medium** with two methods.
			1. **Spirit Mediums** can use their powerful **Spirituality** to triumph over the will of the other person, engaging in a barbaric method of communication.
						2. They can use medication to make the other party relax. Some examples are using **Amantha Essence** and **Eye of the Spirit** medication.
				- They have the means to protect themselves under the risk of being infected by a living **Beyonder's** potent **Spirituality**, which can't be learned or used by **Beyonders** of other [Pathways](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways"). This is the case even when those means are explained to them.[^13]
		- A **Spirit Medium** is able to directly **Channel** recently deceased **Spirits**, such as fallen enemies, in order to obtain even more information from them.[^14]
		- A **Spirit Medium** could communicate with surrounding **Spirits** or through specific directions and descriptions without needing [Ritualistic Magic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ritualistic_Magic "Ritualistic Magic").[^15]
		- However, unlike a **[Dancer](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Eternal_Aeon_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_9:_Dancer "Eternal Aeon Pathway/Abilities")**, they cannot rely on a **Summoning Dance** and a location's uniqueness to attract more distant, hidden **Spirits**.
- **Zombie Disguise**:<sup>⁂</sup> They can also **Disguise** themselves as a **Zombie** in order to better endure the erosion of **Decay**, **Cold**, **Death** and other auras.

**Strengthened Abilities** [^2]

- **Knowledge** (**Mysticism**): They gain knowledge of various kinds of [Ritualistic Magic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ritualistic_Magic "Ritualistic Magic") related to **Spirits**.
	- They are armed with knowledge on how to deal with the dangers that come with **Channeling** a living creature's **Spirit**, as well as the effective communication methods in such a state.[^13]
- **Eye of Death**: This **Beyonder** power becomes more powerful.

## Sequence 6: Spirit Guide

**Description**

It is clear that **Spirit Guides** are very powerful against **Zombies** and **Wraiths**, and can even effectively target [Evil Spirits](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Evil_Spirits "Evil Spirits"). They can directly control groups of ownerless **Undead** that are of similar status to themselves; however, when facing **Higher Sequence Beyonders** of the, their slaves are very prone to rebelling, and so are they themselves.

**New Abilities** [^2] [^4]

- **Language of the Dead**: By speaking a mystical language, they can urge a target's **Spirit** to leave a body, bypassing the physical protection provided by flesh and blood to target the **Spirit Body**.[^4] [^16]
	- This is an advancement of a **Spirit Medium's** powers. It could go from direct communication with the **Spirit Body** to that of commandeering and **Enslavement**.[^16]
		- As **Marionettes** are considered dead, a **Spirit Guide** can interfere and influence a **Marionette**; however, at **Sequence 4**, the core of a **Marionette** can have a **Worm of Spirit** inside. Thus, at those stages it also depends on multiple factors like relative strength of both sides, condition, how much effort is exerted, etc.[^17]
- **Resurrection**: They can turn corpses into living skeletons and **Zombies**; however, this kind of **Resurrection** does not allow the reanimated corpse to have any will or vitality.[^18]

**Strengthened Abilities** [^2]

- **Spirit Channeling**: They can influence and are involved in the [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World"), able to recruit messengers on their own, as well as obtain help from certain [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World") creatures.
	- Compared to a **Spirit Medium** who needs to perform **Spirit Channeling** on site to achieve different effects, they can control and drive deceased and natural **Spirits** to fight in advance.
		- However, making these deceased and natural **Spirits** always follow their commands without being discovered and affected by other factors is a significant problem for a **Spirit Guide**.
				- In comparison with a **Spirit Medium's** capabilities, **Spirit Guides** are now capable of controlling groups of ownerless **Undead** that are of similar status to themselves.
			- The upper limit of **Undead** and **Spirits** they can control numbers in the thousands.[^7]
		- The strength of a **Spirit Guide** depends on the deceased or a natural **Spirits** they have found and controlled. This is the same with **[Spirit Warlocks](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Darkness_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_5:_Spirit_Warlock "Darkness Pathway/Abilities")**.
		- However, the inclination for **Spirit Guides** are deceased **Spirits**, while **[Spirit Warlocks](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Darkness_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_5:_Spirit_Warlock "Darkness Pathway/Abilities")** are better at controlling natural **Spirits**.[^19]
		- They can swap their own **Spirit** with the **Undead** under their command to be effectively immune to attacks that target the **Soul Body**.
		- To combat the **Spell of Harrumph**, [Burman](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Burman "Burman") used this technique to set a trap for [Lumian Lee](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee").[^20]
		- Some examples of the creatures that have been shown to be under their command are:
		- **Shadow-Swallowing Python**: A decaying, skeletal python oozing yellowish-green pus. An **Undead** creature specialized in consuming shadows and shadowy creatures.[^21]
			- Its fangless mouth resembles a vortex, emitting a hurried, piercing sound. It can emit a suction force that tugs at the surrounding shadows, drawing them in.
						- Under their command, they can make the python target only their enemy instead of indiscriminately swallowing their allies or the **Spirit Guide** themself hidden in shadows.
				- **Death Knight**: A towering figure—a knight adorned in tattered black armor and a broadsword.[^21]
			- Pale flames flicker in its eye sockets and putrid liquid seeps from its armor’s crevices, with only sticky flesh clinging to its exposed skin.
				- **Living Shadow**:<sup>⁂</sup> A tall, thin shadow that can emerge from the shadows in translucent form.[^21]
			- It can lunge at a target, resembling the possession of **Wraiths** and evil spirits, but lacks the speed to complete the process in a mere blink.
				- **Goddess of the Lake**: A powerful type of spirit, one that brings horror.[^22]
			- It appears as a foggy lake shimmering with light, producing a tranquil beauty. In the middle, concentric circles ripple out as beautiful and illusory figures float up.
				- Various **Undead** creatures can appear from the void and help them complete different tasks.
			- They can drag the **Spirit Guide** into the shadows, disappearing without a trace, even if their master is knocked unconscious.[^21]
						- They can make the **Spirit Guide** swiftly soar, as if dragged by an unseen force.[^21]
						- They can grab onto a target to restrain them.[^20]
						- They can control a weak spirit from great distances for communication, reaching from [Backlund](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Backlund "Backlund") to [Tingen City](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tingen_City "Tingen City").[^23] [^24]
- **Knowledge** (**Spirit World**): They have considerable information about the [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World").

## Sequence 5: Gatekeeper

**Traits**

- **Physical Enhancement**: They become stronger and quicker, one step can allow them to suddenly close the distance against opponents and thrust at lightning speed, bringing vigorous wind currents.[^16]
- **Spiritual Perception**: They can acutely sense if an unknown creature is crossing the [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World") and approaching a place near them.[^25]

**New Abilities** [^2]

- **Door to the Underworld**: They can sense the entrance to the [Underworld](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Underworld "Underworld"), allowing them to control the **Undead** inside, as though they watched over the gates that separated the dead from the living.
	- They can use the powers of the **Door to the Underworld** to some extent. They can imprint the door's mark onto the palm of their hand and summon it at critical moments.
		- It can be used to drag targets behind the gate which led to the [Underworld](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Underworld "Underworld"), where all living creatures immediately die.
			- The **Door** has a terrifying suction force. Most of it would be directed towards a target, though the remnant forces would continue to pull on the surroundings.[^26]
				- The main target can't help but stumble and be drawn in, while those nearby would find it difficult to even take a step.
								- The suction force generated from the **Door** can also be used to clear the environment of airborne poison.
								- **Gatekeepers** are able to use the **Door** to absorb the **Fog of War** of the [Red Priest Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Priest_Pathway "Red Priest Pathway").[^27]
						- Aside from the suction force, bloody, skinless arms, slimy tentacles with teeth, and bluish-black vines with baby faces emerge from the **Door to the Underworld** to grab targets and drag them within.[^28]
				- It can be used let the [Underworld](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Underworld "Underworld") descend upon reality.[^22]
- **Internal Underworld**: Using their bodies as a cage, they can house numerous [Souls](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul"), deceased, and natural **Spirits**, allowing them to bring an **Undead** army wherever they go in a non eye-catching manner.[^29]
	- Normally, the entrance to the **Internal Underworld** is at the center of the **Gatekeeper's** brow.
		- With this, they gain various unique abilities and possess powerful helpers, for example:
		- **Death Envoy**: A unique ability of [Ludwell's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ludwell "Ludwell"). It turns his arms illusory, letting it instantly cover a significant distance and either remotely extract a target's **Spirit Body** [^30] or squash them.[^26]
			- As the pale palm approaches, the target would seem to be possessed by a **Wraith** or [Evil Spirit](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Evil_Spirits "Evil Spirits"). The soft sobbing sounds of the **Death Envoy** causes the target's body to go numb, as though their blood had frozen.
				- This makes it difficult to produce an effective response, the target can only watch in despair as death approaches, feeling their vitality deplete at an increasing rate.
						- It also allows [Ludwell](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ludwell "Ludwell") to produce a silent screech, striking and temporarily freezing attacks midair.[^16]
				- **Pus of Man**:<sup>⁂</sup> A strange deceased spirit that [Ulika](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ulika "Ulika") fused with, it will quickly dissipate if it doesn't use a human body as a "house".[^22]
			- Even after undergoing **Purification** from the [Golden Sundial](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Golden_Sundial "Golden Sundial"), [Ulika](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ulika "Ulika") survived by transforming into a miniature form with raven-black skin and flowing, sticky pus.
		- Powerful **Spirits** and **Undead** creatures may erode their body from within.
		- The **Death Envoy** within [Ludwell](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ludwell "Ludwell") turned him into a half-human, half-dead existence, allowing him to enter the [Underworld](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Underworld "Underworld") without perishing.[^29]
		- As this can be considered a miniature [Underworld](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Underworld "Underworld"), **Gatekeepers** are a prime target of possession for [Evil Spirits](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Evil_Spirits "Evil Spirits") as they provide an adequate environment for their existence.

**Strengthened Abilities**

- **Spirit Channeling**: The maximum number of **Undead** and **Spirits** that a **Gatekeeper** can control has slightly increased, but it doesn't change much.[^7]
	- Some examples of the **Spirits** they have been shown to use are:
		- **Pale Girl**:<sup>⁂</sup> A nearly illusory girl with pale skin, bluish-green eyes, and jet black lips.[^22]
			- She has ghastly pale and translucent limbs that could bring a chill to the **Spirit Body**, which can help the **Gatekeeper** resist the influence of a **[Nightmare](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Darkness_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_7:_Nightmare "Darkness Pathway/Abilities")**.
				- Aside from controlling the **Undead** manning the [Black Tulip](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Black_Tulip "Black Tulip"), [Ludwell](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ludwell "Ludwell") summoned translucent, illusory ferocious creatures from the sea, charging forward like an unending tidal wave.[^9]

## Sequence 4: Undying

**Traits** [^2]

- **Physical Enhancement**: Physical attacks are no longer fatal unless their body is completely destroyed.
- **Spirit Body**: An **Undying** is able to exist in a state that is between a physical body and a **Spirit Body**.[^31]

**New Abilities** [^2]

- **Reincarnation**: They possess a long lifespan; however, every *60 years*, they will die and then **Resurrect**, forgetting all their memories and resetting their madness levels. From there, they must then slowly retrieve their memories, as if starting a new life.[^32] [^33]
	- This is not completely without risk, the **Undying** would leave behind a death imprint in the [River of Eternal Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/River_of_Eternal_Darkness "River of Eternal Darkness") with each **Death**. Once the number of **Deaths** reaches a certain level, their true bodies might be attracted to this [Sefirah](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirot "Sefirot") one day, causing them to truly die.[^34]
		- Dying too many times leads to an **Undying's** death imprints being washed away from the [River of Eternal Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/River_of_Eternal_Darkness "River of Eternal Darkness"), making their memories impossible to recover; their intelligence and rationality will degrade over time, eventually turning into wandering **Undead** monsters.
- **Underworld Authority**: They are more closely connected to the [Underworld](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Underworld "Underworld") and can directly utilize its power.
	- **Sealing**: They can use the [Underworld](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Underworld "Underworld") to **Seal** objects. This includes [Sealed Artifacts](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sealed_Artifacts "Sealed Artifacts"), allowing them to reduce their negative effects.[^35]
		- Initially, [Creeping Hunger](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Creeping_Hunger "Creeping Hunger") needed to be fed a human’s soul and flesh every day.[^36] After [Azik Eggers](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Azik_Eggers "Azik Eggers") **Sealed** it, it only needed to be fed every time it was used.[^37]
				- After fusing with [Mr. A](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mr._A "Mr. A"), [Creeping Hunger](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Creeping_Hunger "Creeping Hunger") lost its **Seal**, gained living characteristics, and periodically praised the [True Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/True_Creator "True Creator").[^38] After [Azik Eggers](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Azik_Eggers "Azik Eggers") **Sealed** it once more, it reverted to how it was before,[^39] though it would praise the [True Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/True_Creator "True Creator") if told to do so.[^40]
		- **Spirit World Traversal**: They can use their connection to the [Underworld](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Underworld "Underworld") as an anchor to gain a certain degree of **Spirit World Traversal**.[^41]

**Strengthened Abilities** [^2]

- **Door to the Underworld**: Directly uses the power of the [Underworld](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Underworld "Underworld") to create a combat environment favorable to themselves.
	- This battlefield will inflict enemies with the attraction, summoning, and ravings from within the **Underworld**.
		- Additionally, if lucky or well-connected in the [Underworld](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Underworld "Underworld"), they could summon dangerous **Undead** creatures into reality.
		- This includes **Styx Servants**, which are equivalent to **Ferrymen**.[^42]
- **Language of the Dead**: At this stage, the **Language of the Dead** will evolve into a **Spell** system related to **Death**, **Spirits**, **Zombies**, **Withering**, and **Enslavement**.
	- **Withering Wind**:<sup>⁂</sup> It brings about a gust of **Cold** wind that silently causes **Withering**.
		- [Patrick Bryan](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Patrick_Bryan "Patrick Bryan") created a cold gust of **Withering Wind** that caused a glass window to quickly crumble into countless "snowflakes".[^41]

**Mythical Creature Form**

- As **Sequence 4 Demigods** they receive some level of **Godhood**, and with it comes an incomplete [Mythical Creature Form](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mythical_Creature_Form "Mythical Creature Form"). Disregarding all consequences, they can release their incomplete [Mythical Creature Form](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mythical_Creature_Form "Mythical Creature Form") to become an incomplete **Quetzalcoatl**, also known as a **Feathered Serpent**.

## Sequence 3: Ferryman

**Traits** [^2]

- **Physical Enhancement**: Most forms of attack, aside from **Purification** (like that of the [Tyrant](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tyrant_Pathway "Tyrant Pathway") or [Sun Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sun_Pathway "Sun Pathway")), are ineffective against the **Ferryman**, as though no one can kill a dead person.[^43]

**Authorities** [^2]

- **Death**: They wield partial authority over **Death**.
	- Any living being that makes eye contact with the **Ferryman** will die instantly; even entities with **Godhood** will suffer devastating damage.[^43]
		- Even without direct eye contact, those caught by the **Ferryman's** gaze will slowly **Wither** away, as if being ferried across the river of **Death** inch by inch.
		- From here on, they will become a part of the [Underworld](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Underworld "Underworld") and **Death** itself.[^44]
		- Near the **River Styx** or its tributaries, **Ferrymen** can effectively utilize the power of those rivers.
				- They can float on the **River Styx** without sinking. Even when facing the true [River of Eternal Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/River_of_Eternal_Darkness "River of Eternal Darkness") or its tributaries, they can remain afloat for a very short time— *two seconds* at their current stage.
		- The **Ferryman** traverses back and forth between **Life** and **Death.**
		- Those touched by their left hand will almost irreversibly approach **Death**.
				- Those touched by their right hand will reverse the process of **Death**, regaining **Life.**

**Strengthened Abilities** [^2]

- **Undying**: Instead of dying, **Reviving**, and losing all their memories, they would instead gradually transform into a sentient **Undead** creature over time.[^45]
	- This also means that they will not instantly die in the **Underworld**.

**Mythical Creature Form**

- As a **Sequence 3 Beyonder**, they can respond to prayers within a certain range.

## Sequence 2: Death Consul

**Authorities** [^2]

- **Death**: "They" wield partial authority over **Death**.
	- **Absolute Death**: "They" can strip all vitality from an area. Before "Them", most **Resurrection** or **Substitution** abilities become ineffective.
		- A [**Miracle Invoker's**](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fool_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_2:_Miracle_Invoker "Fool Pathway/Abilities") **Resurrection** that involves **History** and **Change** is an exception.
		- **Nation of the Dead**: "They" can create a large city-sized domain filled with **Death** and **Pallor** where life not strong enough will instantly die and transform into **Undead** creatures.
		- Even beings with **Godhood** will begin to slowly **Wither** and **Perish**.
		- **Dead Death Consuls** are still **Death Consuls**. The boundary between **Life** and **Death** no longer applying to "Them", making "Them" extremely hard to kill.
		- Only by relying on special powers and methods can a **Death Consul** are be completely erased and reverted back into [Beyonder Characteristics](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristics "Beyonder Characteristics").
				- This doesn't mean "They" are immortal as "Their" consciousness would gradually erode, becoming an eternally living walking corpse.
		- "They" can manipulate the objects produced by the corruption from the power at [the source of the **River Styx**](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/River_of_Eternal_Darkness "River of Eternal Darkness").[^46]
		- The spring water of the [Samaritan Women's Spring](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Underground_Trier#Samaritan_Women's_Spring "Underground Trier") has multiple effects including repairing **Souls**, inducing **Sleep**, bringing complete **Death** or true **Dreams**, and granting lucidity in **Dreams**. "They" can make it manifest only one quality and enhance that effect.
			- [Azik Eggers](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Azik_Eggers "Azik Eggers") made it so that anyone who touches the spring water will fall into a long **Sleep**, "Himself" included.
				- It has a slight effect on **True Gods**, but not much, unless that deity is already very weak or in a state of slumber.
								- Reversing its effects requires more spring water processed in a similar way but with the opposite effect or help from a **True God** with the corresponding authority.
- **Enslavement**: **Death Consuls** are capable of issuing commands that **Beyonders** below **Sequence 2** of the are nearly incapable of resisting.
	- Even **Undead** creatures of the [Angel](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Angels "Angels") level—including [Evil Spirits](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Evil_Spirits "Evil Spirits") —are extraordinarily vulnerable when facing "Them".
- **Undead Judgment**: "They" do more damage against the **Undead** and have a suppression effect based on status, this applies to all **Undead**.[^44] [^47]

**Strengthened Abilities**

- **King of the Dead**: A **Death Consul** is a **King of the Dead**, who is capable of housing a vast **Undead** army within "Their" own **Internal Underworld**.
	- At the same time, "They" can find and directly summon helpers from the [Underworld](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Underworld "Underworld").
- Other **Beyonder** powers from previous **Sequences** have been enhanced.

**Mythical Creature Form**

- "They" become a complete [Mythical Creature](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mythical_Creature_Form "Mythical Creature Form"). "Their" [Mythical Creature Form](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mythical_Creature_Form "Mythical Creature Form") is a **Feathered Serpent** ([Quetzalcōātl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quetzalc%C5%8D%C4%81tl "wikipedia:Quetzalcōātl")), a serpent that is illusory and real. "Their" entire body is covered with huge dark-green scales while white feathers stained with pale yellow oil grow out from the gaps. On each feather, illusory black tubes extend outward, and each scale and feather bears strange symbols of different shapes.[^42]
	- Just the mere sight of "Them" would cause one's flesh to decompose, turning all those who look at "Them" into **Zombies** and various other **Undead** creatures.[^48]
		- A phantom image of "Their" [Mythical Creature Form](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mythical_Creature_Form "Mythical Creature Form") can give the sensation of rapid death, a type of damage that **Mirror Substitution** couldn't transfer.[^46]
		- In "Their" **Mythical Creature Form**, "They" are capable of spatting out a **Pale-White Flame** that is able to cover an entire mausoleum.[^48]

## Sequence 1: Pale Emperor

**Authorities** [^2]

- **Pallor** (**Pale World**): "They" can create a world that is devoid of all color, leaving only **Pallor**.
	- In this **Pale World**, colors **Die** and **Beyonder** powers **Wither**. Any **Beyonder** power that manifests color will suffer immense weakening and will eventually fail completely.
		- This heavily restricts the **Painted Worlds** of the [Sublunary Eye Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sublunary_Eye_Pathway "Sublunary Eye Pathway").[^49]
				- Black [^50] and white [^51] both count as a colors, they, too, will turn **Pale**.
			- A clean and bright spear of flame, an **Unshadowed Spear**, will also be eroded, but it itself would disperse the **Pallor**.[^51]
		- Inanimate objects and abstract concepts can also be weathered. Even the concept of **Death** itself can weather away and dissipate.[^50]
		- The effects of being weathered under the **Pallor** happens extremely quickly; *thousands* of *years* would appear to be washed away in a short span of *2 seconds*.
				- Weathered objects would display some characteristics of a **Feathered Serpent**, namely, they would grow white feathers soaked with light-yellow oil.
			- What was labeled as “ **Death** ” wasn’t the **Endpoint**. When those affected become weathered or degraded to a certain extent, they would reform, as ethereal auras quickly grow.
				- The power of the **Earth** and the **Rules** of the world would also be eroded.
			- In the Pale-white Nation of the [Underworld](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Underworld "Underworld"), falling *40-50 meters* is no different than jumping from a *5-6 meter* building.[^52] [^1]
- **Enslavement**: "They" possess the authority of **Enslavement**.
	- **Soul Control**: "They" have a chance to directly extract the target's **Spirit Body** from their body. Even if unsuccessful, it will cause the target severe mental chaos and diminished vitality.
		- **Sovereign of Spirits**: Without deliberate effort, "They" appear extreme noble, devoid of the aura of the living—like an illusory deity that lorded over the countless **Undead**.
		- "They" can suppress and even **Enslave** any creature with a [Soul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul"), no longer limited to **Dead** creatures and **Beyonders** of the.
				- Through this aura, the living's thoughts will gradually **Die** along with their body until they become a corpse and "Their" **Slave**.[^53] [^50]
				- **Sovereign of Spirits** is not a **Beyonder** power, but rather a trait derived from the authority of **Enslavement**.
- **Death**: They wield partial authority over **Death**.
	- **Death Decree**: Any target seen or pointed at by "Them" will **Die** instantly.
		- This works on all beings below the level of a [True God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/True_God "True God"), and is capable of harming [True Gods](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/True_Gods "True Gods").[^54]
				- This is a mechanistic ability, so the way to avoid it is not to be seen or pointed at by "Them", or use other methods that can counter it mechanically.[^54]
		- "They" can create a symbol which appears like a long-feathered bird or a **Coiled Feathered Serpent**, in order to **Seal** targets.[^55]
		- The symbol absorbs the surrounding light and will quickly turn corporeal, before flying towards the target, causing the surroundings to become increasingly dim, suffering another round of **Weathering** as if the **Deity** in charge of **Death** had passed the **Final Judgment**, until it fuses with them. The symbol constantly influences the target until "They" stop responding.

## Sequence 0: Death (Eternal Sleeper)

**Authorities** [^2]

- **Death**: The authority of **Death** ascends to a conceptual level.
	- **Life** will **Die**, memories will **Die**, the connections between things will **Die**, and even the very imprints of existence will **Die**.
		- Although [Beyonder Characteristics](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristics "Beyonder Characteristics") are **Indestructible**, they too will briefly **Die** when facing this authority.
			- "They" can directly sever the connection between the **[Fool's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fool_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Fool "Fool Pathway/Abilities")** [Beyonder Characteristics](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristics "Beyonder Characteristics") and the [Historical Void](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Historical_Void "Historical Void") or directly kill the [Beyonder Characteristics](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristics "Beyonder Characteristics") themselves, thereby completely killing the **[Fool](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fool_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Fool "Fool Pathway/Abilities")** and preventing "Their" **Resurrection**.[^56]
				- "They" can make the **Death** of **Marionettes**, **Paper Figurine Substitutes**, and other similar clones and **Substitutes** equal to the **Death** of the main body.[^57]
			- "They" can't affect the **[Lord of Mysteries](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lord_of_Mysteries "Lord of Mysteries")**, but the **[Eternal Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Eternal_Darkness "Eternal Darkness")** can do so.
- **Pallor**: The ultimate form of **Withering**, the fading of all things.
	- Under **Pallor**, the mind, will, and **Beyonder** powers alike gradually fall silent and succumb to deathly stillness.
		- The **Purification** of the **[Sun](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sun_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Sun "Sun Pathway/Abilities")** restrains **Pallor**.[^58]
- **Endpoint**: "They" are the final **Destination** of all things, the common **Endpoint** of all **Life**.
	- This authority overlaps with the authority of the **[Mother Goddess of Depravity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Goddess_of_Depravity "Mother Goddess of Depravity")** and **[Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Darkness_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Darkness "Darkness Pathway/Abilities")**.[^59]
		- By relying on the [Door Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Door_Pathway "Door Pathway") 's powers, the [Underworld](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Underworld "Underworld") was derived from this authority.
		- Within the [Underworld](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Underworld "Underworld") is the **River Styx**, which is a projection of the [River of Eternal Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/River_of_Eternal_Darkness "River of Eternal Darkness") ' tributary.[^60]
		- "They" could establish "Their" own **Cycle of Reincarnation** with aid from the **[Mother](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Mother "Mother Pathway/Abilities")**.
		- This will be similar to the **Cycle of Reincarnation** formed from the **[Eternal Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Eternal_Darkness "Eternal Darkness")** and **[Mother Goddess of Depravity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Goddess_of_Depravity "Mother Goddess of Depravity")**, albeit limited to a single star system.[^61]
- **Undead**: "They" are the very source of **Undead** existence, the master of all **Undead**.
	- "Their" influence over **High-Sequence Beyonders** of the and other high ranking **Undead** can bypass barriers and **Seals**.
		- [Artificial Death](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Artificial_Death "Artificial Death") 's low level of [activation](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Activation "Activation") allowed it to influence **High-Sequence Beyonders** of the that failed their [advancement](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Advancement "Advancement"),[^12] including Elder [Hawick](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hawick "Hawick") of the [City of Silver](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/City_of_Silver "City of Silver").[^62] [^63]
				- Using [Artificial Death](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Artificial_Death "Artificial Death"), the [Evernight Goddess](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Evernight_Goddess "Evernight Goddess") influenced [Bladel](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Bladel "Bladel") to interfere with [Amon](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Amon "Amon") [^63] and controlled a special **Wraith** to assist in the hunt for [Kotar](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Kotar "Kotar").[^64] Both of these events took place in the [Forsaken Land of the Gods](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Forsaken_Land_of_the_Gods "Forsaken Land of the Gods").
		- This authority overlaps with the authority of the **[Mother Goddess of Depravity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Goddess_of_Depravity "Mother Goddess of Depravity")** and **[Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Darkness_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Darkness "Darkness Pathway/Abilities")**.[^59]
		- The **Eternal Sleeper** leans toward the **Undead** while the **[Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Darkness_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Darkness "Darkness Pathway/Abilities")** leans toward **Natural Spirits**.[^65]
- **Enslavement**: "They" are the **Enslaver** of all things who have **Souls**.[^66]
