## Sequence 7: Nightmare

![Potion - Darkness Sequence 7 - Nightmare](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/lord-of-the-mystery/images/4/49/Potion_-_Darkness_Sequence_7_-_Nightmare.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/200?cb=20260330032909)

Potion - Darkness Sequence 7 - Nightmare

### Potion Properties

> Illusory liquid filled with countless phantasms, as if materialized from dreams.[^2]

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> The gem-like object possessed an aura of deep blackness, exuding an enigmatic and profound presence.[^5] [^6] A dark, gem-like gelatinous substance of profound depth.[^2]

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- Dream-Eating Raven's Heart.[^1]
- Nightmare Shadow.[^1]

Or

- **A [Nightmare](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Darkness_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_7:_Nightmare "Darkness Pathway/Abilities")** [Beyonder Characteristic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristic "Beyonder Characteristic").

Supplementary Ingredients

- 100 milliliters of [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World") Moon Water (Derived from the information of the Crimson Moon in the Spirit World).[^1]
- Dream-Eating Raven's Illusory Feather.[^1]
- 1 Paper documenting personal nightmares (within the last two weeks).[^1]
- 9 drops of Moonflower Hydrosol.[^1]

### Lingering Effect

> Undigested potion causes terrible memory and schizophrenia.[^7] It is more so to say that one cannot effectively differentiate between dreams and reality.[^8]
> 
> The exact dreams vary:
> 
> - [Dunn](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Dunn_Smith "Dunn Smith") in particular dreams about a house of his dead companions, including [Kenley](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Kenley_White "Kenley White"). Whenever he eats his companion's [Beyonder Characteristics](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristics "Beyonder Characteristics"), they would live in his dreams.[^9]
> - [Leonard](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Leonard_Mitchell "Leonard Mitchell") would see [Dunn](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Dunn_Smith "Dunn Smith") and [Klein](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") waiting for him around the corner.[^10]
> 
> They can either indulge in it or try to remind themselves to keep the line between reality and dreams straight.

### Acting Method

- Enter people's dreams and be a nightmare to them.

> Dark liquid immersed with countless black hairs.[^2]

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

- Dark Brain.[^1]
- Malignant Moon Guardian's Heart.[^1]

Or

### Advancement Ritual

Collect bloodline characteristics of [True Deities](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Gods "Gods") ' descendants and use them to draw a soul-soothing magic circle.

- **Note 1**: Different Deity bloodlines will grant distinct Nightwatcher abilities.[^1]
- **Note 2**: Pathway-switchers must use bloodlines from one of the 3 corresponding Deities of the [Eternal Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Eternal_Darkness "Eternal Darkness") group.[^1]
- **Note 3**: Both descendants born after becoming a god and before becoming a god work. Even the bloodline from before becoming a god will have special properties.[^14]

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- Pelt of a Dark Demonic Wolf.[^1]
Or

- The [Beyonder Characteristic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristic "Beyonder Characteristic") of the [Mother of the Sky Philanias](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_of_the_Sky "Mother of the Sky").[^1]

Or

- **A [Servant of Concealment](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Darkness_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_2:_Servant_of_Concealment "Darkness Pathway/Abilities")** [Beyonder Characteristic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristic "Beyonder Characteristic").

Supplementary Ingredients

- 300 milliliters of Demonic Wolf of Secrecy's Blood.[^1]
- 1 portion of Spirit World intelligence from a hidden location.[^1]
- 1 Feather of a Feathered Serpent.[^1]
- 10 drops of Giant's Blood ( [Twilight Giant Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Twilight_Giant_Pathway "Twilight Giant Pathway")).[^1]

### Advancement Ritual

- Sever all social ties and external influences.[^1]
- Avoid influencing others or being influenced by others.[^1]
- Live isolated in darkness and silence for 3 years without going mad.[^1]
- **Note 1**: [Worms of Time](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_1:_Worm_of_Time "Error Pathway/Abilities") can normally use a Bug whenever there are time limits, however, the passage of time cannot be directly accelerated to reduce the real time required because this invalidates the second point of the ritual.[^1]
	- **Note 2**: Ritual assistance would need to come from the [Fool Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fool_Pathway "Fool Pathway") or the [Wheel of Fortune Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Wheel_of_Fortune_Pathway "Wheel of Fortune Pathway").[^1]
	- **Note 3**: Slumber doesn't count. So, transmigrators like Klein or the Evernight Goddess didn't meet the condition since there is a restriction: living alone.[^15]
