## Sequence 8: Barbarian

![Potion - Black Emperor Sequence 8 - Barbarian](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/lord-of-the-mystery/images/9/96/Potion_-_Black_Emperor_Sequence_8_-_Barbarian.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/200?cb=20260331061307)

Potion - Black Emperor Sequence 8 - Barbarian

### Potion Properties

> A translucent black liquid with miniature organs, such as internal organs and tongue, sinking and emerging. [^1]

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> Crystalline horn-like structures covered with short black hairs.[^1]

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

Or

- **A [Barbarian](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Black_Emperor_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_8:_Barbarian)** [Beyonder Characteristic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristic "Beyonder Characteristic").

Supplementary Ingredients

- 1 [Deep-Grained Walnut](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/List_of_Mysticism_Ingredients#Deep-Grained_Walnut).[^3]

- 10 milliliters of the [extract obtained from soaking poplar bark](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/List_of_Mysticism_Ingredients#Soaking_Poplar_Bark_Extracted).[^3]
- 100 milliliters of [Liquor](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/List_of_Mysticism_Ingredients#Liquor).[^3]

— No data —

### Acting Method

For every system that imposes order, there must be other systems that resist that order and adhere to their own rules. Sometimes, the best way to deal with these heterogeneous systems is not to impose your order on them, but to meet them head-on.[^2]

## Sequence 7: Briber

![Potion - Black Emperor Sequence 7 - Briber](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/lord-of-the-mystery/images/2/22/Potion_-_Black_Emperor_Sequence_7_-_Briber.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/200?cb=20260331061320)

Potion - Black Emperor Sequence 7 - Briber

### Potion Properties

> A clear, black liquid that bubbles constantly and looks delicious.[^1]

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> Black floral patterns that highlight a ghastly white face.[^1]

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

Or

- **A [Briber](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Black_Emperor_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_7:_Briber)** [Beyonder Characteristic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristic "Beyonder Characteristic").

Supplementary Ingredients

- 5 drops of [Golden Jimsonweed Juice](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/List_of_Mysticism_Ingredients#Golden_Jimsonweed_Juice).[^4]
- 5 drops of [Black Jimsonweed Juice](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/List_of_Mysticism_Ingredients#Black_Jimsonweed_Juice).[^4]
- 4 drops of [Fantasy Grass Essential Oil](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/List_of_Mysticism_Ingredients#Fantasy_Grass_Essential_Oil).[^4]
- 80 milliliters of [Red Wine](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/List_of_Mysticism_Ingredients#Red_Wine).[^4]

— No data —

### Acting Method

Every person, organization, or system has a pivot point that can be pressured to force change. A briber doesn't waste energy fighting the whole system, bribing everyone, or using overly luxurious bribes to buy off top figures. Instead, they find that one person – who, due to their personal desires, fears, or needs, naturally becomes a key hub – and then make that person understand that serving the briber actually serves themselves.[^2]

## Sequence 6: Baron of Corruption

![Potion - Black Emperor Sequence 6 - Baron of Corruption](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/lord-of-the-mystery/images/7/7c/Potion_-_Black_Emperor_Sequence_6_-_Baron_of_Corruption.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/200?cb=20260331061334)

Potion - Black Emperor Sequence 6 - Baron of Corruption

### Potion Properties

> A translucent, light black liquid with a sweet, enticing aroma and shimmering, sparkling light.[^1]

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> Black, swollen human surface organs that emit a rotten smell (often fused with the nose).[^1]

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- The nose of a human-faced baboon,[^1]
- 1 rotting corpse flower;[^1]

Or

- **A [Baron of Corruption](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Black_Emperor_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_6:_Baron_of_Corruption_\(Corruption\))** [Beyonder Characteristic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristic "Beyonder Characteristic").

Supplementary Ingredients

- 60 ml of human-faced baboon blood,[^1]
- 10 drops of gray amber perfume,[^1]
- 60 ml of strong fruit liquor,[^1]
- 10 g of lead powder.[^1]

— No data —

### Acting Method

Infiltrate an existing system (like the mafia, a family, an army) and subtly corrupt its rules and order from within, causing it to artificially lean in your favor and serve you.[^2]

## Sequence 5: Mentor of Disorder

![Potion - Black Emperor Sequence 5 - Mentor of Disorder](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/lord-of-the-mystery/images/5/5c/Potion_-_Black_Emperor_Sequence_5_-_Mentor_of_Disorder.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/200?cb=20260331061347)

Potion - Black Emperor Sequence 5 - Mentor of Disorder

- Overview
- Formula
- Ritual
- Backlash
- Acting Method

### Potion Properties

> A jet-black liquid that generates its own vortexes and waves, sometimes expanding and sometimes contracting.[^1]

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> A tightly clenched, semi-transparent, jet-black fist. The fist gives a direct sense of power and unnerving strangeness. The patterns, light spots and nails on it seem to follow normal rules, yet they are full of incongruous meaning, as if they are hiding a lot of madness and disorder.[^1]

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- 1 Fairy Spark (possesses self-awareness).[^1]
- The head of "Child of the Forest" (it's an almost black dark green baboon with deformed horns resembling a crown).[^1]

Or

- **A [Mentor of Disorder](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Black_Emperor_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_5:_Mentor_of_Disorder_\(Disorder\))** [Beyonder Characteristic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristic "Beyonder Characteristic").

Supplementary Ingredients

- 100 ml of a "Child of the Forest's" blood [^1]
- 7 drops of cedarwood hydrolat,[^1]
- An ear of a serial traitor (has betrayed at least twice),[^1]
- 1 frangipani flower.[^1]

### Advancement Ritual

Become the dominant force in a city's underground order, causing the city's crime rate to increase more than tenfold; this state must last for at least three months.[^1]

### Ritual Essence

— No data —

— No data —

### Acting Method

Mainly focuses on destroying order, teaching others to create disorder.[^2]
