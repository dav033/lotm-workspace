> This is taken from [Lumian](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee") ’s advancement. Only parts of it genuinely come from the potion itself, albeit exaggerated due to his fragile balance. Low sequence normally doesn't bring someone to death's door.

### Acting Method

- Everyone has two identities. One, the prey, and the other, the hunter.[^8]
- Even the weakest hunter is a hunter. It's possible for them to harm stronger prey.[^8]
- Observe the environment, be familiar with the environment, and use the environment..[^8] [^9] [^10]
- Distinguish what can be eaten in the wild and what can't.[^9]
- Staying calm is crucial. When faced with unexpected prey and no time to prepare, calmness is even more vital.[^10]

- Grasp a lot of information and rumors, and also understand the provocation target. Only by doing this will you be able to make him lose his mind and cause his brain to be burnt out with a single sentence.[^14]

- Provoke someone stronger, a higher being, or even fate itself.[^16] [^17]

### Acting Method

- Pyromaniacs not only ignite matter but also the mind and society.[^22]

- Flames aren't just lethal; they can also be used as deterrence, intimidation, and a signal.It is necessary for Pyromaniacs to combine their flames with traps.
- The Pyromaniac wreaked havoc and caused utter catastrophe. As for Pyromaniacs, they could willingly unleash disaster and destruction upon anyone.[^24]

> 
> They would feel excruciating pain in their body, mind, and spirit, their thoughts growing increasingly hazy, as if about to dissipate into this area, this [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World").

### Ritual Effect

> Right after performing the ritual, the [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World") 's information of the area had not yet fully changed, still partially corresponding to the typhoon. There was a rapid influx of information about them relying on their own abilities to change the weather, creating an extremely strong connection between them, this area, and the corresponding [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World").[^43]
> 
> This area, this [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World"), resisted the fusion of their spirit and consciousness, because they were the destroyer, the changer—they would inevitably be rejected in the short term.
> 
> At this moment, their blurry mind would hear voices—coming from their [Anchors](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Anchor "Anchor").
> 
> Their body tears apart, shattering, turning to ash, including their mirror self and the them slumbering within the mirror if they are a [Unaging](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Demoness_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_3:_Unaging_\(Demoness_of_Unaging\) "Demoness Pathway/Abilities").
> 
> The next moment, a wavering, violet-tinged blue flame ignited amidst the remnant ashes.
> 
> This flame rapidly expanded and intensified, forging within it black iron bones, an indescribable tangle of complex symbols and patterns.
> 
> They would gradually form their complete [Mythical Creature Form](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mythical_Creature_Form "Mythical Creature Form") at this moment. But the exact form depends on the combined traits of their pathways.
> 
> A Mythical Creature form blended with [Demoness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Demoness_Pathway "Demoness Pathway") traits:
> 
> > They gradually form a towering, five-to-six-meter figure, like the legendary fire giant comes to life.
> > 
> > Immediately, their face coalesce, its iron-black hue gleaming with a metallic luster, yet still clearly handsome and masculine in appearance.
> > 
> > Blood-red hair also sprouted, each strand as thick as a python, some rotating black-and-white eyeballs at the top, others opening jaws with venomous fangs.
> 
> Their body will then rapidly shrink, the violet-tinged blue flames on its surface dissipating. Returning to their human form, now male if they were female, exuding a powerful, masculine aura.
