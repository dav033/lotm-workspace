## Sequence 9: Lawyer

**New Abilities** [^2] [^3]

- **Eloquence**:<sup>⁂</sup> **Lawyers** are masters of eloquence and reasoning, able to influence judgment, thoughts, and conclusions through their words, actions, and established processes.[^4]
	- They can, to some extent, distort or guide the target's thinking, making them seem approachable and trustworthy.[^2]
		- Instead of negotiating further, [Klein Moretti](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") readily agreed to a major financial transaction without any hesitation.[^5]
		- This influence is difficult to detect as **Spiritual Intuition** alone might not be enough to notice their deception.
		- [Klein Moretti](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") was unable to realize that his thoughts had been distorted until [Jurgen Cooper](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Jurgen_Cooper "Jurgen Cooper") mentioned the abnormality, and even then, he wasn’t certain.[^5]
		- This power can be passively triggered without the explicit intent of the **Lawyer**.[^5]

## Sequence 8: Barbarian

**Description**

Problems that cannot be solved by the law will be solved by force. This is also one of the rules.[^6]

**Traits [^2] [^8]**

- **Physical Enhancement**: Upon advancement, a **Barbarian** will gain various physical improvements such as a physical strength and constitution that breaks the "rules" of a normal human body.[^6] [^3]
- **Mental Resistance**: In addition to their **Physical Enhancement**, upon advancement, a **Barbarian** will also possess a high resistance to psychological influences.[^6]

## Sequence 7: Briber

**New Abilities** [^2] [^3]

- **Bribe**: The core ability of a **Briber**. By giving their target an actual or symbolic **Bribe**, they would be able to achieve several domineering effects over their target.
	- A **Bribe** can be anywhere between giving a target money to throwing an object near the direction of the target; even if the target does not "accept" the object, it still counts as a **Bribe**.[^9]
		- There are *4 types* of **Bribes**: **Weaken**, **Charm**, **Arrogance**, and **Connection**.
		- **Weaken**: For a certain amount of time, this significantly weakens the target's attacks, defenses, and control over the **Briber**.[^9]
				- **Arrogance**: The target will become proud and **Arrogant**, lowering their intelligence and leading them to make erroneous judgments.
				- **Charm**: The target will feel a great sense of fondness, making it difficult to have any thoughts of animosity or even wish to fight the **Briber**.
			- If the conditions are suitable, the person who received the **Bribe** has an extremely small chance of attacking their companions.[^10]
				- **Connection**: This establishes a connection between the target and the **Briber**.

## Sequence 6: Baron of Corruption (Corruption)

**Description**

They appear to be the compliant to the rules, but in fact **Distorts** them, constantly drawing on the power of **Order** to serve themself.[^2] [^11]

**Traits**

- **Physical Enhancement**: A **Baron of Corruption's** physique is further enhanced.
	- Their punches resemble that of a cannonball, and can cause an entire building to shake.[^9]

**New Abilities** [^2]

- **Distortion**: They can use the loopholes found in **Order**.[^12] By **Distorting** the target’s words, actions, and intent, they could formulate a certain **Order** that provided themself with an advantage, so as to achieve the goal of restraining and influencing their opponent.[^9]
	- They can **Distort** the direction and target of attacks.
		- [Mithor King](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mithor_King "Mithor King") **Distorted** the trajectory of a bullet upwards slightly, causing it to miss.[^9]
				- [Klein](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") used [Creeping Hunger](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Creeping_Hunger "Creeping Hunger") to **Distort** the target of [Ludwell](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ludwell "Ludwell") 's **Door to the Underworld**, using the skeletons and zombies to substitute himself.[^13]
				- Using [Creeping Hunger](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Creeping_Hunger "Creeping Hunger"), [Klein](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") **Distorted** [Kircheis](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Kircheis "Kircheis") ' **Sulfur Fireballs**, they lost their trajectories and acted chaotically as though in random motion, some even flew backwards at Kircheis himself.[^14]
		- They can **Seal** ordinary rooms or **Unseal** confined spaces.
		- The action of "closing the door" can be **Distorted** into the rule " **Seal** this place, isolate the outside from the inside",[^15] one that can withstand the bombardment of several **Sulfur Fireballs** from a **[Desire Apostle](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Abyss_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_5:_Desire_Apostle "Abyss Pathway/Abilities")** afflicted with **Bribe-Weaken**.[^14]
				- [Mr. A](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mr._A "Mr. A") **Distorted** [Bakerland](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Bakerland "Bakerland") 's order to stay away from his room and to not be disturbed, which made it as though the room became a completely isolated world.[^11] [^16]
				- [Lumian](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee") **Distorted** the action of "opening the door" with the concept of " **Unsealing** this confined space" to open the **Bottle of Fiction**.[^17]
		- Other uses of this ability include:
		- [Klein](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") used [Creeping Hunger](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Creeping_Hunger "Creeping Hunger") and **Distorted** the goal of "separate and intercept" into "separate and take action alone", making [Kircheis](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Kircheis "Kircheis") and his companions separate.[^14]
- **Corrosion**: It can turn the hearts of people within *10 meters* dark and greedy, making them make irrational choices.[^12]

## Sequence 5: Mentor of Disorder (Disorder)

**Traits**

- **Physical Enhancement**: A **Mentor of Disorders** physique is further enhanced.

**New Abilities [^2] [^10]**

- **Disorder**: They can produce a **Disorder** effect on a target or the surrounding area.[^10]
	- A **Mentor of Disorder** can use **Disorder** to make it difficult for attacks to land on them.
		- They can cause the **Wind Blades** of an **[Ocean Songster](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tyrant_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_5:_Ocean_Songster "Tyrant Pathway/Abilities")** to move in every direction at random to perfectly avoid hitting the target.[^18]
		- They can make enemies easily "choose" to make the wrong judgment.
		- The negative effects of the [Hand of Horror](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hand_of_Horror "Hand of Horror") caused [Audrey](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Audrey_Hall "Audrey Hall") to unknowingly make a mistake, making her take a bath in an abnormal procedure.[^10]
		- They can **Disorder** the standards of distance measurement.
		- [Bird Mustang](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Bird_Mustang "Bird Mustang") covered quite a sizable distance in about eight steps to arrive in front of [Nast Solomon](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Nast_Solomon "Nast Solomon") 's cabin.[^19] [^1]
				- They can make it so walking to the left would cause one to walk forward. This can cause an **[Ocean Songster](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tyrant_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_5:_Ocean_Songster "Tyrant Pathway/Abilities")**, a **Beyonder** with exceptional **Balance**, to hobble forward.[^18]
		- In conjunction with **Distortion**, they can extricate themselves from unfavorable situations.
		- One such situation is a **[Wraith](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chained_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_5:_Wraith "Chained Pathway/Abilities") 's Possession**.[^20]
- **Commanding Presence:** A **Mentor of Disorder** is able to display a sense of great royalty, a majesty that causes others to want to lower their bodies and obey their every command.

**Strengthened Abilities [^2]**

- **Distortion**: The **Baron of Corruption** 's power is elevated to the Sequence 5 level.
	- The **Distortion** of a target's language, actions, and intentions gains enhancements.
		- The **Seal** created by **Distortion** can barely withstand several **Sulfur Fireballs** from a **[Desire Apostle](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Abyss_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_5:_Desire_Apostle "Abyss Pathway/Abilities")** even without the support of **Bribe-Weaken**.[^21]
				- [Lockhart Siakam](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lockhart_Siakam "Lockhart Siakam") was able to **Distort** the stairs to the attic, causing them to rise as if giving him a hand and thus allowing him to get closer to the attic.[^21]
				- [Lockhart Siakam](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lockhart_Siakam "Lockhart Siakam") had **Distorted** the surrounding vases and other objects in his way, causing them to avoid him as if they had a life of their own.[^21]
		- They can now **Distort** the concepts of oneself and the target, causing both parties to equally share the damage from **Curses**, **Diseases**, etc., they receive.
		- [Lockhart Siakam](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lockhart_Siakam "Lockhart Siakam") **Distorted** the concept of himself and [Jason Beria](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Jason_Beria "Jason Beria") such that half of the **Language of Foulness** 's effects were borne by the latter.[^21]
- They gain enhancements of other abilities from previous **Sequences**.

## Sequence 4: Earl of the Fallen (Fallen)

**Description**

There are rules to everything, and everything follows certain rules. As **Demigods** of the, they are good at finding whatever loopholes there are in an **Order** to **Exploit** for themselves. Through **Magnify**, **Exploit** and **Distortion** they can achieve certain things that only **Beyonders** of the [Justiciar Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Justiciar_Pathway "Justiciar Pathway") can accomplish.[^8]

**New Abilities** [^2] [^8]

- **Exploit**: They can directly **Exploit** the **Laws**, extending certain states for longer periods of time or ending it ahead of time. This makes the rules more beneficial to themselves.
	- For example, after jumping in mid air, an **Earl of the Fallen** can **Exploit** the state of being away from the ground, extending it and achieving the effect of floating.
		- By **Exploiting** the jumping process of their jump itself while also **Exploiting** the state of being away from the ground, an **Earl of the Fallen** can achieve a form of flight.
		- They can achieve effect similar to the Manipulation of Laws of nature from **Deceit** of the **[Mentor of Deceit](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_3:_Mentor_of_Deceit "Error Pathway/Abilities")** when paired with **Distortion**.[^22]
- **Bestowment**: They can directly **Bestow** a negative effect onto a target, which can cause them to:
	- Turn sluggish;
		- Become greedily impatient;
		- Lose their fighting spirit; or
		- Be only capable of focusing attention on money.
- **Magnify**: They can boost the effects or influence of their own actions.
	- The interactions and effects an object are able to bring out can also be **Magnified**, causing said object to possess a stronger influence or effect than normal.[^23]
		- They are able to **Magnify** the effects and side-effects of [Sealed Artifacts](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sealed_Artifacts "Sealed Artifacts"), allowing them to make certain lower rank [Sealed Artifacts](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sealed_Artifacts "Sealed Artifacts") affect even **Demigods**.
		- They can **Magnify** the potency of natural laws and phenomena, turning the smallest of weather conditions into a disaster.
		- An **Earl of the Fallen** can **Magnify** the strong winds near them, enhancing their speed and transforming them into a small **Hurricane**.
		- Even the state of a target as well as their actions can be **Magnified**. For some actions, a **Magnification** of them will bring certain effects.
		- **Earls of the Fallen** are able to **Magnify** the act of "grabbing", allowing them to grab someone from nearly *100 meters away.*
				- They are able to **Magnify** a hug and turn it into a restriction. Similar to grabbing someone from nearly *100 meters away*, they can do this across large distances.
				- The injuries they have dealt to their opponents or that are just present can be **Magnified**, making them in some cases simply die on the spot.[^24]
				- An **Earl of the Fallen** is able to **Magnify** the potency of their ordinary attacks to the point where those attacks will become an execution.[^8]
		- Under normal circumstances, **Magnify** could only amplify the effects and influence that strengthened one’s body or actions without helping others.[^23]
		- **Magnify** cannot be used consecutively in a quick succession. It is limited to a one-time use each time.[^23]
		- **Magnify** is effective on abilities above the **Sequence 4** level.[^25]

**Strengthened Abilities [^8]**

- They gain enhancements of their abilities from previous **Sequences**
	- **Disorder**: It influences the structure of objects, the standards of measurement, and the accuracy of attacks.
		- It will make a seemingly massive building collapse, causing what seems like a huge distance to be shortened to that of a few paces, as well as allowing attacks that are aimed at them to miss their mark.
			- [Qonas Kilgor](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Qonas_Kilgor "Qonas Kilgor") unleashed **Disorder** to grasp the patterns of the **Concealed** world that enveloped Maygur Manor, using the feedback to determine the location of an exit.[^26]
		- **Bribe**: Their **Bribes** are stronger and can be used in tandem with **Magnify**.
		- A simple rose being thrown can be **Magnified** to an extent that it causes a violent quake when impacting the ground while at the same time imparting the effects of **Bribe—Weaken**.[^27]
		- **Distortion**: They can better **Distort** a target’s language, actions, intentions, as well as can **Distort** the effects of attacks, turning fatal wounds into serious injuries, strikes into pushes, advances into retreats, and escapes into charges.
		- They can **Distort** the rules of the **Marionette Interchange** power of a **[Bizarro Sorcerer](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fool_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_4:_Bizarro_Sorcerer "Fool Pathway/Abilities")** so that they could only swap between 2 **Marionettes**.[^27]
				- [Klein's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") **Air Cannons** had it's direction and trajectory **Distorted** to the right by [Qonas Kilgor](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Qonas_Kilgor "Qonas Kilgor"), significantly missing him in the process.[^26]
				- They are able to directly create a **Seal** in thin air, effectively creating an invisible wall, through just **Distorting** an act of "closing a door".[^24]
				- [Qonas Kilgor](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Qonas_Kilgor "Qonas Kilgor") was able to fully **Distort** his moving trajectory to a large degree, causing him to change directions instantly. [^26]
				- They are able to distort the level of a mental disease, such as a **Mental Plague** from a **[Manipulator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Visionary_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_4:_Manipulator "Visionary Pathway/Abilities")**, weakening it and slowing down the deterioration.[^23]
				- **Distortion** can cause a bullet's trajectory to be ridiculously off its mark and simultaneously cause its power to be extremely lacking.[^26]

**Mythical Creature Form**

- As **Demigods** at **Sequence 4** who have obtained partial **Godhood**, they are able to manifest their incomplete **Mythical Creature** **Form** if they disregard any of the consequences and become an incomplete **Curly Haired Baboon**.

## Sequence 3: Frenzied Mage (Frenzy)

**Authorities** [^2]

- **Strata**: They possess partial authority over **Strata**, from which the **Superior** ability originates.
	- **Superior**: Their **Commanding Presence** comes from a "rightful role," from social class to their strength and resources, this dignified aura can now be materialized into a weapon.
		- Even their voice can make **Spiritual Bodies** tremble and make subordinates feel a powerful pressure, forcing them to follow orders and being unable to lift their heads.
		- A **Frenzied Mage** can gigantify their body, increasing their height in the process.[^28]
- **Disorder**: They gain a certain degree of authority over **Disorder**, from which the **Frenzy** ability originates.
	- **Frenzy**: In the midst of **Frenzied** fluctuations, their abilities will undergo some random variations, which are often beneficial, while the abilities of their opponents will also undergo random changes, which are often detrimental.
		- Of course, whether beneficial or detrimental, the **Frenzied Mage** themselves are not able to predict the specific effect.
			- [Concerto of Light and Shadow](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Concerto_of_Light_and_Shadow "Concerto of Light and Shadow") turned a volley of **Air Cannons** into illusory doves that don’t pose any threat.[^27]
						- [Concerto of Light and Shadow](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Concerto_of_Light_and_Shadow "Concerto of Light and Shadow") changed the **Distortion** effects' of [Qonas Kilgor](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Qonas_Kilgor "Qonas Kilgor") to summoning a flower from the garden below.[^27]
						- [Concerto of Light and Shadow](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Concerto_of_Light_and_Shadow "Concerto of Light and Shadow") made the **Air Cannons** and the **Deprivation Bullet** explode, turning into red, purple, yellow, or green fireworks.[^27]

**Strengthened Abilities**

- They gain enhancement of previous **Sequences** ' other abilities and traits.
	- **Distortion**: Their **Distortion** can now can directly **Distort** the trajectory of projectiles as well as **Distort** a target's intention to move forward, making them unable to stop.
		- [Nast Solomon](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Nast_Solomon "Nast Solomon") was able to **Distort** [Klein's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") intent of "proceeding forward", making that intent into an action that was impossible for [Klein Moretti](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") to stop.[^29] [^9]

**Mythical Creature Form**

- As **Sequence 3 Beyonders**, they can respond to prayers within a certain range.

## Sequence 2: Duke of Entropy (Entropy)

**Authorities** [^2]

- **Disorder**: "They" gain partial authority over **Disorder**, which gives rise to the **Entropy** ability.
	- **Entropy**: The longer one fights a **Duke of Entropy**, the more random, **Disordered** and uncontrollable one's own abilities, thoughts, and state become, eventually turning into a mess, falling into a strange state of annihilation; the target of **Entropy** can be an enemy, or it can be the environment, rules, etc.
- **Exploit**: "They" gain partial authority over **Exploitation**.
	- "Their" **Exploitation** of the rules have been strengthened to a degree where a **Duke of Entropy** can **Exploit** "Their" airborne state when jumping once to travel from the earth to the crimson moon.[^26]
- **Strata**: "Their" **Superior** ability has been enhanced.
	- **Superior**: Using [Solomon's Mask](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Solomon%27s_Mask "Solomon's Mask"), [Miss Judgement](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Xio_Derecha "Xio Derecha") grew significantly over *6 feet*.[^30]

**Strengthened Abilities**

- "They" gain enhancements of previous **Sequences'** abilities.
	- **Distortion**: "Their" **Distortion** ability has reached the [Angel](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Angels "Angels") level, allowing "Them" to **Distort** the rules created by authorities to a certain degree, twisting them to suit their will.[^30]
		- [Xio Derecha](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Xio_Derecha "Xio Derecha") **Distorted** the rule created by the [Pillar of Severity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pillar_of_Severity "Pillar of Severity"), "The battlefield shall be divided," by intensifying its core principle - **Balance** - to an even more extreme degree.[^30]
			- This caused the **Balance** to focus on [Sealed Artifacts](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sealed_Artifacts "Sealed Artifacts") as well, causing [Xio Derecha](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Xio_Derecha "Xio Derecha") and the [Demoness of Gold](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Demoness_of_Gold "Demoness of Gold") to join the battle between [Derrick Berg](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Derrick_Berg "Derrick Berg") and [Demoness of Orange](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Demoness_of_Orange "Demoness of Orange").
				- With "Their" **Distortion** power, "They" can redirect the change of the battlefield made by a **[Planeswalker](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Door_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_2:_Planeswalker "Door Pathway/Abilities")** to somewhere else.[^31]

**Mythical Creature Form**

- "They" have become a full [Mythical Creature](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mythical_Creature_Form "Mythical Creature Form"); "Their" full [Mythical Creature Form](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mythical_Creature_Form "Mythical Creature Form") takes the appearance of a **Giant Black Baboon** with nine arms and a dark crown on its head.

## Sequence 1: Prince of Abolition (Abolition)

**Authorities** [^2]

- **Distortion** & **Definition**: "They" have partial authorities over **Distortion** and **Definition**.
	- A **Prince of Abolition** is able to re- **Define** and **Distort** the existing **Order** to "Their" advantage in order to achieve "Their" desired goals.
		- For example, "They" can **Define** what counts as a **Substitute**, what is the "original body", what counts as **Bribery**, who is "corrupted", how to instantly transform the original body into a **Substitute**, how to turn normal behavior into **Bribery**, and so on.
			- In the midst of "His" silence, [George Augustus III](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/George_Augustus_III "George Augustus III") silently swapped "Himself" with a **Substitute**, which was the **Imagined** version of "Him" doing the speech.[^32]
		- "Their" **Distortion** directly **Distorts** the corresponding **Order** and **Rules** (not the underlying ones).
		- With great difficulty, [George Augustus III](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/George_Augustus_III "George Augustus III") split off some of “His” strength, and with the help of the preparations "He" had put in place, "He" forcefully **Distorted** the surrounding area and isolated the dark and solemn secret mausoleum from the real world, preventing earthquakes and meteors from approaching the target.[^33]

## Sequence 0: Black Emperor

**Authorities** [^2]

- **Autocrat**: A core authority of the **Black Emperor**, and an authority under the **Shadow of Order** symbolism. Under it are the subordinate authorities like **Distortion**, **Exploit**, **Definition** and **Strata**.
	- Except for the **Strata** authority, all the other subordinate authorities are based on **Order**, by **Exploiting Loopholes** within **Order** or **Distorting** the original intent of it, the **Black Emperor** can make **Order** serve "Their" own purposes.
		- In barren and dead places, both the **[Justiciar](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Justiciar_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Justiciar "Justiciar Pathway/Abilities")** and the **Black Emperor** can rebuild a complete set of **Order** suitable for human survival. The **[Justiciar](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Justiciar_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Justiciar "Justiciar Pathway/Abilities")** relies on the original fundamental **Rules** to recreate an **Order** suitable for human survival, while the **Black Emperor** **Distorts** the original **Rules** to make them suitable for humans. The effect is the same, but the methods of realization differ.
				- In comparison, the **[Sun](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sun_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Sun "Sun Pathway/Abilities") '** s **Order** is more positive, self-centered, and exclusive, and must derive from "Their" previous standard of justice. There is an overlap and intersection in the authority of **Order** between the and the [Sun Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sun_Pathway "Sun Pathway").[^34]
		- **Distortion**: "They" can **Distort** certain underlying rules of the world itself. Also capable of **Distorting** processes and actions, turning them into complete opposites.[^35]
		- The **Black Emperor** can **Distort** **Order** into **Disorder**, making light become shadow, noise shift to silence, mouths transform into nostrils, a lack of people becoming a crowd, and death reverse to life.
			- "They" can **Distort** the extension direction of **Spirit Body Threads**, making those who can see it unable to detect the target in advance.[^36]
						- "They" are able to disrupt **Teleportation** by **Distorting** the destination of the **Teleportation**, turning everything extremely chaotic.[^37]
						- "They" can delay the consequence of an effect by constantly **Distorting** their state back to when the effect's activity is only gradual.[^37]
						- "They" can **Distort** the spatial position between a used **Substitute**, swapping the positions of the **Substitute** and the main body.[^36]
				- Using this on a [Fool Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fool_Pathway "Fool Pathway") **Beyonder** will result in them forcibly appearing in the desired position once they exit the [Historical Void](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Historical_Void "Historical Void").
						- The rules and process to use [Sealed Artifacts](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sealed_Artifacts "Sealed Artifacts") can be disrupted and **Distorted**, stopping the user from activating their power.[^37]
						- "They" are able to **Distort** the **True Vision** of onlookers, causing them to see scene of the past from one or two seconds ago.[^37]
						- "They" can **Distort** the rules of **Concealed** spaces, making its entrance appear directly before "Them". [^37]
				- When "They" advance to become a **Black Emperor**, "Their" ascension will cause many **Distortions** to manifest in reality, disrupting all sorts of **Order** from across the world.[^38]
			- **Spirit World Traversals** will be **Distorted**, causing the user to discover that their intended destination is far away, or that their **Wandering** has ended abruptly.
						- **High Sequence** [Error Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway "Error Pathway") **Beyonders** will have the connection to "Their" external **Worms of Time** be **Distorted**, losing the ability to command "Them".
						- The rules of the **[Circle Inhabitant](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Eternal_Aeon_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_4:_Circle_Inhabitant "Eternal Aeon Pathway/Abilities")** will be **Distorted**, allowing leaving the area of the **Circle Inhabitant** without it triggering the **Loop**.
				- After fulfilling certain conditions, the **Black Emperor** will be able to use the **Distortion** authority to **Distort** authorities, mystical connections, and even faith.
			- [Roselle](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Roselle_Gustav "Roselle Gustav") forcefully **Distorted** the faith of the [Uncertain Mist](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uncertain_Mist "Uncertain Mist") 's followers to "Himself" using "His" authority, the 's connection with the [Uncertain Mist](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uncertain_Mist "Uncertain Mist"), and the corruption of the [Uncertain Mist](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uncertain_Mist "Uncertain Mist") while relying on the [Astral Barrier](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Astral_Barrier "Astral Barrier").[^35]
				- This granted "Him" absolute authority over the [Uncertain Mist](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uncertain_Mist "Uncertain Mist") followers but also deepened "His" connection to the [Uncertain Mist](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uncertain_Mist "Uncertain Mist").
						- [Roselle Gustav](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Roselle_Gustav "Roselle Gustav") **Distorted** the relationship between the main body and the external items, briefly making "Himself" an appendage of the [Genie](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Genie "Genie").[^39]
		- **Resurrection**: Even if "They" were to be truly annihilated and killed, **Death** is a part of the most underlying and core part of the **Order** of reality, thus, the **Black Emperor** can be resurrected. "They" will still be able to awaken and return from one of the mausoleums "They" made for "Their" apotheosis.
		- There are three stages in the process of "Their" **Resurrection**:
			1. The [Uniqueness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniqueness "Uniqueness") leaves the person possessing it, turning into an abstract concept.
						2. The subjects ruled by the **Black Emperor** will hear "Their" mighty voice again.
						3. "They" will fuse with the [Uniqueness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniqueness "Uniqueness") and would reappear in the [Astral World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Astral_World "Astral World").
				- The **Sequence 1** characteristics would be returned to "Their" hands.
					- Whether the **Sequence 1 characteristics** come with the corresponding **Sequences 9-2 characteristics** depends on the state of the Sequence 1 characteristic itself.[^40]
				- "Their" **Resurrection** is an **Order** that other [True Gods](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/True_Gods "True Gods") are unable to **Distort** or prevent. It can be interfered with in the following known scenarios:
			1. The intervention of a [God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/God "God") formed from the + 2 [Black Emperor Sequence 1](#Sequence_1:_Prince_of_Abolition_\(Abolition\)) + 1 [Justiciar Sequence 1](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Justiciar_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_1:_Hand_of_Order "Justiciar Pathway/Abilities").[^41]
						2. The birth of [The Anarchy](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/The_Anarchy "The Anarchy").[^42]
				- With the [Lord of Mysteries](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lord_of_Mysteries "Lord of Mysteries") ' help, "They" could still **Resurrect** through the **Rules**, but "They" would only be able to reclaim two portions of the [Prince of Abolition](#Sequence_1:_Prince_of_Abolition_\(Abolition\)) Beyonder characteristics.
						3. Deliberately terminating the **Resurrection** process "Themselves".[^43]
				- This would leave "Them" as an incomplete Sequence 0. "They" would then live in a special state inside "Their" mausoleum, without the [Uniqueness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniqueness "Uniqueness") and [Beyonder Characteristics](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristics "Beyonder Characteristics").
				- How long it takes for the retrieval of the [Prince of Abolition](#Sequence_1:_Prince_of_Abolition_\(Abolition\)) [Beyonder Characteristics](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristics "Beyonder Characteristics") and the total duration of the **Resurrection** still depends on related arrangements.
			- Unlike [Solomon](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Solomon "Solomon"), [Roselle](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Roselle_Gustav "Roselle Gustav") 's **Resurrection** was fast since [Adam](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Adam "Adam") took the initiative to give the characteristics and the to him.[^44]
				- "Their" **Resurrection** can cleanse the **Corruption** and madness brought by multiple different pathway [Beyonder Characteristics](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristics "Beyonder Characteristics"), but can't cleanse the **Corruption** and madness that's intrinsic to itself. For the latter, it includes the following known scenarios:[^45]
			1. Failing the [Apotheosis Ritual](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Black_Emperor_Pathway/Advancement#Sequence_0:_Black_Emperor "Black Emperor Pathway/Advancement").
						2. The **Corruption** and madness brought along by the [Mother Goddess of Depravity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Goddess_of_Depravity "Mother Goddess of Depravity").
				- Even if "They" were killed and had all nine mausoleums destroyed, if a certain amount of the **Order** set up by "Them" remained, it is still possible for "Them" to mysteriously **Revive**. This appears to be a **Loophole** against the natural **Order** of death.
				- [Ancient Gods](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ancient_Gods "Ancient Gods") with the like the [Mutated King Kvastir](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mutated_King_Kvastir "Mutated King Kvastir") could also **Resurrect** with this mechanism, provided that "They" had prepared thoroughly.[^46]
- **Disorder**: A core authority of the **Black Emperor**. "They" wield the majority of this authority.
	- "They" are no longer merely a parasite, interpreter, or **Distorter** of **Order**; "They" have broken free from **Order**, plunging the very concepts of **Order** and **Rules** themselves into total **Disorder**.
		- "They" can cause a person's thoughts to descend into **Disorder**, making it become active and uncontrollable. This seemed to be a trend that couldn’t be changed — one that led to chaos.[^47]
- **Divine Kingdom**: **Order Hell**, or **False Law Heaven**.
