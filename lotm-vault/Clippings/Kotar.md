**Kotar** is a [Dark Demonic Wolf](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Creatures_and_Plants#Dark_Demonic_Wolf "Creatures and Plants"). "He" was a [Subsidiary God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Subsidiary_Gods "Subsidiary Gods") of the [Annihilation Demonic Wolf Flegrea](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Flegrea "Flegrea") known as the **God of Wishes** in the [Second Epoch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Second_Epoch "Second Epoch"). "He" is a Sequence 2 [Miracle Invoker](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fool_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_2:_Miracle_Invoker) of the [Fool Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fool_Pathway "Fool Pathway").

## Honorific Name

" **执掌愿望的神祇** " (*Zhízhǎng yuànwàng de shénqí, lit: " The Deity who Wields Wishes.", WebNovel translation: "The God of Wishes") is the line used in the Chinese version of the novel as a line in* Kotar'sHonorific Name**.**

**The Dark Lord that exists alongside History,  
The Embodiment of Countless Miracles,  
The God of Wishes...**

— Incomplete [Honorific Name](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Honorific_Name "Honorific Name") of **Kotar** [^1]

**Note**:

- This **Honorific Name** is not complete, lacking other parts that are still unknown.

## Appearance

**Kotar** is a demonic wolf covered in dark fur and has eight legs. "He" is about four to five meters tall.

"He" has a tuft of short, grayish-white fur on "His" forehead. "His" pure black pupils covers at least three-quarters of the space of "His" eyes.[^2]

## Personality

"He" is a cowardly, but daring demonic wolf. "He" systematically puts "His" safety before anything else. This was how "He" was able to survive from the [Second Epoch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Second_Epoch "Second Epoch") until now, the [Fifth Epoch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fifth_Epoch "Fifth Epoch"). “He” always follows "His" true feelings—cowardice. “He” refuses to be greedy when the situation doesn’t allow it.[^3]

## History

In the [Second Epoch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Second_Epoch "Second Epoch"), **Kotar** was the **God of Wishes**, one of the subsidiary gods under the [Annihilation Demonic Wolf Flegrea](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Flegrea "Flegrea"). Early in the [Second Epoch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Second_Epoch "Second Epoch"), "He" was both [Miracle Invoker](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fool_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_2:_Miracle_Invoker) and [Servant of Concealment](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Darkness_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_2:_Servant_of_Concealment).[^4]

After the death of the [Annihilation Demonic Wolf Flegrea](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Flegrea "Flegrea"), "He" was hunted by [Amon](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Amon "Amon") for centuries in the [Forsaken Land of the Gods](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Forsaken_Land_of_the_Gods "Forsaken Land of the Gods") as a [Miracle Invoker](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fool_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_2:_Miracle_Invoker) of the [Fool Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fool_Pathway).[^5]

On June 28, 1349 of the [Fifth Epoch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fifth_Epoch "Fifth Epoch"), a black velvet 'Curtain', containing one set of the [Fool Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fool_Pathway "Fool Pathway") 's Sequences 9 to 1, was spat out of the [Sefirah Castle](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/The_Gray_Fog "The Gray Fog") to the Forsaken Land.[^2] [^6] The **God of Wishes** obtained the 'Curtain' and went to [Nois City](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Nois_City "Nois City"). "He" started to prepare the advancement ritual for the Sequence 1 [Attendant of Mysteries](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fool_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_1:_Attendant_of_Mysteries) by turning all the inhabitants in [Nois City](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Nois_City "Nois City") into marionettes. However, "He" later hurriedly migrated after sensing [Amon](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Amon "Amon") and the [True Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/True_Creator "True Creator").[^7]

Later, after receiving intelligence from the [Evernight Goddess](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Evernight_Goddess "Evernight Goddess"),[^8] [Klein Moretti](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") tried to hunt **Kotar** to get the Beyonder characteristic needed for his advancement. After an intense battle, **Kotar** was defeated by Klein and had to commit suicide to escape, leaving the black velvet 'Curtain' behind. As for **Kotar**, "He" revived "Himself" and fled to safety.[^3]
