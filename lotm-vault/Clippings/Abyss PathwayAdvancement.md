## Sequence 5: Desire Apostle

![Potion - Abyss Sequence 5 - Desire Apostle](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/lord-of-the-mystery/images/a/a1/Potion_-_Abyss_Sequence_5_-_Desire_Apostle.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/200?cb=20260331055605)

Potion - Abyss Sequence 5 - Desire Apostle

- Overview
- Formula
- Ritual
- Backlash
- Acting Method

### Potion Properties

> A brightly colored, aesthetically pleasing liquid, resembling rainbow cocktail.[^1]

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

- A black-fog-like characteristic.[^3]
- **Corrupted by a **[Wraith](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chained_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_5:_Wraith "Chained Pathway/Abilities")** boon:** A whimsical and flamboyant cluster of black crystals. Various crystals extended in all directions, sharp and crooked. Multiple illusory, translucent faces emerged from each black crystal, their expressions alternating between malevolence, pain, madness, and confusion.[^4]

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- 1 Succubus skin.[^1]
- A Son of Sorrow's eyes.[^1]

Or

- **A [Desire Apostle](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Abyss_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_5:_Desire_Apostle "Abyss Pathway/Abilities")** [Beyonder Characteristic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristic "Beyonder Characteristic").

Supplementary Ingredients

- 60 ml of "Succubus" blood.[^1]
- 70 ml of a Son of Sorrow's blood.[^1]
- 3 pure white poppies.[^1]
- 9 types of pituitary glands of intelligent beings.[^1]

### Advancement Ritual

- Serial killings targeting those who have fallen from grace; the minimum is 13 people, the maximum is 49, the more complete the ritual, the greater the chance of advancement.[^1] [^5]
- There must be at least a three-day interval between two killings, otherwise it's easy to lose control, the interval also cannot exceed nine days, otherwise the ritual will reset.[^1] [^5]
- After each killing, after each part of the ritual, the Devil must devour the victim's internal organs.[^5]
- From this point on, they will remain in a state of violent bloodlust, wanting to harm others, until that desire is satisfied again.

### Potion Properties

- The Reproductive Organs of a "Desire Demon".[^1]

Or

- **A [Demon](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Abyss_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_4:_Demon "Abyss Pathway/Abilities")** [Beyonder Characteristic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristic "Beyonder Characteristic").

- The hearts of 99 Beyonders.[^1]

### Advancement Ritual

- Induce at least 10.000 intelligent beings to voluntarily sell their souls to the performer.[^1]
- The performer must successfully acquire the corresponding souls.

Main Ingredients

- The head of a "Demon Prince".[^1]
- The heart of a "Twin Serpent Lord".[^1]

Or

- **A [Bloody Archduke](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Abyss_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_2:_Bloody_Archduke "Abyss Pathway/Abilities")** [Beyonder Characteristic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristic "Beyonder Characteristic").

- 1 drop of a [True God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/True_God "True God") 's blood.[^1]

- The ritual involves sacrificing 13 settlements, each containing at least 10.000 intelligent beings, to the [Abyss](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Abyss "Abyss").[^1]
- Each sacrifice must be made within three months of each other.
	- Furthermore, the sacrifice must be performed using a "Blood Ritual," which involves killing each creature in the settlement one by one, then using their gushing blood to cleanse the altar and repaint the corresponding symbols and patterns.

## Sequence 1: Filthy Monarch

![Potion - Abyss Sequence 1 - Filthy Monarch](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/lord-of-the-mystery/images/0/01/Potion_-_Abyss_Sequence_1_-_Filthy_Monarch.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/200?cb=20260331055750)

- Overview

- Ritual
- Backlash

### Potion Properties

> A dark black pus emitting a corrosive, dark green gas.[^1]

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> A corrosive yet strangely ethereal flaky like black sheet.[^1]

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- **A [Filthy Monarch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Abyss_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_1:_Filthy_Monarch "Abyss Pathway/Abilities")** [Beyonder Characteristic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristic "Beyonder Characteristic")

Supplementary Ingredients

- 1 abomination from a land of ruin, defilement, or curse.[^1]
- e.g., The [Forsaken Land of the Gods](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Forsaken_Land_of_the_Gods "Forsaken Land of the Gods").
- 300 ml of blood from an Angel that has lost control.[^1]
- 9 completely corrupted divine creatures.[^1]
- 100 souls drowning in desire.[^1]

### Advancement Ritual

- Corrupt a nation with at least 10 million inhabitants.[^1]
- The Upper Class shall be controlled by desires, everyone of them becoming a Devil.
	- The Lower Class becoming destitute and without any hope of escaping their predicament, ultimately even their own children and organs become the property of others.
	- The Middle Class becoming like climbing a series of collapsing cliffs, watching as their own layer collapses and falls into the Abyss below, desperately trying to climb upwards in fear.
- Just as they are going to find stability and catch their breath, the cliff collapses again, while above them the Upper Class watches with theatrical laughter.
