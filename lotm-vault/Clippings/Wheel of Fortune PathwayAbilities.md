## Sequence 9: Monster

**Description** [^1]

[Beyonders](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonders "Beyonders") of this [Sequence](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sequence "Sequence") often fall into a kind of trance, sometimes whispering things others cannot understand, making others consider them some kind of real **Monster**.

**Traits** [^2]

- **Perception**: They have super high **Spiritual Perception**. They often heard sounds others couldn't hear, and also see things others couldn't see. This allowed them to occasionally see the future and have an acute intuition for danger.
	- This is the specialty of the **Fate Pathway**, making them able to see things that even [Pathways](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways") with strong [Spirit Vision](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_Vision "Spirit Vision") abilities can't see.
		- Their intuition for danger often allows them to avoid looking at things they should not see, but it is not foolproof.[^3] [^4] [^5]

## Sequence 8: Robot

**Traits** [^1] [^2]

- **Physical Enhancement**: All aspects of their physical abilities have been significantly improved.
	- This makes **Robots** naturally talented in fighting and shooting.
- **Mental Enhancement**: They have powerful calculation skills and very precise control.
- They gain proficiency in **[Divination](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Divination_Art "Divination Art")** and **Anti-Divination** skills.

Later [Sow](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sow "Sow") tried to kill [Camus](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Camus_Castiya "Camus Castiya") [^7]

## Sequence 7: Lucky One

**New Abilities** [^1] [^2]

- **Passive Luck <sup>⁂</sup>**: **Lucky Ones** will often encounter **Lucky** events in their life, such as finding money on the street, having enemies miss attacks that were aimed at them, getting desired dice rolls, and having women they happen to like coincidentally also like them back.
	- Their **Luck** isn't fixed and fluctuates: at times, they are especially **Lucky**, while at other times, they are no different from an ordinary person.
		- This unpredictable **Luck** isn't something that they should heavily rely upon, and as such, they have to temper their expectations.

## Sequence 6: Calamity Priest

**Traits** [^1] [^2]

- **High Spirituality**: Their **Spirituality** surpasses that of other [Pathways](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways").

**New Abilities** [^1] [^2]

- **Calamity Attraction <sup>⁂</sup>**: Their body will now attract **Calamities**, mainly in two ways.
	- **Passive Attraction**: They will passively suffer all kinds of **Calamities**, but can see them in advance via their **Foresight** and make the preparations needed to eliminate or mitigate their effects.
		- Because of their **Foresight**, **Calamity Priests** can use the dangers of their own passive encounters and preparation to attack their enemies.
		- **Active Attraction**: They can attract **Calamities** to varying degrees, affecting targets and enemies as a result. Then through their **Luck**, they can avoid most of the danger from it.
		- In essence, it allows them to pull their enemies into a situation where they can take advantage of their strengths the best.
- **Psyche Storm**: Using their **Spirituality** that surpassed that of other [Pathways](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways"), they can directly affect their opponent's [Spirit Body](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul#Soul_Body "Soul"), causing them to feel dizzy and lost.
	- If a **Calamity Priest's** enemy entered such a state, they would be extremely prone to making errors, thus causing the **Calamity** to snowball and eventually devour them.

## Sequence 5: Winner

**Traits**

- **Foresight**: They have a very keen sense of **Foresight**, making them mysticism experts in the **[Divination](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Divination_Art "Divination Art")** and **Anti-Divination** domains.[^8]
	- Just before [Enzo](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Enzo "Enzo") was about to run off after successfully evading [Oaf's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti/Equipment "Klein Moretti/Equipment") attack, he had a premonition, causing him to subconsciously curl up and protect his vital spots.
		- Fully believing in his intuition, [Enzo](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Enzo "Enzo") lunged forward, jumping off the staircase and making two rolls onto the lower street, causing [Klein](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") to briefly lose track of him.
		- Upon [Klein Moretti](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") phasing in front of him to block his escape, [Enzo](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Enzo "Enzo") immediately tightly shut his eyes, which unintentionally saved his life for the short term.
		- [Enzo](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Enzo "Enzo") had a hunch that if he stalled the situation for a little longer, a number of [Nighthawks](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Nighthawks "Nighthawks") would catch up to him, making the situation more chaotic.

**New Abilities** [^1] [^2]

- **Curse of Misfortune**: They can **Curse** their enemies with limited **Misfortune**, making them more **Unlucky**. This can range from missing attacks to falling into a dangerous situation.

**Strengthened Abilities** [^1] [^2]

- **Passive Luck <sup>⁂</sup>**: To an extent, they can now begin to control their own **Luck**.
	- A **Winner** can now rely on their own self-control to accumulate a lot of **Luck**, and repeatedly avert fatal crises or dangerous situations in dramatic ways at critical moments.
		- By enduring **Misfortune** without using their own **Luck** they can accumulate and gain **Luck**.[^9]
		- They will begin to be able to encounter things and events with a low probability of happening in their daily life more occasionally than when they were a **Lucky One**.
		- These can be things such as a **Winner's** enemies getting lost or making poor judgments, gaining an inheritance due to a series of mistakes by another person, or having their mistakes or flaws be interpreted as funny actions by others, making them more well-liked.[^8]
			- [Enzo](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Enzo "Enzo") accidentally tripped on a rock while his **Spirit Body Threads** was controlled by [Klein Moretti](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti"), the pain that caused snapping himself out of that controlled state.
						- [Enzo](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Enzo "Enzo") felt a brief stabbing pain in his knee as though his previous fall had given him some light injuries, causing him to crouch down and fully evade [Oaf's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti/Equipment "Klein Moretti/Equipment") attack.
						- During raid on Ulika from [Numinous Episcopate](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Numinous_Episcopate "Numinous Episcopate") [Enzo](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Enzo "Enzo") was overlooked by [Nighthawks](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Nighthawks "Nighthawks") allowing him to escape core region easily.
						- When pursuing [Enzo](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Enzo "Enzo") [Daly Simone](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Daly_Simone "Daly Simone") and [Leonard Mitchell](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Leonard_Mitchell "Leonard Mitchell") repeatedly made errors of judgement causing them to fail to catch him.
						- Thanks to his **Luck**, [Enzo](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Enzo "Enzo") had avoided most of the damage caused by the **Flesh** and **Blood Bomb** that was planted on [Oaf](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti/Equipment "Klein Moretti/Equipment"), only making him suffer light injuries.
- **Psyche Storm**: They can spread their arms and allow invisible waves to ripple out of their body, creating a pure **Psyche Storm** that sweps all **Spirit Bodies** in the vicinity, bringing with it an intense sense of drowsiness and **Calamity**.[^8]

## Sequence 4: Misfortune Mage

**Traits**

- **Physical Enhancement**: Their body has undergone a qualitative change and became a **Mercury Body** with passive **Anti-Divination** and **Anti-Prophecy** properties.
	- Their overall physical abilities have also been significantly improved.

**New Abilities** [^1]

- **Misfortune Field**: Upon activation, all beings who enter within a *100-300 meter* range (depending on potion digestion and **Misfortune Mage's** state) of them will be **Cursed** with **Bad Luck**.
	- The branch that [Mr. Harry](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mr._Harry "Mr. Harry") was stepping on after landing on a tree immediately snapped after merely being within the **Misfortune Field** for a moment.[^10]
- **Group Blessing**: This is the same as **Blessing**, but can only be achieved by the **Misfortune Mage** consuming a significant amount of their own **Luck** reserves.
	- It can be given to any creature within a 50-meter radius of the **Misfortune Mage**.
		- The **Misfortune Mage** can choose who to give and who not to give **Luck** to.
- **Blessing**: By using their own **Luck** or pulling the target's future **Luck** into the present, they can **Bless** a chosen target, making them **Lucky.**
	- They can use **Blessing** to remove a prolonged period of **Misfortune**.[^9]
		- This is because **Luck** and **Misfortune** counteract each other.
		- Examples of the **Blessing** ability being used are:
		- [Ricciardo](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ricciardo "Ricciardo") **Blessed** [Darkwill](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Darkwill "Darkwill") with precisely enough **Luck** to win around 700 pounds.[^11]
				- [Ricciardo](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ricciardo "Ricciardo") removed the **Misfortune** from [Anderson](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Anderson_Hood "Anderson Hood") by utilizing a **Blessing**.[^4]
- **Absolute Perception**: They possess an **Absolute** **Perception** over the changes in **Fate**, the existence of **Beacons**, as well as those of higher mystical status.
	- [Ricciardo](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ricciardo "Ricciardo") knew the day he would meet [Gehrman Sparrow](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") he must not see anything him and thus wore an eye mask on that day.[^4]

**Strengthened Abilities** [^1]

- **Curse of Misfortune**: A **Misfortune Mage** can **Curse** the target with **Bad Luck**, making them extremely **Unlucky.** This kind of **Misfortune** is one that can threaten one's life.
	- Due to the **Dream** having ended before [Anderson](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Anderson_Hood "Anderson Hood") could ask for [Klein Moretti's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") help, causing [Klein](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") to think he was already mutated which made him attack [Anderson](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Anderson_Hood "Anderson Hood") with several **Wind Blades**.[^12]
		- The axe that [Anderson Hood](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Anderson_Hood "Anderson Hood") used in the **Dream** broke in two, and as the crack was towards the back, the fragment shot at him, making his right chest and abdomen bleed like a fountain.[^13]
		- Due to the streak of bad **Luck**, the treasure hunting team [Anderson](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Anderson_Hood "Anderson Hood") was a part of failed to reach the island on time before the storm arrived, which sank their ship and treasures.[^14]
		- The rest of the treasure hunting team either drowned, got struck by **Lightning**, or got eaten by the underwater monsters, while [Anderson](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Anderson_Hood "Anderson Hood"), being relatively stronger and **Luckier** than them, was sent flying by the waves and successfully swam to the shore of an island.
		- Soon after [Anderson](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Anderson_Hood "Anderson Hood") stated that he may attract monsters due to his bad **Misfortune**, the island they were on quaked as a **Stone Giant** arose from the primitive forest.[^14]

		- It caused the mushrooms that [Frank](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Frank_Lee "Frank Lee") was working on to enter the [Future](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Stars_Pirates "Stars Pirates") and merge with it, making it spurt out milk all across the ship.

	- Their **Psyche Storm** not only affects the opponents' **Spirit** **Bodies** through their extremely high **Spirituality**, causing dizziness and disorientation, it also causes confusion within the mind and thoughts of the affected targets, bringing a bit of madness.

- As **Demigods** at [Sequence 4](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sequence#High-Sequence_\(Demigod\) "Sequence") who have obtained partial **Godhood**, they are able to manifest their incomplete [Mythical Creature Form](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mythical_Creature_Form "Mythical Creature Form") if they disregard any of the consequences.

## Sequence 3: Chaoswalker

TRANSLATION NOTE

In the original Chinese text, 混乱 (hǔnluàn), the term used for " **Disorder** ", is sometimes translated as " **Chaos** ", as is done in the English WebNovel translation, this conflicts with a similar term, 混沌 (hùndùn). Since these terms are both distinct authorities, this page will consistently use the same English words to refer to the same Chinese words throughout for clarity and distinction.
- **Disorder** (混乱 *hǔnluàn*)
	- Refers to a state of confusion, disorder, or lack of order. It implies the breakdown of established systems, rules, or expectations.
- **Chaos** (混沌 *hùndùn*)
	- Refers to primordial chaos—the formless and undifferentiated state that precedes or underlies creation. Usually used in creation myths.
Note: The "Chaos" in **Chaoswalker** (混乱行者 *hǔnluàn xíngzhě*) uses the characters for Disorder (混乱 *hǔnluàn*).

**New Abilities** [^1]

- **Fate Keeper**: They can keenly discern the **Disordered** and insane elements of a **Disordered** and complex **Fate**, either evading or **Sealing** them away.
- **Spiritual Baptism**: Just like **Baptizing** a baby, **Spiritual Baptism** makes the target's spirit clean, stable, no longer **Disordered**, just like the beginning.

**Authorities**

- **Disorder**: They have gained partial authority over **Disorder**.
	- They can make **Predestined Fate** become **Disordered**, making the result no longer **Predestined**.
		- They can let the spirit and **Fate** of a target develop in a more **Disordered** and crazy direction.

**Strengthened Abilities**

- Their previous **Beyonder** powers from [Sequence 4](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sequence#High-Sequence_\(Demigod\) "Sequence") have been improved.

**Mythical Creature Form**

- As [Sequence 3](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sequence#High-Sequence_\(Demigod\) "Sequence") **Beyonders**, they can respond to prayers within a certain range.

## Sequence 2: Soothsayer

**Traits**

- **High Spirituality**: "Their" **Spirituality** can utilize the [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World") to influence **Dreams** and **Mirrors**.[^16] [^17] [^18]
	- "They" can locate one's [Astral Projection](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul#Astral_Projection "Soul") in the [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World") and inject information in one's **Dreams**, creating a **Revelation** that evolved into a specific **Dream**.[^19]

**New Abilities**

- "They" are able to access and utilize the **Connection** that things have with others' **Fate**.
	- "They" can access the interlayers of **Destiny** formed by things and arrange certain things.
		- Since [Penglai](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Penglai "Penglai") happened to be near the boundary of the seal, some fishermen went out to sea to fish or adventure, and these people happened to remain outside the **Seal** of the [Western Continent](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Western_Continent "Western Continent"). However, they can still be considered connected by **Fate** with the [Western Continent](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Western_Continent "Western Continent") itself, which could create some interlayers of **Destiny**. This allowed [Cohinem](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Cohinem "Cohinem") to enter these interlayers and hide some arrangements for **Resurrection** there.[^20]
		- "They" can use the **Connection** "They" have with an object to store and channel "Their" abilities through it, even if "They" are not in the vicinity.
		- [Klein Moretti](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") summoned [Will Auceptin](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Will_Auceptin "Will Auceptin") 's ice cream from the past to establish a subtle **Connection** with "Him" in order to be blessed with good **Luck**.[^21]
				- [Ouroboros](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ouroboros "Ouroboros") imbued "His" murals with many of "His" powers, be it giving people extreme **Misfortune** or trapping them in a **Cycle of Fate**.[^22] [^23] [^13]
				- [Will Auceptin](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Will_Auceptin "Will Auceptin") utilized **Reboots** through the medium of "His" folded paper cranes to help [Klein Moretti](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") in battle.[^24] [^25]
				- [Cohinem](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Cohinem "Cohinem") used her golden wine cup to cause her power to affect [Alger](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Alger_Wilson "Alger Wilson").[^26]

**Authorities** [^1] [^27]

- **Fate**: "They" possess partial control over the **Fate** authority, which encompasses the subordinate authorities of **Good Luck**, **Bad Luck**, and **Foresight**.
	- **Prophecy**: "They" are able to **Prophesy** how different results will occur between certain people and events due to good or bad **Luck**.
		- "Their" spoken **Prophecies** will have a high probability of becoming a reality and even if the final outcome does not match that of a **Soothsayer's** **Prophecy**, it will be similar.

						- If the **Revelation** is not correctly interpreted, the inquirer will suffer immensely.

		- **Words of Fortune & Misfortune**: "They" are able to bring **Good** or **Bad Luck** upon any living beings within a *30 kilometer radius*.

				- The **Misfortune** "They" can bring can make any [Beyonder](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder "Beyonder") under the **Demigod** level die miserably within an extremely short period of time.

## Sequence 1: Snake of Mercury (Giant Serpent, Snake of Fate, Tail-Devouring Serpent of Fate)

**Authorities** [^1]

- **Cycle**: "They" gain partial authority over **Cycle** and the corresponding **Save** and **Select** authorities.
	- **Cycle of Fate**: "They" can make people fall into a strange state, where **Time** maintains a certain degree of flow, but is fixed within a certain range, circulating over and over again.[^22] [^23]
		- Those affected by it wouldn't even realize they are trapped.
			- It can repeatedly trap other supernatural individuals of similar power level until the target realizes it, but each time consumes "Their" own **Luck**.[^34]
				- By breaking the medium, one can break free from it.
			- The medium for the **Cycle of Fate** that had placed the [City of Silver](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/City_of_Silver "City of Silver") members in a past [Rose Redemption](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Rose_Redemption "Rose Redemption") meeting was the hall itself, and destroying it would end the loop.

				- This balance will continue, that is, relative stillness.

## Sequence 0: Wheel of Fortune

**Description**

To an extent, "They" are equivalent to the embodiment of **Fate**, capable of roaming freely in the [River of Fate](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/River_of_Fate "River of Fate").

**Authorities** [^1]

- **Fate**: This is a core authority, the **Fate** authority derived from the **Fate** symbolism. It is not a complete authority, as other [Pathways](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways") also have partial **Fate** authority. "They" are the embodiment of **Fate**, both **Good Luck** and **Bad Luck**, with a particular aptitude for **Foresight**.
	- **Foresight**: "They" gain partial authority of **Foresight,** able to grasp the [River of Fate](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/River_of_Fate "River of Fate") to obtain visions of the future as well as **Destined** **Beacons**.
		- "They" can profoundly influence short-term tributaries of the [River of Fate](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/River_of_Fate "River of Fate") in a short-period of time, ensuring that "Their" **Prophecies** will be precisely fulfilled.
			- This is akin to a grand **Prophecy** technique.
				- The **Wheel of Fortune** has the strongest **Prophetic** ability.[^41]
		- As the embodiment of " **Fate** ", **Fooling** "Them" is also equivalent to **Fooling Fate**.[^42]
		- "They" can perform the [Tunnel Effect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_tunnelling), but it would consume "Their" own **Luck**.[^43]
		- "They" can make the atoms in "Their" body perfectly align to let a blade pass through "Them", which has a **Probability** of around 10^{-10^{21}} of happening.
		- As the embodiment of " **Fate** ", "They" can roam freely in the [River of Fate](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/River_of_Fate "River of Fate").
- **Probability**: Another core authority derived from the symbol of **Fate**, it lets "Them" manipulate the **Probability** of events and the behavior of peoples and objects from all around the world.[^44]
	- Everything can be **Probabilistic**. This is one of the manifestations of the **Disordered** nature of **Fate**. If one masters **Probability**, they master the **Fate** of all things.
		- The **Wheel of Fortune** can amplify the **Probability** of adversely affecting a target to the limit, causing them to fail in everything they do while also having rare unlucky events become extremely likely and common for the target. Likewise, "They" can do the opposite.[^44]
			- Due to the [Die of Probability's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Die_of_Probability "Die of Probability") pip being 1, [Darkwill](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Darkwill "Darkwill") had almost died in his **Dream** as his head head swelled with a sharp pain and he struggled to breathe.[^45]
				- Soon after, if [Klein](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") hadn't properly controlled his strength as the door slammed into [Darkwill's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Darkwill "Darkwill") head, his skull would have probably cracked open.
								- It had also made it so that even though [Darkwill](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Darkwill "Darkwill") explained his situation and the [Die of Probability](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Die_of_Probability "Die of Probability") with lot's of logic, [Klein](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") would still not believe him.
					- This was due to the fact that [Darkwill](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Darkwill "Darkwill") 's earlier explanation happened to match certain theories of his, making him instinctively believe that weak lie over the recent explanation as being the true reason behind his predicament.
						- With the [Die of Probability's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Die_of_Probability "Die of Probability") pip at 2, the sky suddenly turned dark as the wind howled with increasing strength and the air's humidity clearly climbed.[^46]
				- As [Darkwill](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Darkwill "Darkwill") looked out the window to check on the exact situation within the ship a silver bolt of **Lightning** struck him before [Klein](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") could even react.
- **Cycle**: Derived from the **Return** symbolism, it means **Returning** to the mother body, **Returning** to the primordial source, **Returning** to the oldest origin. This is the instinct of the universe.
	- This authority includes the domain of **Reincarnation**, which is shared with the **[Mother](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Mother)**.[^47]
		- Under the **Cycle** authority are the lesser authorities of **Save** and **Select**.
		- The **Save** authority can reproduce a past segment of **Fate** no more than three minutes away from the current segment that can replace the current segment.
				- The **Select** authority can choose one path among the many **Fate Tributaries** in the **River of Fate**. For example, choosing the one leading to **Reboot**.
- **Disorder**: The **Disorder** authority is one of the manifestations of the **Disorder** symbolism. The essence of **Fate** is **Disorder**, which embodies madness. The **Wheel of Fortune** both keeps watch over them and uses them.
	- This **Chaos** is the **Disorder** of [Fate](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/River_of_Fate "River of Fate"), rooted in the [Original Creator's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Original_Creator "Original Creator") madness.[^48]
- **Light Cone World**: "Their" **Divine Kingdom**, a completely **Probabilistic** realm where every move corresponds to a dice with different numbers.[^49]
	- The **Wheel of Fortune** can manipulate these illusory dice.
