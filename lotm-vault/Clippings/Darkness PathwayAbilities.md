## Sequence 9: Sleepless

**Traits** [^2]

- **High Spirituality**: A **Sleepless** and their corresponding **Pathways** have a **High** **Spirituality** compared to other **Pathways**. Thus they possess high spiritual perception and sensitivity.[^3] [^4] [^5]
	- **Spirit Vision**: They can use **Spirit Vision**; however, they are unable to precisely differentiate the status of different body parts. It can only be used to see spiritual things, but is not as effective as relying on their spiritual perception.
		- **Divination Arts & Ritualistic Magic**: Their potent **Spirituality** allows them to do limited [Divination](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Divination_Art "Divination Art") and [Ritualistic Magic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ritualistic_Magic "Ritualistic Magic"). However, their usage of **Ritualistic Magic** is far inferior to the likes of **[Seers](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fool_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_9:_Seer)** and **[Mystery Pryers](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hermit_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_9:_Mystery_Pryer)**.

**New Abilities** [^2]

- **Nocturnality**: The deeper into the night, the more powerful a **Sleepless** will become. Their physical strength, intuition, and mental capabilities are all the aspects that get strengthened.[^6] [^7]
	- They can see through the darkness even without any lights.
		- They are able to sense dangers that are hidden in the dark.
		- They only need to rest for around 3-4 hours a day.

**New Abilities** [^2]

- **Midnight Poem**: **Midnight Poets** can cast effects through the recital of a poem related to the night or the [Evernight Goddess](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Evernight_Goddess "Evernight Goddess"). These **Midnight Poems** can manifest all sorts of varying effects.[^8]
- **Midnight Poets** also have expertise in combat, marksmanship, climbing and spiritual perception.

**Strengthened Abilities**

- **Nocturnality**: The degree of enhancement has been strengthened.
	- Even at the tail end of a night, after six in the morning when the sun is beginning to rise, they will be strong enough to break a doors lock and the surrounding wood with a single punch.[^15]

## Sequence 7: Nightmare

**Traits**

- **High Spirituality**: Their **Spirituality** gets enhanced significantly.
	- They are able to use their strong **Spirituality** to resonate the air around them, allowing them to recite **Midnight Poems** without the use of their throat.[^9]

**New Abilities** [^2]

- **Nightmare State**: A **Nightmare** can separate their [Soul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul") from their physical body while they sleep, allowing it to move around freely within an initial *city-wide range* and infiltrate **Dreams**.[^16]
	- Normal light will appear abnormally dim while in this **Nightmare State** and everything will be tainted with a sense of blurriness. **Dreams** will appear as blobs of illusory oval lights, its structure allowing a **Nightmare** to directly see what a person is dreaming.
- **Dream Shaping**: Similar to that of a **[Dreamwalker](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Visionary_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_5:_Dreamwalker)**, a **Nightmare** is able to cause different changes within a target's **Dreams**, guiding their target into displaying several behaviors.
	- This ability can be used to interrogate a target or get them to show their innermost secrets.
		- They can also use **Dream Shaping** to give fear and crush the target's spirit and psyche.
- **Dream Invasion**: A **Nightmare** is able to forcefully drag their targets into **Dreams** without alerting them of having done so under most normal circumstances.[^17]
	- **Dream Invasion** has a limited range if done quickly, but a **Nightmare** is able to slowly coax their target into sleeping and thus **Dreaming** if they are within *100 meters*.
		- They get pulled into the **Dream** along with their target and cannot move while using this ability. In addition at **Sequence 7,** they can coax to sleep a maximum of 10 targets at the same time, but can only enter the **Dream** of a single target.[^2] [^17]
		- The ability to induce **Slumber** originated from being forcefully drawn into a **Dream**, not the other way around. Essentially, it was a **Dream**, not **Sleep**.[^18]
- **Nightmare Limbs**: A **Nightmare** is able to generate tentacle-like **Nightmare Limbs** protruding from their back as a means to fight at close range.[^19]
	- Due to the fact that these tentacle-like limbs are partially formed from their own flesh and blood, if they break, they will suffer the corresponding amount of damage.[^19]

## Sequence 6: Soul Assurer

**Traits** [^2] [^20]

- **High Spirituality**: A **Soul Assurer's** **Spirituality** will be enhanced slightly.
	- **Ritualistic Magic & Divination Arts**: They gain improvements in some spiritual abilities.
		- This gives them the ability to hold more complex **Ritualistic Magic**.[^21]
		- They can directly see spiritual lifeforms without the need to activate **Spirit Vision**.

**New Abilities** [^2] [^20]

- **Requiem**: They can cause the [Spirit Body](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul") to sleep, suppressing and soothing their targets' desires, moods, and thoughts, this causes them to slow down or freeze on the spot.[^22]
	- This can also suppress spiritual abilities such as the **Spiritual** **Intuition** of the targeted entity.[^23]
		- They are able to directly sooth [Souls](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul"), ghosts, and the undead into silence using this ability.
		- Using **Requiem**, a **Soul Assurer** can also treat the emotional states of teammates, akin to a **[Psychiatrist](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Visionary_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_7:_Psychiatrist_\(Psyche_Analyst\))**.
		- Compared to a **[Psychiatrist's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Visionary_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_7:_Psychiatrist_\(Psyche_Analyst\))** **Placate**, a **Soul Assurer's** **Requiem** targets the [Soul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul").[^24]

**Strengthened Abilities** [^2]

- A **Soul Assurer** has gained enhancements in previous **Sequence** abilities.
	- **Dream Invasion**: They can force targets in **Dreams** within a *150 meter range*.
		- **Midnight Poem**: They can use signing as a way to manifest effects.
		- **Agitate**:<sup>⁂</sup> A **Soul Assurer** can agitate the [Spirit Body](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul") of a target, heightening the target(s) corresponding frustrations and destructive urges.
			- They can discern any latent problems in their target's [Soul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul") or mind through this.
- **Nocturnality**: They only need *two hours* of rest per day.[^15]

## Sequence 5: Spirit Warlock

**New Abilities** [^2]

- **Spirit Commanding**:<sup>⁂</sup> Their control over **Spirits** is greatly enhanced, they can **Seal** various **Spirits** within their teeth, then release them by tapping their teeth.
	- Their strength often depends on the **Deceased** and **Natural Spirits** they can find and control. They are better at controlling **Natural Spirits** compared to **[Spirit Guides](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_6:_Spirit_Guide)**, who are more suited toward **Deceased Spirits**.[^8]
		- The number of **Spirits** a **Spirit Warlock** can **Command** is limited by the strength of their own [Spirit Body](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul"). At this **Sequence**, they can only control *4-5 **Spirits*** at most.
				- The **Sealing** condition is defeating the **Spirits**. They can't **Seal** something exceeding their own level, otherwise it won't hold, unless other methods are used to complete the **Sealing**.[^25]
		- With the aid of these **Spirits**, they can effectively multitask, coordinating *2-3 tasks simultaneously*.
		- This is similar to the powers of a **[Gatekeeper](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_5:_Gatekeeper)**, though it is more focused on **Sealing** and **Sleeping** than an **Internal Underworld**.[^26]
		- Different types of **Spirits** have different strengths. Aside from **Wraiths**, **Shadows**, and **Evil Spirits**, the **Spirits** they have been shown to use are:
		- **Terror Banshee**: A powerful **Spirit World Creature** with a beautiful face and rotting body.[^27]
			- It has a pair of huge eagle wings and is good at draining the consciousness of others, injecting feelings of terror in them.
				- **Mud Spirit**:<sup>⁂</sup> A rare **Natural Spirit** that resembles a series of illusory mud.[^28]
			- By enveloping the **Spirit Warlock**, they can sink to the ground and fuse into the earth. It allows them to quickly traverse the soil at a speed far faster than a steam metro.
				- **Shapeshifting Spirit**:<sup>⁂</sup> A **Spirit** that can **Shapeshift** into a human, which can be used to fool attackers.[^29]
			- After being hit, the **Spirit** will rapidly turn transparent and dissipate.
				- **Severed Hands**:<sup>⁂</sup> A pair of pale, translucent palms with bloody wrists.[^8]
			- It can be used to exert strength from afar, it can stop a **[Gatekeeper](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_5:_Gatekeeper)** from fully opening the **Door to the Underworld**.
				- Suitable **Spirits** can allow them to **Fly** [^30] or **Float** in midair.[^8]

**Strengthened Abilities** [^2]

- **Dream Invasion**: The range has increased to *200 meters*.

## Sequence 4: Nightwatcher

**Individual Differences** [^31]

- **Bloodline Abilities**: When they become a **Nightwatcher**, the bloodline traits of a [True Deities'](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Gods "Gods") descendants they have used for their advancement will grant them new abilities.
	- Different [True Deity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Gods "Gods") bloodlines will grant each **Nightwatcher** distinct abilities; however, this only changes the abilities that they can use to deal with spiritual bodies.[^32]

**New Abilities** [^2]

- **Night Domain**: **Nightwatchers** can make pure **Darkness** surge out of their body like a tide to form a domain that can boost their capabilities and weaken enemies trapped inside.
	- This can be used for defense, offset negative effects, as well as provide a good and comfortable combat environment for the **Spirits** controlled by the **Nightwatcher**.
		- The **Darkness** created by [Ince Zangwill](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ince_Zangwill "Ince Zangwill") was able to devour a **Lightning Storm.**[^33]
		- Their **Night Domain** can weaken the enemy in many ways. It can corrode them and make them lose vitality, as well as also blessing them with **Misfortune**.
- **Hair Entanglement**: **Nightwatchers** are able to create and manipulate countless hairs like a **[Demoness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Demoness_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_6:_Pleasure_\(Demoness_of_Pleasure\))** that can be used bind and immobilize enemies.
	- It brings coldness to those who touch them, making their thoughts still and their bodies peaceful, as if they no longer want to resist a peaceful slumber.[^34]
		- The hair that the **Nightwatcher** can create can be invisible, as if they have emerged from the **Darkness** in a **Dream**, or they can appear black.
		- From the **Hair Entanglement** ability, one can vaguely glimpse the interchangeability of the and the [Demoness Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Demoness_Pathway).
- **Concealment**: **Nightwatchers** possess some powers and abilities over **Concealment**.[^35]
	- A **Nightwatcher** is able to completely **Conceal** someone else's mystical connections in a state of secrecy by shrouding them with **Darkness**.
		- A **Nightwatcher** is able to **Conceal** someone else's exact form and physical appearance through shrouding them in a dreamy but dark mist.
- **Curse of Misfortune**:<sup>⁂</sup> They can give a certain amount of **Misfortune** to others.[^36]
- **Luck Sensation**: They can sense when their **Luck** has been altered.
	- This can manifest in the form of them “smelling an aura of danger”.[^37]

**Strengthened Abilities** [^2]

- **Spirit Commanding**:<sup>⁂</sup> Their ability to control various **Spirits** has been enhanced.
	- For every tooth they have, that is 1 more **Spirit** they can **Command**.
		- At the **Nightwatcher** level, different species (due to differences in number of teeth) do indeed make a difference in the number of **Spirits** that can be commanded; however, at levels after that, numerical limits become largely meaningless anyway, since there can be so many.[^38]
		- They are very good at dealing with **Spirit** creatures.
- **Requiem**: This **Beyonder** power has undergone a qualitative change.
	- It will extinguish a point of light and stain it with the densest and deepest **Darkness**. Within, there will be the sounds of chanting poetry, which is both peaceful and tranquil.
		- It can induce people to sleep as well as cause deceased **Spirits** and wraiths to become slow and **Pacified**, as if they have gained spiritual salvation.
- **Nocturnality**: All they need is a dark environment to be enhanced.
	- The darker the environment, the stronger their strength and the faster the recovery speed. The opposite of this is true as well, making them significantly weaker in the midday sun.
		- Of course, this can be offset by relying on the **Darkness** created by **Night Domain**.
- **Dream Invasion**: This has expanded to *1000 meters.*
	- **Dream Invasion** can also affect multiple targets and bring them in the same **Dream**.
		- Even a **[Soul Assurer](#Sequence_6:_Soul_Assurer)** won't know they've been pulled into a **Dream**.[^34]

**Mythical Creature Form**

- As **Sequence 4 Demigods** they receive some level of **Godhood**, and with it comes an incomplete [Mythical Creature Form](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mythical_Creature_Form "Mythical Creature Form"). Disregarding all consequences, they can release their incomplete [Mythical Creature Form](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mythical_Creature_Form "Mythical Creature Form") to become an incomplete **Eight-Legged Demonic Wolf**.

## Sequence 3: Horror Bishop

**New Abilities** [^2]

- **Sword of Darkness**: It is a bone sword covered with strange patterns drawn from the depths of the **Darkness**, combining the power of **Repose** and **Horror**.
	- Swinging the sword can cause the **Darkness** to surge, making a tide of **Darkness** that symbolizes the danger in the **Darkness** as well as silent destruction and vanquishment.
		- When the target is submerged by the surging **Darkness**, it will be infected by the power of **Darkness** and directly melt, becoming part of the tide.
			- If not prevented in advance, it is unavoidable and cannot be escaped.
				- As this tide of **Darkness** created from waving this sword also symbolizes silent destruction and vanquishment, it is partially **Concealed**.[^39]
- **Nightmare World**: They can create a **Dream** world and pull everyone in range into it.
	- A **Horror Bishop's** **Nightmare World** range is around that of a small city*.*
		- It can protect a target's [Soul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul") from **Evil** or other kinds of **Corruption**.
		- They can move around while others are in the **Nightmare World**.
		- Examples of this in use are:
		- [Ilya](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ilya "Ilya") created a **Dream** that enveloped the entire Faoltec city in order to protect the soldiers and citizens from the aura of madness and evil emanated from [Suah](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Suah "Suah"), who was over two thousand meters away.[^40]

**Authorities**

- **Horror Aura**: A lower manifestation of the authority of **Horror**.
	- When looking directly at them or being actively influenced by them, a target's body and mind will tremble involuntarily, which is difficult to contain and **Conceal**, and greatly affects their state.
		- **Beyonders** who are not strong enough may even be frightened to death by the **Horror Bishop**, or be scared out of control and become crazy.
				- As for "influence", a **Horror Bishop** merely entering a target's **Dream** can already cause that target to either lose control and/or die.
		- Newly promoted **Horror Bishops** cannot restrain their own terrifying aura, it is best that they don't appear in front of **Low-Mid Sequence** **Beyonders** and ordinary people.

**Strengthened Abilities**

- Their previous abilities were drastically enhanced.
	- **Midnight Poem**: This ability of theirs have been greatly enhanced.
		- **Pacify**:<sup>⁂</sup> Their chants and poems are able to put an entire city to sleep.
			- It makes the sunlight in a room suddenly dim, as if clouds had drifted in, causing the target to enter a deep **Slumber**.[^41]
		- **Spirit Commanding**:<sup>⁂</sup> Their abilities with spirit have been enhanced.
		- **Intangible Spirit** <sup>⁂</sup>: An intangible spirit that grows enormous and envelops the **Horror Bishop** entirely, carrying them into the [Mirror World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mirror_World "Mirror World").[^42]

**Mythical Creature Form**

- As a **Sequence 3 Beyonder**, they can respond to prayers within a certain range.

## Sequence 2: Servant of Concealment

**Authorities** [^2]

- **Concealment**: "They" possess the authority of **Concealment** to some extent.
	- **Concealed**: "They" can make the target enter a **Concealed** state that cannot be sensed by the outside world. Regardless of the target wants—the target will be erased like an eraser.
		- In the **Concealed** state, the real world the target can see is covered with dark fog, and the target can only roughly distinguish where they are and what is around them.
			- In the **Concealed** state, the target can't exert influence on the outside world, and it is difficult for the outside world to influence them.
				- When a **Historical Projection** of "Themselves" is summoned, "They" can sense it and enter a state of **Concealment**, allowing "Them" to take control of the **Projection**.
		- "They" can **Conceal** secrets. These secrets may be inferred by others through clues left behind; however, if the knowledge is still in a **Concealed** state, it cannot be said out loud.
		- This lets "Them" also prevent everything that happens near "Them" from being seen.
			- "They" can enclose an area with a field, making all the actions in the area a secret. Actions within are impossible to be peeked at or [Divined](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Divination_Art "Divination Art") by even [Sequence 1 Angels](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Angels "Angels").
				- The secrets "They" keep can be presented to the others in the forms of scenes.
		- **Reverse Concealment**: Just as "They" can cause a target to enter a state of **Concealment**, **Servants of Concealment** can remove the **Concealed** state from a target.
		- Through cooperation with **Concealment**, "They" can directly extract [Beyonder Characteristics](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristics "Beyonder Characteristics") from a target that has no mental imprints.
		- **Concealed World**: "They" are able to create an entire area that is **Concealed** by itself. A target can be directly **Sealed** there through cloaking and uncloaking.
		- Battles that happen inside **Concealed World** won't damage the real world.[^43]
		- "They" can ensure the element of surprise in a sudden attack.[^44]
		- [Zaratul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Zaratul "Zaratul") needed the help of a [Knowledge Emperor](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hermit_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_1:_Knowledge_Emperor) or a **Servant of Concealment** in order to ensure the element of surprise in a sudden attack against [Klein](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti").[^44]
		- **Servants of Concealment** are also able to **Conceal** a variety of other different things, including but not limited to identities, actions, contents, etc.
		- **Servants of Concealment** can use **Concealment** to detect **Angels** gathering outside of the [Protected Zones](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Protected_Zones "Protected Zones").[^45]

**Strengthened Abilities**

- "They" gain an improvement in previous **Sequences'** **Beyonder** powers.[^2]
	- **Nightmare World**: The range "Their" **Dream** world can affect is a large city.
		- **Requiem**: "Their" mere presence can bring extreme peace.
		- This can make "Them" capable of quickly suppressing the alarm raised by a [Bizarro Sorcerer](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fool_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_4:_Bizarro_Sorcerer), even after the latter has already witnessed "Them".[^46]

**Mythical Creature Form**

- "They" possess a complete [Mythical Creature Form](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mythical_Creature_Form "Mythical Creature Form"). "Their" [Mythical Creature Form](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mythical_Creature_Form "Mythical Creature Form") is that of a **Giant Eight-Legged Demonic Wolf** with six hands; four of which grew from their ribs, and two legs.

## Sequence 1: Knight of Misfortune

TRANSLATION NOTE

The "Misfortune" (厄难) authority of the **[Knight of Misfortune](#Sequence_1:_Knight_of_Misfortune)** uses slightly different characters compared to the "Misfortune" or "Bad Luck" (厄运) of the **[Wheel of Fortune](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Wheel_of_Fortune_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Wheel_of_Fortune)** or **[Darkness](#Sequence_0:_Darkness)**.
- **[Knight of Misfortune](#Sequence_1:_Knight_of_Misfortune)**: **Misfortune** (厄难, *Ènàn*):
	- 厄 (Adversity) + 难 (Disaster, Catastrophe); Can be translated as "Calamity", "Disaster", or their synonyms.
		- The second character, 难, is also present in the "Catastrophe" (灾难, *Zāinàn*) of a **[Demoness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Demoness_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_2:_Catastrophe_\(Demoness_of_Catastrophe\))**.
- **[Wheel of Fortune](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Wheel_of_Fortune_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Wheel_of_Fortune)** / **[Darkness](#Sequence_0:_Darkness)**: **Misfortune** (厄运, *Èyùn*):
	- 厄 (Adversity) + 运 (Fortune, Fate, Luck); Bad Luck

**Traits**

- **Physical Enhancement**: "Their" Strength, agility, physique, and speed are true to the title of **Knight**, a **Knight** at the level of an [Archangel](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Angels "Angels").[^2]

**Authorities** [^2]

- **Misfortune**: "They" can give **Misfortune** to enemies—the **Darkness'** authority over **Misfortune**.
	- While "Their" **Misfortune** leans more towards **Bad Luck**, national-scale **Misfortune** is possible.[^47]
		- This can manifest in the decline of a country's fortune accompanied by natural disasters, or as **Bad** **Luck** affecting every citizen of that country.
		- The **Misfortune** authority can bestow **Misfortune** upon [Wheel of Fortune](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Wheel_of_Fortune_Pathway), [Eternal Aeon](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Eternal_Aeon_Pathway), and [Eternal Edict](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Eternal_Edict_Pathway) [Beyonders](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonders "Beyonders") that are at the same level level as "Them".[^48]
- **Servant of Darkness**: In the **Dark Night**, a **Knight of Misfortune** is almost impossible to be truly killed, every time "They" die, "They" can return from the **Darkness** — a specific manifestation of the **Darkness** authority.
	- However, this doesn't mean immortality, since, eventually, one will integrate into the **Darkness**, becoming part of the **Darkness**.[^49]
		- Similar to the [Demoness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Demoness_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Demoness_\(Chaos_Demoness,_Primordial_Demoness\)) ' **Dark Magic** authority, there will be a significant degree of mitigation of **Darkness** -related attacks against "Them".[^50]

**Strengthened Abilities**

- **Sword of Darkness**: The **Darkness** brings **Eternal Rest**, **Despair** and **Destruction**.
- **Commander of Spirits**: "They" can put **Natural** and **Undead Spirits** to sleep and command them.[^2]

## Sequence 0: Darkness

**Authorities** [^2]

- **Concealment**: An authority that belongs to the **Singularity of All** symbolism. "They" can **Conceal** traces in all aspects, mislead related prying and [Divination](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Divination_Art "Divination Art"), make anything **Concealed**, undo such **Concealment**, and intuitively perceive and grasp many secrets.[^51] [^52]
	- "They" can directly drag "Their" targets into a **Concealed World**. Victims of this ability would seem to be wiped away from the real world, like a sketch being erased.[^53]
		- The **Concealed Worlds' Fate** isn't connected to the **Reality's** [Fate](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/River_of_Fate "River of Fate").[^54]
				- Victims are able to attempt escape; however, doing so will take some time.[^53]
				- "They" can transform into many symbols that symbolized **Concealment** and terror, enveloping "Their" enemies into a **Concealed** world.[^55]
				- "They" can turn hidden realms into **Concealed Worlds**.
			- These **Concealed Worlds** can then be used as **[Divine Kingdoms](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Divine_Kingdoms "Divine Kingdoms")** or as **Seals**.
		- "They" can silence others in various ways, ensuring that the secret "They" want **Concealed** will not be revealed by the target.
		- "They" are the most difficult [God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Gods "Gods") to be spied on or detected by others of the same rank.[^56]
- **Darkness**: "They" can induce **Dreams**, bring peace or **Eternal Rest**, and dissolve targets into **Darkness**, making them part of it. This can also be seen as partial authority over the **Dream** domain.
	- "They" can release the **Darkness** that is the **Destination of All Things**, which is extremely dangerous and may result in immediate death—even if "They" are trying to control it for an ally.[^57]
		- The remnants of "Their" own divine power can cause countless creatures within "Their" **Darkness** to vanish completely.[^58]
				- "They" can release a dense, profound, and silent **Darkness** that rapidly engulfs the target. This causes the projections of brilliant stars within the [Special Mirror World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Special_Mirror_Worlds "Special Mirror Worlds") to be submerged, extinguishing their radiance one after another.[^59]
		- As **Darkness** is one of the most fundamental symbols of the universe and an important motif, "They" cannot die in **Darkness** and can **Resurrect** with its help.
		- The **Tranquillity** and **Eternal Slumber** brought by the **Darkness** authority can resist **Corruption** and mental attacks by soothing the target.[^60]
		- When confronting a [Visionary](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Visionary_Pathway) **Beyonder** at the same level, "Their" **Dream** (**Darkness**) authority would lean towards **Nightmares**.[^61]
		- Even [True Deities](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Gods "Gods") can be pulled into a **Dream** through the **Darkness** authority via deep sleep inducement.[^62] [^63]
- **Spirit Body & Spirituality**: "They" are the destination of **Spirits** and their Master.
	- "Their" authority over the **Spirit Body** and **Spirituality** overlaps with the authority of the [Mother Goddess of Depravity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Goddess_of_Depravity) and [Death](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway).
		- **[Death](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Death_\(Eternal_Sleeper\))** leans towards the **Undead** while the **Darkness** leans towards **Natural Spirits**.[^64]
- **Misfortune & Catastrophe**: "They" can bring **Catastrophes** via **Misfortune**.
	- "Their" authority over **Misfortune** and **Catastrophe** overlaps with the authority of the [Demoness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Demoness_Pathway), [Red Priest](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Priest_Pathway), and [Wheel of Fortune](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Wheel_of_Fortune_Pathway) [Pathways](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways").
- **Horror**: "They" are the embodiment of the emotion of **Horror**.
	- "They" are able to make others feel tremendous fear and **Horror**, interrupting their actions.[^65] [^66]
		- [Evernight Goddess](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Evernight_Goddess "Evernight Goddess") ' eyes seemed to compress the starry night sky. It made one feel at peace, but it also made one feel an uncontrollable fear.[^53]
		- This authority overlaps with the authority of the [Mother Tree of Desire](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Tree_of_Desire).
