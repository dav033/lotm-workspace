## Sequence 9: Arbiter

**Traits** [^1]

- **Order**: An **Arbiter** will subconsciously maintain the current **Order**, unwilling to disrupt its fabric.
	- If they bear [official identities](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/List_of_Orthodox_Organizations "List of Orthodox Organizations"), this tendency only intensifies.[^2]
- **Physical Enhancement**: They have outstanding combat skills to deal with the unexpected.[^3]

**New Abilities** [^1]

- **Authority**: **Arbiters** possess a convincing charm and have a considerable **Authority**,[^3] causing people to be more likely to believe and obey their commands and words.
	- When in conflict against an **Arbiter**, their enemies are often less confident about their chances and will have the feeling of wanting to give up.

## Sequence 8: Sheriff

**Traits** [^1]

- **Physical Enhancement**: Their physical constitution is effectively enhanced.

**New Abilities** [^1]

- **Jurisdiction**: A **Sheriff** is able to designate an area as their **Jurisdiction**.[^1]
	- A **Sheriff** and their own area of **Jurisdiction** are always connected.[^8]
		- The more familiar they are with the place, and the more trust or support they receive from the people there, the more they can exert abilities beyond their own capabilities and receive some assistance from the environment.
			- Once they exceed their **Jurisdiction** range, or if they have just acquired **Jurisdiction** and are not yet familiar with it, they can only rely on their own **Beyonder** powers.
		- Their area of **Jurisdiction** is approximately the size of a small town.[^1]
		- If they receive an official appointment from the authorities, the area under their administration can be larger, but it must not exceed *5 times the normal limit.*
- **Recognition**: As long as they see a person in the flesh, in a photo, or a sketch, they'll be able to firmly remember the target’s appearance and obtain an additional sense on an extraordinary level.[^1] [^5]
	- They possess memory, in the mystical sense, of the routes they have traversed.[^1] [^9]
- **Weapon Proficiency**: A **Sheriff** has also gained proficiency in portable melee weapons and portable firearms such as *pistols* and *shotguns*.

## Sequence 7: Interrogator

**Traits** [^1]

- **Physical Enhancement**: Their bodies improve to a certain extent.

**New Abilities** [^1]

- **Illusory Torture Devices**:<sup>⁂</sup> They can summon **Illusory Torture Devices** (like branding irons) in their hands which can directly damage the **Spirit Body**; however, there must be physical contact as these devices cannot be used from a distance.[^10]
	- [Xio Derecha](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Xio_Derecha "Xio Derecha") used an illusory branding iron to stop an [Ancient Wraith](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Creatures_and_Plants#Ancient_Wraith "Creatures and Plants") from attacking her and [Fors](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fors_Wall "Fors Wall").[^10]
- **Psychic Lashing**: They can coat their weapons in bolts of illusory lightning which lets them turn regular cold weapons into things capable of affecting **Spirit Body** -type creatures.[^10]
	- [Xio Derecha](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Xio_Derecha "Xio Derecha") used **Psychic Lashing** alongside her blade to attack an [Ancient Wraith](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Creatures_and_Plants#Ancient_Wraith "Creatures and Plants").[^10]
- **Psychic Piercing**: This **Beyonder** power causes bolts of lightning appear within and shoot out from their eyes to attack their target at an effective distance of *5 meters.[^11]*
	- It can impose pressure on the **Spirit Body**,[^12] focus the mind,[^13] ignore the physical body, pierce the target's **Soul**, and directly attack their **Spirit Body**, bringing unbearable pain.[^1]
		- **Psychic Piercing** isn't a **Beyonder** power that disrupted with thoughts or affected the mind, but is a means to directly attack the **Spirit Body**. In essence, while other abilities act on the **Body of Heart and Mind,** **Psychic Piercing** ’s target is the **Soul Body** instead, the two are significantly different.[^1] [^14]
- **Whip of Pain**: This directly attacks the mind, making the target feel like an electric current are running through their mind, forming a barbed whip that constantly lashes their **Soul**.[^15] [^1]
	- This "feeling" is both painful and numb, as if it comes from the depths of the brain, making it irresistible. The affected target can only tremble, their knees going weak, and they have to confess as well as dare not lie.
- **Demolition Proficiency**: They have also become demolition experts.

**Strengthened Abilities** [^1]

- **Weapon Proficiency**: They are proficient in using a variety of weapons.[^1]

## Sequence 6: Judge

**Strengthened Abilities** [^1]

- **Authority**: A **Judge's** **Authority** have undergone a qualitative change, not only making them have an indescribable aura that makes people around them subconsciously avoid their gazes but also being able to influence the environment.
	- They can use **Mystical Languages** such as **Ancient** **Hermes** to establish new **Rules** or make a corresponding **Verdict** for a certain area. Everything within that area must abide by the **Rules** or the **Verdict's** outcome and violations will result in being bound by the **Rules**.
		- Both the **Judge's Spirituality** and **Rules** limit **Verdict** to a degree.[^16]
		- Examples of common **Verdicts** are:[^1]
		- **Prohibition**: Pushing forward with an open palm, a **Judge** can use **Ancient Hermes** to establish a rule forbidding certain actions or **Beyonder** powers, forcing everyone within a certain range to comply with the **Prohibition**.[^1]
			- **Prohibitions** generally follow this format: "'X' is **Prohibited** here".
				- Some of the examples of the **Beyonder** powers and actions a **Judge** can **Prohibit** include the following: **Flight**,[^17] floating,[^17] **Teleportation**,[^18] **Invisibility**,[^19] etc.
					- When it comes to the **Prohibition** of **Invisibility**, those who are hidden in shadows won't be compelled to reveal themselves, as they weren't invisible to begin with. They had merely shape-shifted into a shadow being, blending with the shadows.[^20]
						- At its current level, **Prohibition** targeting specific races or individuals cannot directly kill the target; it can only cause continuous suppression or repel them from the **Prohibition's** area of effect.[^1]
				- Such **Prohibitions** that are directly aimed at a particular existence will be quickly suppressed if there is a level difference.[^17]
								- Examples of this are **Prohibiting** the existence of beings such as **Specters** and **[Wraiths](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chained_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_5:_Wraith)** within an area.[^17]
						- With preparation and only providing assistance, it can influence even **Saints** to a certain extent, though not necessarily effectively, but it can cause some disruption.[^21] [^1]
				- [Frunziar](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Frunziar_Edward "Frunziar Edward") 's **Prohibition** of flight made [Ulyssan](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ulyssan "Ulyssan") 's wings seemingly weighed down by invisible objects weighing a hundred times its own body weight, though it eventually managed to fly with great difficulty.[^22]
								- [Xio](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Xio_Derecha "Xio Derecha") caused [Botis](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Botis "Botis") 's normally instantaneous **Teleportation** to be blocked by a rusty, abnormally heavy door—something that couldn't keep him from leaving after making adjustments.[^18]
				- **Imprison**: This **Verdict** creates layers upon layers of transparent walls as well as a flowing, extremely viscous, transparent liquid that freezes the target in place, making it difficult for even **Spirits** to pass through.[^1]
			- **Imprison** can root **Spirit Body** type creatures in place [^23] but can't prevent **Spirit Bodies** from escaping via abilities like **Substitution**.[^20]
				- **Release**: The effect can be ended early using the word " **Release** ".
						- It will not trigger abilities such as **Mirror Substitution** unless they have been used beforehand.
				- **Confinement**: This is **Imprison** applied to a certain area, creating an immaterial wall—a transparent cell—with their target at its center, impenetrable by **Spirit Bodies**.
			- This can be used to circumvent **Substitution** abilities by **Confining** a small area.
				- This is because targets would reappear within the **Confinement's** boundaries after using **Substitution** abilities.[^20] [^24]
						- This can be used to **Seal** a room to prevent entry or exit.
				- **Confinement** caused the dining room of [Capim](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Capim "Capim") to "freeze", as if an additional layer of invisible wall surrounded the area.
				- **Maintain Secrecy**: By making eye contact with the target, their eyes flash with a strange light, making the other person feel that they must do as instructed and not reveal secrets.[^25]
				- **Death**: After declaring the target as **Dead**, their body merges with some strange force, dragging a blur at speeds that create afterimages as they crash into the enemy.
			- Their fist strikes the target's head with an unavoidable force, causing devastating damage and it can also be aimed at a specific spot on the target, inflicting a severe injury.
				- **Flog**: Their hand is swung so that the target appears to be struck by an invisible soft whip, which can tear clothes, lacerate flesh, and expose bone.[^26]
				- **Exile**: By saying the word " **Exile** ", they can send an immense and invisible power that forces targets out at high speeds.
			- This affects **Spirit Bodies** as well, causing them to be blown away.
- **Jurisdiction**: A **Judge's** area of **Jurisdiction** is enhanced.
	- The upper limit of its maximum range approaches the size of [Backlund](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Backlund "Backlund").[^8]

## Sequence 5: Disciplinary Paladin

**Traits** [^1]

- **Physical Enhancements**: Their body is sufficiently strong enough and sufficiently robust.[^1]
- **Enhanced Mental Attributes**: They have a mystical sense of self-restraint.
	- This makes them experts at resisting interrogation and prying.[^27]
		- They can resist truth serum and **Hypnosis**.[^28]
- Their intuition and **Spiritual** **Perception** have been enhanced.
	- This makes them able to pinpoint the source of the threads of a **[Demoness of Pleasure](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Demoness_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_6:_Pleasure_\(Demoness_of_Pleasure\))**.[^19]
		- They would be able to perceive any surreptitious modification of the terms the moment they sign a contract with an **[Overseer](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chaos_Mist_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_4:_Overseer)**.[^28]

**New Abilities** [^1]

- **Punishment**: They can select a target for **Punishment**, after setting it, they would emanate a dawn-like glow, enhancing their influence and offensive power against the target.[^20]
	- Targets for **Punishment** include but are not limited to:[^1]
		- Those who attack the **Disciplinary Paladin** or affect their actions.
				- Those who are not part of the surrounding environment.
				- Those who violate a certain law or morality.
				- Those who should not affect **Reality**.
				- Those who violate their own rules.
		- They can choose non-living objects as targets for **Punishment**.
		- [Stratford](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Stratford "Stratford") chose the "bindings" restricting him to be a target of punishment, tearing the **Abyss Chains** apart with immense strength.[^29]
		- They can **Punish** the offender in different ways, for instance:
		- A **Disciplinary Paladin** can restrict the offender, seemingly immobilizing their target's feet with invisible shackles, making their movements rigid.[^17]
		- As the target is selected for **Punishment**, the **Disciplinary Paladin** will the seem to grow taller in the target's eyes, striking fear into their heart. The target will sense that the **Disciplinary Paladin** is a formidable figure who could end their existence with a mere thought.[^20]

**Strengthened Abilities** [^17]

- **Authority**: The aura of their **Authority** causes surrounding beings to feel inexplicable horror. The affected target's movements become slow, and they unconsciously wish to lower their heads, prostrate themselves, listen to every word of the **Disciplinary Paladin** and obey every order given by them.
	- Some of the words they can utilize have undergone a qualitative change.
		- **Prohibition**: This is the **Judge** ability at **Sequence 5**, it can be stacked to further impose restrictions on targets that can resist the rules to some extent.[^30]
			- If an enemy violates the rules set by the user, the **Disciplinary Paladin** will be enhanced by an unknown force, attacking the enemy with a guaranteed hit.[^22]
				- **Death**: It has undergone a qualitative change. At this level, this **Verdict** can be used by them to target a specific location on the target, inflicting severe damage.
			- For example, shortly after [Siatas's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Siatas "Siatas") successful attack, [Frunziar Edward](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Frunziar_Edward "Frunziar Edward") used “ **Death** ” with the [North King’s](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ulyssan "Ulyssan") wound as the target, causing all the flesh and blood in the surrounding area to wither, making the wound open all the way to the bones.[^30]
- They have gained improvements to other abilities and traits from previous **Sequences**.

## Sequence 4: Imperative Mage

**Traits**

- **Physical Enhancement**: They have gained enhancement to their physical fitness.

**New Abilities** [^1]

- **Imperatives**: The core ability of an **Imperative Mage**; It allows them to set broad restrictions and maintain them for a period of time.[^1]
	- The broader the restrictions set by an **Imperative**, the shorter the duration.
		- Generally speaking, the shortest duration is *30 seconds* and the longest is *15 minutes*.[^1]
		- Examples of the **Imperatives** they can employ are:
		- " *Weaken Mysticism, Enhance Reality* " will greatly weaken all **Beyonders** and mysterious effects within an area (including their side), while strengthening **Reality's** influence.[^31]
			- This is a powerful tool for the military in **Reality** to combat **Beyonders**.
				- " *Weaken Reality, Enhance Mysticism* " is the opposite **Imperative** of the former, which is able to enhance **Beyonder** effects while weakening the influence of **Reality**.
				- Appropriate **Imperatives** are also able to help the them accomplish many things/effects that are normally impossible.[^1]
			- For example, levitation, or being able to slow down bullets and cannonballs as though they sunk into a quagmire formed of air.[^31]
- **Contracts**: **Imperative** **Mages** can also create **Contracts** that conform to the **Rules** of the world and internalize these contracts into various restrictions on the physical and **Spirit** **Bodies**.[^1] [^32]
	- Unlike a **[Notary](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sun_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_6:_Notary)**, the binding force of an **Imperative Mage's** **Contracts** comes from the rule-based **Order**, rather than from the witness of [Gods](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Gods "Gods") or higher beings.[^1]

**Strengthened Abilities**

- The abilities of all the previous **Sequences** have been massively strengthened.
	- **Supernatural Intuition**: As practitioners of **Order**, **Imperative Mages** can distinguish between ordinary people and **Beyonders** based on their "positions" and can even determine the level of a **Beyonder**, that is, their "position".[^1] [^32] [^33]
		- **Beyonders** who have **Concealment** powers to some degree, such as **[Bizarro Sorcerers](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fool_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_4:_Bizarro_Sorcerer)**, are able to hide their **Sequence** level from them but not their status as a **Beyonder**.[^33]
				- They can also perceive the intertwining **Beyonder** powers of their targets, manifesting in different colors and states. This allows them to use **Deprivation** effectively.[^34]
		- **Authority**: Their **Authority** has been amplified to the point that it can intimidate the target and leave them rooted to the spot, also their **Verdicts** have been enhanced as well.
		- **Prohibition**: They can now **Prohibit** more actions and **Beyonder** powers.
			- They can **Prohibit** an area from having any interaction from the outside world.[^35]
						- They can **Prohibit** stand-ins, preventing any usage of **Substitution** abilities.[^36]
						- They are able to **Prohibit Marionette Interchange**.[^37]
				- **Deprivation**: **Deprives** a target of a certain **Beyonder** power.
			- Stripped **Beyonder** powers are made unusable for a certain period of time.[^38]
				- **Deprivation** is unavoidable and could only be resisted or weakened through one's level or **Sequence's** characteristics.[^37]
						- Different from a **[Depriver](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tail-Devourer_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_5:_Depriver) 's** **Deprivation** which takes away the corresponding Beyonder Characteristics or the [Error Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway) 's **Theft** which is within the **Inconceivable** symbolism, the **Deprivation** of an **Imperative Mage** only **Deprives** the target from using that power and does not completely take it away from them.[^39]
				- **Exile**: Saying the word " **Exile** ", they can **Exile** their targets into a void, the destination unknown. This allows them to deal with enemies that cannot be resolved quickly.[^40] [^1]
				- **Execution**: An **Imperative Mage** can make a fist with their right hand and swing it down hard, which can directly **Execute** the target and kill them from afar.
				- **Confinement**: By making the left hand loosely clenched, they can **Confine** the target.
				- **Intimidation**: This **Verdict** **Intimidates** the target and keeps them in place.

**Mythical Creature Form**

- As **Demigods** at **Sequence 4** who have obtained partial **Godhood**, they are able to manifest their incomplete **Mythical Creature** **Form** if they disregard any of the consequences and become an incomplete giant **Brass Pillar**.

## Sequence 3: Chaos Hunter

TRANSLATION NOTE

In the original Chinese text, 混乱 (hǔnluàn), the term used for " **Disorder** ", is sometimes translated as " **Chaos** ", as is done in the English WebNovel translation, this conflicts with a similar term, 混沌 (hùndùn). Since these terms are both distinct authorities, this page will consistently use the same English words to refer to the same Chinese words throughout for clarity and distinction.
- **Disorder** (混乱 *hǔnluàn*)
	- Refers to a state of confusion, disorder, or lack of order. It implies the breakdown of established systems, rules, or expectations.
- **Chaos** (混沌 *hùndùn*)
	- Refers to primordial chaos—the formless and undifferentiated state that precedes or underlies creation. Usually used in creation myths.
Note: The "Chaos" in **Chaos Hunter** (混乱猎手 *hǔnluàn lièshǒu*) uses the characters for "Disorder" (混乱 *hǔnluàn*).

**New Abilities** [^1]

- **Balancing**: They can initially apply **Balance** capabilities to divide the battlefield—bringing **Order** to the battlefield—but it cannot prevent this **Order** from sliding further into **Disorder**.

**Authorities** [^1]

- **Disorder**: They gain a small portion of authority over **Disorder**.
	- **Chaos Hunters** have the direct ability to sense, lock onto, and track **Disorder**, and can also quickly quell environmental **Disorder**.
		- Seizing the moment, [Judgment](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Xio_Derecha "Xio Derecha") reached the cathedral's dome and pressed down with her right palm, like **Chaos** descending from the heavens to the earth.[^24]
- **Judgment**: They gain partial authority over **Judgment**.
	- **Sword of Judgment**: **Chaos Hunters** are able to make a direct **Judgment** on **Disorderly** things, which cannot be avoided.
		- The more **Disorderly** the target is (that is, the more it deviates from or disrupts the current **Order**), the stronger the **Judgment** effect will be.
				- Through this **Judgment**, they can use the **Order** of the environment to instantly jump to the target location or in front of the target.
			- [Georgina Augustus](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Georgina_Augustus "Georgina Augustus") erased the distance and appeared directly above the [Tutanssess II](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tutanssess_II "Tutanssess II") mummy, [Mahmosi](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mahmosi "Mahmosi"), and [Zatwen](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Zatwen "Zatwen").[^31]
				- This can be used to deal with certain negative effects on their own body, like for instance, a **[Disciple of Silence's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chained_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_3:_Disciple_of_Silence_\(Cursed_Artifact\))** **Transfiguration Curse**.
			- After he and his steam chariot were **Cursed** into a goat, [Celt](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Celt "Celt") cleaved through the **Disorder** and **Evilness**, returning his original appearance.[^41]

**Strengthened Abilities** [^1]

- The **Beyonder** powers of their previous **Sequences** have become enhanced.
	- **Imperatives**: They gain enhancements to the core power of an **Imperative Mage**.
		- " *All the dead will receive their eternal peace* " has the effect of creating a region that induces eternal rest to the **Dead**.[^31]
				- " *Truth recedes here, while Illusion grows stronger* " will cause **Truth** to be weaker while **Illusion** becomes stronger, making the line between real and false weaker.[^42]
		- **Jurisdiction**: The **Jurisdiction** has been expanded to include metropolitan areas.
		- If a **Chaos Hunter** is extremely familiar with the locations within their **Jurisdiction**, they can arrive at them almost instantly.
		- **Verdict**: Their ability to impose **Verdicts** on their target has been enhanced.
		- **Prohibition**: They can now **Prohibit** more **Beyonder** powers and existences.
			- They can directly **Prohibit** **Marionettes**, such animated corpses would abruptly cease their movements, and this effect can work against even **[Miracle Invokers](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fool_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_2:_Miracle_Invoker)**.[^43]
						- By saying " *Concealment is **Prohibited** here.*", they can make a **Concealed** target to instantly appear out of nowhere in the area they were.[^44]
				- **Exile**: By declaring the word " **Exile** ", they can create an invisible and majestic force that coalesces into a terrifying hurricane, materializing directly where the target is.[^34]
				- **Deprivation**: The number of abilities that can be **Deprived** has been greatly increased.

**Mythical Creature Form**

- As a **Sequence 3 Beyonder**, they can respond to prayers within a certain range.

## Sequence 2: Balancer

**Authorities** [^1]

- **Balance**: "They" gain partial authority over **Balance**.[^1]
	- "They" can divide the battlefield, separating the various sides into different areas while roughly ensuring a **Balance** of power in each battlefield.
		- Before ending their own battle and making the corresponding battlefield cease to exist, neither [Angels](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Angels "Angels") nor [Saints](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Saints "Saints") can interfere in other battles.
			- Even a [Planeswalker](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Door_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_2:_Planeswalker) will be locked in their respective area, preventing them from joining other battlefields.[^45]
				- If one side becomes too strong, members who exceed the **Balance** limit will be excluded from the battlefield within a short period of time.
			- This will last anywhere between *10 seconds to 2 minutes*, within that time, those affected will be unable to participate in the battle.
						- Alternatively, these members could freely choose their own battlefield if the **Balancer** does not prevent them from doing so.[^45]
				- The underlying **Rule** is to end **Disorder**, restore **Order**, and enforce **Balance**, however, this may not necessarily mean perfect **Balance**. Consequently, this **Balance** can **Distorted** by a **[Duke of Entropy](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Black_Emperor_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_2:_Duke_of_Entropy_\(Entropy\))**.
			- The **Rule** created by the **Pillar of Severity** created a disparity in strength between the two sides when including [Sealed Artifacts](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sealed_Artifacts "Sealed Artifacts").[^46] The [Sun](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Derrick_Berg "Derrick Berg") could not use the [Proof of Glory](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Proof_of_Glory "Proof of Glory") due to its characteristics, while the [Demoness of Gold](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Demoness_of_Gold "Demoness of Gold") could freely use the [Eye of God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Eye_of_God "Eye of God").[^45]
						- Using [Solomon's Mask](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Solomon%27s_Mask "Solomon's Mask"), [Judgment](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Xio_Derecha "Xio Derecha") **Distorted** the **Balance** to become more extreme, allowing her and the [Demoness of Orange](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Demoness_of_Orange "Demoness of Orange") to join the [Sun](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Derrick_Berg "Derrick Berg") and the [Demoness of Gold](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Demoness_of_Gold "Demoness of Gold"), while forcing [Mirror Roselle Gustav](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mirror_Roselle_Gustav "Mirror Roselle Gustav") and the [Demoness of Black](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Clarice "Clarice") to face [Temperance](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sharron "Sharron") who wielded the [Box of the Great Old Ones](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Box_of_the_Great_Old_Ones "Box of the Great Old Ones").[^46]
		- In a one-on-one situation, a **Balancer** can forcibly reduce or increase the enemy's strength to a level roughly equivalent to their own within *1 minute*.
		- The stronger the enemy, the shorter the duration of this **Balance** with *1 minute* being the upper limit. In other words, this meant that the **Balancer** can be evenly matched with anyone, even a [True Deity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Gods "Gods"), though this will only last for *3 seconds*.
		- "They" can sense imbalances and **Disorder** in the surrounding area, allowing them to detect targets in a **Concealed** state.[^47]

**Strengthened Abilities** [^1]

- **Laws**: They gain the ability to enact **Laws and Decrees** which allow multiple **Prohibitions** or **Enhancements** to take effect simultaneously by defining the environment.[^48]
	- [0-02](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/0-02 "0-02") declared *"This battlefield is a Land of Light, a domain of righteousness. All depravity and evil shall find no footing."*
		- This caused the [Mirror World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mirror_World "Mirror World") to be illuminated by an unknown source of light and the head of [The Setting Sun Rooster](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/The_Setting_Sun_Rooster "The Setting Sun Rooster") to droop lifelessly with a snap, unable to utter corrupted words or emanate **Depravity**. It also made the [Demoness of Cyan](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Yalenna "Yalenna") unable to cast **Curses** or other **Dark Magic**.
- "They" gain an enhancement of abilities from previous **Sequences**.

**Mythical Creature Form**

- "They" have become a full **Mythical Creature**; "Their" **Mythical Creature Form** is that of a giant **Brass Pillar** with rules written on it.[^49]

## Sequence 1: Hand of Order

**Authorities** [^1]

- **Order**: "They" gain partial authority over **Order**.

Those specific methods need to be figured out.[^53]
		- "They" can sense the changes in **Order**.
		- "They" can observe the tug-of-war between **Disorder** and **Balance**, the evolving **Order** of the battlefield, and the enemy forces' formation, carefully calculating the **Rules,** and what kind of **Order** should be established.[^54]
				- [William Augustus I](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/William_Augustus_I "William Augustus I") managed to sense the death of [George III](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/George_Augustus_III "George Augustus III") through the changes in **Order**.[^55]

## Sequence 0: Justiciar

TRANSLATION NOTE

In the original Chinese text, 律法 (lǜfǎ) and 规律 (guīlǜ) are distinct terms with different nuances, though both can be translated as “ **Laws** ”, as is done in the English WebNovel translation. Since these terms are both distinct authorities, this page will consistently use the same English words to refer to the same Chinese words throughout for clarity and distinction.
- **Laws** (律法, *lǜfǎ*):
	- Refers to codified, enforceable laws—rules established by authority, often carrying judicial or binding force. It aligns with legal systems, decrees, and formal structures of governance.
		- Officially translated as "laws and decrees" [^48] and "set of laws" [^57] on *some* relevant chapters.
- **Principles** (规律 *guīlǜ*):
	- Refers to inherent patterns or natural laws—the underlying order of reality that exists independently of authority. It is closer to universal principles or the fundamental workings of the world.
		- In other contexts this term can also denote simpler patterns or regularity, not a strict law, but rather an observable consistency or recurring behavior over time such as everyday routines or behavioral patterns.

On the other hand, the symbolism of **Laws** also uses different Chinese characters, 定律 (dìnglǜ),[^58] which are also the same characters used for the "Laws" in the Laws of Beyonder Characteristics.

- **Laws** (定律, *dìnglǜ*)
	- Refers to fixed laws or established principles—a stable, universally applicable rule that does not change.

**Authorities** [^1]

- **Order**: The **Justiciar** possesses the **Order** authority and its corresponding subordinate authorities over **Rules**, **Laws**, **Principles**, etc.
	- "They" can modify the underlying **Rules** of the world to some extent.
		- Compared to the **[Paragon's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Paragon_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Paragon)** ability to change **Laws of Physics** versus the **Justiciar's** ability to change the underlying **Rules**, the **Justiciar** is stronger as it also effects mystical **Rules**.[^59]
				- The [Trunsoest Brass Book](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Trunsoest_Brass_Book "Trunsoest Brass Book") has **Rules** presented in grayish-black that, if it were able to awaken further, it could change and do the following modifications:[^60]
			- " **[Scholar of Yore](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fool_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_3:_Scholar_of_Yore)** has the ability to summon **Historical Void Projections**. The success rate of **Summoning** depends on the familiarity and friendliness of the target..."
				- It might have reduced the success rate of summoning **Historical Void** **Projections**.[^60]
						- "When one’s rationality drops to 20% of its original value, there would be signs of losing control..."
				- It may cause [Beyonders](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonders "Beyonders") to show signs of losing control if their rationality falls a little.[^60]
				- "They" can make certain modifications to the **Rules** of the **[Red Priest's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Priest_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Red_Priest)** **Sacrifices**.[^53]
		- "They" are able to remove existing, non-underlying **Rules**.
		- "They" are able to establish various new **Rules** for the world.
		- These **Rules** can't be fundamental but they will be permanent, with an effect ranging from small to large, from a single nation to a planet or a star system.
			- The **[Son of Chaos](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Son_of_Chaos)** can even influence the **Laws of Beyonder** **Characteristics** such as **Indestructibility**, **Convergence**, and **Separation** to a certain extent, making limited, low-level, and small-scale modifications.
				- **Rules** have to be publicized or announced before it could truly be effective. Thus, **Concealing** the announcement of those **Laws** will make them ineffective.[^61]
				- The **Rules** can be made stricter over time with each additional clause. It can reach a level where it will be impossible for a person to abide by "Their" **Laws**.[^62] [^63]
			- The **Rules** that had been created will restrict "Them" as well. However, "They" could get rid of those **Rules** to break the restriction.[^60]
				- "They" can theoretically create a method for **Resurrection**; however, the **Rules** involved must be mutually reinforcing and can't directly stipulate "Their" **Revival**.[^16]
			- For instance, "They" can establish that the **Souls** of the deceased in this area remain preserved and do not enter the [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World"). Then "They" need to specify the duration of **Soul** preservation before stipulating that **Ghosts** can retain their memories. Only then, through step-by-step regulations, can **Resurrection** be achieved.
				- However, this approach is overly complex and easily disrupted.
				- The [Trunsoest Brass Book](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Trunsoest_Brass_Book "Trunsoest Brass Book") set several different **Rules**, each with its own corresponding **Judgment** when broken.[^60]
			- *"Lost items are regarded as abandonment."* [^64]
						- *"All living beings are equal before the **Law**. Even [Angels](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Angel "Angel") can be killed by ordinary people"*.[^64]
						- *“Resurrection of the deceased is prohibited here!”* [^65]
				- This weakened the will of the [Celestial Worthy](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Celestial_Worthy "Celestial Worthy") awakening inside the body of [Klein](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti").
						- *"Without [my](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Trunsoest_Brass_Book "Trunsoest Brass Book") permission, no living being can leave [this place](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Belltaine_City "Belltaine City")."* [^66]
				- This prevented [Klein Moretti](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") 's **Spirit Body** from leaving and entering **[Sefirah Castle](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirah_Castle)**.
		- In barren and dead places, both the **Justiciar** and the **[Black Emperor](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Black_Emperor_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Black_Emperor)** can rebuild a complete set of **Order** suitable for human survival.
		- The **Justiciar** relies on the original fundamental **Rules** to recreate an **Order** suitable for human survival, while the **[Black Emperor](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Black_Emperor_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Black_Emperor)** will **Distort** the original **Rules** to make them suitable for humans. The effect is the same, but the methods of realization differ.
		- The **Justiciar** can grant **Immunity** to specific individuals.
		- This will enable those individuals to disregard the effects of indiscriminate **Rules**.
		- **Order Proxy**: "They" can reach the [strongest tier of Half Great Old Ones](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Half_Great_Old_Ones#Tier_1 "Half Great Old Ones").[^52]
		- "They" can completely obtain all the abilities and symbolisms on the level of the [Celestial Thearch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Celestial_Thearch "Celestial Thearch") in [The Apocalypse](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/The_Apocalypse "The Apocalypse") war,[^67] but "They" would die and could only maintain this state for *less than 3 seconds*.[^68]
- **Judgment**: Anyone who violates the **Order** can be **Judged**.
	- Each **Judgment** will be supported by the will and power of the entire world, and this can be performed either remotely or in person.
		- The [Trunsoest Brass Book](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Trunsoest_Brass_Book "Trunsoest Brass Book") could **Punish** targets each time its **Laws** had been broken, which would also increase the corresponding level of **Punishment** until it reaches **Death**.[^57]
- **Disorder**: "They" have partial authority over **Disorder**.
	- The **Justiciar** is able to restore **Disorder**, quell **Disorder**, and extinguish **Disorder**. **Order** can also create **Disorder**.
- **Balance**: "They" are able to force **Balance**.
	- In addition to the previously manifested **Balances**, it can also **Balance** many abstract things, such as restoring the **Balance** between reason and madness for a certain period of time, but only *3 minutes* at most.
- **Divine Kingdom**: **Ancient Court of Judgment** or **Heaven of Laws**.
