- 20 grams of Ancient Tomb Shadow dust.[^1]

## Sequence 3: Arcane Scholar (Demon of Arcana)

![Potion - Paragon Sequence 3 - Arcane Scholar](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/lord-of-the-mystery/images/c/c2/Potion_-_Paragon_Sequence_3_-_Arcane_Scholar.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/200?cb=20260330044351)

Potion - Paragon Sequence 3 - Arcane Scholar

- Overview
- Formula
- Ritual
- Backlash
- Acting Method

### Potion Properties

> A transparent liquid in which sparks unceasingly appear and vanish.[^1]

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> An illusory, flame-shaped flower containing boundless knowledge.[^1]

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- 1 Spirit World Meteor.[^1]
- 1 Crystallization of an Annihilation Ghost.[^1]

Or

- **A [Arcane Scholar](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Paragon_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_3:_Arcane_Scholar_\(Demon_of_Arcana\) "Paragon Pathway/Abilities")** [Beyonder Characteristic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristic "Beyonder Characteristic").

Supplementary Ingredients

- Choose one of:
- 1 type of combustible material from the Spirit World Star-Fire.[^1]
	- 30 grams of dust from the Annihilation Ghost.[^1]
Or

	- 40 grams of dust from an **[Arcane Scholar](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Paragon_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_3:_Arcane_Scholar_\(Demon_of_Arcana\) "Paragon Pathway/Abilities")**.[^1]
	- 1 object once possessed by an **[Arcane Scholar](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Paragon_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_3:_Arcane_Scholar_\(Demon_of_Arcana\) "Paragon Pathway/Abilities")**.[^1]
- 90 milliliters of water contaminated by the Spirit World.[^1]
- 50 grams of a highly radioactive material.[^1]

### Advancement Ritual

Select a certain special [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World") knowledge and find a way to establish a close connection with it, achieving integration at the spiritual level.[^1]

- **Note**: "Certain special Spirit World knowledge" refers to knowledge that can contaminate you, lock onto you, or knowledge that has come to life.[^3]
