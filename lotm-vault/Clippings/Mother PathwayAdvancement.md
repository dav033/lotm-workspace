## Sequence 4: Ancient Alchemist

![Potion - Mother Sequence 4 - Ancient Alchemist](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/lord-of-the-mystery/images/9/91/Potion_-_Mother_Sequence_4_-_Ancient_Alchemist.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/200?cb=20260331054053)

- Ritual
- Backlash

### Potion Properties

> A clear liquid, like the source of life, tinged with a dreamy pale red.[^1]

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- Life Crystal of a Desolate Servant.[^1]
- Heart of a Harvest Giant Priest.[^1]

Or

- **A [Ancient Alchemist](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_4:_Ancient_Alchemist_\(Classical_Alchemist,_Human_Alchemist\) "Mother Pathway/Abilities")** [Beyonder Characteristic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristic "Beyonder Characteristic").

### Advancement Ritual

Personally refine the Stone of Life.[^7] [^1]
- This ritual is a tribute to the Philosopher's Stone.[^8]

The essence of the ritual is needing a large amount of life force to refine the stone [^9]

## Sequence 3: Pallbearer

![Potion - Mother Sequence 3 - Pallbearer](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/lord-of-the-mystery/images/2/20/Potion_-_Mother_Sequence_3_-_Pallbearer.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/200?cb=20260331054105)

Potion - Mother Sequence 3 - Pallbearer

- Overview
- Formula
- Ritual
- Backlash
- Acting Method

### Potion Properties

> A dark liquid concealing numerous illusory figures.[^1]

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> A shadowy, unusually indistinct figure that appears like it is weightless [^1]

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- Heart of the Dark Treant.[^1]
- Skin of the Harvest Giant Elder.[^1]

Or

- **A [Pallbearer](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_3:_Pallbearer "Mother Pathway/Abilities")** [Beyonder Characteristic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristic "Beyonder Characteristic").

Supplementary Ingredients

- 200 milliliters of the blood of the Harvest Giant Elder.[^1]
- One fruit of the Dark Treant.[^1]
- One piece of rotten plank from a coffin in a tomb.[^1]
- One type of food already used for the sacrifice (a bouquet of flowers can also be used instead).[^1]

### Advancement Ritual

- Personally bury a Sequence 3 of the [Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Darkness_Pathway "Darkness Pathway"), [Death](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway "Death Pathway"), or [Twilight Giant](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Twilight_Giant_Pathway "Twilight Giant Pathway") pathways, witnessing their entire journey from birth to death and return to the earth.[^1]
- Or, bury a Mythical Creature of one of these three pathways, thus not needing to witness their Birth and Death.[^1]

![Potion - Mother Sequence 2 - Desolate Matriarch](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/lord-of-the-mystery/images/f/fd/Potion_-_Mother_Sequence_2_-_Desolate_Matriarch.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/200?cb=20260331054117)

### Potion Properties

> A brownish-yellow liquid resembling the residue of rotting flesh, causing abnormal nausea.[^1]

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> A clump of brown soil covered with grass, grains, and vines.[^1]

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- Reproductive organs of a [Child of Harvest](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Creatures_and_Plants#Child_of_Harvest "Creatures and Plants").[^1]
Or

- Heart of a Daughter of Desolation. [^1]

Or

- **A [Desolate Matriarch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_2:_Desolate_Matriarch "Mother Pathway/Abilities")** [Beyonder Characteristic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristic "Beyonder Characteristic").

Supplementary Ingredients

- 300 milliliters of blood from a [Child of Harvest](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Creatures_and_Plants#Child_of_Harvest "Creatures and Plants") or Daughter of Desolation (other equivalents are also acceptable).[^1]
- 10,000 souls of creatures that died of starvation.[^1]
- One type of a desertified oasis remnant.[^1]
- A section of a creature's limb with vigorous vitality and extremely strong self-healing ability (such as [Sanguines](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sanguines "Sanguines")).[^1]

Conceive, reproduce, and nurture an entire race.

## Sequence 0: Mother

### Potion Properties

> A blood-red, bird's nest-like object covered in a translucent amniotic membrane, nurturing a phantom infant.[^1]

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- The.
- Three **[Naturewalker](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_1:_Naturewalker "Mother Pathway/Abilities")** [Beyonder Characteristics](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristics "Beyonder Characteristics").

## Trivia

The advancement rituals of the **Mother Pathway** are particularly sinister, especially that of the [Desolate Matriarch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_2:_Desolate_Matriarch "Mother Pathway/Abilities"), because they correspond to the **Evil** aspect of the [Mother Goddess of Depravity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Goddess_of_Depravity "Mother Goddess of Depravity").[^10]
