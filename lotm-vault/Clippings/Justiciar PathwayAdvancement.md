Potion - Justiciar Sequence 8 - Sheriff

### Potion Properties

> A liquid from the three colors iron black, dark red and silver white interweaving.[^1]

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> The ball-shaped object, resembling a child's fist, exhibited a captivating blend of three colors—iron-black, dark red, and silver. Its beautiful spiritual radiance emanated from the intertwining hues of black iron, dark red, and a prominent swath of silver.[^3] [^4]

Main Ingredients

- A [pair of Terror Demon Worm's eyes](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/List_of_Mysticism_Ingredients#Terror_Demon_Worm's_Eyes).[^2]

Or

- **A [Sheriff](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Justiciar_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_8:_Sheriff)** [Beyonder Characteristic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristic "Beyonder Characteristic").

Supplementary Ingredients

- 100 ml of blood from a Silver-white War Bear [^1]
- 20 ml of blood from a Fear Demon Worm [^1]
- 1 designation letter for a sheriff or similar position (does not need to be one's own),[^1]
- 10 drops of Monkey Flower essential oil.[^1]

## Sequence 3: Chaos Hunter

![Potion - Justiciar Sequence 3 - Chaos Hunter](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/lord-of-the-mystery/images/e/ec/Potion_-_Justiciar_Sequence_3_-_Chaos_Hunter.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/200?cb=20260331062148)

Potion - Justiciar Sequence 3 - Chaos Hunter

- Overview
- Formula
- Ritual
- Backlash
- Acting Method

### Potion Properties

> A brass colored liquid, silvery letters intertwining within it.[^1]

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> A fist sized spar brass cube with numerous mysterious characters written on it.[^1]

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- The palm of a Bronze Elf [^1]
- The tongue of a Nature Guardian (a humanoid, golden ape-like, Beyonder Creature whose mission is to strictly maintain the natural order of its region) [^1]

Or

- **A [Chaos Hunter](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Justiciar_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_3:_Chaos_Hunter)** [Beyonder Characteristic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristic "Beyonder Characteristic").

Supplementary Ingredients

- 120 ml of blood from a Nature Guardian [^1]
- 10 strands of hair from a Copper Elf [^1]
- 1 piece from the corpse of a Demigod hunted by oneself (it does not necessarily have to be the whole corpse) [^1]
- 30 grams of a Gallow's wood chips on which at least 1000 criminals have been hanged [^1]

### Advancement Ritual

- Handling a mysticism event involving at least Angel level powers on their own, restoring normal order to the affected area.[^1]
- The less people and property is lost in the area, the better the ritual.

## Sequence 1: Hand of Order

![Potion - Justiciar Sequence 1 - Hand of Order](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/lord-of-the-mystery/images/5/5d/Potion_-_Justiciar_Sequence_1_-_Hand_of_Order.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/200?cb=20260331062240)

Potion - Justiciar Sequence 1 - Hand of Order

- Overview
- Formula
- Ritual
- Backlash
- Acting Method

### Potion Properties

> A pure, transparent and unblemished brass colored liquid.[^1]

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> A brass 'fist' with clearly defined knuckles that clenches tightly.[^1]

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- **A [Hand of Order](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Justiciar_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_1:_Hand_of_Order)** [Beyonder Characteristic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristic "Beyonder Characteristic")

Supplementary Ingredients

- 1 code of law that has remained unchanged for over 300 years [^1]
- 9,000 portions of blood from lawbreakers (each portion must be at least 5 ml; the higher the level of the lawbreaker, the fewer portions are required) [^1]
- 9 abolished laws that violated normal circumstances [^1]
- 100 grams of contaminants caused by a fallen True God [^1]

### Advancement Ritual

Equivalent Rituals:[^1]

- Establish a nation with a population of at least 10 million, making one's will the order and a strict system of rules within that nation.[^1] or
- Control a nation with a population of at least 50 million, making one's will the order and a strict system of rules within that nation.[^1]
- In both rituals, the more Beyonders and the higher their level among the citizens, the fewer people are required, but the minimum population cannot be less than 1 million.[^1]
