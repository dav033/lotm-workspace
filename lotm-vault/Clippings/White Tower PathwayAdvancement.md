## Sequence 5: Mysticism Magister

![Potion - White Tower Sequence 5 - Mysticism Magister](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/lord-of-the-mystery/images/7/74/Potion_-_White_Tower_Sequence_5_-_Mysticism_Magister.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/200?cb=20260329184720)

Potion - White Tower Sequence 5 - Mysticism Magister

- Overview
- Formula
- Ritual
- Backlash
- Acting Method

### Potion Properties

> A dark red liquid with brass colored scales rising and sinking.[^1]

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> A translucent, curved "blood sausage".[^1]

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- The brain fluid of a young Brass Dragon or Brass Giant Lizard.[^1]
- The small intestine of a Shadow Naga.[^1]

Or

- **A [Mysticism Magister](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/White_Tower_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_5:_Mysticism_Magister)** [Beyonder Characteristic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristic "Beyonder Characteristic").

Supplementary Ingredients

- 3 scales of a young Brass Dragon or Brass Giant Lizard.[^1]
- 120 ml of blood from a Shadow Naga.[^1]
- 1 Mysticism Spell that has been conceived and verified but not yet created.[^1]
- 1 piece of a spell's notes recorded by another person (can be a copy and come from [Warlock](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hermit_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_7:_Warlock), [Witch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Demoness_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_7:_Witch), or other [Polymath](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/White_Tower_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_6:_Polymath) or [Mysticism Magister](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/White_Tower_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_5:_Mysticism_Magister) of the Pathway, etc.).[^1]

### Advancement Ritual

Train at least
- 50 Erudites [^1] or
- 5 Mid Sequence Beyonders who have independently comprehended the [Acting Method](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Acting_Method "Acting Method").[^1]
- The former is the all-rounder polymath direction. The latter is about which pathways of knowledge one is more proficient in depending on which pathways the Beyonders you taught are from.[^2]

### Ritual Essence

— No data —

— No data —

### Acting Method

— No data —

TRANSLATION NOTE

Erudite "通才" does not correspond to any sequence, it means Polymath or Universal Genius when literally translated, but when used as an adjective, it means an all rounder.

![Potion - White Tower Sequence 4 - Prophet](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/lord-of-the-mystery/images/c/c5/Potion_-_White_Tower_Sequence_4_-_Prophet.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/200?cb=20260329184733)

### Advancement Ritual

Find fragments of information about one's own future in the chaotic, overlapping spacetime of the [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World") and make them a reality.[^1]

## Sequence 3: Cognizer

![Potion - White Tower Sequence 3 - Cognizer](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/lord-of-the-mystery/images/7/71/Potion_-_White_Tower_Sequence_3_-_Cognizer.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/200?cb=20260329184745)

Potion - White Tower Sequence 3 - Cognizer

- Overview
- Formula
- Ritual
- Backlash
- Acting Method

### Potion Properties

> A brass-colored sanguine liquid that looks as if a miniature spirit world is concealed within.[^1]

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> A miniature tree formed by clusters of white, pointed bones, with blood-red brains hanging from its branches and numerous brass-colored eyes opening on its trunk.[^1]

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- The Eyes from a Greatwood of Written Doom.[^1]
- Brain of an ancient Brass Dragon.[^1]

Or

- **A [Cognizer](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/White_Tower_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_3:_Cognizer)** [Beyonder Characteristic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristic "Beyonder Characteristic").

Supplementary Ingredients

- 100 ml of sap from a Greatwood of Written Doom.[^1]
- 100 ml of blood from an ancient Brass Dragon.[^1]
- 1 item frequently used by a [Knowledge Magister](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Paragon_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_2:_Knowledge_Magister_\(Master\)) or [Sage](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hermit_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_2:_Sage).[^1]
- 1 remnant from the annihilation of a Demigod-level Spirit World creature.[^1]

### Advancement Ritual

Analyze either the Flesh and Blood or the [Beyonder Characteristics](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristics "Beyonder Characteristics") of an [Angel](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Angels "Angels") from the [Hermit](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hermit_Pathway) Pathway or the [Paragon](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Paragon_Pathway) Pathway and grasp at least one-third of the knowledge they contain.[^1]

- Or obtain the blessing of one of them.[^1]
- Can be substituted by a corresponding [Sealed Artifact](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sealed_Artifact "Sealed Artifact") [^3]

## Sequence 2: Wisdom Angel

![Potion - White Tower Sequence 2 - Wisdom Angel](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/lord-of-the-mystery/images/d/d4/Potion_-_White_Tower_Sequence_2_-_Wisdom_Angel.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/200?cb=20260329184759)

Potion - White Tower Sequence 2 - Wisdom Angel

- Overview
- Formula
- Ritual
- Backlash
- Acting Method

### Potion Properties

> A viscous, grayish-white liquid with wrinkles everywhere; multiple brass eyes are concealed within the wrinkles.[^1]

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> A grayish-white brain with densely packed brass-colored eyes and a blood-red tinge.[^1]

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- The Beyonder Characteristic of the ["Dragon of Wisdom"](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/God_of_Knowledge_and_Wisdom "God of Knowledge and Wisdom").[^1]
Or

- The Beyonder Characteristic of the [Knowledge Dragon](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Knowledge_Dragon "Knowledge Dragon") (a certain being from the [Second Epoch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Second_Epoch "Second Epoch") [^4]).[^1]

Or

- **A [Wisdom Angel](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/White_Tower_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_2:_Wisdom_Angel)** [Beyonder Characteristic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristic "Beyonder Characteristic").

Supplementary Ingredients

- 150ml of blood from either the [Dragon of Wisdom](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/God_of_Knowledge_and_Wisdom "God of Knowledge and Wisdom"), the [Knowledge Dragon](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Knowledge_Dragon "Knowledge Dragon"), or a [Wisdom Angel](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/White_Tower_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_2:_Wisdom_Angel).[^1]
- 10,000 souls of creatures that helped enlighten the concoctor's wisdom after their natural death.[^1]
- 1,000 heartfelt titles of "teacher" or "mentor" from diverse intelligent beings (voice collected and documented in a record).[^1]
- A complete grasp of a certain period of history (has to cover at least 100 years, incorporated into the potion in the form of data).[^1]

### Advancement Ritual

Use intellect rather than strength to bring down an angel.[^1]

### Ritual Essence

It's about establishing a connection with the concept of wisdom in advance.[^5]

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- **A [Omniscient Eye](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/White_Tower_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_1:_Omniscient_Eye)** [Beyonder Characteristic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristic "Beyonder Characteristic")

- (Note: All of the ingredients above can be replaced with ones of higher quality)

### Advancement Ritual

Briefly enter a semi-chaotic state, but retain basic consciousness and rationality, and take the potion in that state.[^1]
- The methods include, but are not limited to, the Chaos influence from [Demoness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Demoness_Pathway), the corruption of the [Chaos Sea](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chaos_Sea "Chaos Sea"), and so on.[^1]

## Sequence 0: White Tower

![Potion - White Tower Sequence 0 - White Tower](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/lord-of-the-mystery/images/4/4d/Potion_-_White_Tower_Sequence_0_-_White_Tower.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/200?cb=20260329184936)

Potion - White Tower Sequence 0 - White Tower

- Overview
- Formula
- Ritual
- Backlash
- Acting Method

### Potion Properties

> Glimmering white light points that spiral into a giant tower reaching to infinity, each light point seems to contain a book.[^1]

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- The.
- Three **[Omniscient Eye](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/White_Tower_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_1:_Omniscient_Eye)** [Beyonder Characteristics](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristics "Beyonder Characteristics").

Supplementary Ingredients

- A tall tower filled with all the knowledge the concoctor possesses, containing relics of a [Great Old Ones](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Old_Ones "Great Old Ones") (relics of a [Half Great Old One](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Half_Great_Old_One "Half Great Old One") also count).[^1]

### Advancement Ritual

Participate in a calamity brought by an existence beyond Sequence 0 and survive (the conditions are also met by a [Sefirah](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirah "Sefirah") if the ritual performer has the knowledge of the corresponding Sefirah's [Great Old One](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Old_One "Great Old One")).[^1]

- The principle behind the ritual is the symbolism of "Knowledge leading to Calamity".[^6]
