## Sequence 9: Criminal

**Description**

**Criminals** do not suppress themselves. They do not feel constrained, and their body and heart are ruled by evil desires, but their conscience has not been completely annihilated, and their heart is not dead.

**Traits**

- **Physical Enhancement**: They possess a strong body with keen instincts.[^1]

**New Abilities** [^2] [^3]

- **Criminal Proficiency (Weapon**, **Criminal)**:<sup>⁂</sup> Upon advancement, **Criminals** will gain knowledge of a variety of abilities that designate one a convict in addition to having great combat ability.
	- Regardless of the weapon, be it a knightly sword, a dagger, a longbow, a pistol, a rifle, a six-barrel machine gun, or a spoon; they can utilize all of them to kill a target effectively.

## Sequence 8: Unwinged Angel (Coldblooded)

**Description**

Becoming an **Unwinged Angel** means that from that moment forth, they lose their conscience and indulge in their evil desires, possessing bodies that look even more inhuman. They don't have the concept of good and evil anymore.[^1] [^4]

**Traits**

- **Physical Enhancement**: Their bodies are further strengthened and more inhuman.[^5]
- **Enhanced Mental Attributes**: An **Unwinged Angel** can remain extremely calm and almost emotionless, even during a fierce battle.[^6]

**New Abilities** [^2] [^3]

- **Demonic Attribute**:<sup>⁂</sup> Upon entering this **Sequence**, they will undergo inhuman changes, acquiring two or three **Devil** **Spell** -like abilities in the process that vary from person to person.[^7]
	- Some could wield and create poisonous flames, cast weak **Curses** with **Language of Foulness**, or create a toxic black smoke. The most common of these **Spell** -like abilities is **Slowness**.
		- By raising their arms then abruptly pressing both of their palms down, they can make their target sink, slowing their movements, as though the wings they used for flying had been snapped.[^8]

## Sequence 7: Serial Killer

**Traits**

- **Physical Enhancements**: After undergoing the initial stages of dehumanization, they possess a robust physique.
	- Even after having his chest caved, ribs cracked, face swelled, his left shoulder crumpled entirely, and a skull fractured, with charred marks covered his upper body after the brutal assault, [Bram Andariel](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Bram_Andariel "Bram Andariel") was far from unconsciousness. His mind still functioned, and thoughts raced before settling on [Lumian](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee") 's smile.[^9]
- **Enhanced Mental Attributes**: Their intelligence and mental capabilities will be enhanced.

**New Abilities** [^2] [^3]

- **Knowledge (Devil Worship)**:<sup>⁂</sup> Upon advancement, a **Serial Killer** will gain a wealth of and master all kinds of knowledge related to **Devil** worship and its **Ritualistic Magic**.
	- By creating special or unique killing sprees, they would please and gain favors from **Devils**.
		- **Serial Killers** often tend to choose those who exhibit degraded behaviors, often linked to their personal traumas- as victims of their serial killing sprees.[^10]
				- Such **Serial Killers** enjoy creating unique serial killings but also commit their crimes not merely as a role, but also to please the **Devils** of the [Abyss](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Abyss "Abyss").
			- Once the serial killing cases are closed they would be able to perform the final step of this **Ritual**, letting them summon the corresponding **Devil** projection from the [Abyss](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Abyss "Abyss") and obtain it's assistance to achieve their desired outcome.
				- By saying foul words in the **Devil Language**, a mystical connection seemed to form with the **Serial Killer**, linking them based on their location, creating sinister symbols and patterns, marking the initiation of a ritual to summon the projection of an **Abyssal Devil**.[^11]
					- Even if one's string of murders aren't entirely complete and fell short by a few targets, so long as they meet the minimum threshold, they can expedite the ritual and still summon the **Devil**; however, this shortcut wouldn’t yield the same effectiveness as the standard procedure.[^11]
								- **Serial Killers** can summon these projections because the **Devils** respond to the 's unique abilities, not because those **Devils** themselves have attained the corresponding status.
						- Theoretically, the summoned **Devil** projection will obey the [Ritual](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ritualistic_Magic "Ritualistic Magic") performer; however, if it's rank is too high or it finds a loophole to bypass the ritual's protection, it will obey the corresponding true **Devil** in the [Abyss](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Abyss "Abyss").[^12]
- **Interference**: They can effectively interfere with [Divination](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Divination_Art "Divination Art") and **Spirit Channeling.**[^13]

**Strengthened Abilities**

- **Demonic Attribute**: Their ability has been enhanced.
	- Releasing a dense black smoke, [Bram Andariel](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Bram_Andariel "Bram Andariel") morphed, adopting the color of a chameleon, seamlessly blending with the spreading smoke. Swift and silent, he approached the door, opened it with precision, and lunged outside.[^14]

## Sequence 6: Devil

**Description**

**Devils** biggest weakness is their tendency to easily lose control. Even if they do not lose control, they will often exhibit a kind of insanity that is a culmination of both cruelty and cold-bloodedness. They are very easily possessed and controlled by their desire for blood, murder, and various other things.[^2]

**Individual Differences**

**Traits**

- **Physical Enhancement**: The first stage of qualitative changes in the is the comprehensive enhancement of the **Devil's** strength, speed, and defense.
	- Their skin has been mutated, equivalent to wearing a layer of hard armor while their their blood and flesh can act as a retardant that can block objects and reduce damage.[^15]
		- A **Devil** also possesses strong resistance capabilities, being immune to most toxins/poisons and not fearing any **Curses** or **Flames** within a certain level.
		- A **Devil** is fast enough to create afterimages.[^18]

**New Abilities** [^2] [^3]

- **Danger Premonition (Malice Perception)**: They can sense and grasp the place of origin of danger, as well as who this danger is from. In this way, the **Devil** can precisely devise countermeasures, take revenge, or nip the threat in the bud. Different **Devils** have different degrees of this intuition.[^2]
	- Unlike a **[Monster's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Wheel_of_Fortune_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_9:_Monster) Danger Premonition**, theirs doesn’t stem from **Fate** but from the [Abyss](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Abyss "Abyss").[^19]
		- Their **Danger Premonition** requires 3 conditions to be met before it can activate.
		1. If an existence is a fatal threat to the **Devil** and can cause life-threatening harm to them.
				2. Said existence is intending on doing such harm within a certain period and range.
				3. Said existence proceeds to actually act upon their intentions to harm the **Devil**.
		- Different variations of **Devils** would possess different degrees of **Danger Premonition**.[^15]
		- The "certain period" of sensing danger via **Danger Premonition** for some **Devils** can range from just a measly *few minutes*, to a whole entire *24 hours*.
				- The range of a **Devil’s** **Danger Premonition** may only span from a few miles- or in other cases- span the range of an entire metropolitan area.
		- Apart from this, they aren’t good at [Divination](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Divination_Art "Divination Art") or other methods of sensing danger.[^20]
- **Language of Foulness**: **Devils** can speak demonic words originating from the [Abyss](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Abyss "Abyss"), filled with **Filth** and **Corruption**, and unleash their corresponding abilities. At this stage, they can use the following:
	- **Slow**: This causes all targets within a *7-8 meter radius* to instantly turn numb and slow or even come to a halt, however, it can only be maintained for *2 seconds*.
		- **Death**: This **Spell** causes the target's blood to rust and clot. Victims will cough out rusted clumps of blood and have rusted red marks appear on their skin.
		- If the enemy fails to avoid or resist the effect of a **Devil's Death**, it can directly cause them to die or become very close to dying.
			- This will make the target's heart feel as if it is being squeezed tightly by an invisible hand as they die covered in mottled red rust.
		- **Corruption**: An area of effect attack that can cause the targeted area and individuals within to start showing signs and symptoms of **Abyss** **Corruption**.
		- This will cover the enemy's body with a slightly viscous black liquid, which then explodes into shattered pieces of paper and corrupted black fog.
- Upon advancing into this **Sequence**, **Devils** will gain access and mastery over a wide range of **Spells**, encompassing the fields of **Fire**, **Poison**, and **Filth/Foulness**.
	- **Sulfur Fireball**: They can launch multiple pale blue **Fireballs** that create a powerful explosion and poison anyone or anything that comes into contact with the **Flames**.
		- No more than *3 at a time* can be launched and conjured.
		- **Sword of Lava**: They can conjure up a massive blade shaped **Flaming** sword that looks like a scimitar in design and holds extremely high attack power.
		- A **Sword of Lava's** damage against non-undead and non-corrupted creatures is significantly higher than the [**Priest of Light's**](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sun_Pathway/Abilities "Sun Pathway/Abilities") **Holy Light Summoning**.
				- One swing from a **Sword of Lava** is capable of slicing a thick stone pillar in two, with the impact surface looking like it has been seared and melted.
		- **Flame Cage**: Within a certain range, a **Devil** will be able to conjure up a large **Cage** of **Flames** that can be used to block targets from escaping.
		- A pale blue **Flame** can also be ignited in the palm of their hand, causing bullets to be trapped in the **Cage** and solidify in the pale blue **flame**.
		- In addition, **Devils** also possess **Spells** and abilities such as **Poisonous Flame Manipulation**, **Volcanic Eruption**, **Highly Toxic Vapor**, etc.
- **Devil Transformation**: **Devils** can transform into their true forms temporarily, gigantifying their bodies, giving them bat wings and goat horns, while also increasing their strength, speed, and defense as well. This is akin to breaking free of their bindings.[^5] [^15]
	- When they enter their **Devil Transformation** state, their skin will deeply mutates, appearing in a dull but actually deep, sinister black and their entire body seems to be covered in a thick, hard armor.
		- Their blood and flesh becoming extremely elastic and resilient.
		- While in their **Devil Transformation** state certain restrictions to **Spells** have been lessened. For example, they can now launch *10-20* **Sulfur Fireballs** simultaneously as opposed to just 3.
		- When creatures and people of different races become **Devils**, their outward appearance will differ; however, they will all share several common characteristics.
		- These details include gigantic bodies, deep black and sinister skin, curved and terrifying ram horns covered with countless mysterious patterns, and pale blue sulfur flames.[^21]

**Strengthened Abilities**

- Their foundation as a **Serial Killer** and **Criminal** has been improved. They excel at using all kinds of items and objects to cause harm, hold strong hand-to-hand capabilities, and don't panic or experience fear.[^2]
	- This is effectively an enhancement to a **Serial Killer's Enhanced Mental Attributes** and a **Criminal's Criminal Proficiency** when it comes to the **Combat** field.

## Sequence 5: Desire Apostle

**Individual Differences**

- **Devil Bloodline**:<sup>⁂</sup> Different **Devils** will develop different special abilities after advancing into a **Desire Apostle**, such as **Shadow Transformation** and **Desire Symbiosis**.
	- **Desire Symbiosis**: Links the **Desire Apostle** to a certain **Desire** of the target.
		- Once this is done, as long as the corresponding **Desire** or goal is not destroyed, the **Desire Apostle** who possesses this ability cannot be completely killed.

**New Abilities** [^2] [^3]

- **Desire Control**: **Desire Apostles** can utilize and control every existence's emotions and **Desires**.
	- As long as a target manifests an apparent emotional fluctuation (i.e anger/fear/despair) or has an increase in any type of **Desire**, they can immediately control that target from afar.
		- From there, they can either bury a **Defiling Seed** or catalyze the expressed emotion/ **Desire**.
				- When a target's **Desire** gets catalyzed or a **Defiling Seed** sprouts the target would either:
			- Gradually become **Corrupted** by the catalyzed **Desire**, lose control over the targeted emotion, being unable to fight back or resist against the influence that emotion has on their actions and thoughts, or suffer an explosion of **Desires**.
				- When the amount of catalyzed **Desire** exceeds the maximum volume the target's body can withstand, the target will suffer a grave injury, a fatal wound, or damage that could even cause death if severe enough.
								- In cases where the amount of catalyzed **Desire** does not exceed the maximum volume the target's body can withstand, the target will be exposed to numerous problems, including but not limited too:
					- Emotional breakdown at crucial moments.
										- Gradual **Degeneration**.
										- Inability to resist.
								- If the target's alertness, tension, and vigilance are amplified by a catalyzed **Desire**, this can disrupt the target's sense of timing.
		- **Mental Shock**: They can ignite one of the 2 curved horns on their head to forcefully attack an enemy’s psyche, inducing emotions to a certain degree in an unavoidable manner.
		- It can't be used after the devil horns have burned out.
				- This belongs to the **Desire Control** branch.
- **Desire Incarnation**: **Desire Apostles** can transform their body into being an illusory, thick, black liquid form that is a collection of condensed emotions and **Desires** given a semi-visible form.
	- The liquid is like a collection of the most wicked and shameful **Desires** in the human heart, sweeping around and surging towards the target with a corrupting influence.
		- While in this **Desire Incarnation** form, a **Desire Apostle** is quite effective at avoiding damage, hiding their trails, and even affecting their enemies with emotions/desires.

**Strengthened Abilities** [^2] [^3]

- They have gained improvement of abilities in the previous **Sequence**.

## Sequence 4: Demon

**Description**

The " **Demons** of the **Body** " and " **Demons** of the **Mind** ".

**New Abilities** [^2] [^22] [^3]

- **Demon of the Mind**: **Demons** are an advanced version of a **Serial Killer**. They are cold, sharp-minded, and sly. They are the textbook example of a standard high IQ creature.
	- They are cunning and enjoy misleading others.
		- In addition, they can effectively influence targets within a certain range, subtly and imperceptibly diminishing their thinking abilities.
			- This will make those affected careless and prone to errors.
- **Demon of the Body**: Being a " **Demon** of the **Body** ", they have physical attributes that are ranked pretty high on the total ranking list compared to other **Demigods**.
- As a **Demon**, they possess **Spells** and **Spell** -like abilities that are ranked pretty high on the total ranking list when compared to other **Sequence 4 Demigods**.
	- They have gained new **Spells** such as **Acid Swamp**, **Hellfire**, **Corrosive Fog**, **Filthy Illusion**, and **Projection** that does not rely on the [Abyss](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Abyss "Abyss").

**Strengthened Abilities** [^2] [^22]

- **Desire Control**: They have undergone a qualitative change. Now, **Demons** no longer need to wait for an expression or increase in a target’s **Desires**. They can now directly manipulate it through their actions.
	- **Demons** are able to make their targets fall into an unavoidable illusion. Their sorrow, anger, pain, greed, vanity, arrogance, and other **Desires** would rapidly swell as illusions of those **Desires** will manifest around them in the form of themselves exhibiting those emotions.
		- Some individuals would have their mind explode from the illusion while others lose their sanity or experience a loss of control due to the illusion of themselves exhibiting that **Desire**.
		- They can use sound, eye contact, movements, gestures, and various other means to directly influence those within a certain radius, making them enter into a state of delusion while their emotions and **Desires** will automatically expand at flying speeds.
		- After which, one of three things can happen:
			1. The target(s) cognition or sanity will be completely destroyed.
						2. The target(s) mind will be lit up like a fuse and explode.
						3. The target(s) will start the process of losing control.
- **Danger Premonition (Malice Perception)**: Their ability to sense danger has been enhanced.
	- **Demons** are exceptionally sensitive to danger and sometimes, they can even predict the possibility of a threat to their lives *one* to *two days* early in advance.
- **Filthy Language**: A **Demon** will possess a more complete set of **Language of Foulness**.
	- Words like **Confusion**, **Sloth** and **Blasphemy** have been added to their repertoire.
		- The [Montsouris Ghost](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Montsouris_Ghost "Montsouris Ghost") said " *Human nature is evil!*", causing all [Saints](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sequence "Sequence"), but the three [Unshadowed](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sun_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_4:_Unshadowed), to suddenly feel an intense malice. It was resentment from being assigned to dangerous missions.[^23]

**Mythical Creature Form** [^2]

- As **Demigods** at **Sequence 4** who have obtained partial **Godhood**, they are able to manifest their incomplete **Mythical Creature** **Form** if they disregard any of the consequences. Their **Mythical Creature Form** is mainly determined by race, individual personality, **Desires**, and will.[^24]
	- **Devil Transformation**: Even though manifesting the incomplete **Mythical Creature** **Form** can easily bring about losing control, **Demons** have a semi-solution around it. They can let a certain part or area of their body manifest the appearance and characteristics of their **Mythical Creature** **Form**. After which, they can maintain it that way for a prolonged period of time.
		- This way, they are often able to obtain unique, and bizarre effects and abilities related to the corresponding body part that has been **Demonized**.

## Sequence 3: Blatherer

**Authorities [^3]**

- **Corruption**: A **Blatherer** has gained a certain degree of the **Corruption** authority, which is primarily concentrated in the realms of the **Mind** and **Spirit**.
	- Their voice and each and every word they utter can cause **Beyonders** to either lose control, suffer severe damage, experience mental confusion, or be hexed with all sorts of **Curses**.
		- Once a connection has been made, they can project their voice from an extremely distant location into a target’s ears, causing the target to inexplicably die suddenly in a safe location.
		- Similarly, if the **Blatherer** foresees the source of danger (for example via **Malice Perception**), they have the opportunity to exert a countering influence on the spot.[^2]

**Mythical Creature Form**

- As a **Sequence 3 Beyonder**, "They" can respond to prayers within a certain range.

## Sequence 2: Bloody Archduke

**Description**

"They" wield portions of the authorities of **“Malice”** and **“Body”**. "Their" core abilities revolve around **Blood**, **Bodily Fluids**, **Fear**, and **Malice**.

**Authorities** [^2] [^3]

- **Malice**: "They" have authority over the domain of **Malice**.
	- **Incarnation of Malice**: *" **Malice** never dies, neither do **Demons** ". "* They" can plant a **Seed of Malice** deep within a target's psyche. When "Their" main body is destroyed, "They" can **Resurrect** within the target's **Mind**, igniting the target's **Desires** and **Blood** and killing the target.[^25]
		- If "Their" target's main body has a connected psyche to other bodies (e.g. an **[Unaging's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Demoness_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_3:_Unaging_\(Demoness_of_Unaging\)) Mirror Self**), those psyches will also be eroded as well.[^25]
			- Using the [Robe of Divine Blood](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Robe_of_Divine_Blood "Robe of Divine Blood"), [Emlyn White](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Emlyn_White "Emlyn White") revived within [Katarina Pelle's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Katarina_Pelle "Katarina Pelle") **Mirror Self** after she herself resurrected, killing her in the process.[^25]
				- A **Bloody Archduke's** **Seed of Malice** is also able to catalyze the **Malice** of the target, making the affected target temporarily **Cold-Blooded**.
			- If the target commits a truly **Malicious** act during this period, they will fall further into **Depravity** until they fall into an [Abyss](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Abyss "Abyss").
				- This can both cause a target to fall into **Depravity** as well as be transported to the actual [Abyss](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Abyss "Abyss") because of the fact that the [Abyss](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Abyss "Abyss") is in everyone's heart.[^26]
						- Affected by this, indescribable ravings echoed in [Katarina Pelle](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Katarina_Pelle "Katarina Pelle") 's ears, as if her innermost **Malicious** emotions and desires were singing in unison, causing her to show signs of losing control.[^27]
		- As an [Angel-level](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Angels "Angels") **Devil**, "Their" intense **Malice** and **Desires** can overpower other entities' mental control abilities over "Them", be it from active influence or passive effect.
		- Rather than succumbing to a **[Demoness of Catastrophe's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Demoness_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_2:_Catastrophe_\(Demoness_of_Catastrophe\)) Charm**, "They" would develop an intense possessiveness, enabling "Them" to act decisively instead of being frozen in place.
		- **Malice** brings forth **Fear** to the target. This is a pure and instinctive **Fear** that can cause the **Mind** to go blank, making difficult for those affected to think and act.
- **Body**: "They" also have authority over the domain of **Body**.[^28] [^25]
	- **Blood Sovereign**: "They" command the **Blood** and **Bodily Fluids** of "Themselves" and others.
		- As long as a single drop of **Blood** or **Bodily Fluid** has not been destroyed, a **Bloody Archduke** can quickly return and regenerate "Their" body.
				- A **Bloody Archduke** can use "Their" own **Blood** and **Bodily Fluids** in order to **Corrode** and **Pollute** the external world and targets.
			- Being corroded under "Their" influence or touch would make "Their" opponents bleed from all orifices of their body as both their **Blood** and **Desires** get ignited until death.

**Strengthened Abilities**

- All abilities from "Their" previous **Sequences** have been further enhanced.

**Mythical Creature Form**

- "They" have become a full **Mythical Creature**, with "Their" main characteristic being that they seem to be made of congealed **Blood**, capable of liquefying and coalescing.[^21]

## Sequence 1: Filthy Monarch

**Authorities** [^2] [^3]

- **Filth**, **Corruption**, **Corrosion**, **& Flames**: A **Filthy Monarch** wields portions of the authorities of **Filth**, **Corruption**, **Corrosion**, and **Flames**.
	- **King of Filth**: "They" are the ruler of all that is **Dirty**, **Sinful**, and **Unclean**.
		- "They" are able to summon those things at any time to provide assistance, and can also directly affect targets that possess those characteristics.
				- Even [Angels](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Angels "Angels") of the same **Sequence** level will suffer a degree of contamination when "They" witness or approach the **King of Filth**.
			- The only exception is if those [Angels](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Angels "Angels") belong to domains such as the **Sun** or similarly **Purifying** authorities.
		- **The Corroder**: Even the strongest barrier will be penetrated by continuous **Corrosion** over a certain period of time.
		- The **Flames**, **Fog** and the environment that is created by **The** **Corroder** all reflect this characteristic.
		- **Flames of the Abyss**: This is a **Flame** that combines **Burning**, **Corrosion**, **Toxins**, and **Corruption** properties.
		- Most **Beyonders** ’ defenses against **Flames** only address **Burning**, leaving them dangerously vulnerable to the **Flames of the Abyss'** other aspects.

**Strengthened Abilities**

- All **Beyonder** abilities from "Their" previous **Sequences** have been further enhanced.

**Mythical Creature Form**

- The **Mythical Creature Form** of a **Filthy Monarch** presents a terrifying image like that of a nightmare in a **Filthy** and deep black expanse.[^21]

## Sequence 0: Abyss

**Description [^3]**

The **Abyss** wields partial authorities of **Degeneration**, **Filth**, **Corruption**, **Desire**, **Curses**, and **Deviants,** which are further divided into subordinate authorities such as **Corrosion**, **Malice**, **Toxins**, **Flames**, and **Body** and is characterized by "The **Abyss** is me, and I am the **Abyss**."

**Symbolism [^3]**

- **Abyss of the Heart**: The authorities of **Degeneration**, **Filth**, **Corruption**, **Desire**, **Curse** all involve the **Mind** authority, or rather, only when the **Mind** authority is combined with them, or with parts of them, can they be considered truly complete.
	- This also reflects that the **Abyss** is a symbol of the **Abyss of the Heart/Mind**.
		- Because the **Abyss** exists both materially and emotionally, with previous **Corruption** from the [Tenebrous World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tenebrous_World "Tenebrous World") in "His" early days as a relatively low-Sequence **Devil**, even though the **Seal** that separated the [Western Continent](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Western_Continent "Western Continent") appeared before "He" became the **Abyss**, the [Devil Monarch Farbauti](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Farbauti "Farbauti") by relying on the **Abyss of the Heart** paired with that slight bit of previous **Corruption** from the [Tenebrous World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tenebrous_World "Tenebrous World"), "He" was able to bypass the [Celestial Worthy](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Celestial_Worthy_of_Heaven_and_Earth_for_Blessings "Celestial Worthy of Heaven and Earth for Blessings") ’s seal to a certain extent and engage in interactions akin to spiritual communion with the [Master of the Shadow Cottage](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Master_of_the_Shadow_Cottage "Master of the Shadow Cottage").[^29]

**Authorities** [^2] [^3]

- **Degeneration (Depravity)**: "They" have partial authority over **Degeneration**.
	- If the **[Hanged Man](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hanged_Man_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Hanged_Man)** embodies the **Degenerate Nature** of every living being, then the **Abyss** is the environment that inevitably leads all those within it to **Degenerate**, not only inducing **Degeneration** but also creating it.
		- Violating one's three outlooks, values, and morals is **Degeneration**. Violating the **Rules** established by the [Trunsoest Brass Book](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Trunsoest_Brass_Book "Trunsoest Brass Book") is also **Degeneration**.[^30]
- **Corruption**: "They" have partial authority over **Corruption**.
	- It is both an outward extension of **Depravity** and **Corruption**, and an inevitable outcome of the explosion of **Desire** and the loss of rational control.
- **Filth**: The **Abyss** possesses partial authority over **Filth**.
	- **Filth** is a component of the **Abyss**, a manifestation of all unclean things, which brings multiple impacts and influences on the **Body**, **Mind**, and **Soul**.
		- **Toxins**: The **Abyss** possesses a partial authority over **Toxins**, a subordinate authority under **Filth**.
		- **Demons** and **Toxins** coexist; and "They" master most types of **Toxins** and those **Toxins** possessing the characteristics of the **Demon Pathway.**
- **Desires**: "They" have partial authority over **Desires.**
	- The **Abyss’s** authority over **Desire** lies more in inducing, catalyzing, and igniting **Desires**, rather than embodying **Desire** itself like the remnants within the [Mother Tree of Desire](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Tree_of_Desire "Mother Tree of Desire").
- **Curses**: "They" have partial authority over **Curses**.
	- Compared to the **[Chained](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chained_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Chained)**, "Their" **Curses** are primarily in the domains of **Spirit** and **Mind.**
- **Deviants**: "They" have partial authority over **Deviants**.
	- The impact on lower levels of this authority manifests as **Demons** exhibiting different **Mutations** based on personal traits and environmental factors.
		- At the higher levels, it's benefit is that the **Abyss** possesses and encompasses all such different **Mutations**, embracing everything.
		- **Body**: "They have partial authority over **Body**, focused on controlling and transforming the **Body** which is a subordinate authority under **Deviants**.
- **Malice**: "They" are the wielder of **Malice**, the embodiment of **Malice** —a subordinate authority born from **Degeneration** and **Desires**.**[^31]**
	- "They" can turn into a pool of black liquid which manifests **Malice**, **Desire**, and evil intent that is real and illusory and that can enter an entity's body, mind, and soul as a **Seed of Malice**.
		- Not only can this be used to travel with the target across various places (including following a target during a **Secret Deed Ritual**), this form can also be used to travel across other living beings due to the concept that there is a *mental* **Abyss** in everyone's heart.[^32]
- **Flame**: "They" have partial authority over **Flames**, which is subordinate to **Deviants** and **Curses**.
	- "They" control the **Flame** of **Degeneration**, **Corruption**, **Toxins**, and **Corrosion**.
- **Corrosion**: All things can be **Corroded**, this is a subordinate authority derived from **Filth** and **Corruption**.
- **Abyssal Sea of the Mind/Heart**: Essentially, the **Divine Kingdom** of this **Pathway** is the.
	- This **Divine Kingdom** exhibits different characteristics and has different names depending on the tendency of its **Sequence 0**.

**Mythical Creature Form**

- The **Mythical Creature Form** of the **Abyss** is not fixed as one will see what they fear most or what they yearn for the most, which is similar to the adjacent [Chained Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chained_Pathway), reflecting the fact that there is an [Abyss](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Abyss "Abyss") at the bottom of the human heart. [^21]
	- Everyone's [Abyss](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Abyss "Abyss") is different, and the **Abyss** presents a different image in their eyes.
