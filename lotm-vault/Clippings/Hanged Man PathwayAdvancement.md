## Sequence 9: Secrets Suppliant

![Potion - Hanged Man Sequence 9 - Secrets Suppliant](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/lord-of-the-mystery/images/6/6a/Potion_-_Hanged_Man_Sequence_9_-_Secrets_Suppliant.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/200?cb=20260329183916)

Potion - Hanged Man Sequence 9 - Secrets Suppliant

### Potion Properties

> A sacred black liquid intermittently bubbling with a bitter taste.[^1]

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> A semi-illusory black lip that seems to emit whispers.[^1]

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

Or

- **A [Secrets Suppliant](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hanged_Man_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_9:_Secrets_Suppliant "Hanged Man Pathway/Abilities")** [Beyonder Characteristic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristic "Beyonder Characteristic").

Supplementary Ingredients

— No data —

### Acting Method

- A [Secrets Suppliant](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hanged_Man_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_9:_Secrets_Suppliant "Hanged Man Pathway/Abilities") 's ability is based around praying and secrets.[^2]
- Therefore their acting is based on receiving secret knowledge through prayers or 'pleading' for it. [^2]
- The more dangerous the secret knowledge they receive is, the more they can digest their potion.[^2]

## Sequence 8: Listener (Whispered)

![Potion - Hanged Man Sequence 8 - Listener](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/lord-of-the-mystery/images/7/74/Potion_-_Hanged_Man_Sequence_8_-_Listener.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/200?cb=20260329183931)

Potion - Hanged Man Sequence 8 - Listener

### Potion Properties

> A hallucinogenic, multicolored liquid with a dark core; induces spiritual euphoria upon consumption.[^1]

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> A darkened, sinister ear.[^1]

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- Ear of a Black Cat of Misfortune.[^1]
- Heart of a Burrow Crawler (a pangolin-like Beyonder creature).[^1]

Or

- **A [Listener](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hanged_Man_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_8:_Listener "Hanged Man Pathway/Abilities")** [Beyonder Characteristic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristic "Beyonder Characteristic").

Supplementary Ingredients

- 100 milliliters of Red Wine.[^1]
- 1 portion of Bread.[^1]
- Heart of a Black Cat of Misfortune.[^1]
- 10 drops of Begonia Flower Essential Oil.[^1]

— No data —

### Acting Method

- [Listeners](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hanged_Man_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_8:_Listener "Hanged Man Pathway/Abilities") are like priests in a church, listening to the sins of others.[^3]
- Their primary goal is to avoid being influenced by those whispers they hear.[^3]
	- It is a state of listening without being corrupted by it, and if used good enough, it will eventually help them in advancing to a [Profane Presbyter](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hanged_Man_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_2:_Profane_Presbyter "Hanged Man Pathway/Abilities") in the future.[^3]

## Sequence 7: Shadow Ascetic

![Potion - Hanged Man Sequence 7 - Shadow Ascetic](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/lord-of-the-mystery/images/e/e0/Potion_-_Hanged_Man_Sequence_7_-_Shadow_Ascetic.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/200?cb=20260329183943)

Potion - Hanged Man Sequence 7 - Shadow Ascetic

### Potion Properties

> A gray-black liquid reflecting countless faces of the drinker; cold, bitter, and stinging.[^1]

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> A cluster of gray-black shadows.[^1]

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- Shadow Worm.[^1]
- Horn of a Blood-Eyed Black Goat.[^1]

Or

- **A [Shadow Ascetic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hanged_Man_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_7:_Shadow_Ascetic "Hanged Man Pathway/Abilities")** [Beyonder Characteristic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristic "Beyonder Characteristic").

- Tail of a Blood-Eyed Black Goat.[^1]
- 1 Item that as Desecrated a Church(any type).[^1]
- 10 drops of Wild Rose Hydrosol.[^1]

— No data —

### Acting Method

- It involves unity, preaching, and serving their "god".[^2]
- It encompasses meanings such as "practising in seclusion", "cultivating in the shadows" as well as "acting within the shadows to uphold the doctrine".[^2]

## Sequence 6: Rose Bishop

![Potion - Hanged Man Sequence 6 - Rose Bishop](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/lord-of-the-mystery/images/8/89/Potion_-_Hanged_Man_Sequence_6_-_Rose_Bishop.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/200?cb=20260329183956)

Potion - Hanged Man Sequence 6 - Rose Bishop

### Potion Properties

> Blood soaked with pale flesh chunks; induces excruciating pain akin to skin peeling and flesh disintegration.[^1]

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> A putrid mass of bloody flesh with visible tendons and blood vessels.[^1]

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- Immortal Flesh Fungus.[^1]
- Largest Fragment of a Vengeful Specter.[^1]

Or

- **A [Rose Bishop](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hanged_Man_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_6:_Rose_Bishop "Hanged Man Pathway/Abilities")** [Beyonder Characteristic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristic "Beyonder Characteristic").

Supplementary Ingredients

- 100 milliliters of One's Own Blood.[^1]
- 1 segment of Symbiotic Vine from the Immortal Flesh Fungus.[^1]
- 1 portion of Residual Powder from the Vengeful Specter.[^1]
- 10 grams of Cinnamon.[^1]

— No data —

### Acting Method

- By Chinese definition, Roses symbolize Love, Friendship, Peace and signify Blood. [^2]
- A [Rose Bishop](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hanged_Man_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_6:_Rose_Bishop "Hanged Man Pathway/Abilities") is the leader of an [Ascetic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hanged_Man_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_7:_Shadow_Ascetic "Hanged Man Pathway/Abilities") community/group who will spare no effort to spread their Love and Peace, even if it is radical and involves bloodshed.[^2]

## Sequence 4: Black Knight

![Potion - Hanged Man Sequence 4 - Black Knight](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/lord-of-the-mystery/images/0/0f/Potion_-_Hanged_Man_Sequence_4_-_Black_Knight.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/200?cb=20260329184120)

- Ritual
- Backlash

### Potion Properties

> Viscous liquid resembling a black gemstone; ingestion causes the body to collapse anew.[^1]

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> A segment of translucent black hand bone.[^1]

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- Remnant Curtain of a Shadow Knight.[^1]
- Flesh and Blood Condensation of a Griegel(Griegel is a Beyonder creature also known as the Crimson Giant).[^1]

Or

- **A [Black Knight](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hanged_Man_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_4:_Black_Knight "Hanged Man Pathway/Abilities")** [Beyonder Characteristic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristic "Beyonder Characteristic").

Supplementary Ingredients

- Shadow Traces of a Griegel.[^1]
- Black Liquid of a Shadow Knight.[^1]
- 1 drop of Blood from Each Ritual Participant.[^1]
- 10 drops of Lemon balm Essential Oil.[^1]

### Advancement Ritual

- Induce the complete degeneration of over 10,000 individuals without using violence.
- The higher the sequence of the degenerate individuals, the better the ritual effect.
- The more concentrated the transformation from normalcy to degeneracy in time, the more effective the ritual becomes.[^1]

### Ritual Essence

— No data —

— No data —

### Acting Method

- Carrying out divine will in the darkness, they are a knight willing to bear the Sins for their God's will.[^2]

## Sequence 2: Profane Presbyter

![Potion - Hanged Man Sequence 2 - Profane Presbyter](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/lord-of-the-mystery/images/1/1c/Potion_-_Hanged_Man_Sequence_2_-_Profane_Presbyter.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/200?cb=20260329184146)

- Ritual
- Backlash

### Potion Properties

> Filthy, repulsive liquid resembling sewer waste; taste matches its appearance.[^1]

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> A paper-thin, semi-illusory shadow suffused with foulness.[^1]

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- Pus of the Shadow King.[^1]
- Crystal of the Flesh Monarch.[^1]

Or

- **A [Profane Presbyter](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hanged_Man_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_2:_Profane_Presbyter "Hanged Man Pathway/Abilities")** [Beyonder Characteristic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristic "Beyonder Characteristic").

- 50 grams of Flesh of the Flesh Monarch.[^1]

### Advancement Ritual

- Separate, defeat, and fully control one's own shadow.
- Enter the Shadow World, confront dangers, and establish a personal "domain" within it.
- The Shadow World is an alternate dimension intersecting with the Mirror World.[^1]

### Ritual Essence

— No data —

— No data —

### Acting Method

- "They" are the prophets and punishers of heretics for the God "They" serve.[^2]
- Being an evolved form of a shepherd "They" guide people with revelations [^2]
- A [Profane Presbyter](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hanged_Man_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_2:_Profane_Presbyter "Hanged Man Pathway/Abilities") has to have the willingness to become corrupted for the sake of punishment.[^2]

- Endure for 15 minutes to merge with the shadow of light.[^1]

### Ritual Essence

— No data —

— No data —

### Acting Method

- "They" embody the Degenerate Nature, carrying the heavy burden of some kind of other sin.[^3]
