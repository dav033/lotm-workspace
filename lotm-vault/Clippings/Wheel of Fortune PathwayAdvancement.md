Without prior preparation, and without seeking a way to negate or alleviate the effects, directly face a major calamity while at the lowest ebb of your luck—and survive.[^1]

### Potion Properties

Or

- **A [Chaoswalker](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Wheel_of_Fortune_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_3:_Chaoswalker "Wheel of Fortune Pathway/Abilities")** [Beyonder Characteristic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristic "Beyonder Characteristic").

### Advancement Ritual

Fix a node, or "beacon", that your destiny must inevitably reach within the next ten years.[^1]

### Potion Properties

> A pure, viscous mass of mercury covered in countless tiny symbols and patterns.[^1]

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- Horn of a Fate Grey Tiger.[^1]
Or

- The Heart of a Scale-covered Treant Elder.[^1]

Or

- **A [Soothsayer](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Wheel_of_Fortune_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_2:_Soothsayer "Wheel of Fortune Pathway/Abilities")** [Beyonder Characteristic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristic "Beyonder Characteristic").

Peer into the [River of Fate](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/River_of_Fate "River of Fate"), uncover an important revelation concerning at least one nation, and ensure that this revelation ultimately manifests exactly as you "saw" it.[^1]

## Sequence 1: Snake of Mercury (Giant Serpent, Snake of Fate, Tail-Devouring Serpent of Fate)

![Potion - Wheel of Fortune Sequence 1 - Snake of Mercury](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/lord-of-the-mystery/images/a/a0/Potion_-_Wheel_of_Fortune_Sequence_1_-_Snake_of_Mercury.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/200?cb=20260330045755)

Potion - Wheel of Fortune Sequence 1 - Snake of Mercury

- Overview
- Formula
- Ritual
- Backlash
- Acting Method

### Potion Properties

> A head-to-tail mercury-colored small serpent (more like a flow of water) encased in a semi-transparent shell.[^1]

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> A mercury ring with countless cyclic symbols.[^1]

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- The Crystal Core of a Mercury Phoenix.[^1]
Or

- The Crystal Core of a Mercury Giant Serpent.[^1]

Or

- **A [Snake of Mercury](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Wheel_of_Fortune_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_1:_Snake_of_Mercury_\(Giant_Serpent,_Snake_of_Fate,_Tail-Devouring_Serpent_of_Fate\) "Wheel of Fortune Pathway/Abilities")** [Beyonder Characteristic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristic "Beyonder Characteristic").

Supplementary Ingredients

- One's own umbilical cord from birth or eggshell remnants.[^1]
- 9 drops of "water" closely related to oneself extracted via a special method from the [River of Fate](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/River_of_Fate "River of Fate") (each drop is a segment of fate) [^1]
- One item contaminated by the [Gray Fog](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fog_of_History "Fog of History") of the [Sefirah Castle](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirah_Castle "Sefirah Castle") (can be replaced by the blessing or bestowed item from Sequence 0 [Fool](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fool_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Fool "Fool Pathway/Abilities") [^4]).[^1]

### Advancement Ritual

- Find a way to extract all of your memories, then, after consuming the potion, infuse them back into yourself.[^1]
- Alternatively, with the aid of a **[Visionary](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Visionary_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Visionary "Visionary Pathway/Abilities")** during the ritual, regress to the state of a fetus, stripped of all memory, and awaken the full breadth of cognition buried deep within your soul and mind.[^1]
- **Note 1:** Specific term from the [Western Continent](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Western_Continent "Western Continent"): [Unveiling the Enigma of the Womb](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Western_Continent/Glossary#Unveiling_the_Enigma_of_the_Womb "Western Continent/Glossary").
	- **Note 2:** Here memory mean ALL memory, conscious and unconscious [^1]

### Ritual Essence

The core of the [Snake of Mercury](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Wheel_of_Fortune_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_1:_Snake_of_Mercury_\(Giant_Serpent,_Snake_of_Fate,_Tail-Devouring_Serpent_of_Fate\) "Wheel of Fortune Pathway/Abilities") is "reboot". The purpose of the ritual is to ensure the promoter does not get lost during the reboot and lose control.[^1]
