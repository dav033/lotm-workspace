### Consumption Backlash

> The pain would jolt them awake, recalling memories. They would gain fresh insights, a better understanding of the true intentions and thoughts of those involved in it. They would sense malevolent thoughts that had either come to fruition or were waiting to be acted upon.[^5]
> 
> Soon, their heart would be filled with rage, loathing, and a desire to obliterate those individuals and matters. They would feel an urge to cease holding back and unleash their emotions.
> 
> They have to resist these feelings of hatred, anger, and desire from taking reins.

### Note

> This is after Year 1352 where [Advancement](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Advancement "Advancement") via potion has gotten easier, to the point that potions for low sequences aren't as strictly necessary.[^8]

### Acting Method

- Instigate people to commit crimes and spread dissension.
- To instigate a group of people, just need to understand the situation and ignite the spark in a few initial individuals. The ignited ones would, in turn, become agents of instigation, rallying more people to their cause in a snowballing effect.[^6]
- Instigation is means, not an end. The essence of Instigation lies in understanding the core of the matter and the conditions of the people involved, not in the use of abilities. Instigation will inevitably bring about consequences; it just depends on whom you want to face those consequences.[^7]

### Lingering Effect

> To be in an unstable state of emotions and desire. Even facing an equally-leveled Demoness's Charm, they would find it difficult to resist, unless she is deeply in love or had just experienced a soul-stirring passion she would die for.[^32]

> 
> At this moment, they would feel their spirit float into the air, arriving at their mirror self’s side, watching as their form turns into a stone, seemingly becoming a statue.
> 
> Their mirror self are also undergoing the corresponding changes, just much more slowly, much more gradually.

### Ritual Effect

> When the [Mirror Person](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mirror_People "Mirror People") was also severely petrified, their spirit would float up again, returning to their body.[^28] [^29]
> 
> By now, their body had adapted to the potion and, under the returning consciousness's drive, gradually shed its petrified state.
> 
> Their mirror self would also have a transformation, as for what it is, that would depend on the method used.
> 
> > When doing reconciliation, they can see their mirror self changing, transforming toward femininity (if they were originally men), and not far away, one after another version of themself emerged from the darkness-them when they had just become a Demoness of [Despair](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Demoness_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_4:_Despair_\(Demoness_of_Despair\) "Demoness Pathway/Abilities"), as well as them during their previous states in their life.
> 
> Every time they look in a mirror, it leaves certain traces in the mirror world, and now they all appear.

### Acting Method

- Maintain an unaging mentality over the erosion of time, over hundreds or thousands of years.[^31]

### Potion Properties

> As if the pre-storm sky was bottled whole.[^1]

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> Transparent crystal ball composed of various disaster scenes.[^1]

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

Or

- **A [Catastrophe](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Demoness_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_2:_Catastrophe_\(Demoness_of_Catastrophe\) "Demoness Pathway/Abilities")** [Beyonder Characteristic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristic "Beyonder Characteristic").

Supplementary Ingredients

- 30 grams of soil from fertile land.[^1]
- 100 milliliters of rainwater from the first annual flood.[^1]
- One [Mirror World Fragment](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mirror_World_Fragment "Mirror World Fragment") (not from [Special Mirror Worlds](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Special_Mirror_Worlds "Special Mirror Worlds")).[^1]
- 10,000 curses from dead victims of disaster (Higher [Sequence](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sequence "Sequence") targets require fewer).[^1]

### Advancement Ritual

As a participant, cause a disaster affecting the entire continent, and advance during the disaster.[^3]

1. Advance at the universally acknowledged end of an epoch or the beginning of the next.
2. Advance during the signs and process of the apocalypse's arrival.
3. Advance when one's own involvement leads to the fall of a deity.

## Sequence 0: Demoness (Chaos Demoness, Primordial Demoness)

![Potion - Demoness Sequence 0 - Demoness](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/lord-of-the-mystery/images/e/e8/Potion_-_Demoness_Sequence_0_-_Demoness.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/200?cb=20260330035432)

Potion - Demoness Sequence 0 - Demoness

- Overview
- Formula
- Ritual
- Backlash
- Acting Method

### Potion Properties

> An exquisitely charming female human skin.[^1]

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- The.
- Three **[Apocalypse](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Demoness_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_1:_Apocalypse_\(Demoness_of_Apocalypse\) "Demoness Pathway/Abilities")** [Beyonder Characteristics](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristics "Beyonder Characteristics").

Supplementary Ingredients

- One portion of genuine love originating from oneself (This is illusory, it must be transformed into something between real and illusory before being used).[^1]
- Half of the [Mirror World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mirror_World "Mirror World").[^1]

### Advancement Ritual

- **Altered Ritual:** Descend into the depths of the earth, find the [Chaos Sea](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chaos_Sea "Chaos Sea"), endure a degree of its corruption while maintaining oneself—neither falling nor transforming.[^35]
- This ritual shown in the [Second Blasphemy Slate](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Second_Blasphemy_Slate "Second Blasphemy Slate") was influenced by the [Primordial God Almighty](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Primordial_God_Almighty "Primordial God Almighty") to be beneficial to "Him" as a resurrection plan.
- **Alternative Ritual:** Find a way to borrow the special traits from the Sequence 1s of five [Pathways](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways") related to the [Chaos Sea](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chaos_Sea "Chaos Sea"), combine them into a power close to the [Chaos Sea](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chaos_Sea "Chaos Sea"), and control it.[^1]
	- This ritual was not stated in the [Second Blasphemy Slate](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Second_Blasphemy_Slate "Second Blasphemy Slate").
