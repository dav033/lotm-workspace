## Sequence 4: Mysticologist

![Potion - Hermit Sequence 4 - Mysticologist](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/lord-of-the-mystery/images/e/e1/Potion_-_Hermit_Sequence_4_-_Mysticologist.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/200?cb=20260330043541)

Potion - Hermit Sequence 4 - Mysticologist

- Overview
- Formula
- Ritual
- Backlash
- Acting Method

### Potion Properties

> "A phantom, dark liquid from which purple sparks are constantly being ignited deep within" [^1]

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

- A vague, gem-like orb emanating a gloomy aura, as if a gaze peered out from within to scrutinize everything.[^3]
- An illusory gemstone shaped like an eyeball.
- Its radiance subdues and exudes a murky aura, as though a gaze emanates from within to scrutinise all things.[^1]

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- 1 Scalp of an Arcane Spirit.[^1]
- 1 Eye of Evil God's Pupil.[^1]

Or

- **A [Mysticologist](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hermit_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_4:_Mysticologist)** [Beyonder Characteristic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristic "Beyonder Characteristic").

Supplementary Ingredients

- 13 drops of an Arcane Spirit's Tears.[^1]
- 80ml from an Eye of Evil God's Ash Liquid.[^1]
- 1 relic related to a mystical event.[^1]
- 1 Warlock potion created by the concoctor (any effect is acceptable).[^1]

### Advancement Ritual

Completely analyze a drop of blood from a [Mythical Creature](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mythical_Creature_Form "Mythical Creature Form") and obtain complicated and massive amounts of knowledge.[^6]

- Note 1: Due to the differing types of Mythical Creature blood employed, should they succeed, their specialisation within the 'Mysticologist' stage will also exhibit certain variations.[^1]
- Note 2: Although the advancement ritual of a Mysticologist doesn't have any requirements on the Mythical Creature's pathway, the best choice is still the [Wheel of Fortune Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Wheel_of_Fortune_Pathway).[^7]
- Note 3: The blood of an Angel-level creature that is one of the main materials for a Sequence 2 angel would work, but the effect is not as good as a complete one.[^8]
- Note 4: The blood of a [Boon](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Boon "Boon") -recceiving Angel would also work, as long as "Their" physical state has changed.[^8]

### Ritual Essence

— No data —

— No data —

### Acting Method

- Search for mysticism knowledge, the closer to the truth the more beneficial.[^4] [^5]

## Sequence 2: Sage

![Potion - Hermit Sequence 2 - Sage](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/lord-of-the-mystery/images/5/59/Potion_-_Hermit_Sequence_2_-_Sage.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/200?cb=20260330043609)

Potion - Hermit Sequence 2 - Sage

- Overview
- Formula
- Ritual
- Backlash
- Acting Method

### Potion Properties

> A bubbling concoction where each bubble contains a transparent eye.[^9]

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

- "Crystals" formed from seemingly transparent information.[^10]
- A fist-sized diamond shimmering with sacred and wise radiance, each facet seeming to reflect indifferent, merciless eyes.[^1]

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- Crystal of an Ancient Fantasy Demon.[^1]
- Tentacle of the Mind Wanderer.[^1]

Or

- **A [Sage](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hermit_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_2:_Sage)** [Beyonder Characteristic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristic "Beyonder Characteristic").

Supplementary Ingredients

- 130ml blood from a Fantasy Ancient Devil (can be replaced by a Sage's blood).[^1]
- 9 Mind Wanderer's light spots (Can be replaced by a Sage's information torrent's remaining light spots).[^1]
- 1 widely circulated ballad praising the concoctor's own deeds.[^1]
- 1 item gathering tens of thousands of sincere blessings.[^1]

### Advancement Ritual

Prevent a disaster that involves a higher level of power.[^11]

### Ritual Essence

- The essence of a ritual is to establish a connection with the spiritual information generated by the act.[^1]
- This information has a distinct imprint and is exceptionally solid due to its high level of involvement. It will not be dispersed by other information in a short period of time, helping the ritual performer to initially stabilize their own consciousness and gradually gather the scattered and diluted torrent of their original spiritual information.[^1]

### Consumption Backlash

> As the potion enters their mouth, their body would turn illusory at a discernible pace.[^9]
> 
> They would break down into thick and complicated knowledge, changing into an existence that was a flux of information.
> 
> The surrounding area would lose their sense of reality, it was as though they had been restored to the most fundamental blocks of information.
> 
> For most of Sequence 3 [Beyonders](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonders "Beyonders") of the [Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways"), such a state is extremely dangerous. If one’s willpower isn’t strong enough, their luck isn’t good enough, and they aren’t prepared enough, the knowledge that they transform into would be infiltrated by all kinds of information within seconds. They would be washed away, assimilated, and thus quickly lose consciousness. They won’t be able to reassemble their bodies, turning into a very strange and difficult monster to deal with in mysticism. A [Knowledge Demon](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Creatures_and_Plants#Knowledge_Demon "Creatures and Plants"), it was also known as an Information Creature.
> 
> > [Bernadette](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Bernadette_Gustav "Bernadette Gustav") had relied on the [Sage Frontlet](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sage_Frontlet "Sage Frontlet") and had previously transformed into a flux of information on several occasions. Although it was limited to two to three seconds and didn’t last too long, it was still considered experience.

### Ritual Effect

> At that moment, they would try their best to maintain their consciousness and establish a connection with the information produced in the spirit world by preventing a high-level disaster. This information has a distinct imprint and is exceptionally solid due to its high level of involvement. It will not be dispersed by other information in a short period of time, helping the ritual performer to initially stabilize their own consciousness and gradually gather the scattered and diluted torrent of their original spiritual information.
> 
> The information has a clear imprint belonging to them, and it involves a very high level of power. It is exceptionally “solid” and couldn’t be dispersed by other information for short periods of time. It would help them to stabilize their consciousness and slowly gather the dissipating flux of information around their body.
> 
> Time would tick by. There may be several times when they waver on the border of losing consciousness, but in the end, they have to manage to tide through it. They would gather all the information that belonged to them and begin to reconstruct their body.
> 
> At that moment, they would gradually feel their anchors. This would stabilise their condition better, allowing them to resist the ancient will that is slowly developing in their body.
> 
> > For [Bernadette](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Bernadette_Gustav "Bernadette Gustav"), it was the concern Admiral of Stars [Cattleya](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Cattleya "Cattleya") had for her, members of the [Element Dawn](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Element_Dawn "Element Dawn") and her crew making their daily prayers.

### Interference from the Hidden Sage to Bernadette

> At this moment, a series of secret information came from nowhere. Taking the opportunity while [Bernadette](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Bernadette_Gustav "Bernadette Gustav") was reforming her body, it attempted to fuse with her.
> 
> As an embodiment of this world’s knowledge and information, as a quasi-Sequence 0 existence of the [Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways"), the [Hidden Sage](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hidden_Sage "Hidden Sage") had a certain influence on [Beyonders](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonders "Beyonders") of a lower Sequencer than “Him.”
> 
> Without giving [Bernadette](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Bernadette_Gustav "Bernadette Gustav") the chance to use the [pale-white mask](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pale_Death "Pale Death"), the “curtain” draped over [Klein](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") gently rose.
> 
> [Klein](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") distorts the space-time around [Bernadette](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Bernadette_Gustav "Bernadette Gustav"), completely isolating her from the outside world. Even information couldn’t be interchanged.

### Finalising

> They would have to reassemble their body using their own [anchors](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Anchor "Anchor") to balance out the terrifying will that is surfacing on their body.
> 
> At that moment, they would truly become a “Them”, a Sequence 2 angel of the pathway—an important figure in the mysterious world that could be addressed as a secret existence.
