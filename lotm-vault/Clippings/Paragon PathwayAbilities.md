## Sequence 9: Savant

**New Abilities** [^1] [^2] [^3] [^4]

- **Knowledge** (**Mysticism and Mechanics**): **Savants** believe that knowledge is power and through drinking the potion, they gain new information about mysticism and mechanics.
	- They have a rough understanding of the mysticism, but are more knowledgeable about the formation and use of aqua regia, nitroglycerin, and other complicated bolts and gears.
		- To an average person, they appear to know everything.
- **Recall** (**Memory**): This is the core ability of a **Savant**. With it, they can recall every single piece of knowledge and experience they have ever seen.
	- At the same time, they also possess increased learning and comprehension skills.

## Sequence 8: Archaeologist

**Traits** [^1] [^2] [^3] [^4]

- **Physical Enhancements**: **Archaeologists** possess a strong enough physique.
- **Spirituality**: **Archaeologists** will barely be able to apply some [Ritualistic Magic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ritualistic_Magic "Ritualistic Magic").

**New Abilities**

- **Adaption**: They have the ability to face many problems.

**Strengthened Abilities**

- **Knowledge** (**History, Survival, Mysticism**): **Archaeologists** possess a good deal of knowledge about history and outdoor survival, as well as knowledge of taboos and mysticism about ruins.

## Sequence 7: Appraiser

**New Abilities** [^1] [^2] [^3] [^4]

- **Appraisal**: **Appraisers** are able to use this ability to rapid identify the powers and problems of most **Beyonder** items without **Divination** or reasoning.
	- This allows them to use those items while keeping the danger to the minimum.
		- If the target item contains a higher status than their **Sequence**, or they lack certain knowledge of by **Recall**, their **Appraisal** will be incomplete or misunderstood.
		- The **Appraiser** also has the ability to **Appraise** other things as well.[^5]

## Sequence 6: Artisan (Machinery Specialist)

**Traits** [^3]

- **Spirituality**: Only the level of their [Ritualistic Magic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ritualistic_Magic "Ritualistic Magic") will be enhanced.

**New Abilities** [^1] [^3] [^4]

- **Manufacturing**: Whether in reality or in the realm of the mystical, **Artisans** are undisputed masters of creation, being able to make **Beyonder** items and mechanical artifacts; however, those **Beyonder** items and mechanical artifacts will not be particularly powerful.
	- With **Manufacturing** as the "hammer" and **Spirituality** as the "fuel", **Artisans** can reshape raw [Beyonder Characteristics](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristics "Beyonder Characteristics") into completely different materials and shapes through the use of forging, assembling, inlaying, and other procedures.[^6]
		- There are still limitations in regards to the form of the [Beyonder Characteristics](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristics "Beyonder Characteristics") used as raw materials, which is based off the [Sequence](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sequence "Sequence") of the [Beyonder Characteristics](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristics "Beyonder Characteristics") itself.
		- Materials that are "symbolic" of a [Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways") can grant extra benefit from that symbolism, but this depends entirely on the specific material being used and not all materials that seem symbolic actually work this way or provide any benefit.[^6]
		- By using **Beyonder Characteristics** or the main ingredients for potions, **Artisans** are able to craft [Sealed Artifacts](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sealed_Artifacts "Sealed Artifacts") which have more powerful effects than normal while also possessing less negative and severe side effects than naturally formed or created [Sealed Artifacts](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sealed_Artifacts "Sealed Artifacts").
		- If a **Beyonder** item is made into a mechanical part, it takes effect on the entire construct, so multiple [Pathways](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways") **Beyonder** abilities can appear in a single mechanical creation.[^7]
			- This is so long as the balance is maintained without intense conflict.
				- The negative effects of the corresponding **Sealed Artifacts** depend on both the **Sequence** level of the **Artisan** and the [Sealed Artifacts'](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sealed_Artifacts "Sealed Artifacts") uniqueness themselves.[^8]
			- The abilities of their created **Sealed Artifacts** aren't necessarily weaker than a **Beyonder** of the same **Sequence**, and can even be stronger, but that is often a downside.[^9]
				- This means the stronger ability is the downside.
		- By imbuing items with **Spirituality**, they can produce enchanted **Beyonder** weapons such as axes, guns and bullets, cannons and cannonballs, bombs, etc. or worn [Mystical Items](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mystical_Item "Mystical Item") such as belts, brooches, rings, etc. with **Beyonder** effects but have a limited lifespan.[^10]
		- As such, they can directly fix the summoning rituals of [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World") creatures like **Messengers** into an item to make it more convenient.[^11]
		- Due to an **Artisan's** ability to know all the combinations of different **Beyonder** effects and negative effects while also not lacking [Mystical Items](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mystical_Item "Mystical Item"), their strength can reach the level of a **Sequence 5**.[^12]

## Sequence 5: Astronomer (Astromancer)

**New Abilities** [^4] [^4]

- **Astronomy**: If **Astronomers** have a preliminary understanding of the nature of different **Astronomical** phenomena, they can make use of them to a certain extent.
	- When there is a **Solar Storm**, they can guide the energy and matter that has arrived within the [Astral Barrier](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Original_Creator "Original Creator") and is within their own capability to concentrate to a certain extent.
		- This can be used to interfere with or attack the target.
		- When there is a **Meteor Shower**, they can guide the fire rain with a certain weight.
		- The strength of the capabilities is within the scope of a **Sequence 5 Beyonder**.
		- Of course, the universe is dangerous, and thus this power is dangerous.
		- If they are not careful, they will encounter all sorts of terrifying and bizarre things.
			- For example, when studying the **Blood Moon** phenomenon and trying to utilize it, they may find that they are inexplicably pregnant or are no longer themselves.
- **False Constellations**: **Astronomers** can create a fake **Starry** **Sky** in order to confuse or interfere with the astrological predictions made by the affected targets.
	- It can also be used to simulate astronomical phenomena, such as solar storms or meteor showers, provided the they mastered the essence of the corresponding phenomenon.[^13]
		- By utilizing **False Constellations**, they can reproduce similar effects to **Astronomy**, though these are weaker than those generated by genuine astronomical events.[^13]
		- The changes in constellations caused by this can also cause corresponding phenomena within a *100 meter range*, such as tides or electromagnetic disturbances.
- **Star of Curses**: They are able to fire rays formed from condensed **Starlight** that can cause various effects such as weakness, mutation, and trauma.
- **Blessing of Stars**: **Astronomers** are able to attract **Starlight**, altering the **Gravity** acting upon a targeted area or corresponding target.

## Sequence 4: Alchemist

**Description**

**Mechanical Heaven** is not only a description of the **Divine Kingdom** of a **Paragon** but also an acknowledgment of the **Alchemists'** usual performance.

**New Abilities** [^4]

- **Alchemy**: The core ability of an **Alchemist**.
	- An **Alchemist** can infuse the items they create with [Souls](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul"), giving them a certain **Life**, but its **Alchemical** life is not as good as that of the **[Ancient Alchemist](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_4:_Ancient_Alchemist_\(Classical_Alchemist,_Human_Alchemist\))**.
		- The [Souls](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul") and **Life** force are not created out of thin air. They come from fragments of remnant souls in the [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World") and the extraction of life from the surrounding environment.
				- They can create a machine that looks indistinguishable from humans via injecting it with a [Soul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul"), giving it the ability to move, talk, and behave just like a human.[^14] [^15]
				- The specialty of an **[Ancient Alchemist](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_4:_Ancient_Alchemist_\(Classical_Alchemist,_Human_Alchemist\))** can just barely be emulated to create different golems and human beings using incomplete spirits and various metals.[^16] [^17]
				- They can create spirituality gems out of humans to power mechanical prosthetics.[^18]
		- They can quickly create various temporary dolls and objects using readily available materials, achieving different effects which depends on the environment and materials.
		- They can also prepare long-term magical dolls with more powerful abilities in advance.
				- The dolls created by an **Alchemist** can disguise themselves as humans.[^19]
				- This is a tribute/reference to [Fullmetal Alchemist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fullmetal_Alchemist).
		- If they have high **Sequence** materials, they can even create things that can fight against [Angels](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Angels "Angels").

**Strengthened Abilities**

- **Manufacturing**: Beyond combat, **Alchemists** play a wider role in daily life.
	- They can make a mechanical body that has powerful mechanical tools installed inside their bodies such as a cannon, a device that allows **Flight**, and functional cybernetic eyes.[^20] [^21] [^18]
		- [Claude](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Claude "Claude") modified his body, becoming a mechanical being with height of over three meters. One of his cybernetic eyes resembled an emerald, while the other resembled a ruby, supported by numerous components. His temples were encased in a transparent special material, revealing the squirming grayish-white brain within.[^18]
			- This made him able to perform any task just like a normal person, including combat. However, since his Sequence isn't high enough, he wasn't able to replace the human brain.[^18]
						- He was also able to create a **Spirituality Gems** using human lives as power sources.[^18]
		- They are able to create an unusual circular disk covered in complex patterns with a mirror-like luster that is able to track down entities related to the items placed on it.[^22]
		- This can even apply on items that are actually **Historical Projections**.
			- Using the disk was something he had made just two days ago, [Jack Walton](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Jack_Walton "Jack Walton") put the Gustav Family Crest (that was a **Historical Projection**) on this device which served as a medium to point to [Louis Gustav](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Louis_Gustav "Louis Gustav").

Mythical Creature Form

- As **Demigods** at **Sequence 4** who have obtained partial **Godhood**, they are able to manifest their incomplete **Mythical Creature** **Form** if they disregard any of the consequences.

## Sequence 3: Arcane Scholar (Demon of Arcana)

**New Abilities** [^4]

- **Knowledge Spirit**: They can transform into a certain amount of knowledge within a certain time limit (ranging from *5 minutes to* *days* depending on the knowledge they turn into).
	- In other words, when a person or a **Beyonder** opens a book and reads the text or learns knowledge, that **Information** may be the manifestation of a **Knowledge Spirit**.
		- The **Knowledge Spirit** uses this to enter the target's body of mind, subtly influencing their thinking and intelligence, completing the process of erosion and even **Corruption**.
				- It can also be active in the [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World"), giving certain misleading information or **Corruption** to those who obtain **Information** from the **Spirit World**.
- **Environment Controller**: An **Arcane Scholar** is able to accurately extract information about the surrounding environment and deduce what happened from it.
	- Though they cannot fully reconstruct the scene, they can confirm the battle that occurred in a certain area, the **Sequence** level involved, and the residual aura.
		- [Horamick Haydn](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Horamick_Haydn "Horamick Haydn") was able to instantly perceive upon his arrival that the battle that had taken place was at the [Saint](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Saints "Saints") Level, and also the residual aura created by the [Box of the Great Old Ones](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Box_of_the_Great_Old_Ones "Box of the Great Old Ones").[^21]
		- Regardless of the environment, an **Arcane Scholar** can effectively use the environment to enhance themselves and weaken the target.
- **Absorption**: They can extract **Life** force from organisms within a *five-kilometer radius*.

**Authorities** [^4]

- **Dissociation**: They can emit a beam of light capable of destroying the structures of most objects. This application is derived from the **Analysis** and **Principles** authorities.
	- Targets that are affected will be decomposed into fragments that fall to the ground, with even their color evaporating and dissipating quickly.[^23] [^24]
		- [Horamick](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Horamick_Haydn "Horamick Haydn") pressed his extended palms against the wall. Without a sound, the majestic mural **Disintegrated**, turning into pieces of stone fragments that landed on the ground. Even the color quickly evaporated, disappearing without a trace.[^20]
				- Using the [Face of the Arcane](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Face_of_the_Arcane "Face of the Arcane"), [Franca](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Franca_Roland "Franca Roland") shot out from her eye a clear, misty light, which caused the invisible barrier to instantly reduce to its most basic, minute components and ceased to exist. Immediately after, the clear, misty light hit the bone- like figurine of the [Primordial Demoness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Primordial_Demoness "Primordial Demoness"), causing the figurine to begin to crumble.[^25]

**Strengthened Abilities** [^23] [^24]

- Their previous **Sequence** capabilities have been improved.
	- **Manufacturing**: **Arcane Scholars** possess extraordinary **Manufacturing** abilities, being able to create mystical items, complex mechanical devices, or combine the two together.
		- They can **Manufacture** giant robots.[^26]
		- **Appraisal**: **Arcane Scholars** are able to quickly grasp the relevant information when using [Mystical Items](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mystical_Item "Mystical Item"), effectively avoiding its negative effects completely.

**Mythical Creature Form**

- As a **Sequence 3 Beyonder**, they can respond to prayers within a certain range.

## Sequence 2: Knowledge Magister (Master)

TRANSLATION NOTE

In the original Chinese text, 规律 (guīlǜ) carries multiple meanings and in this context, can be translated as " **Laws** ", as is done in the English WebNovel translation. However, as the term " **Law** " is already used for a similar word, 律法 (lǜfǎ), and both are treated as distinct authorities, this page will consistently use a single English term (**Principles**) to represent it for clarity and distinction.
- **Laws** (律法, *lǜfǎ*):
	- Refers to codified, enforceable laws—rules established by authority, often carrying judicial or binding force. It aligns with legal systems, decrees, and formal structures of governance.
- **Principles** (规律 *guīlǜ*):
	- Refers to inherent patterns or natural laws—the underlying order of reality that exists independently of authority. It is closer to universal principles or the fundamental workings of the world.
		- In other contexts this term can also denote simpler patterns or regularity, not a strict law, but rather an observable consistency or recurring behavior over time such as everyday routines or behavioral patterns.

**Authorities** [^4]

- **Manufacturing**: "They" possess a weakened authority over **Manufacturing**.
	- "They" are able to create most of the items "They" imagine, even those related to the supernatural.
		- "They" can create futuristic wonders beyond the scope of current world-era technologies such as nuclear fusion-based technology, giant mechanical robots (such as the ones in [Gundam](https://pt.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gundam)), and interstellar travel starships with just a simple gesture.[^27] [^28]
		- "They" can also can have temporary substitutes in the absence of materials.
- **Principles**: "They" wield a certain degree of authority over **Principles**.[^29]
	- "They" can alter the physical **Principles** to a certain extent, making combustion not necessarily produce flames, frost bringing warmth, and even the earth no longer bearing weight.
		- "They" can weaken **Gravity**, causing nearby items to float even in the absence of **Wind**.[^30]
- **Analysis**: "They" have an enhanced **Analysis** authority.
	- "They" can see the essence of [Angels](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Angels "Angels") and formulate tactics accordingly.
		- [Bornova Gustav](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Bornova_Gustav "Bornova Gustav") saw an invisible vortex wearing a silk top hat and a black trench coat in essence. Inside the vortex, there were transparent and distorted maggots squirming about in the cluster when [Gehrman Sparrow](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") appeared before "Him".[^30]
- **Mentor**: This is derived from the authority of **Education**.
	- "They" possess the ability to **Test** others and are highly skilled at teaching others.
- **Learning**: "They" have a weakened **Learning** authority.
	- **Learning** something after just one look is no longer a myth.

**Strengthened Abilities**

- "Their" previous **Sequence** capabilities have been improved.

**Mythical Creature Form**

- "They" have become a full **Mythical Creature**. "Their" full **Mythical creature Form** is an **Informational** being that is composed of numerous depictions of civilizations, though still unable to manifest a complete depiction of **Civilization**.
	- "They" are able to transform into a substantial amount of pale-white fog to move around, which can contort and writhe later to rebuild "Their" body.
		- This is essentially a form of **Information** reconstruction; however, since the **Paragon** is [Steam](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/God_of_Steam_and_Machinery "God of Steam and Machinery"), this manifests as transforming into steam.[^31]

## Sequence 1: Illuminator (Civilization Enlightener, Light of Darkness)

**Authorities** [^4]

- **Civilization**: "They" have a certain degree of authority over **Civilization**.
	- "They" can allow **Chaos** to settle into a sliver of **Order**, allowing **Destruction** to give rise to a faint new **Life**, allowing **Darkness** to burst forth with the first **Light**.
		- "They" can rely on the weight of a **Civilization** in order to bind, impact, and crush a target.
		- "They" can reveal picture-like projections that record the vast and magnificent history of humanity and the development of **Civilization** to protect against attacks.[^32] [^33]
			- This is especially useful to contain the **Knowledge Injection** of a **[Knowledge Emperor](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hermit_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_1:_Knowledge_Emperor)** and can block them from passing through even in the state of **Informatization**.
		- "They" are able to prevent targets from correctly using **Civilization** technology.[^33]
		- **Illuminators** can even use **Civilization** to realize certain **Civilization** effects.[^33]
		- This is almost similar to **Miracles**, because if something is a core element of **Civilization**, "They" can do the corresponding **Civilization** effects such as:
			- Clearing certain sources of **Corruption**, which embodies the influence of **Civilization** in public health and sanitation, such as sewer systems.
						- Rebuilding a damaged city at extremely fast speed, because **Civilization** is embodied in the field of infrastructure construction.
						- Rapidly transport materials or personnel, or impede it because **Civilization** is also part of the transportation industry.
- **Manufacturing**: "Their" **Manufacturing** authority has been enhanced.
	- Since artistic and cultural expressions—like music, painting, literature, and other art forms—are components of **Civilization**, "They" can manifest "Them" with **Mechanical Creations**, while non- **Mechanical** **Creations** can exhibit certain **Beyonder** properties.[^34]
		- **Illuminators** can use this to compose music that directly influences the audience's emotions, or create paintings that can psychologically affect, inspire, attract, or even draw the viewer into the artwork.
- **Education**: "They" have a certain degree of authority over **Education**.
	- "They" can enhance the intelligence of a target, provide enlightenment and inspiration, trap the enemy in a mathematical maze, or achieve similar effects.

**Strengthened Abilities**

- "Their" previous **Sequence** capability have been improved.

**Mythical Creature Form**

- "Their" **Mythical Creature** now is capable of manifesting a complete depiction of **Civilization**.

## Sequence 0: Paragon

**Symbolism** [^35] [^4]

- **Knowledge** (**Information**): Derived from this symbolism, "They" hold the portion of authority that leans toward **Reality**.
	- This symbolism allows the [Gods](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Gods "Gods") of the two subordinate [Pathways](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways"), or even the **Sequence 1** or **Sequence 2**, to transform into a vast and complex torrent of **Information**, an illusory, overwhelming, and terrifying flood of **Information**.

**Authorities**

- **Reality Knowledge**: A **Paragon** possesses all the **Knowledge** that has been discovered in the **Real** world and is able to modify it to a certain extent.
	- This authority takes effect immediately at the continental level, rapidly at the planetary level and gradually at the star system level, but is ineffective outside of these levels.
		- The **Paragon** is able to cause a target to temporarily lose the corresponding **Knowledge** and be rejected by **Knowledge** or have **Knowledge** controlled by a target turn into a lurking agent or **Corrupter** beneficial to "Him".
				- "They" can make videos, photos, novels, or messages collapse into pure snow-like static, effectively destroying the **Information** structure it holds, which will also rapidly spread to form a huge **Information Vortex**.
			- Not only is this an effective way to interfere with general **Information**, it also can be used to destroy the origin of those **Information** or deal with the **Information** abilities of the [Hermit Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hermit_Pathway).
- **Civilization**: The rise and fall of **Civilizations**, the weight of **Civilizations**, and the vast **Knowledge** that has been accumulated by **Civilizations**.
	- "They" can designate a group, a country, a continent, or even a planet, keeping them in a state of barbarism and ignorance forever, or rapidly iterate and evolve the **Civilization**.
		- "They" can turn others into **Knowledge**, **Information**, and words, and imprint them into an illusory book, trapping those things which "They" can use for many different matters.[^36]
- **Principles**: "They" have partial authority over **Principles**, which leans toward **Reality**.
	- Compared to "Their" ability to change **Principles** of physics versus the **[Justiciar](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Justiciar_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Justiciar) '** s ability to change the underlying **Rules**, the **Justiciar** is stronger as it also effects mystical **Rules**.[^37]
		- **Principles** are a part of **Knowledge** and thus by mastering the **Principles**, a **Paragon** can make the world operate in a way that is more favorable to "Themselves".
		- "They" can release a blurry light that seeps out from the void and envelopes the surroundings, which has the effect of restoring the most basic structure of various objects.[^38]
- **Education**: "They" have partial authority to grant **Knowledge** to others.
	- Unlike the **[Hermit](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hermit_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Hermit)**, the **Paragon's** authority over **Education** places more emphasis on enlightenment, guidance, testing, and systematization.
- "They" have the subordinate authorities of **Learning**, **Manufacturing** and **Analysis**.
	- **Learning**: The authority of **Learning** can make the **Paragon** learn something easily, or make it impossible for others to **Learn** it no matter what.
		- **Manufacturing**: **Knowledge** combines in order to create miracles, almost divine things as well as things that are beyond the ordinary.
		- "They" can make advanced programs and effects that carry within it "Their" **Beyonder** powers and authorities that can then be bestowed onto everyday electronic devices like phones.
			- [Franca](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Franca_Roland "Franca Roland") used the **Information Shredder** to destroy all image of a painting created by [Luo Shan](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Luo_Shan "Luo Shan"). This in the end resulted in the destruction of the original painting.[^39]
						- With various help and assistance, the **Information Shredder** created by the [God of Steam and Machinery](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/God_of_Steam_and_Machinery "God of Steam and Machinery") was used to kill off the [Hidden Sage](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hidden_Sage "Hidden Sage").
				- The [Mystical Items](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mystical_Item "Mystical Item") that "They" create without containing characteristics will still naturally fade over time—some last a few months, others a few years.[^40]
				- "They" are able to create an object that lets "Them" temporarily wield a [Uniqueness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniquenesses "Uniquenesses").[^41]
		- **Analysis**: The **Paragon** can quickly **Analyze** or even directly see the **Essence** of things, and then make use of them.
		- The **Analysis** authority heavily counters the **[Error](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Error)**, which relies on **Deceit**, or the **[Visionary](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Visionary_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Visionary)**, which relies on **Envisioning** false things.[^42]
- **Spirit World Mentor**: The [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World") is a place where all kinds of **Information** converge, and **Information** is **Knowledge**.
	- It is naturally influenced by **Knowledge** [Pathways](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways"). Therefore, both the **[Hermit](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hermit_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Hermit)** and the **Paragon** have partial authority over the [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World").
- **Divine Kingdom**: **Mechanical Heaven** or **Civilization's Cemetery**, **Land of Perfection**.
