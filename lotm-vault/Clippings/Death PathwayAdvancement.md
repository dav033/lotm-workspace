## Sequence 4: Undying

![Potion - Death Sequence 4 - Undying](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/lord-of-the-mystery/images/8/81/Potion_-_Death_Sequence_4_-_Undying.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/200?cb=20260330033806)

Potion - Death Sequence 4 - Undying

- Overview
- Formula
- Ritual
- Backlash
- Acting Method

### Potion Properties

> A pale potion that remains perfectly still—no ripples, even when exposed to wind or shaken.[^2]

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> Hollow, blackened, and greenish serpent skin.[^2]

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- Gallbladder of a Decaying Feathered Serpent.[^2]
- Molt of the Immortal Cicada.[^2]

Or

- **A [Undying](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_4:_Undying)** [Beyonder Characteristic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristic "Beyonder Characteristic").

Supplementary Ingredients

- 90 milliliters of bodily fluid from the Decaying Feathered Serpent.[^2]
- 9 pieces of bark from the tree where the Immortal Cicada once resided.[^2]
- One Eternal Darkness Stone.[^2]
- One Pale Red Spider Lily.[^2]

### Advancement Ritual

Locate an underground river tainted by death. Hold a genuine funeral by its banks, then bury yourself and the potion there for sixty days. If you fail to survive or retain sufficient sanity after sixty days, the ritual fails.[^4]

- **Note 1:** Failure invariably results in the birth of a dangerous [Corpse King](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Western_Continent/Glossary#Corpse_King "Western Continent/Glossary").
- **Note 2:** Many [Western Continent](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Western_Continent "Western Continent") cultivators misunderstand this ritual's true purpose. Lacking Beyonder characteristics, they instead focus on the "failure" outcome, believing it to be the true success—a chance to refine terrifying [Jiangshi](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Western_Continent/Glossary#Corpse_King "Western Continent/Glossary").
- **Note 3:** Some cultivators have modified this ritual, creating Xian Ascension Techniques akin to **[Supreme Yin Body Refinement](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Western_Continent/Glossary#Supreme_Yin_Body_Refinement "Western Continent/Glossary")**. The "Supreme Yin" essence represents the moon's purity, the water's brilliance, the far north's intent, winter's zenith, and the duality of yin and yang. The flaws and root causes of this method's issues… well, I don't need to elaborate further, do I?

## Sequence 3: Ferryman

![Potion - Death Sequence 3 - Ferryman](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/lord-of-the-mystery/images/7/75/Potion_-_Death_Sequence_3_-_Ferryman.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/200?cb=20260330033818)

Potion - Death Sequence 3 - Ferryman

- Overview
- Formula
- Ritual
- Backlash
- Acting Method

### Potion Properties

> Dark and deep; even falling dust sinks swiftly into the liquid and vanishes.[^2]

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> A core-like object stained with pale yellow grease, containing a dim, pus-like essence.[^2]

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- Pus Core of a Styx Servant.[^2]
Or

- The brain of an adult Feathered Serpent.[^2]

Or

- **A [Ferryman](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_3:_Ferryman)** [Beyonder Characteristic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristic "Beyonder Characteristic").

Supplementary Ingredients

- 90 milliliters of one's own blood.[^2]
- Choose one of:[^2]
- 9 drops of pus from a Styx Servant
	- 9 feathers from an adult Feathered Serpent
- One Undead from the depths of the [Underworld](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Underworld "Underworld") (both Styx Servants and Undead from the depths of the Underworld can be summoned via a personal **Door to the Underworld**).[^2]
- A flower blooming from one's own decayed flesh.[^2]

### Advancement Ritual

Step through a self-summoned **Door to the Underworld**, ingest the potion, and die within.[^4]

- **Note 1:** From then on, the **Ferryman** becomes part of the [Underworld](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Underworld "Underworld") and death itself.
- **Note 2:** Before the [Phoenix Ancestor](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Phoenix_Ancestor "Phoenix Ancestor") established the Underworld and before the [First Blasphemy Slate](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/First_Blasphemy_Slate "First Blasphemy Slate") appeared, this ritual didn't exist.
- **Note 3:** In the [Western Continent](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Western_Continent "Western Continent"), whether it's the [Haoli Land](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Western_Continent/Glossary#Haoli "Western Continent/Glossary"), the [Yellow Springs](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Western_Continent/Glossary#Yellow_Springs "Western Continent/Glossary"), or the [Netherworld](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Western_Continent/Glossary#Netherworld "Western Continent/Glossary"), each serves the same effect as the Underworld.

## Sequence 2: Death Consul

![Potion - Death Sequence 2 - Death Consul](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/lord-of-the-mystery/images/6/69/Potion_-_Death_Sequence_2_-_Death_Consul.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/200?cb=20260330033831)

Potion - Death Sequence 2 - Death Consul

- Overview
- Formula
- Ritual
- Backlash
- Acting Method

### Potion Properties

> A deep black liquid formed from the condensed aura of death, radiating an overwhelming presence, with countless numb faces protruding from it.[^2]

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> A crown made of black bones.[^2]

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- Skull of an Ancient Feathered Serpent.[^2]

Or

- **A [Death Consul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_2:_Death_Consul)** [Beyonder Characteristic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristic "Beyonder Characteristic").

Supplementary Ingredients

- 90 milliliters of fluid from an Ancient Feathered Serpent.[^2]
- A coffin that once held one's own body.[^2]
- A crown that once symbolized royal authority.[^2]
- A pale object from the depths of the [Underworld](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Underworld "Underworld").[^2]

### Advancement Ritual

Become the ruler of one layer of the [Underworld](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Underworld "Underworld") or transform an entire nation into a land of the dead, then anchor the **Door to the Underworld** within it.[^4]

- **Note 1:** For descendants acknowledged by the [God of Death](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Salinger "Salinger"), the first method is straightforward.
- **Note 2:** **Death Consuls** hold authority to judge the dead. In the [Western Continent](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Western_Continent "Western Continent"), figures like the **[Ten Yama Kings](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Western_Continent/Glossary#Ten_Yama_Kings "Western Continent/Glossary")** and **[Five Ghost Emperors](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Western_Continent/Glossary#Five_Ghost_Emperors "Western Continent/Glossary")** share similar duties.
- **Note 3:** The [Western Continent](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Western_Continent "Western Continent") 's Netherworld also holds authority over reincarnation.
- **Note 4:** Before the [Underworld](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Underworld "Underworld") was created by the [Phoenix Ancestor](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Phoenix_Ancestor "Phoenix Ancestor"), one can only advance to Sequence 2 by luck.[^5]

## Sequence 1: Pale Emperor

![Potion - Death Sequence 1 - Pale Emperor](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/lord-of-the-mystery/images/1/1b/Potion_-_Death_Sequence_1_-_Pale_Emperor.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/200?cb=20260330033932)

Potion - Death Sequence 1 - Pale Emperor

- Overview
- Formula
- Ritual
- Backlash
- Acting Method

### Potion Properties

> Absolute paleness, absolute silence—no other color, no other trace.[^2]

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> A throne formed from pale bones.[^2]

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- The left claw of [Gregrace](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Gregrace "Gregrace").[^2]
Or

- The right eye of [Salinger](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Salinger "Salinger").[^2]

Or

- **A [Pale Emperor](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_1:_Pale_Emperor)** [Beyonder Characteristic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristic "Beyonder Characteristic").

Supplementary Ingredients

- 9 drops of River Styx water (River Styx is a branch of the [River of Eternal Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/River_of_Eternal_Darkness "River of Eternal Darkness") that emerged in the Underworld).[^2]
- 1 [Angel](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Angel "Angel") corpse (without [Beyonder Characteristics](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristics "Beyonder Characteristics")).[^2]
- 1 [Inverted Mausoleum](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Inverted_Mausoleum "Inverted Mausoleum").[^2]

### Advancement Ritual

Ensure your name becomes synonymous with death and the afterlife, remembered and recited by most sentient beings in your domain.[^4]

- **Note 1:** Due to the [Barrier](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Astral_Barrier "Astral Barrier"), the [Northern](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Northern_Continent "Northern Continent") and [Southern Continents](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Southern_Continent "Southern Continent") are isolated from the outside world, and are therefore treated as a separate domain in mysticism—otherwise, completing this ritual would be far harder.
- Without the Barrier, the range required for this ritual is a [Star System](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Star_System "Star System").[^6]
- **Note 2:** The same applies to the [Western Continent](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Western_Continent "Western Continent").
- **Note 3:** The same applies to the [Forsaken Land of the Gods](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Forsaken_Land_of_the_Gods "Forsaken Land of the Gods").[^7]
- **Note 4:** In the [Western Continent](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Western_Continent "Western Continent"), figures like the **[Great Emperor of Fengdu](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Western_Continent/Glossary#Great_Emperor_of_Fengdu "Western Continent/Glossary")** and the Buddhist **[Ksitigarbha](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Western_Continent/Glossary#Ksitigarbha "Western Continent/Glossary")** possess comparable authority and status.
- **Note 5:** Since the [God of Death](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Salinger "Salinger") is still widely regarded as the embodiment of death in the Northern and Southern Continents, "His" [descendants](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Eggers_Family "Eggers Family") can borrow "His" name to shape themselves and complete the ritual. The drawback? They grow increasingly similar to the [God of Death](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Salinger "Salinger") —until they become "Him".
