## Sequence 9: Sailor (Keeper of the Seas)

**Traits** [^1]

- **Aquatic Affinity**: **Sailors** possess certain attributes commonly found in sea creatures.
	- Even without any equipment, they are able to remain underwater for at least ten minutes.
		- **Sailors** can swim through the sea like fish and move underwater with the agility of marine animals.
		- They are able to dive 15 meters into the **Water** without any protection or equipment.
- **Illusory Scales**: They possess illusory scales hidden beneath their skin.
	- These scales makes them slippery and as hard to grasp as fish.[^2]
		- They also help a **Sailor** resist a certain degree of damage.[^3]
- **Balance**: A **Sailor** possesses an exceptional sense of **Balance**.
	- Even on a boat during a rainstorm, a **Sailor** can walk around freely as though standing on solid, stable ground.[^4]
- **Physical Enhancement**: **Sailors** possess outstanding strength.

## Sequence 8: Folk of Rage (Guardian of the Storm)

**Traits** [^1]

- **Physical Enhancement**: A **Folk of Rage's** strength, speed, and resistance are all greatly enhanced—facing them is like facing a storm.[^5]

**New Abilities** [^1] [^5]

- **Wrath**: When enraged, they can release several strikes that surpass ordinary limits.
	- This greatly enhances both a **Folk of Rage's** strength and speed.[^5]
- **Raging Blow**: This is a terrifying blow delivered through accumulated anger.
	- A **Folk of Rage** must first accumulate "hits" to activate this ability.[^6]
		- Being intoxicated can aid the process of accumulating "hits".[^6]

## Sequence 7: Seafarer (Storm Priest)

**Traits** [^1]

- **Aquatic Affinity**: **Seafarers** are even more beloved by the oceans and gain an overall enhancement to every aspect of themselves when at sea.
	- They can act and move freely underwater for more than half an hour.[^5]
- **Enhanced Mental Attributes**: Their calculation skills are improved.[^5]
	- Having spent the night in the area before, [Alger Wilson](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Alger_Wilson "Alger Wilson") calculated that the probability of encountering **Beyonder** creatures at the **Demigod** level depended mostly on **Luck**, and that the chances of him and [Gehrman](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") encountering one again were not too high.[^7]
- **Enhanced Memory**: **Seafarers** possess enhanced memory.[^8]

**New Abilities** [^1] [^5]

- **Navigation**: They are scholars of astronomy and geography. They have an intuitive grasp of magnetic fields, ocean currents, wind direction, and clouds, while also possessing excellent sailing capabilities.[^5]
	- They can accurately judge distances, allowing them to precisely throw weapons at a target with their eyes closed after determining the target's location.[^9]
		- A boat with a **Seafarer** on board will never get lost at sea due to their newfound grasp of, and affinity with, the fields of **Navigation**.

## Sequence 6: Wind-blessed

**Description**

They are irascible at times, matching the characteristics of a gale.[^14] They possess great **Beyonder** powers in domains related to **Water**, **Wind**, and the **Weather**.[^15]

**New Abilities** [^1]

- **Wind Control**: Blessed by the **Wind**, a **Wind-blessed** can unleash gales from within their own body to create pressure waves or surround themselves with howling **Hurricanes**.
	- **Glide**: They can use the **Winds** to **Glide** through the air if **Flight** has been **Prohibited** by a **[Judge](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Justiciar_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_6:_Judge "Justiciar Pathway/Abilities")**.[^16] [^17]
		- A **Wind-blessed** can generate **Wind** beneath their feet to more than double their running speed.
		- **Windblades**: They can create howling, sharp, invisible **Windblades** to slice targets from afar.[^16] [^18]
		- **Flight** (**Short-distance Flight**): They can **Fly** for short durations and distances.[^16] [^10]
		- They can imbue their hands with spiraling **Winds** to increase their penetrative power.[^18]
		- They can cause objects to be swept up by a gust of **Wind** and fly into their palms.[^19]
		- They can **Eavesdrop** on people's hidden conversations using the **Wind**.[^9]
		- They can create air cushions to reduce damage taken from impact.[^20]
		- **Floating Wind**: A **Wind-blessed** is able to hover in midair.
- **Water Control**:[^16] They can manipulate and use **Water** to a certain degree.
- **Dive**:[^16] A **Wind-blessed** can **Dive** to a depth of *100 meters*.
- **Float**:[^16] They can **Float** on the **Water** without sinking.
- **Night Vision**: They can see in the dark.[^21]

## Sequence 5: Ocean Songster

**Traits**

- **Aquatic Affinity**: They have more comprehensive and longer-lasting talents for underwater areas.
	- They are now able to move freely underwater for even longer periods of time.
		- Even many [Saints](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sequence "Sequence") couldn't dive into the depths to fight them.[^22]
		- They are now able to understand and communicate with aquatic animals.
		- They are now able to freely extract oxygen from the water.
		- Underwater, they fear no [Beyonder](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonders "Beyonders") below [Demigod](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sequence "Sequence") level.[^22]
- **Physical Enhancement**: When they sense an abnormality of intense danger, their heart will contract and expand like the source of a **Storm** as their blood will surge through their veins and arteries like a tidal wave.[^23]

**New Abilities** [^1] [^24]

- **Lightning Manipulation**: **Ocean Songsters** have superficial control over **Lightning**, and at this level, other than **Lightning Strike**, they must use other mediums to employ **Lightning**, such as arrows.[^25]
	- **Lightning Bolt Arrow**: With the aid of a bow and arrow, an **Ocean Songster** can condense a solid arrow from **Lightning** that deals massive damage to enemies.
		- It moves at an unavoidable speed, as if it were from a god of thunder.
				- The steps involved in a **Lightning Bolt Arrow** are as follows.
			- Their hair unfurls, defying nature, each strand distinct and swirling with **Lightning**. Upon drawing a bow, the sky darkens, clouds gather, and **Lightning** dances.
						- They then release the silver-white **Lightning** from their hair, entwining it with the arrow before releasing the string and shooting the arrow out.
						- Dark clouds lump the sky and a thick **Lightning Bolt** descends superimposing itself on the arrow, making it completely silver white.
		- **Lightning Strike**: An **Ocean Songster** can produce silver-white electric light capable of paralysis, generating a **Lightning Strike** to smite their targets.[^26] [^27]
		- **Lightning Strikes** contain **Purification** attributes that can damage **Undead** and [Evil Spirits](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Evil_Spirits "Evil Spirits") and can be infused into weapons such as bullets, and daggers to augment them.
			- The power of an **Ocean Songster's Lightning Strike** will cause the target's body to tremble and convulse as if performing a grotesque dance as their skin will quickly char.[^28]
				- It can't be dodged—only preemptively avoided. Even if it does not hit, it will spread to nearby conductive materials in the form of countless small "electric snakes".
			- **Lightning** makes even non-humanoid individuals (such as **Shadow** creatures) be paralyzed.[^28]
				- They can cast down a **Lightning Strike** with just sight as well as from their back.[^4] [^28]
		- They are capable of transmitting sound over long distances in the form of **Lightning**, easily passing through various obstacles.[^29]
		- Upon reaching the target, the bolt of silver **Lightning** will turn back to the **Ocean Songster's** voice, relaying the message contained within.[^30]
- **Singing**: The ability to influence targets with one's voice, it varies based on the user's individual traits.
	- This ability will be different because of every **Ocean Songster's** uniqueness, producing different branching paths, which includes:
		1. Using beautiful **Singing** to interfere with the enemy's [Spirit Body](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul"), causing them to turn adrift and fall into a daze, or to enhance one's own explosive power.
				2. An **Ocean Songster** using chaotic and unpleasant **Singing** to leave the enemy frustrated, causing them to lose their rationality.
			- With [Alger Wilson's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Alger_Wilson "Alger Wilson") **Singing**, [Gehrman](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") who already stuffed his ears and converged his **Spirituality** suddenly felt his forehead’s blood vessels throb, instantly having the urge to kill the singer and destroy everything before him.[^19]
				- In addition, his mind had the feeling of being ripped apart with his muscles and vessels squirming as a result.
				3. Simulating a **Thunderous** boom to leave others in awe.
		- Blocking one's ears and converging one's **Spirituality** cannot fully eliminate the negative effects of this ability. Even a deaf person can hear it. This is because this includes an "exchange" at the spiritual level, making it difficult to defend against.[^31]
		- In addition, it is very difficult to avoid an **Ocean Songster's** **Singing** through **Paper Figurine Substitutes** within the affected area.[^32]
- **Pressure Resistance**: A **Spell** -like effect that makes them not fearful of deep sea pressure.

**Strengthened Abilities** [^1]

- **Water Control**: A **Ocean Songster's** ability to control water has been greatly improved.[^33]
	- **Water Curtain**: They can create a **Water Curtain** for covering or defense.
		- They can now create enormous water spheres and corrosive rainwater.
		- They can create sticky liquid that has lubrication effects.[^34]
		- This can be used to get rid of friction [^35], and combined with the **Wind-blessed's** ability to create an air cushion, it can make the opening of a stone door not create any sound at all.[^34]
- **Wrath**: By drawing in a gasp, their eyes can burn with rage as their muscles swell.[^36]
- **Wind Control**: Their ability as a **Wind-blessed** has been moderately enhanced.
	- **Wind Binding**: They are able to create spiraling **Winds** to **Bind** a target's actions.[^37]
		- **Flight**: An **Ocean Songster** can now **Fly** for longer periods of time.[^33]
		- They can wrap their body in a whirlwind and **Fly** through the air, avoiding a gun shot that has just been sent as well as the **Black Flame** from a **[Demoness of Pleasure](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Demoness_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_6:_Pleasure_\(Demoness_of_Pleasure\) "Demoness Pathway/Abilities")**.[^28]

## Sequence 4: Cataclysmic Interrer

**Traits**

- **Underwater Vision**: They no longer have any reading troubles caused by insufficient light.
	- Even in the dark deep sea, they can see the surrounding objects clearly.[^1]
- **Scale Armor**: Their **Illusory Scales** have strong defensive ability.[^1]
- **Spiritual Intuition**: They are sensitive to the level of a high-ranking person.[^38]

**New Abilities** [^1]

- **Hurricane**: They can make violent **Winds** collide with the air, creating an explosive sound, while a terrifying **Hurricane** visible to the naked eye appears, swirling and winding.
	- The airflow of the **Hurricane** swirls in circles. If unchecked, it will suddenly rise from the ground, sweeping everything on the ground into the air while soaring into the sky is like a terrifying giant. Wherever it passes, the ground is clean and not a single thing is left behind.
		- The visible gales and howling **Winds** that a **Hurricane** causes sight-based mystical connections to be severed and obstructed.[^39]
				- The strength of a **Hurricane** depends on current potion digestion of the **Cataclysmic Interrer** and the environmental factors.\[?\]
				- A **Cataclysmic Interrer** is able to choose to control the directions of their **Hurricane** or simply let it spiral out of control.[^40]
- **Tsunami**: They are able to call forth huge **Water** waves approaching *10 meters high* to crash down on their targets, washing everything away in a powerful torrent.[^9]
	- [Alger Wilson](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Alger_Wilson "Alger Wilson") let the tidal waves he has made via a **Tsunami** envelop the **Cathedral of Waves** in a thick barrier with silver bolts of **Lightning** flashing within it.
- **Earthquake**: They can easily create an **Earthquake** on any land, and in doing so, make the land split into two and have lava erupt from within the fault line.
	- Relying on their own power to cause ground subsidence and the eruption of magma, an **Earthquake's** power will be limited to level 6 and can be concentrated in one area.
		- If the earth itself is accumulating power and there are signs of a terrifying **Earthquake**, the upper limit depends on the magnitude of the **Earthquake** brewing in the area.
		- The lava formed from an **Earthquake** is enough to cause the **Darkness** from the underground of the **Cathedral of Waves** to shrink back a little and pause for a second.[^41]
- **Torrential Downpour**: They can quickly form a **Torrential Downpour** across an extremely large area, obstructing any perception within that area.
- In addition, they can create other **Disasters** like **Snowstorms**, **Rainstorms** and **Floods**, situated in the **Domain** of **Calamities**.

**Strengthened Abilities**

- **Water Manipulation**: Their ability to control **Water** has undergone a qualitative change.
	- **Water Wall**: For protection, they can make a wall formed out of blue seawater besides them.[^30]
		- This blue seawater wall can effectively slow down the **Petrification** coming from the underground chamber within the **Cathedral of Waves**.
		- They can form a silent stream of **Water** that can surge out, eroding the area.[^30]
		- **Cataclysmic Interrers** also have the ability of **Wave Manipulation**.
- **Lightning Manipulation**: They possess a stronger control and power over **Lightning**.
	- **Thunder Strike**: A **Lightning Strike** hitting their target will have more intensity and accuracy, instantly scorching them and dealing heavy **Purification** damage.[^42]
		- **Chain Lightning**: A **Cataclysmic Interrer** can create several **Lightning Bolts** at the same time, and those **Lightning Bolts** can **Chain** between targets.[^1]
		- A **Cataclysmic Interrer** can create **Lightning Calamities**.[^43]
- **Singing**: Their capabilities when singing has been enhanced qualitatively.
	- Through singing a melody of death, a **Cataclysmic Interrer** is able to influence their surroundings and disrupt the operations of [Sealed Artifacts](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sealed_Artifact "Sealed Artifact") within the vicinity.[^9]
		- [Alger Wilson](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Alger_Wilson "Alger Wilson") sang a melody of death, influencing his surroundings and disrupting the operations of the other 2 [Grade-1 Sealed Artifacts](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sealed_Artifact "Sealed Artifact") that weren't under his attention before.[^41]
		- **Roar**: A **Cataclysmic Interrer's** vocal cords have enough strength to produce a **Roar** with enough intensity to create a sound wave that can topple a ship.[^42]
- **Wind Control**: Their ability to control the **Wind** has been enhanced.[^1]
	- **Windblade Legion**: They can manifest *hundreds* to *thousands* sharp and tiny **Windblades** that move towards the target from all directions.
		- **Flight**: A **Cataclysmic Interrer** possesses real **Flight** capability now and can move at speeds of up to *500 kilometers* per hour.
		- Besides **Windblade Legion** and **Flight**, a **Cataclysmic Interrer** also possesses the **Beyonder** power **Son of the Wind**.

**Mythical Creature Form**

- As **Demigods** at **Sequence 4** who have obtained partial **Godhood**, they are able to manifest their incomplete **Mythical Creature** **Form** if they disregard any of the consequences.

## Sequence 3: Sea King

**Description**

Even with range limitations, within the waters they control, **Sea Kings** are undoubtedly powerful rulers, able to walk freely on the seabed, wantonly stir up **Tsunamis**, cause **Sea** levels to rise, and dominate marine life. In their home turf, they are absolutely capable of overwhelming all **Demigods** of the same level.[^1]

**Traits**

- **Enhanced Mental Attributes**: They possess a potent psyche that is terrifying.[^44]
- **Aquatic Affinity**: They are able to freely walk on the **Ocean** seafloor.
- **Physical Enhancement**: They have **Sea** -monster like strength.
- **Balance**: They possess unimaginable levels of **Balance**.

**Authorities** [^1]

- **Wrath of the Sea**: Part of the **Sea** authority, a **Sea King** can cause the entire sea area to repels the corresponding target, blocking and attacking them through various methods.
	- One such method are **Tsunamis**.[^1]
- **Sea Master**: Formed from the parts of the **Aquatic Life** authority and **Tyrant** authority, **Sea Kings** are equivalent to rulers of an entire expanse of the **Sea**.[^45]
	- **Sea Kings** can command many **Sea** creatures to do their bidding as well as transform themselves into any **Sea** creature, making the seabed be their home turf.
		- Their psyche is so potent that it is terrifying, and with that psyche, they are able to search for any possible abnormal spots within an area of the **Sea**.[^44]
		- This will cause external prying to be affected, turning everything indistinct.
		- A **Sea King** is able to turn the surrounding waters into a calm, windless lake, which can reflect the scenes of the past within the area.[^45]
		- When fighting in the **Ocean**, they can unleash their fullest potential and absolutely overwhelm any and all same level **Demigods**.[^45]

**Strengthened Abilities**

- **Hurricane**: They can shatter **Hurricanes** into countless streaks of silvery-white **Lightning**, bursting with a destructive, violent aura that engulfs the area, forming a **Lightning Storm**.[^39]
- **Torrential Downpour**: A **Torrential Downpour** could spread a **Sea King's** **Spirituality**, making every dark cloud and every drop of water from that **Calamity** become their eyes.[^46]
	- They can quickly cover a very large area of [Calderón City's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Calder%C3%B3n_City "Calderón City") periphery with dark clouds that release bean size *raindrops* that form a catastrophic storm. Such weather heavily restricts the use of **Flame** based abilities like **Flaming Jump**.[^46]
- **Tsunamis**: With a rise in **Sequence** level, a **Sea King** is now able to set off *40* to *50 meter high* **Tsunamis** wantonly as well as raise the **Sea** level of an entire region.[^47]
	- In the port of [Bayam](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Bayam "Bayam"), the blue **Water** rose slowly but firmly, gradually approached the dyke as the city was being flooded with **Water** in many places, making the environment was greatly suppressed. Eventually the dyke collapsed, and the rising seawater poured into [Bayam City](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Bayam "Bayam").[^48]
- **Water Manipulation**:<sup>⁂</sup> A **Sea King** can create **Destructive Droplets** through **Water**.
	- **Destructive Droplet**: By disintegrating or reorganizing **Water** droplets, a **Sea King** can release terrifying destructive power, which works by disintegrating hydrogen atoms (E = MC²).[^49]
		- This creates **Fusion** of a certain yield.[^50]
		- Even when about to die, they can cause **Water** to gush in from all directions, collapsing into a whirlpool that emits a terrifying suction force that can swallow a cargo ship.[^51]
		- This can cause many objects within a warehouse, along with the soil to be dragged into the whirlpool as even people will find themselves being flown up and dragged in.
				- Even if one uses a **Paper Figurine Substitute**, they will still soar into the air and be unable to extricate themselves from the terrifying suction force.
				- A **[Zombie](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chained_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_6:_Zombie "Chained Pathway/Abilities")** can avoid being flown around, but can't avoid being sucked in by the suction force.
				- The combination of this whirlpool and a **Hurricane** can interfere with the ignition of **Flames**.
				- The **Ice Wall** of a **[Zombie](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chained_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_6:_Zombie "Chained Pathway/Abilities")** would only last a *second* before being shredded into pieces.
				- Beyond the range of a *warehouse*, the suction force of this vortex can't affect anyone.
		- A **Sea King** would be able to rapidly make the flesh and blood of ordinary people lose moisture, to the point if becoming desiccated corpses.[^48]
- **Lightning Manipulation**: **Sea Kings** are able to create **Lightning Storms**.
	- **Lightning Storm**: They can make silver-white "electric snakes" burst forth, slashing densely at the surroundings as "branches" that envelop an area of *hundreds of meters* in a **Lightning** "forest".
		- Any creatures caught within the **Lightning Storm** will be shattered and vaporized. This is a ranged attack that doesn't distinguish between friend and foe.
				- A **Sea King's** **Lightning Storm** carries a destructive aura so intense it seems tangible, stunning enemies in place and interfering with their thinking.
			- When [Xio](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Xio_Derecha "Xio Derecha") and [Fors](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fors_Wall "Fors Wall") were searching for **Ghosts** in East Borough, they were alarmed by the sky that was lit up by a **Recorded Lightning Storm** whose twisted form and sense of horror left them trembling and not daring to look straight at it despite the distance.[^40]
				- They can generate **Lightning Storms** from the **Sky** or from thin air.
		- Even when about to die, they can release a silver ball of **Lightning** that transforms into streaks of stunning **Lightning** bolts that quickly expand outwards, occupying an entire warehouse.[^51]
		- Those outside the area will still suffer effects, their body convulsing and hair standing up.
		- **Thunderclap**: A **Sea King** can create a sonorous **Thunderclap**, one that strikes awe into one's mind and spirit. This **Thunderclap** is one that can also awe **Spirit Bodies**.[^46]
- **Wind Manipulation**:<sup>⁂</sup> The power of a **Sea King's** **Winds** have been enhanced.
	- The speed of the **Winds** a **Sea King** can now conjure is able to break the sound barrier, creating **Sonic Booms** which could make a pirate ship fly out.[^44]
		- **Flight**: This also allows a **Sea King** to **Fly** at speeds faster than the speed of sound.[^40]
				- **Sonic Boom**: A **Sea King** can now create **Sonic Booms** at a distance.[^1]
		- They can make clouds to gather in midair, weaving into a massive sword, which can descend to stand before an ally.[^52]
- **Navigation**: A **Sea King** can never get lost.
	- This is assuming there is no superposition of other powerful factors.

**Mythical Creature Form**

- As a **Sequence 3 Beyonder**, they can listen and respond to prayers within a certain range. A **Sea King's** range for responding to prayers is relatively far-reaching compared to other **Sequence 3 Beyonders**.[^53]
	- While commanding them, they can bestow **Sea** creatures (such as fishes) with remnant **Beyonder** **Spirituality** to a degree, allowing them to transmit the scenes they saw by praying to achieve a form of surveillance.[^54]

## Sequence 2: Calamity

**New Abilities** [^1]

- **Sound of Horror**: Through sound, "They" can instill extreme **Horror** in living beings and even [Sealed Artifacts](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sealed_Artifact "Sealed Artifact") within a radius of *hundreds* of *kilometers*, forcing them to obey.
	- The arrival of [Gaard II](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Gaard_II "Gaard II") spread extreme fear, as if the sound of a booming musical instrument had exploded in the hearts of every [Beyonder](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonders "Beyonders") present.[^55]

**Authorities** [^1]

- **Calamity**: "They" hold partial authority over **Calamities**.
	- **Descent of Calamity**: Includes **Thunder Storms**, **Snow Storms**, **Tsunamis**, etc., which can be freely summoned, each capable of destroying a port or even an entire island.
		- "They" can summon a **Calamity** that creates an area of extreme coldness that can **Freeze** everything within the surroundings in seconds.[^56]
		- **Storm Incarnation**: "They" are able to unleash **Hurricanes** and **Storms** capable of destroying a large city. This is derived from part of the **Calamities** authority.
		- "They" can cause dark clouds within an area to quickly converge together in the sky, forming brows and a mouth, as though a face was hidden within.[^57]
			- This can be done from "Their" position in [Bayam](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Bayam "Bayam") to a mountain outside the city.[^57]

**Strengthened Abilities** [^1]

- "Their" previous **Beyonder** powers have been enhanced.
	- **Source of Wrath**: **Wrath** can temporarily enhance one's power to the next **Sequence**.
		- The price of **Source of Wrath** is the abandonment of rational thought.
				- **Source of Wrath** is not able to enhance one's status.
				- It can be used on oneself or to influence a target.
		- **Lightning Manipulation**: "Their" ability to use **Lightning** has been enhanced.
		- **Magnetic Pole Pull**: "They" can create or manipulate **Magnetic Fields** to push, pull, or rotate targets. The rotation of a **Magnetic Field** itself can accumulate power.
			- "They" can cast down a thick bolt of **Lightning** out of nowhere which can illuminate the surroundings and form a **Lightning** cage that can enclose around the arm of an **Abomination** that is transferring "Their" powers away across a wide distance.[^57]
				- **Electromagnetic Disruption**: "They" can cause severe **Information** interference in a designated city cluster, large island, or corresponding **Sea** area.
			- This will render communication methods such as telegraphs ineffective.
				- "They" can send **Lightning** from "Their" position in [Bayam](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Bayam "Bayam") to a mountain outside the city.[^57]
		- **Wind Manipulation**: "Their" **Flight** speed has reached a qualitative change.
		- **Flight**: "They" can **Fly** at the speeds of a ballistic missile.
		- **Singing**: "Their" **Singing** has been greatly enhanced.
		- "They" can **Sing** an ethereal **Song**, causing targets to involuntarily recall their past, causing Indescribable grief to surge from the bottom of one's heart.
			- Within the **Dream**, [Cohinem](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Cohinem "Cohinem") used this to affect [Alger Wilson](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Alger_Wilson "Alger Wilson"), causing him to involuntarily recall the past of his mother who had long passed away, and the days when he was a bullied child, which prevented him from waking up immediately.[^58]
				- "They" can **Sing** a **Song** that spells out an extreme sense of sadness and melancholy, causing one's [Spirit Body](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul") to produce pale, non-existent arms that constantly tore at them.
			- This will cause the affected target to begin showing signs of losing control as well as have the thoughts that originally existed in their mind disrupted by the **Song** and were cut off by the pain, unable to take form any further.[^58]
				- This was also done by [Cohinem](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Cohinem "Cohinem") within [Alger Wilson's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Alger_Wilson "Alger Wilson") **Dream**.

**Mythical Creature Form**

- "Their" [Mythical Creature Form](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mythical_Creature_Form "Mythical Creature Form") has become complete. "Their" [Mythical Creature Form](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mythical_Creature_Form "Mythical Creature Form") is that of a giant octopus-like creature with thick, terrifying tentacles surrounded by silver-white **Lightning**.[^1]

## Sequence 1: Thunder God

**New Abilities** [^1]

- **Incarnation of Light**: "They" can transform "Themselves" into an energy body, capable of flying or attacking at speeds approaching, but not quite reaching, that of light.

**Authorities** [^1]

- **Thunder**, **Wind**, **& Calamity**: "They" hold authority over **Thunder**, **Wind**, and **Calamities**.
	- **Heavenly Punishment**: "They" can manifest a massive bolt of **Lightning** to descend from the sky, imbued with the powers of **Disintegration**, **Purification**, and **Destruction**.
		- It is very potent against targets from the **Darkness**, **Death**, **Corruption**, and **Filth** domains.
				- It can attack the weaknesses of targets that attempt to transcend nature or matter.
			- Targets that attempts to take on a non-natural form such as by becoming a torrent of **Information** or through **Symbolization** would still be harmed. It will also prevent them from evading the **Lightning** strike by entering other domains.[^25]
				- **Heavenly Punishment** is derived from a portion of the **Thunder** authority.
		- **Storm Hell**: "They" can designate a **Sea** area, a large island, or a city cluster to be engulfed in a destructive **Storm Hell**, with the effect potentially lasting indefinitely.
		- This is derived from part of the **Calamities** authority combined with the **Wind** authority.
		- **Dance of Thunder**: A **Thunder God** is able to use invisible **Thunder** that disrupts, interferes with, and destroys **Information** and electromagnetic structures.
		- This makes it the nemesis of **Information** -based systems.
		- **Ocean of Lightning**: "They" can make a **Sea** composed of terrifying **Lightning** with intense electromagnetic effects.
		- It can be paused to a halt by a **Replicated** **Time** authority of the **[Worm of Time](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_1:_Worm_of_Time "Error Pathway/Abilities")**, but only for a moment.[^59]
				- A **Thunder God's** **Ocean of Lightning** is capable of covering an entire large island.
		- "They" can reach or even surpass other [Sequence 0](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Gods "Gods") s in the domain of **Thunder** depending on "Their" mastery over the corresponding authority.[^60]
		- Compared to a [Sequence 0](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Gods "Gods") without a similar authority, "They" are naturally stronger. Compared to a [Sequence 0](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Gods "Gods") with a similar authority, it depends on how much authority permissions the [Sequence 0](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Gods "Gods") itself has delegated.

**Strengthened Abilities** [^1]

- "Their" previous **Beyonder** powers have been enhanced.
	- **Source of Wrath**: "They" can reach close to a [True Deity's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Gods "Gods") power through "Their" anger.[^61]
		- **Singing**: "Their" ability to **Roar** has been greatly enhanced.
		- **Roar of the Thunder God**: This is a sonic attack that spreads at the speed of **Lightning**, inducing **Horror**, **Intimidation**, and other effects. It is particularly effective against targets from the **Darkness**, **Death**, **Corruption**, and **Filth** domains.

## Sequence 0: Tyrant

**Authorities [^1]**

- **Matter**: This authority is partially derived from the **Maker** symbolism and intersects with the **Thunder** authority. **Matter** can be converted into **Energy**, and **Energy** can be condensed back into **Matter**, encompassing the **Electromagnetic**, **Strong**, and **Weak Forces**.
	- "They" are able to approach the speed of **Light** while retaining mass, unlike the **[Sun](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sun_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Sun "Sun Pathway/Abilities")**, who moves at the speed of **Light** without mass.[^62]
		- "They" can transform into layers of entangled ball **Lightning**, shooting down from the [Astral World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Astral_World "Astral World") at near- **Light** speed and illuminating the skies and the world below.[^63]
			- This mass of **Lightning** can tear through the void. When its explosion and shockwave unfurl, it erupts in a deafening roar that can shatter and dissipate the natural light of the [Crimson Moon](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Goddess_of_Depravity "Mother Goddess of Depravity"), leaving behind a breach.
				- "They" can accelerate a portion of **Matter** toward the upper limit of speed, creating a violent "wave" that could destroy a planet.[^64]
			- This manifests as a points of **Light** that carry a **Storm**.
		- "They" can transform into a current that briefly reaches the speed of **Light**.
- **Thunder** (**Electricity** & **Magnetism**): This authority is partially derived from the **Maker** symbolism. "They" command the forces of **Electricity** and **Magnetism**, allowing "Them" to unleash powerful **Electromagnetic Storms** capable of destroying all **Information** structures.[^65]
	- The remnants of "Their" divine power can create bolts of silver **Lightning** capable of covering the entirety of [Nois City](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Nois_City "Nois City") and fully collapsing it. However, their power is only enough to injure an [Angel](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Angels "Angels").[^66] [^67]
		- These bolts of silver **Lightning** can tear through the **Darkness** that envelops the area, as well as all the **Corruption**, **Concealment**, **Darkness**, vileness, and **Evil**. Those within the eye of the **Lightning** storm will dissipate, while terrifying, silvery-white, twisted bolts of **Lightning** snake toward the surroundings.
				- It can also summon thousands of **Lightning** bolts as thick as an anaconda, causing them to descend from the sky, smite the surrounding area, and transform it into a **Lightning** forest emitting a powerful destructive aura.
			- This **Lightning** can rapidly break down the barrier created by the black velvet 'Curtain' (**Realm of Mysteries**). Targets within it burst into ephemeral illusions under the piercing strike, becoming instantly charred black and reduced to ashes.
		- "They" can send down a house-sized ball of **Lightning**, dragging a silvery-white stream of light behind it, capable of striking and damaging a target that has transformed into a figment of **Imagination**.[^68]
		- A [Kings of Angels](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Kings_of_Angels "Kings of Angels") of the [Error Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway "Error Pathway"), possessing the [Error Uniqueness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniquenesses#Error_Pathway "Uniquenesses") and *two* **Sequence 1 Beyonder Characteristics**, can endure this by distributing the damage among "Their" **Avatars** after transforming into a figment of **Imagination** to escape the surroundings. Even then, "They" will still suffer damage that takes time to recover from. In addition, "Their" [Error Uniqueness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniquenesses#Error_Pathway "Uniquenesses") would appear to crack.
		- "They" can instantly turn the sky gloomy, as though it is filled with the indescribable wrath of nature. The night sky would instantly erupt with **Lightning** of different colors, baring fangs and brandishing claws as it expanded wantonly. It seems to envelop the entire sky in a sea of **Lightning**, with tempestuous bolts striking down like **Torrential Rain**.[^29]
		- The **Lightning** can illuminate all of [Utopia](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Utopia "Utopia") and more than half of the [Berserk Sea](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Berserk_Sea "Berserk Sea").[^30]
				- The might of the **Storm** can stun an **[Attendant of Mysteries](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fool_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_1:_Attendant_of_Mysteries "Fool Pathway/Abilities")**.[^30]
		- "They" can create countless silver-white electric snakes that intertwine into huge, flamboyant **Lightning**, capable of submerging the surroundings in a dazzling and intense **Ocean of Thunder**.[^69]
- **Tyrant**: The King of Kings. This authority encompasses **Intimidation**, **Wrath**, and a portion of **Horror**. The ability of **Singing** is also derived from this authority.
	- "They" can exude a domineering, savage, **Tyrannous**, and terrifying aura.[^70] "Their" **Storms** contain immense **Horror**.[^71]
		- The [Lord of Storms](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lord_of_Storms "Lord of Storms") generated **Thunderclaps** that awed all living beings, including [Angels](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Angels "Angels") (except [0-17](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Angel_of_Concealment "Angel of Concealment")), interrupting them and rendering them stiff.[^72]
				- "Their" **Tyrannical** aura can stop a [Chained Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chained_Pathway "Chained Pathway") **Beyonder** from **Possessing** someone, repel them, and force them to lose their **Invisibility**.[^9]
- **Water**: This authority originates from the intersection of the **Maker** and **Omnipotence** symbols. It encompasses the authority of the **Seas** and a portion of **Aquatic Life**.
	- This allows the **Tyrant** to influence any source related to the concept of **Water**, such as the [Sea of Collective Subconscious](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mind_World#Sea_of_Collective_Subconscious "Mind World"), [Chaos Sea](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chaos_Sea "Chaos Sea"), and [River of Fate](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/River_of_Fate "River of Fate").[^73]
		- Some of these, like the [River of Eternal Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/River_of_Eternal_Darkness "River of Eternal Darkness"), can only be influenced indirectly to a relatively low degree.[^73] [^74]
				- **Fog** does not fall under this authority as it can be smoke, dust, or a collection of conceptually illusory things.[^75]
		- **Seas**: The **Seas** fall under the broader **Water** authority.
		- "They" can cause the **Seas** to surge upward toward the clouds, intersecting with the **Sky**.[^76]
		- **Aquatic Life**: This is another aspect of the **Water** authority.
		- This authority can alter certain forms of **Aquatic Life**, making them more octopus-like.[^77]
- **Divine Kingdom**: "Their" **Divine Kingdom** is formed from the fusion of the **Tyrant**, **Thunder**, and **Calamity** authorities.[^1]
	- **Chasm of Storms**: This **Divine Kingdom** focuses on the **Thunder** and **Calamity** authorities.
		- **Dreadful Heaven** (**Sole Obedience to the Storm**): This emphasizes the **Tyrant** authority.
- **Calamity**: "They" possess partial authority over **Calamity**, which is subordinate to the broader authority of **Catastrophe**.
	- "They" can banish others into a never-ending **Storm**, using it to **Seal** them.[^78] [^79]
		- "They" can trigger **Earthquakes** powerful enough to destroy the [Earth](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Map_of_the_World "Map of the World").[^49]
- **Wind**: The **Wind** authority intersects with the authority of **Calamities**.
