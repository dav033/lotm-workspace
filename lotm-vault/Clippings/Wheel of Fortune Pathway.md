## Overview

**Wheel of Fortune Pathway**, more commonly referred to as **Fate Pathway**, represents the **Possibility** of one's **Fate** and represents **Chaos**.[^4] It holds authorities over **Fate**, **Probability**, **Cycle**, and **Disorder**, as well as the subordinate authorities of **Good Luck**, **Bad Luck**, **Foresight**, **Save**, and **Select**.

Beyonders of this pathway are known for their high spirituality, their abilities to sense danger, foresee the future and are experts in [divination](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Divination_Art "Divination Art"). They become lucky but also attract calamities. They can control the luck and misfortune of others and their own to a certain extent.

Low and mid-Sequence Beyonders of the **Monster Pathway** are really extreme. Apart from the spirituality and **Fate** domain, they almost have zero **Beyonder** powers. Even their constitution and calculation skills are provided at **Robot**. The subsequent advancements give rather limited improvements. It could be said that they are the ideal model for a charlatan. They do not have any offensive or defensive abilities, simply relying on their premonition and how **Fate** blesses them.[^5]

At High-Sequences, they can bestow misfortunes, find the best possibilities within chaos, manipulate the fate of living beings, trap others in an endless loop of fated events, and avoid danger by restarting an encounter or through reincarnation, and manipulate probability, becoming the embodiment of the endless outcomes of **Fate**.

The title of [Above the Sequence](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Above_the_Sequence "Above the Sequence") of the **Wheel of Fortune Pathway** is [Key of Light](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Key_of_Light), and the corresponding [Sefirah](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirah "Sefirah") is the [Key of Light](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Key_of_Light_\(Sefirah\)). This Pathway is the only one among the [Standard Pathways](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Standard_Pathways "Standard Pathways") that does not have any neighbouring Pathway.

## Corresponding Tarot Card

![TencentVideo - 10 WHEEL OF FORTUNE](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/lord-of-the-mystery/images/6/6b/TencentVideo_-_10_WHEEL_OF_FORTUNE.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/120?cb=20250812082541)

The corresponding tarot card of the **Wheel of Fortune Pathway** is **Wheel of Fortune**.[^6] [^9]

**Wheel of Fortune (X)** is the number tenth of 22 Major Arcana. The Wheel of Fortune is generally an indicator of luck or destiny. A common aspect to most interpretations of this card within a reading is to introduce an element of change in the querent's life, such change being in station, position or fortune: such as the rich becoming poor, or the poor becoming rich.[^10]

## Mythical Creature Form

**Snake of Mercury** [^11]

A giant scaleless silver snake covered with dense patterns and symbols on its surface, the symbols being various wheels that are connected to each other. Its tail and head wheels were each halved, only if the giant silver snake could bite its own tail with its mouth, the wheel would be complete.[^12] Each wheel is different and they represent different futures that might happen.[^13] They have cold, bright red eyes.[^14]

- [Klein Moretti](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") once made two guesses: The pathway that focuses on [Fate](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/River_of_Fate "River of Fate") is destined to be lonely and its [Beyonders](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder "Beyonder") don't wear many [Mystical Items](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mystical_Item "Mystical Item"). This temporarily causes one's own characteristics to die or fall dormant, allowing a switch under such conditions. However, this is very dangerous and will most likely result in actual death.[^20]
