## Sequence 9: Mystery Pryer

**Traits**

- **Spirituality**: Their **Spirituality** will get enhanced upon drinking this potion.
	- **Magic & Divination Arts**: They have a comprehensive but rudimentary understanding and grasp of magic, Enchantment, witchcraft [Divination Arts](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Divination_Art "Divination Art"), [Ritualistic Magic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ritualistic_Magic "Ritualistic Magic"), and other mystical knowledge.[^1]
		- A **Mystery Pryer's** use of **Ritualistic Magic** allows them to summon [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World") creatures and form **Contracts** with them which enables them to eventually obtain messengers and helpers with unique powers as they progress forward in their studies.
			- As a **Mystery Pryer** progresses through the [Sequences](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sequence "Sequence"), they will be able to form **Contracts** with even more and more powerful and unique **Spirit World** creatures.[^2]
				- They can use many **Divination** methods, including astrology, cartomancy, spirit pendulums, spiritual numbers, and scrying in order to gain revelations from the [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World"), however, they are less effective in this matter than [Seers](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fool_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_9:_Seer "Fool Pathway/Abilities").[^3] [^4]
		- **Spirit Vision**:<sup><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_Vision" title="Spirit Vision">?</a></sup> **Mystery Pryers** are able to utilize their **Spirit Vision** to deduce a person's health and emotions, as well as if an object or person contains some sort of magical aura.[^5]

**New Abilities** [^1]

- **Eyes of Mystery Prying**: A **Mystery Pryer's** eyes are special and allow them to be able to see things that normally aren't visible.[^6]
	- They can **Pry** into things that shouldn't or can't be seen. This lets them peer through attempts to trap them in **Dreams** or **Illusions**, see truth and reality, discern the light of **Spirituality**, the state of a target's [Astral Body](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul#Astral_Projection "Soul"), [Ether Body](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul#Ether_Body "Soul"), and [Body of Heart and Mind](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul#Body_of_Heart_and_Mind "Soul"), as well as examine the various details of a target's [Astral Body](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul#Astral_Projection "Soul").[^7] [^8] [^9] [^10]
		- They can identify if their target is a **Beyonder** or not through the target's [Astral Projection](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul#Astral_Projection "Soul").[^6]
				- Utilizing the [Eye of Truth](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Eye_of_Truth "Eye of Truth"), [Lumian](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee") was able to view the concealed [Mirror Gardner Martin](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Gardner_Martin "Gardner Martin").[^11]
				- [Cattleya](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Cattleya "Cattleya") could view the light of **Spirituality** of the different items [Klein](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") was carrying.[^11]
		- **Mystery Pryers** have to be careful and show proper restraint with their powers as a **Beyonder** as they often see things they shouldn't see, leading to them being most exposed to **Corruption**.[^7]
		- This ability is instinctive and cannot be deactivated. **Mystery Pryers** can prevent it from affecting their daily lives through **Seals** or using special [Mystical Items](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mystical_Item "Mystical Item") of sufficient power.[^11] [^12]
- **Knowledge** (**Knowledge Pursuit**): **Mystery Pryers** will occasionally be chased by knowledge brought to life, imbuing them with knowledge in random intervals.
	- This can either lead to them losing control or gaining a benefit, though often it is the former due to its lack of predictability and interference from the [Hidden Sage](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hidden_Sage "Hidden Sage"), or other hidden entity such as the [Inextinguishable Ravings](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Inextinguishable_Ravings "Inextinguishable Ravings").[^13]
		- **Knowledge** **Pursuit** (and by extension, **Angel** -level **Education** abilities) cannot be treated with **Placate**. This is because **Knowledge Pursuit** is not mental confusion or an emotional outburst. The targets minds simply cannot withstand being flooded with so much knowledge in a short time, leaving them not even able to utilize self-protection mechanisms and pass out.[^14]
- **Quick Rituals**: They can quickly complete [Ritualistic Magic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ritualistic_Magic "Ritualistic Magic") without the need for most ritual items.[^10]
	- They can cast an invisible barrier with various sprays, powders, and an incantation.[^15]
		- They can cast a **Spell** that aids a **Mystery Pryer** in drawing someone's portrait.[^15]

**Traits**

**New Abilities**

## Sequence 7: Warlock

**Traits**

**New Abilities** [^2]

- **Spellcasting**: **Warlocks** use their knowledge of magic, gained through study, analysis, learning, and sometimes their connection to the [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World"), to create their **Spells** using their knowledge and mind.
	- This makes them very versatile, and they can be considered as a traditional fantasy mage.[^18] [^19]
		- The more knowledge they have the more comprehensive and powerful their **Spells** become.
		- They can't cast **Spells** quickly as even the simplest of **Spells** require 3 steps to finish:
		1. **Focus**: Concentrating one's own **Spirituality**.
				2. **Mental component**: Outlining the corresponding symbol of the **Spell** in their mind which can be replaced by reciting the **Spell's** corresponding incantation aloud.
			- While casting steps are required for a **Spell**, they can be omitted later.[^20]
				3. **Material component**: Using the materials to cast the **Spell**. The materials can either serve as a medium, or part of the **Spell**.[^21]
			- They can also use certain [Sealed Artifacts](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sealed_Artifact "Sealed Artifact") as a substitute for certain materials.[^21]
		- Some of the **Spells** they can cast include:
		- **Scene Depiction**: Using some powder to display the scenes they see from their **Eyes of Mystery Prying** onto mirrors or directly tossing powder to form a realistic picture.[^22] [^23]
				- **Soul Summoning**: A supplementary **Spell** designed to aid spirits in separating from the flesh or to help [Astral Projections](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul") find their spirits when adrift in the spirit realm.[^24]
				- **Hand of Force**: Using an iron-black powder to create a strong **Invisible Hand** which can be used as a way to exert force on their targets from afar.[^25]
				- **Wind Creation**: Using a slightly transparent powder to create strong **Winds**, this is useful to blow away airborne diseases or toxic gas.[^25]
				- **Exorcism**: Using pearl-like powder to clear weeds and vines, this is also useful to combat plant-based **Beyonder** powers.[^25]
				- **Flames**: Using powder that accurately lands on their targets, bursting into scarlet **Flames** that silently burn whatever they touch.[^26]
				- **Purification**: Using **Sun Flower** powder to create Area-of-Effect **Purification** effects, allowing them to **Purify** the undead.[^27]
				- **Lightning**: Using powder from a type of tree to create **Lightning** which can be used to stun enemies or clear areas.[^28]
				- **Plant Snare**: This uses specific leaves, petals and brambles that grow in size and join together to ensnare their targets.[^29]
				- **Sea Wave**: Using a light blue powder they can send out dark blue waves in all directions that can extinguish flames.[^30]
				- **Invisibility**: Using fluorescent powder, they can begin to fade, growing more transparent, vanishing in the blink of an eye.[^31]

**Strengthened Abilities**

- **Knowledge** (**Witchcraft**): Having grasped the corresponding abilities in the mysticism domains, they can use their acquired mysticism knowledge to make potions and better [Mystical Items](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mystical_Item "Mystical Item").[^32] [^21]
	- Due to the influence of the [Mother Goddess of Depravity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Goddess_of_Depravity "Mother Goddess of Depravity"), **Warlocks** from the [Moses Ascetic Order](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Moses_Ascetic_Order "Moses Ascetic Order") used newborns not only as components in potions but also in witchcraft and better [Mystical Items](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mystical_Item "Mystical Item").
- **Eyes of Mystery Prying**: Their special eyes have been greatly enhanced.
	- As a **Warlock**, they can naturally **Pry** into the mysteries of various supernatural powers.
		- They can **Pry** into special blood, remnant auras, [Sealed Artifacts](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sealed_Artifact "Sealed Artifact") and **Beyonder** powers to gain the symbols and knowledge to make **Spells** from different domains like **Evil** or **Life**.[^19]
			- However, this relies on intellect and knowledge, and is quite dangerous.

## Sequence 6: Scrolls Professor

**New Abilities**

- **Scroll Making**: They can make scrolls from various materials to cast **Spells**. After chanting the corresponding incantation, the scrolls will burn, releasing their abilities.
	- This greatly enhances their casting time and combat ability.[^10]
		- Some of the **Scroll Spells** they can create include:
		- **Secret Voice**: This **Scroll Spell** forms a channel linking 3 to 5 people within 50 meters, allowing for communication without distance obstruction.[^33]
				- **Freezing**: When released, this **Scroll Spell** creates a stream of crystal clear light that can travel mid-air and fall onto a target, freezing the target.[^34]
				- **Storm**: When released, this **Scroll Spell** creates a wide thunderstorm, that has both abundant wind and lightning and deals great damage. [^33]
				- **Numb**: When released, this **Scroll Spell** creates a light green luster that can travel mid-air and fall onto a target, paralyzing them.[^35]
				- **Burning**: When released, this **Scroll Spell** releases burning flames that engulf the nearby area, burning all those in its range**.**[^33]
				- **Wind**: When released, this **Scroll Spell** forms a swift wind that envelops a selected target, boosting their speed.[^35]
				- **Healing**: When released, this **Scroll Spell** releases a green light that heals targets that are enveloped by it.[^33]
				- **Sun**: When released, this **Scroll Spell Purifies** targets who are affected by its aura of holy light.[^33]

## Sequence 5: Constellations Master

**Description**

**Astrology**, **Constellations**, **Stars**, and **Starlight** are all concepts that possess dual meanings with both mystical and real astronomical significance. A **Constellations Master** has a basic grasp of the mystical aspects of these concepts, enabling them to utilize corresponding abilities.[^10]

**New Abilities** [^10]

- **Starlight Cage**: Upon reciting the corresponding incantation, the brilliant **Starlight** swirling around the caster will begin to detach in successive streams, descending upon the target.
	- The **Starlight** instantly coalesces, forming a vast transparent amber that completely envelops the target, confining them to their original position.[^35]
- **Star Pillar**: A **Constellation Master** can scatter the **Stars** they create one after another and merge them into a magnificent pillar of **Starlight**, covering a certain area of space.
	- Any target within the affected area of space will be dissolved in this **Starlight**, instantly or extremely quickly having it's structure collapse and/or dissipate.
- **Stellar Self**: This ability makes the **Constellations Master's** form instantly transparent, as though transformed into a statue composed of countless **Starlight** particles [^26]
	- This "statue" can then shatter abruptly, as a brilliant **Starlight** surges forth in scattered points, allowing the **Constellation Master** to slip through the fissures as fragments of **Starlight** will coalesce anew within, revealing the caster's silhouette.
- **Starry Sea Shuttle**: They can make ships fly through the use of **Starlight**.
	- [Cattleya](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Cattleya "Cattleya") made bits of **Starlight** swirl around [The Future](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Stars_Pirates "Stars Pirates"), causing a squall to stir as the **Starlight** descended to form a swath of “floating ice”, dragging the ship into a “rapid flight".[^36]
- **Star Concealment**: **Constellation Masters** are able to use the **Stars** they have formed to **Conceal** the targeted area and nullify any outside interference within.
	- This could allow them to isolate certain **Beyonder** powers from affecting them.[^37]
- **Star Bridge**: They can manifest an illusory **Starry** bridge from their refined **Stars** to stand upon.
- **Star Illumination**: They can also utilize their **Stars** as a light source in dark areas.[^37]

**Strengthened Abilities**

- **Eyes of Mystery Prying**: **Constellation Masters** will receive a great increase in their ability to sense hidden existences, making most things within a range of several tens if meters be visible to them in an unobstructed yet chaotic manner.
	- A **Constellation Master** can vaguely see layers of thick, shadowy curtains surrounding them, and behind these curtains, something seems to be watching them, watching all living beings.[^38] [^39]
		- They can also spy on the condition of an environment from a certain distance.
- **Spellcasting**: Their ability to cast **Spells** have been enhanced. Now, they do not necessarily need materials to cast some **Spells** and can instead cast them via simple gestures.[^26]
	- [Cattleya](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Cattleya "Cattleya"), through a few simple gestures and a whistle, was able to summon illusory ropes to bind two [Primordial Moon](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Primordial_Moon "Primordial Moon") believers.[^26]
		- **Flame**: They are able to summon a colorless **Flame** that is able to burn away things while selectively leaving other things unharmed.[^40]
		- [Cattleya](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Cattleya "Cattleya") summoned a colorless **Flame** to burn away the mutated vines affected by [Earth Mother](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lilith "Lilith") 's remnant aura while sparing the sailors.
		- **Myopia**: A special **Warlock** spell that weakens the effect of the **Eye of Mystery Prying** to avoid sudden death, but it couldn't be completely sealed.[^41]
		- [Nikila](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Nikila "Nikila") pulled out a brown leather scroll from an inner pocket, softly chanting a word in [Hermes](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Languages "Languages") -" *Myopia*." The scroll immediately ignited with black flames, quickly turning to ash. The mysterious deep purple in [Nikila](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Nikila "Nikila") 's eyes faded into the black depths of his pupils.
		- With the flick of her hand, [Cattleya](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Cattleya "Cattleya") was able to make the drops of blood of [Cielf June](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Cielf_June "Cielf June") to float and fly to her hands.[^42]

## Sequence 4: Mysticologist

**Description**

The power of a **Mysticologist** stems from their extensive knowledge of **Mysticism**, making them extremely dangerous. In addition, among all **Sequence 4 Beyonders**, a **Mysticologist** is the **Sequence** that encounters danger the easiest. This is because they often see things they shouldn't see, hear things they shouldn't hear, and know secrets they shouldn't be aware of. Thus, they must learn to restrain their curiosity if they wish to live a long life.[^10]

**Individual Differences**

- **Mythical Creature Blood Spell**: Depending on the type of blood that was analyzed for their [**Sequence 4** Advancement Ritual](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hermit_Pathway/Advancement#Sequence_4:_Mysticologist "Hermit Pathway/Advancement"), a **Mysticologist's** strengths and abilities will vary.[^43]
	- For example, those who advance using the blood of a **[Snake of Mercury](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Wheel_of_Fortune_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_1:_Snake_of_Mercury_\(Giant_Serpent,_Snake_of_Fate,_Tail-Devouring_Serpent_of_Fate\) "Wheel of Fortune Pathway/Abilities")** will find it easier to become a **Clairvoyant** than others, and will also gain the **Spell Brief Luck**.
		- **Brief Luck**: This **Spell** gives the caster great amount of **Luck** for a short period of time.[^44]
			- Regardless of the terrifying **Lightning**, burning-white spear, or the slash of the **Black Knight**, none of them hit the target. [Cattleya](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Cattleya "Cattleya") seemed to be standing at the eye of the storm. No matter how dangerous the surroundings were, she was unaffected.
				- Those attacks and the attempts at control either narrowly passed her by or were canceled out by “friendly” forces. They were unable to achieve the desired effect, and they even helped her weaken the **Abyss** **Shackles** holding her down.

**New Abilities**

- **Mystical Re-enactment**: The most core ability of a **Mysticologist**, a manifestation of their **Divinity** and knowledge. They are able to draw power from the different mysticism knowledge they grasped, and through that, create all sorts of magic or witchcraft.[^45]
	- The exclusivity of the mystical knowledge affects the strength of the **Spell**; the less that certain mysticism knowledge was known by others and the less that knowledge has spread, the more powerful **Spells** of this category will be and vice-versa.
		- There can be a chance of retaliation when using high level knowledge.
			- **Mysticologists** can draw power from the battle between [The Fool and Error](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/The_Fool%27s_Gambit "The Fool's Gambit") as well as the battle between [The God of Combat and Evernight Goddess](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/God_of_Combat#Divine_War's_Conclusion "God of Combat"); however, if they dare utilize it, they shouldn't be afraid of retaliation.[^46]
						- If a **Mysticologist** used the **Mystical Re-enactment**; “ **The Feast of Betrayal** ”, once one of the three main leads of the “betrayal feast” sensed it, the **Mysticologist** would definitely die for obscure reasons. They would be unable to resist their death and die in an abnormally horrifying way, which is why [Cattelya](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Cattleya "Cattleya") only used this **Spell** with the blessing of [Mr. Fool](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti").[^45]
		- With their core ability of **Mystical Re-enactment**, **Beyonders** of the **Hermit** **Pathway** can potentially live longer and grow stronger through witnessing various mystical events, mastering much mystical knowledge, and creating powerful **Spells**.[^32]
		- Some examples of **Mystical Re-enactments** are as follows:
		- **The Feast of Betrayal**: it conjures up an image of the [Ancient Sun God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ancient_Sun_God "Ancient Sun God") getting devoured. It originates from the **Mysticism Knowledge** of the [Ancient Sun God's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ancient_Sun_God "Ancient Sun God") Death.
			- Its effect is to temporarily awaken or imbue items in the target's possession with sentience, causing them to betray the target.
				- **The Chess Game of Time**: This **Spell** slows down the actions of the target. It is like entering a region with a different flow of time.[^47]
		- **Mystical Re-enactments** that are created from the Fairy Tales of Earth's Original Civilizations are called **Fairy Tale Magic**. A few examples are:[^44] [^44] [^48] [^49] [^45]
		- **Avalon (Utopia)**: The **Utopia** is neither in reality nor in the [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World"). It is completely hidden somewhere, and only those who are invited or qualified can enter. Moreover, one must also master the corresponding sequence of numbers, 365 digits in total.
				- **Spear of Longinus**: A **Spell** that derives power from the knowledge of the [one true spear](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holy_Lange "wikipedia:Holy Lange") that pierced Jesus Christ in the Bible. It emits a strong sense of destruction and is able to lock against the targeted enemy, following them wherever they go.
			- To avoid the **Spear of Longinus**, a **[Secrets Sorcerer](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Door_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_4:_Secrets_Sorcerer "Door Pathway/Abilities")** would have to keep on "entering" into different **Concealed** spaces and hiding within illusory **Doors** over and over again as they were destroyed.
				- **The Ugly Duckling**: A **Spell** that derives power from the story of " [The Ugly Duckling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Ugly_Duckling "wikipedia:The Ugly Duckling") ". It allows the caster to temporarily use their **Mythical Creature Form** without having to worry about losing control but only for 10 seconds and once per day.
				- **Snow White**: A **Spell** made from the story " [Snow White](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snow_White "wikipedia:Snow White") ". It shares similar characteristics with **Divination** **Spells** and can counter **Divination** and clears the battlefield of the caster's tracks. It takes the form of a illusory, ancient **Mirror**.
			- It can also be used to increase the success rate and accuracy of **Prophetic** abilities.
				- **The Emperor's New Clothes**: A **Spell** derived from the story of *" [The Emperor's New Clothes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Emperor%27s_New_Clothes "wikipedia:The Emperor's New Clothes") "*. The caster becomes something that doesn't exist at all, allowing them to temporarily avoid any kind of attack once.[^50]
				- **Ariadne's Thread**: A **Spell** derived from the mythology of " *[Ariadne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ariadne "wikipedia:Ariadne")* ". This **Spell** creates an illusory ball of yarn that, when thrown into the void, leaves behind a bright colored thread which allows the caster to traverse the [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World") by following the thread.[^49] [^48]
			- It is also able to guide the user's direction.
				- **Jack and the Beanstalk**: A spell made from the story *" [Jack and the Beanstalk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_and_the_Beanstalk "wikipedia:Jack and the Beanstalk") "*. It can change the environment to a forest of long beanstalks and vines.[^51]
				- **The Little Mermaid**: A **Spell** invented from the story *" [The Little Mermaid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Little_Mermaid "wikipedia:The Little Mermaid") ".* With the **Spell**, the caster can turn **Invisible** and disappear into thin air.[^52] [^53]
				- **The Peach Blossoms' Source**: Inspired by " *[The Peach Blossom Spring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Peach_Blossom_Spring "wikipedia:The Peach Blossom Spring")* ", this **Spell** creates a barrier that is difficult to connect with the outside world.
				- **Sleeping Beauty**: A **Spell** derived from the story *" [Sleeping Beauty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sleeping_Beauty "wikipedia:Sleeping Beauty") "* that **Hypnotizes** the enemy and makes them tired and wanting to fall asleep.
				- **The Boy's Magic Horn**: From [The Boy's Magic Horn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Des_Knaben_Wunderhorn_\(Mahler\) "wikipedia:Des Knaben Wunderhorn (Mahler)"). This **Spell** condenses into a dark, ancient horn, carrying an extremely heavy and powerful connotation.
			- It's a horn of destruction and its low, resonant sound, combined with sonic waves, can shatter enemies and even **Space** itself.
				- **Hades' Invisible Helmet**: A **Spell** made from the myth of the " [Helm of Hades](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cap_of_invisibility "wikipedia:Cap of invisibility") ". It allows the caster to become **Invisible**.
				- **The Little Match Girl**: It conjures up a burning match, causing the enemy to hallucinate.
			- This **Spell** can cause a **[Secrets Sorcerer](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Door_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_4:_Secrets_Sorcerer "Door Pathway/Abilities")** to be influenced for a moment before breaking free.[^45]
				- **Cinderella**: It transforms the user's appearance, image, and temperament.

**Strengthened Abilities** [^43]

- **Eyes of Mystery Prying**: The is the second core ability of a **Mysticologist**, their **Eyes of Mystery Prying** can now detect more **Concealed** objects and attributes.
	- They gain better control over their eyes, allowing them to deactivate the power of **Mystery Prying**.
		- A **Mysticologist's** mere gaze will be able to pierce into the [Cosmos](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Cosmos "Cosmos") and the world barrier.
		- [Cattleya](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Cattleya "Cattleya") upon advancing could see the various celestial objects and [Outer Deities](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Outer_Deities "Outer Deities") that were occupying the area as well as "Their" gaze on earth.[^43]
		- They can plant their **Eyes of Mystery Prying** onto someone else's body to spy on them.
		- Once it goes into a **Concealed** state it will be very difficult to detect it through other means.[^54]
- **Stellar Guidance**: Their previous capabilities involving **Stars** has been enhanced.
	- **Star Pillar**: The **Star Pillar** that they can make can envelop the surrounding area, preventing even a **[Secrets Sorcerer](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Door_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_4:_Secrets_Sorcerer "Door Pathway/Abilities")** from **Teleporting** and leaving the enveloped area.[^55]

Mythical Creature Form [^50]

- As **Demigods** at **Sequence 4** who have obtained partial **Godhood**, they are able to manifest their incomplete **Mythical Creature** **Form** if they disregard any of the consequences.
	- Their **Mythical Creature** **Form** looks like an abstract "black blob" appearing to be in a higher spatial dimension covered in cracks and eyes which have clear blacks and whites, coldly scanning their surroundings, as though they were formations of multifarious knowledge.
		- In this **Mythical Creature Form** a **Mysticologist** will be able to induce a knowledge storm in the minds of their targets, disrupting and distracting their thoughts.
				- Certain **Spells** (like **The Ugly Duckling**) that a **Mysticologist** possesses can allow them to temporarily reveal their **Mythical Creature Form** without danger.

## Sequence 3: Clairvoyant

**Authorities** [^10]

- **Mystery Prying**: They have gained a partial authority over **Mystery Prying**.
	- Having truly come into contact with the authority of **Mystery Prying**, they can not only glimpse hidden secrets, knowledge, and existence, but also the secrets of **Destiny**.
		- **Clairvoyance** (**Prophecy**): **Prying** into the secrets of **Fate**, a **Clairvoyant** is able to foresee a choice's consequences through making a **Prophecy** of what would happen next, glimpse the best option for oneself, and even visualize past events.[^52] [^56]

## Sequence 0: Hermit

**Symbolism** [^10]

- **Knowledge** (**Information**): Derived from this symbolism, "They" hold the portion of authority that leans toward **Mysticism**.
	- This symbolism allows the [Gods](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Gods "Gods") of the two subordinate [Pathways](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways"), or even the **Sequence 1** or **Sequence 2**, to transform into a vast and complex torrent of **Information**, an illusory, overwhelming, and terrifying flood of **Information**.

**Authorities** [^10]

- **Mysticism Knowledge**: "They" are the embodiment of **Mysticism Knowledge**.[^84]
	- One of its core applications is to imbue **Digital Objects** with power, granting them genuine, distinct symbolic power in the true sense, though significantly weakened compared to the original.
		- **"0"** represents chaos, the unknown, what existed before the start. **"1"** represents the beginning, the [Oldest One](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Oldest_One "Oldest One") who made the world. **"2"** represents the world and various **Divinities** that were produced from the [Oldest One](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Oldest_One "Oldest One") 's body. **"3"** represents the contact between **Divinities** and material objects existing in the world to create all things.[^85]
			- By connecting **"1"** and **"0"**, end to end, "They" can form a digital world, which "They" can reduce to nothingness by changing **"1"** to **"0"**.[^84]
				- In terms of **Symbolism**, everything and anything is able to become **Numbers** with the corresponding occult implications and all of such **Numbers** possess **Spirituality**.[^86] [^84]
		- **Backup**: A subordinate authority of **Mysticism Knowledge**.
		- This allows for the **Revival** of **Information** after a **Backup**. For a specific **Backup** to receive the [Uniqueness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniquenesses "Uniquenesses"), it must be activated before its destruction. If not activated, [Uniqueness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniquenesses "Uniquenesses") will naturally transfer to the most recent **Backup**, following the proximity principle.[^84]
			- With repeated **Backups**, certain **Information** will undergo loss, degradation, **Errors**, and chaos in the process. The more times the overall **Information** is **Backed Up**, the higher the **Probability** of problems arising, and the greater the latent **Chaos**.
				- This is a fundamental rule of the entire world, a manifestation of **Error**, **Decay**, and increasing **Entropy**.
					- Self-destruction leads to more significant **Information** decay and **Chaos** than being killed, because it starts from the inside, the denial of self-awareness.
										- More **Revivals** may cause some hidden **Loopholes** and **Errors** to appear, which is what those in the [Error Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway "Error Pathway") can capitalize on.
		- After the [First Blasphemy Slate](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/First_Blasphemy_Slate "First Blasphemy Slate") emerged, the **Hermit Uniqueness** would master the knowledge of all potion formulas even without having seen the Slate.[^87]
		- "They" have the corresponding subordinate partial authorities of **Copy,** **Deletion**, **Revision**, etc.
- **Hiding**: "They" possess an authority of **Hiding**.
	- The **Hermit** can **Hide** the **Information** of others or "Themselves", making it impossible for other [True Deities](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Gods "Gods") to track or locate it.
		- Foreseeing danger, the [Hidden Sage](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hidden_Sage "Hidden Sage") **Concealed** "Itself" and the surrounding area.[^59]
- **Education**: This is the authority to grant **Knowledge** to others.
	- "They" are now able to automatically and directly infuse **Knowledge**, either positive or negative, into the minds of all **Beyonders** from the.[^88]
		- The [Hidden Sage](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hidden_Sage "Hidden Sage") routinely injects **Knowledge** into every **Beyonder** of the, causing those who fail to quickly digest and master it to experience 1 of 3 outcomes:
			1. Accept the modifications made by "Him".
						2. Tenaciously resist "His" influence.
						3. Lose control and/or die.
				- "Their" scream can be heard by every **Beyonder** from the when "They" die.[^73]
		- While the **Education** authority of the **[Paragon](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Paragon_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Paragon "Paragon Pathway/Abilities")** leans toward **Enlightenment**, the **Education** authority of the **Hermit** leans toward **Indoctrination**.[^89]
- **Mystery Prying**: No matter what the secret is, be it a kind of **Knowledge**, a part of " **Mysticism Knowledge** " or something else, it can be **Pried** into.
	- "They" are able to tell the specialties of "Their" enemies and the nature of their corresponding abilities and arrangements within the environment.[^84]
		- The [Hidden Sage](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hidden_Sage "Hidden Sage") was able to know the **Symbolisms** controlled by [The Fool](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") and the role the barrier **Sealing** the unknown realm played at a glance without further analysis.
