## Sequence 5: Reaper

### Potion Properties

* Fresh red blood with specks of black, iron-fillings-like matter floating in it, as though an iron weapon had shattered inside.^{[33]}^{[34]}
  * It has a pungent smell of blood.^{[33]}
  * There is an unfamiliar taste of rust.^{[33]}
  * And there's burning sensation that instantly fills their mouth and pierces their brain on consumption.^{[33]}

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> A finger, creating an unusually sharp, bone-blade emitting an icy glint. The holder needed to be cautious to avoid accidental injury.^{[35]} The bone-blade-shaped Beyonder characteristic.^{[36]}

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

  * Gray Demonic Wolf's Front Claws.^{[32]}
  * Forest Hunter's Tongue.^{[32]}

Or
  * A Reaper Beyonder Characteristic.

Supplementary Ingredients

  * Choose one of:
    * 80 milliliters of Gray Demonic Wolf's blood.^{[32]}
Or
    * The Reaper Beyonder Characteristic's predecessor's blood.
  * Choose one of:
    * 2 fangs of the Forest Hunter.^{[32]}
Or
    * The Reaper Beyonder Characteristic's predecessor's 2 teeth.
  * 10 drops of Colorful Bearded Horned Lizard Venom.^{[32]}
  * 10 drops of Hornbeam Essential Oils.^{[32]}

### Advancement Ritual

* Plan and execute a successful capture of a target with a Sequence higher than your own. Flaunt the completed conspiracy before them, and consume the potion as they witness your victory, filled with fear and despair.
    * Note: The increased number and higher Sequence of the captured targets and the greater their fear, regret, and anger, the more potent the ritual's effect.

## Sequence 4: Iron-blooded Knight

### Potion Properties

> Like molten iron, blazing red, high temperature, and vivid.^{[29]}^{[38]}

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> Usually fuses with an arm bone, forming an iron-black sword-shaped object, heavy, solid, and burning in its own flames.

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

  * A Magma Giant's core.^{[37]}
  * A Stone of Catastrophe.^{[37]}

Or
  * A Iron-blooded Knight Beyonder Characteristic.

Supplementary Ingredients

  * 80 milliliters of boiling magma.^{[37]}
  * 20 grams of flora powder corrupted by a Stone of Catastrophe.^{[37]}
  * 20 grams of soil soaked in soldier's blood from a large battlefield.^{[37]}
  * 1 acorn.^{[37]}

### Advancement Ritual

Form a team of at least thirty people, cultivate a deep friendship with them, make them strong one after another. The higher the strength and tacit understanding of the team, the better the effect of the ritual.^{[39]}

## Sequence 3: War Bishop

### Potion Properties

> Half red, half black, with light constantly flickering between the two.^{[29]}^{[41]}

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> A phantom core intertwined with red flames and black metal.^{[29]}

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

  * Heart of a Bipolar Centaur.^{[40]}
  * Core of a War Comet.^{[40]}

Or
  * A War Bishop Beyonder Characteristic.

Supplementary Ingredients

  * 100 milliliters of Bipolar Centaur Blood.^{[40]}
  * War Comet Fragments.^{[40]}
  * 1 Blood Tudor Iris.^{[40]}
  * 8 gram of Flame Giant Tree bark.^{[40]}

### Advancement Ritual

Lead your own team to defeat a powerful enemy force in a war.

  * Note 1: The imminent atmosphere of war will attract comets symbolizing war through the spirit world. They will streak across the night sky bringing revelations, but among such comets, those with corporeal substance that can enter the real world are limited in number.
  * Note 2: In the advancement ritual, the stronger the enemy force, the better the ritual's effect.^{[40]}

## Sequence 2: Weather Warlock

### Potion Properties

> A formed, purplish liquid that burns.^{[43]}^{[44]}

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> A palm-sized, crystalline and porous pillar. The various holes in the pillar contained mists, hall, rainwater, lightning, and other meteorological phenomena, sometimes diffusing outwards. sometimes receding back inside.^{[45]}

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

  * Skull of a Weather Giant^{[42]}
  * Core of a Mist Jellyfish^{[42]}

Or
  * A Weather Warlock Beyonder Characteristic.

Supplementary Ingredients

  * 30 milliliters of blood each from three deceased individuals who died in different extreme weather conditions.^{[42]}
  * 500 strands of a Weather Giant's hair.
  * Five Mist Jellyfish Crystals.^{[42]}
    * Note: After the Fourth Epoch, Weather Giants and Mist Jellyfish no longer exist. Supplementary materials can substituted with the corresponding parts from a Weather Warlock.
  * A segment of a tree branch struck by lightning.^{[42]}
  * 20 milliliters of the first snow melt of a region each year.^{[42]}

### Advancement Ritual

Forcefully change the weather of a region without external aid in a very short time.^{[42]}

## Sequence 1: Conqueror

### Potion Properties

> A dazzling, blazing purple sphere of light in the shape of a crown.^{[29]}^{[46]}

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> A strange crown covered in rust and blood. A blood-stained iron crown, formed by the broken skull in hand.^{[47]}^{[48]}

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

  * A Conqueror Beyonder Characteristic

Supplementary Ingredients

  * 90 milliliters of blood from a Conqueror or contaminated liquid from a Conqueror Beyonder Characteristic.^{[29]}
  * A genuine crown.^{[29]}
  * A drop of fingertip blood from each member of one's own team.^{[29]}
  * Nine heads of powerful enemies (at least Sequence 4 in status).^{[29]}

### Advancement Ritual

Conquer a nation with a population of more than 30 million inhabitants, making all beings there fear, obey, and revere you.^{[29]}
