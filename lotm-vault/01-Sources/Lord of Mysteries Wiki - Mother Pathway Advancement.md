## Sequence 3: Pallbearer

### Potion Properties

> A dark liquid concealing numerous illusory figures.^{[1]}

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> A shadowy, unusually indistinct figure that appears like it is weightless^{[1]}

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

  * Heart of the Dark Treant.^{[1]}
  * Skin of the Harvest Giant Elder.^{[1]}

Or
  * A Pallbearer Beyonder Characteristic.

Supplementary Ingredients

  * 200 milliliters of the blood of the Harvest Giant Elder.^{[1]}
  * One fruit of the Dark Treant.^{[1]}
  * One piece of rotten plank from a coffin in a tomb.^{[1]}
  * One type of food already used for the sacrifice (a bouquet of flowers can also be used instead).^{[1]}

### Advancement Ritual

* Personally bury a Sequence 3 of the Darkness, Death, or Twilight Giant pathways, witnessing their entire journey from birth to death and return to the earth.^{[1]}
    * Or, bury a Mythical Creature of one of these three pathways, thus not needing to witness their Birth and Death.^{[1]}

## Sequence 2: Desolate Matriarch

### Potion Properties

> A brownish-yellow liquid resembling the residue of rotting flesh, causing abnormal nausea.^{[1]}

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> A clump of brown soil covered with grass, grains, and vines.^{[1]}

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

  * Reproductive organs of a Child of Harvest.^{[1]}

Or
  * Heart of a Daughter of Desolation. ^{[1]}

Or
  * A Desolate Matriarch Beyonder Characteristic.

Supplementary Ingredients

  * 300 milliliters of blood from a Child of Harvest or Daughter of Desolation (other equivalents are also acceptable).^{[1]}
  * 10,000 souls of creatures that died of starvation.^{[1]}
  * One type of a desertified oasis remnant.^{[1]}
  * A section of a creature's limb with vigorous vitality and extremely strong self-healing ability (such as Sanguines).^{[1]}

### Advancement Ritual

Conceive, reproduce, and nurture an entire race.

## Sequence 1: Naturewalker

### Potion Properties

> A rich, grassy liquid brimming with vitality.^{[1]}

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> Crystal clusters composed of five colors: red, green, gold, black, and brown.^{[1]}

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

  * A Naturewalker Beyonder Characteristic

Supplementary Ingredients

  * 100 grams of soil blessed by one's own mother (note: either a real mother or a mystical mother is acceptable).^{[1]}
  * One spark of fire from the earth's core.^{[1]}
  * One branch of a thousand-year-old oak tree.^{[1]}
  * 10 drops of Ichor.^{[1]}
  * 100 grams of newly born gold.^{[1]}

### Advancement Ritual

Find a way to create a world that exists only as a concept, without nature or elements, and completely encompass it, then use it to enter the Astral World and consume the potion.^{[1]}
