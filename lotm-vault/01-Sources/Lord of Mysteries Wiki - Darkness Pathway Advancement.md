## Sequence 4: Nightwatcher

### Potion Properties

> Dark liquid immersed with countless black hairs.^{[2]}

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> Black "candle" with brain-like folds and numerous vascular structures.^{[2]}

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

  * Dark Brain.^{[1]}
  * Malignant Moon Guardian's Heart.^{[1]}

Or
  * A Nightwatcher Beyonder Characteristic.

Supplementary Ingredients

  * 90 milliliters of Malignant Moon Guardian's Blood.^{[1]}
  * 1 Dark Brain's Contaminant.^{[1]}
  * 1 Bonfire maintained over 10 years.^{[1]}
  * 10 drops of Crimson Tree Sap.^{[1]}

### Advancement Ritual

Collect bloodline characteristics of True Deities' descendants and use them to draw a soul-soothing magic circle.

  * Note 1: Different Deity bloodlines will grant distinct Nightwatcher abilities.^{[1]}
  * Note 2: Pathway-switchers must use bloodlines from one of the 3 corresponding Deities of the Eternal Darkness group.^{[1]}
  * Note 3: Both descendants born after becoming a god and before becoming a god work. Even the bloodline from before becoming a god will have special properties.^{[14]}

Or

Supplementary Ingredients

  * 1 portion of Spirit World intelligence from a hidden location.^{[1]}
  * 1 Feather of a Feathered Serpent.^{[1]}
  * 10 drops of Giant's Blood (Twilight Giant Pathway).^{[1]}

### Advancement Ritual

* Sever all social ties and external influences.^{[1]}
  * Avoid influencing others or being influenced by others.^{[1]}
  * Live isolated in darkness and silence for 3 years without going mad.^{[1]}
    * Note 1: Worms of Time can normally use a Bug whenever there are time limits, however, the passage of time cannot be directly accelerated to reduce the real time required because this invalidates the second point of the ritual.^{[1]}
    * Note 2: Ritual assistance would need to come from the Fool Pathway or the Wheel of Fortune Pathway.^{[1]}

## Sequence 0: Darkness

### Potion Properties

> Soft, dreamlike and serene black gauze.^{[2]}

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

  * The Darkness Uniqueness.
  * Three Knight of Misfortune Beyonder Characteristics.

Supplementary Ingredients

  * 1 Moon or moon-like celestial body (mystically equivalent).^{[1]}
    * Note 1: The reason why the Moon is a supplementary ingredient for the Darkness is because of the "Light within Darkness" connection.^{[15]}
    * Note 2: "They" can either physically devour a real moon, or "They" can just use conceptual guidance to integrate it into the potion.^{[16]}^{[17]}

### Advancement Ritual

* Induce eternal slumber upon either:
    * A Uniqueness
    * A Divine Kingdom
    * A True Deity
  * The higher the difficulty of the ritual, the better the effects.^{[1]}
