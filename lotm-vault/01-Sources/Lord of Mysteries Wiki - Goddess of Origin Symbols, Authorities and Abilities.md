is the title of the [Great Old One](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Old_One "Great Old One") of the [Mother](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Pathway) and [Moon](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Moon_Pathway) [Pathways](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways").

The [Sefirah](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirah "Sefirah") and [Pathways](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways") under this [Great Old One](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Old_One "Great Old One") originally belonged to the [Outer Deity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Outer_Deity "Outer Deity") [Mother Goddess of Depravity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Goddess_of_Depravity). "She" was once one of the three [Pillars](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pillars "Pillars") before losing the [Brood Hive](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Brood_Hive) and its corresponding [Pathways](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways") to Earth.

Right after the separation, the [Brood Hive](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Brood_Hive) was incomplete, but the [Sefirot](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirot "Sefirot") have the ability to mend themselves. The present [Mother Goddess of Depravity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Goddess_of_Depravity "Mother Goddess of Depravity") 's [Sefirah](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirah "Sefirah") isn’t incomplete, and the [Brood Hive](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Brood_Hive) can also create a [Great Old One](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Old_One "Great Old One") itself. However, the [Mother Goddess of Depravity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Goddess_of_Depravity "Mother Goddess of Depravity") can only use a portion of the [Pillar](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pillar "Pillar") "She" symbolizes, unless "She" has fused with the [Brood Hive](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Brood_Hive) again.[^1]

## Symbols, Authorities & Abilities

"She" possess full **Authority** over the [Mother](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Pathway) and [Moon](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Moon_Pathway) [Pathways](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways") and the corresponding **Symbols**, which include but are not limited to the following:

- [Mother](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Pathway): Authorities over **Mother**, **Earth**, **Nature**, **Death**, and **Life**.
- [Moon](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Moon_Pathway): Authorities over **Summoning**, **Beauty**, **Spirituality**, **Life**, **Darkness**, and **Bizarreness** with the symbol of the **Moon**.

The **Symbols**, **Authorities**, and **Abilities** "She" possesses and displays at the [Above the Sequence](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Above_the_Sequence "Above the Sequence") level include:

- **Goddess of Origin**: From the **Origin** it comes, to the **Origin** it returns.[^2]
- **Brood Hive of Filth**: "She" holds the corresponding symbolism of **Brood Hive of Filth**.**[^2]**
- **Death**: "She" holds the symbolism of **Death**, corresponding to **Goddess of Origin** and **Brood Hive of Filth**.
	- Returning to the **Origin** is **Death**, while the **Brood Hive of Filth** part of this symbolism points towards **Pollution**, **Loss of Control**, and **Bodily** **Mutation**.**[^2]**
