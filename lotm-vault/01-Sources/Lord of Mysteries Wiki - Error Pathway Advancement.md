# Lord of Mysteries Wiki - Error Pathway Advancement

Community-maintained potion/advancement inventory for the Error Pathway (Marauder through Error). Used to record potion properties, Beyonder Characteristic appearance, ingredient formulas, and advancement rituals, cross-checking Cuttlefish's WeChat formula posts.

## Classification

- Provenance: unofficial
- Reliability: medium

## Summary

Fandom compilation of the Error Pathway's per-Sequence potion data: potion properties (appearance/taste/effect on drinking), Beyonder Characteristic appearance, main and supplementary potion ingredients, and (for Sequences with published rituals) the advancement ritual required to reach that Sequence. Most Sequences currently show "No data" for Ritual Essence, Consumption Backlash, and Acting Method.

## Extracted facts

- Sequence 9 Marauder: deep blue-black, bitter potion; Beyonder Characteristic is a translucent black severed finger. No advancement ritual recorded.
- Sequence 8 Swindler: crimson-foamed, dreamlike potion; Characteristic is a red transparent lip-shaped crystal. No advancement ritual recorded.
- Sequence 7 Cryptologist: misty, glimmering potion causing scalp-piercing pain; Characteristic is a colorless mist-emitting crystal. No advancement ritual recorded.
- Sequence 6 Prometheus: red-yellow potion with illusory swirling flames; Characteristic is a miniature unlit crystal torch. No advancement ritual recorded.
- Sequence 5 Dream Stealer: dark, murky, lethally suffocating potion; Characteristic is a small pale infant's hand. Advancement ritual: become a key antagonist or supporting figure in the unconscious dreams of at least 30 people, regardless of dream duration, as long as it is happening at the moment of drinking the potion.
- Sequence 4 Parasite: yellow-green insect-derived potion with floating carcasses; failed promotion (without death or loss of control) turns the drinker into a carrier Parasitized by the potion. Characteristic is a diamond with translucent worms inside. Advancement ritual: convince nine targets to willingly offer all they possess to sustain the drinker.
- Sequence 3 Mentor of Deceit: emerald, deceptively ordinary potion causing bodily/spiritual fragmentation; Characteristic is a colorless fungal curtain-like drape. Advancement ritual: without violence, exploit rule loopholes to temporarily collapse order in a region no smaller than a village (the smallest entity able to form a Spirit World projection).
- Sequence 2 Trojan Horse of Destiny: mercury-colored potion with looping worm-shadow symbols, evoking drowning in the River of Fate; Characteristic is a multi-faceted mirror crystal reflecting different identities. Advancement ritual: assume another's identity through non-Beyonder disguise/deception and earn their social circle's trust until that identity dies naturally (exceptions for someone already near death or with under three years to live); higher-tier targets shorten the required duration to a minimum of six months.
- Sequence 1 Worm of Time: ethereal, shimmering, tasteless cyan-black potion that subjects the drinker to erosion/cutting by time; Characteristic is a crystalline pillar housing 12-segmented Worms of Time, freezing everything nearby. Advancement ritual: plunge a city into temporal chaos for at least seven days.
- Sequence 0 Error: potion is formed from overlapping layers of segmented worms; main ingredients are three Worm of Time Beyonder Characteristics plus three Beacons Extracted from the River of Fate. Advancement ritual: replace someone during their own Sequence 0 apotheosis ritual (not necessarily the exact potion-drinking moment); does not require becoming a Dual God of Error and the target's Pathway.
