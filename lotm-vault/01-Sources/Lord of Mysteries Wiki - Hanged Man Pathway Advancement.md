## Sequence 4: Black Knight

### Potion Properties

> Viscous liquid resembling a black gemstone; ingestion causes the body to collapse anew.^{[1]}

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> A segment of translucent black hand bone.^{[1]}

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

  * Remnant Curtain of a Shadow Knight.^{[1]}
  * Flesh and Blood Condensation of a Griegel(Griegel is a Beyonder creature also known as the Crimson Giant).^{[1]}

Or
  * A Black Knight Beyonder Characteristic.

Supplementary Ingredients

  * Shadow Traces of a Griegel.^{[1]}
  * Black Liquid of a Shadow Knight.^{[1]}
  * 1 drop of Blood from Each Ritual Participant.^{[1]}
  * 10 drops of Lemon balm Essential Oil.^{[1]}

### Advancement Ritual

* Induce the complete degeneration of over 10,000 individuals without using violence.
    * The higher the sequence of the degenerate individuals, the better the ritual effect.
  * The more concentrated the transformation from normalcy to degeneracy in time, the more effective the ritual becomes.^{[1]}

## Sequence 0: Hanged Man

### Potion Properties

> A shadow of absolute darkness that isolates all surrounding light.^{[1]}

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

  * The Hanged Man Uniqueness.
  * Three Dark Angel Beyonder Characteristics.

Supplementary Ingredients

  * The Shadow World.^{[1]}

### Advancement Ritual

* Walk upon the Chaos Sea, dissolve one's shadow into it while ensuring:
    * The body does not sink
    * The mind does not fall
  * Meeting the requirement entails the body not fully sinking and the spirit not fully descending into degeneracy.^{[1]}
