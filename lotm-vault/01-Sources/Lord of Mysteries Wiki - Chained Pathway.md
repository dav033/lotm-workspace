## Overview

The **Chained Pathway** has different types of mutant abilities, but they're also cursed. They can summon zombies, jump through things with reflections, resist bullets with powerful defence, possess others, control ghosts, cast death-related spells, regenerate and heal themselves, and sacrifice rationality for more power. They have high strength, speed, and defence, but have some difficulty in controlling themselves. They also lose power or control during the full moon.

At High-Sequences, they have the ability to bind their foes, control all inanimate objects within a specific radius, employ dreadful curses and curse others through all connections, and transform into terrifying, evil, distorted, chaotic Abominations that are filled by pure and extreme madness.

Since the **Chained Pathway** symbolises **Binding** / **Restraint** and **Unity**, from the [Mid-Second Epoch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Second_Epoch "Second Epoch") onwards, the main Ingredients of each sequence, starting from **Sequence 7**, have essentially merged into one. There have been almost no more instances of them splitting.[^4]

The [Beyonder Characteristics](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristics "Beyonder Characteristics") of this pathway originated from the [Outer Deity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Outer_Deity "Outer Deity") [Mother Tree of Desire](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Tree_of_Desire).[^5]

The [Above the Sequence](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Above_the_Sequence "Above the Sequence") of the **Chained Pathway** is the [Father of Devils](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Father_of_Devils), and the corresponding [Sefirah](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirah "Sefirah") is the [Tenebrous World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tenebrous_World).

## Corresponding Tarot Card

Further information: [Tarot Cards](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tarot_Cards "Tarot Cards")

The corresponding tarot card of the **Chained Pathway** is **Temperance**.[^8]

**Temperance (XIV)** is the number fourteenth of Major Arcana. The Temperance card is a reference to the soul. It signifies balance, peace, patience, and moderation. One should take the middle road, avoid extremes, and maintain a sense of calm.[^9]

## Mythical Creature Form

An invisible collection of various curses. Therefore, the **Mythical Creature Form** manifested by the **Chained Pathway** is essentially the vessel that binds this aggregate, and is dependent on the self-awareness and choices made during "Their" advancements to [Sequence 4: Puppet](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chained_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_4:_Puppet) and [Sequence 2 Ancient Bane](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chained_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_2:_Ancient_Bane), which bind and fixes itself into various curses, such as oil trees, dolls, mummies, etc.[^10] [^11]

- At [Sequence 4](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chained_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_4:_Puppet), an embalmed mummy is one type of anchor, but it's not strictly necessary to use a demigod-level mummy, as any item with a similar function and the same rank can serve the purpose. The [Rose School of Thought](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Rose_School_of_Thought "Rose School of Thought") 's [Temperance Faction](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Rose_School_of_Thought#Temperance_Faction "Rose School of Thought") traditionally uses mummies for their advancement, because this practice is tied to the imagery and history of the [Chained God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chained_God "Chained God") and has even influenced the burial customs of the [Highlands Kingdom](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Highlands_Kingdom "Highlands Kingdom").
- Similarly, the advancement to [Ancient Bane](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chained_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_2:_Ancient_Bane) requires finding an ancient, evil item capable of bearing [angelic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Angels "Angels") power. This item becomes the core of the final **Mythical Creature Form**.

[Innate Mythical Creatures](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Innate_Mythical_Creatures "Innate Mythical Creatures") like [Suah](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Suah "Suah") and [Tirié](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tiri%C3%A9 "Tirié"), who have not completed the corresponding Sequence advancements, exhibit forms that are essentially amplifications of "Their" [Beyonder Characteristics](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristics "Beyonder Characteristics").[^11]

## Neighboring and Compatible Pathways

**Neighbouring Pathways** to the **Chained Pathway** include:

- Other Pathways under the [Father of Devils](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Father_of_Devils):
	- [Abyss](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Abyss_Pathway)
- Pathways under the [Mother Tree of Desire](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Tree_of_Desire):[^1]
	- [Patriarch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Patriarch_Pathway)

**Compatible Pathways** to the **Chained Pathway** include, but might not be limited to, the following:

- Pathways under the [Inextinguishable Ravings](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Inextinguishable_Ravings):[^2]
	- [Everlasting](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Everlasting_Pathway)
