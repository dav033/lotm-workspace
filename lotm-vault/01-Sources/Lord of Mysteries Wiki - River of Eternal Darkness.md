The **River of Eternal Darkness** (永暗之河, *Yǒngàn Zhī Hé*, lit. 'River of Perpetual Darkness') is one of the nine [Sefirot](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirot "Sefirot") on Earth. It corresponds to the [Above the Sequence](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Above_the_Sequence "Above the Sequence") [Eternal Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Eternal_Darkness).

The [Fourth Pillar](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Pillar "Fourth Pillar") would be formed by the convergence of the [City of Calamity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/City_of_Calamity "City of Calamity") and the **River of Eternal Darkness**.[^1] [^2]

## Names

**[River Styx](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Styx "wikipedia:Styx")** (冥河, *Míng hé*, lit. 'Underworld River'): The distributary of the **River of Eternal Darkness** that emerged in the [Underworld](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Underworld "Underworld").[^3]

**[Yellow Springs](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Western_Continent/Glossary#Yellow_Springs "Western Continent/Glossary")** (黄泉, *Huángquán*): The [Western Continent](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Western_Continent "Western Continent") 's name of the **River of Eternal Darkness**.[^4] [^5]

## Appearance

A broad, straight, dark, and colorless river flows silently, heralding the stillness of fate and the proclamation of death. Within the illusory dark waters, figures dressed like the [Haoli](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Haoli "Haoli") Sect Master appeared, floating and sinking.

This was a river that did not Loop. It always moved forward.

Even death itself would dissipate within its currents.[^6] [^7]

## History

After the [Original Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Original_Creator "Original Creator") awakened at the end of the [Pre-Epoch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pre-Epoch "Pre-Epoch"), "His" two dominant split entities - the [Primordial God Almighty](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Primordial_God_Almighty "Primordial God Almighty") and the [Celestial Worthy of Heaven and Earth for Blessings](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Celestial_Worthy_of_Heaven_and_Earth_for_Blessings "Celestial Worthy of Heaven and Earth for Blessings") - leveraged "Their" long-prepared ancient schemes across the times of the [Old Earth](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pre-Epoch "Pre-Epoch") to gradually gain advantages in "Their" fight against the other [Sefirot](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirot "Sefirot"). The two [Pillars](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pillars "Pillars") eventually defeated the other [Sefirot](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirot "Sefirot") and shattered "Their" wills (excluding the [Brood Hive](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Brood_Hive "Brood Hive") and the [Key of Light](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Key_of_Light_\(Sefirah\) "Key of Light (Sefirah)")),[^8] making those wills take a very long time to recover and awaken again.[^9]

During the early-mid stages of the [First Epoch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/First_Epoch "First Epoch"), the [Celestial Worthy of Heaven and Earth for Blessings](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Celestial_Worthy_of_Heaven_and_Earth_for_Blessings "Celestial Worthy of Heaven and Earth for Blessings") accommodated the **River of Eternal Darkness**. This left "His" consciousness and psyche in the [Sefirah](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirah "Sefirah") after "His" death. As a result, [Beyonders](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonders "Beyonders") on these Pathways would need to contend with a mixture of the [Celestial Worthy](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Celestial_Worthy "Celestial Worthy") 's [Godhood](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Godhood "Godhood").[^10]

After the [Primordial God Almighty](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Primordial_God_Almighty "Primordial God Almighty") and the [Celestial Worthy](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Celestial_Worthy "Celestial Worthy") 's death at the hands of each other, the **River of Eternal Darkness** was sealed in the [Western Continent](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Western_Continent "Western Continent") by the [Celestial Worthy](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Celestial_Worthy "Celestial Worthy") along with all other [Sefirot](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirot "Sefirot") on Earth, except the [Sefirah Castle](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirah_Castle "Sefirah Castle") and the [Chaos Sea](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chaos_Sea "Chaos Sea").[^11]

In the [Western Continent](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Western_Continent "Western Continent"), the **River of Eternal Darkness** became the [Sefirah](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirah "Sefirah") of the [Haoli Lineage](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Haoli_Lineage "Haoli Lineage").[^12] After the fusion and influence of generations of [Dao Integrators](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Dao_Integrators "Dao Integrators") from the [Haoli Lineage](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Haoli_Lineage "Haoli Lineage"), the **River of Eternal Darkness** became **Semi-Activated**.

As time went by, the seal of the [Western Continent](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Western_Continent "Western Continent") gradually weakened. The [Sefirot](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirot "Sefirot") would instinctively or consciously corrode the [Uniqueness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniqueness "Uniqueness") and High-Sequence [Beyonder Characteristics](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristics "Beyonder Characteristics") within their symbolic range to influence the outside world. The first to seep out energy was the **River of Eternal Darkness**. In the [Second Epoch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Second_Epoch "Second Epoch"), it formed a distributary through the [Phoenix Ancestor Gregrace](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Phoenix_Ancestor_Gregrace "Phoenix Ancestor Gregrace"). The **River Styx** is a projection of the **River of Eternal Darkness** 's tributary within the [Underworld](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Underworld "Underworld").[^13] "She" later hid the clues concerning the **River of Eternal Darkness** in "Her" [Divine Kingdom](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Divine_Kingdoms "Divine Kingdoms"), [Calderón City](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Calder%C3%B3n_City "Calderón City").[^14]

In the [Fourth Epoch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Epoch "Fourth Epoch"), traces of more [Sefirot](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirot "Sefirot") began to appear in the outside world. This is an important factor hidden behind many of the anomalies in this era.[^11]

After the [War of the Four Emperors](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/War_of_the_Four_Emperors "War of the Four Emperors"), [Salinger](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Salinger "Salinger") forcefully tried to use a distributary of the **River of Eternal Darkness** to accommodate the [Red Priest](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Priest_Pathway) [Uniqueness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniqueness "Uniqueness") to create a new path to become a [Great Old One](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Old_One "Great Old One"). "He" became a [Half Great Old One](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Half_Great_Old_One "Half Great Old One"), but also went mad.[^15] [^11] Before "He" fell, "He" left "His" foreshadowing of resurrection in the **River of Eternal Darkness**.[^16]

Sometime during the [Fourth Epoch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Epoch "Fourth Epoch") or [Fifth Epoch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fifth_Epoch "Fifth Epoch"), another distributary formed in [Underground Trier](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Underground_Trier "Underground Trier") in the form of the **Samaritan Women's Spring**. Its river water had been used by subsequent Beyonders of the [Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Darkness_Pathway "Darkness Pathway"), [Death](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway "Death Pathway") and [Twilight Giant](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Twilight_Giant_Pathway "Twilight Giant Pathway") Pathways to conduct [Ritualistic Magic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ritualistic_Magic "Ritualistic Magic") and Advancement Rituals.[^17] [^18] In 1358 of the Fifth Epoch, [Hela](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hela "Hela") and [Audrey Hall](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Audrey_Hall "Audrey Hall") obtained a bottle of distributary water respectively due to [Lumian Lee](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee") 's [Tarot Club](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tarot_Club "Tarot Club") mission.[^19] When following [Harrison](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Harrison "Harrison"), [Franca Roland](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Franca_Roland "Franca Roland") obtained some water from the Spring later at [Mr. Star](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Leonard_Mitchell "Leonard Mitchell") 's behest.[^20] Another distributary was encountered in [Tizamo Town](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tizamo_Town "Tizamo Town") 's [Dream Festival](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Dream_Festival "Dream Festival") at a black tomb sealing [Naboredisley](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Naboredisley "Naboredisley").[^21]

Some time before [The Day of Many Disasters](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/The_Day_of_Many_Disasters "The Day of Many Disasters"), the [Evernight Goddess](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Evernight_Goddess "Evernight Goddess") had established a connection with the source **River of Eternal Darkness** through working with [Amandina](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Amandina "Amandina"), the [Underworld Daoist](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Underworld_Daoist "Underworld Daoist") disciple. "She" was able to manifest it to kill the [Eternal Blazing Sun](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Eternal_Blazing_Sun "Eternal Blazing Sun") before "He" was taken over by the [Primordial God Almighty](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Primordial_God_Almighty "Primordial God Almighty").[^22]

Prior to [The Apocalypse](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/The_Apocalypse "The Apocalypse"), the [Evernight Goddess](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Evernight_Goddess "Evernight Goddess") had preemptively gained partial control of the **River of Eternal Darkness**, satisfying the condition necessary to accommodate the Sefirah. During [The Apocalypse](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/The_Apocalypse "The Apocalypse"), the seal over the [Western Continent](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Western_Continent "Western Continent") was lifted. After the [Evernight Goddess](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Evernight_Goddess "Evernight Goddess") made an oath on the **River of Eternal Darkness**, the [Underworld Daoist](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Underworld_Daoist "Underworld Daoist") transferred the position of the [Haoli](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Haoli "Haoli") Sect Master to "Her", allowing "Her" to fully accommodate the Sefirah and become the [Eternal Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Eternal_Darkness) to fight against the [Outer Deities](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Outer_Deities "Outer Deities").[^12]

## Power & Abilities

The **River of Eternal Darkness** is the symbol of *death, repose, destination, endpoint, and darkness...etc.*.

It records in the form of **Death Imprints** the **Deaths** and **Revivals** of all beings. Those **Death Imprints** would take the form of those **Dead** and **Revived** beings, being present in the river. As such, every time someone dies, even if they come back to life, they will leave an imprint in the **River of Eternal Darkness**. Once the number of deaths reaches a certain level, their true bodies might be attracted to the **River of Eternal Darkness** one day, ending up as one of the figures that wander around there for an eternity.[^23] If the original self is ever near their corresponding **Death Imprints**, those **Death Imprints** will try to drag them into the **River of Eternal Darkness.**[^24] [^25]

The distributaries show changing colors between dark-black and pale-white.[^26]

For the [Twilight Giant](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Twilight_Giant_Pathway), [Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Darkness_Pathway), and [Death](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway) pathways, the river water of the **River of Eternal Darkness** serves diverse purposes determined by its utilization, rituals, and compatibility. It can be used for temporarily clearing memories, healing essential damage to the spirit, improving one's spiritual perception, becoming an ingredient for important rituals, and for unlocking various abilities, among other uses. It can also be used by Beyonders of any pathway to erase all memories and feelings, becoming an entirely new person.[^17]

The River also has an oath binding-contract property "Oath of the River Styx" that even a [Sequence 0](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sequence_0 "Sequence 0") cannot undo, even after said [Sequence 0](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sequence_0 "Sequence 0") had accommodated the [Sefirah](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirah "Sefirah") and ascended to a [Great Old One](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Old_One "Great Old One").[^12]

Among the three [Pathways](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways") of the **River of Eternal Darkness**, [Death](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway) is closest to its essence, [Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Darkness_Pathway) is the most similar to its exterior, and [Twilight Giant](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Twilight_Giant_Pathway) can slightly utilize it. This is one reason the [Giant King](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Aurmir "Aurmir") could contend with the [Dragon of Imagination](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ankewelt "Ankewelt").[^27]
