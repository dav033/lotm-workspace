The **Mother Tree of Desire** is one of the [Outer Deities](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Outer_Deities "Outer Deities") from the [Cosmos](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Cosmos "Cosmos") that attempted to invade Earth. "She" is the second child of the [Original Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Original_Creator "Original Creator"), and is one of "His" three direct children along with the [Mother Goddess of Depravity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Goddess_of_Depravity "Mother Goddess of Depravity") and the [Son of Chaos](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Son_of_Chaos "Son of Chaos").[^1] [^2]

"She" was the original owner of the [Abyss](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Abyss_Pathway "Abyss Pathway") and [Chained](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chained_Pathway "Chained Pathway") Pathways. "She" is also the current owner of the [Patriarch Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Patriarch_Pathway "Patriarch Pathway").

Even though "失心"'s literal translation is "lost heart", the term's actual meaning is "lost mind" (for example, "失心疯" means "madness" or "insanity"). Thus, a more correct translation of "失心之神" is "God of Lost Minds" instead of "Heartless God" from the official Webnovel English translation.

## Symbols, Authorities & Abilities

"She" possesses full **Authority** over the [Patriarch Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Patriarch_Pathway "Patriarch Pathway") and partial **Authority** over the [Abyss](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Abyss_Pathway "Abyss Pathway") and [Chained](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chained_Pathway "Chained Pathway") [Pathways](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways") (through accommodating the [Tenebrous World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tenebrous_World "Tenebrous World") but without the [Uniquenesses](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniquenesses "Uniquenesses")) and the corresponding **Symbols**, which include but are not limited to the following:

- [Abyss](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Abyss_Pathway "Abyss Pathway"): Partial authorities over **Degeneration**, **Filth**, **Corruption**, **Desire**, **Curses**, and **Deviants**, and the symbol of **Abyss of the Heart/Mind**. Partial *via* the [Tenebrous World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tenebrous_World "Tenebrous World").
- [Chained](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chained_Pathway "Chained Pathway"): Authorities over **Curses**, **Deviants**, **Binding**, and **Objects**, and the symbol of **Binding**. Partial *via* the [Tenebrous World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tenebrous_World "Tenebrous World").
- [Patriarch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Patriarch_Pathway "Patriarch Pathway"): Authorities over **Love**, **Envy**, **Reason**, and **Sentient Beings**, and Symbol of **Desires**.
	- This Pathway is a mix of two different Pathways:[^5]
		- The Pathway of **Desires** (**Reasons**).
				- The Pathway of **Blathering**, the corresponding authorities are **Raving**, **Madness** and **Propagation**.[^6] The Sequence 0 name is [Patriarch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Patriarch_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Patriarch "Patriarch Pathway/Abilities").[^7]

The **Symbols**, **Authorities**, and **Abilities** "She" possesses and displays at the [Above the Sequence](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Above_the_Sequence "Above the Sequence") level include:

- **Chaos**: As one of the three Children of The [Original Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Original_Creator "Original Creator"), "She" symbolizes **Chaos**. Even though "She" doesn't have a corresponding **Sequence 0** to denote this **Chaos**, "She" is the [Father of Devils](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Father_of_Devils "Father of Devils") and the **Mother Tree of Desire**, which makes "Her" symbolically, inherently **Chaotic**.[^8]
	- **Father of Devils** (Former): "She" was the original [Father of Devils](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Father_of_Devils "Father of Devils") before "She" lost the [Tenebrous World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tenebrous_World "Tenebrous World") and the two corresponding Pathways when the [Original Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Original_Creator "Original Creator") awakened.
		- Because of "Her" connection, "She" held corruptive influence over "Her" former two pathways after the [Astral Barrier](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Astral_Barrier "Astral Barrier") had sufficiently weakened.
				- After [The Apocalypse](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/The_Apocalypse "The Apocalypse"), "She" is re-accommodating the [Tenebrous World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tenebrous_World "Tenebrous World") but without the [Chained Uniqueness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniquenesses#Chained_Pathway "Uniquenesses"), [Abyss Uniqueness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniquenesses#Abyss_Pathway "Uniquenesses"), and their corresponding [Beyonder Characteristics](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristics "Beyonder Characteristics").[^9]
		- **Mother Tree of Desires**: "She" is the **Mother Tree of Desires** and carries the corresponding symbols.
- **Perpetual Blatherer**: "She" is the initiator of **Reason** and endless **Desires**.[^1] [^10]
	- "She" can awaken the survival instincts of all sentient beings in a region, including other [Great Old Ones](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Old_Ones "Great Old Ones"), which can cause "Them" to flee.[^11]
		- "She" can manipulate the **Desires** within the heart of all creatures.[^12]
		- "She" can rely on the symbolism of **Desire** to locate the true body of the [Lord of Mysteries](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lord_of_Mysteries "Lord of Mysteries").[^12]
		- With "Her" birth came the rise of **Life** and the introduction of **Evil** and **Desire**.[^13]
		- In comparison, the [Abyss](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Abyss_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Abyss "Abyss Pathway/Abilities") 's authority over **Desire** lies more in inducing, catalyzing, and igniting **Desires**, rather than embodying **Desire** itself like the remnants within the **Mother Tree of Desire**.[^14]
- **Reason**: "Her" birth symbolizes of the emergence of free will and the distinction between self and others.[^15]
	- Before "Her" birth, the [Mother Goddess of Depravity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Goddess_of_Depravity "Mother Goddess of Depravity") was essentially still part of the [Original Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Original_Creator "Original Creator") as a single entity. After "She" appeared, the [Mother Goddess of Depravity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Goddess_of_Depravity "Mother Goddess of Depravity") and the lives that appeared with the Mother Goddess's birth were no longer considered part of the [Original Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Original_Creator "Original Creator") as a single entity.
- **Tree Supplication:** "She" is able to **Supplicate** powers from the [Mother Goddess of Depravity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Goddess_of_Depravity "Mother Goddess of Depravity").
	- However, it will depend on the mood of the [Mother Goddess of Depravity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Goddess_of_Depravity "Mother Goddess of Depravity") whether "Her" prayer will be accepted or not.[^16]
- **Ravings**: "Her" authority emphasizes the effects of **Ravings**, and is not **Ravings** itself.[^17]
- **Fear**: "She" is the embodiment of the emotion of **Fear**.[^18]
	- This authority overlaps with the authority of the [Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Darkness_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Darkness "Darkness Pathway/Abilities").

### Countering Symbols, Authorities & Abilities

Certain **Symbols**, **Authorities**, and **Abilities** of other [Great Old Ones](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Old_Ones "Great Old Ones") can counter some of "Her" powers, which include but are not limited to the following:

- [Lord of Mysteries](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lord_of_Mysteries "Lord of Mysteries"):
	- **Marionettes** and **Grafting** counter **Desires**:
		- **Marionettes** were dead people, puppets devoid of **Desires**.[^19]
				- The [Lord of Mysteries](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lord_of_Mysteries "Lord of Mysteries") can **Graft** the surging **Desires** within "Their" true body onto **Marionettes**, then release them from manipulation, killing them.[^12]
- [High-Dimensional Overseer](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/High-Dimensional_Overseer "High-Dimensional Overseer"):
	- **Dimensionality** partially counters **Desires**.
		- Beings in higher dimensions or within the [High-Dimensional Overseer](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/High-Dimensional_Overseer "High-Dimensional Overseer") 's dimensional painting will not be fully consumed by the explosion of desires from the [Father of Devils](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Father_of_Devils "Father of Devils"), "They" would only enter a brief state of stagnation.[^11]

## Appearance

**True Form:** "She" resembled a humanoid giant tree covered in chaotic scales. Its surface was adorned with enticing crystalline fruits, glistening, alluring flowers, various metallic emblems, black hearts, bare tumors, and coins from different civilizations. At the tree's upper reaches were two heads. The lower head was male, eyes closed, exuding bizarre desires. The upper head was female, with green hair and a face of extraordinary beauty, possessing a charm that spoke directly to affection.[^19]

## History

### Genesis

On the first day, the [Original Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Original_Creator "Original Creator") awoke and created this world, but **[Reality](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Reality "Reality")**, the **[Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World")**, the **[Astral World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Astral_World "Astral World")**, and **[Fate](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/River_of_Fate "River of Fate")** were still mixed together.

On the second day, the [Oldest One](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Oldest_One "Oldest One") birthed the [Mother Goddess of Depravity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Goddess_of_Depravity "Mother Goddess of Depravity"). [Reality](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Reality "Reality") gained a foundation, the [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World") and [Astral World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Astral_World "Astral World") were separated, and the [River of Fate](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/River_of_Fate "River of Fate") began to flow. Soon after, the **Mother Tree of Desire** was born, giving rise to life and introducing evil and desire. Then came the [Son of Chaos](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Son_of_Chaos "Son of Chaos"), who established order-and with it, shadows naturally emerged.[^1]

### First Epoch

As one of the direct children of the [Original Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Original_Creator "Original Creator"), the **Mother Tree of Desire** was most affected by the [Law of Convergence](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Law_of_Convergence "Law of Convergence") when the [Original Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Original_Creator "Original Creator") awoke, causing part of "Her" [Sefirah](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirah "Sefirah") (later self-completed into the [Tenebrous World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tenebrous_World "Tenebrous World")) and the two [Pathways](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways") of [Chained](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chained_Pathway "Chained Pathway") and [Abyss](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Abyss_Pathway "Abyss Pathway") to be separated from "Her" and were attracted to Earth.

During the [Lost Era](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lost_Era "Lost Era"), the [Primordial God Almighty](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Primordial_God_Almighty "Primordial God Almighty") and [Celestial Worthy](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Celestial_Worthy "Celestial Worthy") 's strong and still-perfect-and-whole power deterred the [Outer Deities](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Outer_Deities "Outer Deities"), who can only settle for observing Earth from afar for now.[^20]

### Fourth Epoch

In the [Fourth Epoch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Epoch "Fourth Epoch"), the [Tree of Shadow](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tree_of_Shadow "Tree of Shadow") was planted into the ruins of [Trier](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Trier "Trier") by the **Mother Tree of Desire**. This series of event is connected to the [Tenebrous World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tenebrous_World "Tenebrous World") 's leakage after [Alista Tudor](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Alista_Tudor "Alista Tudor") 's attempt to merge with the [Mirrored Original Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mirrored_Original_Creator "Mirrored Original Creator"), causing the powers of [Sefirot](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirot "Sefirot") in the [Western Continent](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Western_Continent "Western Continent") to leak to the outside world during the [War of the Four Emperors](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/War_of_the_Four_Emperors "War of the Four Emperors").[^21] Nobody realized the secret planting of the [Tree of Shadow](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tree_of_Shadow "Tree of Shadow") due to the urgency of constructing the city and countering certain underground threats.[^22]

### Fifth Epoch

"She" has a direct influence over the Pathways "She" originally owned. With the weakening of the [Astral Barrier](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Astral_Barrier "Astral Barrier") at the end of the [Fourth Epoch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Epoch "Fourth Epoch"), "She" was able to send more power through the barrier onto Earth, increasing "Her" control over [Beyonders](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonders "Beyonders") of the two Pathways, including the [Chained God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chained_God "Chained God") and the [Devil Monarch Farbauti](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Devil_Monarch_Farbauti "Devil Monarch Farbauti").

During the beginning of the [Fifth Epoch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fifth_Epoch "Fifth Epoch"), "She" suddenly appeared and corrupted the [Chained God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chained_God "Chained God"), turning the [Rose School of Thought](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Rose_School_of_Thought "Rose School of Thought") into an evil organization that believes in releasing desires and indiscriminately commit rapes, murders, and sacrifices.[^23]

"She" has a conflict with the [Primordial Moon](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Goddess_of_Depravity "Mother Goddess of Depravity") within the [Rose School of Thought](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Rose_School_of_Thought "Rose School of Thought"),[^24] and is believed to possess some High-Sequence [Beyonder Characteristics](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristics "Beyonder Characteristics") of the [Moon Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Moon_Pathway "Moon Pathway").[^25] "She" also corrupted the [Devil Monarch Farbauti](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Devil_Monarch_Farbauti "Devil Monarch Farbauti") but didn't manage to completely control "Him" like "She" did with the [Chained God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chained_God "Chained God"). Perhaps due to "Her" corruption, the current [Abyss](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Abyss "Abyss") fell into a state where most devils were unable to live in.[^26]

### Lord of Mysteries

The **Mother Tree of Desire** was first introduced in Volume 3 when Klein was acting as [Admiral Amyrius](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Amyrius_Rieveldt "Amyrius Rieveldt"). Under "Her" guidance, [Cynthia](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Cynthia "Cynthia"), Amyrius' mistress was corrupted by the forces in the [Proliferation Necklace](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Proliferation_Necklace "Proliferation Necklace") and mutated into a tree monster.[^27]

Klein was later reminded by [Orange Light Hilarion](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hilarion "Hilarion") to beware of the **Mother Tree of Desire**.[^28]

### Circle of Inevitability

> [Madam Magician](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fors_Wall "Fors Wall") to [Lumian Lee](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee")

"She" possesses the authority of the [Patriarch Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Patriarch_Pathway "Patriarch Pathway").

It was revealed that beneath the depths of [Trier](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Trier "Trier"), the [Tree of Shadow](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tree_of_Shadow "Tree of Shadow") had its roots buried long before the construction of present-day Trier. The ambiance in Trier gradually transformed according to the **Mother Tree of Desire** 's desired path.[^29]

"Her" influence has grown significantly over the past year or two. According to [Madam Magician](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fors_Wall "Fors Wall"), [Mr. Poet](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Leonard_Mitchell "Leonard Mitchell") noticed some rudimentary cooperation among these organizations which worship the **Mother Tree of Desire**. Some members of the Devil families also show signs of worshiping "Her".[^30]

According to [Madam Magician](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fors_Wall "Fors Wall"), among the **Great Existences**, the only one who didn't preach using an alias was the **Mother Tree of Desire**, because " ***Mother Tree of Desire*** " was a small segment of the corresponding existence's long string of honorific names. There were two reasons why understanding and reciting it didn't directly bring corruption: First, it was only a small segment, incomplete; second, the **Mother Tree of Desire** indirectly controlled the one worshiped by the [Rose School of Thought](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Rose_School_of_Thought "Rose School of Thought") in the real world and the [Devil Monarch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Farbauti "Farbauti") in the [Abyss](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Abyss "Abyss"), so when this honorific name was mentioned, under conditions of limited understanding and while the barrier still existed, the proximity-based mystical phenomenon occurred, making it not so dangerous.[^31]

#### Project Vortex

The **Mother Tree of Desire** participated in the Vortex by giving the [Shadow of the Beauty Goddess](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Shadow_of_the_Beauty_Goddess "Shadow of the Beauty Goddess") "She" possessed to the [Mother Goddess of Depravity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Goddess_of_Depravity "Mother Goddess of Depravity"). In turn, "She" received the corruption inside [Roselle Gustav](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Roselle_Gustav "Roselle Gustav"), which was then transplanted into the [Chained God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chained_God "Chained God"). Though [Amon](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Amon "Amon") managed to reduce the corruption, it was still enough for [Tirié](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tiri%C3%A9 "Tirié") to be born, stripping the Chained God of "His" last Abomination Characteristic.

#### The Fool's Dream

"She" exerted influence inside Klein's dream by running Mushu Hospital, alongside the [Mother Goddess of Depravity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Goddess_of_Depravity "Mother Goddess of Depravity"). Together, "They" held the various traumas of Klein stored in the hospital basement. "She" heightened the sexual urges of a morgue assistant, making him engage in intercourse with the corpse of [Panatiya](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Panatiya "Panatiya") shortly before it came to life.

Lumian theorized "She" was corrupting the security members of the Intis Group due to them being mummified, which matches "Her" aesthetics in the [Rose School of Thought](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Rose_School_of_Thought "Rose School of Thought").

"She" withdrew from the Dream following the scrutiny from the main awareness alongside the [Great Mother](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Goddess_of_Depravity "Mother Goddess of Depravity") before the final confrontation between the [Celestial Worthy](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Celestial_Worthy "Celestial Worthy") and Klein.

#### The Day of Many Disasters

During the event, "She" took advantage of the chaos to attempt to fully grow the [Tree of Shadow](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tree_of_Shadow "Tree of Shadow") and descend onto Earth. [Suah](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Suah "Suah") and [Tirié](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tiri%C3%A9 "Tirié") merged with the [Tree of Shadow](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tree_of_Shadow "Tree of Shadow"),[^32] which was later destroyed by [The Fool](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti"), leaving only its root. However, before he could eliminate [Suah](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Suah "Suah") and [Tirié](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tiri%C3%A9 "Tirié"), he had to enter the [Astral Barrier](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Astral_Barrier "Astral Barrier") to prevent it from being broken after the death of the [Eternal Blazing Sun](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Eternal_Blazing_Sun "Eternal Blazing Sun").[^33]

It was revealed later on that this was part of a contract between the **Mother Tree of Desire** and the [Mother Goddess of Depravity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Goddess_of_Depravity "Mother Goddess of Depravity"), witnessed by the [Primordial God Almighty](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Primordial_God_Almighty "Primordial God Almighty"). After accommodating the [Brood Hive](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Brood_Hive "Brood Hive"), the [Mother Goddess of Depravity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Goddess_of_Depravity "Mother Goddess of Depravity") would assist the **Mother Tree of Desire**, in exchange for the loss of the [Tree of Shadow](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tree_of_Shadow "Tree of Shadow").[^34] [^11]

#### The Apocalypse

In the early stage of [The Apocalypse](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/The_Apocalypse "The Apocalypse"), [Suah](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Suah "Suah") and [Tirié](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tiri%C3%A9 "Tirié") conducted a ritual to sacrifice the [Chained God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chained_God "Chained God") to forcibly open a passage in the [Astral Barrier](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Astral_Barrier "Astral Barrier") with the [Mother Goddess of Depravity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Goddess_of_Depravity "Mother Goddess of Depravity") 's aid to allow the **Mother Tree of Desire** to descend within the barrier. "They" merged into the [Chained God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chained_God "Chained God"), intending to return to the **Mother Tree of Desire**, but were **Fooled** and **Deceived** by [Klein](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti"), who had become a [Lord of Mysteries](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lord_of_Mysteries "Lord of Mysteries"), to mijudge the timing. Klein then **Grafted** the mass of [Chained](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chained_Pathway "Chained Pathway") materials onto an area above [the gray fog](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirah_Castle "Sefirah Castle").[^35]

During the battle between Earth's [Above the Sequence](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Above_the_Sequence "Above the Sequence") and the [Outer Deities](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Outer_Deities "Outer Deities"), the **Mother Tree of Desire** was held off by the [Lord of Mysteries](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lord_of_Mysteries "Lord of Mysteries") Klein, along with the [Supernova Dominator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Supernova_Dominator "Supernova Dominator") and the [Goddess of Fate](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Goddess_of_Fate "Goddess of Fate"). In another battlefield, the [Master of the Shadow Cottage](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Master_of_the_Shadow_Cottage "Master of the Shadow Cottage") and [Farbauti](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Farbauti "Farbauti"), who had merged with each other and the [Tenebrous World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tenebrous_World "Tenebrous World") to become a Quasi- [Father of Devils](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Father_of_Devils "Father of Devils"), lured the [Inextinguishable Ravings](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Inextinguishable_Ravings "Inextinguishable Ravings") into "Them" and then cursed "Themselves". [Amon](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Amon "Amon"), with permission from Klein, used the [Chained](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chained_Pathway "Chained Pathway") [Uniqueness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniqueness "Uniqueness") to establish a mystical connection between the [Tenebrous World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tenebrous_World "Tenebrous World") and the **Mother Tree of Desire**, severely wounding "Her" through the self-curses of the [Tenebrous World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tenebrous_World "Tenebrous World"). However, the **Mother Tree of Desire** in turn used the mystical connection to drag "Herself" toward the [Tenebrous World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tenebrous_World "Tenebrous World") 's battlefield. With the help of the [Mother Goddess of Depravity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Goddess_of_Depravity "Mother Goddess of Depravity"), "She" managed to shake off Klein's influence and took away the [Tenebrous World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tenebrous_World "Tenebrous World"). "She" then immediately fled into the [Astral World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Astral_World "Astral World"), leaving the [Chained Uniqueness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniquenesses#Chained_Pathway "Uniquenesses") and [Abyss Uniqueness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniquenesses#Abyss_Pathway "Uniquenesses") and corresponding [Beyonder Characteristics](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristics "Beyonder Characteristics") behind.[^12] [^11]

After [The Apocalypse](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/The_Apocalypse "The Apocalypse"), the **Mother Tree of Desire** would need a long time to fully re-accommodate the [Tenebrous World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tenebrous_World "Tenebrous World"). Due to the fusion and influence of various Dao integrators, the [Tenebrous World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tenebrous_World "Tenebrous World") had developed a new consciousness and spirit. Although both "It" and the integrators fused within "It" had been destroyed by "Their" self-curses, "Its" spirit remained indestructible, chaotic, and extreme. Moreover, the **Mother Tree of Desire** was still in a severely wounded state, requiring at least decades, if not longer, to fully accommodate the [Tenebrous World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tenebrous_World "Tenebrous World") and completely recover. Furthermore, "She" had also gone into hiding to recover and accommodate in secrecy, preventing interference or being hunted by other [Outer Deities](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Outer_Deities "Outer Deities"), further ruling out any possibility of receiving healing from the [Mother Goddess of Depravity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Goddess_of_Depravity "Mother Goddess of Depravity"). Thus, "She" was unlikely to be a factor in the near term.[^36]

## Trivia

- If "She" were to reclaim the [Tenebrous World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tenebrous_World "Tenebrous World") and its associated authorities, "She" would become the most powerful [Great Old One](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Old_One "Great Old One") outside of the [Pillars](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pillars "Pillars"). Furthermore, "She" would still be able to accommodate a non-Pillar [Sefirah](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirah "Sefirah"), gaining power comparable to a Pillar but lacking a corresponding Pillar symbolism.[^10]
- If a [Great Old One](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Old_One "Great Old One") symbolizing **Impotence** existed, the **Mother Tree of Desire** would be able to make "Them" stand up again.[^37]
