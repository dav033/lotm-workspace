**Haiter** is the current leader of the [Numinous Episcopate's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Numinous_Episcopate "Numinous Episcopate") [Artificial Death](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Artificial_Death "Artificial Death") faction. "He" is a Sequence 2 [Death Consul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_2:_Death_Consul) of the [Death Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway "Death Pathway").

## Honorific Name

The King from the depths of Hell;  
An Angel playing the Music of Death;  
The Ruler above the [River Styx](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/River_of_Eternal_Darkness "River of Eternal Darkness")

— [Honorific Name](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Honorific_Name "Honorific Name") of **Haiter** [^1]

**Note**: The reason this three-line [Honorific Name](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Honorific_Name "Honorific Name"), which is exclusive to a [True God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/True_God "True God"), can point to a Sequence 2 because the "above the River Styx" in the name borrows the status of the [River of Eternal Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/River_of_Eternal_Darkness "River of Eternal Darkness").[^2]

## Background

**Haiter** used to be the High Priest of the [Church of Death](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Church_of_Death "Church of Death"), and later received the grace of God, becoming an [Angel](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Angel "Angel").

"He" was severely eroded in a ceremony and has been unable to leave "His" tomb ever since.[^3]

"He" is committed to awakening [Death](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death "Death").

## History

As [Patrick](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Patrick_Bryan "Patrick Bryan") starts to stall for time, unable to gather the materials or the ultimate failure of the experiment. The [Numinous Episcopate](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Numinous_Episcopate "Numinous Episcopate") ’s [Artificial Death](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Artificial_Death "Artificial Death") faction began to question if there was something wrong with Patrick. They instructed Patrick to perform a special ritual to recover **Haiter** from his eroded state.[^4]

When the ritual began, **Haiter** attempted to use the power of enslavement that higher Sequence Beyonders of the [Death Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway "Death Pathway") hold over lower Sequence Beyonders, allowing "Him" to access [Patrick's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Patrick_Bryan "Patrick Bryan") information directly. However, with [Klein’s](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") interference using Angel’s Embrace, Patrick was freed from "His" influence and responded to Haiter in a half-truthful manner, leaving Haiter unsuspecting.[^5]

Somehow, **Haiter** is cooperating with [Sauron-Einhorn-Medici](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sauron-Einhorn-Medici "Sauron-Einhorn-Medici"), as he sends Red Angel Evil Spirit to assist [Patrick Bryan](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Patrick_Bryan "Patrick Bryan").[^5] Klein suspected that **Haiter** being warned or hinted by [Sauron-Einhorn-Medici](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sauron-Einhorn-Medici "Sauron-Einhorn-Medici") about the [Uniqueness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniqueness "Uniqueness") of the [Death Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway "Death Pathway") 's situation.[^6]
