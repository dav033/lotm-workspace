## Sequence 4: Mysticologist

### Potion Properties

> "A phantom, dark liquid from which purple sparks are constantly being ignited deep within"^{[1]}

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

* A vague, gem-like orb emanating a gloomy aura, as if a gaze peered out from within to scrutinize everything.^{[3]}
  * An illusory gemstone shaped like an eyeball.
    * Its radiance subdues and exudes a murky aura, as though a gaze emanates from within to scrutinise all things.^{[1]}

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

  * 1 Scalp of an Arcane Spirit.^{[1]}
  * 1 Eye of Evil God's Pupil.^{[1]}

Or
  * A Mysticologist Beyonder Characteristic.

Supplementary Ingredients

  * 13 drops of an Arcane Spirit's Tears.^{[1]}
  * 80ml from an Eye of Evil God's Ash Liquid.^{[1]}
  * 1 relic related to a mystical event.^{[1]}
  * 1 Warlock potion created by the concoctor (any effect is acceptable).^{[1]}

### Advancement Ritual

Completely analyze a drop of blood from a Mythical Creature and obtain complicated and massive amounts of knowledge.^{[6]}

  * Note 1: Due to the differing types of Mythical Creature blood employed, should they succeed, their specialisation within the 'Mysticologist' stage will also exhibit certain variations.^{[1]}
  * Note 2: Although the advancement ritual of a Mysticologist doesn't have any requirements on the Mythical Creature's pathway, the best choice is still the Wheel of Fortune Pathway.^{[7]}
  * Note 3: The blood of an Angel-level creature that is one of the main materials for a Sequence 2 angel would work, but the effect is not as good as a complete one.^{[8]}
  * Note 4: The blood of a Boon-recceiving Angel would also work, as long as "Their" physical state has changed.^{[8]}

## Sequence 3: Clairvoyant

### Potion Properties

> A semi-solidified, translucent purple liquid streaked with fine, mercury-coloured 'veins'^{[1]}

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> A vertically shaped item with a mercury sheen.^{[1]}

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

  * Eyeballs of a Stargazer (outwardly, they are deformed fish with eyes protruding like pillars).^{[1]}
  * Stardust Skin of a Treant High Priest.^{[1]}

Or
  * A Clairvoyant Beyonder Characteristic.

Supplementary Ingredients

  * 100 ml of the sap of a Treant High Priest. ^{[1]}
  * 10ml of brain fluid from a Stargazer.^{[1]}
  * 1 book of prophecies with a higher accuracy rate than error rate.^{[1]}
  * 9 hairs of people with abnormal fates (each one from a different source).^{[1]}

### Advancement Ritual

It involves gathering a vast amount of information, analyzing, deducing, and divining to 'Prophesy' a certain event that will occur three years later.(It must involve demigods.)^{[1]}

  * Ultimately the performer has to witness its fulfillment. Throughout the process, they cannot rely on external forces or directly drive the event to happen; the ritual performer can only guide it indirectly.^{[1]}

## Sequence 1: Knowledge Emperor

### Potion Properties

> A chaotic mass of intermingled bubbles in every colour imaginable^{[1]}

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> A transparent, illusory crown formed from the convergence of diverse information.^{[1]}

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

  * A Knowledge Emperor Beyonder Characteristic

Supplementary Ingredients

  * 1 artifact engraved with knowledge related to the concoctor.^{[1]}
  * 1 crown, in the truest sense.^{[1]}
  * 9 types of knowledge closely related to Sequence 0.^{[1]}
  * 1 tome compiling the knowledge discovered by concoctor.^{[1]}

### Advancement Ritual

Analyze oneself and master all knowledge related to oneself, then understand and find the most core, key part (in the Western Continent, this process is called "Illuminating the Mind and Perceiving the True Nature") and use it as a link to find a way to integrate the remaining parts and other knowledge they have to initially form a whole.^{[1]}

  * The whole form is known as a Primordial Spirit/Nascent Soul.

## Sequence 0: Hermit

### Potion Properties

> A giant eyeball that conceals another world.^{[1]}

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

  * The Hermit Uniqueness.
  * Three Knowledge Emperor Beyonder Characteristics.

Supplementary Ingredients

  * Sufficient knowledge concerning the Great Old Ones.^{[1]}

### Advancement Ritual

Gather all the information one has left in the world. Then, find a suitable place and way to completely hide them, leaving no traces behind.^{[1]}
