# Lord of Mysteries Wiki - Visionary Pathway Advancement

Community-maintained potion/advancement inventory for the Visionary Pathway (Spectator through Visionary), compiling Cuttlefish's official WeChat post "Complete Formulas of the Spectator Pathway" alongside novel excerpts.

## Summary

Per-Sequence potion data for the Visionary Pathway. Several Sequences (Psychiatrist, Hypnotist, Dreamwalker, Manipulator) include detailed narrative excerpts of the consumption backlash and ritual effect.

## Extracted facts

- Sequence 9 Spectator: sticky silver, bubbling potion; Characteristic is a silver-white vertical eye. Acting method: a Spectator only ever watches, never taking the stage.
- Sequence 8 Telepathist: shifting-luster potion; Characteristic is a pair of transparent, dreamlike eyes. Consumption backlash blanks vision for longer than a Spectator's before clearing.
- Sequence 7 Psychiatrist: slightly golden, rippling potion; Characteristic is a huge golden iris. Consumption backlash grows scales on exposed skin and turns the irises vertical and faintly golden before the drinker recovers by entering a Spectator-like state to calm down.
- Sequence 6 Hypnotist: translucent, mirror-like, colorless potion; Characteristic is a grayish-white colloid resembling an unfixed mirror. Consumption backlash merges the drinker's Soul Body, Astral Projection, and Ether Body into the Body of Heart and Mind, briefly exposing them to the Sea of Collective Subconscious before scales and hair mutations recede, leaving a bottomless-whirlpool quality in the eyes as a sign of dispersing spirituality.
- Sequence 5 Dreamwalker: grayish-white speckled potion; Characteristic resembles a child-fist-sized heart with wrinkles, cracks, and patterns nearly identical to a Manipulator's. Advancement ritual: sign a contract with a Spirit World harpy (or equivalent nightmare-giving entity/method) and consume the potion holding one tail feather amid intense joy or anger, so the harpy can snap the drinker awake if needed.
- Sequence 4 Manipulator: potion dotted with points of light resembling the Sea of Collective Subconscious. Advancement ritual: resonate with the emotions of a grand event of at least 10,000 people, then consume the potion; the ritual effect immerses the drinker directly in the Sea of Collective Subconscious and forces empathic resonance with the crowd's emotions.
- Sequence 3 Dream Weaver: dark, shifting mist-like potion; Characteristic is an unreal, grayish-white dream-brain. Advancement ritual: cause at least 100,000 people to share the same dream on the same night.
- Sequence 2 Discerner: ghastly pale potion with opening/closing illusory vertical eyes; Characteristic is a pale, hole-riddled heart inlaid with vertical eyes. Advancement ritual: delve into the subconscious of at least 10,000 humans, uncover their deepest fears/desires/psychological roots, and leave a mark of oneself.
- Sequence 1 Author: transparent potion precipitating starlight-like points; Characteristic is a crystal-clear, pen-like finger bone. Advancement ritual: using real people as characters, write a medium-to-long novel and have their actual fates follow the plot to its conclusion, leaving imprints in the Spirit World and River of Fate.
- Sequence 0 Visionary: golden potion seeming to contain a fantasy world; supplementary ingredient is a self-created Realm from Fantasy or Dreams (which later evolves into a Divine Kingdom). Advancement ritual: align the development of the times with one's own expectations, then drink at a suitable node in that trend.

## Short quotations

- "Come, see, record, but do not interfere, be a Spectator." (Dreamwalker acting method)
- "Cause at least 100,000 people to have the same dream on the same night." (Dream Weaver advancement ritual)
- "Make the development of the times align with one's expectations. Then, consume the potion for advancement when it's at a certain time node in that trend." (Visionary advancement ritual)
