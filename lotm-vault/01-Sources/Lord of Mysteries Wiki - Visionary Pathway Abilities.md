# Lord of Mysteries Wiki - Visionary Pathway Abilities

Community-maintained English ability inventory for the Visionary Pathway (Spectator through Visionary). Compiles novel excerpts and Cuttlefish's official WeChat post "Abilities of the Spectator Pathway" to translate and standardize terminology.

## Summary

Per-Sequence abilities for the Visionary Pathway, tracing a progression from a Spectator's enhanced observation, through Telepathist/Psychiatrist/Hypnotist mind-reading and hypnosis, Dreamwalker/Manipulator/Dream Weaver dream and Mind World manipulation, to the Discerner and Author's partial Authorities over Discernment and Envisioning, culminating in the Sequence 0 Visionary's near-total Envisioning, Psyche, Discernment, Dream, and Loss of Control authorities.

## Extracted facts

- Sequence 9 Spectator: enhanced inferential/analytical/observational/identification ability and memory (Body of Heart and Mind); an animal that drinks the potion gains human-level intelligence and speech.
- Sequence 6 Hypnotist: Psychological Invisibility keeps the user in the target's blind spot of consciousness, unaffected by Spirit Vision and resistant even to a Hunter's Danger Intuition; strengthened Psychological Cue becomes Hypnosis with Non-Combat and Battle variants, the latter unable to end a target's life or force suicide and lasting only briefly before the target snaps awake.
- Sequence 5 Dreamwalker: Dream Traversal is capped at 500 meters between two dreams belonging to intelligent creatures; Alteration is milder/more furtive than Hypnosis and can affect Demigods with sufficient preparation.
- Sequence 4 Manipulator: can create up to 13 Virtual Personas at the start; incomplete Mythical Creature Form (incomplete Giant Mind Dragon) can be maintained for about 1 minute via Psychoanalysis/Hypnosis support before negative effects set in.
- Sequence 2 Discerner: Psychological Cue is elevated to an Authority (Pure Idealism); Discernment can sometimes penetrate or be blocked by Concealment, Deceit, and Fooling, mutually restraining each other.
- Sequence 1 Author: Envisioning/Fantasy into Reality range is limited to a city the size of Backlund; Discernment authority means anything spoken about the Author becomes known to them, blockable by beings with comparable Concealment.
- Sequence 0 Visionary: full Envisioning authority can separate two Sequence 1 Beyonder Characteristics and replace them with Imagined counterparts while keeping their own rank; can achieve a form of Omnipotence/Omniscience that cannot resist authorities that erase Illusions or pierce Fantasies; holds partial Psyche, Discernment (limited to the Mind World, extending into Fate/Reality/Illusion Domains), Dream, and Loss of Control authorities.
