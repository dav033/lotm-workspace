## Sequence 3: Silver Knight

### Potion Properties

> It looks like as if chunks of Mercury were scattered throughout the pale white liquid^{[1]}

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> A mass of silvery-white luster that resembled a heart-like miniature sun.^{[7]}

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

  * The essence of Aurmir's blood.^{[1]}

Or
  * The Heart of a "King's Court Chaser" (a type of giant).^{[1]}

Or
  * The heart of a "King's Court Watchman" (another type of giant).^{[1]}

Or
  * A Silver Knight Beyonder Characteristic.

Supplementary Ingredients

  * 300 milliliters of Water contaminated by the power of Death.^{[1]}
  * One item worn or carried by a Sequence 3 or above from the Twilight Giant Pathway (for armor-type items, only one armor plate is required).^{[1]}
  * One piece of Amber with a trapped ancient insect inside.^{[1]}
  * One piece of paper recording 100 or more kills of the oneself.^{[1]}

### Advancement Ritual

Arrange a complex altar and place the remains of six powerful creatures that were hunted by oneself in the correct order and receive the blessing of a God (Angel-level at minimum).^{[7]}

The six creatures shouldn't necessarily be monsters, six Demigod-level Beyonders would also suffice.^{[8]}

## Sequence 2: Glory

### Potion Properties

> A liquid formed from pure, holy, and profound light.^{[1]}

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> A pure square stone that radiates divine light.^{[1]}

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

  * The Crystallization of a Glorious Giant (e.g: The One Eye of Bladel).^{[1]}

Or
  * The Crown of Aurmir.^{[1]}

Or
  * A Glory Beyonder Characteristic.

Supplementary Ingredients

  * 300 milliliters of blood from the Glorious Giant.^{[1]}
  * One item corrupted by Dark Power.^{[1]}
  * One Corpse buried for over a thousand years.^{[1]}
  * One Poem praising itself (has to be sung for at least 10 years).^{[1]}

### Advancement Ritual

Fight an Angel or a Creature of equal power on your own (using beyonder items is allowed).^{[1]}
Or kill enough Enemies or Blasphemers on behalf of the God who blessed you.^{[1]}

## Sequence 1: Hand of God

### Potion Properties

> A bluish-black liquid tinged with the colors of Dusk/Twilight, containing numerous transparent faces.^{[1]}

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> A translucent bluish-black giant hand with various divine patterns.^{[1]}

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

  * One of the Hands of Aurmir (content on the First Blasphemy Slate.)^{[1]}

Or
  * One of the Hands of Badheil (content on the Second Blasphemy Slate).^{[1]}

Or
  * A Hand of God Beyonder Characteristic.

Supplementary Ingredients

  * 100 milliliters of a True God's Blood (the type that doesn't have Beyonder Characteristics).^{[1]}
  * 9 drops of Water from the River Styx (or something similar).^{[1]}
  * A religious text recording one's own legend and deeds of loyalty.^{[1]}
  * A Flower blooming in a True God's Divine Kingdom.^{[1]}

### Advancement Ritual

Swear allegiance to a certain god or become a Servant of the River Styx .^{[1]}

Any tributary of the River of Eternal Darkness suffices to become a Servant.^{[1]}

## Sequence 0: Twilight Giant

### Potion Properties

> A huge, orange-red phantom, as if dragging a Shadow of Twilight.^{[1]}

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

  * The Twilight Giant Uniqueness.
  * Three Hand of God Beyonder Characteristics.

Supplementary Ingredients

  * The Remains of a True God.^{[1]}

### Advancement Ritual

Witness and participate in the entire process of a race or continent from its rise to its decline and eventual extinction.^{[1]}
