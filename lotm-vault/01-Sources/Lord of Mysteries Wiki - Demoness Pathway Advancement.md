## Sequence 1: Apocalypse (Demoness of Apocalypse)

### Potion Properties

> Deep black liquid filled with destruction.^{[1]}

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> A dark orb.^{[1]}

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

  * A Apocalypse Beyonder Characteristic

Supplementary Ingredients

  * A piece of completely lifeless darkness.^{[1]}
  * 81 people obsessed with themselves.^{[1]}
  * One self-created ongoing apocalypse scenario.^{[1]}
  * 60 milliliters of blood from a Weather Warlock.^{[1]}

### Advancement Ritual

Any one of the three rituals is effective:^{[3]}^{[1]}

  1. Advance at the universally acknowledged end of an epoch or the beginning of the next.
  2. Advance during the signs and process of the apocalypse's arrival.
  3. Advance when one's own involvement leads to the fall of a deity.

## Sequence 0: Demoness (Chaos Demoness, Primordial Demoness)

### Potion Properties

> An exquisitely charming female human skin.^{[1]}

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

  * The Demoness Uniqueness.
  * Three Apocalypse Beyonder Characteristics.

Supplementary Ingredients

  * One portion of genuine love originating from oneself (This is illusory, it must be transformed into something between real and illusory before being used).^{[1]}
  * Half of the Mirror World.^{[1]}

### Advancement Ritual

* Altered Ritual: Descend into the depths of the earth, find the Chaos Sea, endure a degree of its corruption while maintaining oneself—neither falling nor transforming.^{[35]}
    * This ritual shown in the Second Blasphemy Slate was influenced by the Primordial God Almighty to be beneficial to "Him" as a resurrection plan.
  * Alternative Ritual: Find a way to borrow the special traits from the Sequence 1s of five Pathways related to the Chaos Sea, combine them into a power close to the Chaos Sea, and control it.^{[1]}
    * This ritual was not stated in the Second Blasphemy Slate.
