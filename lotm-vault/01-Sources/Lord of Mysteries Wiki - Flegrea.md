The **Annihilation Demonic Wolf Flegrea** is one of the [Ancient Gods](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ancient_Gods "Ancient Gods") from the [Second Epoch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Second_Epoch "Second Epoch"). "He" was an [Ancient God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ancient_God "Ancient God") of the [Darkness Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Darkness_Pathway) and the [Fool Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fool_Pathway).[^1]

## Personality

"He" is crazy, cruel, bloodthirsty, and filled with an instinct for destruction.[^2]

## Pathway Composition as an Ancient God

"His" Pathway composition is as follows:[^3]

- [Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Darkness_Pathway): Uniqueness + 3 Sequence 1.
- [Fool](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fool_Pathway): Uniqueness + 1 Sequence 1.

"He" was crazier even among the [Ancient Gods](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ancient_Gods "Ancient Gods") who possessed multiple non-adjacent [Uniquenesses](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniquenesses "Uniquenesses") because:[^4]

- Firstly, "His" two Pathways were of a darker, more terrifying type.
- Secondly, "He" was originally a wild beast.

## History

According to the [Myth of Creation](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/First_Epoch#Lore "First Epoch"), "He" and the rest of the demonic wolves were formed from the ears of the [Original Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Original_Creator "Original Creator").[^5]

In truth, "He" was a remnant wolf of the [Pre-Epoch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pre-Epoch "Pre-Epoch"), a survivor after the [Original Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Original_Creator "Original Creator") woke up.[^6]

As an [Ancient God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ancient_God "Ancient God") of two incompatible pathways, "He" had attempted instinctual mating and produced many children to reduce the influence of excessive conflicting Beyonder characteristics; however, due to it being uncontrollable with no way to guarantee progress and "His" destructive instinct, "He" also had killed several descendants. After "His" death, at least two of "His" children survived; "They" fled together and crossed the sea to the [Northern Continent](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Northern_Continent "Northern Continent"), founding the concealed [Nation of the Evernight](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Nation_of_the_Evernight "Nation of the Evernight").[^2] One of "Them" later named "Himself" [Antigonus](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Antigonus "Antigonus"), and the other called "Herself" [Mother of the Sky](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_of_the_Sky "Mother of the Sky").

**Flegrea** knew the existence of the [Sefirah Castle](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirah_Castle "Sefirah Castle"); this was the name that the **King of Demonic Wolves** gave. However "He" failed to enter the [Sefirah Castle](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirah_Castle "Sefirah Castle") even to "His" death.[^7]

Among the [Ancient Gods](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ancient_Gods "Ancient Gods"), the [Devil Monarch Farbauti](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Devil_Monarch_Farbauti "Devil Monarch Farbauti") and the [Annihilation Demonic Wolf Flegrea](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Annihilation_Demonic_Wolf_Flegrea "Annihilation Demonic Wolf Flegrea") were independent beings who wanted to subvert all order and corrupt all living beings; thus, "He" fought all of the other ancient gods.[^8]

At the end of the [Early Era of Fire](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Early_Era_of_Fire "Early Era of Fire"), the [Sanguine Ancestor Lilith](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sanguine_Ancestor_Lilith "Sanguine Ancestor Lilith"), [Annihilation Demonic Wolf Flegrea](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Annihilation_Demonic_Wolf_Flegrea "Annihilation Demonic Wolf Flegrea"), and [Mutated King Kvastir](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mutated_King_Kvastir "Mutated King Kvastir") perished together in a conspiracy [^9] that the [Ancient Sun God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ancient_Sun_God "Ancient Sun God") was a part of.[^10] Although "He" did not directly act in the killing of the **Flegrea**, "He" did exert influence.[^11] [Antigonus](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Antigonus "Antigonus") was present when **Flegrea** died, and "He" saw the blood of **Flegrea** splattered all over the body of the [Goddess of Misfortune Amanises](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Amanises "Amanises"). In the chaos, the [Darkness Uniqueness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniquenesses#Darkness_Pathway) and a [Sequence 1 Knight of Misfortune](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Darkness_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_1:_Knight_of_Misfortune) [Beyonder Characteristic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristic "Beyonder Characteristic") were pulled into "Her" hands.[^2]

Only the [Forsaken Land of the Gods](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Forsaken_Land_of_the_Gods "Forsaken Land of the Gods") still remembers the [Annihilation Demonic Wolf Flegrea](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Annihilation_Demonic_Wolf_Flegrea "Annihilation Demonic Wolf Flegrea"). The rest of the world has already forgotten "His" existence, as the god of the [Darkness Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Darkness_Pathway) had been replaced by the [Evernight Goddess](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Evernight_Goddess "Evernight Goddess").
