The **Primordial God Almighty** is the former [Great Old One](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Old_One "Great Old One"), [God Almighty](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/God_Almighty), who was most active on Earth from the [Pre-Epoch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pre-Epoch "Pre-Epoch") to the middle of the [First Epoch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/First_Epoch "First Epoch"), and the former owner of the [Chaos Sea](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chaos_Sea). "He" is the **Lord of the [Astral World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Astral_World "Astral World")**.

## Appearance

The **Primordial God Almighty** 's full appearance has never been shown, but "His" consciousness inside other beings has manifested in the forms below.

***[Eternal Blazing Sun](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Eternal_Blazing_Sun "Eternal Blazing Sun")***: A colossal luminous humanoid figure surrounded by chaos.[^3] [^4]

***[Lumian Lee](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee")***: A vortex-like face-no eyes, nose, or mouth, no bones-composed entirely of a chaotic liquid embodying every color.[^5]

***[Malevolent Dragon](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Malevolent_Dragon "Malevolent Dragon")***: A head that was a swirling amalgamation of liquid, encompassing all colors and possibilities. At its facial features—eyes, nose, ears, and mouth—holes had been drilled, one or two in each location.[^6]

## History

### Pre-Epoch

In a time more ancient than the Modern Era of the [Pre-Epoch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pre-Epoch "Pre-Epoch"), the sleeping [Original Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Original_Creator "Original Creator") had naturally split into multiple personas. "They" were also in a state of slumber, but "They" began to use the authority and [Beyonder Characteristics](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristics "Beyonder Characteristics") "They" controlled to influence the world, making many preparations for the true dissociation which would occur when the [Original Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Original_Creator "Original Creator") 's body awakened. Among the multiple personas, the **Primordial God Almighty** and the [Celestial Worthy of Heaven and Earth for Blessings](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Celestial_Worthy_of_Heaven_and_Earth_for_Blessings "Celestial Worthy of Heaven and Earth for Blessings") were the most powerful and active on Earth.[^7] During this time, the **Primordial God Almighty** 's holy temple was in the West.[^8]

In this era, there was a myth: the **God Almighty of Heaven** created a divine child. Several Angels, led by an Archangel, rebelled. They were defeated, cast into the Abyss, creating Hell, and turning them to devils. This myth might never have actually occurred; instead, it represented a tendency—a connection between Heaven and the Abyss.[^9]

The underground corruption from the [Chaos Sea](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chaos_Sea "Chaos Sea") (and thus, the **Primordial God Almighty**) had always been influencing the human world in an unobvious manner. It can intermittently bring about certain mysterious events. Since oil itself is a product derived from the [Chaos Sea](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chaos_Sea "Chaos Sea") 's corrosion of underground regions,[^10] when the [Chernobyl](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chernobyl "Chernobyl") research facility probed deeper into the dried-up oil field and discovered some strange material, the [Original Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Original_Creator "Original Creator") fully awakened, turning people into oil and causing them to go mad, summoning them to the underground.[^11] As a researcher from [Chernobyl](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chernobyl "Chernobyl"), the [Ancient Sun God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ancient_Sun_God "Ancient Sun God") fell into the [Chaos Sea](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chaos_Sea "Chaos Sea"), and would only awakened in the [Second Epoch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Second_Epoch "Second Epoch").

### First Epoch

#### Lost Era

After the [Original Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Original_Creator "Original Creator") awakened and split apart, the **Primordial God Almighty** and the [Celestial Worthy](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Celestial_Worthy "Celestial Worthy") leveraged "Their" long-prepared ancient schemes across the times of the [Old Earth](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pre-Epoch "Pre-Epoch") to gradually gain advantages in "Their" fight against the [Sefirot](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirot "Sefirot"). The two [Pillars](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pillars "Pillars") eventually defeated the other [Sefirot](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirot "Sefirot") (excluding the [Brood Hive](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Brood_Hive "Brood Hive") and the [Key of Light](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Key_of_Light "Key of Light")) and shattered "Their" wills, making those wills take a very long time to recover and awaken again.[^12] [^13]

After the **Primordial God Almighty** and the [Celestial Worthy](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Celestial_Worthy "Celestial Worthy") absorbed "Their" first additional [Sefirah](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirah "Sefirah"), "They" exceeded the limit of the [Pillar](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pillar "Pillar") - the highest level of stability one can achieve. This made "Their" inclination to converge more intense, making it impossible for "Them" to control "Themselves", and "They" began taking actions against each other.[^14] The devouring of "Their" first additional [Sefirot](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirot "Sefirot") marked the end of the [Lost Era](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lost_Era "Lost Era").[^15]

- The **Primordial God Almighty** first accommodated the [City of Calamity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/City_of_Calamity "City of Calamity") because of a mystical connection, an instinctive tendency that had long existed. Beyond "His" deep desire for it, even though "He" was unaware of the setup and contingency plans left by the [Original Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Original_Creator "Original Creator") to stabilize existence through the [Mirrored Original Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mirrored_Original_Creator "Mirrored Original Creator"),[^16] "He" likely foresaw the dangers. Thus, "He" intervened personally by inserting "His" own consciousness into the [City of Calamity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/City_of_Calamity "City of Calamity") as a **Chaotic Head** to act as a barrier to prevent yin and yang from fully merging and forming the [Mirrored Original Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mirrored_Original_Creator "Mirrored Original Creator").[^6]
- Afterwards, "He" accommodated the [Tenebrous World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tenebrous_World "Tenebrous World") because, already under the convergence instinct, "He" prioritized the association between **Heaven** and the **Abyss**, which was also consistent with the mystical similarity between the degenerate nature of the [Hanged Man](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hanged_Man_Pathway) and the [Abyss](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Abyss_Pathway) [Pathways](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways").[^9]

#### Era of Destruction

Some time in the mid- [First Epoch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/First_Epoch "First Epoch"), foreseeing parts of the future, the **Primordial God Almighty** and the [Brood Hive](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Brood_Hive "Brood Hive") gave birth to the twins [Omebella](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Omebella "Omebella") and [Zedus](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Zedus "Zedus").[^15] [^17] Both the **Primordial God Almighty** and the [Celestial Worthy](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Celestial_Worthy "Celestial Worthy") made various arrangements, some to ensure "Their" own revival, and some to prevent the other's revival.[^18]

- The **Primordial God Almighty** also had undisclosed backup plans related to the [Western Continent](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Western_Continent "Western Continent").[^19]

After the [Celestial Worthy](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Celestial_Worthy "Celestial Worthy") completed the creation of the [Magic Wishing Lamp](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Magic_Wishing_Lamp "Magic Wishing Lamp"), "He" and the **Primordial God Almighty** engaged in full-scale war, extinguishing the "sun" and plunging the world into darkness.[^15] No one, not even the [Seven Lights](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Seven_Lights "Seven Lights") of the [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World"), knows the exact situation of the battle between the [Celestial Worthy](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Celestial_Worthy "Celestial Worthy") and the **Primordial God Almighty**. After the battle, both [Pillars](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pillars "Pillars") perished.

- The **Primordial God Almighty** 's corpse formed the [First Blasphemy Slate](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/First_Blasphemy_Slate "First Blasphemy Slate") in the [Chaos Sea](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chaos_Sea "Chaos Sea"), and beside it was the [Error Uniqueness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniquenesses#Error_Pathway),[^7] which was intentionally used by the [Celestial Worthy](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Celestial_Worthy "Celestial Worthy") to stop the **Primordial God Almighty** 's first resurrection within the [Ancient Sun God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ancient_Sun_God "Ancient Sun God").[^20]
- The [Celestial Worthy](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Celestial_Worthy "Celestial Worthy") sealed all [Sefirot](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirot "Sefirot") apart from the [Sefirah Castle](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirah_Castle) and the [Chaos Sea](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chaos_Sea) to the [Western Continent](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Western_Continent "Western Continent").[^14] [^7]

Before "Their" deaths, the **Primordial God Almighty** and [Celestial Worthy](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Celestial_Worthy "Celestial Worthy") 's strong and still-perfect-and-whole power deterred the [Outer Deities](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Outer_Deities "Outer Deities"), who could only settle for observing Earth from afar for the time being. During this era, the **Primordial God Almighty** served as the sun. After "His" death, the world was casted into darkness until the ascension of the [Ancient Sun God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ancient_Sun_God "Ancient Sun God").[^15]

At some point in the [First Epoch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/First_Epoch "First Epoch") or [Second Epoch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Second_Epoch "Second Epoch"), [Edefana](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Edefana "Edefana") was polluted by the [Chaos Sea](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chaos_Sea "Chaos Sea"), then influenced and driven by the [godhood](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Godhood "Godhood") of the **Primordial God Almighty**. This was the reason why "He" was able to survive to the [Fifth Epoch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fifth_Epoch "Fifth Epoch").[^3]

### Second Epoch

According to [Sasrir](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sasrir "Sasrir"), not long after the [Ancient Sun God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ancient_Sun_God "Ancient Sun God") walked out of the [Chaos Sea](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chaos_Sea) in the [Second Epoch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Second_Epoch "Second Epoch"), "He" sensed that the **Primordial God Almighty** was awakening inside "His" body. Therefore, "He" deliberately split a portion of "His" persona, fusing the [authorities of **Degeneration**](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniquenesses#Hanged_Man_Pathway) with the [Hanged Man Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hanged_Man_Pathway "Hanged Man Pathway") 's [Beyonder Characteristic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristic "Beyonder Characteristic"), creating [Sasrir](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sasrir "Sasrir") to control the [Chaos Sea](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chaos_Sea) and to isolate it from "His" true body to prevent any contamination and corruption. Later, "He" made another attempt to fight against the awakening of the **Primordial God Almighty** in "Him", by having children to decrease "His" Beyonder Characteristics, leading to the birth of [Adam](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Adam "Adam") and [Amon](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Amon "Amon").[^21] But in the end, the **Primordial God Almighty** still awoke in "His" body.

### Third Epoch

Just as the [Ancient Sun God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ancient_Sun_God "Ancient Sun God") and "His" faction were about to win against the [Evernight Goddess](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Evernight_Goddess "Evernight Goddess"), [God of Combat](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/God_of_Combat "God of Combat"), and [Earth Mother](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Earth_Mother "Earth Mother") completely, "He" began to have problems with the revival of the **Primordial God Almighty** inside "Himself". This was why "He" convened the various [Kings of Angels](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Kings_of_Angels "Kings of Angels") and [Subsidiary Gods](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Subsidiary_Gods "Subsidiary Gods") with the [Evernight Goddess](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Evernight_Goddess "Evernight Goddess") to establish the [Rose Redemption](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Rose_Redemption "Rose Redemption") to assassinate "Himself", so that after reviving and escaping the **Primordial God Almighty**, "He" would truly gain control of the [Chaos Sea](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chaos_Sea) and the corresponding five pathways. Ultimately, the [Ancient Sun God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ancient_Sun_God "Ancient Sun God") was successfully killed, but "His" revival plan failed because of the betrayal of the three [Kings of Angels](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Kings_of_Angels "Kings of Angels").[^21] The [Ancient Sun God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ancient_Sun_God "Ancient Sun God") was unable to foresee the betrayal of the three [Kings of Angels](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Kings_of_Angels "Kings of Angels") because of interference from the **Primordial God Almighty**.[^22]

### Fourth Epoch

In the [First Epoch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/First_Epoch "First Epoch"), the **Primordial God Almighty** left another revival plan in the two pathways of [Calamity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Calamity_of_Destruction). When the [Chaos Demoness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Demoness_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Demoness_\(Chaos_Demoness,_Primordial_Demoness\)) combined with the [Red Priest](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Priest_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Red_Priest), "He" would become the Trinity, in the [City of Calamity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/City_of_Calamity) and within the [Chaos Demoness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Demoness_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Demoness_\(Chaos_Demoness,_Primordial_Demoness\)) and the [Red Priest](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Priest_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Red_Priest), simultaneously resurrecting, ultimately forming a whole, returning in the mirror. But "He" perished too early, and "His" arrangements were not as expected, despite the union of the [Primordial Demoness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Primordial_Demoness "Primordial Demoness") and [Alista Tudor](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Alista_Tudor "Alista Tudor") in the [Fourth Epoch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Epoch "Fourth Epoch"). The [City of Calamity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/City_of_Calamity "City of Calamity") was sealed in the [Western Continent](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Western_Continent "Western Continent") by the [Celestial Worthy](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Celestial_Worthy "Celestial Worthy"), preventing "Him" from quickly becoming the Trinity; in addition, there was also another change on the [City of Calamity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/City_of_Calamity "City of Calamity") 's side. "His" consciousness revived in the [Primordial Demoness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Primordial_Demoness "Primordial Demoness") and [Alista Tudor](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Alista_Tudor "Alista Tudor"), but without the [City of Calamity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/City_of_Calamity "City of Calamity") 's resonance and linkage, that consciousness grew very slowly, and was suppressed and controlled by the two. With the combination of the initial feminine and masculine sides of the [Original Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Original_Creator "Original Creator") symbolized by the [Chaos Demoness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Demoness_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Demoness_\(Chaos_Demoness,_Primordial_Demoness\)) and the [Red Priest](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Priest_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Red_Priest), the consciousness of the **Primordial God Almighty**, and the [Special Mirror World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Special_Mirror_Worlds "Special Mirror Worlds") created by [Bethel Abraham](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Bethel_Abraham "Bethel Abraham") who was influenced by the [godhood](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Godhood "Godhood") of the [Celestial Worthy](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Celestial_Worthy "Celestial Worthy"), the development of things deviated from the **Primordial God Almighty** 's arrangement, and led to the consciousness of the [Mirrored Original Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mirrored_Original_Creator "Mirrored Original Creator") to be born, though "His" consciousness was very weak, like all newborn infants.[^23]

### Fifth Epoch

#### The Fool's Dream

In [The Fool's Dream](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/The_Fool%27s_Dream "The Fool's Dream"), the five figures of "Peng Deng", "Grisha", "Nie Zhen", "the boss of Aurora Company", and "Zhang Yujia" corresponded to (not in order) the five identities: the [Ancient Sun God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ancient_Sun_God "Ancient Sun God"), the [True Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/True_Creator "True Creator"), [Adam](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Adam "Adam"), the **Primordial God Almighty**, and [Sasrir](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sasrir "Sasrir").[^24] [^25]

- "Peng Deng" and "Grisha" ("Peng Deng's" roomate) symbolized [Adam](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Adam "Adam") and the **Primordial God Almighty**, but their specific positions are not fixed. They can swap at any time, symbolizing fusion and confrontation.
- "Nie Zhen" ("Peng Deng's" girlfriend) symbolized [Sasrir](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sasrir "Sasrir"), representing the part that split off from the [Ancient Sun God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ancient_Sun_God "Ancient Sun God"). Adam's rib is his Eve.
- "The boss of Aurora Company" symbolized the [True Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/True_Creator "True Creator").
	- The [True Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/True_Creator "True Creator") and [Adam](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Adam "Adam") had already achieved consciousness fusion, so "They" don't need to share rent anymore. They can act under different identities through [The Fool](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/The_Fool "The Fool") 's cognition, but "Their" thoughts are interconnected.
- "Zhang Yujia" ("Zhou Mingrui's" and "Peng Deng's" childhood friend, who once sent "Zhou Mingrui" a postcard with an image of the [Corpse Cathedral](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Corpse_Cathedral "Corpse Cathedral")) symbolized the [Ancient Sun God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ancient_Sun_God "Ancient Sun God"). The [Corpse Cathedral](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Corpse_Cathedral "Corpse Cathedral") itself belonged to "Him". "Zhang Yujia" wandered outside the dream city, symbolizing that the [Ancient Sun God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ancient_Sun_God "Ancient Sun God") had already left this stage. Now "His" successors and the **Primordial God Almighty** were confronting each other.

The **Primordial God Almighty** was one of the people who least wanted the [Celestial Worthy](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Celestial_Worthy "Celestial Worthy") to resurrect. As "Peng Deng", "He" was the previous renter of the [Magic Mirror Arrodes](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Arrodes "Arrodes") from the Star Dream Provisions Store since the birth of that magic mirror was related to "Him",[^26] presumably to set up the trap regarding the [Mirrored Original Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mirrored_Original_Creator "Mirrored Original Creator") to the [Celestial Worthy](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Celestial_Worthy "Celestial Worthy") later on.

"He" did various actions to help [Lumian](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee") 's team to oppose the [Celestial Worthy](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Celestial_Worthy "Celestial Worthy"). "He" appeared as "Grisha" and specifically arranged to guide [Lumian](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee") to discover the corresponding psychological trauma of [Klein](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") in the underground area of Mushu Hospital. Later on, "He" appeared as "Peng Deng" when talking with [Anthony](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Anthony_Reid "Anthony Reid") and gave him the hint that "He" was not only "Zhou Mingrui"'s childhood friend, but also the [Celestial Worthy](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Celestial_Worthy "Celestial Worthy") 's childhood friend.[^24] [^25]

As a top-tier Dreamweaver, the **Primordial God Almighty** also helped [Medici](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sauron-Einhorn-Medici "Sauron-Einhorn-Medici"), who already had a corresponding manifestation in the dream city, to come into the Dream.[^25]

#### The Day of Many Disasters

After [The Hostel](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/The_Hostel "The Hostel") incident,[^27] the **Primordial God Almighty** made a contract with the [Mother Goddess of Depravity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Goddess_of_Depravity "Mother Goddess of Depravity"): "He" would help "Her" bypass the barrier and reclaim the [Brood Hive](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Brood_Hive "Brood Hive") and the corresponding [Uniquenesses](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniquenesses "Uniquenesses") and [Beyonder Characteristics](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristics "Beyonder Characteristics"), and "She" would help "Him" revive. In addition, the [Mother Goddess of Depravity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Goddess_of_Depravity "Mother Goddess of Depravity") also entered a contract with the [Mother Tree of Desire](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Tree_of_Desire "Mother Tree of Desire"), witnessed by the **Primordial God Almighty**, where the [Mother Tree of Desire](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Tree_of_Desire "Mother Tree of Desire") would sacrifice the [Tree of Shadow](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tree_of_Shadow "Tree of Shadow") to help the [Mother Goddess of Depravity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Goddess_of_Depravity "Mother Goddess of Depravity") descend on Earth in [The Day of Many Disasters](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/The_Day_of_Many_Disasters "The Day of Many Disasters"). In return, the [Mother Goddess of Depravity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Goddess_of_Depravity "Mother Goddess of Depravity") would need to give assistance to the [Mother Tree of Desire](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Tree_of_Desire "Mother Tree of Desire") later.[^28] [^29]

During [The Day of Many Disasters](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/The_Day_of_Many_Disasters "The Day of Many Disasters"), the **Primordial God Almighty** had two chances to resurrect. The first chance was when [Lumian](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee") was forced to fuse with a split-off self from the [Primordial Demoness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Primordial_Demoness "Primordial Demoness"), the mirrored [Alista Tudor](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Alista_Tudor "Alista Tudor"), both the masculine and feminine aspect of the [Mirrored Original Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mirrored_Original_Creator "Mirrored Original Creator"), and a [Apocalypse](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Demoness_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_1:_Apocalypse_\(Demoness_of_Apocalypse\)) and [Conqueror](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Priest_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_1:_Conqueror) characteristic. The balance was lost with [Jenna](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Jenna "Jenna") 's advancement to [Demoness of Unaging](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Demoness_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_3:_Unaging_\(Demoness_of_Unaging\)) which attracted the feminine [Mirrored Original Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mirrored_Original_Creator "Mirrored Original Creator") away from [Lumian](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee"), before it was regained with the addition of the [Mother](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Goddess_of_Depravity "Mother Goddess of Depravity") 's powers [^5] via [Omebella](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Omebella "Omebella") 's bloodline and [Zedus](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Zedus "Zedus") 's vitalized soul fragments inside Lumian. After the balance was regained, [Lumian](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee") 's body became the resurrection cornerstone for the **Primordial God Almighty**, who manifested as a **Chaotic Vortex Face**. The [Primordial Demoness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Primordial_Demoness "Primordial Demoness") was also forced to retreat to resist against the **Primordial God Almighty** 's reviving consciousness. The **Chaotic Vortex Face** was eventually sealed by [The Fool](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") using the [dark-golden mask](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Demon_Mask "Demon Mask") taken from [Loki](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Loki "Loki").[^3]

The second chance happened during [Omebella](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Omebella "Omebella") 's birth. Since the **Primordial God Almighty** was [Omebella](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Omebella "Omebella") 's father, "Her" birth strengthened the symbolism of the **Father** on the [Eternal Blazing Sun](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Eternal_Blazing_Sun "Eternal Blazing Sun"), causing "Him" to lose control of the fragile balance. This attempt was thwarted when the [Eternal Blazing Sun](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Eternal_Blazing_Sun "Eternal Blazing Sun") voluntarily choose death at the hands of the [Evernight Goddess](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Evernight_Goddess "Evernight Goddess").

During the event, [Naboredisley](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Naboredisley "Naboredisley") also experienced the revival of the malicious consciousness and frenzied spirit of the **Primordial God Almighty** because "He" had accommodated the [Tenebrous World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tenebrous_World "Tenebrous World") in the [First Epoch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/First_Epoch "First Epoch"). However, the revival of the **Primordial God Almighty** was short-lived, with "His" consciousness retreating to the [Chaos Sea](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chaos_Sea "Chaos Sea"), leaving only "His" malicious spirit behind, preventing a true balance to be reached with the [Mother Tree of Desire](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Tree_of_Desire "Mother Tree of Desire") 's corruption, though it did improve [Naboredisley](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Naboredisley "Naboredisley") 's state.[^9]

The **Primordial God Almighty** retracted "His" powers and influence over [Edefana](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Edefana "Edefana") to focus on "His" two resurrection opportunities, causing [Edefana](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Edefana "Edefana") to age and eventually died.[^3]

Lastly, the **Primordial God Almighty** successfully persuaded [Grisha Adam](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Grisha_Adam "Grisha Adam") to enter the [Chaos Sea](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chaos_Sea "Chaos Sea") and merge with "Him", as the [Mother Goddess of Depravity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Goddess_of_Depravity "Mother Goddess of Depravity") would soon descend onto Earth through the strengthened symbolism of [Omebella](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Omebella "Omebella") 's birth, and time was running out.[^17] This gave [Grisha Adam](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Grisha_Adam "Grisha Adam") enough strength to cooperate with [The Fool](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/The_Fool "The Fool") to create the [Protected Zones](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Protected_Zones "Protected Zones") to save Earth from the initial impact of the [Mother Goddess of Depravity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Goddess_of_Depravity "Mother Goddess of Depravity") 's descend on Earth.[^30]

#### Before The Apocalypse

In the next one year when the [Mother Goddess of Depravity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Goddess_of_Depravity "Mother Goddess of Depravity") was chasing and accommodating the [Brood Hive](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Brood_Hive "Brood Hive") within the gray fog sealing the [Western Continent](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Western_Continent "Western Continent"), [Grisha Adam](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Grisha_Adam "Grisha Adam") and the **Primordial God Almighty** were locked in a fierce struggle, with the **Primordial God Almighty** gaining the upper hand.

When [Lumian](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee") went to meet with [Grisha Adam](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Grisha_Adam "Grisha Adam"), he removed the [dark-golden mask](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Demon_Mask "Demon Mask") originated from the [Celestial Worthy](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Celestial_Worthy "Celestial Worthy"), which gave the **Primordial God Almighty** a choice. If "He" diverted "His" focus to influence [Lumian](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee") 's head and face to resurrect there, symbolically, it would mean abandoning the contest with [Grisha Adam](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Grisha_Adam "Grisha Adam"), causing "Him" to gain back the advantage. If "He" refused to retreat or give up, the revival of [Lumian](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee") 's head and face would stagnate, yet their unique qualities will remain intact, allowing [Lumian](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee") to make use of their powers. The **Primordial God Almighty** chose not to revive on [Lumian](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee").[^5]

#### The Apocalypse

When [The Apocalypse](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/The_Apocalypse "The Apocalypse") began, [Grisha Adam](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Grisha_Adam "Grisha Adam") merged with the [Uniquenesses](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniquenesses "Uniquenesses") and [Beyonder Characteristics](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristics "Beyonder Characteristics") from the [Lord of Storms](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lord_of_Storms "Lord of Storms") and the [God of Knowledge and Wisdom](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/God_of_Knowledge_and_Wisdom "God of Knowledge and Wisdom") to temporarily achieve a fragile balance using "Their" remnant psyche.[^31] This gave him the strength of a Quasi- [God Almighty](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/God_Almighty) and allowed "Him" to hold off the [Primordial Hunger](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Primordial_Hunger "Primordial Hunger") for the duration of the battle. However, during the battle, [Grisha Adam](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Grisha_Adam "Grisha Adam") was first affected by the [Inextinguishable Ravings](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Inextinguishable_Ravings "Inextinguishable Ravings"),[^32] and then the [Mother Goddess of Depravity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Goddess_of_Depravity "Mother Goddess of Depravity") 's **New Life**,[^29] which strengthened the consciousness of the **Primordial God Almighty** and made "His" resurrection imminent, even when put into a slumber by the [Eternal Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Evernight_Goddess "Evernight Goddess").[^33]

Towards the end of the battle, [Klein](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") proposed a temporary truce to the [Mother Goddess of Depravity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Goddess_of_Depravity "Mother Goddess of Depravity"). He modified the contract drafted by the **Primordial God Almighty**, which was originally exceptionally tight and without exploitable **Loopholes**, into producing one: "The time limit is until the conclusion of this event". As the [Mother Goddess of Depravity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Goddess_of_Depravity "Mother Goddess of Depravity") hadn't yet reclaimed the [Mother Uniqueness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniquenesses#Mother_Pathway) and [Moon Uniqueness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniquenesses#Moon_Pathway), nor achieved all "Her" objectives, the current event wasn't considered over, allowing "Her" to retreat and return back at a later time.[^34]

After the battle finished, [Grisha Adam](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Grisha_Adam "Grisha Adam") accepted "His" death at the hands of [Lumian Lee](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee") to stop the awakening of the **Primordial God Almighty**. After the **Primordial God Almighty** was killed, "He" hadn't tried to resurrect within Lumian, as adding anything else to [Lumian](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee") 's body would render even [the most powerful and genuine Angel of Redemption](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Circle_of_Inevitability "Circle of Inevitability") unable to redeem the situation.

After the **Primordial God Almighty** 's near resurrection in [The Apocalypse](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/The_Apocalypse "The Apocalypse"), "His" consciousness had significantly strengthened. Without hundreds or thousands of years to wear it down, none of the [God Almighty](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/God_Almighty) 's five pathways are suitable for birthing a corresponding Sequence 0.[^35]

## Trivia

- It is strongly implied that the **Primordial God Almighty** corresponds to the God of the Abrahamic faiths.
	- The term **[God Almighty](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/God_Almighty "God Almighty")** (上帝, *Supreme Thearch*, *[Shàngdì](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shangdi "wikipedia:Shangdi")*) in Chinese refers to the absolute god of the universe. It is commonly used as a non-religious translation for the Abrahamic God.
		- With regards to the [Chaos Sea](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chaos_Sea "Chaos Sea"), in biblical literature, the sea frequently symbolizes chaos and disorder. In Genesis 1:2, the sea is depicted as a primordial abyss over which the Spirit of God hovers. This imagery underscores the sea as a symbol of the chaotic forces that God orders and subdues.[^36]
		- The novel mentioned the [Pre-Epoch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pre-Epoch "Pre-Epoch") myth of the **God Almighty of Heaven** creating a divine child, who, as an Archangel, led several Angels in rebellion before being defeated and casted into the Abyss, creating Hell and turning them to devils.[^9] This myth likely refers to the story of [Lucifer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lucifer "wikipedia:Lucifer").
		- The author confirmed that the **Primordial God Almighty** accommodating the [City of Calamity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/City_of_Calamity "City of Calamity") corresponds to the God of War and Storm, the Lord of Armies [YHWH](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yahweh "wikipedia:Yahweh") in [Canaanite mythology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canaanite_religion "wikipedia:Canaanite religion").[^37]
