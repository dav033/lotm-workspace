## Summary

Describes the Door Pathway as granting unrestrained freedom of movement (phasing through walls, tricky spells, Astrology-based divination, recording others' Beyonder powers, and Spirit World travel achieving teleportation), expanding at High Sequence into direct space manipulation, Concealment, replication without prior Recording, gravity manipulation, universal location, and alternate-space creation. States the Pathway represents the concepts of Door, Alternate Worlds, Seals, and Space. Above-the-Sequence figure is the Lord of Mysteries; corresponding Sefirah is Sefirah Castle. Pathway exchange is possible only from Sequence 3 upward.

## Extracted facts

- Symbol: "Numerous Layers upon Layers of Illusory Doors."
- Corresponding Tarot card: The Magician (I).
- Mythical Creature Form: a huge humanoid figure composed of light-balls formed by Worms of Star, constructed from layers of Doors; the Pinnacle Mythical Creature Form is described as the Door of all Doors, a key to all mysterious worlds, with Worms of Stars condensed from starlight roaming countless Doors, twisting Space and traversing the Spirit World.
- Neighbouring Pathways (other Pathways under the Lord of Mysteries): Fool, Error.
- Compatible Pathways: Sublunary Eye (High-Dimensional Overseer), Tail-Devourer (Primordial Hunger).
- Non-adjacent Pathway with a hidden neighbouring relationship: Justiciar (The Anarchy), due to authority overlap with the Lord of Mysteries group.
- It is uncertain whether the Sublunary Eye Pathway can match Door's command of alternate/illusory-space domains.
