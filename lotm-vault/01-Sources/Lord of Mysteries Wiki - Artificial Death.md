The **Artificial Death** was an attempted plan during the [Fifth Epoch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fifth_Epoch "Fifth Epoch") to turn the [Uniqueness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniqueness "Uniqueness") of the [Death Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway "Death Pathway") into a sentient being by the [Numinous Episcopate](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Numinous_Episcopate "Numinous Episcopate") 's **Artificial Death** faction after their [God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/God "God"), [Death](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death "Death"), perished at the hands of the [Seven Orthodox Gods](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Seven_Orthodox_Gods "Seven Orthodox Gods") at the end of the [Fourth Epoch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Epoch "Fourth Epoch").

The **Artificial Death** is made from the [Death Uniqueness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniquenesses#Death_Pathway) along with half of [Death Consul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_2:_Death_Consul) [Azik Eggers](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Azik_Eggers "Azik Eggers") 's soul. It was originally a resurrection plan left by [Death](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death "Death").

The **Artificial Death** is an [Activated](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Activated "Activated") [Uniqueness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniqueness "Uniqueness") that's combined with two types of [Sefirot](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirot "Sefirot") corruption brought by [Death](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death "Death").[^1] It doesn't have any Sequence 1 characteristic attached.[^2]

## Appearance

It was a massive feathered serpent that seemed to occupy an entire island. It had huge, dark green-nearly black-scales. Amidst the gaps were feathers covered in yellow oily stains, on every feather, there were thin illusory black tubes extending outwards. The exaggerated feathered serpent was both illusory and real, its actual form was hardly describable. It seemed to be a combination of things incomprehensible to humans. Its eye sockets were burning with pale-white flames. Its face was that of a human's, it looked like another [Azik Eggers](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Azik_Eggers "Azik Eggers").[^3]

## History

### Origins

The original goal of the project was to revive [Death](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death "Death"). The present upper echelons of the [Numinous Episcopate](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Numinous_Episcopate "Numinous Episcopate") had gained inspiration from the [Hidden Sage](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hidden_Sage "Hidden Sage") 's sudden coming to life and turning anthropomorphic.[^4] They used a series of sacrificial rituals and the transformation of their own High-Sequence Beyonders across hundreds of years to influence [Death](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death "Death") 's remains via backward propagation, causing the [Death Uniqueness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniquenesses#Death_Pathway) to generate some form of sentience.[^5] Though they failed in reviving [Death](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death "Death"), the [Numinous Episcopate](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Numinous_Episcopate "Numinous Episcopate") managed to create an artificial god. It also gains certain innate abilities as it began influencing any High-Sequence Death Beyonders who failed their advancements, like the former chief of the [City of Silver](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/City_of_Silver "City of Silver"), [Hawick](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hawick "Hawick"). However, it was still unable to reply to prayers and rituals.[^6]

### Destruction

[Azik Eggers](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Azik_Eggers "Azik Eggers") and Klein explored the [Death](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death "Death") 's treasure trove at the [Berserk Sea](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Berserk_Sea "Berserk Sea") where the **Artificial Death** is, resulting in the attempted fusion of both halves of [Azik Eggers](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Azik_Eggers "Azik Eggers") ' soul and starting [Death](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death "Death") 's revival. But the [Evernight Goddess](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Evernight_Goddess "Evernight Goddess") prevented the resurrection of [Death](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death "Death") by appearing at the last minute and taking the [Uniqueness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniqueness "Uniqueness") of the [Death Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway "Death Pathway").[^7]

### Consequences

Because the [Evernight Goddess](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Evernight_Goddess "Evernight Goddess") obtained the [Uniqueness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniqueness "Uniqueness"), the [God of Combat](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/God_of_Combat "God of Combat") and "His" allies started the [1350 World War](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/1350_World_War "1350 World War") to prevent "Her" progression.
