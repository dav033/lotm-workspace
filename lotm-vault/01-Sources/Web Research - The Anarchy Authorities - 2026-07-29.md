# Web Research - The Anarchy Authorities - 2026-07-29

## Method

- WebNovel was used only to verify the official works, author, and publication records. Relevant paid chapter text was not independently inspected during this pass.
- The community wiki ability pages were used as secondary indexes to archived author supplements and named novel chapters.
- An archived author-post transcript remains unofficial/medium evidence until its original publisher and exact text are independently verified.

## Black Emperor Findings

- Sequence 0 is indexed with Autocrat as the core Authority beneath Shadow of Order. Distortion, Exploit, Definition, and Strata are subordinate expressions used to bend an existing order toward sovereign advantage.
- Distortion changes the operational relationship among words, actions, intentions, targets, outcomes, and rules. Its lower progression redirects attacks or seals spaces; its higher progression can affect authority-created rules, destinations, substitutions, and mystical connections under conditions.
- Disorder is a separate Authority, not the parent of Distortion. It progresses from disrupted judgment, direction, distance, structure, and accuracy into Frenzy, Entropy, and large-scale breakdown of ordered concepts and thought.
- The Black Emperor holds the larger, active share of Disorder. This is distinct from the title term 失序 and from the fate-oriented Disorder of the Wheel of Fortune Pathway.

## Justiciar Findings

- Sequence 0 is indexed with Order as the core Authority and Rules, Laws, and Principles beneath it. Judgment, Balance, and a limited share of Disorder remain distinct Authorities.
- Rules are operative prescriptions and conditions. Laws are codified, enforceable decrees (律法, *lüfa*). Principles are regularities or patterns (规律, *guilü*). English translation can blur the last two categories.
- Order can establish, amend, or remove rules within scope. Lower expressions apply local verdicts and prohibitions; high-Sequence expressions can regulate battlefields, cities, nations, or larger domains, with stronger resistance around underlying rules.
- Judgment applies consequences to violations of an established order. Balance partitions conflicts or temporarily reduces asymmetry. Justiciar Disorder is mainly used to perceive, calm, restore, or extinguish disorder, although Order can also introduce controlled disorder.

## Novel Anchors Indexed by the Ability Pages

- Lord of Mysteries: Chapters 425, 559, 599, 659, 684, 808-809, 1025-1027, 1144, 1148, 1150, 1280, 1283, 1285-1289, 1304-1305, and 1379 for Distortion, Disorder, Definition, Order, Judgment, and the Trunsoest Brass Book.
- Circle of Inevitability: Chapters 698, 875, 883, 977, 1132-1133, 1138, and 1162 for Balance, high-level Distortion, mystical connections, and Order interactions.

## Boundary

This note records secondary web verification. It does not convert indexed author supplements or chapter references into `official/high` evidence, and it does not support claims that Distortion can rewrite anything without conditions or that Balance creates permanent equality.
