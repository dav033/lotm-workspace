## Sequence 3: Arcane Scholar (Demon of Arcana)

### Potion Properties

> A transparent liquid in which sparks unceasingly appear and vanish.^{[1]}

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> An illusory, flame-shaped flower containing boundless knowledge.^{[1]}

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

  * 1 Spirit World Meteor.^{[1]}
  * 1 Crystallization of an Annihilation Ghost.^{[1]}

Or
  * A Arcane Scholar Beyonder Characteristic.

Supplementary Ingredients

  * Choose one of:
    * 1 type of combustible material from the Spirit World Star-Fire.^{[1]}
    * 30 grams of dust from the Annihilation Ghost.^{[1]}
Or
    * 40 grams of dust from an Arcane Scholar.^{[1]}
    * 1 object once possessed by an Arcane Scholar.^{[1]}
  * 90 milliliters of water contaminated by the Spirit World.^{[1]}
  * 50 grams of a highly radioactive material.^{[1]}

### Advancement Ritual

Select a certain special Spirit World knowledge and find a way to establish a close connection with it, achieving integration at the spiritual level.^{[1]}

  * Note: "Certain special Spirit World knowledge" refers to knowledge that can contaminate you, lock onto you, or knowledge that has come to life.^{[3]}

## Sequence 2: Knowledge Magister (Master)

### Potion Properties

> An icy liquid stretched into a distorted humanoid shape.^{[1]}

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> A semi-illusory, semi-real book composed of a diversity of knowledge.^{[1]}

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

  * 1 "Otherworldly Brain" (a brain-like creature capable of creating a realm with physical rules that differ from reality).^{[1]}

Or
  * A Knowledge Magister Beyonder Characteristic.

Supplementary Ingredients

  * 5,000 milliliters of water from the "Knowledge Sea" formed after the death of a Knowledge Magister or the "Otherworldly Brain".^{[1]}
  * One copy of one's own work that has been cited or utilized by more than 100,000 people (recorded on paper).^{[1]}
  * A blessing from any of the Seven Lights of the Spirit World.^{[1]}
  * Heartfelt tokens of gratitude from different students of the concoctor (at least 1,000).^{[1]}

### Advancement Ritual

Make a groundbreaking and systematically outstanding contribution in any existing field.^{[1]}

## Sequence 1: Illuminator (Civilization Enlightener, Light of Darkness)

### Potion Properties

> A pool of black liquid with a faint but clear radiance shining from within.^{[1]}

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> A weighty yet ethereal picture scroll of a civilization.^{[1]}

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

  * A Illuminator Beyonder Characteristic

Supplementary Ingredients

  * 100,000 souls of those who perished during a civilization's decline.^{[1]}
  * 1 History book recording a civilization.^{[1]}
  * 1 of the most outstanding mechanical creations from a civilization (must be near perfect).^{[1]}
  * 12 miniature idols sculpted by one's students out of worship and faith.^{[1]}

### Advancement Ritual

Popularize new knowledge to promote the civilizational renewal of at least one country.^{[1]}

## Sequence 0: Paragon

### Potion Properties

> A flawless, ethereal humanoid form emitting a faint glow.^{[1]}

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

  * The Paragon Uniqueness.
  * Three Illuminator Beyonder Characteristics.

Supplementary Ingredients

  * Knowledge gleaned from the Aura or remnants of the Great Old Ones.^{[1]}

### Advancement Ritual

Nurture a Civilization, witnessing and recording its entire process from enlightenment, development and prosperity to decline, demise or replacement.^{[1]}

  * Note: A regional civilization is sufficient. The main point is the long duration, and it cannot be interrupted.^{[4]}
