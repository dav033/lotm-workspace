## Sequence 9: Warrior (Divine Blood Warrior, Beyonder Warrior)

**Traits** [^2] [^3] [^4] [^5]

- **Physical Enhancement**: They possess supernatural strength and agility that greatly transcends those who are not **Beyonders**.[^6]

**New Abilities** [^2] [^3] [^4] [^5]

- **Combat Mastery** (**Weapon**, **Armor**, **Martial Arts**): They have a mastery of all kinds of **Weapons** and **Armor**, with there being no **Weapon** they can't use.
	- **Warriors** also possess a mastery of all forms of **Combat** and **Martial Arts**, with there being no fighting style they can't learn.

## Sequence 8: Pugilist (Gladiator)

**Traits** [^2] [^3] [^7]

- **Physical Enhancement**: Their physical abilities alone can weaken the effects of some **Beyonder** powers targeting them.

**New Abilities** [^2] [^3] [^7] **[^5]**

- **Supernatural Resistance**: **Pugilists** are experts in **Combat** that are able to use their own bodies in order to mitigate the effects of certain supernatural forces.[^7]

**Strengthened Abilities** [^2] [^3] [^7]

- **Combat Mastery** (**Close Combat**): **Pugilists** are more adept at close-quarters **Combat** than any other **Sequence**.

## Sequence 7: Weapon Master

**Traits**

- **Physical Enhancement**: They possess a superhuman strength, physique, and agility.

**New Abilities** [^2] [^3] [^5]

- **Weapon Mastery**: With their **Physical Enhancement**, as long as it is a **Weapon**, once it is in their hands, they can use it at a master-level standard.
	- This includes **Beyonder Weapons**, [Mystical Items](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mystical_Item "Mystical Item"), and [Sealed Artifacts](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sealed_Artifact "Sealed Artifact") in the form of swords, guns, etc. and they can effectively reduce the damage caused by said items negative effects.

## Sequence 6: Dawn Paladin

**Traits** [^2] [^3] [^8] [^9] [^10] **[^5]**

- **Strength of Giants**: **Dawn Paladins** possess **Giant** -like strength, powerful offensive capability, and relatively high defense.[^11] [^12]
	- By now, their bodies are muscular, well attuned to combat and their height is further increased.
		- **Dawn Paladins** can burst in speeds akin to a steam train accelerating at its highest speed.[^13]
		- With a low growl, [Father Utravsky](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Utravsky "Utravsky") charged through the flames created by [Klein Moretti](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") at the maximum speed of a steam train.
		- Even after experiencing a long drawn fight, a **Dawn Paladin's** stamina will seem to be limitless.[^13]
		- [Utravsky](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Utravsky "Utravsky") was able to attack multiple times in long sessions with his two-handed broadsword despite having been weakened as if experiencing a long drawn fight.
		- The force that a **Dawn Paladin** can strike with their weapons can create strong gusts.[^13]
		- The winds created by a weakened [Father Utravsky's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Utravsky "Utravsky") two-handed broadsword were so strong that it caused a **Sequence 7** [Klein Moretti](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") to almost lose his balance.
		- Aside from their natural height becoming taller, **Dawn Paladins** can further temporarily increase their height.[^12] [^11]
		- Descendants might be born with grayish-blue skin possessed by common **Giants**.[^14]

**New Abilities** [^2] [^3] [^8] [^9] [^10] **[^5]**

- **Light of Dawn** **(Sunrise Gleam)**:[^1] A **Dawn Paladin** can fill an area of roughly 40 to 50 meters around them with pure, **Sacred Dawn Light**, which dispel **Illusions**, **Exorcise Wraiths** and **Shadows**, and even weaken [Evil Spirits](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Evil_Spirits "Evil Spirits").[^8] [^9]
	- It can dispel powers or energies leaning toward **Evil**, **Corruption**, and **Degeneration**, but in the **Domain** of the **Holy** and **Light**, the **Light of Dawn** is still inferior to a **[Light Suppliant's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sun_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_8:_Light_Suppliant)** powers.[^15] [^2]
		- The **Light of Dawn** can erase **Shadows** and nullify **Concealment** effects, such as **[Witches'](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Demoness_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_7:_Witch) Invisibility**. For **[Wraiths](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chained_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_5:_Wraith)** and **[Shadow Ascetics](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hanged_Man_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_7:_Shadow_Ascetic)**, their peculiarities are diminished.[^10]
		- A **Dawn Paladin's Light of Dawn** is also able to **Purify** mystical **Pathogens** in the environment, reduce radiation, and interfere with **Electromagnetic Fields**.
		- Except when dealing with **Wraiths** and **Shadows**, its **Mysticism** level is very low.
- **Dawn Armor****:** They can conjure a silver **Armor of Dawn** around their bodies which is equivalent to a refined full-body armor that doesn't have noticeable weight or inhibit their movement in any way.
	- It can recover from damage, but their stamina will automatically be drained during the process.[^16]
		- It will also take a certain amount of time to recover from that damage.
		- A **Dawn Paladin's Dawn Armor** includes gauntlets, breastplates, and a helmet.[^13]
- **Hurricane of Light**: Their most powerful attack. By inserting the **Sword of Dawn** into the ground and shattering it, they will form countless fragments of **Dawn** -like light that create a shimmering, violently sharp **Hurricane** filled with destructive power, cutting every **Shadow** and **Corrupted** entities.
	- **Evil** creatures like **[Devils](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Abyss_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_6:_Devil)** or **[Zombies](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chained_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_6:_Zombie)** would be subjected to the effects of **Purification** and the damage of a **Fragmentary Blade**. The combination of these aspects causes their **Hurricane of Light** to become excellent at purging **Evil**.
		- **Purification** weakens defense and inflicts harm on the **Evil** creature's spirit and flesh, while the **Fragmentary Blade** utilizes **Purification** to weaken defense and cut flesh. The more wounds and the deeper they were, the better the **Purification** effect.
			- **Hurricane of Light** would be even more effective on [Spirits](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Evil_Spirits "Evil Spirits") (such as a **[Wraiths](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chained_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_5:_Wraith)**) leaving the target close to death.[^20]
		- The **Hurricane of Light** can directly destroy a person's body, eliminate **[Wraiths](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chained_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_5:_Wraith)**, and even heavily wound [Evil Spirits](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Evil_Spirits "Evil Spirits"). It also possesses a unique strength that can more easily destroy creatures of the **Evil** and **Undead Domains**.[^20]
		- Their **Hurricane of Light** is stronger than a **Sword of Lava** (which is stronger than the **Light of Holiness**) when it comes to damaging non- **Evil** domain beings.
				- Although **Hurricane of Light** is an area-of-effect ability, a **Dawn Paladin** is able to control its direction to a certain extent.
		- After activation, the **Sword of Dawn** cannot be condensed for a short time. In addition, a **Dawn Paladin** would need at least more than *2 minutes* of recovery before they can form another **Hurricane of Light**.[^21] [^22]

**Strengthened Abilities**

- **Dawn Paladins** experience a qualitative change.[^9] [^10]
	- Each of a **Dawn Paladin's Combat** related abilities, whether it be **Combat Mastery** or **Weapon Mastery**, will be enhanced massively.

## Sequence 5: Guardian

**Traits**

- **Strength of Giants**: Compared to a **Dawn Paladin**, a **Guardian** defense and strength is like an adult comparing their physique and strength to that of a child.
	- A **Guardian** would probably be able to withstand the bullets of a steam rifle, but in the wars of the steam era, they wouldn't die unless they were focused on by large-caliber cannons.[^23]

**New Abilities** [^2] [^3] [^5]

- **Protection**: **Guardians** are rarely injured. By kneeling on one knee and planting the Sword in front of them, **Dawn** -like light will emerge, forming two indestructible invisible walls on the left and right. This allows them to take on damage for companions within a certain range, **Protecting** them.[^19]
	- Once fully committed to defense and abandoning offense, few can break through a **Guardian's** defense below that of the **High-Sequences**. This is regardless of the types of damage the enemy can inflict and their corresponding effects.
		- Both **Protection** and **Dawn Armor** can be used simultaneously.[^24]
				- When they attack, their defense will be reduced significantly; however, it will still be far superior to that of refined full-body armor.
				- **[Reapers](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Priest_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_5:_Reaper)** are one of the few non **High-Sequence Beyonders** that can penetrate through a **Guardian's** defenses.
- **Illusion Immunity**: They can't be deceived by **Illusions**.[^23]

**Strengthened Abilities**

- They have gained improvements to other **Beyonder** powers from previous **Sequences**.
	- Their **Sword of Dawn**, **Hurricane of Light**, and other abilities can damage any creature.[^2]
		- **Supernatural Resistance**: This **Beyonder** power has been enhanced.
		- Within the same **Sequence** level, a **Guardian** can slow down the time of having their **Spirit Body Threads** controlled and thus being turned into a **Marionette**.[^25]

## Sequence 4: Demon Hunter (Witcher)

**Traits**

- **Physical & Mental Enhancement**: **Demon Hunters** possess a terrifying **Physical** strength and a **Mind** that stands between that of a [God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Gods "Gods") and a human.
	- They are capable of running at such high speed that it whips up hurricane-force winds.
- **Spirituality**: A **Demon Hunter's Spirituality** will be enhanced significantly.
	- They will possess a strong **Spiritual Intuition**.[^26]

**New Abilities** [^2] [^3] [^27] **[^5]**

- **Eye of Demon Hunting**: This is not a true visual ability but rather a general term for their capacity to identify a target's nature, weaknesses, condition, and whether they are **Corrupted** or **Possessed** by relying on their own senses, mental probing, and extensive experience.
	- They become sensitive to any traces of **Evil**, **Degeneration**, and **Corruption**. They can also rely on their sixth sense, **Spiritual Intuition**, and life experiences to judge the situation.[^28]
		- Veteran **Witchers** can use this to judge whether a target is lying.
				- Under their sixth sense, they can find the crux of the problem.[^29]
			- While not understanding how or the principles behind it, [Colin Iliad's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Colin_Iliad "Colin Iliad") rich experience in exploration and the intuition as a **Demon Hunter** made him believe that the statues were the reason for [Murskogan](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Murskogan "Murskogan") still being alive.
		- They can seemingly detect a **Silver Knight's** location when they use **Silver Rapier** within a short distance as well as be able to detect and block a **Silver Rapier**.[^30]
		- Using this will cause a complex, dark green symbol to appear in each of their eyes.
- **Knowledge** (**Hunting**, **Alchemy**): They are highly adept at dealing with all kinds of situations and possess knowledge of how different monster parts and herbs can be used. They are also skilled at discovering the weaknesses of various creatures and identifying the uses of various materials.[^31]
	- In a special **Cogitation** state, they can, based on the former and with the aid of the latter, concoct the corresponding *magical potions*, *holy ointments*, *essential oils* and *special marks.*
		- By ingesting, applying, or using these items, they can produce different extraordinary effects such as **Lightning Strikes**, **Freezing**, **Purification**, **Burning**, **Decay**, and **Exorcism**.
			- This allows them to counter targets in a highly targeted manner.
				- Orthodox **Demon Hunters** will typically wield dual swords so that different ointments can take effect simultaneously, enabling them to handle more complex situations.
- **Mind Concealment**: They can **Conceal** one's actions and intentions.
	- This effectively interferes with **Divination** and **Prophecy**, preventing even targets capable of foreseeing danger from detecting it—making them a nemesis of [Demons](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Abyss "Abyss").[^32] [^33]

**Strengthened Abilities**

- All of a **Demon Hunter's Beyonder** powers from the **Dawn Paladin** and **Guardian** stages undergo a qualitative transformation.
	- **Protection**: They can create an invisible and indestructible wall of defense around them. Once it is anchored to the ground, the power of **Protection** becomes extremely difficult to shake.
		- The power of **Protection** can even block traversal through the [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World") as well as block or weaken **Curse** -type abilities.[^34]
		- **Light of Dawn**: Their **Light of Dawn** can be condensed into objects other than weapons.
		- **Witchers** can blanket their weapons with **Dawn's** glow.[^35]

**Mythical Creature Form**

- As **Sequence 4 Demigods** they receive some level of **Godhood**, and with it comes an incomplete [Mythical Creature Form](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mythical_Creature_Form "Mythical Creature Form"). Disregarding all consequences, they can release their incomplete [Mythical Creature Form](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mythical_Creature_Form "Mythical Creature Form") to become an incomplete **Cyclops.[^36]** Due to their enhanced **Willpower**, the **Saints** of this Pathway have a higher probability of possessing a relatively routine [Mythical Creature Form](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mythical_Creature_Form "Mythical Creature Form").[^37]
	- Their incomplete [Mythical Creature Form](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mythical_Creature_Form "Mythical Creature Form") looks like a 4 meter tall **Cyclops**. Their skin turns bluish-black and muscular, and along with its pores and flesh, seemingly violating the normal confines of a human body. Their head swells up significantly, with the area from their forehead to nose cracking open to reveal a black vertical eye-like rift. On their body's surface contains symbols and the corresponding effects of *immense power*, *infinite mystery*, and a *strange psychic* *influence*.[^36]
		- If those under **Sequence 4** saw this form, they will suffer a strong backlash. Their brains might suffer an excruciating pain that would cause them to lose their minds, even their **Spirituality** would be corrupted. And if they couldn't withstand such an influence, going crazy, losing control, or even sudden death were certain outcomes.[^30]
				- On one hand, manifesting this incomplete [Mythical Creature Form](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mythical_Creature_Form "Mythical Creature Form") of a 4-meter tall one-eyed **Giant** will enhance a **Demon Hunter's** strength and level significantly, on the other hand, it would result in intense madness and provide strong inclinations towards the **Demon Hunter** losing control and becoming a [Rampager](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder#Rampager_\(Losing_Control\) "Beyonder").
			- While still at a disadvantage, a **Demon Hunter** in this state would be able to withstand the barrage of attacks from a **Silver Knight** who has not entered their **Mythical Creature Form**.[^30]

## Sequence 3: Silver Knight

**Individual Differences**

- **Blessing**: **Silver Knights** are very good at frontal assaults. There will be small differences in the abilities between them due to different **Blessings** during their [Advancement Ritual](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder#The_Advancement_Ritual "Beyonder").
	- [Murskogan](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Murskogan "Murskogan") had received **Blessings** from the [Giant King Aurmir](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Giant_King_Aurmir "Giant King Aurmir"), which caused the various aspects of his **Silver Knight Beyonder** powers (**Mercury Liquefaction**, **Silver Rapier**, and **Light Concealment**) to be much stronger than normal.
		- [Colin Iliad](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Colin_Iliad "Colin Iliad"), who received [Klein's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") **Blessings** could occasionally make himself turn into a **Silver Rapier** and **Teleport** randomly, bringing along beneficial tendencies. In addition, his **Mercury Liquefaction** had gained some uniqueness.

**New Abilities** [^2] [^3] **[^5]**

- **Mercury Liquefaction**: **Silver Knights** are able to melt into a malleable silvery metallic liquid that can restrict enemies and move rapidly in order to evade attacks.
	- The liquid volume of a **Silver Knight's Mercury Liquefaction** form is solely based on the **Silver Knight's** current mass upon ability activation.
		- They are also able to turn oneself into a **Silver Armor** for other targets (to defend an ally or to restrict and suffocate an opponent).
		- When getting suffocated, the enemy would appear as if they are wearing a full-body **Silver Armor** that has not a single gap.
- **Silver Rapier**: **Silver Knights** can condense silvery white beams of light (which take the appearance of swords) [^30] out of thin air. These **Silver Rapiers** tear apart everything around it and are hard to detect.[^30]
	- These **Silver Rapiers** can **Teleport (Blink)** and bypass most barriers and obstacles, directly attacking their target. They can even erupt from within a target's body to deal heavy damage.
		- With a mere swipe of his finger after advancing into **Silver Knight**, [Colin Iliad](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Colin_Iliad "Colin Iliad") unleashed a single **Silver Rapier**, whose destructive power was enough to cleave a small hill in 2.
				- Enraged, [Murskogan](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Murskogan "Murskogan") utilized his **Silver Rapier**, forming a “long snake" that completely lifted the solid ground of the [King’s Court](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Giant_King%27s_Court "Giant King's Court"), tearing open a deep chasm.[^29]
		- Their **Silver Rapiers** are able to manifest almost without any indication, making it extremely difficult for an enemy to detect and predict where they will appear next.
- **Light Concealment**: Also known as **Light Occultation** or **Hide by Light**. A **Silver Knight** can utilize any light source as a way of dodging, evasion, and movement.
	- **Light Concealment** may pierce through objects depending on light-source connection.
		- It will take an **Ancient Bane** to prolongedly observe the area to discover the whereabouts of 2 **Silver Knight** statues (who lack life and intelligence) chasing an enemy out of instinct while **Concealing** their **Evil** intentions and being **Concealed** by the **Light**.[^38] [^39]
		- An **[Unshadowed's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sun_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_4:_Unshadowed) Unshadowed Domain** is able to fully nullify **Light Concealment**.
		- **Silver Knights** can also let other people enter their **Light Concealment**.
- **Silver Armor**: At this level, their **Protection** materializes as a full-body **Silver Armor** which is much stronger than a [**Giant Dragon**](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Visionary_Pathway "Visionary Pathway") in terms of withstanding blows.
	- It's defensive capabilities can shield a **Silver Knight** against a limited number of [Angels'](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Angels "Angels") attacks [^40] while able to be permanently materialized as **Silver Armor**.[^41]
		- [Colin Iliad](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Colin_Iliad "Colin Iliad") had fully materialized his **Silver Armor** for days on end without experiencing any **Spirituality** expenditure upon advancing as a **Silver Knight**.

**Strengthened Abilities**

- They have gained improvement of other **Beyonder** powers in the previous **Sequence**.
	- **Sword of Dawn**: At the **Sequence 3** level, a **Silver Knight** is now able to permanently materialize their **Sword of Dawn**, making it usable as a regular weapon.
		- A **Silver Knight's Sword of Dawn** possesses top sharpness and **Purification** attributes, while also being capable of tearing apart a small hill with a single strike.
		- **Hurricane of Light**: They can ignite their body with a transparent flame, emitting the bright light of **Dawn**, creating a terrifying **Hurricane of Light**.[^41]
		- [Colin Iliad](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Colin_Iliad "Colin Iliad"), upon using this ability, emanated beams of light that came from his **Soul**, body, **Beyonder Characteristics** as the two swords he struck out merged together, turning into a raging, violent **Hurricane of Light** that swept out and enveloped the [Dark Angel](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sasrir "Sasrir").
			- Once enveloped, the **Hurricane of Light** tore at the [Dark Angel's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sasrir "Sasrir") body, interrupting the powers "He" was going to use while making "Him" fade away into fragments of light due to losing the source of power that maintained "His" existence.
		- **Combat Mastery** (**Direct Combat**): They are extremely skilled in direct **Combat**.
		- **Physical Enhancement**: Their physical strength has been greatly enhanced.
		- When [Murskogan](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Murskogan "Murskogan") smashed his **Sword of Dawn** onto the ground, the resulting force caused the area to shake violently as the pillars snapped and fell into the orange cloud beneath and the ground between the two buildings exploded layer by layer, sending countless gravel flying.[^29]

**Mythical Creature Form**

- Their [Mythical Creature Form](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mythical_Creature_Form "Mythical Creature Form") develops further, they are now a nearly 6 meter tall, one-eyed **Cyclops**. Their body is grayish-blue and their muscles are bluish-black. There is a dark crack on their forehead that attracts the [Souls](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul") around it. Their eyes are missing, replaced by smooth skin.
	- Every inch of their skin and flesh contains *immense power*, *infinite mystery,* and a *strange spiritual influence*. It makes everyone who witnesses them inevitably feel a sharp pain in their psyche, transforming them into a rampaging mess that wants to destroy everything.
		- These destructive tendencies also include themselves.
- As a **Sequence 3 Beyonder**, they can listen to prayers within a certain range.

## Sequence 2: Glory

**Description** [^5]

The three authorities of the 's **Sequence 0** — **Holiness**, **Twilight**, and **Combat** —are all manifested at this stage. For [Angels](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Angels "Angels"), this is a kind of **Glory**.

**Traits** [^5]

- **Strength of Giants**: "They" can grow to an enormous size.[^42]
	- A steel puppet of [Lumian Lee](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee"), possessing a temporary [Angel](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Angels "Angels") -level **Boon** from the [Evernight Goddess](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Evernight_Goddess "Evernight Goddess") grew to an enormous size, being comparable to [Lumian](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee") "Himself".

**Authorities** [^5]

- **Twilight**: "They" hold the simplified authority of **Twilight**.
	- "They" can age "Themselves" rapidly, evaporate, and then be reborn in the [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World"), returning to normal before departing. Likewise, "They" can also delay one's aging process.[^43]
		- [Larrion](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Larrion "Larrion"), after accomplishing "His" arrangements, attempted to evade [Arianna](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Arianna "Arianna") and escape through the [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World") by rapidly aging "Himself" with the authority of **Twilight**.[^26]
		- The simplified **Twilight** authority can cause a single target or a group within an area to gradually age, have their lifespan reduced, and enter the countdown of life.[^44]
		- As dying of aging is something that the majority of **Resurrection** abilities cannot take effect against, one should never engage in a prolonged battle with a **Glory**.
			- A **[Beauty Goddess](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Moon_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_1:_Beauty_Goddess)** will be affected by this authority, but "They" will remain **Beautiful**.[^45]
						- A **[Miracle Invoker's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fool_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_2:_Miracle_Invoker) Resurrection** is an example of one that can reset age.[^46]
				- "They" can also cause a single target or group within an area to gradually age and have their lifespan reduced with each strike.[^5]
- **Combat**: "They" hold the simplified **Combat** authority, thus being the top **Sequence 2** in frontal combat.
	- **Protection**: Its subordinate **Protection** can, at this stage, form a **Cage**, which in addition to defense, also gains a **Sealing** effect—a fusion with the decaying power of **Twilight**.
		- "Their" **Cages** are able to fully **Seal** off dangerous, corruptive [Sealed Artifacts](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sealed_Artifacts "Sealed Artifacts") (e.g., the [Gift of the Land](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Gift_of_the_Land "Gift of the Land")) and [Ritualistic Magic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ritualistic_Magic "Ritualistic Magic"), but requires "Their" presence to constantly fuel it, only being able to leave in short intervals during crises.[^47]
			- [Proof of Glory](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Proof_of_Glory "Proof of Glory") was used during an event to isolate and **Seal** the detrimental influences caused by deity-related [Ritualistic Magic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ritualistic_Magic "Ritualistic Magic") via the formation of a **Cage**.[^48]
				- "Their" **Cage** provides extremely powerful defensive and restraining capabilities, capable of **Protecting** companions and preventing "Their" enemies from escaping.[^49]
			- The [Tamara's Everchanging Card](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tamara%27s_Everchanging_Card "Tamara's Everchanging Card") 's effects couldn't break through the **Divine Barrier** created by the [Proof of Glory](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Proof_of_Glory "Proof of Glory"), nor could it allow the [Demoness of Orange](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Demoness_of_Orange "Demoness of Orange") to bypass it and **Blink** directly to her targets.[^50]
				- Its range is enough to envelop and **Protect** the [City of Silver](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/City_of_Silver "City of Silver").
		- **Twilight Sword**: An evolution of the **Silver Knight's Silver Rapier**. "They" can instantly condense a great sword composed of orange-red **Twilight** radiance that can pierce through the void.[^51]
		- It can use **Twilight's** relationship with **Space-Time** to manifest directly at the target's location—displaying **Devastation**, which is subordinate to the **Combat** authority.
				- "They" are able to wield this **Twilight Sword** directly as a hand-held weapon.[^52]
		- **Hurricane of Light**: The **Twilight Sword** will let "Them" transform the **Hurricane of Light** into an orange-red storm capable of annihilating a wasteland.[^5]
		- Countless shards of **Twilight** would tear apart everything around them, cutting all objects to pieces, causing them and the void to **Decay** until they collapse.[^51]
				- In the storm, affected targets will quickly break into tiny fragments, each piece dim and rusty, as if washed by the passage of endless time or having reached its **Twilight** years.[^51]
- **Holiness**: "They" hold the simplified authority of **Holiness**.
	- **Glory** is part of **Holiness**, and death is likewise one of the most sacred and solemn matters of life, therefore "Their" **Glory** is **Dawn**, yet also **Twilight**; it is **Devastation**, yet also **Purification**.
		- "Their" **Cages** bear a sense of **Holiness** and **Glory**.[^51]

**Mythical Creature Form**

- "They" become a complete [Mythical Creature](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mythical_Creature_Form "Mythical Creature Form"). "Their" [Mythical Creature Form](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mythical_Creature_Form "Mythical Creature Form") is a dozen of meters tall **Cyclops** with elongated limbs, grayish-blue skin, and a single vertical eye on the forehead. Every inch of "Their" skin, every pore, and every piece of "Their" flesh seems to defy normal human senses, being combined in a unique way and containing an unimaginable sense of impact.[^53]

## Sequence 1: Hand of God

**Authorities [^5]**

- **Proxy**: Like the **[Left Hand of Decay](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Second_Law_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_3:_Left_Hand_of_Decay)** in the same domain, "They" can act on behalf of the [Deity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Gods "Gods"), [Uniqueness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniqueness "Uniqueness"), or [Great Old One](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Old_One "Great Old One") "They" serve to manifest divine power and punishment.
	- Due to this authority, "They" cannot acquire new authorities aside from the **Proxy** authority and can only get enhancements to "Their" previous **Sequence** abilities.
		- If "They" don't harm "Themselves", "They" can for the short-term reach [Sasrir's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sasrir "Sasrir") level. If "They" harm "Themselves", "They" can be a consumable [Half Great Old One](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Half_Great_Old_One "Half Great Old One").[^54]
		- In a certain-death situation, "They" can theoretically kill a **[Red Priest](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Priest_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Red_Priest)** who had not **Sacrificed** to the [Sefirah](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirah "Sefirah"), but it's also difficult because the duration is very short.[^55]
			- The **[Red Priest](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Priest_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Red_Priest)** would most likely only be at a disadvantage for a short time, unless an opportunity is created in advance where "They" cannot dodge or block.
		- A **Hand of God** can only manifest such power briefly and with restraint. Otherwise, "They" will gradually lose "Their" sanity and become a true servant.
		- In addition, if the [True Deity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Gods "Gods") that the **Hand of God** had pledged "Their" allegiance too dies, the power that "They" can wield will decrease.
		- "Their" right hand will embody certain properties of the entity "They" serve.[^53]
		- Having ascended to this [Sequence](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sequence "Sequence"), the **Hand of God** [Dabomachie](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Dabomachie "Dabomachie") 's right hand seemed to be made entirely of glowing orange **Twilight**.[^53]
		- "They" can condense "Their" own power into a mark to lend to others.[^56]
- **Combat**: Some of "Their" corresponding abilities to the **Combat** authority have been enhanced.
	- **Protection**: "They" can summon **Cages** made out of dense light imbued with orange hues and another layer of unyielding radiance outside the targeted area that can prevent even an **[Observer](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sublunary_Eye_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_1:_Observer)** or **[Dimensional Shadow](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sublunary_Eye_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_2:_Dimensional_Shadow)** from crossing through.[^57] [^53]
		- "They" can then drive the **Twilight Sword** into the barrier of the **Cage** to repair it after withstanding attacks from multiple **Angels**.[^57]
				- Its range is enough to envelop and **Protect** all the [Protected Zones](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Protected_Zones "Protected Zones") created by [The Fool](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") and [Grisha Adam](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Adam "Adam").

## Sequence 0: Twilight Giant (Twilight, Giant)

**Description**

"They" hold partial authority over **Holiness**, **Twilight**, and **Combat** (which contains subordinate authorities like **Strength**, **Gigantism**, **Devastation**, and **Protection**) as well as a portion of the **Proxy** authority—where the symbol of **Enslavement** belonging to the **[River of Eternal Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/River_of_Eternal_Darkness)** manifests in the **Giant Pathway**.

**Authorities [^5]**

- **Twilight**: This authority is derived from symbols of **Light**, **Space-Time**, and **Decay**.
	- "They" can cause **Matter**, will, and even the void to **Decay**, collapse, and vanish, radiating the aura of approaching **Twilight** and the **Decay** of all things.
		- **Twilight** and the **Passage of Time** counters the **Light**, **Holiness**, and **Sun** authorities of the [Sun Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sun_Pathway) as **Twilight** represents the end of all these.[^58]
				- A **Twilight Giant's** authority over the **Passage of Time** is able to even influence the will within **Beyonder Characteristics** to a limited extent.[^59]
				- "Their" **Twilight Sword** would cause even the void to shatter and **Decay** as the objects hit would wane uncontrollably and break down.[^60]
		- The **Twilight Giant's** mere existence causes matter and the void around "Them" to bend, making all things begin to uncontrollably **Decay**.
		- The [God of Combat](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/God_of_Combat "God of Combat") 's aura leaking from across the [Astral World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Astral_World "Astral World") into reality made the **Fog of War** that covered the battlefield around [Backlund](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Backlund "Backlund") vanish, and the [Saints](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sequence#Saints "Sequence") present found themselves abnormally weak, having difficulty in raising their arms.[^61]
		- "They" can solidify **Twilight** and slow the **Passage of Time**, and while solidification is only possible within **Twilight**, "They" can create a **Twilight** domain within a certain range.
		- By slashing towards "Their" target using the **Twilight Sword**, it will instantly envelop them in **Twilight's** glow, freezing them within as their thoughts and actions slow down, as if they existed in a different world.[^42]
			- A [Kings of Angels](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Kings_of_Angels "Kings of Angels") [Cheek](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Primordial_Demoness "Primordial Demoness") was affected by this from [Lumian's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee") **Angelic** -level **Twilight Giant Pathway** soldier, causing "Her" thoughts and actions to slow down and delaying "Her" attempt to destroy "Herself"
				- **Land of Twilight**: "Their" **Divine Kingdom** formed through **Twilight**, completely frozen within the **Passage of Time**. Those inside will find themselves weakened unless acknowledged.[^62]
			- It can persist for millennia even after "Their" death (e.g. [Giant King's Court](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Giant_King%27s_Court "Giant King's Court")).
		- "They" are able to cause targets to rapidly **Age**, **Decay**, and **Rot** —preventing the **Resurrection** methods of all beings with finite lifespans.
		- As [True Gods](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/True_Gods "True Gods") do not have the concept of lifespan, "They" won't die from this. However, "They" can still be affected by **Aging** and get weakened.
		- "They" can induce instability and **Loss of Control** in targets, with those targets' symptoms continuing to appear more and more severe.
		- "They" can slow the formation and opening of **Doors**.
		- "They" can weaken targets progressively.
		- The **Twilight** of the **Twilight Giant** and the **Decay** of the [Second Law Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Second_Law_Pathway) are both **Time** -related and have similar manifestations, but **Twilight** does not affect an organization's state.[^63]
		- **Dawn**:"They" also possess the authority of **Dawn**. This is because endings are inherently connected to beginnings.[^64]
		- **Dawn** can be used to directly **Purify** a **[Beyonder Characteristic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristic "Beyonder Characteristic")**, much like the [Sun Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sun_Pathway), though **Purification** from the is achieved through equal destruction and **Decay**.[^65]
- **Holiness**: "They" hold partial authority over **Holiness**.
	- **Death** is among the most solemn events of life and of the world—thus profoundly **Sacred**. Hence, the **Twilight Giant** also excels in **Purification** and discerning **Evil** and **Corruption**.
- **Combat**: "They" hold the authority of **Combat**.
	- The most terrifying **Strength**, the most **Gigantic** form, the most potent **Devastation**, the most unyielding **Protection** —together with **Skill** and experience, these compose individual **Combat**.
		- The one who commands all this is the [God of Combat](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/God_of_Combat "God of Combat"), who is able to strip "His" opponents of **Skill**, forcing them to fight by instinct alone.
		- **Devastation** is a subsidiary authority of **Combat** because it is **Devastation** caused by the action of **Combat**, making it a concept that is attached to **Combat's** actions.[^66]
		- In contrast, **Destruction** is not only the action but also a state of being for things. It is a state of being that can be achieved through **Combat** (which includes **Devastation**). It can be achieved by prematurely bringing forth the end of an event/being that is currently running its natural course. And last but not least, it is also just simply a final state of being, a state of finality.
				- In a vacuum, **Devastation** can counteract and neutralize **Humanity**, which is a subordinate authority; however, **Devastation** cannot erase **Humanity** from the root. It functions more like a continuous suppression through attrition. Hence it is not capable of erasing other authorities from existence, only being able to subject them to being damaged and depleted by attrition.[^66]
			- By contrast, the authority of **Destruction** is far more formidable, as it directly imposes the state of **Destruction** on a conceptual level.
		- The authority of **Combat** is a **Sequence 0** authority, making it the same level as **Sequence 0** authority **Destruction**, but it's lower than the symbolism of **Destruction**.[^66]
		- At the core of the lies **Decay** and **Decay** is a process. Thus, **Combat** itself is also a form of attrition, centering on using individual strength to "go against" another, which in turn generates "attrition".
- **Proxy**: "They" hold a portion of the **Proxy** authority.
	- Among the three [Pathways](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways") of the [River of Eternal Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/River_of_Eternal_Darkness), **[Death](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Death_\(Eternal_Sleeper\))** aligns most closely with the [Sefirah](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirot "Sefirot") 's essence, **[Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Darkness_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Darkness)** most resembles it outwardly, yet the **Twilight Giant** can partially employ it. This is one reason the [Giant King](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Aurmir "Aurmir") could contend with the [Dragon of Imagination](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ankewelt "Ankewelt").
		- [Sefirot](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirot "Sefirot") is power, but it is also danger. There is no way for "Them" to utilize the power of [Sefirot](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirot "Sefirot") without paying a price.[^67]
		- By using **Proxy**, the [God of Combat](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/God_of_Combat "God of Combat") can theoretically reach the [strongest tier of Half Great Old Ones](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Half_Great_Old_Ones#Tier_1 "Half Great Old Ones") for 2-3 seconds. This was how the [Giant King](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Aurmir "Aurmir") was able to escape from the [Ancient Sun God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ancient_Sun_God "Ancient Sun God") and passed on "His" characteristics to "His" successor.[^68]
		- However, in reality, it's impossible because "He" couldn't possibly completely pledge allegiance to the [River of Eternal Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/River_of_Eternal_Darkness "River of Eternal Darkness"), as this would mean never becoming a [Great Old One](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Old_One "Great Old One").
			- Thus, in practice, "His" self-detonation would only be a bit stronger than the [Evernight Goddess](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Evernight_Goddess "Evernight Goddess") before accommodating the [River of Eternal Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/River_of_Eternal_Darkness "River of Eternal Darkness").[^69]
				- There were also other factors preventing the true theoretical maximum from being achieved:[^69]
			- Firstly, the [Evernight Goddess](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Evernight_Goddess "Evernight Goddess") can also use items to call upon a bit of the [River of Eternal Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/River_of_Eternal_Darkness "River of Eternal Darkness") 's power to counteract, not be completely suppressed, and unable to **Resurrect**.
						- Secondly, if the [God of Combat](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/God_of_Combat "God of Combat") truly self-detonates, it would threaten the [Astral Barrier](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Astral_Barrier "Astral Barrier") that protects and separates [Earth](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Map_of_the_World "Map of the World") from the [Outer Deities](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Outer_Deities "Outer Deities").
				- Someone like the [White Tower](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/White_Tower_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_White_Tower), who can influence [Astral World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Astral_World "Astral World") concepts, would use "Their" own methods to interfere with the **Proxy**.
						- Thirdly, there are other interferences, like the [Underworld Daoist](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Underworld_Daoist "Underworld Daoist"), etc.
				- Even counting the strongest level, there's a possibility of counter-kill, but it's not certain.[^69] [^68]
			- If the [God of Combat](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/God_of_Combat "God of Combat") self-detonates at the highest level, "He" could obliterate everything on that battlefield, but that doesn't necessarily mean it could completely kill the [Earth Mother](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Earth_Mother "Earth Mother") and the [Evernight Goddess](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Evernight_Goddess "Evernight Goddess") because of "Their" respective authorities.[^69]
				- Furthermore, at least more than half of those 2-3 seconds would have to be used for fleeing, arranging final affairs, arranging for an heir, or activating a resurrection backup, just like the [Giant King](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Aurmir "Aurmir") back then.[^70]
