**Azik Eggers** is a former lecturer at [Klein](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") 's almamater. A mysterious man with amnesia and unknown supernatural abilities related to the [Corpse Collector Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway).

In fact, it is later revealed that **Azik** is a Sequence 2 [Death Consul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_2:_Death_Consul) of the [Death Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway) since the [Fourth Epoch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Epoch "Fourth Epoch") and is a son of [Death](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Salinger "Salinger"). "He" is the **Angel of Death** under [The Fool](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti"), and is mentioned in the bible of the [Church of the Fool](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Church_of_the_Fool "Church of the Fool").[^3]

## Appearance

Bronze skin, average height and build, black hair, brown eyes, and gentle facial features. Being in "His" presence brought on an indescribable feeling, as though you could see in the man's eyes that "He" had been through the vicissitudes of life. Under "His" right ear was a tiny mole that one wouldn't notice unless closely examined.

**Death Consul Azik**: Unlike the normal **Mr. Azik**, "His" gaze was cold. "He" wore a deep black robe embroidered with golden threads. "He" wore a golden bird-shaped crown, as though "He" was looking down on all living creatures.[^4]

## Personality

From the original Klein’s impression of **Mr. Azik**, "He" is a dependable and trustworthy person. "He" had never once said anything baseless. Even if "He" was always quarreling with Mentor, it was limited to academic topics, and each of them had their reasons.[^5]

**Azik Eggers** has an easygoing personality and is, in general, a very kind and gentle person. "He" has never refused to help Klein when asked. All the previous experiences during "His" memory loss: living as a normal human, getting married and having children, have changed "Him" into a gentle person.

However, it has been stated by [Katarina Pelle](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Katarina_Pelle "Katarina Pelle") that before "He" lost "His" memories, as the **Death Consul**, "He" was an extremely cold and ruthless person.[^6]

## Background

**Azik Eggers** was a Sequence 2 [Angel](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Angel "Angel") in the [Fourth Epoch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Epoch "Fourth Epoch"). "He" is the son of [Death](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Salinger "Salinger") and used to be the emperor of the [Balam Empire](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Balam_Empire "Balam Empire"). "His" earliest memory traces back to the [War of Four Emperors](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Epoch#War_of_Four_Emperors "Fourth Epoch"). "He" had once looked up at the [Black Emperor](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Solomon "Solomon") and [Blood Emperor](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Alista_Tudor "Alista Tudor"), and lost consciousness when the [Blood Emperor](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Alista_Tudor "Alista Tudor") looked at "Him".[^7]

"He" later fought the Saints and Angels of the [Church of the Evernight Goddess](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Church_of_the_Evernight_Goddess "Church of the Evernight Goddess") together with [Katarina Pelle](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Katarina_Pelle "Katarina Pelle") in the Pale Death era. The last time they met was 1,300 years ago.[^8]

At the last moment before dying, "His" father, [Death](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Salinger "Salinger"), pulled **Azik** 's soul out of "His" body and divided it into two halves. The first half was fused with the [Uniqueness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniqueness "Uniqueness") of the Death pathway and became the main subject of the " [Artificial Death](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Artificial_Death "Artificial Death") " plan, which was started and operated by the [Numinous Episcopate](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Numinous_Episcopate "Numinous Episcopate"). The second half of **Azik** 's soul was fused with a golden, bird-shaped ornament and returned to **Azik** 's body. Due to the fact that **Azik** 's soul isn't complete, "He" has been repeatedly dying and resurrecting while losing "His" memories every 60 years. When two parts of **Azik** 's soul come together again, Death will revive from **Azik** 's body.[^9]

During every cycle of memory loss, **Azik** would slowly rediscover "His" memories. However, during past cycles, "He" was never fast enough to discover the source of his amnesia, and subsequently, a solution. In "His" first cycle, "He" joined the [Loen Kingdom](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Loen_Kingdom "Loen Kingdom") 's army, became a knight, and fought in the chaos of the Fourth Epoch’s wake in the [Northern Continent](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Northern_Continent "Northern Continent").[^10] "He" eventually became the first Baron Lamud.[^11]

Although **Azik** had been pursued by the [Church of the Evernight Goddess](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Church_of_the_Evernight_Goddess "Church of the Evernight Goddess") ' High-Sequence Beyonders, "He" had ultimately never suffered any real threats. Klein speculated that this is because the [Evernight Goddess](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Evernight_Goddess "Evernight Goddess") had been waiting for the development regarding the [Artificial Death](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Artificial_Death "Artificial Death").[^12]

![Mr](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/lord-of-the-mystery/images/0/0c/Tianwen_Kadokawa_-_Mr._Azik%E2%80%98s_Choice.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20251207051851)

Mr. Azik's choice

When the two parts of **Azik** 's soul fuse together again to revive the late [Death](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Salinger "Salinger"), at the last moment before the soul-merging process was complete, the [Evernight Goddess](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Evernight_Goddess "Evernight Goddess"), who descended by using the [Angel of Concealment](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Angel_of_Concealment "Angel of Concealment") as "Her" Vessel, came to "His" rescue. Then "He" chose to live with an incomplete soul, stuck at "His" current sequence with no way of advancing further. "He" would still die over and over again, waking up with no memories, and repeatedly search for "His" past experiences.[^13]

"He" is now the **Angel of Death** of the [Church of the Fool](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Church_of_the_Fool "Church of the Fool").[^3] In 1359, "He" is able to wake up earlier, with no more memory loss, and no longer unable to advance because "He" was helped to heal "His" soul by the [Tarot Club](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tarot_Club "Tarot Club") members using the water of the [River of Eternal Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/River_of_Eternal_Darkness "River of Eternal Darkness") distributary.[^14] [^15] [^16]

## Trivia

- The name **Azik** (阿兹克) comes from [Aztecs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aztecs "wikipedia:Aztecs") (阿茲特克), a civilization that flourished in central Mexico in the post-classic period from 1300 to 1521. Not only the name of **Azik**, but also the ability of [Death Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway) and the history of the [Balam Empire](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Balam_Empire "Balam Empire") are closely related to the religion and development of this civilization.
- Because of [Leonard Mitchell](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Leonard_Mitchell "Leonard Mitchell"), official Beyonder organizations believe **Azik Eggers** is one of the members of the [Tarot Club](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tarot_Club "Tarot Club"), as the codename **The Death**.[^20]
- The [author](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Cuttlefish_That_Loves_Diving "Cuttlefish That Loves Diving") replied in WeChat that **Azik** was born in a **Feathered Serpent** form.[^21]
- The [MI9](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/MI9 "MI9") offered a 150 pounds bounty to find "Him".[^22] [^2] Based on [Danitz](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Danitz_Dubois "Danitz Dubois") case, "His" bounty was likely removed after the year 1352 because "He" became an 'official' Beyonder under the [Church of the Fool](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Church_of_the_Fool "Church of the Fool").[^3]
- List of [Klein's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") letters to **Azik** chapters: 483, 724, 856, 979, 1012, 1124, 1275, 1359, 1390.
- In the side story " [An Ordinary Person's Daily Life](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/An_Ordinary_Person%27s_Daily_Life_\(Side_Story\) "An Ordinary Person's Daily Life (Side Story)") ", [Vernal](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Vernal_Fnarr "Vernal Fnarr") used "His" work, " *Private Historical Research of Sivellaus County* ", as a reference when applying for funding from [Loen Relic Search and Preservation Foundation](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Loen_Relic_Search_and_Preservation_Foundation "Loen Relic Search and Preservation Foundation").[^23]
- In the side story " [In Modern Day](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/In_Modern_Day_\(Side_Story\) "In Modern Day (Side Story)")," "He" is the owner of a themed bookstore, with a daughter who has long black hair and loves to swing. After [Zhou Mingrui](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") saves "His" daughter from a car accident, **Azik** invites him to visit the store. The books available in the bookstore include: " *[Groselle's Travels](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Groselle%27s_Travels "Groselle's Travels"),*" " *[Book of Secrets](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Book_of_Secrets "Book of Secrets"),*" " *Roselle and his 100 Mistresses,*" " *The Self-Cultivation of Evil Gods,*" and " *How to Make the Believers Offer Money.*" The store also offers iced drinks, such as sweet iced tea.[^24]
