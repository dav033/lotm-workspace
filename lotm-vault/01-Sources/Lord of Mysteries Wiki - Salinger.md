**Salinger** or **Death** was a Sequence 0 [God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/God "God") of the [Death Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway "Death Pathway"). "He" was a [Subsidiary God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Subsidiary_God "Subsidiary God") of the [Annihilation Demonic Wolf Flegrea](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Annihilation_Demonic_Wolf_Flegrea "Annihilation Demonic Wolf Flegrea") and later the [Phoenix Ancestor Gregrace](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Phoenix_Ancestor_Gregrace "Phoenix Ancestor Gregrace"), known as the **God of the Dead** in the [Second Epoch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Second_Epoch "Second Epoch"). "He" then founded and controlled the [Balam Empire](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Balam_Empire "Balam Empire") and was known as the **Underworld Emperor**. "He" later used the [River of Eternal Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/River_of_Eternal_Darkness "River of Eternal Darkness") distributary to fuse with the [Red Priest Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Priest_Pathway "Red Priest Pathway") [Uniqueness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniqueness "Uniqueness") to create a brand new [Great Old One](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Above_the_Sequence "Above the Sequence") path. This led to "Him" becoming a [Half Great Old One](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Half_Great_Old_One "Half Great Old One"), although "He" went mad as a result.[^1] [^2]

Salinger perished at the end of the [Pale Era](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pale_Era "Pale Era") of the [Fourth Epoch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Epoch "Fourth Epoch"), at the hands of the [Seven Orthodox Gods](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Seven_Orthodox_Gods "Seven Orthodox Gods").

## Honorific Name

The Essence of Death;  
Lord of the Dead;  
Final Home to all Living-beings

— [Honorific Name](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Honorific_Name "Honorific Name") of **Death** [^3]

## Symbol

"His" symbol is the [Inverted Mausoleum](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Inverted_Mausoleum "Inverted Mausoleum").

## Appearance

**In the Rose Redemption Mural:** A middle-aged man with facial features resembling the natives of the [Southern Continent](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Southern_Continent "Southern Continent").[^4]

## Personality

[Antigonus](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Antigonus "Antigonus") found **Salinger** to be gloomy and solitary, exuding a rotting and disgusting aura.[^5]

Because "He" was originally a [Subsidiary God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Subsidiary_God "Subsidiary God") with mixed characteristics ( [Death](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway) Sequence 2 + [Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Darkness_Pathway) Sequence 2),[^6] even after expelling the characteristic, there would still be severe aftereffects.[^7] "His" mental state was never particularly good.[^8]

## History

### Second Epoch

In the [Second Epoch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Second_Epoch "Second Epoch"), "He" was known as the **God of the Dead Salinger** and was a [Subsidiary God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Subsidiary_God "Subsidiary God") of the [Annihilation Demonic Wolf Flegrea](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Annihilation_Demonic_Wolf_Flegrea "Annihilation Demonic Wolf Flegrea"), but after [Flegrea](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Flegrea "Flegrea") 's death, "He" turned to the [Phoenix Ancestor Gregrace](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Phoenix_Ancestor_Gregrace "Phoenix Ancestor Gregrace").[^9] "He" was initially a mix of [Death](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway) [Sequence 2](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_2:_Death_Consul) and [Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Darkness_Pathway) [Sequence 2](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Darkness_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_2:_Servant_of_Concealment).[^10]

When [Gregrace](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Gregrace "Gregrace") perished, "She" turned into fragments scattered across the [Southern Continent](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Southern_Continent "Southern Continent").[^11] When **Salinger** acquired the [Pale Emperor](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_1:_Pale_Emperor) characteristic, it had already changed hands twice, roughly around the year 24xx, which was over a hundred years before the end of the [Second Epoch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Second_Epoch "Second Epoch").[^12] [^13]

### Third Epoch

In the [Third Epoch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Third_Epoch "Third Epoch"), "He" joined [Rose Redemption](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Rose_Redemption "Rose Redemption") as a [Kings of Angels](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Kings_of_Angels "Kings of Angels") of the [Subsidiary Gods](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Subsidiary_Gods "Subsidiary Gods") faction under the [Evernight Goddess](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Evernight_Goddess "Evernight Goddess"), which eventually caused [Ancient Sun God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ancient_Sun_God "Ancient Sun God") 's death.[^4] [^14]

### Fourth Epoch

During the beginning of the [Fourth Epoch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Epoch "Fourth Epoch"), "He" advanced to Sequence 0 and became known as **Death**. At that time, all deities had "Their" own countries in poorer places and acknowledged the first [Solomon Empire](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Solomon_Empire "Solomon Empire") and the [Black Emperor Solomon](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Solomon "Solomon") 's rule over the [Northern Continent](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Northern_Continent "Northern Continent") in order to fight against opposing factions. **Death** had a conflict with the [Evernight Goddess](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Evernight_Goddess "Evernight Goddess") and the [God of Combat](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/God_of_Combat "God of Combat"). For the sake of balance, the [Black Emperor](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Solomon "Solomon") also didn't attack the [Southern Continent](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Southern_Continent "Southern Continent"), and allowed **Death** to unite the plains and the ancient forest to build the [Balam Empire](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Balam_Empire "Balam Empire").[^15]

"He", along with the [Black Emperor Solomon](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Solomon "Solomon"), [Night Emperor Trunsoest](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Trunsoest "Trunsoest"), and [Blood Emperor Tudor](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Alista_Tudor "Alista Tudor"), participated in the [War of the Four Emperors](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/War_of_the_Four_Emperors "War of the Four Emperors"). In Tudor's last moment, "He" wanted to forcibly complete the merger with the [Mirrored Original Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mirrored_Original_Creator "Mirrored Original Creator"), using this to pull all other [Sefirot](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirot "Sefirot") from the [Western Continent](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Western_Continent "Western Continent"), dragging the entire world to die, waiting for the [Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Original_Creator "Original Creator") 's symbolic manifestation in death. The [Primordial Demoness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Primordial_Demoness "Primordial Demoness") decided to let "Him" die for a while, and then resurrect "Him" later. "She" dealt Tudor the critical blow, while [Death](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death "Death") completed "His" life's culling, sending "His" soul into the [River of Eternal Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/River_of_Eternal_Darkness "River of Eternal Darkness").[^16] After the [War of the Four Emperors](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/War_of_the_Four_Emperors "War of the Four Emperors"), **Death** was said to have benefited the most from the war, gaining the [Red Priest Uniqueness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniquenesses#Red_Priest_Pathway).

During the [Conflicts of the Seven Gods](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Conflicts_of_the_Seven_Gods "Conflicts of the Seven Gods"), **Death** relied on a distributary of the [River of Eternal Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/River_of_Eternal_Darkness "River of Eternal Darkness") to forcibly accommodate the [Red Priest Uniqueness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniquenesses#Red_Priest_Pathway) to create a brand new [Great Old One](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Old_One "Great Old One") path. This has the influence of the [True Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/True_Creator "True Creator"), as well as the [Celestial Worthy of Heaven and Earth for Blessings](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Celestial_Worthy_of_Heaven_and_Earth_for_Blessings "Celestial Worthy of Heaven and Earth for Blessings") who had once accommodated the [River of Eternal Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/River_of_Eternal_Darkness "River of Eternal Darkness") which included the [Death Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway "Death Pathway").[^17] After over a century of "digestion",[^18] "He" went mad, but also became a lot stronger, even stronger than the War of the Four Emperors era's [True Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/True_Creator "True Creator"). "He" wasn't able to resist the desire for converging with the [Twilight](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Twilight_Giant_Pathway "Twilight Giant Pathway") and [Evernight](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Darkness_Pathway "Darkness Pathway") pathways,[^2] and then wanting to devour a certain few gods from among the [Seven Gods](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Seven_Gods "Seven Gods").[^18] [^2]

In the middle of this era, the [Primordial Demoness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Primordial_Demoness "Primordial Demoness") approached [Death](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death "Death") for an alliance. "She" wanted to manipulate the half-mad [Death](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death "Death") to collect the [Blood Emperor](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Blood_Emperor "Blood Emperor") 's corpse in [Fourth Epoch Trier](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Epoch_Trier "Fourth Epoch Trier"), merge it with "Himself", and manipulate "Him" to touch the [Special Mirror Worlds](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Special_Mirror_Worlds "Special Mirror Worlds") to resurrect the [Blood Emperor](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Blood_Emperor "Blood Emperor").[^19] However, [Death](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death "Death") 's forced accommodation of the [Red Priest Uniqueness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniquenesses#Red_Priest_Pathway) caused an aberration in the [Mirrored Original Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mirrored_Original_Creator "Mirrored Original Creator"), producing a certain self-awareness, which negatively affected "Her" state.[^16]

- The reason for the [Celestial Worthy](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Celestial_Worthy "Celestial Worthy") 's influence which eventually led to this aberration was to prevent the [Primordial Demoness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Primordial_Demoness "Primordial Demoness") ' plan regarding the [Mirrored Original Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mirrored_Original_Creator "Mirrored Original Creator"). This is because if there were no mutation, and "She" continued down the path, succeeding would make "Her" the [Mirrored Original Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mirrored_Original_Creator "Mirrored Original Creator"); failing would still birth the [Mirrored Original Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mirrored_Original_Creator "Mirrored Original Creator"). With the aberration, now both sides have formed a certain balance until [The Apocalypse](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/The_Apocalypse "The Apocalypse"), leading to new changes.[^20]

Some time after **Death** went mad, "He" interacted with the [Underworld Daoist](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Underworld_Daoist "Underworld Daoist"), who guarded the [River of Eternal Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/River_of_Eternal_Darkness "River of Eternal Darkness") and the [Blood Emperor](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Blood_Emperor "Blood Emperor") 's afterimage. For some reason, "He" agreed to [Naboredisleys](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Naboredisley "Naboredisley") request for help, causing "His" [Abomination](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chained_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_1:_Abomination) corpse to slumber in a [special dream](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Dream_Festival "Dream Festival"). Therefore, be it the [Underworld Daoist](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Underworld_Daoist "Underworld Daoist") or [Naboredisley](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Naboredisley "Naboredisley") 's corpse, even if they only had their instincts left, they would still treat the [Eggers Family](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Eggers_Family "Eggers Family") 's bloodline specially.[^21]

In the [Pale Era](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pale_Era "Pale Era"), **Death** and the [Primordial Demoness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Primordial_Demoness "Primordial Demoness") joined forces to wreak havoc and create a catastrophe on the [Northern Continent](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Northern_Continent "Northern Continent").[^18] The [Seven Gods](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Seven_Gods "Seven Gods") were defeated and retreated at every turn, until "They" eventually joined hands again. After a carefully schemed war with cataclysmic consequences, [Death](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death "Death") fell, the ocean between the [Northern Continent](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Northern_Continent "Northern Continent") and [Southern Continent](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Southern_Continent "Southern Continent") was widened by one fold, becoming the [Berserk Sea](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Berserk_Sea "Berserk Sea") that's perpetually engulfed by turbulent waves and storms, separating the North and South.[^22]

### Fifth Epoch

#### Resurrection Plan

The [Eggers Family](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Eggers_Family "Eggers Family") are the descendants of **Death**, including [Azik Eggers](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Azik_Eggers "Azik Eggers"), the son of **Death**. Before "He" perished, **Death** left a piece of "His" soul in Azik in order to resurrect in the future. [Numinous Episcopate](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Numinous_Episcopate "Numinous Episcopate") had also been trying to revive **Death** by creating an [Artificial Death](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Artificial_Death "Artificial Death") from the [Death Uniqueness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniquenesses#Death_Pathway). However, the [Evernight Goddess](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Evernight_Goddess "Evernight Goddess") eventually retrieved the [Death Uniqueness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniquenesses#Death_Pathway).[^23]

When [Roselle](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Roselle_Gustav "Roselle Gustav") created the [Pale Death](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pale_Death "Pale Death") mask, "He" sensed that **Death** might not have completely perished, and might have left behind hidden means of revival involving the [River of Eternal Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/River_of_Eternal_Darkness "River of Eternal Darkness").[^24] It was later revealed that "His" death imprint was left wandering around the River. If another being accommodated the [Death Uniqueness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniquenesses#Death_Pathway), and if **Death** couldn't use that opportunity to revive in "Their" body, "His" imprint would sink into the water of the [River of Eternal Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/River_of_Eternal_Darkness "River of Eternal Darkness") and would further melt away.[^25]
