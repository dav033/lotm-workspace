**Lord of Mysteries** is the title of the [Great Old One](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Old_One "Great Old One") of the [Fool](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fool_Pathway), [Error](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway), and [Door](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Door_Pathway) [Pathways](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways").

This [Great Old One](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Old_One "Great Old One") is one of the three [Pillars](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pillars "Pillars").[^2]

TRANSLATION NOTE

**诡** (" Guǐ ") which means "Bizarre, Deceitful, or Cunning." it's also shared with **Bizarreness** " 诡 异", (Guǐyì).

**秘** (" Mì ") which means "Secret". it's also shared with **Concealment** "隐 秘 ", (Yǐnmì).

## Symbols, Authorities & Abilities

"They" possess full **Authority** over the [Fool](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fool_Pathway), [Error](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway), and [Door](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Door_Pathway) [Pathways](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways") and the corresponding **Symbols**, which include but are not limited to the following:

- [Fool](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fool_Pathway): Authorities and the surface level symbolisms of **Fooling**, **Reassembly**, **Miracles**, **Anchor of Destiny**, and **History**.
- [Error](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway): Authorities and the surface level symbolisms of **Errors**, **Time**, **Theft**, **Destiny**, and **Deceit**.
- [Door](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Door_Pathway): Authorities and the surface level symbolisms of **Doors**, **Space**, **Alternate Worlds**, **Position**, and **Replication**.

The **Lord of Mysteries** is the best and most proficient at utilizing symbolism. As such, the Pathways under "Them" naturally are able to use surface level symbolism application of their authorities.[^4]

- High-level [Fool](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fool_Pathway "Fool Pathway"), [Door](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Door_Pathway "Door Pathway"), and [Error](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway "Error Pathway") Beyonders are the best at using mysticism similarities and loopholes.[^5]

**Note**: A [Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways") having certain symbolism doesn't mean at the level of [Above the Sequence](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Above_the_Sequence "Above the Sequence") that it entirely stems from that [Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways"). It might stem from the entire group or certain parts from it.

The **Symbols**, **Authorities**, and **Abilities** "They" possess and display at the [Above the Sequence](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Above_the_Sequence "Above the Sequence") level include:

- **Dominator of the Spirit World**: The most important symbolism of the **Lord of Mysteries** is "Their" symbolism as a [Pillar](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pillar "Pillar"). "They" symbolize and are the controller of the **[Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World")**, one of the three layers of the world.
	- A **Pillar** is slightly stronger but has no fundamental difference compared to the other [Great Old Ones](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Old_Ones "Great Old Ones"). In general, by relying on the unique symbolism of a **Pillar**, "They" will have the status and power to contend with two other [Great Old Ones](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Old_Ones "Great Old Ones") at most.[^6]
		- However, for the **Lord of Mysteries** who is capable of achieving **Omnipresence** state through **Blink**, **Marionettes**, **Avatars**, etc., as long as there is no [Great Old Ones](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Old_Ones "Great Old Ones") with specific symbols or abilities that counter "Their" specialty, there is no fundamental difference between battling two, eight, or countless [Great Old Ones](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Old_Ones "Great Old Ones").[^6] [^7]
			- Even though [Sefirot](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirot "Sefirot") and [Uniquenesses](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniquenesses "Uniquenesses") can only be used by one **Marionette** or **Avatar** at a time, "They" can still fight multiple opponents by relying on the [Door Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Door_Pathway "Door Pathway") to control various battlefields and then use **Marionettes** and **Avatars** in different battlefields to interfere and affect with "Their" enemies.[^8]
				- "They" would undoubtedly be able to hold off the [Goddess of Fate](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Goddess_of_Fate "Goddess of Fate"), [Supernova Dominator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Supernova_Dominator "Supernova Dominator"), [Mother Tree of Desire](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Tree_of_Desire "Mother Tree of Desire") (without the [Source of Curses](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Father_of_Devils) symbolism), and [Circle of Inevitability](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Circle_of_Inevitability "Circle of Inevitability") on "Their" own at the same time.
					- While "They" might not secure a decisive victory, "They" would certainly not lose or allow "Their" enemies disrupt other battlefields significantly.[^9]
				- If the battlefield contained a single [Great Old Dominator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Old_Dominator "Great Old Dominator") that could render the ubiquitous nature of "Their" avatars ineffective, "They" would have to rely on "Their" true body in combat, and could only leverage [Pillar](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pillar "Pillar") symbolisms while contending with an additional [Great Old One](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Old_Dominator "Great Old Dominator").[^9]
		- Unlike normal [Great Old Ones](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Old_Ones "Great Old Ones") who can have "Their" will shattered—like how the [Sefirot](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirot "Sefirot") had "Their" wills shattered by the [Celestial Worthy of Heaven and Earth for Blessings](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Celestial_Worthy_of_Heaven_and_Earth_for_Blessings "Celestial Worthy of Heaven and Earth for Blessings") and the [Primordial God Almighty](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Primordial_God_Almighty "Primordial God Almighty") in the [First Epoch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/First_Epoch "First Epoch") [^10] —the will of a [Pillar](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pillar "Pillar") cannot be shattered, even by another [Pillar](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pillar "Pillar").[^11]
		- "They" can **Seal** the entire [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World") across the whole universe, severing others' connections and influence over it, ensuring that for a set period, no creature can enter or utilize the **Spirit World** without "Their" permission.
		- This renders all abilities, authorities, and symbols related to the [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World") ineffective,[^9] as well as blinding other creatures, including other [Great Old Ones](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Old_Ones "Great Old Ones"), to spiritual premonitions, making "Them" unable to gather information from the **Spirit World**.[^12]
				- "Their" **Seal** over the **Spirit World** could block other [Great Old Dominators](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Old_Dominators "Great Old Dominators") from entering, but could not prevent a [Great Old Dominator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Old_Dominator "Great Old Dominator") inside from leaving or breaking the seal.[^13]
			- The [Goddess of Fate](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Goddess_of_Fate "Goddess of Fate") could leave the [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World") through the [River of Fate](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/River_of_Fate "River of Fate") but could not use it to enter the [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World").
						- The [Supernova Dominator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Supernova_Dominator "Supernova Dominator") would be able to break through the **Spirit World's** **Seal** with "His" attacks from the inside.
		- "They" are able to utilize the power within the [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World") to achieve different effects.
		- The **Lord of Mysteries** can use the power of the **Spirit World** to allow subordinates to surpass their current characteristic level. This is similar to granting [Boons](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Boons "Boons").[^14]
			- The upper limit that "They" could grant is a [King of Angels](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/King_of_Angels "King of Angels") level, this is the same way [Spirit World Creatures](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Creatures_and_Plants#Spirit_Beings "Creatures and Plants") get their power, level, and status.
				- When fighting within the [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World"), the **Lord of Mysteries** can generally mobilize the entire [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World") 's power to attack.[^15]
		- If information is not hidden, as the **Dominator of the Spirit World**, "They" can theoretically see and know every piece of information within the **[Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World")**.[^16]
		- The **Lord of Mysteries** can instantly receive information of the events happening in a different, significantly distant [Star Dominion](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Star_Dominion "Star Dominion") from the [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World").[^12]
			- This lets "Them" effectively shift "Their" focus accordingly to any change in the battlefield.
		- **Decryption** & **Traveling**: The **Decryption** power of the [Error Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway) leverages the **Spirit World**, and the [Door Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Door_Pathway) primarily utilizes the **Spirit World** for **Travelling**.[^17] "They" can combine these powers and authorities to crack open secrets and invade other protected realms.
		- The [Dual-Pathway True God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Dual-Pathway_True_God "Dual-Pathway True God") [Amon](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Amon "Amon") combined the **Decryption** power from the [Error Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway) and the authority over **Positions** from the [Door Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Door_Pathway) to crack the secrets of [The Fool](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/The_Fool "The Fool") 's [Divine Kingdom](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Divine_Kingdom "Divine Kingdom"), the **Realm of Mysteries**.[^18]
			- [Amon](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Amon "Amon") made countless symbols, patterns, and labels appeared. They moved quickly, interweaving, reforming, or changing, as though they were making calculations, before they formed the scene of [Klein](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") 's true location.
				- By making the [Celestial Worthy](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Celestial_Worthy "Celestial Worthy") awaken to a certain extent, using the [Door](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Door_Pathway) authority to go anywhere, and creating a back door and using **Bugs**, the [Dual-Pathway True God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Dual-Pathway_True_God "Dual-Pathway True God") [Amon](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Amon "Amon") can invade [Sefirah Castle](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirah_Castle "Sefirah Castle") and make it treat "Him" as the [Lord of Mysteries](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Celestial_Worthy_of_Heaven_and_Earth_for_Blessings "Celestial Worthy of Heaven and Earth for Blessings") and give "Him" the corresponding authority.[^19]
		- **Replication** / **Recreation**: An authority related to the **Spirit World**.[^20] "They" can **Replicate** the powers of other [Pathways](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways").
		- Because **Replication** is brought about by the **Inconceivable** symbolism, a [Sequence 0: Door](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Door_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Door) can **Replicate** **Authorities** of another Pathway's [Sequence 0](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sequence_0 "Sequence 0").[^21] However, the **Lord of Mysteries** cannot copy the **Symbols** of [Great Old Ones](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Old_Ones "Great Old Ones").[^22]
				- Examples of **Replication** include:
			- [Klein](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") **Replicated** the **Distortion** power from the [Black Emperor Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Black_Emperor_Pathway "Black Emperor Pathway"), and used it to influence the contract drafted by the [Primordial God Almighty](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Primordial_God_Almighty "Primordial God Almighty").[^23]
						- [Klein](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") could have **Replicated** the **Balancing** abilities of the [Justiciar Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Justiciar_Pathway "Justiciar Pathway") to evenly split forces across battlegrounds, but this would have had limited effect on the [Great Old Dominators](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Old_Dominators "Great Old Dominators") and would have hindered his ability to intervene in the other battlefield.[^24]
						- If the plan with [Lumian Lee](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee") and [Evernight Goddess](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Evernight_Goddess "Evernight Goddess") to create a power akin to the [Fourth Pillar](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Pillar "Fourth Pillar") to annihilate both the [Monarch of Decay](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Monarch_of_Decay "Monarch of Decay") and [Lumian](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee"), along with the other consciousnesses inside [Lumian](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee"), had gone as expected, the **Lord of Mysteries** [Klein](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") would have **Replicated** **New Birth**, employed **Grafting**, and other methods to potentially save [Lumian](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee").[^25]
		- Among the three [Pathways](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways") of the **Lord of Mysteries**, the [Fool Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fool_Pathway) wields the biggest portion of this symbolism.
		- Compared to the [Door Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Door_Pathway) that merely uses the **Spirit World** for **Traveling** and the [Error Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway) that uses the [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World") for **Decryption** and influencing the **Body of Heart and Mind**, the [Fool Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fool_Pathway) controls the majority of the [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World") authorities, being almost akin to a lesser **Lord of the Spirit World**.[^17]
- **Inconceivable** / **Bizarreness**: A symbolism corresponding to one of the [Original Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Original_Creator "Original Creator") 's labels.[^26] It can make things originally impossible to achieve realized to a certain extent.[^27]
	- The [Original Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Original_Creator "Original Creator") is the 'Exception', the 'Inconceivable', the 'Incomprehensible', the 'Indescribable'. All things that shouldn't happen might happen when encountering "Him"—unknowable, undiscussable, unnameable.[^28] The **Inconceivable** is one of "His" labels, inherited by the **Lord of Mysteries**.[^26]
		- The **Mysteries** in the **Lord of Mysteries** title points directly to the [Original Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Original_Creator "Original Creator") 's aspects of **Unknowable**, **Unnameable**.[^29] As a result, "They" symbolize the **Inconceivable**.[^30]
				- Because the **Inconceivable** is one of the [Original Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Original_Creator "Original Creator") 's labels, the **Lord of Mysteries**, like the [God Almighty](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/God_Almighty "God Almighty") and the [Mother Goddess of Depravity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Goddess_of_Depravity "Mother Goddess of Depravity"), can briefly tap into a bit of the [Original Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Original_Creator "Original Creator") 's power.[^26]
		- Since **Bizarreness** is also called **The Inconceivable**, it inherently contains the concept of the **Incomprehensible**. That is, it naturally carries a certain degree of **Secret/Concealment**.[^17]
		- The **Secret** part of the [Fool](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fool_Pathway) symbol actually originates from this; it is a manifestation of **Bizarreness**.
		- The **Inconceivable** symbolism, brought through the [Special Mirror World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Special_Mirror_Worlds#Fourth_Epoch_Trier "Special Mirror Worlds") by the [Celestial Worthy](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Celestial_Worthy "Celestial Worthy") 's interference via [Bethel Abraham](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Bethel_Abraham "Bethel Abraham"), combined with other factors made it possible for the [Mirrored Original Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mirrored_Original_Creator "Mirrored Original Creator") to be born.[^31] [^32]
		- Depending on the situation, the **Lord of Mysteries** can utilize the **Inconceivable** to lower the degree of countering and suppress the other symbolisms similar to **The Omnipotent and Omniscient** of the [God Almighty](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/God_Almighty).[^33]
		- "Their" **Change** and **Bizarreness** symbols are intrinsically connected: one is a transformation that can be understood, while the other is one that cannot be described or reasoned out.[^17]
		- **Error** plus **Miracles** is the foundation upon which **Change** can ascend to become **Inconceivable** Symbolism.[^34]
		- **Error & Loopholes (Bug)**: One of the two foundations that ascend **Change** into becoming **Inconceivable**.[^34] "They" are the symbol of **Errors** and **Loopholes** [^35] —the **Loopholes** in rules, the manifestation of all **Errors**. Thus, "They" are the expert at exploiting **Loopholes** and grasping the problem.[^36] [^37] [^38] [^39]
		- In [The Fool's Dream](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/The_Fool%27s_Dream "The Fool's Dream"), the master of the dream city was a symbol of **Loopholes** and **Errors**, so no matter how real this dream is, there will still be inconsistencies, **Loopholes**, and places that make people feel terrified upon deep thought. It will still express the "dream" aspect.[^35]
				- [Klein](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") relied on the shared authority between him and the [Circle of Inevitability](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Circle_of_Inevitability "Circle of Inevitability") to devise a vague and inaccurate [Honorific Name](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Honorific_Name "Honorific Name") that could refer to both of them. He also designed a ritual that allowed [Lumian](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee") to automatically extract power from [Termiboros](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Termiboros "Termiboros") every time he carried out the ritual without Klein's explicit attention through a 'back door' that has already been opened.[^36]
				- When [Lumian Lee's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee") destiny was sealed, and his madness was unavoidable, even a **Miracle** could not fully reverse it. As such, the only option was to exploit **Loopholes** and lower the difficulty of the **Wish,** granting [Lumian](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee") occasional "moments of clarity, rationality, and humanity" during madness. As for how "occasional" was defined, that was left to **Fooling** and **Error** to decide.[^40]
			- The exploited **Loophole** was that nothing dictated that inevitable madness precluded occasional moments of lucidity.
				- If the [Mother Goddess of Depravity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Goddess_of_Depravity "Mother Goddess of Depravity") used the **Rebirth** symbolism on "Herself" when accommodating the [Brood Hive](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Brood_Hive "Brood Hive"), [Klein](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") could use the **Error** or **Fooling** symbolism to **Fool** the target of **Rebirth**, causing the [Brood Hive](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Brood_Hive "Brood Hive") 's consciousness to be reborn within the [Mother Goddess of Depravity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Goddess_of_Depravity "Mother Goddess of Depravity"), thereby making "Her" fall into internal conflict.[^41]
				- By using the authority of the [Error Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway) and with a suitable medium, "They" can elevate the mystical connection between two separate entities that were originally one into a connection akin to that between an avatar and its true body, or oneself and oneself—allowing the damage and consequences experienced by one to be transmitted to the other.[^12]
			- After borrowing the authority of the [Error Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway) from [Klein](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti"), [Amon](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Amon "Amon") was able to "reconstruct" the mystical connections between:

			- Normally, similar to how a [King of Angels](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/King_of_Angels "King of Angels") with the [Uniqueness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniqueness "Uniqueness") + 3 Sequence 1 characteristics would forcefully become [Sequence 0](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sequence_0 "Sequence 0") (unless "They" can **Seal** the **Convergence**), a [Half Great Old One](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Half_Great_Old_One "Half Great Old One") with all [Uniquenesses](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniquenesses "Uniquenesses") + Sequence 1 characteristics would forcefully become a [Great Old One](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Old_One "Great Old One") due to uncontrollable **Convergence**. However, in [The Apocalypse](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/The_Apocalypse "The Apocalypse"), [The Fool](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/The_Fool "The Fool") prevented this from happening to ensure post-event safety.[^119]
				- [Klein Moretti](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") **Sealed** [Termiboros](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Termiboros "Termiboros"), an Archangel of the [Eternal Aeon Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Eternal_Aeon_Pathway "Eternal Aeon Pathway") inside [Lumian Lee](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee"), siphoned some of "His" power and gave it to [Lumian Lee](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee") as [Boons](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Boons "Boons").
				- [Klein Moretti](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") **Sealed** the [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World"), cutting the connection and influence the [Outer Deities](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Outer_Deities "Outer Deities") have on it.[^9]

### Countering Symbols, Authorities & Abilities

Certain **Symbols**, **Authorities**, and **Abilities** of other [Great Old Ones](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Old_Ones "Great Old Ones") can counter some of "Their" powers, which include but are not limited to the following:

- [Mother Goddess of Depravity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Goddess_of_Depravity):
	- **New Life**, **Death**, **Desolation** mutually counteracts **Marionettes** and **Parasitism**.[^60]
		- The **Marionettes** controlled by the **Lord of Mysteries** can **Wither** or become **Pregnant** as their flesh and blood concentrated into offspring that were not marionettes, or gain **New Life** and consciousness, ceasing to be mere puppets and starting to resist the **Marionettist** controlling them.
				- However, the **Marionettes'** offspring or those reborn can also be turned into **Marionettes**, entering a new cycle and causing a stalemate.
		- **New Life** counters **Foolishness**.[^23]
		- Every part of the [Mother Goddess of Depravity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Goddess_of_Depravity "Mother Goddess of Depravity") is in a state of constant **New Life**, including "Her" soul, consciousness, spirit, and intellect. This causes "Her" to recover "Her" intellect as soon as "She" becomes **Foolish**.
			- However, this doesn't mean that "She" cannot be affected by **Foolishness** and be **Deceived** if "She" were to face the **Lord of Mysteries** directly.[^65]
		- **Sovereign of the Physical World** counters **Grafting**, **Theft**, **Seal**, **Banishment**, and **Blink**.
		- The [Mother Goddess of Depravity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Goddess_of_Depravity "Mother Goddess of Depravity") can fix mystical connections, spirits, consciousness, etc. in place, thus preventing them from being **Grafted** or **Stolen**.
			- "She" solidified the mystical connection between the [Mother Tree of Desire](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Tree_of_Desire "Mother Tree of Desire") and [Tenebrous World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tenebrous_World "Tenebrous World"), disrupting [Klein](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") 's **Marionettes'** attempt to **Graft** the connection back to themselves.[^12]
						- "She" fixed the [Goddess of Fate](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Goddess_of_Fate "Goddess of Fate") 's consciousness and spirit in place, disrupting [Klein](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") 's **Theft** and saving "Her" from the fate of being **Sealed** into the [Chained](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chained_Pathway "Chained Pathway") materials.[^112]
				- "She" can intertwine "Herself" within the fabric of the real universe and become rooted deeply in reality. This makes "Her" unable to be **Banished** or **Sealed**.[^63]
				- "Her" interference could cause [Klein](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") 's **Blinking** form to flicker and become unstable.[^12]
- [God Almighty](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/God_Almighty):
	- **The Omnipotent and Omniscient** mutually counteract **Inconceivable**.[^120]
		- While the [God Almighty](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/God_Almighty "God Almighty") represents **The Omniscient**, thus symbolically **All-Knowing**, the **Lord of Mysteries** symbolizes the **Incomprehensible**.[^46]
		- **The Omniscient** counteracts **Deceit** and **Fooling**, but not entirely.
		- **Deceiving** or **Fooling** [God Almighty](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/God_Almighty "God Almighty") is a very difficult process, as **The Omniscient** cannot be easily tricked.[^121]
		- **Contracts** counters **Deceit** (unless used in tandem with **Distortion**),[^23] and mutually counteracts **Bugs** and **Grafting**.[^122]
		- Using the **Replicated** power of **Distortion** alongside **Deceit**, [Klein Moretti](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") was able to **Distort** a clause of the **Contract** between the [Primordial God Almighty](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Primordial_God_Almighty "Primordial God Almighty") and the [Mother Goddess of Depravity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Goddess_of_Depravity "Mother Goddess of Depravity").[^23]
- [High-Dimensional Overseer](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/High-Dimensional_Overseer):
	- **Dimensionality** and **Change**, which included space-time, mutually counteracts each other in a one-on-one situation.[^9]
		- As the **Lord of Dimensions**, "He" could counter abilities such as **Parasitism**, **Marionettes**, and **Blink**.
			- From higher or lower **Dimensions**, **Spirit Body Threads** are tangible and real-something that can be touched. **Blink** only allows movement on a small scale, while those **Parasitized** are bound together into one entity. This rendered the unique, ubiquitous nature of the **Lord of Mysteries** ' **Avatars** ineffective. Combat against the [High-Dimensional Overseer](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/High-Dimensional_Overseer "High-Dimensional Overseer") could only rely on the **Lord of Mysteries** ' true body.
- [Eternal Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Eternal_Darkness):
	- **Space-Time-in-One** mutually counteracts **King of Space-Time** depending on the occasion and circumstances, with the **Space-Time-in-One** being slightly more dominant.[^123]
		- **Death** counters **Avatars**, **Marionettes**, **Paper Figurine Substitutes**, and other similar clones and substitutions abilities of the **Lord of Mysteries**.[^124]
		- **Death** of these clones and substitutions equals the **Death** of the main body.
- [Monarch of Decay](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Monarch_of_Decay):
	- **Decay** and **Certain Death** counter **Avatars** and **Blink**.[^9]
		- **Death** of an **Avatar** equals the death of the main body.
				- **Decay** of an **Afterimage** equals the **Decay** of the main body.
- [Inextinguishable Ravings](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Inextinguishable_Ravings):
	- **Source of All Ravings** counters **Avatars**, **Marionettes**, and **Blink**.[^9]
		- "He" can ignore the interference of **Marionettes**, **Avatars**, and **Afterimages** to directly project "His" voice into [Sefirot](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirot "Sefirot") and [Uniquenesses](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniquenesses "Uniquenesses") transmitting "His" madness and implanting "His" intent.
- [Primordial Hunger](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Primordial_Hunger):
	- **Convergence** and **Devouring** counter **Avatars**, **Marionettes**, and **Blink**.[^9]
		- **Convergence**: "He" could forcibly pull the **Lord of Mysteries** 's true body to "His" side.
				- **Devouring**: If "He" **Devoured** an **Avatar** or **Marionette**, "He" could absorb and erase the corresponding abilities, reducing or even temporarily nullifying their symbolic influence.
- [Father of Devils](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Father_of_Devils):
	- **Source of Curses** counters **Avatars** and **Marionettes**:[^9]
		- "They" are capable of **Cursing** **Avatars** and **Marionettes** to indirectly **Curse** the true body.
- [Supernova Dominator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Supernova_Dominator):
	- **Density**, **Gravity**, and **Attraction** can influence **Space-Time** and distort **Dimensions**.[^9]
		- However, for the **Lord of Mysteries**, who controlled **Change** and could turn falsehood into truth and vice versa, mere indirect interference was sufficient to stall "Him" without engaging directly.
- [Circle of Inevitability](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Circle_of_Inevitability):
	- **Past**, **Present**, **Future**, **Cycle**, and **Destined** could theoretically impact **Avatars**, **Marionettes**, and **Blink**.[^9]
		- However, the [Circle of Inevitability](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Circle_of_Inevitability "Circle of Inevitability") 's symbols were effectively countered by the **Lord of Mysteries** ' other symbols:
			- **King of Space-Time** counters **Past**, **Present**, **Future**.
						- **Beacon of Destiny** counters **Cycle**.
						- **Error** counters **Destined**.
- [Goddess of Fate](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Goddess_of_Fate):
	- **Weaver of All Fates** mutually counters **Beacon of Destiny**.[^125]
		- Depending on the situation, the [Goddess of Fate](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Goddess_of_Fate "Goddess of Fate") can counter the **Lord of Mysteries'** **Beacon of Destiny**; a tug of war of symbolism where each counters the other and vice versa.
			- In [The Apocalypse](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/The_Apocalypse "The Apocalypse"), the [Goddess of Fate](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Goddess_of_Fate "Goddess of Fate") was drawn in by the **Lord of Mysteries** Klein at the time [^9] because "She" was a node of the current [River of Fate](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/River_of_Fate "River of Fate"), as such "She" would inevitably pass through the **Beacon of Destiny**.
- [The Anarchy](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/The_Anarchy):
	- **Balance** counters **King of Space-Time**.
		- **Balancing** the battlefields would hinder the **King of Space-Time** ability to intervene in the other battlefield.[^24]

## Mythical Creature Form

NOTE

This [Mythical Creature Form](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mythical_Creature_Form "Mythical Creature Form") is taken from [Klein Moretti](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti"), who is the current **Lord of Mysteries**. The Mythical Creature Form of the [Former Lord of Mysteries](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Celestial_Worthy_of_Heaven_and_Earth_for_Blessings "Celestial Worthy of Heaven and Earth for Blessings") is unknown.

Within the depths of the [black trench coat](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniquenesses#Fool_Pathway), a glowing [bluish-black door](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirah_Castle) seemed to flicker in and out of existence. The [star-encrusted cane](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniquenesses#Door_Pathway) and [black gloves](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniquenesses#Error_Pathway) collapsed inward as well, forming a massive vortex within the trench coat — a terrifying vortex. Every gust of wind, droplet of water, and ray of light in the vortex consisted of **Worms of Spirit**, **Worms of Time**, and **Worms of Stars**. Within a second or two, the vortex was also devoured. The inside became an abyss, seemingly infinite, as if it contained an entire universe and a [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World") connected to every planet. Then, within the darkness, smooth tentacles inscribed with mysterious patterns emerged. They slipped into black gloves at the sleeves and lifted the star-encrusted cane.[^70]

## Trivia

- [Book 1](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lord_of_Mysteries_\(Novel\) "Lord of Mysteries (Novel)") in the Mysteries series is named after the title of this [Great Old One](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Old_One "Great Old One").
- When asked if the **Lord of Mysteries** is inspired by the following, the author answered with "All of them":[^126]
	- [The King in Yellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_King_in_Yellow "wikipedia:The King in Yellow").[^1]
		- [Nyarlathotep](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nyarlathotep "wikipedia:Nyarlathotep"), an Outer God in the [Cthulhu Mythos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cthulhu_Mythos "wikipedia:Cthulhu Mythos").
		- As the "God of a Thousand Forms" who delights in deception, [Nyarlathotep](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nyarlathotep "wikipedia:Nyarlathotep") corresponds to the [Faceless](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fool_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_6:_Faceless) and [Swindler](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_8:_Swindler).
		- [The Gods of Pegāna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Gods_of_Peg%C4%81na "wikipedia:The Gods of Pegāna"), which served as a prototype and influence to the [Cthulhu Mythos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cthulhu_Mythos "wikipedia:Cthulhu Mythos").
		- The attendant "Time" corresponds to [Nyarlathotep](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nyarlathotep "wikipedia:Nyarlathotep"). This corresponds to the [Worm of Time](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_1:_Worm_of_Time).
		- [Yog-Sothoth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yog-Sothoth "wikipedia:Yog-Sothoth"), an Outer God in the [Cthulhu Mythos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cthulhu_Mythos "wikipedia:Cthulhu Mythos").
		- [Azathoth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azathoth "wikipedia:Azathoth"), an Outer God in the [Cthulhu Mythos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cthulhu_Mythos "wikipedia:Cthulhu Mythos"), who corresponds to the [Fool](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fool_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Fool) 's authority.
- The author was once asked: "Can The **Lord of Mysteries** make someone immortal till the end of the universe?", this question's answer was: "Turning them into **Marionettes** would make them immortal." [^127]
- The acting principle of "A [Magician](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fool_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_7:_Magician) doesn't preform unprepared" stems from **The Inconceivable** symbolism, it's a lower manifestation of a lower manifestation. The [Magicians'](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fool_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_7:_Magician) performances are always **Inconceivable**.[^128]
- The author added the condition of " **Lord of Mysteries** needs to manifest [Sefirah Castle](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirah_Castle "Sefirah Castle") if they were to use "Their" full symbolisms" just because the magnificent effect of The **Door of Light** would look cooler when manifested.[^85]
