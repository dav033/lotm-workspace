## Sequence 9: Secrets Suppliant

**Traits** [^1]

- **High Spirituality**: **Secrets Suppliants** have a high **Spiritual Perception** which they can use it to detect some mysterious and horrible existences.[^2]
	- **Ritualistic Magic & Divination Arts**: They can use their strong **Spirituality** to perform [Divination](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Divination_Art "Divination Art") and [Ritualistic Magic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ritualistic_Magic "Ritualistic Magic"), but it's not as accurate as [Pathways](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways") that specialize in this field.[^3]
		- Their **High Spirituality** also allows them to detect auras of relatively powerful **Beyonders** (provided that those **Beyonders** did not conceal their aura beforehand).[^3]
		- [Osta Trul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Osta_Trul "Osta Trul") was able to feel that the real world was wrapped in a [thick veil](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Astral_Barrier "Astral Barrier"), and beyond that veil, [emotionless eyes](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Outer_Deities "Outer Deities") were watching them.[^4]

**New Abilities** [^1]

- **Knowledge (Mysticism)**: **Secrets Suppliants** are bestowed with a decent amount of knowledge regarding sacrifices and some knowledge on [Ritualistic Magic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ritualistic_Magic "Ritualistic Magic").[^2] [^5]
	- There are some [Honorific Names](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Honorific_Name "Honorific Name") included in the potion's knowledge, but they're all secret entities with sinister names that includes elements like "depravity", "true kin", or the "gaze of fate".[^5]
		- It is known that the information attached to the potion contains the name of the [True Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/True_Creator "True Creator") and **Angel** of **Fate** [Ouroboros](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ouroboros "Ouroboros"); however, the three parts of the name "gaze of fate" does not fully describe the latter's **Honorific** **Name**. [Aurora Order](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Aurora_Order "Aurora Order") members who gave this name have other purposes, so they have made some processing on the name.
		- The knowledge attached to the potion given will more or less cause distortions in a **Secrets Suppliants'** cognitive perspectives and thus make it easy for them to lose control.

## Sequence 8: Listener (Whispered)

**Description**

[Nighthawk](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Nighthawks "Nighthawks") files state that every **Listener** is a lunatic - even if they look normal on the surface, they are always crazy on the inside. The files also state that it was difficult for **Listeners** to survive the next five years after taking the potion if they were unable to advance, because they frequently came into contact with powerful, distorted entities.[^2]

**Traits**

- **High Spirituality**: Their spiritual perception is further enhanced.

**New Abilities** [^1]

- **Listen**: Every **Listener** can **Listen** directly into the whispers of the secret entities; thus, they frequently come into contact with information from powerful, distorted, and unique entities.[^2]
	- While they can obtain powerful, twisted, and unique abilities, if they cannot advance in **Sequence**, it would be difficult for them to survive for more than 5 years.
		- Every **Listener** is a lunatic - even if they look normal on the surface and behave normally at ordinary times, they are always crazy on the inside.
			- **Listeners** will often hear the ravings of the [True Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/True_Creator "True Creator").[^6]
				- For Oracles of the [Aurora Order](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Aurora_Order "Aurora Order"), the ability to directly hear the ravings of the [True Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/True_Creator "True Creator") and "His" instructions grants them strong autonomy.[^7]
		- Even if they don't actively use **Listening**, one will hear more than ordinary people and most of the **Low-Mid Sequence** **Beyonders** within a normal area.
		- When the source of the sound is close enough to them, a **Listener** will be more affected and will receive more dangerous information.
		- At this stage, this **Beyonder** power is always active (in a passive state). At later **Sequences**, they can choose whether or not to proactively use it.[^8]
		- Entities will always be able to speak to a **Listener**, even if a **Listener** removes their ears.[^9]

## Sequence 7: Shadow Ascetic

**New Abilities** [^1]

- **Summon Shadows**: **Shadow Ascetics** can summon **Shadow** creatures to influence, restrict or attack the target; however, **Summoning** a **Shadow** creature is a risky action.
	- The **Summoned Shadow** creature may be something terrifying and out of control, or it may merely be a monster that only feeds on the **Shadow Ascetic**.
- **Shadow Curse**: Using the target's **Flesh** and **Blood**, the cooperation of a [Ritual](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ritualistic_Magic "Ritualistic Magic"), or part of the target's **Shadow** as a medium, they can inflict a **Curse** on them.
- **Manipulating Shadows**: After **Lurking** in the **Shadows**, **Shadow Ascetics** can influence the target's **Shadow** in the **Shadow** **World**, and even try to capture it.
	- A **Shadow Ascetic** can envelop a target in huge black chrysalis made from **Shadows** that can contain a **[Pugilist](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Twilight_Giant_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_8:_Pugilist_\(Gladiator\))** **Rampager**.[^10]
- **Shadow Shaping**: **Shadow Ascetics** are able to directly utilize the power of **Shadows** and **Shape** those **Shadows** into weapons or animals.
	- Such **Shadow** items can cause damage that makes the target cold and **Degenerate**.

**Strengthened Abilities**

- **Listen**: It is at this **Sequence** where a **Shadow Ascetic** can turn off this **Beyonder** power.[^8]

## Sequence 6: Rose Bishop (Rose Priest)

**New Abilities** [^1]

- **Flesh & Blood Magic**: Every **Rose Bishop** is an expert in **Flesh & Blood Magic**.[^14] [^15]
	- They are extremely skilled at treating physical injuries. Its essence lies in deep control over cells. Regardless of the **Mutations** that occur in the body, as long as it does not involve the [Soul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul"), it can be recovered.
		- In other words, unless their **Flesh** and **Blood** are depleted, as long as their [Soul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul") has not been annihilated, the body of a **Rose Bishop** does not have fatal weaknesses.
				- For reference they can heal from and fully restore entire lost limbs. Even seemingly fatal wounds to their head or heart are not necessarily fatal.[^16] [^17]
		- Manifestations of **Flesh** **&** **Blood Magic** include:[^1] [^18]
		- **Flesh Cloak**: A **Rose Bishop** can create cloaks of sticky **Flesh** (either procured from them or others) that can resist magic and reduce damage.
				- **Flesh Blood Fusion**: **Rose Bishops** can melt like a candle and turn into pure sticky **Flesh** and **Blood** that can quickly penetrate into the ground and move, or hide within other people's stomach in order to avoid detection.
			- To stay better **Concealed**, they are able to integrate with the host's body; however, once they leave the body, their host will die.
						- When a **Rose Bishop** enters the inside of a host body's stomach, they will be able to monitor the host's current status.
						- Parts of a **Rose Bishop's** body (like their face) can melt like a candle as a way to assimilate other creatures.[^19]
				- [Heath Doyle's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Heath_Doyle "Heath Doyle") plastered his face on the *meter long* blue fish, which stiffened and melted into a disgusting pool of **Flesh** and **Blood** that surged into his mouth.[^19]
				- **Flesh Bomb**: Their body's **Flesh** or a part of it can be torn off and made into **Flesh Bombs** that can be thrown as explosives, which will explode into a rain of **Corrosive Blood**.
			- Their **Flesh** and **Blood** **Bombs** can also be buried inside other people's bodies, which can be detonated, turning them into sticky and rich **Flesh** and **Blood**.
						- **Rose Bishops** are able to downsize their **Flesh** **Bombs** into **Flesh** bullets, and can fire those **Flesh** bullets out directly from within their body.[^20]
				- **Flesh Blood Servants**: This creates puppets using **Flesh** and **Blood** which have immense strength, are immune to attacks targeting the **Spirit**, and can self-destruct.
				- **Flesh Blood Curse**: One should never let their **Flesh** and **Blood** fall into the hands of a **Rose Bishop**.
				- **Devouring**: They can envelop a target, corrode their body, and kill them from the inside,[^21] alternatively, they can wrap a corpse within their own body and depart with it.[^12]
				- **Flesh Softening**:<sup>⁂</sup> They can soften blows on their body as though they were boneless piles of rotten **Flesh**. Even gunshots would only sink into their body, failing to do damage.[^16]
				- To some extent, they can modify their own appearance, such as making them more muscular or slightly changing facial features with sufficient skill and practice.[^22]
			- However, a **Rose Bishop** can't adjust their appearance as precisely as a **[Faceless](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fool_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_6:_Faceless)**, so the modifications may look quite weird.
				- They can change their skin color and the hardness of their skin but they can't change the nature of skin being made of **Flesh** and **blood**.[^20]
				- They can form the corresponding male or female organs to change their sex.[^20]
			- However, they probably can only experience orgasm, but they do not have the ability to produce the corresponding reproductive cells (a.k.a. sex cells or gametes).
				- A female **Rose Bishop** can stop having her menstrual period early.[^23]
				- They can form extra limbs on their body (like fighting with 6 arms).[^20]
				- They are able to form weapons using their bones.[^20]
		- A **Rose Bishop** must regularly replenish their **Flesh** and **Blood**.[^16]
		- If they do not do so, a **Rose Bishop** will not be able to fully exert the power of **Flesh** **and** **Blood** **Magic** and will easily lose control and/or die.
				- They can use the **Flesh** and **Blood** of corpses in their immediate surroundings.[^15]

## Sequence 5: Shepherd

**Description**

**Shepherds** are ranked first below [High Sequences.](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sequence "Sequence") They are so all-rounded that if they combined and matched their Beyonder powers well enough, they would almost have zero shortcomings.[^24]

**New Abilities** [^1]

- **Grazing**: **Shepherds** can swallow a target's [Soul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul") and **Beyonder Characteristics** or **Boon** powers into their body, including **Wraiths** and [Evil Spirits](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Evil_Spirits "Evil Spirits"), and use those **Beyonder** powers for themselves.[^25] [^26]
	- They can **Graze** [Saint-level](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Saint "Category:Saint") [Souls](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul"), however, doing so will greatly increase their risk of losing control.
		- If a **Grazed** [Soul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul") is at the level of a **Demigod**, it can be directly released into the real world to fight. This is due to the corporeal **Spirit Bodies** that **Demigods** possess.[^27]
			- Using the **Grazed** [Soul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul") powers will only consume that [Soul's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul") **Spirituality**
						- Releasing a [Soul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul") won't allow the **Shepherd** to use another [Soul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul").
						- Only one of such [Soul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul") can exist in the real world at a time.
		- Losing **Grazing** will make them lose control because of the [Souls'](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul") interaction in their body.
		- Making them unable to use **Grazed** [Souls](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul") will not have much of an effect on them. It is only when they lose the ability fully would they begin to lose control.[^28]
			- A **[Depriver's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tail-Devourer_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_5:_Depriver)** **Deprivation** as well as a **[Prometheus'](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_6:_Prometheus)** **Theft** are examples of **Beyonder** powers that can cause a **Shepherd** to lose control.[^29]
		- **Shepherds** can't activate **Grazing** continuously, which restricts passive abilities.
		- As such **Beyonder** powers that are more passive in nature (like a **[Devil's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Abyss_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_6:_Devil) Danger Premonition**) are effective only when actively **Grazing** that particular [Soul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul").[^26]
		- A **Shepherd**, upon full potion digestion, will be able to **Graze** up to *7 [Souls](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul")*.[^30]
		- They can only use one [Soul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul") at a time, but they can instantly switch between them.
		- They can only use up to *3* **Beyonder** powers from a particular [Soul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul").[^30]
		- As the **Sequence** increases, they will be able to use more abilities from **Grazed** [Souls](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul").
				- When **Grazing** a new [Soul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul"), a **Shepherd** will be able to choose which powers to acquire.
				- They can still use their own **Beyonder** powers when using a [Soul's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul") **Beyonder** powers.[^16]
			- **Beyonder** powers that normally don't allow its user to move freely like a **[Nightmare's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Darkness_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_7:_Nightmare) Nightmare State** can be used without the **Shepherd** needing to stay still.
		- A **Shepherd's** **Grazed** target has to include a [Soul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul").
		- Such a condition won't be changed even at higher **Sequences**. Even the **Hanged Man** needs a [Soul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul") to **Graze** a [Uniqueness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniquenesses "Uniquenesses") or **Beyonder Characteristic** and cannot simply target it alone.[^31]
				- **Grazing** entails a certain reduction in the potency **Beyonder** powers of the **Grazed** [Soul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul"). This is because the [Soul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul") must be damaged before it can be **Grazed** by a **Shepherd**.
			- Even if the [Soul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul") of a target stays **Grazed**, it will gradually wear away afterwards, meaning it can't be permanently kept by them.
						- If a [Soul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul") does not fall in **Depravity**, and instead is forcibly **Grazed**, it won't completely cooperate with the **Shepherd**.
						- Depending on the state of the [Soul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul"), its power might be halved.

**Strengthened Abilities** [^1]

- **Flesh and Blood Magic**: They have further mastered abilities under **Flesh and Blood Magic**.
	- **Devouring**: **Shepherds** can directly **Devour** a target's **Flesh** and **Blood**, along with their [Soul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul"), leaving no traces behind.[^32]
- **Controlling Shadows**: An advanced version of **Manipulating Shadows**, which allows the direct **Manipulation** of deep **Shadows** not belonging to living creatures.
	- They can give these **Shadows** physical substance, make them bind targets, crush or compress objects, or **Corrode** and melt entities.

## Sequence 4: Black Knight

**New Abilities** [^1]

- **Cull of Spiritual Flesh**: This is the core **Beyonder** power of a **Black Knight**, stemming from the **Degeneration** domain. It manifests as a **Black** **Greatsword** and has the following effects:[^33]
	- It can render any living creature with **Degenerate** thoughts to lose their defenses.
		- It **Corrodes** both **Flesh** and **Blood** while also obliterating [Souls](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul").
		- It can cleave through powerful physical barriers.
- **Black Armor**: They can transform into a small giant *2-3 meters tall*, covered in a **Black**, cold **Armor**, with only two balls of deep red light flashing at the position of their eyes.[^33] [^27] [^34]
	- This **Black Armor** is formed from the powers of **Degeneration**, protecting them from various attacks and it can also fracture to redirect damage, protecting the **Black Knight**.
- **Shadow of Depravation**: This peels off one's **Depraved** thoughts into a **Shadow**, forming an uncontrollable, independent creature.
	- This can be used to purge a foreign consciousness out of their mind.
		- For instance, they can eject a **[Manipulator's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Visionary_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_4:_Manipulator) Virtual Persona** from their mind and turn it into a **Shadow**. If the **[Manipulator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Visionary_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_4:_Manipulator)** cannot quickly resolve this, their fractured thoughts would gradually become independent, turning them half-mad and even causing them to lose control.[^35]
		- This can disintegrate into a void of **Darkness**, causing objects it touches to be stained with a layer of thick, sticky blackness—either instantly corroding and breaking apart, or **Degenerating** and turning to attack the **Black Knight's** enemies.
		- The **Corrupted** [Enuni](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Enzo "Enzo") made his illusory black wings disintegrate to defend against [Derrick](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Derrick_Berg "Derrick Berg") 's barrage of **Unshadowed Spears**; some instantly corroded and broke, others left a curved arc in the air as it spun around and tore at [Derrick](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Derrick_Berg "Derrick Berg") himself.[^33]
		- This can also transform the **Shadow** into armor for defense, separate from the **Black Armor**.
		- The dark wings that enveloped [Enuni's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Enzo "Enzo") body blocked influences and attacks such as the **Unshadowed Domain** [^36] and **Hurricane of Light**.[^37]
- **Commandeering Shadows**: This makes the target’s own **Shadow** come alive and surge like rising water, tightly binding them.[^38]
	- This **Shadow** is equivalent to a fraction of the target, therefore severing their **Shadow** will make their spirit lose integrity.

**Strengthened Abilities** [^1]

- Other **Beyonder** powers from previous **Sequences** are enhanced.
	- **Flesh and Blood Magic**: Their grasp on abilities of this domain have been strengthened.
		- **Flesh Rug**:<sup>⁂</sup> They can transform themselves into a thin, **Bloody**, and **Flesh** -dripping **Rug** that covers an area, effectively dividing the battlefield and preventing grouping among enemies.[^27]
		- **Grazing**: Their ability to **Graze** [Souls](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul") have been enhanced.
		- They can use more abilities from **Grazed** [Souls](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul").
				- They can externally release any **Grazed** [Soul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul"), which can use all their **Beyonder** powers in this form.
				- They can hold up to *9 **Grazed** [Souls](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul")*.
		- **Controlling Shadows**: Their ability to control **Shadows** have been enhanced.
		- They can make the surrounding streets quickly fill with a pitch-black and strange liquid that then surge over and quickly coagulated to form a strong cage that surrounds an area.[^39]

**Mythical Creature Form**

- As **Sequence 4 Demigods** they receive some level of **Godhood**, and with it comes an incomplete [Mythical Creature Form](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mythical_Creature_Form "Mythical Creature Form"). Disregarding all consequences, they can release their incomplete [Mythical Creature Form](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mythical_Creature_Form "Mythical Creature Form") to become a black **Shadow** and writhing **Flesh**.[^40]

## Sequence 3: Trinity Templar

**New Abilities**

- **Shadow Substitute**: It allows one's **Shadow** to take damage in place of the main body.[^1] [^38]
	- The amount of times they are able to use **Shadow Substitute** depends on whether the **Shadow** has been destroyed and whether it has recovered.[^41]

**Authorities** [^1]

- **Flesh & Blood:** They gain certain manifestations of the **Flesh** and **Blood** authorities—which are subordinate to the **Mutation** authority.
	- **Trinity**:<sup>⁂</sup> They can sprout *2 **Shadowed*** *heads* from their shoulders— *one young and one old*.
		- Each head can take away a third of the body and, through **Flesh and Blood Magic**, instantly become whole again.
		- **Flesh and Blood Immortality**: So long as there is a single cell left and so long as there is other **Flesh** and **Blood** around to be absorbed, a **Trinity Templar** can rebuild their body.
		- The premise is of course that their spirit is not destroyed.

**Strengthened Abilities [^1]**

- They gain improvements to **Beyonder** powers such as **Cull of the Spirit Flesh** and prior abilities.
	- **Grazing**: The **Grazing** of a **Shepherd** reaches the level of a **Sequence 3**.
		- They can simultaneously release up to *3 [Souls](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul")*, one from each of their three heads.
				- They can **Graze** up to *13 [Souls](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul")* in total.

**Mythical Creature Form** [^1]

- As a **Sequence 3 Beyonder**, they can listen and respond to prayers within a certain range.

## Sequence 2: Profane Presbyter

**Authorities** [^1] [^42]

- **Profane Language**: "They" have mastered the **Profane Language**, a manifestation of the authorities of **Degeneration** and **Filth**.
	- "They" can weaponize a target's responses and words- extracting phrases or magnifying ambiguities to impose a word-spirit like effect such as imprisonment, **Corruption** or separation.
		- Any actions that have the meaning of “exchange” or “communication” can be affected by **Profane Language**. For example, sign language can be distorted or misinterpreted.[^1]
			- When [Klein](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") answered who he was, it ended up separating "a lonely traveler" from him, splitting his [Gehrman Sparrow](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") persona that has the same thoughts and abilities, effectively canceling [Klein's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") **Historical Projection Summoning**.
				- The separation here was when Klein's **Degenerate** thoughts combined with his **Shadow**, which then cancelled out his **Historical Void Summoning**.
						- When [Klein](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") said that he didn’t know where the owner of the city had gone, Angel of the Holy Word [Steph](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Steph "Steph") extracted the words “I don’t know where I went,” trapping him and isolating him from the outside world.
		- "They" can achieve enhancements on "Themselves", making it so that when "They" reveal "Their" identity, each word that references "Them" enhances "Their" level and strength.
		- When [Steph](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Steph "Steph") announced "Himself" to [Klein](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti"), "He" used this aspect of the **Profane Language** authority, effectively enhancing "His" level and strength with each and every word.
		- "They" can induce a target to **Degenerate** via a "spiritual" **Cull of Spiritual Flesh**. Targets who possess **Degenerate** thoughts are not able to resist this influence.
		- "They" can bring out the **Degenerate** thoughts of "Their" opponents as their [Soul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul") plummets, sinking into the viscous black depths of their inner psyche, restraining their actions.[^43]
			- [Alger Wilson](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Alger_Wilson "Alger Wilson") experienced this from the middle head of [The Setting Sun Rooster](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/The_Setting_Sun_Rooster "The Setting Sun Rooster"), causing his [Soul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul") to plummet, sinking into the viscous black depths of his inner psyche. This caused **Malice**, dissatisfaction, and jealousy surged within him as he felt an overwhelming urge to conserve his strength and avoid unnecessary risks.
				- "They" can bring an invisible force pulling a target's consciousness and spirit downward, as though they were an irreversible sunset, making their expression twitch uncontrollably as they begin to succumb to **Depravity**.[^44]
			- However, **Depravity** is a process, not an instantaneous event.
		- Through "Their" authority of **Profane Language**, "They" can create and direct **Curses** onto targets that inflict **Degeneration** and **Corruption**.
- **Flesh & Blood:** Their previous abilities were strengthened.
	- **Trinity Body**: Each of "Their" created heads can now use more than one **Grazed Soul**.
		- [Steph](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Steph "Steph") created a head covered in blood on “His” left shoulder. Around that head were two skinless arms that had mangled flesh. At the same time, another bloody head grew on [Steph](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Steph "Steph") ’s right shoulder, one that looked to be in “His” twenties. Amongst the three heads, on used **Grazed** [Souls](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul") that had powers that can **Steal** [Klein](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") ’s thoughts, while the other was staring coldly at the target, deepening the self-awareness of the lonely traveler.

**Strengthened Abilities** [^1]

- "Their" capabilities in the area of **Flesh and Blood Magic** as well **Shadows** have been strengthened.
	- **Grazing**: "They" can **Graze** up to *18 [Souls](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul")*.

**Mythical Creature Form**

- "Their" [Mythical Creature Form](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mythical_Creature_Form "Mythical Creature Form") has become complete. "Their" [Mythical Creature Form](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mythical_Creature_Form "Mythical Creature Form") is that of a black **Shadow** and writhing **Flesh**.[^40]

## Sequence 1: Dark Angel

**Authorities** [^1]

- **Shadows** (**Shadow Ruler**): A **Dark Angel** is able to control and use the **Shadow World**; however, "They" are not yet the **Shadow World's** full master.
	- Similar to the [Demoness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Demoness_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Demoness_\(Chaos_Demoness,_Primordial_Demoness\)) ' **Dark Magic** authority, "They" are effectively immune to any **Spells** or abilities that utilize **Shadows** or the **Shadow World**, and can alter those powers freely.[^45]
		- This also makes "Them" **Omnipotent** against creatures that reside within the **Shadow World**.
		- After completing the [Advancement Ritual](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonders#The_Advancement_Ritual "Beyonders") and advancing to **Dark Angel**, "They" will be the **Shadow** of the **Sun**, increasing "Their" resistance towards the **Sun's Purification**, making "Them" no longer be severely countered by the [Sun Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sun_Pathway) as before.[^46]
- **Degeneration** / **Depravity**: "They" have mastered the authorities of **Degeneration** domain.[^47]
	- A **Dark Angel** is able to make *people*, *objects*, *projections* and *abilities* **Corrode** and **Betray** their owner. Even a target's own body is no exception.
		- [The Evil Spirit Sasrir](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sasrir "Sasrir") **Degenerated** [Lovia](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lovia_Tiffany "Lovia Tiffany") 's body, making it **Betray** her.
		- **Profane Language**: The **Dark Angel's** voice, being both evil and sinister, carries a strong sense of **Degeneration**.[^48] [^47]
		- With "Their" use of **Profane Language**, "They" can cause accelerated life drain on targets as well as intensify the madness that is within an incomplete [Mythical Creature Form](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mythical_Creature_Form "Mythical Creature Form").
			- Using words like "Blasphemer, die!", "They" could make a **Demigod's** consciousness turn blurry, their energy evaporate, flesh wither inch by inch, and mind losing grasp over sanity.[^48]
		- **Depraved Dependents**: "They" can make released **Grazed** [Souls](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul") into temporary followers, up to 22 [Souls](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul"), but at the same time, they are affected by the **Degeneration** authority and will obey the **Dark Angel's** orders wholeheartedly as well as retain their full original abilities.
- **Darkness**: A **Dark Angel** is able to manifest an embodiment of the **Darkness** authority.
	- "They" can conjure a pitch-black, viscous "ocean" filled with an aura of **Degeneration**, swallowing enemy attacks and nullifying them. Alternatively, it can coat the enemy's surface with a layer of viscous black liquid to disrupt cognition and movement.
		- It can swallow the remaining silvery-white light of the **Silver Rapier** from a **[Silver Knight](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Twilight_Giant_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_3:_Silver_Knight)** and the **Flaring Sun** from an **[Unshadowed](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sun_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_4:_Unshadowed)**, reducing it to nothing.[^49]

**Strengthened Abilities** [^1]

- **Grazing**: "They" can **Graze** up to *22 [Souls](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul")*.

## Sequence 0: Hanged Man

**Description**

The **True God** presiding over the domain of **Degeneration** / **Depravity**, an amalgamation of **Black Shadows** and **Writhing Flesh**, possessing five heads and the corresponding **Shadows**. "He" holds partial authority over the domains of **Shadow**, **Darkness**, **Degeneration** / **Depravity**, **Filth**, and **Mutation**, while also embodying the concepts and partial **Symbolism** of **Sin-Bearing** and **Sacrifice**.[^1] [^50]

**Symbolism** [^1]

- **Sin-Bearing** (**Scapegoat**): "They" can take the damage and death intended for others, redirecting the enemy’s primary attacks toward "Themselves".
	- The [True Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/True_Creator "True Creator") bears the world's **Sins**, making "His" believers innocent in the eyes of the world. As a result, they cannot be found guilty or punished by **Beyonder** powers similar to an **[Overseer's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chaos_Mist_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_4:_Overseer)**.[^51]
- **Sacrifice** (**Hanged Man**): By **Sacrificing** "Themselves", "They" can secure absolute hope.

**Authorities** [^1]

- **Darkness**: Compared to the **[Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Darkness_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Darkness)**, the **Hanged Man’s** **Darkness** leans toward an all-consuming, devouring **Darkness** and a **Darkness** that corrupts the heart.
	- Those who walk into "Their" **Shadows** and **Darkness** without sufficient protection or status will quickly fall into **Depravity**, turning into **Corrupted** monsters.
		- Every year, many individuals within the [Forsaken Land of the Gods](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Forsaken_Land_of_the_Gods "Forsaken Land of the Gods") fall under the **Shadows** and **Darkness**, quickly turning into **Corrupted** monsters.[^50]
- **Mutations**: "They" hold authority over **Mutations** which is the authority that the **Flesh and** **Blood** authority is derived from.
	- "They" can create countless **Corrupted** monsters from nothing in the **Darkness** that possess the particularity of **Resurrection** —a qualitative change of **Flesh and Blood Immortality**.
		- The [Forsaken Land of the Gods](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Forsaken_Land_of_the_Gods "Forsaken Land of the Gods") is cursed with the influence of the [Ancient Sun God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ancient_Sun_God "Ancient Sun God"), spawning countless **Corrupted** monsters in the **Darkness**.[^50]
		- The **Hanged Man's** authority of **Mutation** is also capable of influencing the **Change** of all things to some extent, which partially overlaps with the authority of the **[Fool](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fool_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Fool)**.
- **Shadows** (**Lord of Shadows**): "They" have now become the absolute ruler of the **Shadow** **World**.
	- "Their" **Divine Kingdom** is that of the entire **Shadow World**, or something similar.
		- "Their" **Shadows** inherently possess some **Concealment** abilities.[^52] [^53]
- **Degeneration** / **Depravity** (**Degenerate Nature**): "They" are the **Degenerate Nature** of all living things.
	- "They" can make a **Shadow** rise and adhere to "Their" target's back, while other similar **Shadows** will continually emerge from the depths of the target's psyche, merging within and out and reshaping their form, being the inherent **Degenerate Nature** in all living things.[^44]
		- Apart from certain **High Sequence** **Beyonders** (such as an **[Angel of Redemption](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Eternal_Aeon_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_1:_Angel_of_Redemption)**), no one is able to separate from their own **Depraved Nature**. At most, they will only be able to suppress it's effects on them.
		- While the **[Chaos Primogenitor's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chaos_Primogenitor_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Chaos_Primogenitor) Degeneration** authority focuses on a comprehensive **Corruption** of body, mind, and **Spirit**, the **Hanged Man's** authority over **Degeneration** is self- **Corruption**.[^54]
		- Violating one's three outlooks, values, and morals is **Degeneration**. Violating the **Rules** established by the [Trunsoest Brass Book](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Trunsoest_Brass_Book "Trunsoest Brass Book") is also **Degeneration**.[^55]
		- If the **Hanged Man** embodies the **Degenerate Nature** of every living being, then the **[Abyss](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Abyss_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Abyss)** is the environment that inevitably leads all those within it to **Degenerate**, not only inducing **Degeneration** but also creating it.[^56]
- **Filth** (**Incarnation of Filth**): Being tainted by the **[Chaos Sea](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chaos_Sea)**, "They" can **Defile** all things.
	- When combined with the **Darkness** and **Degeneration**, "They" are capable of projecting traces of the **[Chaos Sea](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chaos_Sea)** 's influence.
		- "He" can plant a "seed" that melts into the **Spirit Body** of "Their" target. Part of the target's body would be merged with the "seed", making it extremely hard to **Purify**. It would influence the target mystically, but would also influence their **Mind** and **Fate**.
		- The [True Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/True_Creator "True Creator") planted a "seed" in Klein that forced him to meet [Sasrir](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sasrir "Sasrir") in the [Giant King's Court](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Giant_King%27s_Court "Giant King's Court"). This was partly due to mystic means but was also because of alterations in the mind as well as in **Fate**.
- **Grazing**: "They" have the authority of **Grazing**, derived from the **Omnipotence** symbolism.[^57]
- **Profane Language**: A manifestation of the authorities of **Degeneration** and **Filth**.
	- Those who heard "Their" **Depraved Language** would feel extreme pain as their bodies began to convulse and tremble uncontrollably, quickly cracking open to reveal flesh and blood. Inside, black worms and white moths squirmed, eventually forming all manner of indescribable eyes.[^58]
