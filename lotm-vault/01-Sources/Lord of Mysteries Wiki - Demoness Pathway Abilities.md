## Sequence 9: Assassin

**Traits**

- **Heightened Senses**: They possess sharp ears and eagle-like vision.[^7]
	- **Night Vision**: **Assassins** can easily see through darkness.[^8]

**New Abilities** [^9]

- **Shadow Concealment**: **Assassins** possess the ability to **Conceal** themselves within darkness and **Shadows**, allowing them to hide from their targets.[^10] [^11]
	- Sprinting at full speed weakens this ability's effectiveness.[^4]

## Sequence 8: Instigator

**Traits** [^12]

- **Physical Enhancement**: Building on the foundation of an **Assassin**, their bodies become stronger, but their combat abilities, other than speed, aren't significantly improved.

**New Abilities** [^9] [^12]

- **Instigation**: **Instigators** are adept at triggering the evil desires deep in people's hearts, causing them to commit violent crimes. This ability has both passive and active effects.[^13]
	- Even without speaking, using **Instigation** actively makes an **Instigator** appear reliable and approachable, causing people think better of them.
		- Along with improved thinking and analytical skills, the passive effect of **Instigation** allows them to sense others' emotions, desires, and even malice.
		- With **Instigation** and clever words, they can plant thoughts or desires in someone’s mind, prompting them to act as intended.
		- **Instigators** can **Instigate** people into starting fights, committing crimes, and escalating conflicts without being noticed.
				- **Instigators** can easily **Instigate** others into believing their lies and misdirection through their words and actions.

## Sequence 7: Witch

**Gender Transition**

- Advancing to a **Witch** results in becoming a female regardless of the original gender.

**New Abilities** [^9] [^14]

- **Charm**: A **Witch's** appearance and charm will be enhanced significantly.[^14]
	- The details of their face will become more exquisite and alluring.
		- The proportions of their body will approach perfection.
		- Their height will increase slightly.
- **Dark Magic**: They gain various forms of **Dark Magic** bestowed by the potion and while they can learn other **Dark Magic**, what they obtain is often sufficient and comprehensive enough.[^15] [^16] [^17]
	- They can conjure **Black Flames** from their body.
		- They can envelop weapons in **Black Flames**. Upon successfully hitting a target with such a weapon, they can detonate the flames coursing within the target's body from a distance.[^18]
				- They can compress a dense **Black Flame** in their palm, which they can throw upon fully forming, it would hit with the force of a cannonball while spreading flames.[^19]
				- These **Black Flames** differ from the more conventional flames like that of a **[Pyromaniac's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Priest_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_7:_Pyromaniac_\(Fire_Mage\))**, **Black Fire** ignites **Spirituality** rather than physical matter.
			- This can also be used against things like the effect of a [Painter's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sublunary_Eye_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_7:_Painter) **Painting**.[^20]
						- This allows **Spirit Bodies**, like **[Wraiths](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chained_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_5:_Wraith)**, to be targeted and incinerated.[^21]
						- This also prevents it from extinguishing underwater.[^22]
				- **Black Flames** are part of the **Chaos** authority.[^16]
		- They gain the blessing of **Frost** and can cast **Spells** related to it.
		- They can open locks by freezing the inside of the keyhole and forming the correct key.[^23]
				- They can freeze the floor which materializes ice, allowing them to control movement.
			- Encasing the floor in ice can be used to restrict enemy movement by making it slippery.[^18]
						- Small patches of **Frost** that sharply reduce friction allow them to run more quickly.[^22]
				- They can freeze a large area to gather ample materials for **Mirror Substitution**.[^24]
				- They can encase a target in layers of **Frost** to create an icy **Seal**.[^19]
				- They can cause a cold, frosty wind to blow around them.[^25]
				- **Frost** is part of the authority of **Catastrophe**.[^16]
- **Mirror Magic**: **Witches** are able to manipulate **Mirrors**, using them in many different ways.
	- **Mirror Substitution**: They have the ability to utilize **Mirrors** as a medium to create **Substitutes** that can take damage in their place.
		- **Witches** are also able to make **Mirror** **Substitutes** for others, provided that they meet these two conditions:[^26]
			1. They have a medium to establish such a connection (blood, hair, etc.).
						2. Their target is within no less than *30 meters* from them.
				- This ability utilizes their **Mirror Self**, meaning each use results in its death, though it will revive by splitting from the real-world counterpart.[^27] [^28]
		- They possess the ability to hide within **Mirrors**, though they are not able to travel between them and are only able to remain inside for a few seconds.[^22]
		- They have a unique method of detecting **Mirrors**.[^22]
		- They can **Curse** targets through blood or other media like a doll made from flesh or hair.
		- Once the connection has been established they can use **Black Flames** to burn the medium, incinerating their target from within.
				- Targets can shield themselves by mystically cutting off their connection to the medium or through **Substitution** abilities.[^25]
				- They can use certain **Ritualistic Magic** to **Curse** their targets, causing them to suffer an early **Catastrophic** death.[^29]
		- They can use other **Dark Magic** spells including but not limited to:
		- They can use their hair to transfer a mystical connection onto a **Mirror** by sticking their hair to the **Mirror's** surface and burning it with **Black Flames**.
				- They can create a communication medium using their hair, **Ice**, and **Black Flame**.
			- This form of **Dark Magic** is related to **Mirror Substitution**.[^20]
				- **Staff Substitution**: Similar to **Mirror Substitution**, **Witches** are able to use staves as a medium for substitutes in order to counteract fatal harm.
			- A **Witch** may also use their staff to better use **Dark Magic**.[^30]
				- **Bloodline Curse**: Through a medium, they can curse any target within the bloodline the medium represents using this special type of **Curse**.[^15]
				- By using black powder and an incantation, a **Witch** can surround their target with ghostly figures that press against their body.[^31]
				- **Invisibility**: By scattering shimmering powder and reciting an incantation, a **Witch** can turn **Invisible** to the naked eye.[^32]
		- **Dark Magic** other than **Black Flames**, **Frost**, **Mirror**, and **Curses** requires certain materials and incantations, making it less practical to use in a fight compared to the others.[^31]
- **Divination**: Upon reaching this [Sequence](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sequence "Sequence"), a **Witch** will possess considerable **Divination** capabilities, naturally mastering the use of **Magic Mirror** and **Staff Divination**.[^33] [^34]
	- **Anti-Divination**: They also are able to interfere with others' **Divination**.
		- Learning other forms of [Divination Arts](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Divination_Art "Divination Art") is also easy for a **Witch**.[^35]

**Traits**

- Their capabilities as an **Assassin** and **Instigator** have also been greatly improved.[^36]
	- **Physical Enhancement**: They have strong throat control. Hence, they are not easily choked.[^37]
		- **Heightened Senses**: Their vision has been enhanced, allowing them to see their own **Threads**.

**New Abilities** [^36]

- **Threads**: **Demonesses of Pleasure** can make thin, almost invisible **Threads** like spider silk, and use them to control and restrict the enemy's movements. Their hair is the source of their spider silk.[^38]
	- A trail of broken **Threads** can give a **Pleasure** information about the enemy's size and movements, and can be delicate enough to go undetected.
		- Their **Threads** can form a cocoon that helps the user recover from damage while also guaranteeing them some protection against attacks.
		- Their **Threads** can envelop a target in true **Pleasure** upon contact, causing them to spasm and lose control over their body.
		- Their **Threads** can be stuck or tied to walls, tangling enemies who walk through them and slowing them down.

**Strengthened Abilities** [^36] [^39] [^37] [^40] [^41] [^42] [^43]

- Their abilities as a **Witch** have been enhanced.
	- **Charm**: They will become more beautiful, making them better at seduction and providing unforgettable pleasure to the same or opposite gender during sex.
		- A **Demoness**, especially one who was in the midst of or having passed the **Pleasure** **Sequence**, had a **Charm** that far exceeded its normal effects when shown to males, making their every action be filled with a **Charm** that makes men who came close to them feel intoxicated and lost.**[^36]**
			- In a sense, a **Demoness of Pleasure's** is like consuming opioids and gradually developing into something more serious, to the point of not being able to extricate oneself from the pleasure given by them. Such a **Charm** can even affect gay men.[^39] [^37]
						- Although they dont possess an innately proactive **Charm**, they could maximize their allure to irresistible levels. Even under normal circumstances, their magnetism could make most men unable to shift their gazes away, their breathing growing labored as their minds raced with desire.[^44]
						- To a **Demoness of Pleasure,** Beyonders who habitually indulge in their desires are natural prey.[^45]
		- **Dark Magic**: Their **Dark Magic** capabilities as a **Witch** have been greatly improved.
		- Their **Frost** and **Black Fire** capabilities have received a qualitative enhancement.
			- They can ignite a quiet **Black** **Flame** inside them that can dispel certain effects.[^46] [^43]
				- This can let a **Demoness of Pleasure** expel any ghosts that inhabit them, dispel **Curses** and negative influences, etc.
						- They can trap **Spirit Bodies** in **Frost** -encased **Mirrors** surrounded with **Black Flames**.[^41]
						- They can make 7 **Black Flames** around them to launch like bullets at their targets.[^47]
						- They can encase themselves in **Ice** to defend themselves from various attacks.[^40] [^43]
						- **Ice Spear Condensation**: They are able to conjure up and throw **Frost Spears** at their targets. If the spear makes direct contact with the target's skin, it induces a freezing effect. [^47]
						- Their **Black Flames** can also burn the spirituality of supernatural constructs, weakening their effects. For example, they can reduce the restraining effects of a Demoness’s **Threads** by burning their spirituality.[^48]
				- A **Demoness of Pleasure** can **Curse** a target by having a mirror reflect their full figure.[^36] [^40]
		- **Mirror Magic**: The speed at which they can substitute themselves via **Mirrors** and **Staves** has increased.
		- They will also gain some measure of recuperation after substituting themselves.[^36] [^40]

## Sequence 5: Affliction (Demoness of Affliction, Lady of Diseases)

**Traits**

- **Physical Enhancement**: A **Demoness of Affliction** can freely manipulate their hair, turning it into an effective close-combat weapon and a precise tool.
	- Their hair can pierce skin, inject **Black Flames**, and cause stiffness to the contacted area.
		- To a **Demoness**, scars aren’t a thing.[^49]
		- Their hair can be used defensively to intercept and alter the trajectory of incoming attacks.
		- [Tracy](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tracy "Tracy") used her hair to deflect [Klein](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") ’s **Air Bullets**, changing their trajectory before they could hit her.[^50]

**New Abilities** [^7] [^2] [^51] [^52]

- **Disease**: A **Demoness of Affliction** can create multiple airborne pathogens that have their infection speed and the onset of diseases supernaturally accelerated.
	- The initial range is *30 meters*, going up to *50 meters* upon full digestion of the potion.
		- They can spread **Diseases** at a larger range if they utilize the wind correctly.
		- The infection rate and onset of **Disease** vary based on the constitution of their targets.
		- Once infected, the initial *10 seconds* are the incubation period, followed by mild symptoms such as allergies, fever, cough, and sneezing.
				- After *20-30 seconds*, the target will begin to experience symptoms of more dangerous illnesses like pneumonia and bronchitis.
				- After *2-3 minutes*, the disease will become critical as the target begins to experience cardiac arrest or a brain aneurysm.
		- **Flames** created by a **[Pyromaniac](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Priest_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_7:_Pyromaniac_\(Fire_Mage\))** (and other similar abilities) are able to kill most of the pathogens created by a **Demoness of Affliction**, reducing the pathogens' impact on them.
		- Undead creatures won't be infected by their **Diseases**.

**Strengthened Abilities** [^2]

- **Threads**: A **Demoness of Affliction's** **Threads** have been significantly enhanced.
	- Now their **Threads** are like extensions of a **Demoness's** arms, capable of more precise and detailed actions such as wrapping around small objects and moving them.
		- Their enhanced **Threads** can precisely stimulate different parts of the target’s body, causing intense physical reactions and disrupting their ability to fight effectively.[^50]
		- Binded layer by layer on his body by invisible **Threads**, [Nikila](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Nikila "Nikila") felt the invisible **Threads** lightly caressing him, from his ears and face to his chest and inner thighs, teasing and scratching with seductive touches. This instantly sent his blood surging, his thoughts sinking deep to his nether regions.
- **Charm**: A **Demoness of Affliction** can now use their **Charm** as an active ability. A simple glance, gesture, or word from a **Demoness of Affliction** can now completely **Charm** a person.
	- When combined with showcasing their appearance, figure, and aura, an active **Charm** becomes more effective. This will most likely lead to 1 of 2 scenarios:
		1. **Charmed** enemies will hesitate to harm them and be unable to use their full strength.
				2. Some **Charmed** enemies might even give up resisting just to gain favor from them.
		- Their own appearance, aura, figure, and passive charm have all been further enhanced.
		- Repeated daily interactions and use will make a target truly fall in love with them.
- **Dark Magic**: Its capabilities have also been enhanced.
	- They can now further **Curse** their targets by reflecting a part of the target's body in a mirror and burning that mirrored region with **Black Fire**.
		- They can release a **Blue Illusory Halo** from their body, which spreads **Frost** in its wake and rapidly freezes the surrounding area, creating a frozen world covered in thick **Ice** and **Frost**.[^50]
		- Their **Invisibility** is powerful enough to temporarily evade detection from enhanced senses, such as a [Clown](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fool_Pathway/Abilities "Fool Pathway/Abilities") ’s **Danger Intuition** and spiritual perception, making it difficult for even a [Sequence 6](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sequence "Sequence") **Faceless** to locate them within a short period of time.[^50]
		- However, strong emotional fluctuations such as rage or hatred can still reveal their location through spiritual perception.
- **Mirror Magic**: They can now use the [Mirror World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mirror_World "Mirror World") to create scene illusions with pre-set **Mirrors** and **Mirror Mazes** to ensure that their enemies don't escape.
	- Their illusions made from **Mirrors** can also deceive various recording objects.[^31]

## Sequence 4: Despair (Demoness of Despair)

**Strengthened Abilities** [^38] [^2] [^53]

- A **Demoness of Despair's** previous **Beyonder** abilities, whether from a **Witch**, **Demoness of Pleasure**, or **Demoness of Affliction**, have all been greatly enhanced.
	- **Plague**: This is the enhanced form of **Disease**; compared to a **Demoness of Affliction**, a **Demoness of Despair** can now spread pathogens and other airborne substances with vastly increased scope, distance, and potency.
		- The following are examples of what they can produce:
			- **Spirituality and Strength Sapping Plague**: They can form a **Plague** that saps the target's strength and **Spirituality**.
				- This **Plague** gradually weakens the target until even their heart can barely beat, while simultaneously draining their **Spirituality**. As a result, it can infect even the dead.
						- **Rigidity Plague**: A **Demoness of Despair** can create a **Plague** that causes the body to become rigid and inflexible.
				- Over time, this **Plague** could even render extremely durable beings, such as **Undead**, completely inflexible.
						- **Flesh Deterioration Plague**: A **Demoness of Despair** can also create a **Plague** that rapidly deteriorates flesh.
				- This **Plague** is an enhanced form of their original **Disease**, primarily infecting organs like the heart, brain, and lungs, with symptoms appearing within just *10 seconds*.
				- **Demonesses of Despair** can also develop their own unique strains of **Plague** and **Disease**.
			- This is done by combining their own unique traits, relevant mystical knowledge, special materials, concepts, and experimental methods. However, this takes a considerable amount of time and can’t yield results quickly.[^38]
				- **Plagues** can spread through fog and smog, covering large areas and causing epidemics.
				- Their **Plagues** can only target beings with **Spirituality**, not inanimate objects.
				- They are able to remove **Plagues** using **Black Fire** and **Frost Magic**.
				- Their **Plagues** can now cover a range of up to *3 kilometres*.
				- Only one of these **Plagues** can be maintained at a time.
				- Their **Diseases** can infect Undead creatures now.[^2]
		- **Threads**: The power of their **Threads**, now within their hair, has been markedly enhanced.
		- Their **Threads** can restrain, bind, interfere, transmit **Black Flames** and **Curses**, possess hardness, and now convey the effects of **Pleasure**.
				- **Petrification**: If an enemy's body is touched by a **Demoness of Despair's** hair, that area will begin to **Petrify**.[^38]
		- **Charm**: Their beauty has been greatly enhanced both physically and spiritually.
		- Their **Charm** can instantly make **Mid Sequence Beyonders** fall in love, and the opioid-like addiction effect from their **Demoness of Pleasure Sequence** now manifests within a short time. Even glancing at them can trigger opioid-like addiction and infatuation.
		- **Dark Magic**: They possess great mastery and skill in using **Frost**, **Curses** and **Black Flames**.
		- Their ways of using **Frost** abilities have magnified.
			- They can forge **Ice** [amulets](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ice_Amulet "Ice Amulet") that allow the wearer to traverse the [Mirror World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mirror_World "Mirror World").
						- **Ice Spear Forest**: Their **Ice Spear Condensation** has evolved into **Ice Spear Forest**.[^54]
				- They can create a forest of crystal-clear but sharp **Frost Spears** that can be suddenly condensed, like a beautiful and clean hedgehog showing its spines.
				- Their **Curses** have also been slightly enhanced.
			- If their enemies fail to **Substitute** themselves before they are locked onto and the **Curse** is completed, they cannot transfer or share the damage, even with a **Mirror Substitution** or **Paper Figurine Substitute**.
				- They can cause **Black Flames** to suddenly surge from their body, spreading ailments in a bid to ignite everything around them and causing a large-scale fire.[^55]
		- **Mirror Magic** has been enhanced.
		- **Mirror Projection**: **Demonesses of Despair** can now project themselves through **Mirrors** within a *city-wide* range, creating multiple different illusions of themselves.
				- **Demonesses of Despair** are experts at locating **Mirrors**, traversing the [Mirror World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mirror_World "Mirror World"), and transmitting information via **Mirrors**, albeit with some risk of interception.
			- Their unique method of detecting **Mirrors** has been greatly enhanced. They can now locate **Mirrors** within a *5 kilometer radius* or locate **Mirrors** they have visited before.
						- When [Mirror World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mirror_World "Mirror World") creatures attempt to intercept their messages, they can imbue the messages with **Curses**, reducing the likelihood of interference.
				- **Demonesses of Despair** can now create **Mirror Mazes** to trap people in the [Mirror World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mirror_World "Mirror World") and use **Magic Mirror** **Divination** with greater precision.

**Mythical Creature Form**

- As **Sequence 4** **Demigods** who gained partial **Godhood**, they can manifest their incomplete **Mythical Creature** **Form** if they disregard any of the consequences and become an incomplete **Gorgon**.
	- Their [Mythical Creature Form](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mythical_Creature_Form "Mythical Creature Form") takes the form of a female humanoid with a great mass of hair that appears to resemble that of various venomous snakes.[^53] [^1]
		- Anyone who sees their form experiences both **Pleasure** and pain, making it difficult to mount an effective counterattack.
		- A **Demoness of Despair** will be able to sustain a **Divine Descent** for a few seconds, but their **Soul** and body would collapse.[^57]

## Sequence 3: Unaging (Demoness of Unaging)

**Description**

Every **Demoness of Unaging** is forever youthful, skilled in **Resurrection**, bizarrely difficult to kill, a darling of the [Mirror World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mirror_World "Mirror World"), and a true **Gorgon**.[^7]

**New Abilities** [^7] [^28] [^58]

- **Mirror Self**: Upon advancing, the consciousnesses of their **Mirror Person** and real-world counterpart will fuse together, allowing them to exist both outside and inside the mirror.
	- The simplest application of their **Mirror Self** is to fix their state within one of their countless **Mirror** **Persons**, allowing them to maintain their youth forever.
		- Because of this, a **Demoness of Unaging’s** lifespan exceeds that of other **Saints** and even **Angels** from many pathways, allowing them to live for *at least 2,000 years*.[^59]
				- This can also be used on other individuals, temporarily fixing their age to match that of a chosen **Mirror Trace**, however it can only last for *no more than 30 minutes*, since the mystical connection is only established with an illusory trace in the mirror—not real nor stable.[^60]
				- This doesn't refer only to facial **Agelessness** but also encompasses one's entire state—physical, spiritual, and all other aspects.[^61]
			- This will maintain their peak state until the end of their long lifespan.
		- When their body dies, a **Mirror Person** can split off from it again and revive them. With the characteristics of a **Mirror Person**, they can now revive through that **Mirror Person**.
		- A **Demoness of Unaging** can use environments with multiple **Mirrors** to temporarily multiply their **Mirror Persons**. As long as one survives, they cannot truly die.
				- The death of either the **Demoness of Unaging's** body or the **Mirror Person** alone is no longer true death for them—it is more like a partial injury that can be healed.
				- **Concealed Resurrection**: They can use their own blood, hair, skin, or other media to create a **Mirror Person** sleeping within a **Mirror**, hiding that mirror in a secret place under **Anti-Divination** conditions, and cutting off corresponding mystical connections to prevent curses and other harm from affecting the sleeping **Mirror Person**.
			- Initially, they can only separate one **Mirror Person**. With full potion digestion, the number of **Concealed** **Mirror Persons** that can be created rises to two.
						- The revival process through a **Dormant Mirror** would take considerable time—dozens of minutes or even hours—preventing further participation in a given battle.[^62] [^63]
						- Through this, they can survive attacks or situations that would cause most **Sequence 3 Saints** from other [Pathways](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways") to die irreversibly.
				- All living beings that entered the [Underworld](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Underworld "Underworld") would die on the spot, however, this type of death does not affect the isolated **Dormant Mirrors**.[^62]
								- Although a **Demoness of Unaging** and their normal **Mirror Person** would still die due to a **[Left Hand of Decay](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Second_Law_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_3:_Left_Hand_of_Decay) 's** left hand, they could revive through a **Dormant Mirror**.[^63]
				- Certain powers or situations can exploit the mystical connection between them and their **Mirror Self**, as well as their shared, connected psyche.[^62]
			- Losing control would affect both their **Mirror Self** and **Dormant Mirrors**.[^64]
						- Through the manipulation of **Principles** and mystical connections, a **[Mentor of Deceit](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_3:_Mentor_of_Deceit)** can ensure that their destruction extends to their **Mirror Selves** and **Dormant Mirrors** alike.[^65]
						- The **Transfiguration Curse** of a **[Disciple of Silence](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chained_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_3:_Disciple_of_Silence_\(Cursed_Artifact\))** is symmetrical, it targets both them and their **Mirror Self**. Moreover, as a defenseless animal, they would be captured, and all their hidden **Dormant Mirrors** could be uncovered through the connection between their body and their revival arrangements.[^62]
						- The seeds of a **Mental Plague** would continue to affect them post revival, which may cause them to suffer issues or even lead to their demise.[^65]
						- **Seeds of Malice** planted deep within their mind would also erode their **Mirror Self's** psyche, this allows a **[Bloody Archduke](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Abyss_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_2:_Bloody_Archduke)** to use these seeds to revive within the **Demoness of Unaging**, igniting their desires and burning their blood.[^62]
						- Deeply understanding the names and information of [Great Existences](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Existences "Great Existences") would severely corrupt oneself, this is something that **Mirror Projections** can't block, and even hidden, sleeping mirrors would be affected.[^66]
		- At the **Demoness of Unaging** stage, their **Mirror Selves** cannot directly engage when battling in the real world. However, if the battlefield is in the [Mirror World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mirror_World "Mirror World") and the main body enters it, **Demonesses** can attack the enemy together with their **Mirror Selves**, essentially creating a two-against-one situation.[^67]
		- Using **Mirror Substitution**, [Clarice](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Clarice "Clarice") 's body devolved into a mirror, split apart completely, and floated down as microscopic fragments. The [Demoness of Black](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Clarice "Clarice") 's **Mirror Self** walked out and was then intercepted by [Franca](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Franca_Roland "Franca Roland") 's **Mirror Self**.

**Strengthened Abilities** [^7] [^28]

- **Petrification**: A **Demoness of Unaging's** **Petrification** ability is no longer limited to their hair, but it still cannot directly **Petrify** targets from a distance.[^28]
	- They can slowly **Petrify** their surroundings, creating a plague-like area that **Petrifies** anyone and anything that touches the infested zone.
		- Alternatively, a **Demoness of Unaging** can use their spider silk as a medium for **Petrification**.
- **Charm**: Their **Charm** from the **Demoness of Pleasure** stage has been greatly strengthened.[^68]
	- They can perfectly present the **Charm** of every stage of womanhood, with the corresponding skin condition and temperament. People caught in their **Charm** find it difficult to resist.
		- When a **Demoness of Unaging's** **Charm** is used actively, it is capable of halting someone’s thoughts even if they possess considerable mental resistance and strength.
		- They can take advantage of an opponent’s emotional fluctuations to increase the effectiveness of their **Charm**, even against another **Demoness of Unaging**. This can be countered by having one’s **Mirror Person** use **Charm** on its original body, restoring clarity and resisting the opposing **Charm**. [^67]
		- Over the course of daily interactions, a **Demoness of Unaging's** **Charm** can make [Angels](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Angels "Angels") fall in love with the Demoness without "Them" realizing it.
		- Their **Charm** is effective against newly promoted **Demoness of Unaging**, since the digestion of the **Unaging** potion usually took a century. Therefore, these newly advanced might still be in an emotionally and lustfully unstable state for several years—perhaps even a decade or more—making them vulnerable to the allure of **Demonesses** of the same tier.[^64]
- **Dark Magic**: Their abilities related to **Mirrors** and **Curses** have been greatly strengthened.
	- Their **Curse** -related capabilities have been enhanced in scope and power.

		- For children born from the union of the [True Gods](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/True_Gods "True Gods") of the [Demoness](#Sequence_0:_Demoness_\(Chaos_Demoness,_Primordial_Demoness\)) and [Red Priest](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Priest_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Red_Priest), it's normal for twins to be a boy and a girl. Any other outcome would be considered unusual.[^101]

**Mythical Creature Form**

- "She" appears enormous, clad in a white dress made of bone. "Her" visage grows increasingly beautiful, yet from different angles exudes distinct temperaments: pure, elegant, majestic, delicate, pristine, and decadent.[^90]
	- "Her" black hair, as thick as pythons, exudes an eerie slickness. Some strands rise and rotate, their black- and-white eyes spinning; others extend forward, sprouting serpent-like heads that flick their tongues. Others coil at "Her" feet, forming a vortex of snakes. Whether one gazed directly at the **Primordial Demoness** or not, everything would react instinctively to "Her" presence.[^90]
		- Anything directly touched by these tentacles is transformed into beautiful women of varying sizes, who then run rampant and destroy all matter.[^90]
