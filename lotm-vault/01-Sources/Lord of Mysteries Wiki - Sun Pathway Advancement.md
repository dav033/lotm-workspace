## Summary

Per-Sequence potion data for the Sun Pathway. Several Sequences (Priest of Light, Unshadowed, Justice Mentor, Lightseeker, White Angel) include advancement rituals and, for the higher-danger Sequences, detailed narrative consumption-backlash excerpts.

## Extracted facts

- Sequence 9 Bard: churning crimson potion; Characteristic is a singing crimson crystal.
- Sequence 7 Solar High Priest: golden, scorching potion; Characteristic is a golden sphere with faint red streaks. Consumption backlash brings the drinker to the brink of losing control (eyes brightening, skin patterning, hair lengthening toward feather-like growth) before returning to normal with lasting resistance to ailments and harsh environments.
- Sequence 5 Priest of Light: hot golden, light-emitting potion; Characteristic is a glowing, translucent crystal. Advancement ritual: bury the entire body in ice that normally doesn't melt, in pure darkness, before drinking.
- Sequence 4 Unshadowed: golden, hot, violent, impurity-free potion; Characteristic is a drop of the Sun's own divine golden blood. Advancement ritual: extract one's strongest, most treasured emotions before drinking, then have them injected back during the ritual; consumption backlash purifies the drinker into a purer, holy-light form as self-awareness and emotion are stripped, reversed only when the extracted emotions are guided back to form a stable foundation for the mind.
- Sequence 3 Justice Mentor: dark golden, intertwined-light potion; Characteristic is a heart or brain condensed from pure radiance. Advancement ritual: clearly define one's own justice and uphold it without violation for three years.
- Sequence 2 Lightseeker: potion of converging pure light; Characteristic is a core of trapped light within a cluster of darkness. Advancement ritual: bind one's most important, core-justice-linked memory to an Angel-rank item with a deep mystical connection to the Sun, and place it before oneself before drinking; the item serves as an anchor through a melting, near-death consumption backlash that only resolves once the drinker's spirit, soul, and body reunite at a symbolic "door."
- Sequence 1 White Angel: pure white, scorching potion; Characteristic is a pure white human silhouette or holy rib. Advancement ritual: receive sincere, exclusive praise/worship from 1,000,000 humans including oneself (higher status lowers the required count, but self-inclusion and retained sense of self are mandatory).
- Sequence 0 Sun: potion resembles an erupting star. Main ingredients: three White Angel Beyonder Characteristics. Advancement ritual: consume the potion either in the darkness that is the destination of all things, or in the all-encompassing color of the Chaos Sea; the former is extremely dangerous and can cause immediate death even to an ally releasing the power.

## Short quotations

- "Clearly define what your justice is and uphold it for three years without the slightest violation or doing anything in conflict with that justice." (Justice Mentor advancement ritual)
