## Symbols, Authorities & Abilities

TRANSLATION NOTE

In the original Chinese text, 混乱 (hǔnluàn), the term used for " **Disorder** ", is sometimes translated as " **Chaos** ", as is done in the English WebNovel translation, this conflicts with a similar term, 混沌 (hùndùn). Since these terms are both distinct authorities, this page will consistently use the same English words to refer to the same Chinese words throughout for clarity and distinction.
- **Disorder** (混乱 *hǔnluàn*)
	- Refers to a state of confusion, disorder, or lack of order. It implies the breakdown of established systems, rules, or expectations.
- **Chaos** (混沌 *hùndùn*)
	- Refers to primordial chaos—the formless and undifferentiated state that precedes or underlies creation. Usually used in creation myths.

"They" possess full **Authority** over the [Wheel of Fortune Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Wheel_of_Fortune_Pathway) and the corresponding **Symbols**, which include but are not limited to the following:

- [Wheel of Fortune](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Wheel_of_Fortune_Pathway): Authorities over **Fate**, **Probability**, **Cycle**, and **Disorder**.

The **Symbols**, **Authorities**, and **Abilities** "They" possess and display at the [Above the Sequence](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Above_the_Sequence "Above the Sequence") level include:

- **Fate of the Original Creator**: As a [Great Old One](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Old_One "Great Old One") that comes directly from the [Original Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Original_Creator "Original Creator"), "They" not only symbolize **Fate** itself, but also have hidden symbolism like " **symbolizing the Fate of the Original Creator**.[^3]
- **Endless Disorder:** The essence of **Fate** is **Disorder**, which embodies **Madness**.[^4]
- **Fate**: The **Key of Light** is the **Incarnation of Fate**.
	- **Light Cone**: Within the **[Light Cone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Light_Cone "wikipedia:Light Cone")** is **Fate**.[^1] This is why "They" are called the **Key of Light**,[^5] and why the [Divine Kingdom](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Divine_Kingdom "Divine Kingdom") of the [Sequence 0: Wheel of Fortune](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Wheel_of_Fortune_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Wheel_of_Fortune) is called the **Light Cone World**.[^4]
		- As the **Incarnation of Fate**, if the **Key of Light** were to accommodate two other **Fate** related [Sefirot](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirot "Sefirot"), or simply only [Sefirah Castle](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirah_Castle), "They" would become the **Controller/Wielder of Fate**, and then be unable to control the instinct, moving closer to the [Oldest One](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Oldest_One "Oldest One").[^6]
		- Examples of "Their" powers include:
		- "They" can conjure a massive tricolor wheel of mercury, lapis lazuli, and deep black, inscribed with complex patterns and symbols that can draw forth a **River of Fate** belonging to a certain entity which "They" can manipulate the **Fate** of by controlling the direction of its flow.[^7]
			- The [Golden Buddha](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Golden_Buddha "Golden Buddha") manipulated the [Inextinguishable Ravings's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Inextinguishable_Ravings "Inextinguishable Ravings") [River of Fate](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/River_of_Fate "River of Fate"), causing "Him" to not be able to escape from the [Tenebrous World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tenebrous_World "Tenebrous World") as if destiny itself entangling "Him" there, forcing "Him" to endure the [Pitch-Black Fiend](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pitch-Black_Fiend "Pitch-Black Fiend") 's **Curse**.
				- As long as the **Key of Light** existed and retained a measure of consciousness without succumbing to madness, "They" would naturally bring **Good Fortune** to "Themselves" and "Their" companions,[^8] and would inevitably have absolute **Good Fortune** at critical moments.[^9]
			- When Earth's four [Quasi Great Old Ones](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Quasi_Great_Old_Ones "Quasi Great Old Ones") were under the influences of the [Inextinguishable Ravings](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Inextinguishable_Ravings "Inextinguishable Ravings"), through **Good Fortune**, the [Celestial Master](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Celestial_Master "Celestial Master") and the [Celestial Thearch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Celestial_Thearch "Celestial Thearch") began to unleash "Their" powers wildly and irrationally upon the outside world.
				- This prevented the [High-Dimensional Overseer](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/High-Dimensional_Overseer "High-Dimensional Overseer") and [Inextinguishable Ravings](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Inextinguishable_Ravings "Inextinguishable Ravings") from seizing the opportunity or achieving "Their" objectives.[^8]
						- When the [Golden Buddha](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Golden_Buddha "Golden Buddha") was in the indiscriminate explosion of **Desires** triggered by the [Pitch-Black Fiend](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pitch-Black_Fiend "Pitch-Black Fiend"), **Fortune** was on "His" side as the **Desire** occupying "His" thoughts was primarily one of "universal salvation and relief from suffering".
				- As a result, the [Golden Buddha](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Golden_Buddha "Golden Buddha") had only froze momentarily before becoming even more fervent in "His" participation in the battle.[^7]
						- Despite having been schemed against by the [Goddess of Fate](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Goddess_of_Fate "Goddess of Fate") which forced "Him" to fall into a slumber to resolve, the [Golden Buddha](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Golden_Buddha "Golden Buddha") 's **Good Fortune** had unwittingly drew the [Circle of Inevitability's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Circle_of_Inevitability "Circle of Inevitability") attention.
				- This granted **Calamity of Destruction** [Lumian Lee](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee") the opportunity to have a one-on-one confrontation with the [Monarch of Decay](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Monarch_of_Decay "Monarch of Decay").[^9]
						- As the **Incarnation of Fate** and due to "Their" **Good Fortune**, even the [Circle of Inevitability](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Circle_of_Inevitability "Circle of Inevitability") cannot fix the tributary of **Fate** representing "Their" successful accommodation of the **Key of Light** - it was simply unfixable.[^9]
- **Return**: The Symbolism of **Return**, meaning **Returning** to the mother body, **Returning** to the primordial source, **Returning** to the oldest origin. This is the instinct of the universe.[^4]
	- In an extreme condition, with sufficient preparation time, and without interference of other [Great Old Dominators](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Old_Dominators "Great Old Dominators"), the **Key of Light** can use this symbolism to reset the state of the entire *universe*.[^10] [^11]
		- **Cycle**: The **Cycle** authority of the [Wheel of Fortune](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Wheel_of_Fortune_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Wheel_of_Fortune) was derived from the **Return** symbolism.
		- The [Golden Buddha](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Golden_Buddha "Golden Buddha") prepared to **Rebooted** the process of the [Celestial Master](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Celestial_Master "Celestial Master") and [Celestial Thearch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Celestial_Thearch "Celestial Thearch") being **Sealed** into a **Painting** by the [High-Dimensional Overseer](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/High-Dimensional_Overseer "High-Dimensional Overseer"), returning "Them" to the reality.[^12]
