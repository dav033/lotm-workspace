## Sequence 1: Hand of Order

### Potion Properties

> A pure, transparent and unblemished brass colored liquid.^{[1]}

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> A brass 'fist' with clearly defined knuckles that clenches tightly.^{[1]}

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

  * A Hand of Order Beyonder Characteristic

Supplementary Ingredients

  * 1 code of law that has remained unchanged for over 300 years^{[1]}
  * 9,000 portions of blood from lawbreakers (each portion must be at least 5 ml; the higher the level of the lawbreaker, the fewer portions are required)^{[1]}
  * 9 abolished laws that violated normal circumstances^{[1]}
  * 100 grams of contaminants caused by a fallen True God^{[1]}

### Advancement Ritual

Equivalent Rituals:^{[1]}

  * Establish a nation with a population of at least 10 million, making one's will the order and a strict system of rules within that nation.^{[1]} or
  * Control a nation with a population of at least 50 million, making one's will the order and a strict system of rules within that nation.^{[1]}
    * In both rituals, the more Beyonders and the higher their level among the citizens, the fewer people are required, but the minimum population cannot be less than 1 million.^{[1]}

## Sequence 0: Justiciar

### Potion Properties

> A spherical, illusory world supported by massive brass pillars.^{[1]}

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

  * The Justiciar Uniqueness.
  * Three Hand of Order Beyonder Characteristics.

Supplementary Ingredients

  * An alternate world whose original rules have been completely replaced by one's own order.^{[1]}

### Advancement Ritual

Hold a sufficiently just trial concerning a serious matter involving a True God, in accordance with the rules one has previously established and ensure that the judgment is truly carried out, then take the potion.^{[1]}
