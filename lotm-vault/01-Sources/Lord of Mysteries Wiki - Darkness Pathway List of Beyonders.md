Spoiler Warning

The list provided below might reveal the latest developments and progression of the characters by the conclusion of the series. Do not read the following section if you wish to avoid spoilers.

NOTE

- This page lists the rank of the character in this Pathway, not their overall rank. For example, a [Great Old One](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Old_One "Great Old One") accommodating a Sequence 1 characteristic of another Pathway will be listed as a Sequence 1 of that Pathway.
- For [Ancient Gods](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ancient_Gods "Ancient Gods"), this chart includes "Them" in the **Ancient Gods** section for the Pathways that have the [Uniquenesses](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniquenesses "Uniquenesses"), and the **Angels** section for the Pathways that only have Sequence 1 or Sequence 2 characteristics.
- For [Multi-Pathway True Gods](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Multi-Pathway_True_Gods "Multi-Pathway True Gods"), if the composition is not listed explicitly, it means Uniqueness + 3 Sequence 1 for that Pathway.

NOTATION

- ⴱ - Characters that have this symbol are no longer in that position because they have switched Pathways when advancing.
- `Vital Status: Former` - Characters that have previously held this position but have since lost the corresponding [Beyonder Characteristics](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristics "Beyonder Characteristics"), [Uniquenesses](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniquenesses "Uniquenesses"), [Sefirot](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirot "Sefirot"), and/or [Boon](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Boon "Boon").
