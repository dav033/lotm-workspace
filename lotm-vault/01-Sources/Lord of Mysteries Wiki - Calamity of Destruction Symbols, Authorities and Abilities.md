**Calamity of Destruction** (毁灭天灾, *Huǐmiè tiānzāi*, lit. 'Destruction Natural Disaster') is the title of the [Great Old One](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Old_One "Great Old One") of the [Demoness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Demoness_Pathway) and [Red Priest](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Priest_Pathway) [Pathways](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways").

The convergence of the **Calamity of Destruction** and the [Eternal Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Eternal_Darkness) will form the [Fourth Pillar](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Pillar "Fourth Pillar"). In addition, the [Monarch of Decay](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Monarch_of_Decay "Monarch of Decay") is also the leftover material of the [Fourth Pillar](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Pillar "Fourth Pillar").[^1] When the formation of the [Fourth Pillar](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Pillar "Fourth Pillar") starts, the [Monarch of Decay](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Monarch_of_Decay "Monarch of Decay") will be converged into and become a part of the [Fourth Pillar](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Pillar "Fourth Pillar").[^2]

- Because of this, [Pathways](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways") of the [Eternal Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Eternal_Darkness) and the **Calamity of Destruction** groups are exchangeable. But in that case, there's no way to become a [Great Old One](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Old_One "Great Old One"), unless one can separate the [Beyonder Characteristics](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristics "Beyonder Characteristics") from before.[^3]
- The [Monarch of Decay](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Monarch_of_Decay) can accommodate either the **Calamity of Destruction** or the [Eternal Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Eternal_Darkness), though "He" could only choose one. The vice versa also applies.[^4]

## Symbols, Authorities & Abilities

TRANSLATION NOTE

The original Chinese novel uses several words meaning "disasters", "calamities", "apocalypses", etc. to denote different nuances and symbols. In the English WebNovel translation, sometimes these Chinese terms are translated interchangeably to the same English words, leading to the loss of importance nuances and the difficulties in determining which symbol the original novel is referring to. As a result, this section will take the original Chinese novel as the source of truth instead of the WebNovel translation, and will consistently use the same English word to refer to the same Chinese word throughout.

| 灾难 (Zāinàn) | Catastrophe | - Demoness of Catastrophe (灾难 魔女, Zāinàn mónǚ) |

"They" possess full **Authority** over the [Demoness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Demoness_Pathway) and [Red Priest](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Priest_Pathway) [Pathways](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways") and the corresponding **Symbols**, which include but are not limited to the following:

- [Demoness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Demoness_Pathway): Authorities over **Chaos**, **Primordium**, **Charm**, **Mirror World**, **Dark Magic**, and **Femininity**.
- [Red Priest](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Priest_Pathway): Authorities over **War**, **Destruction**, **Conquest**, **Sacrifices**, **Charm**, and **Masculinity** with the symbol of **Lord of War**.

**Note**: A [Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways") having certain symbolism doesn't mean at the level of [Above the Sequence](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Above_the_Sequence "Above the Sequence") that it entirely stems from that [Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways"). It might stem from the entire group or certain parts from it.

		- If "They" have the **Chaotic Vortex Face** from [God Almighty](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/God_Almighty) to separate **Yin and Yang**, "They" can exist stably as a three-fold **Calamity of Destruction**.[^7]
		- The two [Uniquenesses](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniquenesses "Uniquenesses") that's closely linked to the [Mirrored Original Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mirrored_Original_Creator "Mirrored Original Creator") are counterbalanced by the **Chaotic Vortex Face** corresponding to the [God Almighty](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/God_Almighty "God Almighty").
			- Thus the trifecta of forces maintains a delicate equilibrium.[^11]
				- Due to **Yin and Yang** not fully merging, "Their" symbolism of **The Oldest One in The Mirror** is incomplete.[^12] "They" can use this incomplete symbolism to achieve various things.
			- Because this incomplete symbolism comes with the corresponding male and female consciousnesses of the **Mirrored Original Creator**, "They" would be barely affected by the [Inextinguishable Ravings](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Inextinguishable_Ravings "Inextinguishable Ravings").
				- This is because because the **Ravings** would also strengthen the masculine and feminine consciousnesses of the [Mirrored Original Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mirrored_Original_Creator "Mirrored Original Creator"), which would control the awakened spiritual imprint of the [Original Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Original_Creator "Original Creator") in "Them" through the **Ravings**. This allowed "Them" to retain "Their" ability to think and fight, although it could significantly shorten "Their" fragile balance.[^12]
						- "They" can amplify the **Original Creator's** spiritual imprint within others.
				- After the [Monarch of Decay](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Monarch_of_Decay "Monarch of Decay") was affected by the [Inextinguishable Ravings](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Inextinguishable_Ravings "Inextinguishable Ravings"), [Lumian Lee](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee") used the incomplete symbolism of the **Mirrored Original Creator** to amplify the [Original Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Original_Creator "Original Creator") 's spiritual imprint within "Him".[^12]

				- [Demoness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Demoness_Pathway): the **Arrival of the Apocalypse** and the **Return to Chaos**.
		- The [Fourth Pillar](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Pillar "Fourth Pillar") is formed by the convergence of the **Calamity of Destruction** and the [Eternal Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Eternal_Darkness).[^14]

- **Disasters**: "They" are the symbol of **Disaster**.[^21]
	- "Their" two [Pathways](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways") represent **Disasters** in every sense of the word, both **Calamities/Natural Disasters** and **Man-made Disasters**.[^22]
		- **Calamity of Destruction**: "They" can manifest physical **Calamities/Natural Disasters** across the [Cosmos](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Cosmos "Cosmos").
		- The **Calamity of Destruction** encompassed universal symbols, including supernova explosions and black holes.[^23] [^24] [^25]
				- Since the **Calamity of Destruction** represents a universal-level symbol, the scope of "Their" **Calamities** could be infinitely small or infinitely vast.[^23]
				- The **Calamity of Destruction** is a [Reality](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Reality "Reality") -oriented symbolism, thus it has a relatively large immediate range among the [Great Old Ones](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Old_Ones "Great Old Ones") ' symbols.[^26]
			- The immediate area of effect and the short term symbolic impacts are usually limited to a single [Star System](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Star_System "Star System") or the radiation zone of a black hole, with only minor, manageable disruptions spreading throughout the universe. The farther from the battlefield, the weaker the disturbance.
						- "They" could reduce or increase the frequency of catastrophes across the entire universe or create catastrophes in any known location, but "Their" sensing range does not exceed a large [Star System](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Star_System "Star System") unless pre-established mystical connections existed in those areas.
						- "They" can unleash disasters across the entire universe (e.g., filling the universe with **Black Holes** or similar phenomena); however, this has to be done gradually.[^27]
				- "Their" initial advancement to a [Great Old One](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Old_One "Great Old One") causes **Calamities** across various [Star Systems](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Star_System "Star System") in the [Cosmos](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Cosmos "Cosmos") to momentarily freeze before either intensifying or subsiding.[^28]
		- **Disaster of Origin**: "They" can trigger latent **Disasters** in other entities, including other [Great Old Ones](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Old_Ones "Great Old Ones").

				- After the [Monarch of Decay](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Monarch_of_Decay "Monarch of Decay") was affected by the [Inextinguishable Ravings](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Inextinguishable_Ravings "Inextinguishable Ravings") —which "He" would normally be able to resist— [Lumian Lee](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee") used the incomplete symbolism of the **Mirrored Original Creator** to amplify the [Original Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Original_Creator "Original Creator") 's spiritual imprint within the [Monarch of Decay](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Monarch_of_Decay "Monarch of Decay"), and simultaneously used the symbolism of the **Disaster of Origin** to make "Him" be swept up entirely by the spiritual storm and fall into chaos.[^12]

				- Unless "They" deliberately restrained "Themselves", "Their" corresponding **Disaster of Origin** symbolism would inevitably influence the surrounding area. This passive influence can affect a [Dual-Pathway True God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Dual-Pathway_True_God "Dual-Pathway True God").[^23]

His body collapsed in the destruction, nearing the apocalypse and returning to chaos.[^21]

				- [Lumian](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee") brought down the massive greatsword that bound madness and chaos, burning with black flames and symbolizing **Destruction** and **Chaos** onto the nonresistant [Grisha Adam](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Grisha_Adam "Grisha Adam"), causing "His" body to instantly disintegrate and collapse.[^30]
				- [Lumian](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee") struck the [Primordial Hunger](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Primordial_Hunger "Primordial Hunger") with the **Sword of Destruction**, causing "Him" to sustain severe injuries and retreat within *two seconds*, carrying the burning **Flames of Destruction** and a body riddled with cracks.[^31]
		- To bring **Disaster** upon oneself and embrace **Destruction** was the truest embodiment of the **Disaster of Origin**, the **Calamity of Destruction**.[^21]

## Mythical Creature Form

NOTE

This Mythical Creature Form is taken from [Lumian Lee](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee"), who is the only **Calamity of Destruction** in existence.

Three bodies connected to each other tightly and partially fused together. On the right was a purely female figure, clad in a sacred white robe, her hands milky and ethereal. Her head featured a female face at the front and a female face at the back, able to rotate freely in any direction, unrestrained by a neck. On the left was a half-male, half-female body, with a head composed of a female face, the chaotic vortex face, and a male face. It wielded [a charred banner](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniquenesses#Red_Priest_Pathway) in its hands. In the center was a purely male body, with a head consisting of a single male face adorned with long, blood-red hair and iron-black eyes. A prominent design of a [true dragon from the Western Continent](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Malevolent_Dragon "Malevolent Dragon") was etched between his brows.[^11]

## Advancement Condition

> Pure Yin does not last, Pure Yang does not grow, Yin contains Yang, Yang contains Yin, the union of Yin and Yang gives rise to Everything

The correct way to approach the **Calamity of Destruction** is to have both male and female souls. Once they have mastered the peak power of the two **Calamity** Pathways, these will become their male and female forms, respectively controlling the masculine and feminine sides of **Calamity**. This is the true union of Yin and Yang, and is the only correct way to approach the **Calamity of Destruction**. Examples of this state include:[^53]

- The evil spirit [Sauron-Einhorn-Medici](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sauron-Einhorn-Medici "Sauron-Einhorn-Medici"), with [Medici](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Medici "Medici") as the male self, and [Sauron](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sauron "Sauron") and [Einhorn](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Einhorn "Einhorn") (who were originally females) as the female self.
- [Lumian Lee](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee"), with [Lumian](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee") as the male self, and [Aurore Lee](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Aurore_Lee "Aurore Lee") as the female self. They are similar to [Sauron-Einhorn-Medici](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sauron-Einhorn-Medici "Sauron-Einhorn-Medici") 's state, but closer to the peak.
- The union between [Alista Tudor](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Alista_Tudor "Alista Tudor") and [Cheek](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Cheek "Cheek") —the two [Sequence 0](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sequence_0 "Sequence 0") of the two **Calamity** Pathways—in the [Fourth Epoch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Epoch "Fourth Epoch").

Once reaching the demigod stage, they need to keep switching between the [Demoness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Demoness_Pathway "Demoness Pathway") and [Red Priest](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Priest_Pathway "Red Priest Pathway") pathways, spiraling upwards. This doesn't need to be symmetrical, depending on the situation, aiming to fulfill the phrase 'Pure Yin does not last, Pure Yang does not grow, Yin contains Yang, Yang contains Yin, the union of Yin and Yang gives rise to everything.' [^53]

As "They" reach closer to the stage of accommodating the [City of Calamity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/City_of_Calamity), "They" need to maintain the balance of Yin and Yang. This means becoming a [Dual-Pathway True Gods](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Dual-Pathway_True_Gods "Dual-Pathway True Gods") of both the [Demoness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Demoness_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Demoness_\(Chaos_Demoness,_Primordial_Demoness\)) and [Red Priest](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Priest_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Red_Priest), with equal amount of [Beyonder Characteristics](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristics "Beyonder Characteristics") of the two Pathways for Sequence 2 and above.[^54] [^55] Even after accommodating the [Sefirah](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirah "Sefirah") and becoming a [Great Old One](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Old_One "Great Old One"), this principle should still be followed.[^56]

The above method was indeed correct, but only up until the final step because in this state, attempting to accommodate the [City of Calamity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/City_of_Calamity) would inevitably lead to disaster. The union of yin and yang signifies a return to the origin - the [Original Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Original_Creator "Original Creator") creating this world - heralding the rebirth of all things. What would return wouldn't be the complete [Original Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Original_Creator "Original Creator") but rather a **[Mirrored Original Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mirrored_Original_Creator "Mirrored Original Creator")**, stable enough to avoid immediately destroying and rebooting the universe.[^7]

> Everything was threefold, giving birth to all things. Only then could it be stable

In the [First Epoch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/First_Epoch "First Epoch"), the [Primordial God Almighty](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Primordial_God_Almighty "Primordial God Almighty") foresaw the danger of the [Mirrored Original Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mirrored_Original_Creator "Mirrored Original Creator") and intervened personally by accommodating the [City of Calamity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/City_of_Calamity "City of Calamity") and inserting "His" own consciousness into the [City of Calamity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/City_of_Calamity "City of Calamity") (manifested as the **Chaotic Head** of the [Malevolent Dragon](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Malevolent_Dragon "Malevolent Dragon") [^57]) to serve as a barrier to prevent Yin and Yang from fully merging, halting their integration. Before accommodating the [City of Calamity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/City_of_Calamity), the advancer also needs to have the manifestation of the [Primordial God Almighty](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Primordial_God_Almighty "Primordial God Almighty") on "Them" (e.g., in the form of a **Chaotic Vortex Face**) to separate the masculine and feminine aspects of the [Mirrored Original Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mirrored_Original_Creator "Mirrored Original Creator").[^7] When "They" accommodate the [City of Calamity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/City_of_Calamity), "Their" masculine and feminine selves will merge with the masculine and feminine selves of the [Malevolent Dragon](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Malevolent_Dragon "Malevolent Dragon") respectively, while "Their" **Chaotic Vortex Face** will merge with the [Malevolent Dragon](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Malevolent_Dragon "Malevolent Dragon") 's own **Chaotic Head**.[^58] After the accommodation, the two [Uniquenesses](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniquenesses "Uniquenesses") that's closely linked to the [Mirrored Original Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mirrored_Original_Creator "Mirrored Original Creator") will be counterbalanced by the **Chaotic Vortex Face** corresponding to the [Primordial God Almighty](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Primordial_God_Almighty "Primordial God Almighty"), thus the trifecta of forces maintains a delicate equilibrium, forming a framework that allows "Them" to exist in a stable manner.[^11]
