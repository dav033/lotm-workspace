This article contains **MAJOR SPOILERS** relating to some of the most pivotal parts of [Circle of Inevitability](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Circle_of_Inevitability_\(Novel\) "Circle of Inevitability (Novel)").

The **Haoli Sect Master** is the previous Sect Master of the [Haoli Lineage](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Haoli_Lineage "Haoli Lineage") before the [Evernight Goddess](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Evernight_Goddess "Evernight Goddess"). "He" is a powerful figure of the [Western Continent](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Western_Continent "Western Continent") who follows the [Dao System](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Daoism "Daoism"). "He" holds authority over the [Eternal Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Eternal_Darkness "Eternal Darkness") group pathways and is connected to their [Sefirah](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirah "Sefirah"), the [River of Eternal Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/River_of_Eternal_Darkness "River of Eternal Darkness").

## Appearance

"He" was draped in a dark robe adorned with strange patterns. "His" face was ashen and bloodless, with eyes so black they seemed to swallow all light.[^1]

## History

During the early stage of [The Apocalypse](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/The_Apocalypse "The Apocalypse"), the [Celestial Master](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Celestial_Master "Celestial Master"), the **Haoli Sect Master**, the [Holy Monk](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Holy_Monk "Holy Monk"), the [Master of the Shadow Cottage](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Master_of_the_Shadow_Cottage "Master of the Shadow Cottage"), and the [Celestial Thearch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Celestial_Thearch "Celestial Thearch") joined forces to aid [Lumian Lee](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee") fighting against the [Malevolent Dragon](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Malevolent_Dragon "Malevolent Dragon") in the [City of Calamity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/City_of_Calamity "City of Calamity"). Since beings of "His" level could not directly intervene, "They" used pre-existing seals to restrict and weaken the [Malevolent Dragon](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Malevolent_Dragon "Malevolent Dragon").[^1]

During [Lumian](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee") 's battle with the [Malevolent Dragon](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Malevolent_Dragon "Malevolent Dragon"), as a high-ranking figure of the [River of Eternal Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/River_of_Eternal_Darkness), the **Haoli Sect Master** used "His" control over the demonic engravings on the chains of the [Malevolent Dragon](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Malevolent_Dragon "Malevolent Dragon") —past Daoists who used their lives to restrained the [Malevolent Dragon](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Malevolent_Dragon "Malevolent Dragon") —to allow them to resist the dragon's power.[^2] Later in the battle, slightly relaxed "His" control and suppression over the demons, allowing them to partially regain their autonomy and join Lumian's army, making them his teammates and soldiers to resist the [Malevolent Dragon](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Malevolent_Dragon "Malevolent Dragon") 's **Conquest** and **Charm**. The mirror selves of the demons originally belonging to the [Haoli Lineage](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Haoli_Lineage "Haoli Lineage") and its related factions obtained approval and blessings from the **Haoli Sect Master** outside the dragon-sealing well and the [Underworld Daoist](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Underworld_Daoist "Underworld Daoist") within the [River of Eternal Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/River_of_Eternal_Darkness "River of Eternal Darkness"), each borrowing a droplet of the river water, which coalesced with Lumian's **Sword of Destruction**, forming a combined attack that managed to kill the consciousness of the [Malevolent Dragon](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Malevolent_Dragon "Malevolent Dragon") one time.[^3]

When the [Evernight Goddess](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Evernight_Goddess "Evernight Goddess") arrived at the [Western Continent](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Western_Continent "Western Continent"), the **Haoli Sect Master** manifested the [River of Eternal Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/River_of_Eternal_Darkness "River of Eternal Darkness") and past Haoli Sect Masters, including the [Underworld Daoist](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Underworld_Daoist "Underworld Daoist"). After the [Evernight Goddess](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Evernight_Goddess "Evernight Goddess") made an oath to strengthen the [Haoli Lineage](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Haoli_Lineage "Haoli Lineage") in the future, the [Underworld Daoist](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Underworld_Daoist "Underworld Daoist") declared "Her" as the next Sect Master, and "She" started [accommodating](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Accommodating "Accommodating") the [River of Eternal Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/River_of_Eternal_Darkness).[^4]
