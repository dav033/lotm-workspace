## Sequence 9: Apothecary

**Traits**

- **Physical Enhancement**: While **Apothecaries** don't gain massive physical improvements as some of the other [Pathways](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways") at this **Sequence**, they do gain a few upon becoming a **Sequence 9 Beyonder**.[^1]
	- Their enhancements make them relatively resistant to poisonous elements and toxins.
- **Spirituality**: Their **Spirituality** gets enhanced as a **Beyonder**.
	- **Spirit Vision**: Their **Spirit Vision** shows them a person's overall health and wellbeing.

**New Abilities** [^2] [^1] [^3]

- **Taming**: **Apothecaries** are good at **Taming** and domesticating plants.

## Sequence 8: Beast Tamer

**Traits**

- **Physical Enhancement**: Their physical attributes have been greatly improved.
	- **Beast Tamers** can fight many wild beasts head-on.

**New Abilities** [^2]

- **Animal Senses**: They can read the thoughts of, utilize the senses of, communicate to, and control **Animals**, gradually **Taming** them and making them into their assistants.
	- **Beast Tamers** are instinctively the natural enemies of animals, being able to **Intimidate** them into complete submission with just their gaze.
		- Upon merely looking at the eyes of a **Beast Tamer**, [Susie](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Susie "Susie") suddenly froze for a moment before quickly running away and disappearing without a trace.[^1]

**Strengthened Abilities** [^4]

- **Taming**: They can **Tame**, domesticate, and use various unique animals rather effectively.
	- A **Beast Tamer's** capability of **Taming** also includes **Beyonder** and supernatural creatures.
		- They can help animals withstand **Beyonder** potions to become **Beyonder** creatures.

## Sequence 7: Vampire

**Traits**

- **Physical Enhancement**: **Vampires** gain several physical improvements to their body, granting them an array of physical benefits that make them truly a different species separate from humanity. They subsist mainly on blood rich in **Spirituality**.
	- Their appearance along with charisma improve as they not only possess an extremely strong agility and speed but also gain superhuman regeneration capabilities.
		- Smaller wounds on their body would be swiftly healed, and even more severe ones would show significant improvement. In a matter of minutes, they would close up on their own, ceasing to impede their movements.[^5]
			- Their self-healing makes them able to withstand infectious diseases against the pathogens from the [Demoness Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Demoness_Pathway) to a certain extent if the infection and illness haven't been exacerbated, outpacing the effectiveness of their self-healing abilities.[^6]
		- A **Vampire's** lifespan will increase to around *300 years* as their corresponding constitution and physical senses have been strengthened and refined.
		- At higher sequences, such as Sequence 3 and 2, they are known to possess even longer lifespan than the lifespan of [Angels](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Angels "Angels") from many pathways, similar to [Beyonders](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonders "Beyonders") of the [Demoness Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Demoness_Pathway).[^7]
		- Althought this Sequence is called **Vampire**, the fact that [Sanguines](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sanguines "Sanguines") have the nickname **Vampire** didn’t mean that they didn’t have a heartbeat. It was just that they were relatively slow, and the heart itself was one of the **Sanguine** ’s fatal weaknesses.[^8]
		- **Purification** powers like the [Tyrant Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tyrant_Pathway) 's **Lightning** are lethal against them.[^9]
				- Due to their vampiric physique they will feel discomfort in the **Sun**.[^10]
		- They don't have to drink raw blood, but can instead process the blood into delicious dish such as blood curd.[^11]

**New Abilities** [^2] [^4]

- **Vampires** will gain access and mastery over a certain number of abilities and **Spells** that fall under the domains of **Darkness**, **Shadows**, and **Moon** which includes but not limited to:
	- **Wings of Darkness**: **Vampires** are able to utilize the **Darkness** around them to form a pair of colossal and rather illusory bat wings that grant them an enhanced speed boost along with some limited flight capabilities.[^12]
		- They can turn into a *swarm* of *illusory bats* that can attack and pester a group of enemies, emit black gases that resemble a bat's wing, or create a quietly spreading **Black Flame**.[^13]
		- **Corrosive Claw**: Their nails can grow an extra section with *mysterious symbols* and *patterns* on it that make their nails able to cut through steel and give strong corrosion properties.
		- The **Claw of Corrosion** a **Vampire** manifests is the bane of all scale, skin, and membrane-based defensive methods, being highly effective against them.
		- **Abyss Shackles**: A **Spell** -like ability belonging to the **Darkness** domain can condense nearby **Darkness** or **Shadows** into *illusory* *shackles* to restrict or control enemies.
		- **The Embrace**: If a **Vampire** possesses excess **Beyonder Characteristics**, they are able to bestow it onto a human, turning that human into one of them.[^14] [^15]

**Strengthened Abilities**

- **Blood Servant Conversion**: This is an upgrade to a **Beast Tamer's Taming** ability and is very similar to **The Embrace**, except that it does not require any excess **Beyonder Characteristics** to activate.
	- This is a **Spell** -like ability within the domain of the **Moon**. With this ability a **Vampire** can **Convert** any living creature into being a **Blood Servant** of them. This comes with a few properties that are both beneficial and detrimental for the **Blood Servant**.
		- When an organism becomes a **Blood Servant**, their constitution and physique will be greatly improved, and they will become immune to many illnesses that could plague them.
				- Any terminal illnesses they originally have before the **Conversion** will either disappear completely or become treatable after the transformation into a **Blood Servant**.
				- Regardless of the benefits, **Blood Servants** can't disobey their master's orders and as such are equivalent to becoming the puppet of a **Vampire**.

## Sequence 6: Potions Professor

*Sanguine Rank: Baron*

**Traits**

- **Physical Enhancement**: A **Potion Professor's** physique has improved from their state as a **Vampire**.
	- Not only has a **Potion Professor's** speed, agility, and strength have all improved to varying degrees, they have also had their natural lifespan improved as well.[^16]
		- A **Potion Professor's** sense of hearing has been greatly enhanced, allowing them to tell if someone is lying or not by the acceleration of their heartbeat.[^17]
		- Their natural sense of smell has been improved as well, allowing a **Potion Professor** to smell corpses, differentiate between the scents of people, and detect entities who can't hide their scent, such as **[Wraiths](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chained_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_5:_Wraith)**.[^17] [^18]
		- However, this sense of smell is not always a good thing. If a **Potion Professor** comes into contact with powerful scents, they can be overwhelmed and find it difficult to focus, and may even come close to fainting.[^18]

**New Abilities** [^2] [^4]

- **Discerning Spiritual Materials**: Upon advancement, they gain the ability and vast knowledge on how to discern spiritual materials needed in various concoctions.
	- This knowledge comes directly from their own **Beyonder** **Characteristics**, and as such, can be considered as the qualitative change to an **Apothecary's** knowledge.
		- This knowledge given to them will now allow a **Potion Professor** to be able to treat terminal illness, as opposed to only being able to slow down its effects.
- **Potion & Perfume Crafting**:<sup>⁂</sup> With the aid of their newfound knowledge, **Potions Professors** can mix and create **Potions** and **Perfumes** with extraordinary effects.
	- **Potions Professors** who made preparations beforehand or who drank the corresponding **Potions** to counter certain challenges are almost always very difficult to deal with.
		- However, that is not to say that a **Potion Professor** does not face limitations, as there are many things a **Potion Professor** must account for when crafting such items.
			- A **Potions Professor's** **Potions** and **Perfumes** are like [Mystical Items](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mystical_Item "Mystical Item"), meaning they lack **Beyonder Characteristics**, and consequently have expiration dates.
						- There might be some conflicts that may occur between different types of **Potions** when drunk together, bringing about all sorts of negative influences.
				- A **Potions Professor** will never tolerate themselves becoming an exploding cocktail and thus will limit the amount and type of **Potions** they drink in battle.
		- A **Potion Professor's** level of new accumulated knowledge and the spiritual materials they have access to are what limit the types of **Potions** and **Perfumes** they can create.
		- Some known **Potions** include, but are not limited to:
			- **Solar Water Potion**: A **Potion** that is powerful against undead or vampire-type enemies.
						- **Dragon's Breath**: By pouring into one's mouth, it allows one to spit out a **Fire Breath**.
						- **Invisibility Potion**: Can be applied to make oneself or objects completely **Invisible**.
						- **Anti-Smell Potion**: It can be used to eliminate the user's smell and body odor.
						- **Anti-Dream Potion**: This **Potion** prevents the effects of **Dream Pulling**.
						- **Shadow Potion**: It allows for the user to utilize **Shadow Movement**.
				- Some known **Perfume** s include, but are not limited to:
			- **Animal-Friendly Perfume**: A **Perfume** that makes animals closer to the user.[^19]
		- A true, outstanding **Potions Professor** will always research and invent new types of **Potions** and **Perfumes** based on the knowledge they've learned or acquired.

## Sequence 5: Scarlet Scholar

*Sanguine Rank: Viscount*

**Traits**

- **Physical Enhancement**: They possess terrifying speed and unimaginable regeneration capability.
	- They are capable of creating afterimages while running.[^20]

**New Abilities** [^2] [^4]

- A **Scarlet Scholar** **'s** corresponding understanding and research on the **Moon** domain directly correlates with the effectiveness of their three core **Beyonder** powers.
	- **Full Moon**: This **Beyonder** power creates an environment that's more advantageous for themselves than for the enemy. This is mainly achieved through the control of **Moonlight**.
		- If the enemy is not good at dealing with **Spirituality** or [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World") related combat and situations, a **Scarlet Scholar** will create an environment covered by a **Full Moon**.
			- Within an environment covered by a **Full Moon**, **Spirituality** will grow, **Death** will become stronger, and one's **Spiritual Perception** will increase.[^4]
				- If the enemy is excellent at dealing with **Spirituality** or [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World") related combat and situations, a **Scarlet Scholar** will create an environment devoid of **Moonlight**.
				- They are able to become immune to the influence of the **Full Moon**.[^21]
		- When the **Moonlight** is shining upon an area, be it through the use of a **Full Moon** or done naturally, **Scarlet Scholars** will be able to use certain abilities:
		- **Moonlight Transformation**: This **Beyonder** power allows a **Scarlet Scholar** to transform into a condensed form of **Moonlight** that can move around near the **Moonlit** area.
			- Even if they were hit and shattered into pieces, a **Scarlet Scholar** could reconstruct and reform themselves either on the spot or in another location under the **Moonlight**.
				- The only way for a **Scarlet Scholar** to be injured and/or die while within their **Spectral** **Moonlight Form** is if they were hit in the heart by an attack.
								- The strength of the **Moonlight Transformation** ability depends on a **Scarlet Scholar's** understanding and research of the **Moon** realm.
						- When used, they become ethereal and tinted by a pale red color, which will then turn into a hazy ray of **Moonlight** that shatters, turning into red fragments that dissipate at the site and quickly reform in the distance.
				- **Flash**: **Scarlet Scholars** are able to **Teleport** to another location within a certain radius so long as it has the **Moonlight** shining on that area.

**Strengthened Abilities**

- Their expertise in the **Moon** domain strengthens various aspects of their previous **Beyonder** powers.
	- **Potion & Perfume Crafting**: The effectiveness of **Potions** and **Perfumes** has been enhanced.
		- **Anti-Smell Potion**: **Scarlet Scholars** can create a **Potion** that not only removes their odor, but also removes their **Spirituality** fluctuations.[^22]
		- Their abilities and **Spells** related to the **Darkness** domain has been strengthened.
		- **Scarlet Scholars** gain a natural resistance to **[Nightmare](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Darkness_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_7:_Nightmare)** -related influences.

## Sequence 4: Shaman King

*Sanguine Rank: Earl*

**Traits** [^23]

- **Physical Enhancement**: They possess a strong physique and constitution, ridiculous speed, and powerful regeneration abilities; however, they still opt a more **Spell** -caster role in fighting.
	- A **Shaman King's** lifespan increases to *1000 years*, which is considered long lived among [Demigod](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sequence "Sequence"). However, they will inevitably experience irreversible aging and decline. Therefore, 1,200 years is often their natural limit. But through special mysticism methods, they can extend their lifespan.
		- Examples of lifespan extension methods include placing **Seals** on one's body to limit lifespan usage, sleeping in a coffin as a form of hibernation, a blessing, etc.
			- Having received the [Primordial Moon](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Goddess_of_Depravity "Mother Goddess of Depravity") 's blessing, [Klarman](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klarman "Klarman") was nearly 1,400 years old yet still brimmed with energy. Because of this, he didn't need to limit his movements.[^24]
		- The eyeball(s) they lose while using **Gaze of Darkness** would grow back not too long after combat, something that will permanently disfigure/blind other **Demigods** of other [Pathways](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways") if they were to perform the same action required to cast **Gaze of Darkness**.
		- When materialized in physical form, a **Shaman King'** s heart remain their weak point, similar to the small **Crimson Moon** within their body.
		- For example, when [Klarman](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klarman "Klarman") manifested the **Crimson Moon** on his forehead, **Moonlight** surged out like a tidal wave. It forced [Sharron](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sharron "Sharron") to float uncontrollably out of his body as the entire port city lit up. In turn, damaging the **Crimson Moon** would also damage [Klarman](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klarman "Klarman").[^25]
			- Destroying his full **Blood Moon** on his forehead would prevent him from establishing another **Door of Summoning** in order to summon [Barranca](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Barranca "Barranca").
- A **Shaman King** is able to let out a cry that causes the body of a **[Scarlet Scholar](#Sequence_5:_Scarlet_Scholar)** to turn cold as if their blood seems to frost up, temporarily delaying their senses.[^25]
- They can sense others nearby by their **Spirituality** fluctuations.[^22]

**New Abilities** [^2]

- **Nature Interaction**:<sup>⁂</sup> **Shaman Kings** can simply "interact with nature", and such interaction alone replaces all the needed preparation and materials required for [Ritualistic Magic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ritualistic_Magic "Ritualistic Magic"). This lets them directly borrow the **Moon's** power on the spot, making them very terrifying opponents to deal with.
	- They can rapidly nourish the **Spirituality** in an entire battlefield, affected corpses would reanimate into **Undead,** while their own **Spiritual Intuition** gains a massive boost, enough to detect and react to an **[Ancient Bane's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chained_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_2:_Ancient_Bane)** attempt to **Possess** their body.[^22]
		- They can, if they possess a **Full Blood Moon** on one's forehead, use the illumination in order to complete a **Summoning**; however, there will be an overall change in their aura, they would lose a lot of their rationality and have their madness deepen.[^26]
		- With the illumination of the **Full Blood Moon** on his forehead, [Klarman](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klarman "Klarman"), who was only at the **Shaman King level**, was able to complete a **Summoning** in order to **Summon** [Barranca](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Barranca "Barranca").
- Every **Shaman King** is proficient in the utilization of **Substitution Spells**. In this case the act of **Substitution** can refer to either the **Shaman King** themselves or their targets.[^2]
	- **Moon Paper Figurine**: **Shaman Kings** are able to transform the surrounding **Moonlight** into a **Moon Paper Figurine** that they can use to **Substitute** themselves with.
		- They can utilize **Moon Paper Figurines** in a similar manner to how **[Magicians](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fool_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_7:_Magician)** use **Paper Figurine Substitutes**, being able to use it to block a fatal blow via **Substitution**.
		- **Gaze of Darkness**: When they gaze on someone with their eyes, it forms a **Substitution** connection that will manifest the state of the eye onto the target.
		- If they were to grab their eyeball on their hand, the target who formed a **Substitution** connection to that eyeball will be engulfed in solidified, restraining **Darkness**.
			- This will have the effect of blocking the target.
				- If they crushed their eyeball to a bloody pulp, the target who formed the **Substitution** connection to that eyeball will be gravely injured or even be destroyed.
- **Illusory Bats**: **Shaman Kings** possess the ability to divide themselves into a swarm of vampire bats that are in a state that is both illusory and real.[^27]
	- While in this form, unless the enemy can kill every single one of those bats, they can reconstruct and reform into their original form again without trouble.
		- They are be able to use **Moonlight Transformation** and **Illusory Bats** to weaken the damage brought by a curse by the **Source of Curses** ability from a **[Puppet](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chained_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_4:_Puppet)**.[^28]
		- By using both abilities, due to constantly moving at high speeds, they would be able avoid attacks while chanting a [Honorific Name](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Honorific_Name "Honorific Name").[^28]

**Strengthened Abilities**

- They are outstanding people who control the power of **Darkness**, **Moon**, **Bizarreness** and other fields and are proficient in various **Spells** of the **Moon** and **Darkness** domain.
	- **Abyss Shackles**: They are able to forcibly uproot and suspend in midair an entire roof with the strength of their **Abyss Shackles**.[^29]
		- **Blood Moon Arrow**: A projectile based attack that is used to shoot at enemies.
		- The [Rose School of Thought](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Rose_School_of_Thought "Rose School of Thought") ’s **Shaman King** shot a **Blood Moon Arrow** against [Klein](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti").[^30]

**Mythical Creature Form**

- As **Demigods** at **Sequence 4** who have obtained partial **Godhood**, they are able to manifest their incomplete **Mythical Creature** **Form** if they disregard any of the consequences.

## Sequence 3: High Summoner

*Sanguine Rank: Marquis*

**New Abilities** [^2] [^31]

- **Door of Summoning**: Extending their hands forward, they can manifest a hazy, ethereal **Door**, inscribed with numerous mystical symbols to **Summon** creatures from the depths of the [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World") to come to their aid, but only once within a certain time frame.
	- Each [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World") creature that passes through this **Door** enters into a **Contract** with the them, granting them service for a limited period of time, typically around *five minutes*.
		- For longer periods of service, the **High Summoner** must personally negotiate with the [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World") creature and renew the **Contract** for a longer period.
		- Normally, the **Summoned** [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World") creature is equal to or slightly stronger than the **High Summoner**, but it can also be weaker or far stronger.
		- Summoning stronger [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World") creature carry the risk of them defying the terms and constraints of the **Door of Summoning** itself, posing a threat to them.
		- A **Door of Summoning** can directly transmit the powers of the one who was **Summoned**.
		- Using [Lilith’s Ring](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lilith%27s_Ring "Lilith's Ring"), the **Door of Summoning** created by [Emlyn](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Emlyn_White "Emlyn White") was able to **Summon** a bright **Silver Moon**, causing [Klarman](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klarman "Klarman") to instantly lose contact with the **Crimson Moon** and all of his related abilities.[^28]
		- When it comes to the **Summoning** of a False entity, such as an image of a being created by the **Mystical Re-enactment** ability from a **[Clairvoyant](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hermit_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_3:_Clairvoyant)**, the entire **Honorific Name** has to be recited.[^32]

**Authorities**

- **Summoning**: They gain partial authority over **Summoning**.
	- **High Summoners** are able to utilize the **Moon's** influence on the [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World") to **Summon** various types of powerful creatures from the depths of the [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World") to help them.
		- If the **High Summoner** has an especially strong social network, they can even sign a **Contract** with [Angels](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Angels "Angels"), directly **Summoning** "Them" when needed.
			- They can **Summon** even a [True Deity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Gods "Gods"), however, respect and ceremony are a must with the preparation of the corresponding altar.[^33]
		- **Summoning** occasionally mutates, resulting in uncontrollable changes, causing strange creatures that are neither from the [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World") nor have signed a **Contract** to descend into reality.
		- Rumor has it that these creatures can be traced back to the endless dark [Cosmos](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Cosmos "Cosmos").
				- These **Summoning** anomalies can be good or bad for a **High Summoner**.
			- The upper limit for a **High Summoner** within the [Barrier](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Original_Creator "Original Creator") when summoning extraterrestrial beings is **Sequence 3**. [Angels](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Angels "Angels") crossing the [Barrier](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Original_Creator "Original Creator") require large-scale [Ritualistic Magic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ritualistic_Magic "Ritualistic Magic"), which is easier to discover.[^34]
						- Some **High Summoners** die from such incidents while others obtained powerful abilities or [Sealed Artifacts](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sealed_Artifact "Sealed Artifact") whose corresponding supernatural abilities exist outside of the 22 [Standard Pathways](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Standard_Pathways "Standard Pathways").

**Strengthened Abilities**

- **High Summoners** are also exceptional magic users of the **Moon** and **Darkness** domain.
	- **Bat Wing Isolation**: **High Summoners** can manifest a pair of giant *bat wings* to enclose an area in **Darkness** and make the surrounding area seem like it has been separated from the real world.[^29]
		- **Full Moon**: They can make a **Crimson Full Moon** ascend high into the sky, which has a **Nourishing** effect, capable of making the vines of a **Demigod** from the [Mother Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Pathway) expand swiftly, quickly encasing streets in a forest.[^35]
- **Physical Enhancement**: Their natural sense of smell has been improved to the point where they can smell and pinpoint the location of blood even from extremely far away.[^36]

**Mythical Creature Form**

- As a **Sequence 3 Beyonder**, they can respond to prayers within a certain range.

## Sequence 2: Life-Giver

*Sanguine Rank: Duke*

**Authorities [^2]** [^4] **[^37]**

- **Life**: "They" master the **Power of Creation** —derived from a portion of the **Life** authority.
	- So long as what "They" are treating is not dead, "They" are capable of direct treatment without needing medicines. This also means "Their" self- **Healing** has reached the highest level.[^4]
		- "They" can regrow bones and flesh from the ashes, and can regrow a complete body from a single drop of blood.[^38]
			- Even after [Julie](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Julie "Julie") and [Wanak](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Wanak "Wanak") attacked with several cannonball-like hailstones, **Black Flames**, **Lightning Strikes**, and **Fireballs**, the damage couldn't keep up with the healing speed of the [Abscessed Hand](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Zedus "Zedus") 's complete body.[^39]
						- Despite receiving a direct hit from a meteor that blew the [Abscessed Hand](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Zedus "Zedus") 's body into pieces—which left "Their" flesh charred or vaporized, with only a broken, black skeleton standing—"They" didn't die, instead, new, tender flesh grew, and the bone slowly shed its charred appearance.[^40]
		- "They" can calm the emotions of those who are suffering from **Filth** or are fighting **Corruption** and strengthen their will to restore a certain balance, or "They" can do the opposite.
		- "Their" own physical body has reached its peak, and the **Vitality** radiating from "Them" allows surrounding **Life** to feel the joy of **Growth** and **New Life**.
		- A **Life-Giver's** lifespan has been greatly enhanced, being much more than that of a normal **Angel**, capable of living for thousands of years.
			- However, "They" still need a variety of means to prolong their life until "Their" final death.
				- [Madam Magician](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fors_Wall "Fors Wall") estimated that [Duke Round Moon](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Olmer "Olmer") is at least 5,000 years old, but “He” still had to live in a coffin in an attempt to prolong "His" life.[^41]
				- Whether it's plants, animals, humans, or other extraordinary races, their **Lifespan** can be extended or shortened.
				- [Duke Olmer](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Olmer "Olmer") 's arrival made all present feel an indescribable sense of joy within them. It was the joy of seeing a flower in a garden slowly bloom after a long night amidst the morning fog. It was the joy from leaving the city and heading to the suburbs, taking in fresh air in a forest after the rain, watching mushrooms grow. It was the joy of seeing everything **Grow** healthily with **New Life** descending upon the world.[^29]
		- It can bring about changes in one's **Life** and short-term bursts of power, akin to a larger-scale **Blood Servant Conversion** that does not require slavery.
		- Of course, if the target is strong-willed and of high status, they can resist this transformation; however, they will suffer a certain degree of damage from their **Life** essence.
				- Creatures can mutate from this change in **Life** caused by this. For example:[^42]
			- Grasses that had mouth-like crevices with fibers flying out to capture insects.
						- A spectrum of extraordinary insects with some having snakes or birds for heads.
						- Flowers that used their leaves as hands to harvest pollen for themselves.
						- Grains that were heavy, occasionally emitting wailing from inside.
		- Through this authority, "They" can cleanse **Filth**, **Corrosion** and other forces.
		- "They" can bestow **Life** to a piece of land the size of [Backlund](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Backlund "Backlund") or [Trier](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Trier "Trier").
		- On the wilderness ground, [Zedus](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Zedus "Zedus") caused countless plants to sprout, creeping toward the [withered trunk](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Gift_of_the_Land "Gift of the Land") with a lush scene of **New Life**.[^43]
- **Spirituality**: "They" gain partial authority over **Spirituality**.
	- "They" can interfere with and affect the **Spirituality** of all targets, and can give effects such as rapid recovery, increased consumption, and/or instability.
		- A **Life-Giver's** mere presence is capable of replenishing the consumed **Spirituality** as if a rainstorm had filled numerous evaporated lakes.[^39]

**Strengthened Abilities**

- **Moonlight Transformation**: "They" are able to transform into condensed **Moonlight** and move around even while not currently being near a **Moonlit** area.[^44]
- **Door of Summoning**: While under the influence of **Moonlight**, "They" can open multiple **Doors of Summoning** at once as opposed to just 1.[^45]
	- Within the **Crimson Moonlight** created by [Zedus](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Zedus "Zedus"), one after another illusory and complex **Doors of Summoning** split open, connecting to different places, with some indescribable horror growing behind them.
- **Bat Wings**: A **Life-Giver** is able to unfurl giant membranous **Bat Wings** from "Their" back, capable of blotting out the sky when unfolded.[^44]
- **Full Moon**: "They" are able to cause a **Crimson Moonlight** to fall from an unknown place to wherever "They" are—evil, cold, and bloody.[^45]
	- "They" are able to transmit "Their" voice with the **Crimson Moonlight**.[^46]
		- [Duke Olmer](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Olmer "Olmer") absorbed the **Moonlight** cast by the **Crimson Moon**, which had been created by the flesh-colored figure soaked in red wine. This **Crimson Moon** was far from a simple **Full Moon**; it was closer to a **Blood Moon**, or even stronger.[^29]

**Mythical Creature Form**

- "They" have become a full **Mythical Creature**; it is a humanoid creature with a phantom **Moon** on its back, bright red eyes, and bat wings. "They" look as if "They" were the embodiment of **Beauty**.

## Sequence 1: Beauty Goddess

*Sanguine Rank: Queen (Sanguines have only Queens and no Male Princes)* [^47]

**Description**

The embodiment of the symbol and concept of **Beauty**. Such a being is inevitably a **Female** who represents **Yin Energy**. "Their" strongest abilities revolve around "Their" appearance and charm.

**Gender Transition**

- Advancing to **Beauty Goddess** results in becoming female regardless of one's original gender, while gaining strong **Reproductive** desires and **Divine** tendencies.

**Traits**

- Unlike other [Angels](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Angels "Angels"), "They" appear to have no limitations on lifespan.[^48]
	- Unless slain in a battle, a **Beauty Goddess** remains the same individual throughout *thousands of years*, never being replaced.

**Authorities** [^2] [^4]

- **Beauty**: "They" possess a lower expression of the authority of **Beauty**.
	- Any lifeform that beholds the **Beauty Goddess** is struck by "Their" overwhelming and transcendent **Beauty**.
		- Those of lower status instantly lose their sanity.
			- A **[Catastrophe](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Demoness_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_2:_Catastrophe_\(Demoness_of_Catastrophe\))** who witnesses "Their" overwhelming **Beauty** will be stunned and hold "Her" breath.[^49]
				- Even [Great Old Ones](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Old_Ones "Great Old Ones"), when facing "Them", may hesitate to cause harm or develop the desire to possess "Them", lingering for a brief moment in admiration.
		- "They" can reach or even surpass other [Sequence 0](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Gods "Gods") s in the domain of **Beauty** depending on "Their" mastery over the corresponding authority.[^50]
		- Compared to a [Sequence 0](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Gods "Gods") without a similar authority, "They" are naturally stronger. Compared to a [Sequence 0](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Gods "Gods") with a similar authority, it depends on how much authority permissions the [Sequence 0](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Gods "Gods") itself has delegated.
				- "Their" **Beauty** is pure, sublime, and **Divine**, making one want to admire and protect "Them" but not dare to approach or desecrate. In contrast, the **[Demoness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Demoness_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Demoness_\(Chaos_Demoness,_Primordial_Demoness\))** ' **Beauty** radiates **Femininity** and allure, stronger in seduction but less **Divine**.[^49]
		- Spells and abilities related to **Darkness** and **Bizarreness** will not mar "Their" **Beauty**, but instead further enhance "Their" allure, adding an aura of danger and mystery.
- **Plants**, **Animals**, **Humans**, **&** **Supernatural Creatures**: This ability arises from both "Their" mastery over the essence of **Life** and the irresistible allure of **Beauty**.
	- A **Beauty Goddess** can thus **Tame** and **Command** such creatures.
		- Likewise, no being of a lower **Sequence** can refuse "Their" **Summons**.
- **Moon**: "They" hold a certain degree of the **Moon's** concept.
	- Just as **Beauty** is derived from **Moon** and **Yin Energy**, the **Beauty Goddess** may, within reasonable limits, draw upon the power of the **Moon** to shape environments favorable to "Themselves".
		- "They" can change the color of the **Moon**.[^51]
			- Using a **Beauty Goddess** [Beyonder Characteristic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristic "Beyonder Characteristic"),[^52] [Lilith](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lilith "Lilith") created a **Silver Moon**, causing [Klarman](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klarman "Klarman") to instantly lose contact with the **Crimson Moon** and all of its related abilities.[^28]
						- Using the [Shadow of the Beauty Goddess](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Shadow_of_the_Beauty_Goddess "Shadow of the Beauty Goddess"), the [Mother Tree of Desire](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Tree_of_Desire) created a **Blood Moon**, causing an indescribable surge of **Spirituality**.[^53]
				- This caused [Sharron](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sharron "Sharron") 's maleficent thoughts to burgeon even under the support of the [Scarlet Lunar Corona](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Scarlet_Lunar_Corona "Scarlet Lunar Corona").
								- The **Moon's** authority over **Fertility** and **Proliferation** manifested in [Earl Mistral](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mistral "Mistral") and the **Marionette** [Qonas Kilgor](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Qonas_Kilgor "Qonas Kilgor") 's inclination to give birth.
				- **Moonlight Transformation**: "They" are able to dissolve into **Crimson Light**, merging with the surrounding **Moonlight**, leaving only a fading Image of "Their" waving hand.[^54]

## Sequence 0: Moon

*Sanguine Rank: True Ancestor*

**Description** [^4]

The **Moon** (which includes the imagery of **Fertility** and **Proliferation**) represents **Spirituality**, **Plants**, **Beauty**, **Summoning**, **Animals**, **Humans**, **Beyonder Creatures**, and portions of **Life**, **Darkness**, and **Bizarreness**.

**Symbolism** [^4]

- **Moon**: The **Moon** itself is more akin to a symbol than to an authority. Just as the **Moon** is Earth’s companion, the **Mother** is the first companion of all life. This is the **Moon** ’s most essential aspect. For that reason, it spans across multiple symbolisms:
	- **Blood Moon**: "She" can induce the [Blood Moon](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Blood_Moon "Blood Moon") to appear every day, making **Yin Energy** runs rampant, causing the [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World") and [Reality](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Physical_World "Physical World") to intertwine for extended periods of times.
		- This will unleash countless unspeakable monsters and [Evil Spirits](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Evil_Spirits "Evil Spirits").
		- **Crimson Moon**: In the **Crimson Moon**, **Yin Energy** intensifies, and the **Spirituality** of beings as well as **Spells** related to **Darkness** and **Bizarreness** are enhanced.
		- Many **Beyonder** powers arise from this symbolism.
		- **Silver Moon**: The **Silver Moon** will restore the moon’s silvery hue as well as cut off the connection between **Beyonders** and the **Crimson Moon**.
		- This renders all **Beyonder** powers tied to the **Crimson Moon** ineffective.
		- **Full Moon**: Under the **Full Moon**, **Life** will flourish, **Spirituality** will stirs, and emotions will calm. The bonds/connections between people would also grow closer.
		- **Waning Moon**: When the **Waning Moon** occurs, it will cause the **Darkness** to deepen and **Bizarreness** to proliferate.

**Authorities** [^4]

- **Summoning**: The **Call** of the **Moon** is able to attract very terrifying things. Examples of this include the [Brood Hive](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Brood_Hive "Brood Hive") or the [Mother Goddess of Depravity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Goddess_of_Depravity "Mother Goddess of Depravity").
	- Even with the **Seal** on the [Western Continent](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Western_Continent "Western Continent") and the **Astral Barrier**, it is not entirely impossible to **Summon** the [Brood Hive](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Brood_Hive) on the [Northern Continent](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Northern_Continent "Northern Continent") or the [Mother Goddess of Depravity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Goddess_of_Depravity) on the planet respectively. This authority is one of the reasons why [Zedus](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Zedus "Zedus") was killed by ["His" sister](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Omebella "Omebella").[^55]
- **Beauty**: "She" possesses the authority of **Beauty**. Whenever one sees the **Moon**, they will be reminded of the most unforgettable **Beauty**.
	- "She" is the second most **Beautiful** thing in the universe after the [Brood Hive](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Brood_Hive).
- **Life**: "She" possesses partial authority over **Life** and has eternal **Life**.
	- "She" can create or alter **Living** beings through this authority.
		- Through the authorities of **Fertility** and **Proliferation**, [Lilith](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lilith "Lilith") created the [Sanguines](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sanguines "Sanguines") race, making them "Her" offspring related by blood,[^56] however, the process is a bit too far-fetched to be called "natural birth".[^57]
		- "She" is able to cleanse **Corruption** attached to **Life**.
		- "She" is able to **Heal** wounds and diseases.
		- "She" is able to grant or take back **Life**.
		- "She" can make a peach that extends one's **Lifespan** for a thousand years when consumed.
- **Plants, Animals, Humans, & Supernatural Creatures**: This is a subordinate authority formed from parts of the authorities of **Life** and **Beauty**.
	- Therefore, "She" can **Tame** **Plants**, **Animals**, **Humans**, and other **Supernatural** **Creatures**.
		- During this process, one of the most important mediums is **Blood**, the symbol of **Life**.
- **Spirituality**: The source of **Spirituality**, the **Mother of All's** **Spiritual** forces.
	- This authority is derived from **Goddess of Origin** and **Mother of All Things** of the [Brood Hive](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Brood_Hive).
		- "She" can make a group's **Spirituality** unable to recover, greatly increasing the cost of usage.
		- "She" can cause the **Spirituality** of a target group to erupt or spiral out of control.
- **Darkness**: "She" possesses partial authority over **Darkness**.
	- Different from the [Darkness Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Darkness_Pathway), the **Moon's** authority over **Darkness** is more about gaining access to **Spells** and traits that are derived from the **Moon**.
- **Bizarreness**: "She" possesses partial authority over **Bizarreness**.
	- The ambiguity and mystery of the **Moon** give rise to uncanny powers such as self- **Substitution**, terrifying **Curses**, **Moonlight Transformation**, etc.
