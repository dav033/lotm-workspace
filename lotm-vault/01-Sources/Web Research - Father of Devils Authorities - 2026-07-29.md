# Web Research - Father of Devils Authorities - 2026-07-29

## Method

- WebNovel was used only to verify the official works, author, and publication records. It did not expose the relevant paid chapter text during this pass.
- The community wiki ability pages were used as secondary indexes to author-post archives and named novel chapters.
- Claims from an archived author post remain secondary until the original publisher and exact text can be independently inspected.

## Abyss Findings

- Sequence 0 is indexed with partial Authorities over Degeneration, Filth, Corruption, Desire, Curses, and Deviants.
- Degeneration is described as an environment that induces and creates depravity; Filth affects body, mind, and soul; Corruption extends degeneration and follows detonated desire and lost rational control.
- Desire is induction, catalysis, and ignition rather than full embodiment. Abyss Curses favor mind and spirit. Abyss Deviants encompass individualized demonic mutations rather than the Chained mode of suppression and control.
- Lower expressions provide the observable progression: Desire Apostle manipulation, Demon mental influence, Blatherer Corruption, Bloody Archduke Malice/Body, and Filthy Monarch Filth/Corrosion.

## Chained Findings

- Sequence 0 is indexed with concentrated Authorities over Curses, Binding, Deviants, and Objects, grounded in Binding and Unity/Collection Symbolisms.
- Binding restrains tendencies of madness, desire, objects, creatures, and Beyonder Characteristics. Removing restraint produces the Indulgence mode contained within Binding.
- Chained Curses operate through connections, objects, information, transformation, body-spirit compatibility, and environmental conditions.
- Chained is King of Deviants: it can assume temporary Deviant forms, suppress the category, and turn low- or mid-Sequence Deviants into servants. This does not grant every Authority owned by a high-Sequence Deviant.
- Objects covers sentient and non-sentient objects. The author-post index says an unclaimed Sefirah can be connected to and indirectly influenced as an Object; it does not say the Chained owns or fully controls that Sefirah.

## Novel Anchors Indexed by the Ability Pages

- Lord of Mysteries: Chapters 726, 881, 1041, 1234-1237, and 1344 for object control, Binding, Suah's environmental/body-spirit effects, Chained curses, and Deviant manifestations.
- Circle of Inevitability: Chapters 1100, 1133-1136, 1147, and 1164-1169 for Abyss mental/physical expressions, Chained transfiguration and curses, and Father of Devils-level curse/desire feats.

## Boundary

This note records a secondary web verification. It does not convert indexed author-post material or chapter references into `official/high` evidence.
