## Sequence 9: Hunter

**Traits** [^1]

- **Physical Enhancement**: They possess inhuman physical qualities, giving them increased strength, speed, reaction, body control, and natural healing ability.
	- They possess the strength of a bear and the agility of a cat, being slightly equivalent to the combination of the two creatures.
		- Their punches are strong enough to easily cause the air to release cracking sounds.
		- Upon advancement, **Hunters** will also gain a few centimeters in height.[^2]
- **Heightened Senses**: a **Hunter's** overall auditory, olfactory, and visual senses are enhanced exponentially, this gives them strong tracking skills.
	- This allows them to accurately discern different scents; "see" invisible footprints and minute details; and hear hushed sounds from an extremely far away area.
		- Their hearing is strong enough to determine the weight of a person from just their footsteps.[^2]
		- Their supernatural sensory abilities would only appear when they focus, taking the form of a weaker version that doesn't disturb them in their everyday lives.
- **Spirituality**: Their **Spirituality** is slightly enhanced as a **Beyonder**, but they are among the weakest in this field, far inferior to other [Sequence 9s](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sequence "Sequence") who excel at it.[^3] [^4]
	- **Ritualistic Magic**:<sup><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ritualistic_Magic" title="Ritualistic Magic">?</a></sup> They can learn and cast only the most basic of **Ritualistic Magic**.
		- **Spirit Vision**:<sup><a href="https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_Vision" title="Spirit Vision">?</a></sup> They can learn to use **Spirit Vision** through **Cogitation**.[^5] [^6]
		- A **Hunter's Spirit Vision** can only discern the **Ether Body** and weak spiritual creatures.
				- The probability of a **Hunter** succeeding in **Cogitation** on their first try is very low.
		- **Danger Intuition**: They can passively sense ill intentions and danger.

## Sequence 8: Provoker

**Traits** [^11]

- **Physical Enhancement**: A **Provoker's** physique becomes slightly stronger, granting them an overall increase in their strength, reflexes, speed, and agility.
	- Their natural regeneration also gets enhanced, but not by much.
- **Spirituality**: Their **Spirituality** is slightly enhanced once again.
	- **Spirit Vision**: They can activate **Spirit Vision** smoothly using a simple gesture.

New Abilities [^12] [^11]

- **Provocation**: **Provokers** possess the ability of **Provocation** which induces a permanent state change on themselves but also requires activation in order to achieve its desired effect.
	- The former refers to an improvement in their observation skills, allowing them to discern which words, actions, and situations would most easily trigger agitation from their target.
		- In extreme cases, they can even cause a target to enter a temporary loss of control.
		- The latter refers to the act of **Provocation**, which when employed, merges insults and humiliation, causing the targets to lose their composure and make irrational decisions.
		- If they encounter a beast, a monster, or a **Beyonder** who is out of control and is unable to communicate, **Provokers** can instead exude a repugnant aura that incites conflict.
				- The more tailored the taunts and humiliation, the more effective the **Provocation**. However, even a word like "Dogshit!" could incite anger to some degree.
				- The ability of **Provocation** is well-suited to lure enemies into traps and ambushes which those in the [Red Priest Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Priest_Pathway "Red Priest Pathway") excel in.

## Sequence 7: Pyromaniac (Fire Mage)

**Traits** [^13]

- **Spirituality**: A **Pyromaniac's** **Spirituality** receives a remarkable improvement.
	- **Spirit Vision**: **Pyromaniacs** can now easily and discreetly activate [Spirit Vision](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_Vision "Spirit Vision"), use it better, as well as be able to discern the various components of the **Ether Body**.
		- **Danger Intuition**: Their danger instinct has been enhanced.
		- They could now activate their intuition pre-emptively, and as a result, they could detect if they were being followed and employ their anti-tracking techniques more effectively and flawlessly.

New Abilities [^14] [^13]

- **Pyrokinesis**: They possess the ability to freely control and conjure flames within their vicinity.
	- Their ability to control and manipulate flames is mostly limited to their own body.
		- Once the conjured flames move away from their body, the pyromaniac is unable to control them efficiently without expending a large amount of **Spirituality**.
		- Their control over flames could be categorized into seven distinct aspects.
		1. **Compression**: A **Pyromaniac** can **Compress** flames before releasing them. The longer they **Compress** their flames, the more flames gathered, resulting in a more potent strike.
			- They can create a highly compressed **Fireball** which can be thrown at their target.
				2. **Fire Armor**: They can ignite a layer of **Flame Armor** over their body, affording them a measure against various forms of assault while still allowing them to breathe in air.
			- It gives some defense against freezing effects, poisonous gases, and other assault.
				3. **Conjure Flames**: They are able to **Conjure** temporary weapons using flames, capable of inflicting scorching, cutting, and piercing damage.
			- At this [Sequence](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sequence "Sequence"), a **Pyromaniac's** **Conjured** weapons lacked a tangible form, and as such they cannot be used to block or parry.
				4. **Area of Effect**: They possessed area-of-effect attacks. By extending the reach of the flames instead of hurling them forth, they could ensure precise control over their detonation, causing them to erupt at a desired location or manifest in different forms.
				5. **Delay Explosions**: They are able to delay explosions. By employing additional spirituality and manipulating the structure, they could fashion a **Flame Bomb** that would detonate at a predetermined time, rather than immediately upon impact.
				6. **Fire Enchant**: They are able to **Enchant** their melee weapons or projectiles with **Fire**, granting it fire damage, it could also be used to give it **Spirit Body** burning properties, making it effective against creatures of a soul-like nature.
				7. **Fire Infusion**: Through close-quarters combat and the forceful clash of strength, they could gradually inject flames into an opponent's body before triggering their detonation, dealing massive damage to the enemy's body.
		- In addition, they receive 4 fire related spells they could cast quickly. Unlike a **[Warlock](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hermit_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_7:_Warlock "Hermit Pathway/Abilities")**, they don’t need materials, symbols or recite incantations in their minds to launch them, in terms of actual combat, it’s faster, and more convenient.[^15]
		1. **Fire Ravens**: Summons multiple ravens made of fire. These ravens are great at intercepting and locking-on multiple targets at the same time making it more difficult to avoid than a regular **Fireball** and can be detonated early to impede their target's movements.
			- This granted them a degree of control even after launching flames, enabling them to momentarily adjust their flight path and lock onto their intended targets.
						- Without this spell, a recently promoted **Pyromaniac**, would need to expend way more **Spirituality** and energy to achieve a similar outcome.
				2. **Wall of Fire**: Summons forth a pair of flaming serpents that slithered toward the enemy, erecting a scorching barrier around them by utilizing the ground as a conduit and drawing upon its own essence to maintain itself without their **Spirituality**.
				3. **Blazing Spear**: Creates a spear made of blazing-white fire which can be thrown. This spear does higher damage then an average **Fireball** but has a lower area of effect. A direct hit would nail their target to the ground before burning them inside-out.
				4. **Giant Fireball**: This spell is akin to that of the **Compression** of numerous crimson **Fireballs** into a single **Giant Fireball**. Such an attack that directly hits a target will lead to a devastating blast with a large area of effect radius.
		- Their flames can burn a **Spirit Body**, making them effective against creatures of a soul-like nature.
		- They can exert minute influence over combustible substances within their vicinity.
- **Fire Resistance**: Their bodies have a remarkable resistance to flames. Even if doused in animal fat and subjected to the flames of a torch for half a day, the damage they suffered would be minimal.
	- However, the concussive force of an explosion could still harm them in a conventional manner.

## Sequence 6: Conspirer

**Description**

The **Conspirer's** direct destructive capability is ranked in the upper-tier among **Sequence 6s**.[^16]

Traits [^17]

- **Enhanced Mental Attributes**: Upon drinking this **Sequence 6** potion, **Conspirers** will gain enhanced insight, as well as sharper and much clearer thinking.
	- They will also gain a series of other specific improvements, such as an enhanced intelligence and the ability to fabricate excuses to convince others.
- **Information-Gathering**: They gain enhancements to their five senses and spiritual perception.
	- This is the strengthened form of **Heightened Senses**.
- **Physical Enhancement**: Their physique has been improved to a certain extent.
- **Spirituality**: A **Conspirer's** **Spirituality** has also been enhanced to a degree.

New Abilities [^17]

- **Incitement** (**Instigation**): **Conspirers** can **Incite** certain thoughts or desires in someone’s mind through conversing with them, such as by consciously igniting the flames in their heart.
	- The way this ability works is very similar to that of an **[Instigator's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Demoness_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_8:_Instigator "Demoness Pathway/Abilities")** **Instigation**.
- **Misdirection**, **Confusion**, **Deception** (**Conspiracy**): These factors of manipulation are the core aspects of **Conspiracies** which **Conspirers** use to manipulate their enemies.
	- [Medici](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sauron-Einhorn-Medici "Sauron-Einhorn-Medici") used various aspects of **Deception** and **Misdirection** in order to make it seem that "He" was unable to enter [4th Epoch Trier](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Underground_Trier "Underground Trier") which "He" then used to commence a sneak attack and regain "His" status as a [King of Angels](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Kings_of_Angels "Kings of Angels").[^18]
		- [Lumian Lee](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee") created a quick **Conspiracy** in order to determine whether a trader was lying about having mystical items. He had also created a **Conspiracy** to kill [Hisoka](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hisoka "Hisoka") in both his real and **Dream Projection** form.[^19] [^20]
- **Flame Transformation**: **Conspirers** are able to merge with their fire weapons conjured from **Pyrokinesis** and travel to the destination that their fire weapon lands.
	- They can take other people with them, but unlike **Conspirers**, they are not immune to the flames.[^21]
		- They are also able to merge with some of the fire spells they can cast, such as a **Fireball**, etc.
		- The initial range is 10 meters. This would increase over time as they digest their potion.

**Strengthened Abilities** [^17]

- **Pyrokinesis**: Their precise control over flames has improved. Their flames are now crimson-white.
	- **Compression**: The difficulty of repeatedly compressing the crimson flames to form incandescent white flames has decreased. The time required has significantly reduced and, depending on the flame's specific appearance and energy density, it ranges from one to three seconds.
		- **Conjure Flames**: They can now **Conjure** their controlled flames into different forms, such as whips and sabers. In addition, due to the more solid and tangible nature of their flames, they are now able to parry with these **Conjured** fire weapons.

## Sequence 5: Reaper

**Description**

The **Reaper** has the highest damage output between all **Sequence 5** **Beyonders**, and from this point onward the **Red Priest Pathway** will have this advantage in every **Sequence**, however, some [Pathways](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways") have abilities with instant death effect, which the **Red Priest Pathway** doesn't have.[^22]

**Traits** [^23]

- **Physical Enhancement**: Their strength, speed, and endurance have improved.
	- However, they are still not resilient enough to withstand a bullet.
- **Spirituality**: Their **Spirituality** has been enhanced to a significant degree.
- **Enhanced Mental Attributes:** A **Reaper's** thinking becomes clearer.

**New Abilities [^24] [^23]**

- **Weakness Investigation**: Through supernatural means, a **Reaper** is able to discern a target’s vulnerabilities and weak points in their defenses.
	- They can also find weaknesses in certain physical structures or supernatural barriers (like the walls from a **[Guardian's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Twilight_Giant_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_5:_Guardian "Twilight Giant Pathway/Abilities") Protection**).
		- Their **Weakness Investigation** discernment also applies to non-physical bodies and weather phenomena as well.
		- This is seen with how [Lumian Lee](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee") attacked [Hisoka's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hisoka "Hisoka") **Dream Projection**.[^19]
- **Cull**: They can infuse any of their attacks with this **Beyonder** power to harvest their target's life, making each attack akin to striking an enemy's vital points and weaknesses.
	- To a certain extent, they could even inflict real damage on **Demigod** -level creatures, provided they didn’t block or successfully evade, and refrained from using mystic defenses.
		- They can damage non-physical bodies, physical structures, and phenomena with **Cull**.[^19]
			- In an attempt to complete the ritual needed to become a **Weather Warlock**, [Lumian Lee](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee") had discerned the weakness of the weather in [Berserk Sea](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Berserk_Sea "Berserk Sea") before attacking it with **Cull**.
		- If they hit a genuine weakness or vital point, it could deliver a fatal blow, making it challenging for the target to withstand three consecutive attacks.
		- This rule applies to even high defense targets like **[Guardians](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Twilight_Giant_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_5:_Guardian "Twilight Giant Pathway/Abilities")**.[^25]
			- [Anderson Hood](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Anderson_Hood "Anderson Hood") hit a weakened **Stone Golem** in the heart with an uppercut infused with **Cull**, causing it to freeze on the spot before crumbling into rubble seconds later.
- **Precision**: A **Reaper** is able to precisely target a predetermined location and carefully manipulate **Fireballs**, **Fire Ravens**, and all sorts of **Fire Spells**.
	- Through this, they can split a colossal **Fireball** into hundreds, striking different targets with accuracy, achieving effective area-of-effect damage.
- Both **Cull** and **Precision** require a substantial amount of **Spirituality**, which makes them hard to repeatedly use when in prolonged fights.
	- The exception to this rule is using the combination of **Precision** and **Fire Raven**, as **Fire Raven** could allocate a bit of **Spirituality** and is easily manipulated, making the overall consumption of a **Reaper's** **Spirituality** low.

**Strengthened Abilities** [^23]

- **Pyrokinesis**: Their ability to manipulate and control flames has been enhanced.
	- **Compression**: They can **Compress** flames into a blazing white state in an incredibly short time.
		- **Conjure Flames**: They appear to be better able to **Conjure** and manipulate flames.
		- A **Reaper** is now capable of **Conjuring** their flames into a burning figure that can move and attack in a simple motion. This serves as a method of attack and deception.[^26]
- **Flame Transformation**: Ignoring the high **Spirituality** consumption, they can now merge with their flames and swiftly cover dozens to hundreds of meters.

## Sequence 4: Iron-blooded Knight

**Gender Transition**

- Advancing to a **Iron-blooded Knight** results in becoming a male regardless of the original gender.

**Traits** [^27]

- **Courage**: **Iron-blooded Knights** have **Courage** comparable to steel, giving them high resistance against mental attacks and ensures they are not knocked down by fear.[^28]
	- However, such **Courage** balances fear without recklessness, ensuring that they don't suffer from falling into any traps due to their enhanced confidence.[^29]
- **Physical Enhancement**: They have gained enhancements to their physical attributes that allow them to be able to fight against [Twilight Giant Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Twilight_Giant_Pathway "Twilight Giant Pathway") **Giants** or **Dragons**.[^30]

**New Abilities** [^27]

- **Weaponizing**: **Iron-blooded Knights** can turn any ordinary object into a terrifying murderous weapon.
	- An ordinary fountain pen could become as dangerous as a fired cannonball. Such "weapons" will also have their corresponding durability/strength match their offensive ability.[^29]
		- Their **Augmented** objects can serve as carriers for their **Fireballs,** allowing for them to divert some power to constantly accelerate, bombing a target area from great distances.[^31]
		- An **Iron-Blooded Knight** can perform such remote bombardments from a *5 to 10 kilometer range*, presumably according to potion digestion.
			- Across such great distances, the time elapsed to its destination is enough that even **Mid-Sequence Beyonders** with strong **Spiritual** **Intuition** could detect it and escape the covered area. As such, its greatest use is to destroy fixed targets.
- **Chain of Command**: **Iron-blooded Knights** have a hidden connection with those under their command.
	- The need of a ritual for this connection depends on several things.[^32]
		- If the mental resonance is strong enough and lack of subconscious resistance is especially high, merely declaring that a target is part of their team can form a connection.
			- Otherwise, they must conduct a ritual for such a connection to be made.
		- The leader of a **Hunter** team must be a **Hunter**. If the team has multiple **Hunters**, only the highest **Sequence** leads; if the team has multiple **Hunters** at the highest **Sequence**, they determine superiority via combat.[^31]
		- There are 3 ways they can use **Chain of Command**:[^29]
		1. **Communication**: Using the connection, they can share senses between their troops.
			- While they can't exactly pass down messages to their troops, an **Iron-blooded Knight** can use the hidden connection to their troops to inform them of certain matters.
				- [Egor Einhorn](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Egor_Einhorn "Egor Einhorn") had, in the midst of his thoughts, planned on using the hidden connection between his troops to inform his adjutant to pay him a visit.[^27]
								- [Egor Einhorn](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Egor_Einhorn "Egor Einhorn"), from hearing a sudden noise, immediately woke up every sailor and soldier in the Nepos to prepare for a sudden battle.[^27]
						- They can share power, damage, vision, hearing, and feelings with their team. [^33]
				2. **Strength Concentration**: They can focus the power of their army onto themselves.
			- At this level, they can only **Concentrate** the **Spirituality** and overall power of their army to themselves, and not the **Beyonder** powers that their teammates may possess.[^29]
						- When it comes to **Concentrating** the power of an army to themselves, it will cause each and every one of their **Beyonder** powers to be enhanced in terms of potency.[^34]
				3. **Alleviation**: They can spread the damage on themselves to their troops.
			- This does not only refer to spreading damage that is of a physical nature onto their troops but is also something that can be used on mental influences.
				- Through the [Sword of Courage](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sword_of_Courage "Sword of Courage"), the negative side effect of being overwhelmed by **Pleasure** was instead turned into a brief daze for [Lumian Lee](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee").[^35]
								- [Egor Einhorn](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Egor_Einhorn "Egor Einhorn") had spread the illusory hearing damage he experienced from [Klein Moretti](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") across his troops, causing them to hear a faint buzz.[^27]
- **Galvanization**: **Iron-blooded Knights** can transform their body and their own flames into steel at will.[^27]
	- This increases the their defensive capabilities, but not to the point where their **Galvanised** form can defend from attacks that will melt or destroy steel.

**Strengthened Abilities** [^27] [^36]

- **Pyrokinesis**: The heat from their flames increases exponentially, the flames taking on a bluish-white color. Such an enhancement also increases their flames' explosive strength by many factors.
	- By coating an **Augmented** item in flames to function as a **Fireball**, an **Iron-blooded Knight** can unleash a violent explosion that can destroy everything within a typical public square.
		- [Lumian Lee](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee") had used the [Sword of Courage](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sword_of_Courage "Sword of Courage") as a **Fireball** within the [Special Mirror World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Special_Mirror_Worlds "Special Mirror Worlds") the size of a plaza, causing a massive explosion that had flames burn everything within.[^37]
		- They can **Conjure** a flame sword and have it inserted in the ground beneath them, causing blazing white-blue **Fireballs** to spread layer upon layer, igniting every corner in a large area.
		- In an effort to kill [Voisin Sanson](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Voisin_Sanson "Voisin Sanson"), [Lumian Lee](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee") brought the [Sword of Courage](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sword_of_Courage "Sword of Courage") and inserted it into the ground, destroying and igniting every corner of the ruins.[^35]
		- Each strike an **Iron-blooded Knight** does that is imbued with their high-temperature bluish-white flames can cause a massive explosion and spread to a target's body.
		- [Lumian Lee](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee") demonstrated this through the strikes caused by his [Sword of Courage](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sword_of_Courage "Sword of Courage") causing massive explosions and spreading onto [Higdon](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Higdon "Higdon").[^38]
		- Their normal flames and power they possess are akin to that of high-class cannons, being capable of resisting its impact and mutually destroying each other.
		- [Egor Einhorn](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Egor_Einhorn "Egor Einhorn") had shot out various flames and **Fireballs** that intersected the trajectory of those fired cannonballs, causing them to explode prematurely mid-flight.[^27]
- **Flame Transformation**: They can now flame their body at will, allowing them to disperse into multiple flames which they can control precisely to move and then coalesce back into their body.
	- Even if most of the flames are extinguished by the enemy, it will not affect the integrity of their physical form, but instead weaken their vitality and **Spirituality** proportionally.
		- In this state, so long as the attack does not harm their spiritual consciousness, they can mostly heal physical-level injuries by devouring natural **Flames**.[^39]
- **Cull**: Their **Cull** ability has reached the **Demigod** level in terms of potency, allowing them to heavily damage **Demigods** through a well hit strike or the use of their **Beyonder** abilities.

**Mythical Creature Form**

- As **Demigods** at **Sequence 4** who have obtained partial **Godhood**, they are able to manifest their incomplete **Mythical Creature** **Form** if they disregard any of the consequences and become an incomplete **Calamity Giant**.

## Sequence 3: War Bishop

**New Abilities**

- **War Song & War Cry**: **War Bishops** can yell out a **War Cry** or sing a **War Song** to enhance oneself and their allies and also weaken any enemies that are near them.[^40]
	- These abilities can boost the team's morale, temporarily enhance of strength, influence the enemy's mentality, and suppress the target's performance.
- **Fog of War**: A **War Bishop** can form a **Fog of War** that disrupts the senses of anyone within it and also isolates the connection of things in the inside.[^40]
	- It reduces visual range, hearing, and smell of all the living beings within, while also enveloping the corresponding [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World"), which suppresses the interaction between spirit entities and the Spirit World, preventing most escape attempts using **Spirit World Traversal** and dulling spiritual warnings and intuitions.[^29]
		- It also disrupts most forms of mystical connections from accurately targeting those within a **Fog of War**, preventing most forms of **Curses** and **Divination** from working.[^41]
		- Within the **Fog of War**, they are unaffected by anything.[^29]
- **Sacrifice**: After offering sufficient **Sacrifices**, each **War Bishop** can petition the or the **[Symbolism](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Symbolism "Symbolism") of War** for assistance once per war.[^29]
	- Through aid from the **Essence of War**, they can manifest a crimson, dripping banner emblem on their forehead that eases the conditions of **Chain of Command**.[^42]
		- This crimson, dripping banner emblem on their forehead will increase the range of connecting to a target along with lowering the difficulty of forming a mental resonance.
		- A **War Bishop** will be able to temporarily obtain the **Beyonder** powers of a **Weather Warlock**.[^43]
		- They can gain an increase in lifespan through **Sacrificial** [Rituals](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ritualistic_Magic "Ritualistic Magic").[^44]

**Authorities**

- **Gathering**: The power of the high-level **Red Priest Pathway** belonged to **War**, and **War** was the art of **Gathering the Masses**, this is the authority of a **War Bishop**.[^45]
	- They could gather power from their army and impart their own power to one of their troops, effectively increasing that member's strength for a period of time.[^46]
		- The maximum range they can **Gather Strength** from others power to use for themselves or **Gather** power for their allies is *3 kilometers*.
				- Not only can they use the **Beyonder** powers of team members, but they can also share their own abilities and power with them.
			- **War Bishops** can only fuse the abilities they get from a teammate of the [Demoness Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Demoness_Pathway "Demoness Pathway"). This is a limitation that won't be broken even for the **Higher Sequences**.[^47]
				- [Franca](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Franca_Roland "Franca Roland") had shared "Her" ability of **Black Flames** with [Medici](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Medici "Medici"), allowing the latter to utilize the **Fire of Destruction** and **Sword of Destruction**.[^48]
						- They can use the **Beyonder** powers of their team members without their consent, but the team members need the **War Bishop's** permission to use theirs.[^32]
				- When they borrow an ally's ability, the ally will temporarily lose it.[^49]
						- If one ally borrows the **Beyonder** powers of the **War Bishop**, other allies cannot borrow other **Beyonder** powers at the same time.
				- The total amount of **Spirituality** and strength of the team remains unchanged.

**Strengthened Abilities**

- **Chain of Command**: **War Bishops** are able to unleash the true strength of an army.[^27]
	- **Crowd Control**: They can fortify and connect members together, allowing them to strongly resist outside interference. This manifests as blood and rust-like beams of light that connect the group.[^50]
		- Even while [Tyrant Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tyrant_Pathway "Tyrant Pathway") **Beyonders** in the [Holy Wind Cathedral](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Church_of_the_Lord_of_Storms#List_of_Buildings "Church of the Lord of Storms") sent several giant bluish-black **Wind** **Blades** akin to surface-to-air missiles, the **War Bishop's** airships remained fine.[^50]
			- When those **Air** **Blades** were about to hit the airbags of the airship, an invisible layer of defense appeared and shielded it from all the attacks, though the transparent "wall" formed from **Crowd Control** did shake from the assault.
						- While a **Hurricane** was formed with the help of [Tyrant Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tyrant_Pathway "Tyrant Pathway") **Beyonders** in the [Holy Wind Cathedral](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Church_of_the_Lord_of_Storms#List_of_Buildings "Church of the Lord of Storms"), the fleet of airships supported by a **War Bishop** were able to move on.[^50]
		- **Communication**: Their previous ability as an **Iron-blooded Knight** has now been enhanced, allowing a **War Bishop** to pass down messages to their own troops.

		- Female [Great Old Ones](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Old_Ones "Great Old Ones") will feel reluctance to harm or desire to possess the **Red Priest**, even briefly pausing to admire "Him", while male [Great Old Ones](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Old_Ones "Great Old Ones") would react with jealousy and deep loathing, wanting to kill "Him" at once.
- **Masculinity**: As a **Masculine** aspect of the [Original Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Original_Creator "Original Creator") and representation of **Yang** **Energy**, "He" possesses some authority over **Male** forces and concepts.
	- "He" is able to transform living beings and inanimate objects into **Males**, bestowing varying degrees of reproductive ability.
		- The authority of **Masculinity** makes the **Red Priest** the strongest, most **Masculine**, and most fertile **Male**.[^88]
		- For children born from the union of the [True Gods](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/True_Gods "True Gods") of the [Demoness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Demoness_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Demoness_\(Chaos_Demoness,_Primordial_Demoness\) "Demoness Pathway/Abilities") and [Red Priest](#Sequence_0:_Red_Priest), it's normal for twins to be a boy and a girl. Anything else would be unusual.[^89]
