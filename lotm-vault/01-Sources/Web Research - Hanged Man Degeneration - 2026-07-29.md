# Web Research - Hanged Man Degeneration - 2026-07-29

## Method

- WebNovel was used to verify the official works, author, and publication records. Relevant paid chapter text was not independently inspected during this pass.
- The community wiki pages were used as secondary indexes to named novel chapters and an archived author supplement.
- The archived transcript remains unofficial/medium evidence until the original publisher and exact text are independently verified.

## Findings

- Hanged Man Degeneration is described as the inherent degenerate or depraved nature within living beings. Its characteristic mode is self-corruption: the target's own body, thoughts, shadows, projections, objects, or abilities can corrode or betray their owner.
- Sequence 4 Black Knight expresses the domain through Cull of Spiritual Flesh, Black Armor, and Shadow of Depravation. Sequence 2 Profane Presbyter combines Degeneration with Filth through Profane Language and can pull a target's consciousness and spirit into depravity.
- Sequence 1 Dark Angel is indexed as mastering the Degeneration domain. Examples include making Lovia's body betray her, accelerating degeneration through Profane Language, and turning released Grazed Souls into obedient Depraved Dependents.
- Sequence 0 is indexed as the Degenerate Nature of living things. Shadows arise from the target's psyche and reshape the target from within. Violating one's values, morals, or an established rule is classified as degeneration in the archived supplement.
- The comparison with Abyss is categorical: Hanged Man uses the target's inherent degenerate nature; Abyss supplies an environment that induces and creates degeneration.

## Novel Anchors Indexed by the Ability Page

- Lord of Mysteries Chapters 1256, 1262, and 1264 for Black Knight and Dark Angel manifestations; Chapter 992 for the Forsaken Land of the Gods context; Chapter 1182 for Profane Language.
- Circle of Inevitability Chapters 1133 and 1137 for Profane Language, spiritual descent, and a target's internal depraved nature.

## Limits

- Sequence 0 is indexed with partial authority over Degeneration, not ownership of every form of corruption, evil, betrayal, or moral failure.
- Depravity can be a process rather than an instantaneous result. Status, resistance, purification, or the ability to suppress or separate the relevant internal nature can limit an application.
- Degeneration, Corruption, Filth, Mutation, Darkness, and Shadows interact in this Pathway but remain separate Authority categories in the source taxonomy.

## Boundary

This note records secondary web verification. It does not convert indexed chapter references or archived author material into `official/high` evidence.
