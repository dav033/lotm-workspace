# Lord of Mysteries Wiki - Door Pathway Advancement

Community-maintained potion/advancement inventory for the Door Pathway (Apprentice through Door). Records potion properties, Beyonder Characteristic appearance, ingredient formulas, advancement rituals, and (where published) consumption backlash and ritual-effect narrative excerpts.

## Summary

Per-Sequence potion data for the Door Pathway. Several mid-to-high Sequences (Scribe, Traveler, Secrets Sorcerer) include detailed narrative excerpts of the consumption backlash and ritual effect describing what the drinker experiences while digesting the potion, beyond the bare ingredient lists seen at lower Sequences.

## Extracted facts

- Sequence 9 Apprentice: black, viscous, herbal-smelling potion; Characteristic is a yellow-and-black key-shaped stone. Acting method emphasizes humility and continued willingness to accept guidance.
- Sequence 8 Trickmaster: vibrant, colorful potion resembling poisonous mushrooms; Characteristic is a rainbow-refracting transparent crystal. Acting method centers on performance, fooling, and deception.
- Sequence 7 Astrologer: transparent, nearly solidified potion; Characteristic is an eye-sized "diamond" constantly refracting starlight-like radiance. Acting method notes Astrology is not all-powerful.
- Sequence 6 Scribe: transparent, nearly solidified potion; Characteristic is a shimmering eye patterned with gray-white marks and starlight flecks. Consumption backlash: the brain rapidly expands and separates into cells forming a haloed "door" circling the brain, accompanied by ravings (including a specific risk of loss of control for Abraham family members during a full moon), before the drinker regains control and gains resonant knowledge of Scribe's Record ability, described as "I came, I saw, I record."
- Sequence 5 Traveler: whitish, half-melted-snow-like potion with light green bubbles; Characteristic is a transparent, nearly illusory crystal. Advancement ritual: set up four special coordinates in the Spirit World, each deep and far apart, which both anchor a return path and help maintain self-awareness. Consumption backlash/ritual effect: chaotic loss of self-awareness while flung into the Spirit World, gradually anchored back by the four coordinates until the drinker can "traverse" back to a familiar location.
- Sequence 4 Secrets Sorcerer: starlight-rippling potion, alternately described as painfully icy water; Characteristic is a crystal refracting illusory resplendent doors (a crystal ball formed from insects). Advancement ritual: seal a hostile demigod-level creature, with better results the less outside help is used. Consumption backlash: sensory loss, a hallucinated night sky with insect-like creatures crawling from the body toward embedded diamond shards, and (for those affected by Bethel Abraham specifically) a calling-for-help voice in an unfamiliar but instantly comprehensible language.
- Sequence 3 Wanderer: deep black potion filled with glittering specks; Characteristic is a miniature starlight door. Advancement ritual: find three pieces of Astral World information projected into the Spirit World, enter the dangerous scenarios they derive from, and fully record the experience.
- Sequence 2 Planeswalker: colorless potion composed of countless illusory doors; Characteristic is a resplendent, slightly burning torch. Advancement ritual: leave legends about oneself at nine different locations outside the home planet.
- Sequence 1 Key of Stars: deep, lightless potion that nonetheless attracts abundant starlight; Characteristic is a simple key seemingly forged from pure starlight. Advancement ritual: locate a continuously rotating, signal-emitting heavy planet, land on it, and establish sufficient mystical connection with it.
- Sequence 0 Door: spherical potion seemingly containing entire worlds; main ingredients are three Key of Stars Beyonder Characteristics plus mastery of astronomical/geographical/folkloric knowledge of at least three galactic systems. Advancement ritual: break out of a True God's seal or banishment.

## Short quotations

- "One shouldn't flaunt one's superiority and be arrogant, and has to understand the insignificance of their own strength." (Apprentice acting method)
- "Find three piece[s] of Astral World information projected into the Spirit World, enter the dangerous scenarios derived from them, and record everything down in full detail and do not forget them." (Wanderer advancement ritual)
- "Break out of the seal or banishment of a True God." (Door advancement ritual)
