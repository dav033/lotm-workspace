The **Underworld Daoist** is a previous Sect Master of the [Haoli Lineage](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Haoli_Lineage "Haoli Lineage") and a powerful figure of the [Western Continent](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Western_Continent "Western Continent") who follows the [Talisman Bestowal System](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Talisman_Bestowal_System "Talisman Bestowal System"). "He" holds authority over the [Eternal Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Eternal_Darkness "Eternal Darkness") group pathways and is connected to their [Sefirah](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirot "Sefirot"), the [River of Eternal Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/River_of_Eternal_Darkness "River of Eternal Darkness").

## Appearance

***Dream Festival's Ancient Black Tomb***: A figure wearing a strange rusted iron crown and a dark robe with peculiar patterns. Its skin was milky-white, and its eyes were so dark they seemed to hold the entire night. A pale-white beard fluttered around its mouth and chin. His hands had pale, dark skin that was still smooth. The backs were cracked, each crack either covered in white feathers stained with pale yellow oil or dripping with decaying yellow pus.[^1]

## History

When the [Armored Shadow](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Armored_Shadow "Armored Shadow") was summoned by [Lumian Lee](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee") to answer questions, it mentioned the **Underworld Daoist**:

> The [Blood Son of Heaven](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Alista_Tudor "Alista Tudor") disrupted the netherworld, and the **Underworld Daoist** sacrificed himself to enter the river.
> 
> [Franca Roland](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Franca_Roland "Franca Roland") 's translation  
> On [Circle of Inevitability](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Circle_of_Inevitability_\(Novel\) "Circle of Inevitability (Novel)"), Chapter 373

Here, the **Blood Son of Heaven** refers to the [Blood Emperor Alista Tudor](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Alista_Tudor "Alista Tudor"). After death, "His" remnant spirit somehow traversed a [mysterious river](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/River_of_Eternal_Darkness "River of Eternal Darkness") to Franca's home world and wrought havoc there. The **Underworld Daoist** then sacrificed "Himself" to enter the river and sealed the Blood Emperor. "He" had been as good as dead since "His" sacrifice. "His" consciousness only partially merged into the [River](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/River_of_Eternal_Darkness "River of Eternal Darkness"), allowing "Him" to act briefly but preventing "Him" from returning to the living world. This fate mirrored those of "His" predecessors and successors in the [Haoli Lineage](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Haoli_Lineage "Haoli Lineage").[^2] [^3]

Later, during the [Dream Festival](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Dream_Festival "Dream Festival"), the **Underworld Daoist** was found sitting at the entrance of a black ancient tomb. "He" was the one who granted boons from the [Darkness Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Darkness_Pathway "Darkness Pathway") to [Amandina](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Amandina "Amandina") and the [Death Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway "Death Pathway") to [Robert](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Robert "Robert").[^1] He sealed the influence of the [Blood Emperor’s](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Alista_Tudor "Alista Tudor") remnant aura on Lumian's right hand.[^4] [^5]

[Madam Magician](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fors_Wall "Fors Wall") speculated that it was highly likely that [Death](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Salinger "Salinger") had some interactions with the **Underworld Daoist**. For some reason, the Underworld Daoist agreed to [Naboredisley](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Naboredisley "Naboredisley") 's request for help, causing Naboredisley's corpse to slumber in a special dream.[^6]

### The Apocalypse

During [Lumian](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee") 's battle with the [Malevolent Dragon](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Malevolent_Dragon "Malevolent Dragon"), the mirror selves of the demons originally belonging to the [Haoli Lineage](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Haoli_Lineage "Haoli Lineage") and its related factions obtained approval and blessings from the [Haoli Sect Master](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Haoli_Sect_Master "Haoli Sect Master") outside the dragon-sealing well and the **Underworld Daoist** within the [River of Eternal Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/River_of_Eternal_Darkness "River of Eternal Darkness"), each borrowing a droplet of the river water, which coalesced with Lumian's **Sword of Destruction**, forming a combined attack that managed to kill the consciousness of the [Malevolent Dragon](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Malevolent_Dragon "Malevolent Dragon") one time.[^7]

When the [Evernight Goddess](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Evernight_Goddess "Evernight Goddess") arrived at the [Western Continent](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Western_Continent "Western Continent"), the [Haoli Sect Master](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Haoli_Sect_Master "Haoli Sect Master") manifested the [River of Eternal Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/River_of_Eternal_Darkness "River of Eternal Darkness") and past Haoli Sect Masters, including the **Underworld Daoist**. After the [Evernight Goddess](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Evernight_Goddess "Evernight Goddess") made an oath to strengthen the [Haoli Lineage](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Haoli_Lineage "Haoli Lineage") in the future, the **Underworld Daoist** declared "Her" as the next Sect Master, and "She" started [accommodating](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Accommodating "Accommodating") the [River of Eternal Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/River_of_Eternal_Darkness).[^8]
