## Sequence 5: Mentor of Disorder

### Potion Properties

> A jet-black liquid that generates its own vortexes and waves, sometimes expanding and sometimes contracting.^{[1]}

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> A tightly clenched, semi-transparent, jet-black fist. The fist gives a direct sense of power and unnerving strangeness. The patterns, light spots and nails on it seem to follow normal rules, yet they are full of incongruous meaning, as if they are hiding a lot of madness and disorder.^{[1]}

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

  * 1 Fairy Spark (possesses self-awareness).^{[1]}
  * The head of "Child of the Forest" (it's an almost black dark green baboon with deformed horns resembling a crown).^{[1]}

Or
  * A Mentor of Disorder Beyonder Characteristic.

Supplementary Ingredients

  * 100 ml of a "Child of the Forest's" blood^{[1]}
  * 7 drops of cedarwood hydrolat,^{[1]}
  * An ear of a serial traitor (has betrayed at least twice),^{[1]}
  * 1 frangipani flower.^{[1]}

### Advancement Ritual

Become the dominant force in a city's underground order, causing the city's crime rate to increase more than tenfold; this state must last for at least three months.^{[1]}

## Sequence 0: Black Emperor

### Potion Properties

> The reflection of a collapsing world.^{[1]}

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

  * The Black Emperor Uniqueness.
  * Three Prince of Abolition Beyonder Characteristics.

Supplementary Ingredients
An alternate world where order has completely collapsed.^{[1]}

### Advancement Ritual

* Possess your own country, linking your name to the title of Emperor and making it common knowledge among the populace.^{[8]}
  * Establish a set of strict and complicated rules that defy normality, including architectural styles.^{[8]}
  * Spur your citizens to secretly establish nine mausoleums in the style of pyramids.^{[8]}
  * Enter one of the mausoleums.^{[8]}
  * Drink the Sequence 0 potion while a large majority of the citizens are scattered across different cities in the corresponding sacrificial rituals involved.^{[8]}
