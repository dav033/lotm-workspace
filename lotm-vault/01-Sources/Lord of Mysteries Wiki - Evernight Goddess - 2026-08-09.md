The **Evernight Goddess** is the Goddess of the [Church of the Evernight Goddess](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Church_of_the_Evernight_Goddess "Church of the Evernight Goddess").

Although it is widely believed that as the Lady of Crimson, the **Evernight Goddess** ' symbol is The Moon, [Mr. Door](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Bethel_Abraham "Bethel Abraham") has said that the Tarot Card that better represents "Her" is The Star.[^2]

"She" was the first god [Klein](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") became familiar with by joining the [Nighthawks](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Nighthawks "Nighthawks").

## Sacred Emblem

![Sacred Emblem - Church of the Evernight Goddess2](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/lord-of-the-mystery/images/7/71/Sacred_Emblem_-_Church_of_the_Evernight_Goddess2.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/180?cb=20230526075650)

Sacred Emblems are the symbols of the Gods which are worshipped and glorified by the people in the [Fifth Epoch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fifth_Epoch "Fifth Epoch").

The symbol of the **Evernight Goddess** is the Dark Sacred Emblem. Its base was black, with a crimson half-moon surrounded by radiant light points.

After the [World War](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/1350_World_War "1350 World War"), "Her" symbol changed from the crimson moon to that of stars.[^4]

## Appearance

As an orthodox god, "She" doesn't have an actual figure for people to worship. However, "Her" appearance is shown in the [ruins](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tudor-Trunsoest_Underground_Ruins "Tudor-Trunsoest Underground Ruins") of the [Fourth Epoch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Epoch "Fourth Epoch").

***In Fourth Epoch:*** The **Evernight Goddess** is an extremely beautiful lady with a hazy face. "She" wears a layered black classical dress that isn't overly complex. A circle of radiating rays is at the bottom of "Her" head.

On this lady’s dress, there are sparkling specks of radiance. They are pieces of bright and resplendent gems. At first glance, it feels as though you are seeing the night and the stars. There is a circular shape under the **Evernight Goddess** ’ head that resembles a full moon.[^6]

***1350 World War:*** "She" wears a layered black dress with countless sparkling specks of radiance like the stars. "Her" ribs and waist each stretch out two arms. Among the six hands, two of them hold a black scythe, two of them hold a crimson "moon", one is empty, and one holds a [golden bird-shaped accessory](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Golden_Phoenix_Accessory "Golden Phoenix Accessory") (that was taken from [Azik](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Azik_Eggers "Azik Eggers")).[^7]

***Twisted Version ([True Creator's Temple](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/True_Creator%27s_Temple "True Creator's Temple"), in the [Forsaken Land of the Gods](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Forsaken_Land_of_the_Gods "Forsaken Land of the Gods")):*** A woman wearing a black, classical dress. "Her" clothes were layered but not complicated, and it was embedded with a starry sheen. "Her" body was relatively ethereal, with signs of rippling outward. "Her" face was blurry, as though "She" was wearing a mask without any facial features.

The darkness around "Her" surged as one strange eye after another faintly became discernible.
