### Potion Properties

> It features numerous unintelligible symbols and patterns, black eyes, a bloodshot mouth, and a swirling, floating pale liquid.[^1]

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- The throat of a Cursed Artifact.[^1]

Or

- **A [Disciple of Silence](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chained_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_3:_Disciple_of_Silence_\(Cursed_Artifact\) "Chained Pathway/Abilities")** [Beyonder Characteristic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristic "Beyonder Characteristic").

### Advancement Ritual

Establish a cult, cultivate at least a thousand followers, so that they can understand your gaze, comprehend your thoughts, implement your ideals, most appropriately act on your will, and speak the words you want to say.[^1]

The higher the level of the followers, the fewer the number required, it is not necessary to reach a thousand.

## Sequence 2: Ancient Bane

![Potion - Chained Sequence 2 - Ancient Bane](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/lord-of-the-mystery/images/b/bb/Potion_-_Chained_Sequence_2_-_Ancient_Bane.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/200?cb=20260331054727)

Potion - Chained Sequence 2 - Ancient Bane

- Overview
- Formula
- Ritual
- Backlash
- Acting Method

### Potion Properties

> A pale, misty liquid, with every tiny droplet exuding an aura of evil and madness.[^1]

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> A pervasive, pale mist stained with blood and black spots, about the size of a head.[^1]

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- The spiritual collection of an Ancient Bane.[^1]

Or

- **A [Ancient Bane](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chained_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_2:_Ancient_Bane "Chained Pathway/Abilities")** [Beyonder Characteristic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristic "Beyonder Characteristic").

Supplementary Ingredients

- 1 commonly used item of an Ancient Bane.[^1]
- 9 ancient bones.[^1]
- 1 completely eradicated curse (the longer the curse lasted the better the effect).[^1]
- 1 architectural sculpture with connotations of constraint.[^1]

### Advancement Ritual

Find an ancient, evil item capable of bearing angelic power. This item will become the core of the final Mythical Creature form.[^1]

### Ritual Essence

The ancient, evil item will be an anchor and base, binding and stabilizing the various curses resulting from the disassembly process.  
Since the ancient, evil item will be the anchor for the future curse conglomerate, using a Historical Projection of the [Fool Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fool_Pathway "Fool Pathway") will cause them to disintegrate when the projection ends.[^1]

## Sequence 1: Abomination

![Potion - Chained Sequence 1 - Abomination](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/lord-of-the-mystery/images/0/05/Potion_-_Chained_Sequence_1_-_Abomination.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/200?cb=20260331054835)

Potion - Chained Sequence 1 - Abomination

- Overview
- Formula
- Ritual
- Backlash
- Acting Method

### Potion Properties

> A disgusting sphere made of pitch-black slime.[^1]

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> A strange, tree-like humanoid object coated in black slime.[^4]

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- **A [Abomination](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chained_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_1:_Abomination "Chained Pathway/Abilities")** [Beyonder Characteristic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristic "Beyonder Characteristic")

Supplementary Ingredients

- 9 types of curses and contamination caused by an Abomination to the surroundings,[^1]
- 1 Corpse of a True God's child.[^1]
- 900 milliliters of blood from a deceased supernatural being.[^1]
- 1 item from a broken or corrupted Divine Kingdom.[^1]

### Advancement Ritual

Receive the blessing of a [True God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/True_God "True God") (at least the blessing of an [Activated](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Activated "Activated") [Uniqueness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniqueness "Uniqueness") or a [King of Angels](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/King_of_Angels "King of Angels") who had [Accommodated](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Accommodated "Accommodated") the [Uniqueness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniqueness "Uniqueness")).[^1]

- **Note**: This is not a simple, verbal blessing; it means that the Deity who gives the blessing will suffer a Mad Curse during the ritual, originated from the [Source of Curses](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Father_of_Devils "Father of Devils") symbol,[^5] which is quite dangerous.

## Sequence 0: Chained

![Potion - Chained Sequence 0 - Chained](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/lord-of-the-mystery/images/0/03/Potion_-_Chained_Sequence_0_-_Chained.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/200?cb=20260331054904)

Potion - Chained Sequence 0 - Chained

- Overview
- Formula
- Ritual
- Backlash
- Acting Method

### Potion Properties

> Layers of terrifying black thorns, yellow oily bandages, and bright, sharp roses.[^1]

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- The.
- Three **[Abomination](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chained_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_1:_Abomination "Chained Pathway/Abilities")** [Beyonder Characteristics](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristics "Beyonder Characteristics").

Supplementary Ingredients

- A "Cage" that once imprisoned or sealed a [True God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/True_God "True God").[^1]

### Advancement Ritual

- Mummify oneself while still alive, then fill one's body with the potion as internal organs, imprisoning it within.[^1]
- Finally, one kills themselves, sealing oneself in a true mummy state within a coffin and mausoleum for at least a hundred years, ensuring that the remaining Spirit and Will do not perish.
- Note: The body is a prison of the Mind and the Potion, the Coffin and the Mausoleum are the prison of the body.[^1]
