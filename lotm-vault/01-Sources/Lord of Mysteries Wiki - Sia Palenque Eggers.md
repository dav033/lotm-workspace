**Sia Palenque Eggers** is the current leader of the [Numinous Episcopate](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Numinous_Episcopate "Numinous Episcopate") ’s strongest faction, the Royal Family faction. "She" is a descendant with a relatively thin bloodline of the [Eggers Family](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Eggers_Family "Eggers Family"). "She" might be an [Angel](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Angels "Angels") or wields an important relic of [Death](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death "Death"). "She" calls herself the **Pale-White Empress**.[^1]

"She" is a [Sequence 1: Pale Emperor](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_1:_Pale_Emperor).

## Background

"She" advanced to Sequence 1 after [Roselle](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Roselle_Gustav "Roselle Gustav") pioneered the new shipping routes,[^2] specifically after Roselle's fall.[^3] "She" satisfies the [Sequence 1 Advancement Ritual](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway/Advancement#Sequence_1:_Pale_Emperor) by relying on being the descendent of [Death](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death "Death").[^4]

### Circle of Inevitability

Two members of the [Numinous Episcopate](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Numinous_Episcopate "Numinous Episcopate") 's Royal Family faction, [Reaza](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Reaza "Reaza") and Maslow, were ordered to take part in the [Dream Festival](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Dream_Festival "Dream Festival") of 1358, conducted in [Tizamo Town](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tizamo_Town "Tizamo Town") of [West Balam](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/West_Balam "West Balam"). According to Maslow, this order apparently came from the **Pale-White Empress** herself.

It was then confirmed by [Termiboros](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Termiboros "Termiboros"), that "She" was watching the [Dream Festival](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Dream_Festival "Dream Festival") all along.[^5]
