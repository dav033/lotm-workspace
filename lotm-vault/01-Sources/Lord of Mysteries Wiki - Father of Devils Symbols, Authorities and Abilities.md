is the title of the [Great Old One](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Old_One "Great Old One") of the [Abyss](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Abyss_Pathway), and [Chained](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chained_Pathway) [Pathways](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways").

The [Sefirah](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirah "Sefirah") and [Pathways](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways") under this Great Old One originally belonged to the [Outer Deity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Outer_Deity "Outer Deity") [Mother Tree of Desire](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Tree_of_Desire), before "She" lost them to Earth.

Right after the separation, the [Tenebrous World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tenebrous_World) was incomplete, but the [Sefirot](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirot "Sefirot") have the ability to mend themselves. The present [Mother Tree of Desire](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Tree_of_Desire "Mother Tree of Desire") 's [Sefirah](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirah "Sefirah") isn’t incomplete, and the [Tenebrous World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tenebrous_World) can also create a [Great Old One](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Old_One "Great Old One") itself.[^1]

## Symbols, Authorities & Abilities

"They" possesses full **Authority** over the [Abyss](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Abyss_Pathway), and [Chained](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chained_Pathway) [Pathways](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways") and the corresponding **Symbols**, which include but are not limited to the following:

- [Abyss](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Abyss_Pathway): Partial authorities over **Degeneration**, **Filth**, **Corruption**, **Desire**, **Curses**, and **Deviants**, and the symbol of **Abyss of the Heart/Mind**.
- [Chained](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chained_Pathway): Authorities over **Curses**, **Deviants**, **Binding**, and **Objects**, and the symbol of **Binding**.[^3]

The **Symbols**, **Authorities**, and **Abilities** "They" possess and display at the [Above the Sequence](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Above_the_Sequence "Above the Sequence") level include:

- **Source of Curses**: "Their" **Curses** reach the level of symbolism.
	- In the mystical world, there are some underlying rules that can be exploited, many ways to defeat powerful existences by harming the weak, which is the basis of many **Curses***.[^4]*
		- As the **Source of Curses**, "They" are fully capable **Cursing** **Avatars** (and **Marionettes**), such as those from the [Lord of Mysteries](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lord_of_Mysteries "Lord of Mysteries"), to indirectly **Curse** the true body.[^5]
				- Anything attached to "Them" could be **Cursed** via **Cursing** "Themselves" in a suicidal manner.**[^6]**
		- Using blood-colored flames, even [Great Old Ones](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Old_Ones "Great Old Ones") can be **Cursed** to morph into an animal's body, consciousness, and spirit.[^6] [^7]
		- Both the [Inextinguishable Ravings](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Inextinguishable_Ravings "Inextinguishable Ravings") and [Mother Tree of Desire](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Tree_of_Desire "Mother Tree of Desire") were heavily **Cursed**, morphing into rabbits and getting heavily injured in the process.[^6] [^7]
- **Desires**: "They" can trigger an explosion of any **Desire** "They" can sense in "Their" surroundings. Those affected are unable to resist the ignited **Desires**.[^6] [^8]
	- The [Inextinguishable Ravings](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Inextinguishable_Ravings "Inextinguishable Ravings") shifted "His" target to the [Tenebrous World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tenebrous_World "Tenebrous World") when "He" was unable to find the [Knowledge Moor](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Knowledge_Moor "Knowledge Moor"), with "His" **Desire** ignited and unable to care about anything else, falling into the trap laid by [Farbauti](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Farbauti "Farbauti") and the [Master of the Shadow Cottage](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Master_of_the_Shadow_Cottage "Master of the Shadow Cottage").[^6]
		- [Farbauti](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Farbauti "Farbauti") and the [Master of the Shadow Cottage](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Master_of_the_Shadow_Cottage "Master of the Shadow Cottage") triggered **Desires** within the **Buddha**. The good fortune of the [Key of Light](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Key_of_Light "Key of Light") made the **Desire** of "universal salvation and relief from suffering" occupy "His" thoughts, as a result, "He" only froze momentarily before becoming even more fervent in "His" participation in the battle.[^7]
- **Binding**: This symbolism qualifies the High-Sequence [Beyonder Characteristics](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristics "Beyonder Characteristics") of the [Chained Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chained_Pathway "Chained Pathway") as ideal **Sealing** materials.[^3]
	- [Klein Moretti](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") **Sealed** the weakened [Supernova Dominator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Supernova_Dominator "Supernova Dominator") within an [Abomination](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chained_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_1:_Abomination) [Beyonder Characteristic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristic "Beyonder Characteristic"), the latter was helplessly trapped for *2 or 3 minutes* even when "His" consciousness, spirit, [Sefirot](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirot "Sefirot"), [Uniqueness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniqueness "Uniqueness"), and [Beyonder Characteristics](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristics "Beyonder Characteristics") were all intact.[^9]

### Countering Symbols, Authorities & Abilities

Certain **Symbols**, **Authorities**, and **Abilities** of other [Great Old Ones](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Old_Ones "Great Old Ones") can counter some of "Their" powers, which include but are not limited to the following:

- [High-Dimensional Overseer](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/High-Dimensional_Overseer):
	- **Dimensionality** counters **Desires**.
		- Beings in higher dimensions or within the [High-Dimensional Overseer](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/High-Dimensional_Overseer "High-Dimensional Overseer") 's dimensional painting will not be fully consumed by the explosion of desires from the **Father of Devils**, "They" would only enter a brief state of stagnation.[^7]
