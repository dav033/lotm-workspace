## Symbols, Authorities & Abilities

"They" possess full **Authority** over the [Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Darkness_Pathway), [Death](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway), and [Twilight Giant](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Twilight_Giant_Pathway) [Pathways](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways") and the corresponding **Symbols**, which includes but are not limited to the following:

- [Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Darkness_Pathway): Authorities over **Darkness**, **Horror**, **Spirit Body** & **Spirituality**, **Concealment**, and **Misfortune** & **Catastrophe**. This pathway also symbolizes **Silence** and **Eternal Darkness**.[^7]
- [Death](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway): Authority over **Death**, **Pallor**, **Undead**, **Enslavement** and the **Endpoint**. This pathway also symbolizes **Death** and **Eternal Rest**.[^7]
- [Twilight Giant](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Twilight_Giant_Pathway): Authorities over **Twilight**, **Holiness**, **Combat**, and **Proxy**. This pathway also symbolizes **Decay** and **Passage of Time**.[^7]

**Note**: A [Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathway "Pathway") having certain symbolism doesn't mean at the level of [Above the Sequence](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Above_the_Sequence "Above the Sequence") that it entirely stems from that [Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways"). It might stem from the entire group or certain parts from it.

The **Symbols**, **Authorities**, and **Abilities** "They" possess and display at the [Above the Sequence](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Above_the_Sequence "Above the Sequence") level include:

- **Termination**: "They" are a major part of the [Fourth Pillar](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Pillar "Fourth Pillar").
- **Singularity of All**: The **Singularity of All** is a [Pillar](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pillar "Pillar") -level symbolism of the [Fourth Pillar](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Pillar "Fourth Pillar"),[^16] an aspect of the **Termination** symbolism.[^17] The **Eternal Darkness** possesses the incomplete symbolism.
- **Space-Time-in-One**: The Space-Time-in-One is a [Pillar](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pillar "Pillar") -level symbolism of the [Fourth Pillar](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Pillar "Fourth Pillar"),[^16] an aspect of the **Termination** symbolism.[^17] The **Eternal Darkness** possesses the incomplete symbolism.
- **Darkness**: The **Eternal Darkness** holds the symbolism of **Darkness**.
- **Eternal Rest**: Under the **Eternal Darkness** ' existence, **Eternal Rest** would have a destination and even **Time** would have an **Endpoint**.
- **Death**:
- **Passage of Time**: As long as "They" exist, one will age and grow weaker under the **Passage of Time**.
- **Enslavement**: "They" have the symbolism of **Enslavement**.[^22]
- **Decay**: "They" have the symbolism of **Decay**.[^22]

### Countering Symbols, Authorities & Abilities

Certain **Symbols**, **Authorities**, and **Abilities** of other [Great Old Ones](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Old_Ones "Great Old Ones") can counter some of "Their" powers, which include but are not limited to the following:

- [Monarch of Decay](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Monarch_of_Decay):
	- **End of the River of Time** counters **Death**.[^25]
		- The [Monarch of Decay](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Monarch_of_Decay) can use the **End of the River of Time** symbolism to delay **Death** until the end of time, leaving the present unaffected.
- [Supernova Dominator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Supernova_Dominator):
	- "He" has some resistance against the **Death** symbolism.
		- "He" cannot be killed instantly by the **Death** symbolism because "His" massive size is much larger than the instant symbolic influence range, making the symbolism need a certain period of time to take its effect on "Him". Essentially, this is **Reality** resisting and dissipating **Mysticism**, one of the lower manifestations of the **Reality** characteristics.[^36]
- [Mother Goddess of Depravity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Goddess_of_Depravity):
	- **Sovereign of Reality** counters **Death**.
		- **Reality** resists and dissipates **Mysticism**. For the [Mother Goddess of Depravity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Goddess_of_Depravity "Mother Goddess of Depravity") who wields the symbol of **Reality**, even though "She" doesn't have a size larger than the instant symbolic influence range, mechanism-wise, "She" cannot be killed by the **Eternal Darkness**.[^36]
- [Lord of Mysteries](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lord_of_Mysteries):
	- **King of Space-Time** mutually counteracts **Space-Time-in-One** depending on the occasion and circumstances, with the **Space-Time-in-One** being slightly more dominant.[^24]
- [Demon of Knowledge](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Demon_of_Knowledge):
	- **Mystery Prying** mutually restrained **Concealment**.[^42]
		- There is a chance that **Mystery Prying** can see through **Concealment**,[^43] but the result will depend on each other's state, whether there are clues, etc.
