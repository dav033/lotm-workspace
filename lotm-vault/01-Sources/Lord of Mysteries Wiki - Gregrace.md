The **Phoenix Ancestor Gregrace** is one of the [Ancient Gods](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ancient_Gods "Ancient Gods") from the [Second Epoch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Second_Epoch "Second Epoch"). "She" was an [Ancient God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ancient_God "Ancient God") of the [Death Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway) and the [Door Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Door_Pathway).

## Appearance

"She" was a giant bird whose body was covered with feathers woven by pale flames and mysterious patterns. "Her" eyes look like they are made of bronze with layers of illusory doors hidden inside.[^1]

## Pathway Composition as an Ancient God

"Her" Pathway composition is as follows:[^2]

- [Death](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway): Uniqueness + 3 Sequence 1.
- [Door](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Door_Pathway): Uniqueness+ 1 Sequence 1.

## History

According to the [Myth of Creation](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/First_Epoch "First Epoch"), the phoenixes were formed from the hair of the [Original Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Original_Creator "Original Creator").[^3]

- Even though "She" is known as the **Phoenix Ancestor**, it's actually more because "She" controlled the souls of similar ethnicity, instead of through reproduction like the [Sanguine Ancestor Lilith](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sanguine_Ancestor_Lilith "Sanguine Ancestor Lilith").[^4]

According to [Azik Eggers](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Azik_Eggers "Azik Eggers"), the [Underworld](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Underworld "Underworld") was created by the **Phoenix Ancestor Gregrace** and exists in the [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World").[^5] [Amon](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Amon "Amon") revealed that **Gregrace** also had possessed partial authority over the [Door](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Door_Pathway).[^6] It is suspected that the Door authority was the reason why "She" was able to conjure the [Underworld](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Underworld "Underworld").

- Even though "She" seized the authorities of [Death](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway) without the [advancement ritual](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway/Advancement#Sequence_0:_Death_\(Eternal_Sleeper\)), "She" was driven by instinct to fulfil this ritual afterwards since this is the authority's inherent desire.[^7]

"Her" [Divine Kingdom](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Divine_Kingdom "Divine Kingdom") was the **City of Dead**, which was renamed [Calderón City](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Calder%C3%B3n_City "Calderón City") by an angel of the [Abraham Family](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Abraham_Family "Abraham Family"). Ever since "She" opened up the Underworld, "Her" divine kingdom was moved there. The City of the Dead gradually became the holy grounds of "Her" descendants and believers, but none of its citizens ever came out again. "She" also left the clues about the [River of Eternal Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/River_of_Eternal_Darkness "River of Eternal Darkness") in "Her" divine kingdom [Calderón City](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Calder%C3%B3n_City "Calderón City").[^8]

In the elves' chronicle, during the [Early Era of Fire](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Early_Era_of_Fire "Early Era of Fire") of the [Second Epoch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Second_Epoch "Second Epoch"), the non-human races fought against the quasi-human races, devils and demonic wolves. This corresponds to the records of the [City of Silver](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/City_of_Silver "City of Silver"). "She", the [Dragon of Imagination Ankewelt](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Dragon_of_Imagination_Ankewelt "Dragon of Imagination Ankewelt"), and the [Mutated King Kvastir](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mutated_King_Kvastir "Mutated King Kvastir") were on one side, fighting together against the [Giant King Aurmir](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Giant_King_Aurmir "Giant King Aurmir"), the [Elf King Soniathrym](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Elf_King_Soniathrym "Elf King Soniathrym"), the [Sanguine Ancestor Lilith](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sanguine_Ancestor_Lilith "Sanguine Ancestor Lilith"), the [Devil Monarch Farbauti](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Devil_Monarch_Farbauti "Devil Monarch Farbauti"), and the [Annihilation Demonic Wolf Flegrea](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Annihilation_Demonic_Wolf_Flegrea "Annihilation Demonic Wolf Flegrea").[^9]

According to records of the [City of Silver](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/City_of_Silver "City of Silver"), "She", along with the other [Ancient Gods](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ancient_Gods "Ancient Gods") all lost "Their" godhood after the [Ancient Sun God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ancient_Sun_God "Ancient Sun God") emerged and reclaimed "His" authorities from "Them". The truth is that **Gregrace** was heavily injured by the [Ancient Sun God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ancient_Sun_God "Ancient Sun God"), and "Her" city was uprooted and thrown deep into the [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World").[^10] Severely wounded, "She" went into hiding as pieces, scattered across the entirety of the [Southern Continent](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Southern_Continent "Southern Continent").[^11] [^12]

Only the [Forsaken Land of the Gods](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Forsaken_Land_of_the_Gods "Forsaken Land of the Gods") still remembers the **Phoenix Ancestor Gregrace**. The rest of the world has already forgotten "Her" existence, as the god of the [Death](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway) had been replaced by the [Death Salinger](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Salinger "Salinger").

After "Her" death, [Salinger](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Salinger "Salinger") placed the body of the **Phoenix Ancestor** in the depths of the [Underworld](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Underworld "Underworld"). "He" apparently thought it would be a waste to destroy the body of this [Ancient God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ancient_God "Ancient God") and believed it could serve a more important purpose, such as helping "Him" better and more completely control the [Underworld](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Underworld "Underworld") or enabling "Him", as Death, to possess stronger power. "He" also claimed that the body of the **Phoenix Ancestor** held some secrets.[^11]

It is later revealed that "Her" death imprint has sunk into the water of the [River of Eternal Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/River_of_Eternal_Darkness "River of Eternal Darkness").[^1]

After the reigning [Death](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Salinger "Salinger") was dead, one of the reasons for the original anomaly in the [Underworld](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Underworld "Underworld") was the reanimation of the **Phoenix Ancestor**. It destroyed Death's palace, killed all of Death's Blessed present, and turned the depth of the Underworld into a Pale-White Nation. For a long time after the Underworld anomaly, the body of the **Phoenix Ancestor** still roamed the Pale-White Nation, keeping the high-ranked beings of the [Death domain](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway "Death Pathway") at bay. It has only fallen asleep in recent years. Aside from "Her" corpse, there is an egg below the belly of the **Phoenix Ancestor**,[^13] allegedly currently incubating [Omebella](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Omebella "Omebella").[^11] This is because at some point before discovering the tributary of the [River of Eternal Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/River_of_Eternal_Darkness "River of Eternal Darkness"), **Gregrace** had been contaminated by the [Brood Hive](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Brood_Hive "Brood Hive").[^14] [^15]
