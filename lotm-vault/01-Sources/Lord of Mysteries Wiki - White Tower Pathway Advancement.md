## Sequence 9: Reader

### Potion Properties

> A white liquid with noticeable dust suspended in its particles.^{[1]}

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> A short white rope that resembles tree roots.^{[1]}

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

  * 1 Worm of Book Biting.^{[1]}
  * 1 Knowledge Addiction Root (a semi-illusory object resembling a miniature tree root, one of its functions is to make people addicted to learning knowledge).^{[1]}

Or
  * A Reader Beyonder Characteristic.

Supplementary Ingredients

  * 50ml of blood from a Worm of Book Biting.^{[1]}
  * 10 g of Knowledge Addiction Root eroding substance.^{[1]}
  * 50ml of strong liquor.^{[1]}
  * 1 notebook used for personal study (must be filled with knowledge).^{[1]}

## Sequence 5: Mysticism Magister

### Potion Properties

> A dark red liquid with brass colored scales rising and sinking.^{[1]}

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> A translucent, curved "blood sausage".^{[1]}

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

  * The brain fluid of a young Brass Dragon or Brass Giant Lizard.^{[1]}
  * The small intestine of a Shadow Naga.^{[1]}

Or
  * A Mysticism Magister Beyonder Characteristic.

Supplementary Ingredients

  * 3 scales of a young Brass Dragon or Brass Giant Lizard.^{[1]}
  * 120 ml of blood from a Shadow Naga.^{[1]}
  * 1 Mysticism Spell that has been conceived and verified but not yet created.^{[1]}
  * 1 piece of a spell's notes recorded by another person (can be a copy and come from Warlock, Witch, or other Polymath or Mysticism Magister of the White Tower Pathway, etc.).^{[1]}

### Advancement Ritual

Train at least

  * 50 Erudites^{[1]} or
  * 5 Mid Sequence Beyonders who have independently comprehended the Acting Method.^{[1]}
    * The former is the all-rounder polymath direction. The latter is about which pathways of knowledge one is more proficient in depending on which pathways the Beyonders you taught are from.^{[2]}

## Sequence 3: Cognizer

### Potion Properties

> A brass-colored sanguine liquid that looks as if a miniature spirit world is concealed within.^{[1]}

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> A miniature tree formed by clusters of white, pointed bones, with blood-red brains hanging from its branches and numerous brass-colored eyes opening on its trunk.^{[1]}

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

  * The Eyes from a Greatwood of Written Doom.^{[1]}
  * Brain of an ancient Brass Dragon.^{[1]}

Or
  * A Cognizer Beyonder Characteristic.

Supplementary Ingredients

  * 100 ml of sap from a Greatwood of Written Doom.^{[1]}
  * 100 ml of blood from an ancient Brass Dragon.^{[1]}
  * 1 item frequently used by a Knowledge Magister or Sage.^{[1]}
  * 1 remnant from the annihilation of a Demigod-level Spirit World creature.^{[1]}

### Advancement Ritual

Analyze either the Flesh and Blood or the Beyonder Characteristics of an Angel from the Hermit Pathway or the Paragon Pathway and grasp at least one-third of the knowledge they contain.^{[1]}

  * Or obtain the blessing of one of them.^{[1]}
  * Can be substituted by a corresponding Sealed Artifact^{[3]}

## Sequence 2: Wisdom Angel

### Potion Properties

> A viscous, grayish-white liquid with wrinkles everywhere; multiple brass eyes are concealed within the wrinkles.^{[1]}

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> A grayish-white brain with densely packed brass-colored eyes and a blood-red tinge.^{[1]}

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

  * The Beyonder Characteristic of the "Dragon of Wisdom".^{[1]}

Or
  * The Beyonder Characteristic of the Knowledge Dragon (a certain being from the Second Epoch^{[4]}).^{[1]}

Or
  * A Wisdom Angel Beyonder Characteristic.

Supplementary Ingredients

  * 150ml of blood from either the Dragon of Wisdom, the Knowledge Dragon, or a Wisdom Angel.^{[1]}
  * 10,000 souls of creatures that helped enlighten the concoctor's wisdom after their natural death.^{[1]}
  * 1,000 heartfelt titles of "teacher" or "mentor" from diverse intelligent beings (voice collected and documented in a record).^{[1]}
  * A complete grasp of a certain period of history (has to cover at least 100 years, incorporated into the potion in the form of data).^{[1]}

### Advancement Ritual

Use intellect rather than strength to bring down an angel.^{[1]}

## Sequence 1: Omniscient Eye

### Potion Properties

> A brass-colored liquid that swirls wildly, forming a vortex that resembles a giant eye.^{[1]}

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> A giant brass eye reflecting the illusory White Tower, which seemed to be built from countless books.^{[1]}

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

  * A Omniscient Eye Beyonder Characteristic

Supplementary Ingredients

  * 100 ml of a Dark Angel's blood.^{[1]}
  * 100 ml of a Thunder God's blood.^{[1]}
  * 100 ml of an Author's blood.^{[1]}
  * 100 ml of a White Angel's blood.^{[1]}
  * (Note: All of the ingredients above can be replaced with ones of higher quality)

### Advancement Ritual

Briefly enter a semi-chaotic state, but retain basic consciousness and rationality, and take the potion in that state.^{[1]}

  * The methods include, but are not limited to, the Chaos influence from Demoness, the corruption of the Chaos Sea, and so on.^{[1]}

## Sequence 0: White Tower

### Potion Properties

> Glimmering white light points that spiral into a giant tower reaching to infinity, each light point seems to contain a book.^{[1]}

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

  * The White Tower Uniqueness.
  * Three Omniscient Eye Beyonder Characteristics.

Supplementary Ingredients

  * A tall tower filled with all the knowledge the concoctor possesses, containing relics of a Great Old Ones (relics of a Half Great Old One also count).^{[1]}

### Advancement Ritual

Participate in a calamity brought by an existence beyond Sequence 0 and survive (the conditions are also met by a Sefirah if the ritual performer has the knowledge of the corresponding Sefirah's Great Old One).^{[1]}

  * The principle behind the ritual is the symbolism of "Knowledge leading to Calamity".^{[6]}
