## Sequence 2: Soothsayer

### Potion Properties

> A pure, viscous mass of mercury covered in countless tiny symbols and patterns.^{[1]}

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> Either a scale-covered horn, a wood-grained heart, or a cracked turtle shell, all in a mercury silver color.^{[1]}

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

  * Horn of a Fate Grey Tiger.^{[1]}

Or
  * The Heart of a Scale-covered Treant Elder.^{[1]}

Or
  * A Soothsayer Beyonder Characteristic.

Supplementary Ingredients

  * 100 milliliters of Fate Grey Tiger blood or 100 milliliters of Scale-covered Treant Elder sap or 100 milliliters of a corresponding Angel's blood (choose one).^{[1]}
  * One important prophecy that has been fulfilled (must have been recorded on stone or paper before it came true).^{[1]}
  * The blessing of one of the Seven Lights of the Spirit World.^{[1]}
  * Luck accumulated over more than a hundred years.^{[1]}

### Advancement Ritual

Peer into the River of Fate, uncover an important revelation concerning at least one nation, and ensure that this revelation ultimately manifests exactly as you "saw" it.^{[1]}

## Sequence 1: Snake of Mercury (Giant Serpent, Snake of Fate, Tail-Devouring Serpent of Fate)

### Potion Properties

> A head-to-tail mercury-colored small serpent (more like a flow of water) encased in a semi-transparent shell.^{[1]}

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> A mercury ring with countless cyclic symbols.^{[1]}

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

  * The Crystal Core of a Mercury Phoenix.^{[1]}

Or
  * The Crystal Core of a Mercury Giant Serpent.^{[1]}
  * Or
  * A Snake of Mercury Beyonder Characteristic.

Supplementary Ingredients

  * One's own umbilical cord from birth or eggshell remnants.^{[1]}
  * 9 drops of "water" closely related to oneself extracted via a special method from the River of Fate (each drop is a segment of fate)^{[1]}
  * One item contaminated by the Gray Fog of the Sefirah Castle (can be replaced by the blessing or bestowed item from Sequence 0 Fool^{[4]}).^{[1]}

### Advancement Ritual

* Find a way to extract all of your memories, then, after consuming the potion, infuse them back into yourself.^{[1]}
  * Alternatively, with the aid of a Visionary during the ritual, regress to the state of a fetus, stripped of all memory, and awaken the full breadth of cognition buried deep within your soul and mind.^{[1]}
    * Note 1: Specific term from the Western Continent: Unveiling the Enigma of the Womb.
    * Note 2: Here memory mean ALL memory, conscious and unconscious^{[1]}

## Sequence 0: Wheel of Fortune

### Potion Properties

> A slowly rotating, semi-illusory, semi-substantial wheel filled with mysterious meaning and symbol.^{[1]}

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

  * The Wheel of Fortune Uniqueness.
  * Three Snake of Mercury Beyonder Characteristics.

Supplementary Ingredients

  * Nine beacons from the River of Fate (from the past, present, or future).^{[1]}

### Advancement Ritual

* One must find the right opportunity in the flow of destiny, then take the potion.^{[6]}
  * The right opportunity cannot be divined and cannot be predicted. It can't be locked in any way. One can only rule out certain obstacles and must patiently look for it.^{[6]}
