### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

## Sequence 2: Calamity

### Potion Properties

> Like a tornado compressed and sealed inside a bottle, accompanied by silver-white lightning.^{[2]}

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> A dark, dangerous cloud mass brewing with terror. ^{[2]}

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

  * The Beyonder Characteristic of Queen of Calamity Cohinem (Note: Cohinem is not a pure Sequence 2 Angel of the Tyrant Pathway).^{[2]}

Or
  * The Beyonder Characteristic of the "King of the Deep Sea".^{[2]}

Or
  * A Calamity Beyonder Characteristic.

Supplementary Ingredients

  * An item from the remains of a historic disaster.^{[2]}
  * 600 ml of the King of the Deep Sea's or another Calamity's blood.^{[2]}
  * 3 leaves of the Elven Ancestor Tree.^{[2]}
  * One hair each from a thousand people who died of fear.^{[2]}

### Advancement Ritual

With only your own strength, trigger a natural disaster affecting at least one million people. At the peak of their fear and despair, reveal yourself and halt the disaster—this stage must also rely solely on your own power.^{[2]}

## Sequence 1: Thunder God

### Potion Properties

> Water of thunder formed by intertwining flashes of multicolored lightning.^{[2]}

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> A "scepter" woven of lightning.^{[2]}

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

  * A Thunder God Beyonder Characteristic

Supplementary Ingredients

  * A fragment of lightning from the Astral World.^{[2]}
  * One Depraved nature of a Hanged Man Pathway Angel.^{[2]}
  * One stone from the underground Nightmare Swamp (Born under the influence of the "Chaos Sea", widely present underground—yet approaching it means coming dangerously close to the Chaos Sea, so High Sequence powerhouses cannot personally retrieve it).^{[2]}

### Advancement Ritual

Find and harness lightning to disrupt at least three Sequence 2 Angels' advancement rituals to Sequence 1 (optimal if the angels belong to the Hanged Man, White Tower, Visionary, Sun, or Tyrant Pathways).^{[2]}

  * Interrupting a ritual to Sequence 0 will count as completing it in one go.
  * Interrupting a ritual isn't limited to drinking the potion but any part of the ritual, such as blowing up the marionette town of a Miracle Invoker that want to advance to Attendant of Mysteries.^{[12]}

## Sequence 0: Tyrant

### Potion Properties

> A massive humanoid form condensed from lightning and storms.^{[2]}

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

  * The Tyrant Uniqueness.
  * Three Thunder God Beyonder Characteristics.

Supplementary Ingredients

  * Blood of a God sufficient to drench the whole body.^{[2]}

### Advancement Ritual

* Gather over 100,000 followers. They must submit and believe in the person out of fear.^{[10]}
  * Challenge a Sequence 0 True God and survive.^{[10]}
  * After surviving the challenge, drink the potion under the watch of the fearful followers.^{[10]}
