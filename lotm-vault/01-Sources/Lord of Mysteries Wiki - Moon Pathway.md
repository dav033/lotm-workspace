## Overview

The **Moon Pathway** has powerful healing abilities, as well as the enhancement of biological targets. They can create powerful healing and buffing potions, tame animals, regenerate, and cast darkness spells.

At [Sequence 7](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Moon_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_7:_Vampire "Moon Pathway/Abilities"), Beyonders of this pathway begin to have red eyes, and this trait can be passed on to the next generation. All [Sanguine](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sanguines "Sanguines") have red eyes, which also occur in some of their descendants.[^5]

At High-Sequences, they can manipulate spirituality, master all sorts of moon domain spells, summon things or people from a far distance, increase the negative energy and spirituality of a region to release monsters and evil spirits, manipulate the power of creation, and become sacred goddesses. Their authority also includes **Fertility** and **Proliferation**.

The [Beyonder Characteristics](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder#Beyonder_Characteristic "Beyonder") of this pathway originated from the [Outer Deity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Outer_Deity "Outer Deity") [Mother Goddess of Depravity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Goddess_of_Depravity "Mother Goddess of Depravity").[^6]

The [Above the Sequence](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Above_the_Sequence "Above the Sequence") of the **Moon Pathway** is the [Goddess of Origin](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Goddess_of_Origin "Goddess of Origin"), and the corresponding [Sefirah](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirah "Sefirah") is the [Brood Hive](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Brood_Hive "Brood Hive").

## Corresponding Tarot Card

Further information: [Tarot Cards](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tarot_Cards "Tarot Cards")

![TencentVideo - 18 THE MOON](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/lord-of-the-mystery/images/f/fe/TencentVideo_-_18_THE_MOON.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/120?cb=20250812082606)

The corresponding tarot card of the **Moon Pathway** is **The Moon**.[^9]

**The Moon (XVIII)** is the number eighteenth of Major Arcana. The card represents the life of the imagination apart from the life of the spirit. The Moon is a card of illusion and deception, and therefore often suggests a time when something is not as it appears to be. The Moon can symbolize fear, anxiety, illusions, and a time of change or uncertainty.[^10]

A humanoid creature with a phantom Moon on its back, bright red eyes, and bat wings. "They" look as if "They" were the embodiment of Beauty.[^11]

## Neighboring and Compatible Pathways

**Neighboring Pathways** to the **Moon Pathway** include:

- Other Pathways under the [Goddess of Origin](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Goddess_of_Origin "Goddess of Origin"):
	- [Mother](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Pathway "Mother Pathway")
- Pathways under the [Mother Goddess of Depravity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Goddess_of_Depravity "Mother Goddess of Depravity"):[^1]
	- [Chaos Primogenitor](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chaos_Primogenitor_Pathway "Chaos Primogenitor Pathway")

**Compatible Pathways** to the **Moon Pathway** include, but might not be limited to, the following:

- Pathways under the [Primordial Hunger](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Primordial_Hunger "Primordial Hunger"):[^2]
	- [Tail-Devourer](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tail-Devourer_Pathway "Tail-Devourer Pathway")

**Non-Adjacent Pathways with Hidden Neighbouring Relationship** to the **Moon Pathway** include, but might not be limited to, the following:

- Some Pathways under the [Calamity of Destruction](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Calamity_of_Destruction "Calamity of Destruction"):[^3]
	- [Demoness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Demoness_Pathway "Demoness Pathway")

In addition, some Pathways have a connection to the **Moon Pathway**, but it's unknown if they are Compatible or not:

- Pathways under the [Supernova Dominator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Supernova_Dominator "Supernova Dominator"):[^4]
	- [Condenser](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Condenser_Pathway "Condenser Pathway")

[^1]: Because the [Goddess of Origin](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Goddess_of_Origin "Goddess of Origin") was originally a part of the [Mother Goddess of Depravity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Goddess_of_Depravity "Mother Goddess of Depravity").

[^2]: As the [Primordial Hunger](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Primordial_Hunger "Primordial Hunger") is the symbol of **Convergence**, it is theoretically possible for "His" Pathway to mutually switch to any Pathway.[^12] However, at higher sequences, especially at the [Angel](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Angel "Angel") stage, it is difficult for "His" pathway to switch out, as it is the symbol of convergence and can only devour others. Despite this, one can cleverly use the symbol of separation to switch; the simplest idea is to "die" once, because symbols of termination naturally carry some separation. This temporarily causes one's own characteristics to die or fall dormant, allowing a switch under such conditions. However, this is very dangerous and will most likely result in actual death.[^13]
