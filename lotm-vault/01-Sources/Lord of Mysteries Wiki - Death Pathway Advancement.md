## Sequence 4: Undying

### Potion Properties

> A pale potion that remains perfectly still—no ripples, even when exposed to wind or shaken.^{[2]}

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> Hollow, blackened, and greenish serpent skin.^{[2]}

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

  * Gallbladder of a Decaying Feathered Serpent.^{[2]}
  * Molt of the Immortal Cicada.^{[2]}

Or
  * A Undying Beyonder Characteristic.

Supplementary Ingredients

  * 90 milliliters of bodily fluid from the Decaying Feathered Serpent.^{[2]}
  * 9 pieces of bark from the tree where the Immortal Cicada once resided.^{[2]}
  * One Eternal Darkness Stone.^{[2]}
  * One Pale Red Spider Lily.^{[2]}

### Advancement Ritual

Locate an underground river tainted by death. Hold a genuine funeral by its banks, then bury yourself and the potion there for sixty days. If you fail to survive or retain sufficient sanity after sixty days, the ritual fails.^{[4]}

  * Note 1: Failure invariably results in the birth of a dangerous Corpse King.
  * Note 2: Many Western Continent cultivators misunderstand this ritual's true purpose. Lacking Beyonder characteristics, they instead focus on the "failure" outcome, believing it to be the true success—a chance to refine terrifying Jiangshi.
  * Note 3: Some cultivators have modified this ritual, creating Xian Ascension Techniques akin to Supreme Yin Body Refinement. The "Supreme Yin" essence represents the moon's purity, the water's brilliance, the far north's intent, winter's zenith, and the duality of yin and yang. The flaws and root causes of this method's issues… well, I don't need to elaborate further, do I?

## Sequence 3: Ferryman

### Potion Properties

> Dark and deep; even falling dust sinks swiftly into the liquid and vanishes.^{[2]}

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> A core-like object stained with pale yellow grease, containing a dim, pus-like essence.^{[2]}

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

  * Pus Core of a Styx Servant.^{[2]}

Or
  * The brain of an adult Feathered Serpent.^{[2]}

Or
  * A Ferryman Beyonder Characteristic.

Supplementary Ingredients

  * 90 milliliters of your own blood.^{[2]}
  * 9 drops of pus from a Styx Servant (Or 9 feathers from an adult Feathered Serpent).^{[2]}
  * One undead being from the depths of the Underworld (both Styx Servants and Underworld undead can be summoned via a personal Underworld Gate).^{[2]}
  * A flower blooming from your decayed flesh.^{[2]}

### Advancement Ritual

Step through a self-summoned Underworld Gate, ingest the potion, and die within.^{[4]}

  * Note 1: From then on, the Ferryman becomes part of the Underworld and death itself.
  * Note 2: Before the Phoenix Ancestor established the Underworld and before the First Blasphemy Slate appeared, this ritual didn't exist.
  * Note 3: In the Western Continent, whether it's the Haoli Land, the Yellow Springs, or the Netherworld, each serves the same effect as the Underworld.

## Sequence 2: Death Consul

### Potion Properties

> A deep black liquid formed from the condensed aura of death, radiating an overwhelming presence, with countless numb faces protruding from it.^{[2]}

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> A crown made of black bones.^{[2]}

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

  * Skull of an Ancient Feathered Serpent.^{[2]}

Or
  * A Death Consul Beyonder Characteristic.

Supplementary Ingredients

  * 90 milliliters of fluid from an Ancient Feathered Serpent.^{[2]}
  * A coffin that once held your own body.^{[2]}
  * A crown that once symbolized royal authority.^{[2]}
  * A pale object from the depths of the Underworld.^{[2]}

### Advancement Ritual

Become the ruler of one layer of the Underworld or transform an entire nation into a land of the dead, then anchor the Underworld Gate within it.^{[4]}

  * Note 1: For descendants acknowledged by the God of Death, the first method is straightforward.
  * Note 2: Death Consuls hold authority to judge the dead. In the Western Continent, figures like the Ten Yama Kings and Five Ghost Emperors share similar duties.
  * Note 3: The Western Continent's Netherworld also holds authority over reincarnation.
  * Note 4: Before the Underworld was created by the Phoenix Ancestor, one can only advance to Sequence 2 by luck.^{[5]}

## Sequence 1: Pale Emperor

### Potion Properties

> Absolute paleness, absolute silence—no other color, no other trace.^{[2]}

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> A throne formed from pale bones.^{[2]}

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

  * The left claw of Gregrace.^{[2]}

Or
  * The right eye of Salinger.^{[2]}

Or
  * A Pale Emperor Beyonder Characteristic.

Supplementary Ingredients

  * 9 drops of River Styx water (River Styx is a branch of the River of Eternal Darkness that emerged in the Underworld).^{[2]}
  * 1 Angel corpse (without Beyonder Characteristics).^{[2]}
  * 1 Inverted Mausoleum.^{[2]}

### Advancement Ritual

Ensure your name becomes synonymous with death and the afterlife, remembered and recited by most sentient beings in your domain.^{[4]}

  * Note 1: Due to the Barrier, the Northern and Southern Continents are isolated from the outside world, and are therefore treated as a separate domain in mysticism—otherwise, completing this ritual would be far harder.
    * Without the Barrier, the range required for this ritual is a Star System.^{[6]}
  * Note 2: The same applies to the Western Continent.
  * Note 3: The same applies to the Forsaken Land of the Gods.^{[7]}
  * Note 4: Figures like the Great Emperor of Fengdu and the Buddhist Ksitigarbha possess comparable authority and status there.
  * Note 5: Since the God of Death is still widely regarded as the embodiment of death in the Northern and Southern Continents, "His" descendants can borrow "His" name to shape themselves and complete the ritual. The drawback? They grow increasingly similar to the God of Death—until they become "Him".
