## Symbols, Authorities & Abilities

TRANSLATION NOTE

In the original Chinese text, 规律 (guīlǜ) carries multiple meanings and in this context, can be translated as " **Laws** ", as is done in the English WebNovel translation. However, as the term " **Law** " is already used for a similar word, 律法 (lǜfǎ), and both are treated as distinct authorities, this page will consistently use a single English term (**Principles**) to represent it for clarity and distinction.
- **Laws** (律法, *lǜfǎ*):
	- Refers to codified, enforceable laws—rules established by authority, often carrying judicial or binding force. It aligns with legal systems, decrees, and formal structures of governance.
- **Principles** (规律 *guīlǜ*):
	- Refers to inherent patterns or natural laws—the underlying order of reality that exists independently of authority. It is closer to universal principles or the fundamental workings of the world.
		- In other contexts this term can also denote simpler patterns or regularity, not a strict law, but rather an observable consistency or recurring behavior over time such as everyday routines or behavioral patterns.

On the other hand, the symbolism of **Laws** also uses different Chinese characters, 定律 (dìnglǜ),[^1] which are also the same characters used for the "Laws" in the [Laws of Beyonder Characteristics.](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristics#Laws_of_Beyonder_Characteristics "Beyonder Characteristics")

- **Laws** (定律, *dìnglǜ*)
	- Refers to fixed laws or established principles—a stable, universally applicable rule that does not change.

"They" possess full **Authority** over the [Hermit](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hermit_Pathway) and [Paragon](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Paragon_Pathway) [Pathways](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways") and the corresponding **Symbols**, which include but are not limited to the following:

- [Hermit](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hermit_Pathway): Authorities over **Mysticism Knowledge**, **Hiding**, **Education**, **Mystery Prying**, and **Spirit World Mentor** with the symbol of **Knowledge (Information)**.
- [Paragon](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Paragon_Pathway): Authorities over **Reality Knowledge**, **Principles**, **Education**, **Civilization**, and **Spirit World Mentor** with the symbol of **Knowledge (Information)**.

*Note: A Pathway having certain symbolism doesn't mean at the level of [Above the Sequence](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Above_the_Sequence "Above the Sequence") the symbolism entirely stems from this pathway, the complete symbolism might stem from the entire pathways group or certain pairs from it.*

The **Symbols**, **Authorities**, and **Abilities** "They" possess and display at the [Above the Sequence](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Above_the_Sequence "Above the Sequence") level include:

- **Demon of Knowledge**: "They" are the symbol of **Knowledge**, the embodiment of all **Information**.
	- **Knowledge**: **Knowledge** is discovered, not invented, and all **Knowledge** originally existed within the [Knowledge Moor](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Knowledge_Moor).[^2]
		- As such, the **Demon of Knowledge** doesn't *master* all **Knowledge**; the **Demon of Knowledge** *is* **Knowledge** itself.
		- **Essence of Knowledge**: This is a symbolism inherited from the [Original Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Original_Creator "Original Creator").[^3]
		- **Knowledge** is **Information** hence the "They" represent the **Grand [Unified Theory of Information](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/277053380_How_to_achieve_a_unified_theory_of_information)**.[^4]
			- **Knowledge** is **Essence**, and so are **Principles**.[^5]
		- The **Manufacturing** authority at the [Great Old One](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Old_One "Great Old One") level can turn a [Sefirah](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirah "Sefirah") into a [Sealed Artifact](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sealed_Artifact "Sealed Artifact").[^9]
- **Civilization**: "They" hold the symbol of **Civilization**.[^11]
- **Cosmological Constants**: "Their" symbols include the **Cosmological Constants**.[^12]
- **Laws**: The [Son of Chaos](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Son_of_Chaos "Son of Chaos") and the **Demon of Knowledge** both hold the symbolism of **Laws** (such as the three [Laws of Beyonder Characteristics](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristics#Laws_of_Beyonder_Characteristics "Beyonder Characteristics")). One focuses on the **Laws** and the universe, the other on the **Knowledge** of the **Laws** itself.[^1]

### Countering Symbols, Authorities & Abilities

Certain **Symbols**, **Authorities**, and **Abilities** of other [Great Old Ones](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Old_Ones "Great Old Ones") can counter some of "Their" powers, which include but are not limited to the following:

- [High-Dimensional Overseer](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/High-Dimensional_Overseer):
	- **Dimensionality** partially counters the **Demon of Knowledge** 's symbols.
		- When the [High-Dimensional Overseer](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/High-Dimensional_Overseer "High-Dimensional Overseer") escaped to the highest **Dimension**, the [Celestial Master](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Celestial_Master "Celestial Master") could only have minimal influence on this domain.[^13]
- [Lord of Mysteries](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lord_of_Mysteries):
	- **Errors** counters **Information** and **Knowledge.**
		- **Information** underwent loss, degradation, **Errors**, and chaos in the process of constant backups—this was a fundamental rule of the entire world, a manifestation of **Error**, decay, and increasing entropy. The more times the overall information is backed up, the higher the probability of problems arising, and the greater the latent chaos. If "They" were to revive using backed up **Information**, the more times "They" revive the more hidden **Errors** that might emerge.[^14]
			- If they encountered a **Bug**, "They" would face phenomena like missing avatars or packet loss, but it would only be for a certain period; the **Information** and **Knowledge** would return very soon.[^15]
- [Eternal Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Eternal_Darkness):
	- **Decay** counters **Information.**
		- **Information** underwent loss, **Degradation**, errors, and chaos in the process of constant backups—this was a fundamental rule of the entire world, a manifestation of error, **Decay**, and increasing entropy. The more times the overall information is backed up, the higher the probability of problems arising, and the greater the latent chaos.[^14]
		- **Concealment** mutually restrained **Mystery Prying**.[^16]
		- There is a chance that **Mystery Prying** can see through **Concealment**,[^17] but the result will depend on each other's state, whether there are clues, etc.
- [Monarch of Decay](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Monarch_of_Decay):
	- **Decay** counters **Information.**
		- **Information** underwent loss, **Degradation**, errors, and chaos in the process of constant backups—this was a fundamental rule of the entire world, a manifestation of error, **Decay**, and increasing **Entropy**. The more times the overall information is backed up, the higher the probability of problems arising, and the greater the latent chaos.[^14]
