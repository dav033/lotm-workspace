# Lord of Mysteries Wiki - Sun Pathway Abilities

Community-maintained English ability inventory for the Sun Pathway (Bard through Sun). Compiles novel excerpts and Cuttlefish's official WeChat post "Sun Pathway Abilities" to translate and standardize terminology.

## Summary

Per-Sequence abilities for the Sun Pathway, tracing a progression from a Bard's supportive Singing, through Light Suppliant/Solar High Priest theurgy and Purification/Exorcism, Notary Contract-based Notarization (Authentication/Amplification/Nullification), Priest of Light and Unshadowed direct Purification, Justice Mentor's personal Order/Contract authority, to Lightseeker/White Angel partial Sun/Light/Holiness/Order/Faith authorities, culminating in the Sequence 0 Sun's full Light, Order, Holiness, and Sun authorities.

## Extracted facts

- Sequence 7 Solar High Priest: Sun Halo affects companions within a 20-meter radius; Purification/Exorcism works on the category of Evil (including depravity) and, less potently but still effectively in practice, on the undead since they are incompatible with the real world.
- Sequence 6 Notary: a signed Contract cannot be violated by a Sequence 5 Beyonder and is very difficult even for a Sequence 4 Demigod to break; Notarization (Authentication/Amplification/Nullification) can affect verbal-command abilities, authorities, and traits.
- Sequence 4 Unshadowed: can Purify a Beyonder Characteristic directly, split the highest-Sequence Characteristic out of a whole one, and lower a Beyonder's Sequence by 1 per hour of Purification (6 hours can reduce a Saint to a normal person); Purification strongly resists Parasitism and can melt a Silver Knight's Silver Rapier.
- Sequence 3 Justice Mentor: Justice Halo operates within a 10-kilometer radius, amplifying actions aligned with the Mentor's personal Justice principles and weakening/nullifying violations; Judgment of Justice single-target version is twice as strong and can affect Angels.
- Sequence 2 Lightseeker: Solar Envoy form can evaporate a 2-kilometer-radius Fog of War instantly; Justice Trial inflicts 3-5x Justice Halo damage on violators of a fully understood core Justice principle.
- Sequence 1 White Angel: Holy Kingdom can cover an area as large as Backlund, within which neither Darkness nor Undead (including friendly Wraiths/Spirits) can exist.
- Sequence 0 Sun: Contracts authority can bind even Great Old Ones lacking the corresponding Symbolism once signed; the Sun's Light/destructive potential can, slowly rather than quickly, destroy an entire Solar System.
- Pathway exchange note (shared framing with Fool/Door/Error on the sibling Door Pathway overview page): applies group-wide, not confirmed specific to Sun/Visionary in this source.

## Short quotations

- "The Sun Pathway requires the use of language to establish Order, be it text or spoken." (Sequence 0 Sun, Order)
- "Once a signature is provided as confirmation, even a Sequence 5 Beyonder cannot violate it, and a Sequence 4 Demigod would have great difficulty breaking the Contract." (Notary, Notarization)
- "They can directly Purify a Beyonder, taking 1 hour to lower their Sequence by 1. With 6 hours, they can turn even a Saint to a normal person." (Unshadowed, Purification)
