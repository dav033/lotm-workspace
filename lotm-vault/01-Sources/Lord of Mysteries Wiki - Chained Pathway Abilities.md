## Sequence 9: Prisoner

**Description** [^1] [^2]

The mind is a **Prisoner** of the body, and the body is a **Prisoner** of the world. This refers to the madness that is bound and the **Desire** that is suppressed.

**Traits** [^1] [^2]

- **Physical Enhancement**: The [Beyonders](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonders "Beyonders") of this **Sequence** possess strong bodies and keen senses, often possessing both a staid appearance and a crazy heart within them.
- **Binding**: **Prisoners** are not only those who are locked in prisons, but also those who have their **Spirituality** and **Desires** constrained by reason, body, and the world.

**New Abilities**

## Sequence 8: Lunatic

**Traits** [^2]

- **Binding**: **Lunatics** are **Bound** and restrained in both body and **Soul**, which makes it difficult to obtain useful information on them by just relying on **Spirit Channeling** and **Divination**.

**New Abilities** [^1] [^2]

- **Berserk**: A **Lunatic's** greatest characteristic is to be able to autonomously sacrifice their rationality and let their **Desires** run amok in exchange for strength and enhancements in every aspect.
	- Apart from not having clear thoughts during this period, there isn’t much of a problem. They can even gain stronger resistances towards **Beyonder** powers that can disrupt one’s thoughts and influence their mind.

**Curse** [^1] [^2]

- From this **Sequence** onwards, the **Curse** will gradually appear. Because frequent eruptions of **Desire** causes their mental state to often operate at a nadir, **Lunatics** are more prone to losing control than most **Beyonders**.

## Sequence 7: Werewolf

**Traits** [^5]

- **Physical Enhancement**: Even without transforming, a **Werewolf's** extreme speed will form afterimages, they also possess terrifying strength, agility, and outstanding self-healing abilities.
	- They can also survive a **Pyromaniac's** blazing-white compressed **Fireball**, which possesses explosive power comparable to their **Giant Fireball** spell but even more concentrated.

**New Abilities** [^1] [^2]

- **Werewolf Transformation**: They can turn into a **Werewolf**, growing short, erect hair as fur, turning their nails sharp, long, and firm, like claws, while possessing some **Spell** -like abilities.[^6]
	- While in this form, they will possess terrifying strength, agility, regeneration, and speed.
		- A **Werewolf's** claws and teeth are equivalent to **Beyonder** weapons of the same **Sequence**, capable of destroying thinner steel plates in addition to being poisonous.[^5]
				- They are particularly weak to the theurgical **Spell** -like abilities or **Spells** from the **Sun** domain such as a [Solar High Priest's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sun_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_7:_Solar_High_Priest "Sun Pathway/Abilities") **Holy Light Summoning**.[^7]
				- A **Werewolf** possesses powerful regenerative abilities. This is a trait that will remain even when they exit their **Werewolf Transformation** form.
			- Even if a hideous wound appears on their abdomen and their intestines spill out, they will only need to wash it with water, stuff the intestines back in, and pinch the side of the gap with one's hands. Eventually the wound will heal.
						- A **Werewolf** can regrow their teeth.[^8]
				- They can outrun a gray wolf while chasing it at full speed.
		- **Werewolves** can transform partially in order to utilize some of their capabilities.
		- For example, instead of fully turning into a **Werewolf** to use **Werewolf Infection** or their sharp claws, they could simply manifest those claws on their hands.[^5]
		- **Werewolves** also possess the ability to use some **Spell** -like abilities.
		- **Werewolf Kin**:<sup>⁂</sup> When their venom infiltrates a target's body over a period of time, they can turn the target into a puppet similar to a **Werewolf** for a period of time.
			- The monsters that are created by **Werewolf Kin** are under the **Werewolf's** control and typically possess a very short lifespan.
				- **Dark Horror**: They can make the **Darkness** grow heavier and condense like frost, slowly but firmly seeping into their target's skin, flesh, and bones.
			- This causes an uncontrollable fear to well up within a target's mind, making it hard for them to think straight or move rationally, this also causes a slight impairment.[^5]
				- **Repel Light**: A **Werewolf** is also able to slightly deepen the surrounding **Darkness**, banishing faint light or the crimson **Moonlight**.
- **Anti-Divination**: Regardless of whether or not the [Beyonder](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonders "Beyonders") has transformed, any [Divination](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Divination_Art "Divination Art") or spiritual means can only reveal a figure covered in black hair, making it impossible to see their true appearance.

**Curse**

- Every time the **Crimson Moon** becomes **Full**, they will lose part of their sanity, grow black fur, and their bloodlust and desire for tasting blood would reach its peak.[^2]
	- A **Werewolf** who is always unable to suppress their instinct to kill and their bloodlust during a **Full Moon** will turn increasingly cold and more twisted, losing their humanity in the process.[^2]
		- Certain [Sealed Artifacts](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sealed_Artifact "Sealed Artifact"), particularly those corresponding to a **[Scarlet Scholar](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Moon_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_5:_Scarlet_Scholar "Moon Pathway/Abilities")**, allow them to maintain their rationality in the event of a **Full Moon**. This also applies to the **Curses** of both **Zombies** and **Wraiths**, which also activate during a **Full Moon**.
		- Its essence lies in the environmental **Spirituality** reaching a certain intensity, allowing the call and attraction of [The Mother](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Mother "Great Mother") to manifest.[^9]
		- As long as the **Moon** remains part of [The Mother](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Mother "Great Mother"), even a **Normal Full Moon** can trigger it, though not as strongly as the **Crimson Moon**.
				- In a world without a **Moon**, it depends on whether there are periods where **Spirituality** becomes active.

## Sequence 6: Zombie

**Traits** [^1] [^2]

- **Zombie Transformation**: Advancing to this **Sequence**, they have become a special type of **Zombie**, one that possesses powerful defense, strength, and **Spell** -like capabilities.
	- A **Zombie's** physical body have been greatly enhanced, making their bodies as hard as steel.
		- A revolver shot directly to their head would make them dizzy at best, with them needing to be shot five times in the same spot to break their defenses.[^10]
			- If their defenses get broken, all physical damage isn't lethal if their head isn't destroyed.
						- Their defense is weak to **Purification** powers, or direct attacks to their [Spirit Body](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul#Soul_Body "Soul").[^11]
						- They are also immune to fire, drowning, and many types of magic.
				- Their supernatural healing has been drastically improved, with bone-deep wounds now being able to heal in a short period of time.[^12]
		- **Zombie Strength**: Their strength has been raised significantly from their **Werewolf** state.[^13]
		- They can run faster than a steam locomotive at its peak speed, unleash a critical attack that is stronger then their normal blows as well as ferocious attacks that can tear opponents apart.[^14]
		- **Ice Control**: They have some **Ice** and **Frost** mastery, which is different from a **[Witch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Demoness_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_7:_Witch "Demoness Pathway/Abilities")**.[^13]
		- **Contact-Freeze**:<sup>⁂</sup> Any physical attack from a **Zombie** that makes contact with a target's skin can **Freeze** their body, stiffening their flesh and blood.
				- **Ice Stun**: **Zombies** are able to **Seal** their opponents by encasing them in **Ice**. This also seems to preserve the target's bodily functions as well.[^15]
				- **Ice Wall**:<sup>⁂</sup> **Zombies** are able to directly **Freeze** the water that they come into contact with and form them into **Ice** walls.[^16]
				- **Ice Floor**:<sup>⁂</sup> **Zombies** are able to quickly condense and create slippery layers of **Ice** to affect their target's movement.[^17]
				- They are able to lower the air temperature, causing strong winds to blow around and beautiful dreamlike snowflakes to fall.
		- They also wield some powers over **Decay**, **Rot**, and **Withering** domain.[^14]
		- **Wither Explosion**:<sup>⁂</sup> They can make the air within a mid-range of them explode into countless black wisps that induce **Withering** upon impact.[^10]
				- **Rotting Body Aura**:<sup>⁂</sup> A **Zombie's** body can effuse out a **Rotting** aura to augment each and every one of their attacks with the trait of **Decay**.[^11]
				- **Aura of Decay**:<sup>⁂</sup> **Zombies** are able to emit an aura of **Decay**, able to make the surrounding vegetation visibly wither away.[^11]
				- **Sludge Creation**:<sup>⁂</sup> They are able to create **Rotting** sludge near them to affect their target's movement and strength.[^17]
		- **Zombie Manipulation**: They possess abilities centered around controlling the **Dead**.[^13]
		- They can easily awaken the dead, cultivate puppets, control ghosts, and command them.

**Strengthened Abilities** [^2]

- The traits and abilities of the previous **Sequence** remained unchanged without significant alteration.

**Curse**

- They constantly struggle against the desires in their heart and the strong malice within.[^2]
	- They thirst for the warm blood and fresh meat of human beings.
		- During a **Full Moon**, this state will be particularly serious.
		- When the **Crimson Moon** is **Full**, it causes them great pain.
		- If they don't give up on self-control, they will be in so much pain that they will lose their ability to fight.
				- If they indulge themselves, they will become less and less like a human, and the risk of losing control is very high.
		- The **Zombie's Curse** replaces the **Werewolf's** and **Lunatic's Curse**. They do not exist together, and it’s the same later on.
		- It can be limited with the use of certain medicines such as sedatives, however, they would eventually build up resistance against that specific type of medicine, rendering it ineffective. Sedatives made by [Beyonders](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonders "Beyonders") such as **[Apothecaries](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Moon_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_9:_Apothecary "Moon Pathway/Abilities")** would be more effective.[^18] [^10]

## Sequence 5: Wraith

**Description**

Their bodies are like a fusion of spirit and flesh, allowing them to transform their state into a true phantom and gain corresponding abilities, such as passing through obstacles, hiding in **Mirrors**, and directly attacking the [Soul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul") of their opponent. In addition, unlike ordinary [Wraiths](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Evil_Spirits "Evil Spirits"), even those with **Spirit Vision** cannot detect these phantoms unless they are at a high **Sequence** level.

**Traits**

- **Zombie Transformation**: Apart from certain abilities, most of their capabilities as a **Zombie** remained largely unchanged, without significant improvement.
	- **Zombie Manipulation**: They can still control the undead like with their **Zombie Form** even in their **Wraith Transformation** state
		- **Ice Control**: The **Zombie's Ice Control** at the **Sequence 5** level, which they can use even during **Wraith Transformation**.
		- They can condense thin, translucent, hard to see **Icicles** to attack their enemies, create reflective surfaces to use **Mirror Blink**, extinguish flames, and wither vegetation.[^15]
- **Spirituality**: **Wraiths** are able to directly communicate with the [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World") to gain information.[^19]
	- Their **Divination** and **Spiritual Intuition** will thus be greatly enhanced at this **Sequence**.
		- A **Wraith** is able to sense traces of living beings, and can sense whether a living person has entered a room within half a years time.[^20]
		- Every part of a **Wraith** is material that is rich with **Spirituality**.[^21]

**New Abilities** [^1] [^2] [^22]

- **Wraith Transformation**: They are able to turn themselves into a [Spirit Body](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul#Soul_Body "Soul"), a **Wraith**, thus obtaining all the general corresponding powers and special traits.
	- As a fusion of spirit and flesh, **Wraiths** have tremendous resilience in both their mind and body, being able to survive after they lose their head.[^12]
		- Psychic attacks like an **[Interrogator's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Justiciar_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_7:_Interrogator "Justiciar Pathway/Abilities") Psychic Piercing** typically wouldn't significantly affect them. A **Wraith** can even cause the psychic attack to backfire on their opponent.[^15]
			- Should a **Wraith** be incapacitated, they will lack the corresponding ability to reflect such psychic attacks onto their opponents.
				- They are weak to **Purification** powers such as **Light of Holiness** from a **[Priest of Light](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sun_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_5:_Priest_of_Light "Sun Pathway/Abilities")** and **Lightning Strike** from an **[Ocean Songster](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tyrant_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_5:_Ocean_Songster "Tyrant Pathway/Abilities")** when in their **Spirit Body** state.[^23]
				- **Demon-Hunting** and **Exorcism** -type [Mystical Items](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mystical_Item "Mystical Item"), [Sealed Artifacts](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sealed_Artifact "Sealed Artifact"), and **Beyonder** powers can hurt a **Wraith**, but it would not be fatal or very dangerous to them.[^23]
				- They can go without eating or drinking for about *2 weeks* and are immune to biological poison, regular flames, and many forms of physical attacks.[^23] [^24]
			- Purely physical weapons or firearms without special powers cannot harm **Wraiths**.
				- Their existence as a **Wraith** allows them to avoid instant death when entering the [Underworld](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Underworld "Underworld"), however, lingering for too long will prevent them from reverting to their human form, forever existing as a true **Wraith**.[^25]
			- A **Wraith** is able to temporarily avoid the [Underworld's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Underworld "Underworld") influence by **Possessing** an undead creature.
		- **Wraiths** also possess various auxiliary and support abilities and traits. Most of these abilities aren't unique as they are common in many normal **Wraiths** and spirits.
		- **Spirit World Traversal**: Since they can be considered [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World") creatures to an extent, they can freely enter and exit the [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World") and move freely within it.
			- However, they can't compare to [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World") creatures specialized in this area.[^26]
				- **Wraiths** also possess the characteristics of being able to pass through physical obstacles, **Fly** through the air, and see through the dark, and hide inside **Mirrors**.
		- **Invisibility**: **Wraiths** are able to turn **Invisible**; however, unlike other ordinary **Wraiths** and spirits, this is more effective.
		- They are hard to detect for most **Low** to **Mid-Sequence Beyonders**. Even with **Spirit Vision**, it will be hard to discover them unless one was a **High Sequence Beyonder**.
			- Since they are unable to hide their scent, **[Potions Professors](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Moon_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_6:_Potions_Professor "Moon Pathway/Abilities")** can detect their presence through their discernment of smell.[^27]
						- Since they are unable to hide their **Spirit Body Threads** through **Invisibility**, they can be seen by **[Marionettists](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fool_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_5:_Marionettist "Fool Pathway/Abilities")**.[^28]
		- **Wraith Shriek**: **Wraiths** are able to let out a sharp shriek that can directly attack the opponents' [Spirit Body](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul#Soul_Body "Soul") and induce spiritual damage across a huge area.[^11] [^29] [^15] [^30]
		- Depending on the target's resilience they could either be knocked unconscious, be close to that state, or be interrupted for a moment and stunned as they experience terrible pain.
		- **Wraith Possession**: **Wraiths** have gained the ability to forcefully invade and **Possess** their target's body by overlapping with the opponent's eyes.
		- Even when they are **Possessing** a target, a **Wraith** will not at all be restrained in their capabilities, being able to become **Intangible** and utilize their abilities freely.[^31]
				- They can accelerate **Beyonder Characteristic** separation from a corpse by **Possessing** the target, killing them in that state, and having full control over the body.[^12]
				- Using a simple **Ritual** they gained along with **Possessing** a target's body, a **Wraith** can help a person draw things realistically from said person's memories.[^31]
				- If their target has a strong body, the **Wraith** can make them directly commit suicide by decapitation using the target's own bare hands.[^12]
			- If the target is killed by a physical attack with the **Wraith** still possessing them, the **Wraith** will be unharmed as they are immune.[^12]
						- If the target is killed by some other type of attack that can affect the **Wraith**, they will suffer the corresponding damage.[^32]
		- **Mirror Blink**: **Wraiths** are also able to instantly jump through most things with reflections, such as mirrors, gold coins, ice, and even eyeballs.
		- The maximum range they can **Mirror Blink** is *300 meters*.[^33]
				- This is also known as **Mirror Flash** or **Mirror Jump**.
		- **Wraiths** wield many **Death** -related **Spells** and abilities.[^34]
		- **Vengeful Wraith's Entanglement**: One of their **Death** -related **Spells**. Its function is to summon a large number of special undead creatures to fix the target in place, paralyzing and stiffening them, making it difficult for one to move.
			- The specific effects vary depending on the type of undead creatures summoned.
		- **Resurrection**: Dying may not be the end for them, as they can turn into a true **Wraith** shortly after death.[^35]
		- This transformation can be halted by either destroying or pacifying their soul.
				- This form might enable them to evolve as an **Evil Spirit** in the future.

**Strengthened Abilities**

- **Anti-Divination**: Their **Anti-Divination** traits as a **Werewolf** has been enhanced.
	- Any **Divination** that is directed at them will only show a transparent, seemingly non-existent face or a face shrouded in **Darkness**.

**Curse**

- On nights where the **Crimson Moon** becomes **Full**, a **Wraith** would either need to consume a certain number of human [Souls](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul") or become extremely weak and both in their body and mind.[^2]
	- Choosing the former is equivalent to being on the brink of losing control at any moment.

## Sequence 4: Puppet

**Traits**

- **Physical Enhancement**: Their physical durability has been enhanced.
	- Aside from **Sun Domain** divine **Spells**, they can withstand more damage than a **[Shaman King](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Moon_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_4:_Shaman_King "Moon Pathway/Abilities")**.
- **Spirituality**: A **Puppet** also possesses an enhanced **Spirituality**.
	- **Spiritual Intuition**: They possess a formidable **Spiritual Intuition**.[^36]
- As **Puppets**, they are able to stop unwanted scenes from surfacing in their mind.[^37]
	- This trait might be more effective if they practice **Temperance** rather than **Indulgence**, and if at **Sequence 2** they used a **Cloth Doll** as the base of their advancement ritual.

**New Abilities** [^1]

- **Source of Curses**: **Puppets** are able to transform themselves into mystical puppets and magical paper figurines identical to that of the target. Then through the established connections, **Puppets** are able to use various different methods as a means to **Curse** their targets.[^7]
	- In order to transform themselves into mystical puppets identical to their target, a **Puppet** will first need to form an established connection.
		- To prepare for a **Curse**, [Sharron](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sharron "Sharron") established a connection by **Possessing** [Klarman](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klarman "Klarman").[^7]
		- At **Sequence 4**, they can only hurt the enemy by hurting themselves. Any damage to them will be reflected on the connected target and vice versa.
		- Upon forming a connection, [Sharron](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sharron "Sharron") grabbed the exaggerated crack that had the **Crimson Moon** on her head and pressed down, causing [Klarman](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klarman "Klarman") to let out a blood-curdling scream.[^7]
				- [Sharron](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sharron "Sharron"), in her state as a figurine of [Klarman](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klarman "Klarman"), had yanked some of her messy, black-and-white hair. This in turn caused a tuff of [Klarman's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klarman "Klarman") hair to fall out as well.[^7]
- **Poltergeist**:<sup>⁂</sup> Every **Puppet** can awaken and control all lifeless objects within a certain range. This allows them to utilize their surroundings as weapons and traps.[^28] [^32] [^38]
	- They can control ownerless [Sealed Artifacts](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sealed_Artifacts "Sealed Artifacts") and [Mystical Items](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mystical_Item "Mystical Item") that are not held or controlled by anyone physically or through **Spirituality**.
		- The [General of the Pupil-less Eye](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/General_of_the_Pupil-less_Eye "General of the Pupil-less Eye") controlled the [Vision of White](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Vision_of_White "Vision of White") immediately after the [Sealed Artifact](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sealed_Artifact "Sealed Artifact") was thrown by [Emlyn White](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Emlyn_White "Emlyn White"), forming a pillar of light that enveloped [Sharron](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sharron "Sharron").[^7]
		- **Puppets** are able to transform inanimate objects in their surroundings into weapons they can shoot out at high speeds.
		- The [General of the Pupil-less Eye](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/General_of_the_Pupil-less_Eye "General of the Pupil-less Eye") let out a scream, shattering the surrounding windows that turned into bullets that shot towards the floating [Sharron](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sharron "Sharron").[^7]
		- They can control objects that are in contact with living beings, including **Demigods**.
		- [Shanks](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Shanks "Shanks") tightened [Klein Moretti's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") clothes as if a bear were giving him a hug, almost shattering his ribs and preventing movement as paper figurines covered his face to prevent him from breathing.[^28]
				- To counteract the actions of [Georgina Augustus](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Georgina_Augustus "Georgina Augustus"), [Zatwen](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Zatwen "Zatwen") first **Stole** her thoughts using a [Error Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway "Error Pathway") [Sealed Artifact](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sealed_Artifact "Sealed Artifact") before restraining her tightly with her own gown.[^36]
				- [Zatwen](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Zatwen "Zatwen") and [Mahmosi](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mahmosi "Mahmosi") controlled the guns and machines of [MI9](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/MI9 "MI9"), including those held by soldiers, to their advantage against [Celt](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Celt "Celt") and [Georgina Augustus](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Georgina_Augustus "Georgina Augustus").[^39] [^36]
- **Performance**: A **Puppet** is able to **Perform** roles such as a **Prisoner**, **Lunatic**, **Werewolf**, **Zombie**, and **Wraith**, elevating those **Beyonder** powers and traits to a weak **Sequence 4** with divinity.
	- For example, a **Wraith** will be able to **Possess** a [Sealed Artifact](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sealed_Artifact "Sealed Artifact").
- **Objectification**: They can transform into a true **Puppet**, forcefully restraining their inner madness and **Desires**, but they cannot influence or interfere with the outside world in return.
	- A **Puppet** can only protect themself through pre-prepared, triggered or area of effect **Curses** and other controlled **Puppets**.
		- The **Objectification Beyonder** power can also be used on others to temporarily control their madness or **Seal** it.
		- However, at this **Sequence** the other party must be willing and there must be physical contact.

**Strengthened Abilities**

- **Evil Spirit Transformation**: Their original **Wraith Transformation** form and it's powers and attributes have entered a qualitative change, allowing them to turn into an [Evil Spirit](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Evil_Spirits "Evil Spirits").[^36]
- **Binding**: At this stage, they can restrain the tendency of [Convergence](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonders#Law_of_Beyonder_Characteristics_Convergence "Beyonders").[^40]
	- However, it is only at **Sequence 0** where it can be at the same degree as the [Door Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Door_Pathway "Door Pathway").

**Mythical Creature Form**

- As **Demigods** at **Sequence 4** who have obtained partial **Godhood**, they are able to manifest their incomplete **Mythical Creature Form** if they disregard any of the consequences and become an incomplete collection of various **Curses** which are both invisible and intangible.
	- The outward appearance of their incomplete **Mythical Creature Form** depends on their own self-awareness and choices made during their advancement. It acts as a base to bind and stabilize the various **Curses** resulting from their disassembly process.[^41]
		- A **Puppet** of the Temperance Faction of the [Rose School of Thought](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Rose_School_of_Thought "Rose School of Thought") would therefore have the outward appearance of a **Demigod** -level mummy.[^41]

## Sequence 3: Disciple of Silence (Cursed Artifact)

**New Abilities** [^1]

- **Silent Curse Brewing**:<sup>⁂</sup> With prolonged silence, a **Disciple of Silence** can brew the most potent **Curse**, rendering a target powerless to resist.

**Strengthened Abilities**

- **Evil Spirit Transformation**: As a **Cursed Artifact**, they can **Possess** objects.
- **Source of Curses**: There has been a qualitative change to this ability.[^7]
	- A **Disciple of Silence** using **Source of Curses** doesn't need to hurt themselves to affect the target but could do so anyways to enhance a **Curse's** effectiveness.
		- [Sharron](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sharron "Sharron") opted to pluck her eye reflecting [Mirror Roselle Gustav](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mirror_Roselle_Gustav "Mirror Roselle Gustav") to enhance a **Curse** that had the capability of banishing the latter deep within the [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World"). Furthermore, the target would be subjected to constant attacks from dangerous creatures in the [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World") while being banished.[^42]
		- They are also capable of using **Source of Curses** to achieve effects like banishment, fixation, and sleep through different methods or corresponding media.
		- [Sharron](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sharron "Sharron") transformed herself into a dim and stiff doll, then encased herself in layers of **Ice** which simultaneously emerged around [Oxyto](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Oxyto "Oxyto") 's child, fixing it in place within an **Ice Cage**.[^43]
- **Transfiguration Curse**: Both a **Curse** and qualitative change to **Objectification**, they can emit a silent scream that transforms their target into a harmless creature, losing most of their traits and **Beyonder** powers for a certain time period.[^44]
	- At this level, a **Transfiguration Curse** can only affect a single entity at a time.
		- Entities contained within a target are treated as part of the same whole, thus turning both into a single animal.[^39]
				- Its effects are symmetrical, transferring across connected bodies, such as a **[Demoness of Unaging](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Demoness_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_3:_Unaging_\(Demoness_of_Unaging\) "Demoness Pathway/Abilities")** and their **Mirror Selves**.[^44]
			- A dormant **Mirror Person** is exempt from this, however, because their true body is reduced to a defenseless animal, their **Resurrection** arrangements become vulnerable.
		- Both living and non-living objects can be affected by this **Curse**.[^39]
		- Traits or **Beyonder** powers that don't get stripped from the corresponding **Beyonders** include:
		- **[Madame](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chaos_Primogenitor_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_4:_Madame_\(Evil_Overlord,_Benevolent_Overlord\) "Chaos Primogenitor Pathway/Abilities")**: The trait of **New Life**, which they can use to corrupt themselves and be reborn.[^43]
				- **[Chaos Hunter](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Justiciar_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_3:_Chaos_Hunter "Justiciar Pathway/Abilities")**: The **Sword of Judgment**, which can return their original appearance.[^39]
				- **[Demoness of Unaging](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Demoness_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_3:_Unaging_\(Demoness_of_Unaging\) "Demoness Pathway/Abilities")**: The trait of **Resurrection** through their dormant **Mirror Self**.[^44]
				- **[Discerner](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Visionary_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_2:_Discerner "Visionary Pathway/Abilities")**: Through usage of a **Psychological Cue**, a **Discerner** is able to believe themselves to still be powerful.[^45]
- They gain improvements to other abilities in the previous **Sequence**.

**Mythical Creature Form**

- As a **Sequence 3 Beyonder**, they can respond to prayers within a certain range.

## Sequence 2: Ancient Bane

**Traits**

- **Dwelling Space**: **Ancient Banes** need a **Dwelling Place**, which is a manifestation of "Their" **Restraint** and also the best way to find and destroy what "They" **Possess**.[^1]
	- If the object "They" are bound to is not destroyed, "They" are immortal and indestructible.[^46]

**New Abilities** [^1]

- **Bane**: As a type of **Deviant**, "They" can cause all sorts of negative influences on a target that approach or witness "Them" while "They" are not in "Their" [Mythical Creature Form](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mythical_Creature_Form "Mythical Creature Form").
	- This can range from causing the affected targets to have very strong **Evil** thoughts, having their values changed, **Desires** explode, or even losing control on the spot.
- **Spirit Influence**:<sup>⁂</sup> "They" possess the ability to directly influence and attack **Spirits**.
	- "They" can open "Their" mouth and inhale, forming a cold breeze that forcibly pulls the enemy's **Spirit**, making it impossible to maintain a state of hiding.[^28]

**Authorities** [^1]

- **Curses**: "They" gain partial authority over **Curses**.
	- **Source of Curses**: "Their" [Mythical Creature Form](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mythical_Creature_Form "Mythical Creature Form") manifests as the vessel used in "Their" [Advancement](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chained_Pathway/Advancement#Sequence_2:_Ancient_Bane "Chained Pathway/Advancement").
		- If "They" understand a target's core information, "They" can create **Paper Figurines**, **Puppets**, or other mediums to **Curse** the other party from afar, or even cause them to die suddenly.
			- [Suah's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Suah "Suah") **Historical Projection** created a doll that looked like [Klein Moretti](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti"), using the latter's **Worm of Spirit** to create a connection between the two, which [Zaratul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Zaratul "Zaratul") used to isolate the main body from all his **Marionettes** and prevent him from entering [Sefirah Castle](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirah_Castle "Sefirah Castle").[^47]
				- The patterns, designs, ornaments, and every detail on the vessel are manifestations of different **Curses**, and any living being that sees it would suffer the corresponding **Curse**.
			- The surrounding environment will also be affected by "Their" [Mythical Creature Form](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mythical_Creature_Form "Mythical Creature Form"), distorted by nature and becoming tainted with various weakened **Curses**.
		- **Transfiguration Curse**: This is "Their" **Transfiguration Curse** now at the [Angel](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Angels "Angels") -level.
		- "They" no longer need to emit a silent scream to affect a target. Now, merely any target that is reflected in "Their" eyes can be **Cursed** and transformed accordingly.[^48]
				- The limbs left behind by a **Beyonder** after them undergoing a **Transfiguration** contain a portion of their **Beyonder Characteristics** that couldn’t return to the main body
				- Multiple targets at a time can be affected by a single **Transfiguration Curse**, and there is almost no limit for the duration a target can be in the **Cursed** state.[^44] [^49]
			- Apart from putting up resistance from someone at the same level, one can use the correct method to remove it. No **Curse** is irreversible. There are always exploitable **Loopholes**.[^49] Otherwise, the **Transfiguration Curse** cannot be dispelled.
		- "They" can create **Curses** rooted in one's hatred, anger, and grudges.[^50]
		- [Reinette Tinekerr](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Reinette_Tinekerr "Reinette Tinekerr"), who had created a **Curse** whose root has lied in the anger, hatred, and "Her" grudge that had emerged after "She" suffered an attack, used it in order to repel a piece of "distorted" wood as well as stagger and stun **Abomination** [Suah](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Suah "Suah") for a second.[^50]

**Strengthened Abilities**

- **Evil Spirit Transformation**: The capabilities regarding "Their" [Evil Spirit](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Evil_Spirits "Evil Spirits") form has been enhanced.
	- **Resurrection**: With the necessary and prior preparations done beforehand, "They" can **Resurrect** in the [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World") in the form of a [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World") creature.[^51]
		- Having been a **Wraith** makes this much simpler compared to other [Pathways](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways").[^52]
				- When **Resurrecting** this way, "They" will be left with an incomplete body.
			- [Reinette Tinekerr](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Reinette_Tinekerr "Reinette Tinekerr"), after "Her" fall in battle against [Suah](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Suah "Suah") and other foes, **Resurrected** in the [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World") and now attempts to complete "Her" incomplete body.[^51]
- "They" gain improvements to other abilities of the previous **Sequence**.

**Mythical Creature Form**

- "They" become a complete **Mythical Creature**. "Their" true **Mythical Creature Form** is that of a collection of various **Curses** which are both invisible and intangible.[^41]
	- The outward manifestation of "Their" complete **Mythical Creature Form** depends on "Their" own self-awareness and the choices made during "Their" [Advancement](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chained_Pathway/Advancement#Sequence_2:_Ancient_Bane "Chained Pathway/Advancement"), which uses an ancient, evil item that can bear [Angel](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Angel "Angel") -level power which is used to **Bind** the aggregate **Curses**.
		- [Reinette Tinekerr](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Reinette_Tinekerr "Reinette Tinekerr") 's **Mythical Creature Form** is a castle-sized doll with blood-red eyes, dressed in a black gothic dress with countless mysterious symbols and sinister vines.[^45]
				- For those like [Suah](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Suah "Suah") and [Tirié](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tiri%C3%A9 "Tirié") who have not completed the corresponding rituals, "They" exhibit outward forms that are amplifications of "Their" **Beyonder Characteristics**.
		- By relying on "Their" inherent vessel and corresponding symbol, an **Ancient Bane** can enter the [Historical Void](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Historical_Void "Historical Void"); however, "They" are not able to surpass the era in which the vessel appeared.[^53]
		- Within the parts "They" can trace back to, an **Ancient Bane** is able to counter the **History** of the [Fool Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fool_Pathway "Fool Pathway") to some extent. This is achieved through **Curses**.

## Sequence 1: Abomination

**Literal Translation**

**Divine Abomination**

**Authorities** [^1]

- **Deviants**: "They" gain partial authority over **Deviants**.
	- **Transformation**: The **Curse** of an **Abomination** can not only deform the surrounding creatures, but also transform them into **Werewolves**, **Zombies**, **Puppets** and other **Deviant** species.
		- However, these **Deviant Puppets** can only be maintained for a short time and will soon be devoured by madness, turning into corpses or flesh tainted by the curse.
		- "They" can display "Their" true form as **Abominations**, gaining the corresponding abilities.
		- In "Their" true form, "Their" arms will lengthen; it's surface dark and sticky with a strange liquid flowing from it. Pale skulls, eyeballs with obvious blood vessels, tongues with fangs, and all sorts of other strange-shaped things will grow out from the strange liquid.
			- "They" can emit an eerie roar that pierces the spirit, causing all surrounding creatures to tremble, be somewhat stiff, and suddenly go blank in the mind, briefly losing all thoughts.
				- **Abomination** [Suah's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Suah "Suah") roar had caused the surrounding individuals near "Him" to suddenly pause in movement, their minds going blank and thoughts lost.[^50]
						- "Their" true form in this state, not "Their" **Mythical Creature Form**, inherently carries the effects of **Madness**, **Evil**, **Distortion**, **Chaos**, **Hatred**, **Indulgence**, and **Curse**.
				- This will cause effects equivalent to revealing "Their" **Mythical Creature Form** in "Their" area as well as a strong inclination for *extreme madness* and **Evil**.[^54]
								- One could say that "Their" true forms were a product of "Their" self-perception combined with "Their" complete **Mythical Creature Form.**
						- In "Their" true form, "They" are able to extend "Their" own deformed arms into the sky and into the ground in order to **Seal** the surroundings.[^32]
- **Curses**: "They" gain more power over this authority.
	- "They" can create **Curses** normally undetectable even by **Saints** and that can hit the main body through any connection, making the target to suffer an unexpected and sudden death.[^38]
		- In an attempt to suppress [Klein Moretti](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti"), a **Historical Projection** of **Abomination** [Suah](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Suah "Suah") which was controlled by [Zaratul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Zaratul "Zaratul") **Cursed** a **Marionette** that "He" had controlled.[^47]
				- **Abomination** [Suah](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Suah "Suah") had formed a **Curse** that hit [Klein Moretti's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") main body (which was in the [Historical Void](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Historical_Void "Historical Void")) through a **Historical Projection** that he was operating.
		- "They" can cause the surrounding **Spirituality** to deviate, seemingly forming layer upon layers of barriers in an attempt to restrain the target in place and restrict their movements.
		- This is the effect of a **Curse** upon the environment.
			- While [Suah](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Suah "Suah") restricted [Klein's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") body and [Soul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul"), the surroundings also formed a barrier as the scarlet flames [Klein](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") came out of from **Flaming Jump** bound him to the ground.[^50]
						- Near [Suah's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Suah "Suah") area, the surrounding **Spirituality** had transformed into layers of barriers that restrained and restricted [Klein](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") and [Reinette Tinekerr's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Reinette_Tinekerr "Reinette Tinekerr") every action.[^32] [^50]
				- Similarly, "They" can cause the vicinity to boil, covering it in viscous black liquid that erodes upwards into the void, disrupting the overlap between [Reality](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Physical_World "Physical World") and the [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World").
			- However, this doesn't immediately disable abilities that use the [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World") like **Teleportation**, needing time to completely take effect.[^50]
		- Even if a target wasn't directly attacked by "Them" or is a main target of "Their" attacks, "They" can still cause them to face constant erosion and **Curses** without their knowledge.
		- This affects and makes one's **Spirituality** "frozen" slightly, seemingly restraining the body and [Soul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul"). As the degree of harmony between their body and [Soul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul") gradually decreases, their body will gradually be a prison for the spirit, making their movements more stiff and heavy.
			- [Suah](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Suah "Suah") used this on [Klein](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti"), causing the harmony of his body and [Soul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul") to decrease, making his actions and movements be more rigid, slow, and heavy

**Strengthened Abilities**

- **Evil Abomination**: "They" can rapidly spread feelings of extreme **Evil** and madness, instantly dry up the surroundings, and cause others' consciousness to be infected with madness.
	- The effect of causing others' consciousness be infected with madness will either result in sudden death, mutation, or attacks on their own people, making them temporarily unable to take effective countermeasures.

**Mythical Creature Form**

- If an **Abomination** has not completed "Their" previous **Sequence** advancement rituals, "They" would exhibit a **Mythical Creature Form** of a grotesque tree-like shape coated in viscous black liquid.[^41]
	- Creatures who are affected by "Their" **Godhood** without a sufficient level or protection will be mutated, turning into **Werewolves** or **Zombies**.[^32]

## Sequence 0: Chained

**Description** [^1]

Because of the Symbolism of **Binding** and **Unity**, the authorities of the **Chained** are highly concentrated, mainly focused on **Curses**, **Binding** (**Temperance**), **Deviants**, and **Objects**.

**Symbolism**

- "They" can apply surface-level symbolism through "Their" authority over **Curses**.[^53]

**Authorities** [^1]

- **Curses**: At **Sequence 0**, "Their" authority over **Curses** initially manifests through symbolic applications, allowing "Them" to cause targets to unknowingly suffer **Curses**.
	- "They" can complete all kinds of **Curses**, and it is very difficult for "Them" to be **Cursed**.
		- Many symbolic connections cannot affect "Them" unless a strong and direct link exists.
		- "They" can slowly burden "Their" enemies with "Their" own predicament.[^32]
		- Compared to the **Chained**, the **[Abyss'](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Abyss_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Abyss "Abyss Pathway/Abilities") Curses** are primarily in the domains of **Spirit** and **Mind**.[^55]
- **Deviants**: The **Chained** is the King of **Deviants**, capable of transforming into all kinds of **Deviants** that can exist only for a short period of time, controlling their corresponding abilities.
	- **Deviants** are monsters formed by the negative effects of **Beyonder Characteristics**, including [Devils](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Abyss_Pathway "Abyss Pathway") and those who have lost control on other [Pathways](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways").
		- All **Deviants** are naturally suppressed when facing the **Chained**, and those at low to mid **Sequences** may directly become "Their" loyal servants.
		- The **Chained** can allow every type of **Deviant** to pray to "Them" in the simplest manner and receive "Their" response and assistance.
		- Using a roar formed from superimposed **Ancient Hermes** words that exceeded human comprehension, [Suah](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Suah "Suah") indirectly received a response from the [Chained God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chained_God "Chained God") who descended with "His" projection.[^32]
- **Binding** (**Temperance**): "They" can **Restrain** or **Seal** the various tendencies of **Madness**, **Desires**, **Objects**, creatures, and **Beyonder Characteristics**.
	- In this aspect, if something does not reach the level of **Sealing**, the **Chained** is stronger than the **[Door](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Door_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Door "Door Pathway/Abilities")**; however, the **[Door](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Door_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Door "Door Pathway/Abilities")** surpasses the **Chained** within the **Sealing** domain.
		- With proper use of **Temperance**, a [King of Angels](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/King_of_Angels "King of Angels") of this [Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathway "Pathway") can make the and *3 **Abomination Beyonder Characteristics*** exist simultaneously within "Their" body without triggering a qualitative change.
			- This prevents immediate loss of control or death on the spot.[^56]
				- The **Chained** can sever the connection between [Reality](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Reality "Reality") and the [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World"), **Restraining Beyonder** powers that rely on direct usage of the [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World").[^32]
		- Conversely, "They" can cause others to lose **Restraint** and abandon **Temperance**, which represents the **Indulgence** authority concentrated within **Binding**.
- **Objects**: "They" can control all **Objects**, whether sentient or not.
	- Since an unclaimed [Sefirah](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirah "Sefirah") can be considered an **Object**, the **Chained** can establish a connection with it and indirectly influence it through "Their" authority over **Objects**.[^57]
- **Thorn Paradise**: This is "Their" **Divine Kingdom**, a place filled with various **Curses** bound to different **Objects**, as well as numerous **Deviants** that exist as statues that can be brought to life.
