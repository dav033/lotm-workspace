## Full Title

TRANSLATION NOTE

" **The Anarchy** " (失序 者, *Shīxù zhě*) contains the same Chinese word as the " **Disorder** " used in "Nation of Disorder " (失序 之国, *Shīxù zhī guó*). This word means "the loss of order", and is a different word compared to the "Disorder" (混乱, *Hǔnluàn*) used in "the bottom-layered rule of the world is Disorder and Madness" and the "Endless Disorder" symbolism of the [Key of Light](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Key_of_Light "Key of Light").

## Symbols, Authorities & Abilities

"They" possess full **Authority** over the [Black Emperor](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Black_Emperor_Pathway) and [Justiciar](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Justiciar_Pathway) [Pathways](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways") and the corresponding **Symbols**, which include but are not limited to the following:

- [Black Emperor](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Black_Emperor_Pathway): Authorities over **Autocrat** and **Disorder**.
- [Justiciar](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Justiciar_Pathway): Authorities over **Order**, **Judgement**, **Balance**, and **Disorder**.

The **Symbols**, **Authorities**, and **Abilities** "They" possess and display at the [Above the Sequence](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Above_the_Sequence "Above the Sequence") level include:

- **Order**: The symbolism of **Order**, established with the birth of the [Son of Chaos](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Son_of_Chaos "Son of Chaos").[^3] The [Justiciar](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Justiciar_Pathway) possesses the corresponding core authority of **Order**, with subordinate authorities of **Rules**, **Laws**, and **Principles**. "He" can modify the bottom-layer **Rules** of the world to some extent, establish new non-bottom-layered **Rules** that stay permanent, as well as remove non-bottom-layered **Rules**.[^4]
- **Shadow of Order**: The symbolism of **Shadow of Order**, naturally emerged from the **Order** established after the birth of the [Son of Chaos](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Son_of_Chaos "Son of Chaos").[^3] The [Black Emperor](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Black_Emperor_Pathway) possesses the core authority of **Autocrat**, which is under the **Shadow of Order** symbolism.[^6]
	- **Distortion**: A subordinate authority of **Autocrat**.[^7]

### Countering Symbols, Authorities & Abilities

Certain **Symbols**, **Authorities**, and **Abilities** of other [Great Old Ones](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Old_Ones "Great Old Ones") can counter some of "Their" powers, which include but are not limited to the following:

- [High-Dimensional Overseer](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/High-Dimensional_Overseer):
	- When the [High-Dimensional Overseer](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/High-Dimensional_Overseer "High-Dimensional Overseer") escaped to the highest **Dimension**, the [Celestial Thearch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Celestial_Thearch "Celestial Thearch") could only have minimal influence on this domain.[^9]
- [God Almighty](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/God_Almighty):
	- **Notarization** mutually counteracts **Distortion**.
		- **Distortion** could **Distort** **Contracts** to a certain degree, but not fully change it.[^9]
