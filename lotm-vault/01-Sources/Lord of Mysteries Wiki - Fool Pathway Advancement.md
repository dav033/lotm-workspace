## Sequence 3: Scholar of Yore

### Potion Properties

* A substance that exists simultaneously as both a liquid and a gas, dark red in color, nearly the size of a baby's head.^{[30]}
  * On consumption, the potion would transform and extend into their mouth, making them feel like devouring a dark red glow. The transparent maggots would swarm back into their body, tearing down the parts of the potion and swallowing them.^{[30]}

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> Dark-red burning flames eyeballs. A peculiar heart composed of wisps of white mist.^{[31]}

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

  * Hound of Fulgrim (also known as Sefirah Castle Keeper) pair of eyes.^{[29]}
  * 1 Demonic Wolf of Fog's Transformed Heart.^{[29]}

Or
  * A Scholar of Yore Beyonder Characteristic.

Supplementary Ingredients

  * 100 milliliters of Hound of Fulgrim Blood.^{[29]}
  * 30 grams of White Frost Crystal of Demonic Wolf of Fog.^{[29]}
  * A large amount of Real Ancient Historical Records.^{[29]}

### Advancement Ritual

Be separated from reality for at least three hundred years and consume the potion after one becomes history and doesn't belong to the present era.^{[29]}

## Sequence 1: Attendant of Mysteries

### Potion Properties

> A potion that was as dark as the night sea and light as air.^{[42]}

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> A translucent black velvet "curtain", madly absorbing all the light around it. The "curtain" became more and more transparent and illusory until it reached a point where one couldn't see it with the naked eye.^{[43]}^{[44]}^{[45]}

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

  * A Attendant of Mysteries Beyonder Characteristic

Supplementary Ingredients

  * Nine Spirit World Specialties.^{[46]}

### Advancement Ritual

* Build a town consisting only of marionettes, and design a trajectory of fate for every marionette. By letting them interact with each other, they would act as a sufficiently real-life painting and create a corresponding area in the spirit world.
  * The larger the town, the more the marionettes involved, the more detailed the daily lives are, and the more realistic and extensiveness the different fates are, the better the ritual's effects would be.^{[46]}
