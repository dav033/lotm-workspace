# Web Research - Fool and Error Authorities - 2026-07-29

## Method

- Existing exact-URL source notes [[Cuttlefish WeChat - Completed Fool Authority]] and [[Cuttlefish WeChat - Marauder Pathway Abilities]] remain the direct `official/high` evidence.
- Indexed archive text was used to check terminology, scope, and stated limits. Fandom ability pages were used only as secondary indexes to novel manifestations and chapter references.
- The official WebNovel listing verifies the authorized English work and author, but paid chapter text was not independently inspected during this pass.

## Fool Findings

- **Fooling** and **Reassembly/Grafting** are the two core Fool Authorities. Fooling makes an existing falsehood real; Reassembly combines previously unrelated entities or concepts into a real but inconceivable configuration.
- **Miracles** belongs under Inconceivable/Bizarreness. **History** combines King of Space-Time and Bizarreness, but cannot rewrite an established past.
- The official post names **Transformation**, not an unrestricted Pathway-level Authority called Change. Transformation belongs under the group-level Change symbolism.
- **Anchor of Destiny** belongs under Beacon of Destiny. Its holder's Fate is difficult to alter and its words can become future nodes, but fulfillment can be distorted.
- Novel-indexed manifestations include Historical Void borrowing, projection and hiding; Miracle Invoker wishes and resurrection; Attendant-level conceptual Grafting; and Klein's final-battle use of Grafting plus Fooling in Chapters 1378 and 1384.

## Error Findings

- **Deceit** has two Chinese terms with overlapping English renderings: `欺瞒` for the lower Mentor of Deceit ability and `欺诈` for the higher Authority/symbolism. The lower form misleads judgments and can deceive natural rules; Sequence 0 has the full Authority.
- **Time** begins at Sequence 1 as a weakened Authority from King of Space-Time and becomes a Sequence 0 Authority. It slows, stops, accelerates, or briefly reverses Time and interacts with Theft of duration or lifespan.
- **Error** is not unrestricted rule breaking. The author post gives five activation routes: sufficient similarity, an existing connection, a logical contradiction, an incomplete rule, or an indirectly created Erroneous environment.
- Novel-indexed manifestations include Amon's rule and avatar exploits during the final battle, especially Chapter 1386; temporal slowing and acceleration; Pallez reversing stolen lifespan; and high-Sequence Deceit redirecting mystical rules.

## Boundaries

- Grafting does not create its components from nothing; Fooling may be needed to make a conceptual graft alter the environment as if it were physically real.
- Fooling Time disrupts sequence and manifestation; Error Time directly slows, stops, accelerates, or briefly reverses temporal flow.
- Fool History manipulates established History temporarily; it does not produce unrestricted time travel or rewrite the past.
- Fool Fate effects are retained as a partial jurisdiction through Fooling, Miracle Invoker probability interference, and Anchor of Destiny. They are not equivalent to the Wheel of Fortune Pathway's broad Fate Authority.
