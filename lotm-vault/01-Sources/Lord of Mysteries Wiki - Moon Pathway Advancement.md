## Sequence 7: Vampire

![Potion - Moon Sequence 7 - Vampire](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/lord-of-the-mystery/images/5/51/Potion_-_Moon_Sequence_7_-_Vampire.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/200?cb=20260331053255)

Potion - Moon Sequence 7 - Vampire

### Potion Properties

> A cup of bright red "blood" brimming with vitality, with a strong fishy taste.[^2]

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

- An item the size of a fist. It was completely red in color and resembled a heart. It was expanding and contracting slightly, and its surface was translucent. The holder could vaguely see a liquid flowing inside it.[^2]
- [Primordial Moon](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Primordial_Moon "Primordial Moon") Believer's MUTATED: Its surface was nearly black in color and had indistinct human-faced patterns. Two transparent objects which resembled hearts—one red with vitality, and the other nearly black.[^7]

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- Blood Essence of a corresponding **[Sequence 7](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Moon_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_7:_Vampire) [Sanguine](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sanguines "Sanguines")** (from this point onward, the main material of the sequence can be replaced by the "legacy" of the corresponding **[Sanguine](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sanguines "Sanguines")** or **Artificial [Vampire](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Moon_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_7:_Vampire)**).[^6] [^2]
Or

- Heart of the Bloodthirsty Beast.[^2]
- Head of the Red-headed Bat.[^2]
- **Note**: These other main materials are quite difficult to collect because they have almost been exhausted by the [Sanguines](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sanguines "Sanguines") or **Artificial Vampire** forces.

Or

- **A [Vampire](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Moon_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_7:_Vampire)** [Beyonder Characteristic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristic "Beyonder Characteristic").

Supplementary Ingredients

- 100 milliliters of blood of the Bloodthirsty Beast.[^2]
- 3 teeth of the Red-headed Bat.[^2]
- **Note**: The above two materials can be replaced with the corresponding parts of **[Sanguines](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sanguines "Sanguines")** or **Artificial Vampires**.
- 10 hairs of the Long-eared Rabbit.[^2]
- 10 drops of Osmanthus Flower pure water.[^2]

## Sequence 2: Life-Giver

![Potion - Moon Sequence 2 - Life-Giver](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/lord-of-the-mystery/images/1/12/Potion_-_Moon_Sequence_2_-_Life-Giver.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/200?cb=20260331053413)

Potion - Moon Sequence 2 - Life-Giver

- Overview
- Formula
- Ritual
- Backlash
- Acting Method

### Potion Properties

> Clear, clean liquid with blood-colored bubbles floating around.[^2]

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> Resembling a womb, covered with a blood-stained translucent membrane and filled with red bubbles, each of which seems to contain a life.[^2]

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- The Beyonder characteristic of a Sanguine Duke or the "legacy" of an Artificial Vampire of this Sequence.[^2]

Or

- **A [Life-Giver](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Moon_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_2:_Life-Giver)** [Beyonder Characteristic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristic "Beyonder Characteristic").

Supplementary Ingredients

- 1 amulet crafted by one's mother.[^2]
	- If the mother has passed away, it can be replaced by an Envisioned product or a Historical Projection. A conceptual mother or a mother in a broad sense can also meet the requirements.[^2]
- 10 hairs from a blood offspring created by oneself.[^2]
- This isn't limited to children of oneself, as creation doesn't necessarily mean giving birth.[^13]
- 100 milliliters of blood from a Sanguine Duke or an Artificial vampire of this Sequence.[^2]
- 1 Oak Seed.[^2]

### Advancement Ritual

Divide oneself into five parts, destroy and seal oneself in different places, then before completely dying, have the part of the body containing the heart take the potion.[^2]

- Note: There are two main difficulties in this ritual. One is how to ensure that one does not die immediately in that state, and the other is how to take the potion in a sealed state.[^2]
- Theoretically, cutting fingers is considered as dividing the body, but considering that one of the ritual's purposes is to lower one's life force and enter a near-death, sealed state to restrain the growth, the reproduction urge, and the spread of madness, doing this essentially nullifies the ritual's effect.[^14]

### Ritual Essence

One of the ritual's purposes is to lower one's life force and enter a near-death, sealed state to restrain the growth, the reproduction urge, and the spread of madness.[^14]
