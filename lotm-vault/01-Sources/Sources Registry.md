# Sources Registry — Lord of the Mysteries

This registry defines sources the wiki may use. An unlisted source stays in `00-Inbox/` until reviewed.

## Decision rule

| Reliability | Wiki use |
| --- | --- |
| High | Establishes canon. Cite a chapter, edition, or specific announcement. |
| Medium | Helps locate information. Verify against a high-reliability source before marking canon. |
| Low | Leads, discussion, or theory only. Never use as fact. |

## Conflict protocol

1. Original novel overrides every other source.
2. Licensed edition overrides wiki or social media, but may differ in translation.
3. An official adaptation can change or omit facts; record the difference, do not rewrite canon.
4. If no high-reliability evidence exists, use the evidence-appropriate `interpretation` or `theory` status and record the uncertainty in [[Pending Review]].

## Spoiler control

- Record volume, chapter, or episode for every piece of evidence.
- Fandom and Reddit can reveal the entire series; do not open without an appropriate spoiler level.
