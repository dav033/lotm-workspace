**Zaratul** was first introduced in [Roselle's Diary](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Roselle%27s_Diary "Roselle's Diary"). "He" is the leader of the [Secret Order](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Secret_Order "Secret Order"). "He" suddenly disappeared after his possible attempt at advancing to Sequence 1.

## Appearance

He usually appears as an old man wearing a black robe with a hood. "His" eyes are as dark as the water without light. The white beard on "His" mouth is long and dense.

## History

### Lord of Mysteries

At some point during the reign of the first [Solomon Empire](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Solomon_Empire "Solomon Empire"), Medici noted that **Zaratul** was still digesting Sequence 4 potion.[^2]

In [Roselle's Diary](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Roselle%27s_Diary "Roselle's Diary"), "He" claimed to be a sequence 2. He provided [Roselle](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Roselle_Gustav "Roselle Gustav") some knowledge on Beyonders and fourth epoch history, and in exchange had asked Roselle to steal [Antigonus Family's Notebook](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Antigonus_Family%27s_Notebook "Antigonus Family's Notebook") from the [Church of the God of Steam and Machinery](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Church_of_the_God_of_Steam_and_Machinery "Church of the God of Steam and Machinery").

Klein has inferred from the diary and other information that **Zaratul** probably went mad. This was later confirmed that "His" soul was ripped into 2 parts during his advancement from sequence 2 to sequence 1.

> Very good. Beyonders from the Seer pathway need to know how to use their brain and not always think of fighting.
> 
> **Zaratul**  
> On [Lord of Mysteries](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lord_of_Mysteries_\(Novel\) "Lord of Mysteries (Novel)"), Chapter 841

Klein met half of "His" soul in the [Foggy Town](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hornacis_Mountain_Range#Foggy_Town "Hornacis Mountain Range") [^3] and was tricked into helping this half escape the town and reconnect with his main body. **Zaratul** got rid of "His" madness once "He" fused the other half.

Later, he went to [Backlund](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Backlund "Backlund") to look for Klein. Klein suspected it was "Him" who warned the Rose School of Thought about the joint operation between Sharron and the Sanguine.[^4]

"He" volunteered to help [George Augustus III](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/George_Augustus_III "George Augustus III") 's advancement ritual to trap and hunt Gehrman Sparrow, and even brought [Suah](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Suah "Suah") of the [Rose School of Thought](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Rose_School_of_Thought "Rose School of Thought") to cooperate with "Him".[^5]

"He" also raided Klein's marionette town, [Utopia](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Utopia "Utopia"), to stop Klein from advancing to sequence 1. However, "He" failed as Klein got captured by [Adam](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Adam "Adam") first inside "His" Divine Kingdom, [Corpse Cathedral](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Corpse_Cathedral "Corpse Cathedral"). Later, "He" suffered a counterattack from Klein, but "He" managed to escape using a pre-planned travel method.

Adam revealed that **Zaratul** would rather help [Amon](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Amon "Amon") because if Amon became the [Lord of Mysteries](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lord_of_Mysteries "Lord of Mysteries") "He" can still retain being a [King of Angels](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Kings_of_Angels "Kings of Angels") instead of being demoted or dying if Klein ascends to being The Fool.[^6]

In "His" last fight against Klein, "He" was betrayed by his own historical projection of Knowledge Emperor Roselle and Klein directly killed "Him" using [Law of Beyonder Characteristic Convergence](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder#Law_of_Beyonder_Characteristic_Convergence "Beyonder") then devoured "His" Beyonder Characteristic, preventing "His" resurrection within the Void of History.[^7] However, Klein suspects **Zaratul** has an additional Miracle Invoker Beyonder characteristic and is in a process of resurrection. [Antoine](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Antoine_\(Secret_Order\) "Antoine (Secret Order)"), a member of the [Secret Order](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Secret_Order "Secret Order") in [Intis Republic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Intis_Republic "Intis Republic"), heard **Zaratul's** raving which related to an ancient castle called [Dylan](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Castle_Dylan "Castle Dylan").[^8]

"He" is currently in the process of resurrecting as of 1352.

> Orville… Dylan… Orville… Dylan…
> 
> Zaratul's raving  
> On [Lord of Mysteries](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lord_of_Mysteries_\(Novel\) "Lord of Mysteries (Novel)"), Chapter 1361

### Circle of Inevitability

As of 1358, **Zaratul** has come back from the dead and become the [Celestial Worthy](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Celestial_Worthy "Celestial Worthy") 's attendant. The target vessel is the original body of the [transmigrator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Transmigrator "Transmigrator") [Loki](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Loki "Loki") of the [April Fool's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/April_Fool%27s "April Fool's"). Through "His" vessel, "He" gains extra Beyonder characteristics of sequence 9 to 5 [Fool Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fool_Pathway "Fool Pathway") blessed by the [Celestial Worthy](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Celestial_Worthy "Celestial Worthy").

The Angel was resurrected inside [Loki](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Loki "Loki") after the latter's resurrection during the [Sea Prayer Ritual](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sea_Prayer_Ritual "Sea Prayer Ritual") of Fall 1358. But "He" hasn't fully taken over [Loki](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Loki "Loki") 's body, and [Loki](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Loki "Loki") hasn't died completely. This has turned them into an unstable, stitched-together monster in mind and soul.[^9]

Possessing [Loki](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Loki "Loki"), **Zaratul** fought against [The Magician](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fors_Wall "Fors Wall"), [The Hermit](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Cattleya "Cattleya") and [Judgement](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Xio_Derecha "Xio Derecha"), but had to escape in the end.[^10]

During the [battle](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_of_Primordial_Demoness "Death of Primordial Demoness") against the [Primordial Demoness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Primordial_Demoness "Primordial Demoness"), Zaratul lay in wait hoping for an opportunity. However, his plans were thwarted and he was parasitized and killed by Amon before he could interfere.[^11]

## Trivia

- In the side story " [In Modern Day](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/In_Modern_Day_\(Side_Story\) "In Modern Day (Side Story)")," "He" is known as **Mr. Zaratulstra**. "He" is a VIP guest of the Intis Group and represents the MISTER Company. "His" modern name alludes to [Zoroaster (Zarathustra)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zoroaster) of the [Avesta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avesta).[^13]
