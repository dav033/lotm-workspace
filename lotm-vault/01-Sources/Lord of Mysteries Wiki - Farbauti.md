The **Devil Monarch Farbauti** is a [Survivor](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Survivors "Survivors") from the [Modern Era](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pre-Epoch "Pre-Epoch") and one of the [Ancient Gods](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ancient_Gods "Ancient Gods") from the [Second Epoch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Second_Epoch "Second Epoch"). "He" was originally an [Ancient God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ancient_God "Ancient God") of the [Abyss Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Abyss_Pathway) and the [Wheel of Fortune Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Wheel_of_Fortune_Pathway), and later a [True God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/True_God "True God") of the [Abyss Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Abyss_Pathway).[^1] In addition, "He" has a Sequence 1 [Abomination](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chained_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_1:_Abomination) Beyonder characteristic that was later given to [Naboredisley](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Naboredisley "Naboredisley"), "His" other identity.[^2]

"He" was the only surviving [Ancient God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ancient_God "Ancient God") from the [Second Epoch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Second_Epoch "Second Epoch") in "Their" original form. In later epochs, "He" was known as the **Dark Side of the Universe** and resided in the [Abyss](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Abyss "Abyss").[^3] "He" was worshiped by the [Blood Sanctify Sect](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Blood_Sanctify_Sect "Blood Sanctify Sect") and [Devil Families](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/List_of_Families "List of Families").

Later on, "He" was corrupted alongside the [Chained God Tolzna](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chained_God "Chained God") by the [Mother Tree of Desire](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Tree_of_Desire "Mother Tree of Desire"). "He" restored lucidness during the [Fifth Epoch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fifth_Epoch "Fifth Epoch") before [The Apocalypse](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/The_Apocalypse "The Apocalypse").[^4]

As a Sequence 0, **Farbauti** possesses an unknown [Honorific Name](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Honorific_Name "Honorific Name").

## Pathway Composition as an Ancient God

"His" Pathway composition is as follows:[^6]

- [Abyss](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Abyss_Pathway): Uniqueness + 3 Sequence 1.
- [Wheel of Fortune](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Wheel_of_Fortune_Pathway): Uniqueness.
- [Chained](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chained_Pathway): 1 Sequence 1.

According to [Madam Magician](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fors_Wall "Fors Wall"), "He" was born with a Sequence 1 [Abomination](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chained_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_1:_Abomination) Beyonder characteristic from a neighboring pathway, making "His" state one of the best among the eight [Ancient Gods](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ancient_Gods "Ancient Gods"). "He" had also become the only [Ancient God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ancient_God "Ancient God") alive in "Their" original form. In fact, if **Farbauti** were to gather the ingredients of the [Abyss Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Abyss_Pathway "Abyss Pathway") as a Sequence 1 [Abomination](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chained_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_1:_Abomination) and complete the deity-ascension ritual to advance, "He" would be equivalent to a [True God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Gods "Gods") now—a stronger [True God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Gods "Gods"). "He" wouldn't be an [Ancient God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ancient_Gods "Ancient Gods") described by words like madness, cruelty, violence, and bloodlust. However, as the Devil Monarch, even if "He" had done so, "He" would inevitably exhibit the characteristics of the [Abyss Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Abyss_Pathway), experiencing strong influence from this pathway. Therefore, **Farbauti** is also one of the two or three most unhinged [Ancient Gods](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ancient_Gods "Ancient Gods"). This doesn't contradict "His" better state.

Later, "He" sacrificed the [Wheel of Fortune Uniqueness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniquenesses#Wheel_of_Fortune_Pathway) to escape from the [Ancient Sun God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ancient_Sun_God "Ancient Sun God") in the [Second Epoch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Second_Epoch "Second Epoch").[^7] Afterwards, because "His" remaining characteristics were only the neighboring [Abyss](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Abyss_Pathway) and [Abomination](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chained_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_1:_Abomination), even without an [Apotheosis Ritual](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Apotheosis_Ritual "Apotheosis Ritual"), "He" would be considered a crazy [True God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/True_God "True God").[^1]

At some point, "He" gave the Sequence 1 [Abomination](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chained_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_1:_Abomination) Beyonder characteristic to [Naboredisley](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Naboredisley "Naboredisley"), "His" other identity.[^2]

## History

According to the [Myth of Creation](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/First_Epoch "First Epoch"), "He" and the rest of the devils were formed from the stomach, intestines, and the other evil parts of the body of the [Original Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Original_Creator "Original Creator").[^8]

The [Blood Sanctify Sect](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Blood_Sanctify_Sect "Blood Sanctify Sect") worship "Him" as the **Dark Side of The Universe**, they believe believe that "He" is the ruler of the [Abyss](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Abyss "Abyss") and that "He" is the devastator of the world who would corrupt and degenerate the real world's entire universe.[^9]

In truth, "He" was a remnant of the [Pre-Epoch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pre-Epoch "Pre-Epoch"), a survivor after the [Original Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Original_Creator "Original Creator") woke up. When "He" recovered fragments of thought and memory, "He" was already a great devil. Those fragments were soon drowned out by madness and blood lust, only returning near the end of the [First Epoch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/First_Epoch "First Epoch").[^10] "He" became one of the eight [Ancient Gods](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ancient_Gods "Ancient Gods") of the [Second Epoch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Second_Epoch "Second Epoch") afterwards.[^11]

Among the [Ancient Gods](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ancient_Gods "Ancient Gods"), the **Devil Monarch Farbauti** and the [Annihilation Demonic Wolf Flegrea](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Flegrea "Flegrea") were independent beings who wanted to subvert all order and corrupt all living beings.[^12]

According to records of the [City of Silver](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/City_of_Silver "City of Silver"), "He", along with the other [Ancient Gods](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ancient_Gods "Ancient Gods") all lost "Their" godhood after the [Ancient Sun God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ancient_Sun_God "Ancient Sun God") reclaimed "His" authorities from the [Ancient Gods](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ancient_Gods "Ancient Gods").[^11] However, the truth is that during the [Dawn Era](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Dawn_Era "Dawn Era") in the [Second Epoch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Second_Epoch "Second Epoch"), "He" was gravely wounded by the [Ancient Sun God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ancient_Sun_God "Ancient Sun God"). By using the majority of the demon race and "His" own power (including the [Wheel of Fortune Uniqueness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniquenesses#Wheel_of_Fortune_Pathway) [^7]) as sacrificial tribute, "He" converted the Islands of Beginning into the [Abyss](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Abyss "Abyss"), sealing it away from the outside world, and using this method to evade the radiance of [The Omniscient and Omnipotent](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ancient_Sun_God "Ancient Sun God").[^13]

Only the [Forsaken Land of the Gods](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Forsaken_Land_of_the_Gods "Forsaken Land of the Gods") still remembers the [Dark Side of the Universe](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Side_of_the_Universe "Dark Side of the Universe"). The rest of the world has already forgotten "His" name and only knows the **Dark Side of the Universe** as the evil god of the [Abyss Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Abyss_Pathway "Abyss Pathway").

At some point in the [Fourth Epoch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Epoch "Fourth Epoch"), "He" was corrupted by the [Mother Tree of Desire](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Tree_of_Desire "Mother Tree of Desire") since "His" pathway originally belonged to "Her".[^14] [^15] For some reason, the [Underworld Daoist](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Underworld_Daoist "Underworld Daoist") agreed to "His" request for help, causing the [Naboredisley](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Naboredisley "Naboredisley") 's corpse that contained the [Abomination](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chained_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_1:_Abomination) characteristic to slumber in [a special dream](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Dream_Festival "Dream Festival"), while being sealed by [Death](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death "Death") 's mask, thus free from the harrassment of the [Mother Tree of Desire](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Tree_of_Desire "Mother Tree of Desire").[^16]

During [Klein's Apotheosis Ritual](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein%27s_Apotheosis_Ritual "Klein's Apotheosis Ritual"), [Amon](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Amon "Amon") placed the first [Blasphemy Slate](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Blasphemy_Slate "Blasphemy Slate") into the [Fog Sea](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fog_Sea "Fog Sea"), near the [Abyss](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Abyss "Abyss") ' entrance. This, combined with the enhanced seal brought about by the corresponding authority of [Door](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Door_Pathway "Door Pathway") and half of the [Lord of the Astral World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/God_Almighty "God Almighty") 's control towards the world barrier prevents the [Outer Deities](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Outer_Deities "Outer Deities") from being able to have "Their" powers penetrate inside for a short period. Without the influence of the [Outer Deities](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Outer_Deities "Outer Deities"), the **Dark Side of the Universe** and the [Chained God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chained_God "Chained God") did not wish to interfere in the battle of gods. "They" would rather seize this opportunity to escape "Their" restraints.[^17]

As per the content of the book *"A Summary of Rumors and Hearsay Before the Cataclysm"* in the [New City of Silver](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/City_of_Silver "City of Silver") 's library, the name **Devil Monarch Farbauti** was mentioned:

After the **Devil Monarch Farbauti** led the Devils back to the [Abyss](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Abyss "Abyss"), occasional rumors persisted of Demons enticing humans.  
  
“The Demons operated under the following names:  
  
“Beelbubli, Almos, Samael, Lilatan, and [Naboredisley](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Naboredisley "Naboredisley").  
  
“A high-ranking Demon Hunter speculated that these names conceal the aliases of **Devil Monarch Farbauti** …

— [Circle of Inevitability](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Circle_of_Inevitability_\(Novel\) "Circle of Inevitability (Novel)"), Chapter 631

The alias of the **Devil Monarch Farbauti** is [Naboredisley](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Naboredisley "Naboredisley"). The [Abomination](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chained_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_1:_Abomination) form chose to sleep and be a corpse in the dream tomb of [Tizamo Town](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tizamo_Town "Tizamo Town"). This will allow "His" corpse to enjoy peace and tranquility, safe from the [Mother Tree of Desire](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Tree_of_Desire "Mother Tree of Desire") 's harassment. "He" chose to continue in this dead state until after [The Apocalypse](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/The_Apocalypse "The Apocalypse").[^2]

In [The Day of Many Disasters](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/The_Day_of_Many_Disasters "The Day of Many Disasters"), because the [Primordial God Almighty](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Primordial_God_Almighty "Primordial God Almighty") nearly resurrected, the **Devil Monarch Farbauti** experienced the revival of the malicious consciousness and frenzied spirit of the [Primordial God Almighty](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Primordial_God_Almighty "Primordial God Almighty") within "Him", because the [Primordial God Almighty](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Primordial_God_Almighty "Primordial God Almighty") had once accommodated the [Tenebrous World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tenebrous_World "Tenebrous World") in the past. To a certain extent, it balanced the corruption of the [Mother Tree of Desire](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Tree_of_Desire "Mother Tree of Desire"), allowing "Him" to regain a semblance of peace. However, since the revival of the [Primordial God Almighty](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Primordial_God_Almighty "Primordial God Almighty") was short-lived, with "His" consciousness retreating to the [Chaos Sea](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chaos_Sea "Chaos Sea"). "He" left only "His" malicious spirit, preventing a true balance. **Farbauti** remained unable to fully escape the control of the [Mother Tree of Desire](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Tree_of_Desire "Mother Tree of Desire"). However, "His" condition had relatively improved—"He" no longer relied on [Lilith](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lilith "Lilith") 's aid.[^10]

[Naboredisley](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Naboredisley "Naboredisley") and [Lumian Lee](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee") had agreed to collaborate. The plan was that, when [Lumian](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee") next performed a deep secret deed and projected his spirit and consciousness into the [City of Calamity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/City_of_Calamity), he would take **Farbauti** 's mental imprint with him. This will help **Farbauti** establish a preliminary connection with the [Tenebrous World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tenebrous_World "Tenebrous World"). This is possible because the [Primordial God Almighty](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Primordial_God_Almighty "Primordial God Almighty") had once accommodated both the [City of Calamity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/City_of_Calamity "City of Calamity") and the [Tenebrous World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tenebrous_World), so the two [Sefirot](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirot "Sefirot") had mutually corrupted each other. Through the [Tenebrous World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tenebrous_World) 's corruption within the [City of Calamity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/City_of_Calamity), **Farbauti** can establish an initial connection. This would also benefit [Lumian](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee") since **Farbauti** 's mental imprint, fused with the [Tenebrous World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tenebrous_World "Tenebrous World") 's corruption, would mitigate related risks when [Lumian](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee") accommodate the [City of Calamity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/City_of_Calamity), reducing potential dangers to some extent. If this plan succeeds, [Naboredisley](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Naboredisley "Naboredisley") 's sealed [Abomination](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chained_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_1:_Abomination) avatar will die, and [Lumian](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee") may claim its resulting Beyonder characteristic as his own.[^10]

Before [The Apocalypse](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/The_Apocalypse "The Apocalypse"), when [Lumian](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee") projected his spirit and consciousness into the [City of Calamity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/City_of_Calamity), **Farbauti** directly infiltrated his mind and spirit, and neutralized the [Tenebrous World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tenebrous_World) 's corruption within the [City of Calamity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/City_of_Calamity).[^4]

When the fight against the [Outer Deities](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Outer_Deities "Outer Deities") first started, the [Master of the Shadow Cottage](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Master_of_the_Shadow_Cottage "Master of the Shadow Cottage") met **Farbauti** in the [Tenebrous World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tenebrous_World). Both beings were unwilling to accept the fate of being devoured by the [Great Old Dominators](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Old_Dominators "Great Old Dominators") and decided to cooperate.[^18] "They" fused together with the [Tenebrous World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tenebrous_World "Tenebrous World") to form a quasi- [Father of Devils](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Father_of_Devils), the [Pitch-Black Fiend](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pitch-Black_Fiend "Pitch-Black Fiend").[^5]
