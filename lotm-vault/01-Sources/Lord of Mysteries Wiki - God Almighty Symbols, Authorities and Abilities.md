**God Almighty** (上帝, *Shàngdì*, lit. 'Supreme Thearch') is the title of the [Great Old One](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Old_One "Great Old One") of the [Visionary](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Visionary_Pathway), [Sun](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sun_Pathway), [Tyrant](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tyrant_Pathway), [White Tower](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/White_Tower_Pathway), and [Hanged Man](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hanged_Man_Pathway) [Pathways](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways").

This [Great Old One](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Old_One "Great Old One") is one of the three [Pillars](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pillars "Pillars").[^1]

TRANSLATION NOTE

" **Maker** " (造物主: Zàowùzhǔ) (literal translation: "Lord Maker of All Things") is the same Chinese word as the " **Creator** " used in "Original Creator " (最初 造物主: Zuìchū zàowùzhǔ) and "True Creator " (真实 造物主: Zhēnshí zàowùzhǔ).  
  
" **Creator** " (创造者: Chuàngzào zhě) (literal translation: "The One who Creates") is a different Chinese word from the **"Creator"** used in "Original Creator" and "True Creator".

## Symbols, Authorities & Abilities

"They" possess full authority over the [Visionary](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Visionary_Pathway), [Sun](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sun_Pathway), [Tyrant](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tyrant_Pathway), [White Tower](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/White_Tower_Pathway), and [Hanged Man](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hanged_Man_Pathway) [Pathways](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways") and the corresponding **Symbols**, which include but are not limited to the following:[^2]

- [Visionary](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Visionary_Pathway): Authorities over **Mind**, **Discernment**, **Dream**, **Loss of Control**, and **Envisioning**.[^3]
- [Sun](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sun_Pathway): Authorities over **Holiness**, **Light**, **Order**, and **Sun** with the symbols of **Energy** and **Inextinguishable Light**.
- [Tyrant](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tyrant_Pathway): Authorities over **Matter**, **Tyrant**, **Water**, **Calamities**, **Wind**, and **Thunder**.
- [White Tower](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/White_Tower_Pathway): Authorities over **Omniscience**, **Wisdom**, **Principles**, **Astral World Ladder**, **Creation**, and **Calamity**.
- [Hanged Man](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hanged_Man_Pathway): Partial authorities over **Darkness**, **Corruption**, **Shadows**, **Mutation**, and **Degeneration** with the symbols of **Sin-Bearing** and **Sacrifice**.[^4]

The **God Almighty's** symbols are both reality and abstraction-oriented.[^5]

**Note**: A [Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathway "Pathway") having certain symbolism doesn't mean at the level of [Above the Sequence](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Above_the_Sequence "Above the Sequence") that it entirely stems from that [Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways"). It might stem from the entire group or certain parts from it.

The **Symbols**, **Authorities**, and **Abilities** "They" possess and display at the [Above the Sequence](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Above_the_Sequence "Above the Sequence") level include:

- **Lord of The Astral World**: The **God Almighty's** most critical symbolism is "Their" symbolism as a [Pillar](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pillar "Pillar").[^6] "They" symbolize and are the controller of the **[Astral World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Astral_World "Astral World")**, one of the three layers of the world.
	- A **Pillar** is slightly stronger but has no fundamental difference compared to the other [Great Old Ones](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Old_Ones "Great Old Ones"). In general, by relying on the unique symbolism of a **Pillar**, "They" will have the status and power to contend with two other [Great Old Ones](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Old_Ones "Great Old Ones") at most.[^7] [^8]
		- Unlike normal [Great Old Ones](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Old_Ones "Great Old Ones") who can have "Their" will shattered—like how the [Sefirot](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirot "Sefirot") had "Their" wills shattered by the [Celestial Worthy of Heaven and Earth for Blessings](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Celestial_Worthy_of_Heaven_and_Earth_for_Blessings "Celestial Worthy of Heaven and Earth for Blessings") and the [Primordial God Almighty](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Primordial_God_Almighty "Primordial God Almighty") in the [First Epoch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/First_Epoch "First Epoch") [^9] —the will of a [Pillar](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pillar "Pillar") cannot be shattered, even by another [Pillar](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pillar "Pillar").[^10]
		- Among the five [Pathways](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways") of the **God Almighty**:
		- The [Visionary](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Visionary_Pathway) is the cornerstone of the **Lord of The Astral World** symbolism.[^11]
				- The [White Tower](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/White_Tower_Pathway) holds the **Astral World Ladder** authority.[^12]
		- A **Quasi-God Almighty** can disrupt the [Astral World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Astral_World "Astral World") by causing abstract concepts and symbolic markers there to descend into **Chaos**. This can delay and disorient other [Great Old Ones](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Old_Ones "Great Old Ones") and prevent "Them" from utilizing the [Astral World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Astral_World "Astral World") to travel for *2-3 seconds*.[^13]
		- The **God Almighty** can partially **Seal** the [Astral World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Astral_World "Astral World") to interfere with certain symbolism.[^14]
		- The [Symbolism](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Symbolism "Symbolism") itself cannot be completely **Sealed**, otherwise a single fight would lead to cosmic apocalypse.[^15]
		- A **Half-God Almighty** can light up the entire [Astral World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Astral_World "Astral World") inside the [Astral Barrier](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Astral_Barrier "Astral Barrier"). When the [Astral World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Astral_World "Astral World") is lit up, there will be no secrets that could be **Concealed**, and it can even reveal the invisible [Astral Barrier](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Astral_Barrier "Astral Barrier") that was formed by the [Original Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Original_Creator "Original Creator").[^16]
		- The light in the [Astral World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Astral_World "Astral World") can also help others regain "Their" senses from the effect of the **[Fool](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fool_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Fool) 's** **Blind Stupidity**.[^17]
		- **Astral Barrier**: The **Lord of the Astral World** can create **Barriers** related to the **Astral World**, and possesses a certain level of control over the **[Astral Barrier](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Astral_Barrier "Astral Barrier")** from the [Original Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Original_Creator "Original Creator").
		- The **Half-Lord of the Astral World's** control over the [world barrier](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Astral_Barrier "Astral Barrier"), combined with the enhanced seal brought about by the corresponding authority of [Door](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Door_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Door) and the usage of the [First Blasphemy Slate](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/First_Blasphemy_Slate "First Blasphemy Slate"), can prevent any [Outer Deity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Outer_Deity "Outer Deity") from being able to have "Their" powers penetrate inside for a short period in year 1352 of the [Fifth Epoch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fifth_Epoch "Fifth Epoch").[^18]
				- A **Half-God Almighty** can create a multi-layered prism-like spots of light that act as a **Barrier**, which can resist the attacks of a [Sequence 0: Sun](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sun_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Sun), [Sequence 0: Tyrant](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tyrant_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Tyrant), and [Sequence 0: White Tower](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/White_Tower_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_White_Tower) at the same time, while trapping "Them" in "Their" respective [Divine Kingdom](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Divine_Kingdom "Divine Kingdom") in the [Astral World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Astral_World "Astral World").[^19]
			- Within the **Barrier**, there is a faint swirling light that repeatedly takes form, changing the critical points of the structure.
				- This can actively resist a [White Tower](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/White_Tower_Pathway) 's investigative and calculative abilities, preventing "Them" from finding weaknesses in the **Barrier**.
						- The **Barrier** can rapidly produce new layers to replace destroyed ones; those layers can withstand a [Sun](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sun_Pathway) 's attacks.
				- This is regardless of how much **Energy** is being released or how many flares of super high-temperature flames are being created.
						- It can ripple like an illusory ocean that seems to contain all colors, solidifying the surrounding environment.
				- This will cause the light spots carrying the **Storm** to slow down uncontrollably, which allows it to resist a [Tyrant](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tyrant_Pathway) 's violent "wave" that can destroy a planet.
				- The death of a **Quasi-God Almighty** can seal off a region of space from the outside world, making the sealed region a separate domain in mysticism. For example, the [Ancient Sun God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ancient_Sun_God "Ancient Sun God") 's death sealed off the **Eastern Continent** and turned it into the [Forsaken Land of the Gods](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Forsaken_Land_of_the_Gods "Forsaken Land of the Gods").
			- [Advancement Rituals](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Advancement_Ritual "Advancement Ritual") like that of the [Pale Emperor](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway/Advancement#Sequence_1:_Pale_Emperor) normally requires a range of a [Star System](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Star_System "Star System"),[^20] but because of the seal, the [Forsaken Land of the Gods](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Forsaken_Land_of_the_Gods "Forsaken Land of the Gods") is sufficient.[^21]
						- The seal prevented other [True Gods](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/True_Gods "True Gods") from accessing the [Forsaken Land of the Gods](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Forsaken_Land_of_the_Gods "Forsaken Land of the Gods") for the duration of the [Fourth Epoch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Epoch "Fourth Epoch") and [Fifth Epoch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fifth_Epoch "Fifth Epoch"). Only the [True Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/True_Creator "True Creator"), as the negative personality born from the corpse and spirit of the [Ancient Sun God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ancient_Sun_God "Ancient Sun God"), can control the entrance into and out of the [Forsaken Land of the Gods](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Forsaken_Land_of_the_Gods "Forsaken Land of the Gods").
						- [Sefirot](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirot "Sefirot") such as the [Sefirah Castle](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirah_Castle "Sefirah Castle") can get pass the seal. For example, if the owner of [Sefirah Castle](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirah_Castle "Sefirah Castle") is already inside the [Forsaken Land of the Gods](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Forsaken_Land_of_the_Gods "Forsaken Land of the Gods"), an item from the outside world can be taken into [Sefirah Castle](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirah_Castle "Sefirah Castle") and transported into the [Forsaken Land of the Gods](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Forsaken_Land_of_the_Gods "Forsaken Land of the Gods").\[?\]
- **Primordium** and **Chaos**: The [Primordial God Almighty](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Primordial_God_Almighty "Primordial God Almighty") is one of the split-off personas of the [Original Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Original_Creator "Original Creator"), and is the symbol of the [Chaos Sea](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chaos_Sea). "He" possesses both the **Primordium** and **Chaos** qualities of the [Original Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Original_Creator "Original Creator").
	- Since the [Primordial Hunger](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Primordial_Hunger "Primordial Hunger") also possesses **Primordium** and **Chaos** qualities, "He" prioritized devouring the [Sefirot](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirot "Sefirot") and [Great Old Ones](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Old_Ones "Great Old Ones") related to those qualities the most. Among the [Sefirot](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirot "Sefirot"), "His" objectives include the [Chaos Sea](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chaos_Sea) with the [Hanged Man Uniqueness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniquenesses#Hanged_Man_Pathway) and others.[^22]
		- **Chaos Sea**: The **God Almighty** is the symbol of the [Sefirah](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirah "Sefirah") **[Chaos Sea](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chaos_Sea)**, a chaotic and strange sea that seemed to encompass all colors and all possibilities.
		- "They" can use the **Chaos Sea** to swallow an enemy to make them suffer immense damage or use it to submerge and protect an area, or to swallow another's attacks.
			- [Adam](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Adam "Adam") used the **Chaos Sea** to engulf the [Protected Zones](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Protected_Zones "Protected Zones") to protect them from the terrifying gravitational pull of the immense star cluster caused by [Supernova Dominator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Supernova_Dominator "Supernova Dominator") 's influence.[^23]
						- [Amon](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Amon "Amon") used the [First Blasphemy Slate](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/First_Blasphemy_Slate "First Blasphemy Slate") to send the **Chaos Sea** to devour the blood-colored robe sent by the [Mother Goddess of Depravity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Goddess_of_Depravity "Mother Goddess of Depravity") through [Bethel Abraham](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Bethel_Abraham "Bethel Abraham").
						- [Adam](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Adam "Adam") used the **Chaos Sea** to swallow the [Black Emperor](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Black_Emperor_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Black_Emperor) [Roselle Gustav](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Roselle_Gustav "Roselle Gustav") and the [Genie](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Magic_Wishing_Lamp "Magic Wishing Lamp").[^24] [^25]
				- The **Chaos Sea** tolerates all conflicts, which is the reason behind the compatibility of the [Hanged Man](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hanged_Man_Pathway) and [Sun](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sun_Pathway) Pathways.[^26]
			- "They" can create a domain of blazing, pure, **Holy Sunlight** that extends everywhere, leaving no **Shadows** behind, but at the same time, containing both **Holiness** and **Degeneration** and only a certain **Degenerated Shadow** to exist, and move within this domain. This **Degenerated Shadow** will attack the target causing them to **Degenerate** while being **Purified**.[^27]
- **The Omnipotent and Omniscient**: "They" hold the symbolism of **Omnipotence and Omniscience**. However, "Their" **Omnipotence** and **Omniscience** are only relative, not absolute in the true sense of the words.[^28]
	- Each of the [Pathways](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways") for the **God Almighty** represent an aspect of this symbolism.
		- If the [Visionary](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Visionary_Pathway) has sufficient understanding and mastery of relevant concepts, "They" can be in a sense **Omnipotent**. As **Lord of The Mind World**, "They" can also be **Omniscient** in a sense. However, that **Omniscience** is only when it's related to the [Mind World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mind_World "Mind World").[^29]
				- The [Hanged Man](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hanged_Man_Pathway) can **Graze** other [Pathways](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways"), and thus represents **Omnipotence** which results in being **Omniscient**, making it interchangeable with the [White Tower](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/White_Tower_Pathway).
				- The [White Tower](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/White_Tower_Pathway) represents **Omniscience**. Being **Omniscient** results in also being **Omnipotent**, making it interchangeable with the [Hanged Man](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hanged_Man_Pathway).[^2]
				- The [Sun](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sun_Pathway) is the symbol and cornerstone of **Energy** for **The Omniscient and Omnipotent**.[^30]
			- Due to the symbolism of the **Inextinguishable Light**, **The Omnipotent and Omniscient** can more easily return via the [Sun](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sun_Pathway) after "They" perish.[^30]
				- The [Tyrant](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tyrant_Pathway) is **Omnipotent** within the **Water**, **Land**, and **Air** **Domains**.**[^2]**
			- The **Water** domain even encompasses the [Sea of Collective Subconscious](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mind_World#Sea_of_Collective_Subconscious "Mind World"), [Chaos Sea](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chaos_Sea "Chaos Sea"), [Spiritual Sea](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul"), [River of Eternal Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/River_of_Eternal_Darkness "River of Eternal Darkness"), and [River of Fate](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/River_of_Fate "River of Fate"), which is why "They" are part of the **Omnipotent** and **Omniscient**; however, not all of them can be interfered with directly, some can only be done indirectly, and the degree of influence isn't very high.[^31]
		- As the **Lord of the Astral World** and **The Omnipotent and Omniscient**, "They" can use "Their" influence over [Astral World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Astral_World "Astral World") concepts to temporarily interfere with, distort, or suppress the symbols and authorities of other [Great Old Ones](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Old_Ones "Great Old Ones").[^32] [^33]
		- **The Omniscient and Omnipotent** symbolism makes it very hard for "Them" to be truly countered or suppressed.[^34]
			- For symbols of other [Great Old Ones](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Old_Ones "Great Old Ones") that counter "Them" or "Their" allies, "They" can temporarily reduce the degree of countering.[^35]
				- "They" can set global parameters to influence other [Great Old Ones](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Old_Ones "Great Old Ones") ' performance, weakening "Them".[^8]
		- "Their" **Omniscience** isn't affected by being isolated from the [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World").[^36]
		- **Prophecy**:
		- As the **Omniscient**, "They" can see portions of the future. This corresponds to the **Prophecy** ability of the [White Tower](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/White_Tower_Pathway).[^12]
			- In the [First Epoch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/First_Epoch "First Epoch"), **Foreseeing** a portion of the future, the [Primordial God Almighty](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Primordial_God_Almighty "Primordial God Almighty") and [Brood Hive](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Brood_Hive) gave birth to [Omebella](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Omebella "Omebella") and [Zedus](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Zedus "Zedus"), and also laid down some preparations.[^37]
						- The [Primordial God Almighty](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Primordial_God_Almighty "Primordial God Almighty") **Foresaw** one of the resuscitation plans of the [Celestial Worthy of Heaven and Earth for Blessings](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Celestial_Worthy_of_Heaven_and_Earth_for_Blessings "Celestial Worthy of Heaven and Earth for Blessings"), and left arrangements spanning several epochs, leaving behind the magic mirror [Arrodes](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Arrodes "Arrodes").[^38]
				- The future "They" proclaim will unfold and become reality. This corresponds to the **Envisioning** authority of the [Visionary](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Visionary_Pathway).[^39]
			- As a **Half-God Almighty**, [Adam](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Adam "Adam") manifested words written in the most ancient language on the surface of the **Chaos Sea** to make two **Prophecies** that affected [Klein's Apotheosis Ritual](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein%27s_Apotheosis_Ritual "Klein's Apotheosis Ritual"): one to stop the [Celestial Worthy](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Celestial_Worthy "Celestial Worthy") 's resurrection,[^40] and one to prevent Klein from becoming [The Fool](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fool_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Fool) through [Antigonus](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Antigonus "Antigonus") ' identity.[^41]
		- The **Omnipotence and Omniscience** symbolism allows "Them" to figure out the different symbolism and deduce suitable formulas, methods and pathways to become different [Great Old Ones](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Old_Ones "Great Old Ones").[^1]
		- **Blasphemy Slate**: When a **God Almighty** or a **Quasi-God Almighty** perished, "They" can use a portion of "Their" body and will to form a **[Blasphemy Slate](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Blasphemy_Slate "Blasphemy Slate")** [^1] inside the [Chaos Sea](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chaos_Sea "Chaos Sea"), which contains "Their" knowledge of the [Paths of the Divine](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways").[^42] "They" can control certain content of the [Blasphemy Slate](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Blasphemy_Slate "Blasphemy Slate") to serve "Their" purpose.[^43]
			- The **Blasphemy Slates** can be used as a medium to control the power of the **Chaos Sea**.[^44] They can also be used to strengthen the [Astral Barrier](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Astral_Barrier "Astral Barrier") against the intervention of the [Outer Deities](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Outer_Deities "Outer Deities").[^45] [^19] [^18]
						- The [First Blasphemy Slate](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/First_Blasphemy_Slate "First Blasphemy Slate") was born after the [Primordial God Almighty](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Primordial_God_Almighty "Primordial God Almighty") perished, and contained the potion names, formulas, and rituals of the 22 [Standard Pathways](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Standard_Pathways "Standard Pathways").
						- The [Second Blasphemy Slate](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Second_Blasphemy_Slate "Second Blasphemy Slate") was born after the [Ancient Sun God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ancient_Sun_God "Ancient Sun God") perished. Beyond the 22 [Standard Pathways](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Standard_Pathways "Standard Pathways"), it also contained the secrets of [Above the Sequence](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Above_the_Sequence "Above the Sequence"),[^42] which comes from the [Ancient Sun God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ancient_Sun_God "Ancient Sun God") 's research.[^1]
						- The **Third Blasphemy Slate** might appear after [Grisha Adam](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Grisha_Adam "Grisha Adam") 's death.[^46]
- **Genesis**: The **God Almighty** and the [Mother Goddess of Depravity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Goddess_of_Depravity) both hold part of the **Genesis** symbolism - opposing the **Termination** symbolism of the [Fourth Pillar](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Pillar "Fourth Pillar") [^47] - with the **God Almighty** holding a larger share.[^48]
	- **Maker**: The **Maker** (造物主: Zàowùzhǔ) is the same title as the [Original Creator (最初 造物主: Zuìchū zàowùzhǔ)](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Original_Creator "Original Creator").[^49] It represents that the **God Almighty** can utilize a part of the [Original Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Original_Creator "Original Creator") 's special traits, similar to the [Mother Goddess of Depravity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Goddess_of_Depravity "Mother Goddess of Depravity") 's [Chaos Primogenitor](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chaos_Primogenitor "Chaos Primogenitor").[^50]
		- Symbolically, the **Maker** is the ability to create and manipulate all things and powers.[^51]
			- In terms of symbolism and concept, some symbolisms fall outside the scope of "all things", such as the **Error** symbolism of the [Lord of Mysteries](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lord_of_Mysteries "Lord of Mysteries").[^51]
				- The **Maker** also refers to the one who **Makes** and shapes living beings, symbolized in the [Hanged Man](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hanged_Man_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Hanged_Man) 's authority of **Mutation**,[^52] which can create monsters out of thin air in the darkness and also affect the **Change** of all things to some extent—an overlapping authority with the [Fool](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fool_Pathway).[^53]
		- **Creator**: The **Creator** is the one who **Creates** existence and concepts through "Their" **Imagination**, symbolized in the [Visionary Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Visionary_Pathway).[^52]
		- "They" can **Create** [Beyonder Characteristics](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristics "Beyonder Characteristics") from any [Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathway "Pathway") but are only able to **Create** [Uniquenesses](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniquenesses "Uniquenesses") from the [Pathways](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways") of the **God Almighty** group and is subjected to a time limit when doing so.[^54] [^55]
			- The **Created** [Uniquenesses](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniquenesses "Uniquenesses") and [Beyonder Characteristics](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristics "Beyonder Characteristics") that are that border between reality and illusion are sufficient to replace "Their" split off [Uniquenesses](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniquenesses "Uniquenesses") and [Beyonder Characteristics](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristics "Beyonder Characteristics"), allowing "Them" to still maintain "Their" state. This can be done so long as it doesn't exceed the necessary limitations.[^56]
						- This principle stems from the [Visionary](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Visionary_Pathway) 's special trait that allows "Them" to achieve a Uniqueness that's not unique and **Envision** a [Half Great Old One](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Half_Great_Old_One "Half Great Old One"),[^57] which is unrelated to [Sefirah](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirah "Sefirah") clearance.[^58] However, a [Visionary](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Visionary_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Visionary) 's own status is insufficient to leverage the relevant aspects of the [Astral World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Astral_World "Astral World"), thus "They" could only actualize this feat if "They" have accommodated the [White Tower](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/White_Tower_Pathway), can utilize [Chaos Sea](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chaos_Sea) clearance, or have been **Corrupted** by another [Sefirah](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirah "Sefirah").[^59]
				- With the [Chaos Sea](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chaos_Sea) as a source, [Adam](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Adam "Adam") used "Himself" and the [True Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/True_Creator "True Creator") as a pillar to **Envision** and accommodate the three [Uniquenesses](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniquenesses "Uniquenesses") of the [Sun](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sun_Pathway), [Tyrant](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tyrant_Pathway), and [White Tower](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/White_Tower_Pathway) Pathways, allowing "Them" to briefly obtain strength that [transcended the Sequences](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Above_the_Sequence "Above the Sequence"), giving "Them" a [Half Great Old One](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Half_Great_Old_One "Half Great Old One") 's strength.[^19]
					- This gave "Him" enough strength to not only forcefully suppress and trap the [Eternal Blazing Sun](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Eternal_Blazing_Sun "Eternal Blazing Sun"), the [Lord of Storms](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lord_of_Storms "Lord of Storms"), and the [God of Knowledge and Wisdom](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/God_of_Knowledge_and_Wisdom "God of Knowledge and Wisdom") within "Their" respective [Divine Kingdoms](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Divine_Kingdoms "Divine Kingdoms"), but also make two **Prophecies** that interfered with [Klein's Apotheosis Ritual](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein%27s_Apotheosis_Ritual "Klein's Apotheosis Ritual").[^19] [^40] [^41]

		- In the mid- [First Epoch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/First_Epoch "First Epoch"), foreseeing parts of the future, the [Primordial God Almighty](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Primordial_God_Almighty "Primordial God Almighty") united with the [Brood Hive](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Brood_Hive "Brood Hive") to give birth to the twins [Omebella](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Omebella "Omebella") and [Zedus](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Zedus "Zedus"), making certain arrangements.[^61] [^37]
				- The [Eternal Blazing Sun](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Eternal_Blazing_Sun "Eternal Blazing Sun") used this symbolism when proselytizing to point to the [Primordial God Almighty](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Primordial_God_Almighty "Primordial God Almighty"), even though it's beyond "Him" in mysticism. This allows "Him" to partially harness the power of the [Chaos Sea](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chaos_Sea).
			- However, it also strengthened the mystical connection between "Him" and the [Primordial God Almighty](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Primordial_God_Almighty "Primordial God Almighty"), allowing the [Primordial God Almighty](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Primordial_God_Almighty "Primordial God Almighty") to awaken on "His" body when this symbolism is strengthened through [Omebella's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Omebella "Omebella") birth in [The Day of Many Disasters](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/The_Day_of_Many_Disasters "The Day of Many Disasters").[^62] [^61]

If a purely female [Beyonder](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder "Beyonder") doesn't obtain this symbolism when reaching the step of becoming **God Almighty**, it would be very dangerous.[^63]

- **Unshadowed**: A symbol of the [Sun](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sun_Pathway), it is capable of suppressing the spiritual imprint of the [Primordial God Almighty](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Primordial_God_Almighty "Primordial God Almighty") and other [Great Old Ones](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Old_Ones "Great Old Ones") in mutual counteraction.[^65]

### Countering Symbols, Authorities & Abilities

Certain **Symbols**, **Authorities**, and **Abilities** of other [Great Old Ones](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Old_Ones "Great Old Ones") can counter some of "Their" powers, which include but are not limited to the following:

- [Lord of Mysteries](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lord_of_Mysteries):
	- **Errors**, **Deceit**, **Bizarreness** mutually counteracts **Contracts**.
		- Using the **Replicated** power of **Distortion** (authority from the [Black Emperor](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Black_Emperor_Pathway)) with **Deceit**, [Klein](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") **Distorted** a clause of the **Contract** between the [Primordial God Almighty](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Primordial_God_Almighty "Primordial God Almighty") and the [Mother Goddess of Depravity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Goddess_of_Depravity "Mother Goddess of Depravity").[^66]
		- **Errors** counter **The Maker**.
		- **The Maker** represents the ability to create and manipulate all things and powers. **Error**, being a **Bug**, happens to not fall within or conform to the scope of all things in terms of concept and symbolism.[^51]
		- **Inconceivable** mutually counteracts **The Omniscient**.[^67]
		- While the **God Almighty** represents **The Omniscient**, thus symbolically **All-Knowing**, the [Lord of Mysteries](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lord_of_Mysteries "Lord of Mysteries") symbolizes the **Incomprehensible**.[^51]
- [Goddess of Fate](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Goddess_of_Fate):
	- The **Weaver of All Fates** partially counters the **Mind** and **Envisioning**.
		- The [Goddess of Fate](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Goddess_of_Fate) is stronger than the **God Almighty** in the domain of arrangements, because "She" is the weaver of all fates.[^68]
- [Eternal Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Eternal_Darkness):
	- **Concealment** mutually counteracts **Omniscience**.[^69]
- [The Anarchy](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/The_Anarchy):
	- **Distortion** mutually counteracts **Contracts**: **Distortion** could **Distort** **Contracts** to a certain degree, but not fully change it.[^66]
		- Using the **Replicated** power of **Distortion** (authority from the [Black Emperor](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Black_Emperor_Pathway)) with **Deceit**, [Klein](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") **Distorted** a clause of the **Contract** between the [Primordial God Almighty](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Primordial_God_Almighty "Primordial God Almighty") and the [Mother Goddess of Depravity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Goddess_of_Depravity "Mother Goddess of Depravity").[^66]

## Mythical Creature Form

NOTE

This Mythical Creature Form is taken from [Grisha Adam](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Grisha_Adam "Grisha Adam"), who temporarily became a Quasi- **God Almighty**.

A radiant giant whose head reached the [Astral World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Astral_World "Astral World"). Beneath "His" feet lay the [Chaos Sea](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chaos_Sea), encompassing all colors and possibilities. Behind "Him" trailed [a long shadow with five heads](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniquenesses#Hanged_Man_Pathway), while [a sun](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniquenesses#Sun_Pathway) blazed at the back of "His" head. [Storms and lightning](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniquenesses#Tyrant_Pathway) swirled around "Him", and in "His" hands, "He" held [a white tower made of books, adorned with brass eyes](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniquenesses#White_Tower_Pathway).[^70]

## Advancement Condition

Aside from the [general advancement condition for all Above the Sequence](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Above_the_Sequence#Advancement_Condition "Above the Sequence"), according to the [Ancient Sun God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ancient_Sun_God "Ancient Sun God") 's research, the easiest path to advance to **God Almighty** is to become a [True God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/True_God "True God") of either the [Visionary](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Visionary_Pathway) or [Hanged Man](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hanged_Man_Pathway) Pathways and gaining initial control over the [Chaos Sea](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chaos_Sea) before taking back the remaining [Uniquenesses](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniquenesses "Uniquenesses") and Sequence 1 Beyonder Characteristics. And in this matter, there was something more special about the [Visionary](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Visionary_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Visionary) than the [Hanged Man](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hanged_Man_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Hanged_Man): the ability to "Create" the [Uniquenesses](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniquenesses "Uniquenesses") of other Pathways to temporarily become a [Half Great Old One](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Half_Great_Old_One "Half Great Old One").[^19]

It's possible for a purely female [Beyonder](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder "Beyonder") to advance to the **God Almighty**, but when reaching the step of becoming **God Almighty**, one must find a way to obtain the symbolism of the " **Father of All Things** "; otherwise, it would be very dangerous.[^63]
