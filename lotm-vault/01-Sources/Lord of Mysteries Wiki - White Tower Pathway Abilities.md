## Sequence 7: Detective (Knowledge Keeper)

**Description** [^4]

A **Detective** is a "profession" that focuses on knowledge acquisition and reasoning ability, along with good fighting and weapon handling skills.

**Traits** [^4]

- **Enhanced Mental Attributes**: They can become **Masters** of reasoning.
- **Weapon Proficiency**: They have become proficient in using weapons.
	- They can become martial **Masters**; however, this term is relative to ordinary people.
		- As **Detectives** gain few improvements to their physical attributes, they are not as strong as **Beyonders** who excel in combat.
- **Physical Enhancement**: **Detectives** have become more agile.

**New Abilities** [^3] [^4]

- **Reconstruction**: **Detectives** can use **Beyonder** methods to analyze and **Reconstruct** events that have occurred within a certain area.

**Strengthened Abilities**

- **Ritualistic Magic Master**: A **Detective** can become a **Master** when it comes to [Ritualistic Magic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ritualistic_Magic "Ritualistic Magic").

## Sequence 6: Polymath

**Description** [^4]

This **Sequence** allows them to learn and understand a great deal. Because of their extensive knowledge, they become Jack-of-all-trades, able to identify, see clearly, remember, and **Imitate** abilities they've encountered.

**New Abilities** [^3] [^4]

- **Analysis**: With prior knowledge, a **Polymath** can **Analyze** the **Beyonder** powers used by an opponent, quickly identify the specific situation, and understand strengths as well as weaknesses. Of course, **Analysis** can also be wrong and fail.
	- The more comprehensive their knowledge and the deeper their understanding of the target's [Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathway "Pathway") and **Beyonder** powers, the more effective their **Analysis** becomes.
		- If the **Polymath** makes errors or an incomplete **Analysis** while dissecting an ability, the ability will mutate strangely or just fail entirely.[^7]
		- The prerequisite for a **Polymath's** **Analysis** to work successfully is to have enough preexisting knowledge of the target's [Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathway "Pathway") and subsequent **Beyonder** powers.
		- **Analysis** let's them rapidly identify a target's **Beyonder** powers, allowing them to know of the target's strengths and weaknesses as well as **Imitate** more quickly.
		- Despite having only directly confronted [Jason Beria](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Jason_Beria "Jason Beria") once, [Isengard Stanton](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Isengard_Stanton "Isengard Stanton") was able to use [2-081](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/2-081 "2-081") to **Imitate** and utilize the **Beyonder** power **Danger Premonition**.[^8]
			- This case of analyzing a passive ability is attributed to the properties of [2-081](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/2-081 "2-081"), whose formation method enhanced the power of **Analysis**.[^9]
		- They can **Analyze** **Demigod** -level **Beyonder** powers.[^10]
		- They can **Analyze** **Fate** -related **Beyonder** powers.[^11]
- **Imitation**: Once sufficiently **Analyzed**, There is a chance for the **Polymath** to **Imitate** an **Analyzed** ability to a certain extent.
	- The more effective their **Analysis** is, the higher the probability that their **Imitation** succeeds.[^3] However, the higher the [Sequence](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sequence "Sequence") of the **Beyonder** power, the higher the chances of failure.[^8]
		- Even if a **Polymath** manages to **Imitate** a **Beyonder** power of the same [Sequence](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sequence "Sequence") level as them, the power they **Imitate** will always be very different from the original.
			- [Edwina Edwards](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Edwina_Edwards "Edwina Edwards") failed in her search for messengers partly because the **Imitation** of undead powers weren't enough to sign a **Contract**.[^12]
		- They can **Imitate** passive **Beyonder** powers such as a **[Devil](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Abyss_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_6:_Devil) 's** **Danger Premonition**. However, such abilities cannot be copied through observation alone and requires anatomical research.[^13] [^14]
		- They can **Imitate** even powers like **Recording**, but it requires a lot of knowledge.[^15]
				- **Polymaths** are also able to **Imitate** **Fate** -related **Beyonder** powers.[^11]
		- At the stage of a **Polymath**, as long as the corresponding knowledge is **Analyzed** and mastered, there is no limit to the number of **Spells** and abilities that can be **Imitated**.
		- However, the effect of **Imitation** will vary depending on the degree of **Analysis**, the degree of knowledge mastery in addition to the degree of potion digestion.
			- It may be *20%* at the worst, and can reach to *50%* of the original at the best.
		- They can **Imitate** powers at the **Demigod** -level, and if the knowledge is fully grasped, its effects are not much different from a **[Scribe](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Door_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_6:_Scribe) 's** **Recording**.[^10] [^1]
		- The chances of successfully **Imitating** a **Demigod** -level power is very low, and they must fully master the knowledge of the corresponding power to reach its maximum level.
				- Such **Imitations** can harm an **[Overseer](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chaos_Mist_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_4:_Overseer)** who is immune to attacks that lack godhood.[^10]
		- This is different compared to **Replication/Recording** of the [Door Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Door_Pathway).
		- Using a computer analogy, **Imitation** is equivalent to reverse-engineering the code yourself and writing a similar one, whereas **Recording** and **Replication** are equivalent to a "Copy" operation.[^16]
		- **Imitation** can't **Imitate** authorities, even when one reaches **Sequence 0**.[^17]
		- The abilities within an authority can still be **Imitated**, but they are inferior to the permission itself, meaning they will be suppressed by the permission.[^18]

## Sequence 5: Mysticism Magister

**New Abilities** [^3] [^4]

- **Mystic Arts**: A **Mysticism Magister** masters various magical, diverse, unique, and rare **Mystic Arts**, and can even invent their own strange yet effective **Spells**. **Mystic Arts** are formed from the **Analysis** of **Principles**.[^19]
	- Depending on the chosen [Advancement Ritual](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/White_Tower_Pathway/Advancement#Sequence_5:_Mysticism_Magister), the area of the **Mystic Arts** created and mastered by the **Mysticism** **Magister** will vary:
		- "Teaching at least five **Mid-Sequence** **Beyonders** who have independently comprehended [Acting Method](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Acting_Method "Acting Method") " means being more adept at creating and using **Mystic Arts** in the corresponding **Mid-Sequence Beyonder's** field.
				- "Teaching at least fifty Erudites" means being well rounded in the **Mystic Arts** field, with no weaknesses but also no strengths.
		- When a **Mysticism Magister** first advances, they can naturally obtain *6 to* *13 **Mystic Arts*** from the current **Omniscience** authority holder.
		- After that, they can acquire **Mystic Arts** through learning and self-creation.
		- **Mysticism Magisters** can achieve the effect of being well-rounded with this ability.
		- So long as they have the required knowledge and materials at hand, they can make any **Spell** from healing to defense, offense, and support as **Omniscience** implies **Omnipotence**.[^20]
		- Their **Spells** include definitions such as **Uncommon**, **Unique to Oneself**, and **Distinctive**, and at the same time, their **Spells** may or may not have casting steps.[^21]
		- Unlike the **Spells** created by **[Warlocks](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hermit_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_7:_Warlock)**, **Mystic Arts** can often omit the casting materials, incantations, and most casting steps, making them faster and more secretive.
			- Of course, not all **Mystic Arts** can omit those. **Mystic Arts** with prior preparations generally either have very special effects or are very powerful.
				- By obtaining a weakened version of an ability and strengthening it through continuous **Analysis**, they can develop unique new skills through independent refinement, fusion, and deduction.[^22]
		- Some of the **Spells** and techniques they can use include but are not limited to:
		- **Mirror Curse**: [Edwina Edwards](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Edwina_Edwards "Edwina Edwards") used a bizarre but powerful spell that uses a brass mirror to reflect [Ulyssan](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ulyssan "Ulyssan") 's body parts, pinching the reflection at the mirror’s surface before pulling outward, causing **Ulyssan'** s wound to instantly worsen.[^23]
				- Theoretically, if a **Mysticism Magister** has mastered sufficient knowledge, they can reproduce the **Spells Evans Freezing Ray** and **Elemental Order** (these **Spells** are from [Cuttlefish's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Cuttlefish_That_Loves_Diving "Cuttlefish That Loves Diving") other novel, [Throne of the Magical Arcana](https://www.novelupdates.com/series/throne-of-magical-arcana/)).[^24]
			- **Evans Freezing Ray**: This is a high-precision **Freezing Spell** that emits a concentrated, colorless dim ray that **Freezes** specific targets, turning their skin into a layer of crystal **Ice** and also **Freezing** the target's surroundings.
						- **Elemental Order**: This is a **Spell** based on the periodic law of basic elements, capable of precisely arranging electrons, allowing the them to *control and combine* elements for attack, defense or recycling.
				- **Mysticism Magisters** can use **Mystic Arts** to delay the loss of control of **Beyonders**, or to permanently or temporarily enhance their own physical abilities.

**Strengthened Abilities** [^4]

- The previous **Sequences** ’ abilities have been enhanced.
	- **Imitation**: Their **Imitated** **Spells** can now have *60%* of the original effect.
		- It is possible to **Analyse** the **Principle** of an **Imitated** abilities and use the new **Knowledge** and **Principles** to create new **Mystic Arts [^19]**

## Sequence 3: Cognizer

TRANSLATION NOTE

In the original Chinese text, 规律 (guīlǜ) carries multiple meanings and in this context, can be translated as " **Laws** ", as is done in the English WebNovel translation. However, as the term " **Law** " is already used for a similar word, 律法 (lǜfǎ), and both are treated as distinct authorities, this page will consistently use a single English term (**Principles**) to represent it for clarity and distinction.
- **Laws** (律法, *lǜfǎ*):
	- Refers to codified, enforceable laws—rules established by authority, often carrying judicial or binding force. It aligns with legal systems, decrees, and formal structures of governance.
- **Principles** (规律 *guīlǜ*):
	- Refers to inherent patterns or natural laws—the underlying order of reality that exists independently of authority. It is closer to universal principles or the fundamental workings of the world.
		- In other contexts this term can also denote simpler patterns or regularity, not a strict law, but rather an observable consistency or recurring behavior over time such as everyday routines or behavioral patterns.

**Authorities** [^3] [^4]

- **Principles**: "They" gain partial authority over **Principles**.
	- **Cognizing**: It is the ability to understand the rules of the world and grasp many profound mysteries, enemies standing in front of a **Cognizer** can rarely hide any secret. Everything, be it their strengths, weakness, or anything else is all laid bare for the **Cognizer** to see.
		- They **Cognize** the essence without any senses or specific forms of cognition, effectively viewing the true essence of things without relying on their senses.[^33]
			- They can use their deep insight to see through and **Analyze** certain symbols and use it to create **Spells** through a process of **Imitation**.
				- For example, **Cognizers** can look and peer into the symbols of [Lumian's Dream](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian%27s_Dream "Lumian's Dream") and derive certain **Spells** based off the symbols they have comprehended.[^34]
				- Because a **Cognizer** possesses the leverage to move the world and achieve their goals, they are often able to solve problems in a very easy way.
			- For instance, a **Cognizer** can cause a mountain to collapse, perhaps even without the ability to cause calamity or destruction, just by simply prying away a rock.
				- A **Cognizer** does not directly manipulate the **Principles**, but rather, after understanding them, employs corresponding methods to leverage these **Principles**.
			- Compared to a **[Mentor of Deceit](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_3:_Mentor_of_Deceit)** who directly deceives **Principles** and **Rules**, a **Cognizer** first engages in targeted, misleading actions before leveraging the **Principles** to accomplish **Deception** in order to achieve a similar effect.

**Mythical Creature Form**

- As **Sequence 3 Beyonders**, they can now respond to prayers within a certain range.

## Sequence 0: White Tower

**Symbolism**

- "They" symbolize the underlying symbolism of **Knowledge leads to Calamity**.[^45] [^46]

**Authorities** [^4]

- **Omniscience**: The **White Tower** has **Omniscience** over all things, which is unaffected by the [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World"). At this level, the scope of "Their" **Omniscience** is **Knowledge**.[^40]
	- As **Omniscience** implies **Omnipotence**, "They" can **Imitate** any ability, achieving the original's power, or even optimize and surpass it; however, this does not include authorities and symbols.
		- Even if the **White Tower** fully grasped the knowledge to the corresponding authorities and symbolisms, "They" cannot acquire them, only imitating their external manifestations.
			- This is because authorities and symbolisms are more like the embodiment of permissions and concepts. Without obtaining them, they cannot be used.
		- Even if the **White Tower** can see the future information, this doesn't mean "They" can see it entirely if it concerns a high level matter. For example, "They" might gain vague foresight that one would gain something unique, however, "They" might not know what it's.[^47]
		- The [God of Knowledge and Wisdom](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/God_of_Knowledge_and_Wisdom "God of Knowledge and Wisdom") had seen via **Omniscience** that [Lumian Lee](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee") would gain something rather unique, but "He" didn't know what that 'thing' would be.[^47]
			- That 'thing' was **Chaotic Vortex Face** from [God Almighty](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/God_Almighty "God Almighty") that can separate **Yin** and **Yang**.
		- **Omniscience** can be influenced by [Symbolisms](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Symbolism "Symbolism") like **Foolishness** and **Inconceivable**.
		- **Principles**: Knowing the **Principles** allows one to grasp and utilize it.
		- At this level, "They" can "open source" "Their" **Spells** to be used by ordinary people, albeit it won't be very strong and has some requirements.[^48]
			- These "open source" **Spells** require materials, incantations, [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World") resonance, **Spirituality** absorption, etc.
				- This partial authority is subordinate to the **Omniscience** authority.
				- Grasping and utilizing the **Principles** allows one to do things like:
			- The [God of Knowledge and Wisdom](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/God_of_Knowledge_and_Wisdom "God of Knowledge and Wisdom") placed [Morora](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Morora "Morora"), along with the surrounding mountains, on his stomach, with the path of exile leading to the [Astral World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Astral_World "Astral World").[^47]

								- Within, abilities like **Teleportation** will only let the user **Teleport** within the mountainous area, and summoning messengers will also fail.[^50]
								- The entrance to [Morora](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Morora "Morora") is located within the underground of the Holy Temple of Knowledge, behind a large double brass door.[^26]
- **Astral World Ladder**: "They" can influence the concept of [Astral World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Astral_World "Astral World") convergence to a certain extent.
	- "They" can influence [Astral World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Astral_World "Astral World") concepts, and while "They" can't yet completely block **Proxy**, **Sacrifice**, compliance, etc., "They" can effectively interfere with it.[^51]
		- For instance, it can cause the concept of " **Allegiance** " to briefly increase in extension or intension, causing the **[Hand of God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Twilight_Giant_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_1:_Hand_of_God) '** s allegiance to suddenly fail to meet all conditions, allowing only proxying of a portion of the originally intended power, or even fail.
			- When it comes to the **[Twilight Giant](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Twilight_Giant_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Twilight_Giant_\(Twilight,_Giant\))**, "They" can use their own methods to interfere with "Their" **Proxy**, reducing the power of the self-detonation.[^52]
		- **Astral World Ladder** also symbolizes the layers of **Knowledge** that lead to the most essential aspects of the universe as well as the most fundamental dangers.
- **Creation**: A partial authority over **Creation**, originating from the [Symbolisms](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Symbolism "Symbolism") of **Maker** and **The Omnipotent and Omniscient**.
	- The **Omniscient** **White** **Tower** can naturally **Create** anything, but it cannot escape the symbolisms and authorities that do not belong to "Them".
- **Calamity**: "They" have a partial authority over **Calamity**.
	- They are capable of inflicting **Madness** and **Destruction** onto others as certain **Knowledge** is the embodiment of those symbols.
- **Wisdom**: "They" have partial authority over **Wisdom**.
	- **Omniscience** means being able to provide the **Wisest** guidance and make the **Wisest** choices.
- **Learning**: "They" have partial authority over **Learning**, which is derived from **Omniscience** and **Wisdom**.
- **Analysis**: "They" have partial authority over **Analysis**, which is derived from **Omniscience** and **Wisdom**.
	- The **Analysis** of **Principles** is the basis of **Mystic Arts** [^19]
- **Tower of Omniscience** (**Tower of Madness**): This is "Their" **Divine Kingdom**.

**Mythical Creature Form**

- An illusory **Tower**, every level was made up of thick books. Each book has a brass eye on it. The higher one looks, the darker it becomes. It is filled with the aura of insanity, destruction, inauspiciousness, and disaster. The tower extends into the sky, as though it encompasses the entire world within itself.[^53]
