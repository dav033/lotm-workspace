# Genesis (Eternal Darkness)

The supplied secondary material attributes only partial Genesis symbolism to [[Eternal Darkness]], derived from its partial [[Termination (Eternal Darkness)]] role. It must not be treated as unrestricted creation symbolism.

## Expression

The [[Twilight Giant Pathway (disambiguation)]] relation between twilight and dawn expresses the transition from a concluding universe toward a new beginning.
