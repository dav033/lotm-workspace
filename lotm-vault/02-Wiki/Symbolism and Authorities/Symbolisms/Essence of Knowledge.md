# Essence of Knowledge

Essence of Knowledge is reported as a Symbolism inherited from the Original Creator and expressed by [[Demon of Knowledge]]. Its exact allocation between the complete Great Old One, [[Knowledge Moor]], and either member Pathway is not established by the available secondary sources.
