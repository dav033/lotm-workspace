# Error and Loopholes (Error Symbolism)

The Error Pathway symbolizes exceptions and exploitable loopholes in an otherwise coherent system. [[Error (Authority)]] is the corresponding jurisdiction rather than a separate claim that every anomaly belongs to this Pathway.
