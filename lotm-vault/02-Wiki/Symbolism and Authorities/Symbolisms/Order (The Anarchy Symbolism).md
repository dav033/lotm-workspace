# Order (The Anarchy Symbolism)

The supplied secondary sources describe Order as a Symbolism established with the birth of the Son of Chaos and inherited by [[The Anarchy]]. [[Order (Justiciar)]] is its core Justiciar-pathway Authority expression.

## Boundary

This Symbolism is not interchangeable with the Sun Pathway's [[Order (Sun)]] Authority or with every rule-based Power.
