# Eternal Darkness (Symbolism)

Eternal Darkness is a Symbolism of the [[Darkness Pathway (disambiguation)]] associated with final, unchanging darkness. It is distinct from the Great Old One [[Eternal Darkness]] and from the operational [[Darkness (Authority)]].
