# Laws (Demon of Knowledge Symbolism)

The source attributes the Symbolism of fixed universal Laws, 定律 (*dinglu*), to both the Son of Chaos and [[Demon of Knowledge]]. Demon of Knowledge is described as focusing on knowledge of those Laws.

## Translation boundary

This Symbolism is distinct from [[Principles (Paragon)]], 规律 (*guilu*), which concerns inherent patterns or workings of reality, and from codified enforceable laws, 律法 (*lufa*).
