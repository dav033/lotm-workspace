# Passage of Time (Twilight Giant)

Passage of Time is a Symbolism of the [[Twilight Giant Pathway (disambiguation)]] through which existence ages and weakens. It does not imply general control over all time; [[Space-Time-in-One]] is a separate group-level symbolism.
