# Knowledge and Information (Demon of Knowledge)

The supplied secondary sources describe [[Demon of Knowledge]] as the embodiment of Knowledge and Information rather than an entity that merely knows everything. This group-level Symbolism is shared by the Hermit and Paragon Pathways, whose Authorities express different aspects of it.

## Pathway expressions

- Hermit: [[Mysticism Knowledge (Hermit)]], [[Hiding (Hermit)]], and [[Mystery Prying (Hermit)]].
- Paragon: [[Reality Knowledge (Paragon)]], [[Principles (Paragon)]], [[Manufacturing (Demon of Knowledge)]], and [[Civilization (Paragon Authority)]].
