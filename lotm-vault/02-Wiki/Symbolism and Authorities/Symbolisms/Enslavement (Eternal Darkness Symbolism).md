# Enslavement (Eternal Darkness Symbolism)

The secondary material assigns Enslavement symbolism to [[Eternal Darkness]]. [[Enslavement (Death Authority)]] and [[Proxy (Twilight Giant)]] are Pathway-specific expressions and must not be treated as identical ownership across all three Pathways.
