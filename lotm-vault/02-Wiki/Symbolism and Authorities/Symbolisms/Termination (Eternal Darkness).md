# Termination (Eternal Darkness)

The secondary sources describe [[Eternal Darkness]] as a major component of the [[Fourth Pillar]] and therefore as an incomplete expression of Termination. Full universal Termination belongs to the Fourth Pillar, not to this Pathway group alone.

## Relations

- Includes incomplete [[Singularity of All]] and [[Space-Time-in-One]].
- Produces only partial [[Genesis (Eternal Darkness)]] through the end-to-beginning relation.
