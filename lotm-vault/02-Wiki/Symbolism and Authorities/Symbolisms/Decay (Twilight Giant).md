# Decay (Twilight Giant)

Decay is a Symbolism explicitly assigned to the [[Twilight Giant Pathway (disambiguation)]]. It describes decline and weakening rather than an unrestricted Authority over every form of decomposition.

## Relation

Together with [[Passage of Time (Twilight Giant)]], it supports the [[Twilight (Authority)]] expression.
