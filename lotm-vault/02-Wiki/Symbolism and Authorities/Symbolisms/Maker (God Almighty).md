# Maker (God Almighty)

Maker represents God Almighty's creation and shaping aspect. The available author material relates it to [[Creation (White Tower)]], [[Matter (Tyrant)]], [[Mutation (Hanged Man)]], [[Thunder (Tyrant)]], and jointly with Omnipotence to [[Water (Tyrant)]].
