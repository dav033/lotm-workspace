# Singularity of All

Singularity of All is described as a Fourth Pillar-level aspect of [[Termination (Eternal Darkness)]]. [[Eternal Darkness]] possesses only an incomplete share of this symbolism.

## Boundary

Its reported erasure effect may be expressed through [[Concealment (Darkness)]], but Concealment remains an Authority rather than the symbolism itself.
