# Space-Time-in-One

Space-Time-in-One is described as an incomplete Fourth Pillar-level aspect of [[Termination (Eternal Darkness)]] held by [[Eternal Darkness]]. It concerns the convergence of space and time toward one point rather than general ownership of every spatial or temporal effect.

## Counter-relation

The supplied Fandom source reports a conditional mutual counter-relation with [[King of Space-Time]], with Space-Time-in-One slightly dominant in the stated comparison. See [[Eternal Darkness Group Counters]].
