# Disorder (Key of Light Symbolism)

This 混乱 (*hunluan*) Symbolism is the supported parent of [[Disorder (Wheel of Fortune)]]. The same Chinese term appears for The Anarchy Pathways' Disorder shares, but no inspected source makes this Key of Light Symbolism their parent. It also remains distinct from the title [[Endless Disorder]].
