# Darkness (Eternal Darkness Symbolism)

The supplied source describes [[Eternal Darkness]] as holding the symbolism of Darkness. This Great Old One-level node remains separate from the pathway-level [[Darkness (Authority)]] and from individual darkness-producing Powers.
