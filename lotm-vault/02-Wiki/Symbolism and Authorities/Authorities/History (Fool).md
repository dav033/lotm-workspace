# History (Fool)

## Meaning

History is the Fool Authority produced by King of Space-Time together with Bizarreness. It grants access to the Fog and Historical Void, established past states, and temporary manipulation of how History manifests. It does not rewrite the established past.

## Expression in the Novels

- Scholar of Yore uses Historical Void Borrowing, Projection, and Hiding to borrow a past self, summon known people or objects, or hide in known historical gaps.
- Miracle Invoker resurrection returns Beyonder Characteristics to past Worms of Spirit and reconstructs the body from the Historical Void.
- At Sequence 0, Klein Fooled History to exchange adjacent past and present conditions and escape a state imposed by Amon.

## Limits

- The author's post states that the past cannot be changed and History has already become fact.
- Projections depend on knowledge, affinity, target level, Spirituality, duration, and a limited simultaneous count.
- Uniquenesses and their holders cannot normally be summoned directly.
- Historical manipulation remains temporary and does not equal unrestricted time travel.

## Community Cross-check

Readers sometimes treat Historical Void use as past alteration. Both author wording and shown mechanics preserve a fixed past: users borrow, project, hide in, or temporarily Fool established History.
