# Spirit World Mentor (Demon of Knowledge)

Spirit World Mentor is reported as an Authority shared by the Hermit and Paragon Pathways. This node records their knowledge-oriented relation to the Spirit World and does not grant either Pathway general dominion over it.

## Symbolic Derivation

The Spirit World is treated as a convergence of information, so this shared Authority belongs under [[Knowledge and Information (Demon of Knowledge)]].
