# Civilization (Paragon Authority)

Civilization is a [[Paragon Pathway (disambiguation)]] Authority over accumulated knowledge, institutions, production, enlightenment, and civilizational development. It is a Pathway expression distinct from [[Civilization (Demon of Knowledge Symbolism)]].

## Symbolic Derivation

The source places this Authority under [[Knowledge and Information (Demon of Knowledge)]]; the same-name Great Old One Symbolism is not established as its immediate parent.
