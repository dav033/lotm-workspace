# Door (Door Pathway)

This Pathway-level Authority governs doors as passages and thresholds, including opening, closing, access, and restriction. The supplied secondary material identifies the Door concept as encompassing [[Space (Door)]], [[Sealing (Door)]], and [[Alternate Worlds (Door)]].

This Authority remains distinct from its parent [[Door (Symbolism)]]. Related group: [[Lord of Mysteries Group]].
