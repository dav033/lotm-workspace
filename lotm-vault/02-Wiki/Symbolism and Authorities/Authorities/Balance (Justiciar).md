# Balance (Justiciar)

## Meaning

Balance is a [[Justiciar Pathway (disambiguation)]] Authority that temporarily reduces an asymmetry between sides, participants, states, or abstract values. It can partition a conflict, exclude excess power, or equalize selected combatants without making everything permanently identical.

## Expression in the Novels

- Sequence 2 Balancer can divide a battlefield and prevent outside interference, exclude an overpowered participant for a limited period, or create a short one-on-one equilibrium.
- *Circle of Inevitability* Chapters 1132-1133 index battlefield partition and a Balance interaction later targeted by Distortion.
- Duration shrinks as the target exceeds the user: an equalized fight may last about a minute, while a deity-level target may be affected for only seconds.
- At Sequence 0, Balance can temporarily equalize abstract states such as reason and madness, indexed with a maximum of roughly three minutes in the secondary ability record.

## Limits

- Balance is approximate, selected, and temporary; it does not create permanent equality.
- Stronger targets sharply reduce duration and may resist or distort the imposed equilibrium.
- It balances a defined relation, not every property of every participant.

## Community Cross-check

Community discussion generally recognizes the short duration against stronger targets. Claims of permanent stat equalization or universal fairness are rejected.
