# Disorder (Justiciar)

## Meaning

The indexed author supplement names this limited Authority share 混乱 (*hunluan*). The [[Justiciar Pathway (disambiguation)]] mainly perceives disorder and restores, calms, or extinguishes it. [[Order (Justiciar)]] can also introduce controlled disorder when doing so serves a larger ordered arrangement.

## Expression in the Novels

- Sequence 3 Chaos Hunter can detect anomalies and disorder, then calm or correct them.
- Sequence 1 Hand of Order can perceive changes in an ordered system and quell disturbances or abnormal states; the George III aftermath is indexed in *Lord of Mysteries* Chapters 1148 and 1150.
- The Sequence 0 supplement broadens this into restoring or extinguishing Disorder, but the available novel index contains fewer direct combat demonstrations than for Black Emperor Disorder.

## Limits

- This is not the Black Emperor's majority, entropy-oriented [[Disorder (Black Emperor)]]. Its clearest use is diagnostic and corrective.
- It must not be conflated with 失序 (*shixu*) in The Anarchy's titles or merged with [[Disorder (Wheel of Fortune)]] merely because both use 混乱.
- Creating controlled disorder through Order does not imply unrestricted ownership of chaos.

## Community Cross-check

Reddit comparisons usually preserve the distinction between corrective Justiciar Disorder and active Black Emperor entropy. Broader claims remain speculative because direct Sequence 0 scenes are sparse.
