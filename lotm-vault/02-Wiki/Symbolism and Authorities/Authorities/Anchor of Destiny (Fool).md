# Anchor of Destiny (Fool)

## Meaning

Anchor of Destiny is the Fool Pathway Authority under [[Beacon of Destiny]]. Its holder's Fate is exceptionally difficult to alter. Unless the holder deliberately restrains it, the holder's words become nodes toward which the River of Fate develops.

## Expression in the Novels

- Lower-Sequence probability interference and Fooling Fate show related Fate access, but do not by themselves prove full Anchor of Destiny.
- Klein reaches Sequence 0 near the novel's conclusion; no unambiguous scene names and isolates this Authority before the later author post supplies its definition.

## Limits

- Words create future nodes, not guaranteed literal outcomes. The route and final manifestation can be highly distorted.
- The holder must self-restrain speech to avoid unintended Fate effects.
- This is not unrestricted command over the River of Fate and does not replace the Wheel of Fortune Pathway's Fate Authority.

## Community Cross-check

Readers disagree over whether Anchor makes Fool the “main” Lord of Mysteries Pathway. The source supports one Pathway expression under a group symbolism, not hierarchy over Error or Door.
