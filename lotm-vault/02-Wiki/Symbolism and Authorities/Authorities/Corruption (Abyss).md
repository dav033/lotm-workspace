# Corruption (Abyss)

The secondary evidence set lists Corruption as a partial Authority of the [[Abyss Pathway (disambiguation)|Abyss Pathway]].

Related group: [[Father of Devils Group]].

## Meaning

Corruption is contaminating alteration of body, mind, spirit, behavior, or environment. The indexed Sequence 0 explanation makes it an outward extension of degeneration and an expected result when desire erupts and rational control fails.

## Expression in the Novels

- Sequence 5 Defiling Seeds and catalyzed desires alter thought and action, making a target unable to resist and progressively corrupted.
- Sequence 3 Blatherer is the first level explicitly indexed with a portion of Corruption Authority: voice and words can cause confusion, damage, curses, loss of control, or remote death after a connection is established.
- *Circle of Inevitability* Chapters 1134 and 1136 are indexed for Angel-level expressions involving corrupted psyche, ignited desire and blood, and polluted bodily fluids.
- Sequence 1 combines Corruption with Filth, Corrosion, toxins, fog, and Abyss flames, allowing defenses against ordinary fire to be bypassed by the other components.

## Limits

- Corruption can cause mutation, but it is not identical to [[Deviants (Abyss)|Deviants]] or all bodily transformation.
- The current source chain indexes novel chapters through a community page; status therefore remains interpretation and `medium-secondary`.

## Symbolic Derivation

Corruption is one of the Mind-oriented Authorities reflecting [[Abyss of the Heart and Mind]].

## Community Cross-check

Reddit often merges Corruption, Filth, Degeneration, and mutation into one effect. Higher-quality indexed material keeps them related but distinct.
