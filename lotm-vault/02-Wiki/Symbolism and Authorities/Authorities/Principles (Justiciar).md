# Principles (Justiciar)

## Meaning

Principles is a subordinate [[Justiciar Pathway (disambiguation)]] Authority under [[Order (Justiciar)]]. It concerns the stable patterns or regularities by which an ordered system operates, associated with 规律 (*guilü*), rather than a single command or written statute.

## Expression in the Novels

- Direct isolated demonstrations are scarcer than for Rules or Judgment. The clearest secondary classification appears in the Sequence 0 taxonomy that places Principles beneath Order.
- High-level Justiciar effects can alter some underlying mystical rules and construct coordinated systems whose provisions behave consistently. The Trunsoest Brass Book conflict in *Lord of Mysteries* Chapters 1283-1289 supplies the main indexed manifestation.
- The difference is functional: a Rule states a condition, a Law organizes enforceable provisions, and a Principle is the regularity that makes the ordered system behave consistently.

## Limits

- The available material does not support ownership of every physical or metaphysical principle.
- Modification of underlying rules is partial and faces greater resistance than modification of non-underlying rules.
- This Authority remains distinct from [[Principles (Paragon)]] and [[Principles (White Tower)]], whose names occur in different Pathway contexts.

## Symbolic Derivation

Principles inherits [[Order (The Anarchy Symbolism)]] through [[Order (Justiciar)]].

## Community Cross-check

Community discussion provides little independent evidence for this subdivision and often merges it into Laws. That absence is retained as uncertainty rather than filled by analogy.
