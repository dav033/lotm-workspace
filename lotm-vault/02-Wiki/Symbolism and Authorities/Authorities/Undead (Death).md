# Undead (Death)

Undead is the [[Death Pathway (disambiguation)]] Authority over undead existence, creation, transformation, and control. It does not make every spirit-related effect native to Death.

## Symbolic Derivation

[[Death (Eternal Darkness Symbolism)]] strongly supports Undead as the state and source from which undead existence is governed.
