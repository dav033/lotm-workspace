# Shared and Partial Authorities

This classification distinguishes genuine shared jurisdiction from partial ownership, overlapping effects, and Great Old One-level synthesis. All entries remain interpretations until their underlying official references are ingested as separate official/high source notes.

## Classification Rules

- **Shared Authority**: multiple Pathways possess real jurisdiction over the same concept, even when their scope or application differs.
- **Partial Authority**: a Pathway possesses only a limited portion or expression of an Authority held more fully elsewhere.
- **Asymmetric shared Authority**: all listed Pathways possess the Authority, but one holds a larger or more central share.
- **Overlap without identity**: different Authorities produce similar results or operate in a common domain; this is not enough to classify them as shared.
- **Great Old One-level Authority**: a combined group Authority does not automatically become native ownership of every member Pathway.

## Similar Names That Are Not Shared

- [[Disorder (Wheel of Fortune)]], [[Disorder (Black Emperor)]], and [[Disorder (Justiciar)]] use 混乱 (*hunluan*) in the author posts but have different Pathway scopes. The titles The Anarchy and Nation of Disorder instead use 失序 (*shixu*), loss of order; neither naming overlap is enough to merge the Authority nodes.
- [[Principles (Paragon)]], [[Principles (White Tower)]], and [[Principles (Justiciar)]] have different conceptual foundations despite sharing an English label.
- Replication, Imitation, Grazing, Envisioning, Grafting, Distortion, and Manufacturing can produce comparable outcomes through different Authorities.
- Purification and Degeneration oppose one another but are not a single shared Authority with two polarities.
- Similar control over spirits by Death, Darkness, Hanged Man, Fool, Door, Mother, or Moon does not establish one universal shared Spirit Authority.

## Group-Level Authorities

[[Astral World Dominion]], [[Contracts (God Almighty)]], and [[Spirit World Dominion]] are modeled at Great Old One or group level. Their `pathways` metadata identifies the contributing group, not native Authority ownership by every member Pathway.
