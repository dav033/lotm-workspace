# Judgement (Justiciar)

## Meaning

Judgement is a [[Justiciar Pathway (disambiguation)]] Authority that turns violation of an established [[Order (Justiciar)]] into an authoritative verdict and consequence. At higher Sequence, the surrounding world supports enforcement; Judgment is distinct from the Rule that was violated.

## Expression in the Novels

- Sequence 3 Chaos Hunter develops a partial form: Sword of Judgment grows more powerful as the target deviates further from the governing order.
- Judgment can pursue an offender within the relevant jurisdiction rather than depending only on physical proximity.
- The Trunsoest Brass Book conflict shows escalating punishment for repeated violations, ultimately allowing a death sentence (*Lord of Mysteries* Chapter 1288).

## Limits

- Judgment requires a normative baseline and a violation; it is not arbitrary execution detached from Order.
- Severity, range, and enforcement depend on Sequence, jurisdiction, accumulated violations, and resistance.
- Ordinary legal judgment and similarly named attacks in other Pathways do not automatically belong to this Authority.

## Community Cross-check

Reddit discussions often describe Judgment as “the world punishes you.” This is useful only when tied to a declared order and demonstrated violation, not as an unconditional attack.
