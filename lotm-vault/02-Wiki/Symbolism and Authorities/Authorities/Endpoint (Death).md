# Endpoint (Death)

Endpoint is a [[Death Pathway (disambiguation)]] Authority concerning the final cessation of living, spiritual, or conceptual processes. It is related to [[Eternal Rest]] but is not interchangeable with that Symbolism.

## Symbolic Derivation

The source describes Endpoint as the common final destination established through [[Eternal Rest]].
