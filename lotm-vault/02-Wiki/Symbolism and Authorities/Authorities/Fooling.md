# Fooling

## Meaning

Fooling is one of the Fool Pathway's two core Authorities. It makes an already established falsehood real and applies limited effects to History, Time, Fate, Change, Concealment, and cognition. [[Foolishness (Fool)|Blind Stupidity]] is its corresponding mind-domain symbolism, not another name for the Authority.

## Expression in the Novels

- Klein Fooled History by separating adjacent states of himself and exchanging a trapped past condition with his present position.
- During the final conflict with Amon, he Fooled Time's order so Amon's Worms of Time split at the wrong moment and moved despite temporal freezing.
- In Chapter 1384, Grafting supplied a star concept and Fooling made the surrounding environment behave according to that concept.

## Limits

- A false premise must exist before Fooling can make it true; it does not create an undefined premise from nothing.
- Fooling Time disrupts order, timing, or temporal restrictions. It is not Error's direct acceleration, slowing, stopping, or reversal of Time.
- Established History cannot be rewritten. The Fool can temporarily play with its manifestation.
- Instinct-driven behavior resists cognitive Fooling better than deliberate reasoning.

## Community Cross-check

Reddit's “false code accepted by reality” analogy captures the premise-to-truth mechanism. Claims that Fooling alone grants unrestricted reality rewriting exceed the author's stated conditions.
