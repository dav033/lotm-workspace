# Enslavement (Death Authority)

Enslavement is a [[Death Pathway (disambiguation)]] Authority for control and suppression of subordinate spirits or undead. It is a pathway expression of [[Enslavement (Eternal Darkness Symbolism)]], not proof that every Eternal Darkness Pathway possesses it natively.

## Symbolic Derivation

The group Symbolism and Pathway Authority are separately reported; their parent-expression relation is a strong inference rather than a direct derivation statement.
