# Manufacturing (Demon of Knowledge)

The Fandom source calls Manufacturing an Authority at Great Old One level and reports that it can treat a Sefirah as material for a Sealed Artifact. The research dossier maps Manufacture principally to Paragon; this note does not promote Technology, Alchemy, or every crafted object to independent Authorities.

## Symbolic Derivation

Manufacturing is reported as a subordinate realization of combined knowledge under [[Knowledge and Information (Demon of Knowledge)]]. Great Old One scale changes its reach, not its parent.
