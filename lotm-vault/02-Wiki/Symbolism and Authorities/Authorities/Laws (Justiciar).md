# Laws (Justiciar)

## Meaning

Laws is a subordinate [[Justiciar Pathway (disambiguation)]] Authority under [[Order (Justiciar)]]. Here Laws refers primarily to codified and enforceable decrees, associated with 律法 (*lüfa*), rather than every natural regularity.

## Expression in the Novels

- At Sequence 2, Balancers can issue several Laws or Decrees that prohibit actions and modify the environment. *Circle of Inevitability* Chapter 1138 indexes a “Land of Light” decree within a larger ordered battlefield.
- The Trunsoest Brass Book turns written provisions into a jurisdiction with escalating punishments, reaching death for repeated or severe violations (*Lord of Mysteries* Chapter 1288).
- At higher scope, Laws operate as coordinated statutes rather than isolated verbal commands, supporting [[Judgement (Justiciar)]] and the stability of [[Order (Justiciar)]].

## Limits

- English translations may render 律法, 定律, and 规律 with overlapping words. The Chinese distinction must be preserved when classifying the Authority.
- Enforcement depends on jurisdiction, promulgation, Sequence, and resistance.
- This note must not be merged with [[Laws (Demon of Knowledge Symbolism)]] or treated as ownership of every physical law.

## Symbolic Derivation

Laws inherits [[Order (The Anarchy Symbolism)]] through [[Order (Justiciar)]].
