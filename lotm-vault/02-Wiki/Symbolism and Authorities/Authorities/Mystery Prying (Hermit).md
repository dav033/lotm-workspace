# Mystery Prying (Hermit)

Mystery Prying is the [[Hermit Pathway (disambiguation)]] Authority for perceiving or uncovering hidden mystical information. It is not equivalent to ordinary observation or general analysis.

## Counter-relation

The supplied source reports conditional mutual restraint with [[Concealment (Darkness)]]. See [[Demon of Knowledge Group Counters]].

## Symbolic Derivation

The source explains secrets as knowledge, placing Mystery Prying under [[Knowledge and Information (Demon of Knowledge)]].
