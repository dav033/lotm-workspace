# Deviants (Chained)

The secondary evidence set lists Deviants as a central Authority of the [[Chained Pathway (disambiguation)|Chained Pathway]].

Related group: [[Father of Devils Group]].

## Meaning

Deviants are monsters produced by negative Beyonder Characteristic effects, including Devils and beings that have lost control. Chained is indexed as their King: it can assume temporary Deviant forms, use the form's corresponding abilities, naturally suppress Deviants, and turn low- or mid-Sequence members of the category into servants.

## Expression in the Novels

- Sequence 4 Performance reproduces Prisoner, Lunatic, Werewolf, Zombie, and Wraith forms at a weak demigod level, establishing the collection of forms later governed by Deviants.
- Sequence 1 Abomination can deform nearby creatures or temporarily transform them into Werewolves, Zombies, Puppets, and related species before madness destroys the unstable result.
- *Lord of Mysteries* Chapters 1234 and 1235 are indexed for Suah's roar, surrounding madness, environmental sealing, and mutation caused by Abomination Godhood.
- *Lord of Mysteries* Chapter 1234 is also indexed for Suah using a prayer that receives a projection response from the Chained God, illustrating the prayer-response relation between a Deviant and Chained.
- Sequence 0 scope, including temporary transformation into different Deviants and suppression of the category, comes from archived author material.

## Limits

- Controlling or suppressing a Deviant does not grant every Authority possessed by that being.
- Low- and mid-Sequence servant conversion is reported; equivalent domination of Angel- or deity-level Deviants is not established by the cited evidence.
- Assumed Deviant forms are temporary.

## Symbolic Derivation

The Chained Deviants Authority is jointly concentrated by [[Binding (Father of Devils)]] and [[Unity-Collection (Chained)]].

## Community Cross-check

Reddit debate directly exposes the common overreach that Chained can use every Authority owned by every lost-control Beyonder. Corrective discussion rejects that inference, matching the narrower evidence.
