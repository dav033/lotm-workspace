# Spirit World Dominion

The supplied Fandom source describes Lord of Mysteries as able to exercise control over the Spirit World. This note records that reported group-level Authority and does not grant it as native ownership to any individual Pathway.

## Scope

- Related Symbolism: [[Dominator of the Spirit World]]
- Group: [[Lord of Mysteries Group]]

## Symbolic Derivation

This group-level Authority is the jurisdictional expression of [[Dominator of the Spirit World]]. It is not native ownership by every member Pathway.
