# Rules (Justiciar)

## Meaning

Rules is a subordinate [[Justiciar Pathway (disambiguation)]] Authority under [[Order (Justiciar)]]. A rule is an operative prescription or condition: it names allowed or forbidden conduct and attaches an effect to compliance or violation.

## Expression in the Novels

- Sequence 6 Judge applies local verdicts such as prohibiting flight, concealment, or escape.
- Sequence 4 Imperative Mage stacks commands and prohibitions to shape a battlefield.
- Sequence 1 expands rule-making to a battlefield, city, or small nation and can temporarily modify or remove non-underlying rules.
- At Sequence 0, the Trunsoest Brass Book's accumulating rules regulate historical projections, rationality thresholds, sacrifices, resurrection, movement, and punishment (*Lord of Mysteries* Chapters 1280 and 1283-1289).

## Limits

- A declared rule is not automatically a fundamental law of reality.
- Rules require sufficient announcement or publicity, operate within scope, and also constrain their maker unless removed or amended.
- Complex rule systems can be disrupted, contradicted, exploited, or resisted; mutually supporting rules are stronger than an isolated command.

## Symbolic Derivation

Rules inherits [[Order (The Anarchy Symbolism)]] through [[Order (Justiciar)]].

## Community Cross-check

Reddit discussion often calls every Justiciar effect “law manipulation.” This note keeps the narrower distinction between an operative Rule, an enforceable Law, and a regular Principle.
