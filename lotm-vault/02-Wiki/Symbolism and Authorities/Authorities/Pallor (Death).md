# Pallor (Death)

Pallor is a [[Death Pathway (disambiguation)]] Authority that withers color, vitality, activity, powers, or manifestations. It is an Authority expression, not a separate claim that every loss of color is metaphysical death.

## Symbolic Derivation

Pallor is strongly associated with the combined finality of [[Death (Eternal Darkness Symbolism)]] and [[Eternal Rest]], but the exact partition is not stated.
