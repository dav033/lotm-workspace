# Order (Justiciar)

## Meaning

Order is the core [[Justiciar Pathway (disambiguation)]] Authority corresponding to [[Order (The Anarchy Symbolism)]]. It establishes a stable normative structure: what is permitted, prohibited, required, and punished. [[Rules (Justiciar)]], [[Laws (Justiciar)]], and [[Principles (Justiciar)]] are subordinate aspects rather than interchangeable labels.

## Expression in the Novels

- Early Sequences develop authority of speech, verdicts, prohibitions, and local rules. Sequence 6 Judge makes declarations such as prohibiting flight or escape enforceable within scope.
- Sequence 4 Imperative Mage turns commands into environmental restrictions; Sequence 1 Hand of Order can establish, modify, or remove non-underlying rules across a battlefield, city, or small nation.
- William Augustus sensing the collapse of George III's order and the isolation of destructive aftershocks are indexed in *Lord of Mysteries* Chapters 1150 and 1148.
- At Sequence 0, the Trunsoest Brass Book demonstrates large rule systems, escalating enforcement, and interference with resurrection and movement in *Lord of Mysteries* Chapters 1280 and 1283-1289.

## Limits

- Rules must be declared or publicized sufficiently for the governed scope, and they can bind the creator as well as the target.
- Modification of underlying world rules is limited; non-underlying rules are easier to establish, alter, or remove.
- Order does not automatically grant every application of law, judgment, balance, or physical regularity without the relevant subordinate Authority and conditions.

## Symbolic Derivation

This is the core Authority expression of [[Order (The Anarchy Symbolism)]].

## Community Cross-check

Community discussion usefully contrasts Justiciar legislation with Black Emperor exploitation. It becomes misleading when it treats every spoken command as absolute or ignores jurisdiction, announcement, resistance, and duration.
