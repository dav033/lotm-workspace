# Binding (Chained)

The secondary evidence set lists Binding, also rendered as Restraint or Temperance, as a central Authority of the [[Chained Pathway (disambiguation)|Chained Pathway]].

Related group: [[Father of Devils Group]].

## Meaning

Binding actively restrains tendencies, states, and connections. Its indexed targets include madness, desire, objects, creatures, Beyonder Characteristics, convergence, and direct use of the Spirit World. Temperance is the restrictive mode; Indulgence is produced by removing that restraint, not by a separate equal Authority.

## Expression in the Novels

- Sequence 9 begins with spirituality and desire constrained by reason, body, and world; Sequence 8 extends Binding across body and soul, interfering with divination and Spirit Channeling.
- Sequence 4 Objectification restrains internal madness and desire by turning the user into an inert object and can temporarily seal another willing target's madness. *Lord of Mysteries* Chapter 881 is indexed for restraining convergence.
- At Sequence 1, Suah makes body and soul lose harmony, turns the body into a prison for spirit, raises environmental barriers, and progressively disrupts Reality-Spirit World overlap; *Lord of Mysteries* Chapters 1041 and 1234 are indexed for these manifestations.
- At Sequence 0, the author supplement extends restraint to creatures, objects, Beyonder Characteristics, and Spirit World-dependent powers.
- At Father of Devils level, *Circle of Inevitability* Chapter 1174 shows an Abomination characteristic acting as a sealing vessel for the weakened Supernova Dominator for two or three minutes. This is group-level symbolism, not a scale automatically available to Chained alone.

## Limits

- Binding overlaps with Sealing but is not identical to the Door Authority: the indexed author explanation gives Chained broader restraint below full Sealing and Door superiority in Sealing itself.
- No evidence supports binding every arbitrary abstraction merely because the word can be used metaphorically.

## Symbolic Derivation

Binding is one of the Chained Authorities jointly concentrated by [[Binding (Father of Devils)]] and [[Unity-Collection (Chained)]].

## Community Cross-check

Reddit correctly emphasizes that Binding exceeds physical chains, but claims about freely binding any concept, Authority, or corruption remain unverified extrapolations.
