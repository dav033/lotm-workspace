# Principles (Paragon)

Principles, 规律 (*guilu*), is the [[Paragon Pathway (disambiguation)]] Authority over inherent patterns and underlying workings of reality. It remains distinct from [[Laws (Demon of Knowledge Symbolism)]] and from the White Tower-specific [[Principles (White Tower)]].

## Symbolic Derivation

The author material states that Principles are part of Knowledge, placing this Authority under [[Knowledge and Information (Demon of Knowledge)]], not [[Laws (Demon of Knowledge Symbolism)]].
