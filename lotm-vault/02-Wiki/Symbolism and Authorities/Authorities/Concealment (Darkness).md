# Concealment (Darkness)

Concealment is a [[Darkness Pathway (disambiguation)]] Authority over hiding information, traces, entities, events, or spaces. Its highest reported erasure expression is related to [[Singularity of All]], but the Authority is not identical to that symbolism.

## Counter-relation

The supplied source reports a conditional mutual restraint with Mystery Prying. See [[Eternal Darkness Group Counters]].

## Symbolic Derivation

[[Silence (Darkness)]] strongly supports Concealment through removal of information and activity. [[Singularity of All]] relates to its highest erasure expression but is not established as the immediate parent.
