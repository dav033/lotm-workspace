# Deceit (Error)

## Meaning

Deceit makes a target, body, natural rule, or mystical mechanism accept a misleading interpretation. The Chinese source distinguishes lower `欺瞒` at Mentor of Deceit from higher `欺诈`, the Sequence 0 Authority and Lord of Mysteries symbolism; English translations often render both as Deceit.

## Expression in the Novels

- Mentor-level Deceit causes opponents to ignore obvious problems, misjudge situations, or let their own body act according to a false premise.
- Amon deceives Klein about motives and mental state while exploiting the resulting wrong judgments.
- At higher Sequences, Deceit misdirects rules that Angels must obey. Klein used Error-pathway power through Sefirah Castle to make resurrection rules accept blessed blood as the relevant corpse location.

## Limits

- Lower Deceit depends on misdirection, observed weaknesses, and a plausible target for the false interpretation; it is not full mind control.
- Deceiving rules does not automatically equal creating an Error. The two Authorities cooperate but remain distinct.
- Translation overlap must not erase the level difference between Mentor of Deceit ability and Sequence 0 Authority.

## Community Cross-check

Community summaries often merge Swindler charm, Mentor of Deceit, rule deception, and Sequence 0 Authority into one effect. The author post presents a progression with distinct levels.
