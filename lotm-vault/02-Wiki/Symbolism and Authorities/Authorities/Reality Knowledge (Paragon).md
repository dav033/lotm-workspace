# Reality Knowledge (Paragon)

Reality Knowledge is the [[Paragon Pathway (disambiguation)]] Authority over knowledge of physical structures, mechanisms, and the workings of reality. Knowledge of a phenomenon does not automatically establish unrestricted ownership of that phenomenon.
