# Twilight (Authority)

Twilight is the [[Twilight Giant Pathway (disambiguation)]] Authority that imposes decline, weakening, and conclusion. The source relates it to [[Decay (Twilight Giant)]], [[Passage of Time (Twilight Giant)]], light, and space-time without reducing it to any one of them.

## Symbolic Derivation

The author material derives Twilight jointly from [[Light (Twilight Giant Symbolism)]], [[Space-Time (Twilight Giant Symbolism)]], and [[Decay (Twilight Giant)]]. [[Passage of Time (Twilight Giant)]] remains related but is not substituted for the named Space-Time parent.
