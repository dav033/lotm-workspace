# Death (Authority)

Death is the [[Death Pathway (disambiguation)]] Authority that imposes death on life, spirit, and higher-order targets within stated limits. It remains separate from [[Death (Eternal Darkness Symbolism)]].

## Limits

The supplied source reports contact, range, target scale, and equivalent symbolism as constraints. See [[Eternal Darkness Group Counters]].

## Symbolic Derivation

The same concept is assigned as both Symbolism and Authority, making [[Death (Eternal Darkness Symbolism)]] the strongest supported parent, although the available secondary source does not state a formal derivation sentence.
