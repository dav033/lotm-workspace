# Miracles (Fool)

## Meaning

Miracles is the Authority belonging to the Inconceivable/Bizarreness symbolism. Its results are called Miracles because their realization is not normally explainable or comprehensible. At Sequence 2 it operates through accumulated fulfilled wishes; Sequence 0 expands its scope and resurrection capacity.

## Expression in the Novels

- Miracle Invokers grant small wishes to accumulate power for larger ones.
- Klein shrank Belltaine's library area to constrain 0-02. Kotar suppressed Godhood and high-Sequence powers in an area. Antigonus brought part of an ancient Divine Kingdom from memory into reality.
- Resurrection rebuilds a Miracle Invoker through past Worms of Spirit in the Historical Void. The Fool can reset this count once and has a higher upper limit.

## Limits

- Large Miracles require suitable prior wish accumulation; stronger or broader wishes distort more easily.
- Similar wishes cannot be repeatedly granted to the same person in a short period.
- Direct changes to high-level targets are weaker, less reliable, or distorted.
- Miracle Invoker resurrection has a limited count, timing, location, and Historical Void mechanism. It is not unconditional immortality.

## Community Cross-check

Community shorthand often calls Miracles unrestricted reality warping. The novel's accumulation, distortion, repetition, target-level, and resurrection limits contradict that reading.
