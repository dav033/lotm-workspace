# Filth (Hanged Man)

Sequence 0 explicitly states "He" holds partial authority over "the domains of Shadow, Darkness, Degeneration/Depravity, Filth, and Mutation" and names **Filth** (**Incarnation of Filth**) directly among the Authorities of the [[Hanged Man Pathway (disambiguation)|Hanged Man Pathway]].

Related group: [[God Almighty Group]].

## Symbolic Derivation

Described as arising from being "tainted by the [[Chaos Sea]]"; cited evidence does not establish an independent parent Symbolism.
