# Transformation (Fool)

## Meaning

Transformation is a Fool Pathway Authority under the group-level [[Change (Lord of Mysteries)]] symbolism. It is the high-level culmination of bodily transformation and shapeshifting, not unrestricted ownership of Change and not another name for Grafting.

## Expression in the Novels

- Faceless controls facial features, body shape, voice, smell, and disguise within physical limits.
- Higher Sequences expand Shapeshifting into animal and Spirit Body forms and use it throughout Klein's identities and combat disguises.
- The author supplement confirms Transformation as a Sequence 0 Authority. The original novel gives little isolated Sequence 0 use because Klein advances near the final battle.

## Limits

- Low and middle Sequences retain height, mass, anatomy, and actual-sex limits; higher Sequences loosen those restrictions.
- The author post names Transformation under Change but does not grant arbitrary transmutation of all matter, concepts, or reality.
- Reassembly changes relationships or configurations; Transformation changes form. Their scopes can cooperate without becoming identical.

## Community Cross-check

Community pages often label this simply “Change Authority.” The exact author wording is narrower: Transformation is one Authority belonging to Change symbolism.
