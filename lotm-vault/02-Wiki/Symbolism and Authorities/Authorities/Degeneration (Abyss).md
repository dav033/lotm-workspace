# Degeneration (Abyss)

The secondary evidence set lists Degeneration, also translated as Depravity, as a partial Authority of the [[Abyss Pathway (disambiguation)|Abyss Pathway]].

Related group: [[Father of Devils Group]].

## Meaning

Degeneration is an imposed condition in which reason, values, conduct, or rule-obedience descend into a degraded state. The indexed author supplement distinguishes it from the [[Hanged Man Pathway (disambiguation)|Hanged Man Pathway]]: Abyss acts as an environment that induces and creates degeneration rather than merely embodying an existing degenerate nature.

## Expression in the Novels

- Sequence 5 Desire Control can catalyze an emotion until the target becomes unable to resist, suffers gradual degeneration, or experiences an explosion of desires.
- At Sequence 4, amplified sorrow, anger, greed, vanity, and similar motives can destroy cognition, produce insanity, or initiate loss of control; the ability index points to *Lord of Mysteries* Chapter 1077 for Demon-level manifestations.
- *Circle of Inevitability* Chapter 1134 is indexed for Katarina hearing her own malice and desires as ravings and showing signs of losing control.
- Sequence 0 scope is supplied by the archived author material, not by an isolated on-page Abyss battle in the novels.

## Limits

- This is a partial Authority. The same evidence says Degeneration reaches its complete Abyss form only when joined with the relevant Mind component.
- Evil conduct is an outcome or sign, not proof that every immoral act was caused by this Authority.

## Symbolic Derivation

Degeneration is one of the Mind-oriented Authorities reflecting [[Abyss of the Heart and Mind]].

## Community Cross-check

Reddit discussion commonly describes Abyss as an environment of degeneration. Claims that it can automatically collapse whole civilizations remain extrapolation, not a demonstrated novel feat.
