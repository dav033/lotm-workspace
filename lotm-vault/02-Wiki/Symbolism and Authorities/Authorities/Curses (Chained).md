# Curses (Chained)

The secondary evidence set lists Curses as a central Authority of the [[Chained Pathway (disambiguation)|Chained Pathway]].

Related group: [[Father of Devils Group]].

## Meaning

Chained Curses impose adverse states through a connection, medium, object, identity, core information, reflection, body-spirit relation, or environment. Their range includes harm, banishment, fixation, sleep, transfiguration, spiritual restriction, and sudden death. At Sequence 0 they can carry surface-level symbolic effects.

## Expression in the Novels

- Sequence 4 Sharron establishes a connection to Klarman and reflects damage through a matching puppet form; *Lord of Mysteries* Chapter 1236 is indexed for the scene.
- Sequence 3 removes the need for symmetric self-harm. *Circle of Inevitability* Chapters 1133, 773, and 1136 are indexed for Sharron's banishment, fixation, and Transfiguration Curse applications.
- Sequence 2 can curse remotely from core information or a prepared vessel. *Lord of Mysteries* Chapter 1344 is indexed for Suah's projection using Klein's Worm of Spirit to isolate the main body from Marionettes and Sefirah Castle.
- *Lord of Mysteries* Chapter 1235 is indexed for Reinette rooting a Curse in anger, hatred, and grievance to stagger Suah.
- Sequence 1 Curses can pass through a Marionette or Historical Projection to the true body and can curse the environment, body-soul harmony, and Spirit World overlap.
- Sequence 0 is indexed as difficult to curse, resistant to weak symbolic connections, and able to complete many types of Curse without the target noticing.

## Limits

- The source explicitly states that no Curse is irreversible; a correct counter, loophole, or equal-level resistance may remove it.
- Full immunity to every Curse, automatic ownership of enemy Curses, and unlimited symbolic reach are not established.

## Symbolic Derivation

The Chained Curse Authority is jointly concentrated by [[Binding (Father of Devils)]] and [[Unity-Collection (Chained)]].

## Community Cross-check

Reddit usefully highlights Curse breadth but often upgrades resistance into total immunity. This note retains only the narrower indexed claim.
