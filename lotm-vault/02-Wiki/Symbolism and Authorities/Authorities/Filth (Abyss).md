# Filth (Abyss)

The secondary evidence set lists Filth as a partial Authority of the [[Abyss Pathway (disambiguation)|Abyss Pathway]].

Related group: [[Father of Devils Group]].

## Meaning

Filth governs the unclean as an active contaminating condition, not ordinary dirt. Its indexed scope includes dirty, sinful, and impure qualities and their effects on body, mind, and soul. Toxins are treated as a subordinate expression.

## Expression in the Novels

- Devil-level Language of Foulness carries Filth and Corruption through words, while sulfurous fire, poison, and foul spells make the condition physical.
- Demon-level powers include Filthy Illusion and expanded Filthy Language. *Circle of Inevitability* Chapter 1100 is indexed for the Montsouris Ghost spreading intense malice through a foul declaration; Unshadowed targets resist through purification.
- At Sequence 1, the author supplement describes the Filthy Monarch as contaminating even same-level Angels by sight or proximity, except those protected by Sun-like purification.
- Abyss flames combine burning, corrosion, toxins, and corruption, showing that Filth is expressed through layered contamination rather than one damage type.

## Limits

- Filth does not equal Corruption: Filth supplies the unclean condition, while Corruption describes alteration or pollution of the target.
- Same-level contamination and the complete Sequence 0 formulation come from archived author material; the current evidence does not identify a standalone Sequence 0 Abyss scene displaying the full Authority.

## Symbolic Derivation

Filth is one of the Mind-oriented Authorities reflecting [[Abyss of the Heart and Mind]].

## Community Cross-check

Community descriptions that treat Filth as generic disease, ecological rot, or automatic control of every evil being exceed the indexed scope.
