# Objects (Chained)

The secondary evidence set lists Objects as a central Authority of the [[Chained Pathway (disambiguation)|Chained Pathway]].

Related group: [[Father of Devils Group]].

## Meaning

Objects governs control of sentient and non-sentient objects and the use of objects as vessels for Curses, identities, restraints, and collected Deviant forms. Objectification makes a being or dangerous state controllable by fixing it into an object condition.

## Expression in the Novels

- Sequence 4 Puppet awakens and controls lifeless objects, ownerless Mystical Items, weapons, clothing, glass, and machinery. *Lord of Mysteries* Chapters 726, 881, and 1236 are indexed for object attacks, clothes restraining a target, machinery control, and control of an ownerless Sealed Artifact.
- Objectification turns the user into an inert Puppet to restrain madness and desire; with consent and contact it can temporarily seal another person's madness.
- Sequence 3 can possess objects and use Transfiguration Curse on living or non-living targets, joining Object identity with Curse.
- Sequence 2 requires a dwelling or vessel that binds the invisible Curse aggregate; destroying that object exposes a major vulnerability.
- Sequence 0 is indexed as controlling all objects. An archived author Q&A says an unclaimed Sefirah can be treated as an Object after a connection is established, permitting indirect influence.
- Thorn Paradise expresses the combined structure: Curses bound into objects and Deviants fixed as statues that can be awakened.

## Limits

- Indirectly influencing an unclaimed Sefirah is not ownership, accommodation, or full control.
- Evidence does not define every concept, Authority, world, or living being as an Object.
- Lower-sequence control has ownership, contact, range, and resistance constraints that should not be erased when discussing progression.

## Symbolic Derivation

Objects is one of the Chained Authorities jointly concentrated by [[Binding (Father of Devils)]] and [[Unity-Collection (Chained)]].

## Community Cross-check

Reddit discussion correctly flags the Sefirah example but often extends it to every Authority or concept. Those extensions are excluded.
