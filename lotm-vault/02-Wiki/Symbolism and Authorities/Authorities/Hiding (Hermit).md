# Hiding (Hermit)

Hiding is a [[Hermit Pathway (disambiguation)]] Authority over concealing knowledge and information. It must not be merged with [[Concealment (Darkness)]], which has a different Pathway and symbolic basis.

## Symbolic Derivation

Hiding belongs to the mystical-facing portion of [[Knowledge and Information (Demon of Knowledge)]].
