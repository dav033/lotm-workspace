# Error (Authority)

## Meaning

Error is the Sequence 0 jurisdiction over Bugs, loopholes, contradictions, and exceptions in normal or supernatural rules. It can create, exploit, redirect, or repair an Error when at least one stated route makes the exploit possible. It is an Erroneous Change expression, not arbitrary omnipotence.

## Expression in the Novels

- Amon equates an Avatar with the main body for selected effects, allowing an Avatar's escape or burden to stand in for the original.
- Chapter 1386 demonstrates final-battle exploit construction through similarities, connections, and imperfect rules.
- Error can redirect attacks or consequences, imitate permissions through a sufficiently similar target, and build a Divine Kingdom whose environment is filled with exploitable Errors.

## Limits

The author gives five activation routes:

1. Medium and target have sufficient similarity.
2. A real connection already exists.
3. The relevant thing contains a logical contradiction.
4. A rule is genuinely incomplete.
5. The user affects the target indirectly by constructing an Erroneous environment.

Without one of these routes, “Amon finds a bug” is not established. Stronger targets and higher-level rules still resist.

## Community Cross-check

Software-bug and speedrun-glitch analogies explain exploitation well. They become misleading when used to claim Error can ignore any law without similarity, connection, contradiction, imperfection, or prepared environment.
