# Proxy (Twilight Giant)

Proxy is a [[Twilight Giant Pathway (disambiguation)]] Authority for acting as the representative of a higher entity or force. The supplied material treats it as a pathway manifestation related to [[Enslavement (Eternal Darkness Symbolism)]], not as native Death-pathway Enslavement.

## Symbolic Derivation

The author material directly describes Proxy as the Twilight Giant manifestation of [[Enslavement (Eternal Darkness Symbolism)]].
