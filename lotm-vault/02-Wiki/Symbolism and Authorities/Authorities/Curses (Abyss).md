# Curses (Abyss)

The secondary evidence set lists Curses as a partial Authority of the [[Abyss Pathway (disambiguation)|Abyss Pathway]].

Related group: [[Father of Devils Group]].

## Meaning

Abyss Curses impose harmful conditions mainly through mind and spirit. Speech, desire, malice, and an established mystical connection act as recurrent carriers. This differs from the Chained emphasis on objects, identity, transformation, imprisonment, and body-spirit relations.

## Expression in the Novels

- Devil-level Language of Foulness uses words such as Slow, Death, and Corruption to impose mental, bodily, or environmental harm.
- Sequence 3 Blatherer can turn every spoken word into confusion, severe damage, loss of control, or a Curse; an established connection permits a voice to reach and kill a distant target.
- Higher Abyss flames combine Curse with degeneration, corruption, toxins, and corrosion rather than acting as ordinary fire.
- At Father of Devils level, *Circle of Inevitability* Chapters 1164 and 1168 show curses propagating through avatars or attachments to reach a true body, and blood-colored flames transforming Great Old Ones into animals. Those combined feats must not be assigned to Abyss alone without direct evidence.

## Limits

- Current material gives no isolated Sequence 0 Abyss scene demonstrating the full Curse Authority.
- Shared outcome does not make Abyss Curses and [[Curses (Chained)]] the same mechanism.

## Symbolic Derivation

The Abyss Curse Authority is one of the five Mind-oriented Authorities reflecting [[Abyss of the Heart and Mind]].

## Community Cross-check

Reddit descriptions often import Chained transfiguration or Great Old One animal curses into Abyss. Those transfers are retained only as rejected extrapolations.
