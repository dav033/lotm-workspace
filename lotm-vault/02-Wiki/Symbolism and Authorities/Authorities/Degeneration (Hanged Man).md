# Degeneration (Hanged Man)

The secondary evidence set lists partial Authority over Degeneration, also translated as Depravity, among the Authorities of the [[Hanged Man Pathway (disambiguation)|Hanged Man Pathway]].

Related group: [[God Almighty Group]].

## Meaning

Hanged Man Degeneration acts through the inherent degenerate or depraved nature within a living being. Its characteristic mode is self-corruption: the target's own thoughts, body, shadow, projection, object, or ability is made to corrode, turn inward, or betray its owner.

This differs from [[Degeneration (Abyss)]]. Hanged Man exposes and weaponizes an internal nature; Abyss creates an environment that induces and produces degeneration.

## Expression in the Novels

- Sequence 4 Black Knight expresses the domain through Cull of Spiritual Flesh, Black Armor, and Shadow of Depravation. These effects corrode flesh and soul or separate depraved thoughts into hostile shadows; the ability inventory indexes *Lord of Mysteries* Chapter 1256.
- Sequence 2 Profane Presbyter combines Degeneration with Filth through Profane Language. It can extract ambiguity from speech, induce spiritual descent, and pull out degenerate thoughts; *Lord of Mysteries* Chapter 1182 and *Circle of Inevitability* Chapter 1133 are indexed manifestations.
- At Sequence 1, Dark Angel can make people, bodies, objects, projections, and abilities corrode or betray their owner. Sasrir making Lovia's body betray her is indexed at *Lord of Mysteries* Chapter 1262; Profane Language's life-drain and madness effects are indexed at Chapter 1264.
- At Sequence 0, the Authority is described as the Degenerate Nature of living things. Shadows emerge from the target's psyche and reshape the target from within; *Circle of Inevitability* Chapter 1137 is the indexed scene.

## Limits

- Sequence 0 retains a partial Authority classification. It does not establish ownership of every form of evil, moral failure, betrayal, or corruption.
- Degeneration can be a progressive descent rather than an instantaneous result. Status, resistance, purification, or suppression of the relevant internal nature can limit the effect.
- [[Corruption (Hanged Man)]], [[Filth (Hanged Man)]], [[Mutation (Hanged Man)]], [[Darkness (Hanged Man)]], and [[Shadows (Hanged Man)]] interact with Degeneration but remain distinct Authorities.

## Ontology Boundary

The source taxonomy identifies Degeneration as a Pathway Authority. Sin-Bearing and Sacrifice remain Symbolisms, while Profane Language, Cull of Spiritual Flesh, and Depraved Dependents are manifestations or Powers rather than separate Degeneration Authorities.

## Community Cross-check

Reddit discussions usually preserve the internal-nature distinction from Abyss. Claims that Degeneration cannot be resisted under any circumstances, or that every immoral act demonstrates this Authority, are rejected as overreach.
