# Grafting and Reassembly

## Meaning

Reassembly, also translated as Tampering or Grafting, is the Fool Pathway's other core Authority. It combines physical entities, locations, abstract concepts, or Authorities that originally lacked a connection, producing an inconceivable but real configuration. It arises from Change mixed with Bizarreness.

## Expression in the Novels

- An Attendant of Mysteries can join a road's beginning and end into one node, trapping travel in a loop, or connect distant locations for movement.
- Klein redirected Amon's Theft target, joined the relevant mental imprints during the Uniqueness accommodation, and connected prayers or anchors to new targets.
- Chapters 1378 and 1384 show high-level target redirection, location Grafting, and the conceptual star later made environmentally real through Fooling.
- Damage Transfer and Air Pipes are lower, specialized applications named by the author.

## Limits

- Grafting connects or reorganizes existing components; it does not create the component concepts from nothing.
- A conceptual connection does not automatically force the environment to behave physically like the concept. The star example required Fooling.
- Removing the Reassembly ends its dependent result. Extremely high-level combinations can have short durations and require preparation or a usable connection.
- Scale and resistance still depend on level, target, arrangement, and available concepts; the post does not grant unlimited range or omnipotence.

## Community Cross-check

Readers broadly agree on “connect first, make reality accept it second” for Grafting plus Fooling. Community claims of arbitrary country-scale or concept-free Grafting remain speculation.
