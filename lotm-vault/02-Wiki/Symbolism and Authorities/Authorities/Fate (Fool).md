# Fate (Fool)

## Meaning

Fate is a restricted Fool jurisdiction, not a separately named unrestricted Authority. It appears through Fooling Fate, Miracle Invoker probability interference, and [[Anchor of Destiny (Fool)]]. It does not equal [[Fate (Wheel of Fortune)]] or [[Destiny (Error)]].

## Expression in the Novels

- Miracle Invoker's Jamming in Fate raises or lowers the probability of a future result.
- Sequence 0 advancement can be completed by Fooling Fate once, establishing that Fate lies within Fooling's partial reach.
- The novel does not clearly dramatize Anchor of Destiny as a separate named technique before the later author supplement defined it.

## Limits

- Jamming changes probability only to a degree; it is explicitly different from Wheel of Fortune manipulation.
- Fooling Fate is an application of Fooling, not proof that the Fool owns all Fate.
- Anchor of Destiny stabilizes and influences future nodes, but does not guarantee the intended form of the result.
- Exact boundaries between these three manifestations remain incompletely stated.

## Community Cross-check

Community debate often treats any relation to Beacon of Destiny as full Fate ownership. The author post instead names Anchor of Destiny as the Pathway Authority and leaves other Fate effects partial.
