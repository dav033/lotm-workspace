# Disorder (Black Emperor)

## Meaning

The indexed author supplement names this Authority 混乱 (*hunluan*). The [[Black Emperor Pathway (disambiguation)]] possesses the larger, active share: it disorganizes judgment, direction, measurement, structure, accuracy, abilities, thoughts, or rules until an ordered process becomes unreliable or collapses.

## Expression in the Novels

- Mentor of Disorder makes attacks miss, induces wrong decisions, disrupts distance, and makes movement point in an unexpected direction (*Lord of Mysteries* Chapters 808-809 and 971).
- Duke of Entropy develops the effect into Frenzy: abilities and battlefield changes can become randomly beneficial or harmful. The Concerto of Light and Shadow fight indexes this at *Lord of Mysteries* Chapter 1027.
- At Sequence 2, Entropy extends randomness and loss of control to abilities, states, surroundings, rules, and thought.
- At Sequence 0, Disorder can break ordered concepts into total disorder and make thought uncontrollable, indexed at *Lord of Mysteries* Chapter 1304.

## Limits

- Disorder is not the source of [[Distortion (Black Emperor)]]. Distortion changes a relationship or meaning; Disorder destroys reliability and arrangement.
- The user can create favorable chaos but does not necessarily control every random outcome.
- This Authority must not be conflated with 失序 (*shixu*) in the titles The Anarchy and Nation of Disorder, the limited [[Disorder (Justiciar)]], or the fate-oriented [[Disorder (Wheel of Fortune)]].

## Community Cross-check

Reddit comparisons generally preserve the distinction between active Black Emperor entropy and corrective Justiciar Disorder. Claims that every unpredictable event belongs to this Authority remain unsupported.
