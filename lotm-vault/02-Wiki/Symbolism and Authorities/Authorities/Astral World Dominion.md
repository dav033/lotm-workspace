# Astral World Dominion

The supplied Fandom source reports that God Almighty can control and partially seal the Astral World. This is modeled as a group-level Authority; it is not assigned as native ownership to a member Pathway.

## Scope

- Related Symbolism: [[Lord of the Astral World]]
- Group: [[God Almighty Group]]

## Symbolic Derivation

This group-level Authority is the jurisdictional expression of [[Lord of the Astral World]], not native ownership by every member Pathway.
