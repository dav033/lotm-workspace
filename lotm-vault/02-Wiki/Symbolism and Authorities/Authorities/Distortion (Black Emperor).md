# Distortion (Black Emperor)

## Meaning

Distortion is a subordinate [[Black Emperor Pathway (disambiguation)]] Authority under [[Autocrat (Black Emperor)]]. It changes how an existing word, action, intention, target, outcome, relationship, or rule operates. The altered result must still be built from a relationship the user can seize; Distortion is not a synonym for arbitrary reality rewriting.

## Expression in the Novels

- At Sequence 6, it redirects a bullet, the Door to the Underworld, and Sulfur Fireballs, or turns closing a door into sealing a room (*Lord of Mysteries* Chapters 599, 659, 684, and 630).
- At Sequence 5, it can make two parties share a curse or disease effect (*Lord of Mysteries* Chapter 425).
- Qonas Kilgor's Sequence 4 battle shows Distortion combined with Exploit, Magnify, and Disorder to redirect attacks, alter injuries, and manipulate states (*Lord of Mysteries* Chapters 1026-1027).
- At Sequence 2, Xio distorts the target of a Balance effect and redirects a Planeswalker battlefield (*Circle of Inevitability* Chapters 1133 and 1138).
- Sequence 1 and 0 expand the target to substitutes, destinations, authority-created rules, and mystical connections, as indexed in *Lord of Mysteries* Chapters 1144, 1148, and 1305 and *Circle of Inevitability* Chapters 875 and 883.

## Limits

- The examples do not establish control of all causality or the ability to produce any desired outcome.
- Stronger targets, underlying rules, and mystical connections impose conditions and resistance.
- Notarization is reported as a mutual counter: Distortion may affect a contract, but it does not automatically erase every clause or enforcement mechanism.

## Counter-relation

The supplied source reports a mutual counter-relation with Notarization. See [[The Anarchy Group Counters]].

## Symbolic Derivation

Distortion inherits [[Shadow of Order]] through its immediate parent Authority, [[Autocrat (Black Emperor)]]. It is not derived from Disorder.

## Community Cross-check

Community summaries frequently say Distortion can “do anything.” This batch rejects that phrasing because the indexed scenes consistently use a prior action, target, rule, intent, or connection as the object of change.
