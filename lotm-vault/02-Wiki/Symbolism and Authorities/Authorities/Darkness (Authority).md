# Darkness (Authority)

The [[Darkness Pathway (disambiguation)]] Authority concerns imposing supernatural darkness and related stillness. It is distinct from [[Darkness (Eternal Darkness Symbolism)]], [[Silence (Darkness)]], and [[Eternal Darkness (Symbolism)]].

## Symbolic Derivation

The native [[Darkness (Darkness Pathway Symbolism)]] is the documented same-concept parent; the Great Old One-level Darkness Symbolism is not assigned as native Pathway ownership.
