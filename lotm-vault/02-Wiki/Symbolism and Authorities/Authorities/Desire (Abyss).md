# Desire (Abyss)

The secondary evidence set lists Desire as a partial Authority of the [[Abyss Pathway (disambiguation)|Abyss Pathway]].

Related group: [[Father of Devils Group]].

## Meaning

Abyss Desire induces, catalyzes, and ignites motives and emotional wants already present in a target. It is not described as complete embodiment of Desire, and it differs from Chained Binding, which restrains or releases desire without owning an independent Desire Authority.

## Expression in the Novels

- Sequence 5 Desire Apostles exploit visible emotion or increased desire, plant Defiling Seeds, create loss of resistance, and detonate desire beyond what body or mind can bear.
- At Sequence 4, sound, gaze, motion, or gesture can directly inflate sorrow, anger, greed, vanity, arrogance, and related drives until cognition fails, sanity breaks, or loss of control begins; the ability index points to *Lord of Mysteries* Chapter 1077.
- *Circle of Inevitability* Chapter 1136 is indexed for a Seed of Malice igniting Katarina's desires and blood during a Bloody Archduke-derived resurrection effect.
- At Father of Devils level, *Circle of Inevitability* Chapters 1167-1169 show sensed desires being explosively triggered: Inextinguishable Ravings becomes fixed on Tenebrous World, while the Buddha's salvation drive is intensified. These are combined Great Old One feats, not proof of Abyss alone reproducing the same scale.

## Limits

- Authority is partial and mind-dependent.
- It needs a desire or emotional vector to exploit; the evidence does not support inventing arbitrary motives without a basis.
- Higher-dimensional protection reduces the Father of Devils explosion to brief stagnation in the cited counter-example.

## Symbolic Derivation

Partial Desire is one of the Mind-oriented Authorities reflecting [[Abyss of the Heart and Mind]].

## Community Cross-check

Community discussion broadly preserves the induction/detonation distinction. Claims of automatic social collapse or limitless mind control remain speculative.
