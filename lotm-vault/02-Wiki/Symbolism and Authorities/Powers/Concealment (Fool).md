# Concealment (Fool)

This node records the Fool Pathway's concrete concealment effects associated with Realm of Mysteries and the secret-bearing aspect of [[Inconceivable and Bizarreness (Lord of Mysteries)]]. It is a partial, derived Power-domain rather than an unrestricted independent Authority.

It remains distinct from the full Pathway Authority [[Concealment (Darkness)]] and from the spatial mechanism [[Concealment (Door)]].
