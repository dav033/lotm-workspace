# Concealment (Door)

This Power-domain covers Door's spatial concealment: dividing, folding, or isolating space to create hidden areas, pockets, or positions. It is a spatial mechanism derived from [[Door (Door Pathway)]] and [[Space (Door)]], not evidence of the unrestricted Authority [[Concealment (Darkness)]].

It is also distinct from the Realm of Mysteries effects recorded at [[Concealment (Fool)]].
