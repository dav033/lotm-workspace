# Spirit World Access (Door)

This Power is Door's operational use of the Spirit World for passage, navigation, Blink, and Teleportation. It is a travel mechanism rather than an Authority over the Spirit World.

At higher Sequences, Door travel expands beyond ordinary Spirit World routes into Astral, cosmic, and higher-dimensional movement. None of these uses establishes native ownership of [[Dominator of the Spirit World]] or [[Spirit World Dominion]].
