# Sequence 5 - Gatekeeper

## Explicit Powers

### Traits

- **Physical Enhancement**: They become stronger and quicker, one step can allow them to suddenly close the distance against opponents and thrust at lightning speed, bringing vigorous wind currents.
- **Spiritual Perception**: They can acutely sense if an unknown creature is crossing the [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World") and approaching a place near them.

### New Abilities

- **Door to the Underworld**: They can sense the entrance to the [Underworld](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Underworld "Underworld"), allowing them to control the **Undead** inside, as though they watched over the gates that separated the dead from the living.
    - They can use the powers of the **Door to the Underworld** to some extent. They can imprint the door's mark onto the palm of their hand and summon it at critical moments.
        - It can be used to drag targets behind the gate which led to the [Underworld](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Underworld "Underworld"), where all living creatures immediately die.
            - The **Door** has a terrifying suction force. Most of it would be directed towards a target, though the remnant forces would continue to pull on the surroundings.
                - The main target can't help but stumble and be drawn in, while those nearby would find it difficult to even take a step.
                                - The suction force generated from the **Door** can also be used to clear the environment of airborne poison.
                                - **Gatekeepers** are able to use the **Door** to absorb the **Fog of War** of the [Red Priest Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Priest_Pathway "Red Priest Pathway").
                        - Aside from the suction force, bloody, skinless arms, slimy tentacles with teeth, and bluish-black vines with baby faces emerge from the **Door to the Underworld** to grab targets and drag them within.
                - It can be used let the [Underworld](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Underworld "Underworld") descend upon reality.
- **Internal Underworld**: Using their bodies as a cage, they can house numerous [Souls](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul"), deceased, and natural **Spirits**, allowing them to bring an **Undead** army wherever they go in a non eye-catching manner.
    - Normally, the entrance to the **Internal Underworld** is at the center of the **Gatekeeper's** brow.
        - With this, they gain various unique abilities and possess powerful helpers, for example:
        - **Death Envoy**: A unique ability of [Ludwell's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ludwell "Ludwell"). It turns his arms illusory, letting it instantly cover a significant distance and either remotely extract a target's **Spirit Body**  or squash them.
            - As the pale palm approaches, the target would seem to be possessed by a **Wraith** or [Evil Spirit](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Evil_Spirits "Evil Spirits"). The soft sobbing sounds of the **Death Envoy** causes the target's body to go numb, as though their blood had frozen.
                - This makes it difficult to produce an effective response, the target can only watch in despair as death approaches, feeling their vitality deplete at an increasing rate.
                        - It also allows [Ludwell](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ludwell "Ludwell") to produce a silent screech, striking and temporarily freezing attacks midair.
                - **Pus of Man**: A strange deceased spirit that [Ulika](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ulika "Ulika") fused with, it will quickly dissipate if it doesn't use a human body as a "house".
            - Even after undergoing **Purification** from the [Golden Sundial](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Golden_Sundial "Golden Sundial"), [Ulika](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ulika "Ulika") survived by transforming into a miniature form with raven-black skin and flowing, sticky pus.
        - Powerful **Spirits** and **Undead** creatures may erode their body from within.
        - The **Death Envoy** within [Ludwell](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ludwell "Ludwell") turned him into a half-human, half-dead existence, allowing him to enter the [Underworld](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Underworld "Underworld") without perishing.
        - As this can be considered a miniature [Underworld](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Underworld "Underworld"), **Gatekeepers** are a prime target of possession for [Evil Spirits](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Evil_Spirits "Evil Spirits") as they provide an adequate environment for their existence.

### Strengthened Abilities

- **Spirit Channeling**: The maximum number of **Undead** and **Spirits** that a **Gatekeeper** can control has slightly increased, but it doesn't change much.
    - Some examples of the **Spirits** they have been shown to use are:
        - **Pale Girl**: A nearly illusory girl with pale skin, bluish-green eyes, and jet black lips.
            - She has ghastly pale and translucent limbs that could bring a chill to the **Spirit Body**, which can help the **Gatekeeper** resist the influence of a **[Nightmare](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Darkness_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_7:_Nightmare "Darkness Pathway/Abilities")**.
                - Aside from controlling the **Undead** manning the [Black Tulip](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Black_Tulip "Black Tulip"), [Ludwell](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ludwell "Ludwell") summoned translucent, illusory ferocious creatures from the sea, charging forward like an unending tidal wave.
