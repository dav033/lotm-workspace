# Sequence 8 - Gravedigger

## Explicit Powers

### Traits

- **Physical Enhancement**: They become physically stronger and more agile.
- **Spirituality**: They gain an increase in their **Spirituality**.
    - **Spirit Vision**: Their ability to use [Spirit Vision](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_Vision "Spirit Vision") is enhanced.

### New Abilities

- **Spirit Communication**: They are able to communicate with nearby **Spirits**, allowing them to provide the **Beyonder** with help.
    - **Gravediggers** can command them to do simple actions such as tightly grasping onto a target to immobilize them.
- **Eye of Death**: By pressing down on their eyes, it will turn gray then colorless, allowing them to quickly determine the weaknesses of even unfamiliar **Undead** and **Spirit World** creatures through observation.
    - It's as though they enter the world of spirits and a kingdom of **Dead** souls, looking down at the enemy from a higher vantage point as they search for a “node.”
        - [Rampagers](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonders#Rampager_\(Losing_Control\) "Beyonders") — **Beyonders** who have lost control—count as **Dead** souls.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- **Eye of Death** (discerning Undead weaknesses) anticipates [[Undead (Death)]]. **Spirit Communication** remains an unattributed supporting ability at this Sequence.
