# Sequence 0 - Death (Eternal Sleeper)

## Explicit Powers

### Authorities

- **Death**: The authority of **Death** ascends to a conceptual level.
    - **Life** will **Die**, memories will **Die**, the connections between things will **Die**, and even the very imprints of existence will **Die**.
        - Although [Beyonder Characteristics](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristics "Beyonder Characteristics") are **Indestructible**, they too will briefly **Die** when facing this authority.
            - "They" can directly sever the connection between the **[Fool's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fool_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Fool "Fool Pathway/Abilities")** [Beyonder Characteristics](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristics "Beyonder Characteristics") and the [Historical Void](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Historical_Void "Historical Void") or directly kill the [Beyonder Characteristics](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristics "Beyonder Characteristics") themselves, thereby completely killing the **[Fool](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fool_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Fool "Fool Pathway/Abilities")** and preventing "Their" **Resurrection**.
                - "They" can make the **Death** of **Marionettes**, **Paper Figurine Substitutes**, and other similar clones and **Substitutes** equal to the **Death** of the main body.
            - "They" can't affect the **[Lord of Mysteries](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lord_of_Mysteries "Lord of Mysteries")**, but the **[Eternal Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Eternal_Darkness "Eternal Darkness")** can do so.
- **Pallor**: The ultimate form of **Withering**, the fading of all things.
    - Under **Pallor**, the mind, will, and **Beyonder** powers alike gradually fall silent and succumb to deathly stillness.
        - The **Purification** of the **[Sun](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sun_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Sun "Sun Pathway/Abilities")** restrains **Pallor**.
- **Endpoint**: "They" are the final **Destination** of all things, the common **Endpoint** of all **Life**.
    - This authority overlaps with the authority of the **[Mother Goddess of Depravity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Goddess_of_Depravity "Mother Goddess of Depravity")** and **[Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Darkness_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Darkness "Darkness Pathway/Abilities")**.
        - By relying on the [Door Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Door_Pathway "Door Pathway") 's powers, the [Underworld](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Underworld "Underworld") was derived from this authority.
        - Within the [Underworld](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Underworld "Underworld") is the **River Styx**, which is a projection of the [River of Eternal Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/River_of_Eternal_Darkness "River of Eternal Darkness") ' tributary.
        - "They" could establish "Their" own **Cycle of Reincarnation** with aid from the **[Mother](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Mother "Mother Pathway/Abilities")**.
        - This will be similar to the **Cycle of Reincarnation** formed from the **[Eternal Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Eternal_Darkness "Eternal Darkness")** and **[Mother Goddess of Depravity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Goddess_of_Depravity "Mother Goddess of Depravity")**, albeit limited to a single star system.
- **Undead**: "They" are the very source of **Undead** existence, the master of all **Undead**.
    - "Their" influence over **High-Sequence Beyonders** of the and other high ranking **Undead** can bypass barriers and **Seals**.
        - [Artificial Death](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Artificial_Death "Artificial Death") 's low level of [activation](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Activation "Activation") allowed it to influence **High-Sequence Beyonders** of the that failed their [advancement](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Advancement "Advancement"), including Elder [Hawick](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hawick "Hawick") of the [City of Silver](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/City_of_Silver "City of Silver").
                - Using [Artificial Death](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Artificial_Death "Artificial Death"), the [Evernight Goddess](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Evernight_Goddess "Evernight Goddess") influenced [Bladel](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Bladel "Bladel") to interfere with [Amon](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Amon "Amon")  and controlled a special **Wraith** to assist in the hunt for [Kotar](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Kotar "Kotar"). Both of these events took place in the [Forsaken Land of the Gods](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Forsaken_Land_of_the_Gods "Forsaken Land of the Gods").
        - This authority overlaps with the authority of the **[Mother Goddess of Depravity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Goddess_of_Depravity "Mother Goddess of Depravity")** and **[Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Darkness_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Darkness "Darkness Pathway/Abilities")**.
        - The **Eternal Sleeper** leans toward the **Undead** while the **[Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Darkness_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Darkness "Darkness Pathway/Abilities")** leans toward **Natural Spirits**.
- **Enslavement**: "They" are the **Enslaver** of all things who have **Souls**.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- Sequence 0 is the capstone: the source directly confirms all five named Authorities of the Pathway.
- **Death** (conceptual-level Death) → [[Death (Authority)]].
- **Pallor** (ultimate Withering) → [[Pallor (Death)]].
- **Endpoint** → [[Endpoint (Death)]], directly "the common Endpoint of all Life," from which "the Underworld was derived" — confirming the Sequence 4/5 inference.
- **Undead** → [[Undead (Death)]], directly stated to lean "toward the **Undead**" versus Darkness's **Natural Spirits** — the mirrored wording of [[0 - Darkness|Darkness's]] own Sequence 0 note.
- **Enslavement** → [[Enslavement (Death Authority)]], "the Enslaver of all things who have Souls" — confirming the Sequence 1/2/6 inference.
