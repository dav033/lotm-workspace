# Sequence 1 - Pale Emperor

## Explicit Powers

### Authorities

- **Pallor** (**Pale World**): "They" can create a world that is devoid of all color, leaving only **Pallor**.
    - In this **Pale World**, colors **Die** and **Beyonder** powers **Wither**. Any **Beyonder** power that manifests color will suffer immense weakening and will eventually fail completely.
        - This heavily restricts the **Painted Worlds** of the [Sublunary Eye Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sublunary_Eye_Pathway "Sublunary Eye Pathway").
                - Black  and white  both count as a colors, they, too, will turn **Pale**.
            - A clean and bright spear of flame, an **Unshadowed Spear**, will also be eroded, but it itself would disperse the **Pallor**.
        - Inanimate objects and abstract concepts can also be weathered. Even the concept of **Death** itself can weather away and dissipate.
        - The effects of being weathered under the **Pallor** happens extremely quickly; *thousands* of *years* would appear to be washed away in a short span of *2 seconds*.
                - Weathered objects would display some characteristics of a **Feathered Serpent**, namely, they would grow white feathers soaked with light-yellow oil.
            - What was labeled as “ **Death** ” wasn’t the **Endpoint**. When those affected become weathered or degraded to a certain extent, they would reform, as ethereal auras quickly grow.
                - The power of the **Earth** and the **Rules** of the world would also be eroded.
            - In the Pale-white Nation of the [Underworld](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Underworld "Underworld"), falling *40-50 meters* is no different than jumping from a *5-6 meter* building.
- **Enslavement**: "They" possess the authority of **Enslavement**.
    - **Soul Control**: "They" have a chance to directly extract the target's **Spirit Body** from their body. Even if unsuccessful, it will cause the target severe mental chaos and diminished vitality.
        - **Sovereign of Spirits**: Without deliberate effort, "They" appear extreme noble, devoid of the aura of the living—like an illusory deity that lorded over the countless **Undead**.
        - "They" can suppress and even **Enslave** any creature with a [Soul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul"), no longer limited to **Dead** creatures and **Beyonders** of the.
                - Through this aura, the living's thoughts will gradually **Die** along with their body until they become a corpse and "Their" **Slave**.
                - **Sovereign of Spirits** is not a **Beyonder** power, but rather a trait derived from the authority of **Enslavement**.
- **Death**: They wield partial authority over **Death**.
    - **Death Decree**: Any target seen or pointed at by "Them" will **Die** instantly.
        - This works on all beings below the level of a [True God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/True_God "True God"), and is capable of harming [True Gods](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/True_Gods "True Gods").
                - This is a mechanistic ability, so the way to avoid it is not to be seen or pointed at by "Them", or use other methods that can counter it mechanically.
        - "They" can create a symbol which appears like a long-feathered bird or a **Coiled Feathered Serpent**, in order to **Seal** targets.
        - The symbol absorbs the surrounding light and will quickly turn corporeal, before flying towards the target, causing the surroundings to become increasingly dim, suffering another round of **Weathering** as if the **Deity** in charge of **Death** had passed the **Final Judgment**, until it fuses with them. The symbol constantly influences the target until "They" stop responding.

### Strengthened Abilities

- "Their" **Beyonder** powers from previous **Sequences** have been improved.

### Advancement Ritual

Ensure your name becomes synonymous with death and the afterlife, remembered and recited by most sentient beings in your domain.

- Note 1: Due to the Barrier, the Northern and Southern Continents are isolated from the outside world, and are therefore treated as a separate domain in mysticism—otherwise, completing this ritual would be far harder.
    - Without the Barrier, the range required for this ritual is a Star System.
- Note 2: The same applies to the Western Continent.
- Note 3: The same applies to the Forsaken Land of the Gods.
- Note 4: Figures like the Great Emperor of Fengdu and the Buddhist Ksitigarbha possess comparable authority and status there.
- Note 5: Since the God of Death is still widely regarded as the embodiment of death in the Northern and Southern Continents, "His" descendants can borrow "His" name to shape themselves and complete the ritual. The drawback? They grow increasingly similar to the God of Death—until they become "Him".
