# Sequence 6 - Spirit Guide

## Explicit Powers

### Description

It is clear that **Spirit Guides** are very powerful against **Zombies** and **Wraiths**, and can even effectively target [Evil Spirits](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Evil_Spirits "Evil Spirits"). They can directly control groups of ownerless **Undead** that are of similar status to themselves; however, when facing **Higher Sequence Beyonders** of the, their slaves are very prone to rebelling, and so are they themselves.

### New Abilities

- **Language of the Dead**: By speaking a mystical language, they can urge a target's **Spirit** to leave a body, bypassing the physical protection provided by flesh and blood to target the **Spirit Body**.
    - This is an advancement of a **Spirit Medium's** powers. It could go from direct communication with the **Spirit Body** to that of commandeering and **Enslavement**.
        - As **Marionettes** are considered dead, a **Spirit Guide** can interfere and influence a **Marionette**; however, at **Sequence 4**, the core of a **Marionette** can have a **Worm of Spirit** inside. Thus, at those stages it also depends on multiple factors like relative strength of both sides, condition, how much effort is exerted, etc.
- **Resurrection**: They can turn corpses into living skeletons and **Zombies**; however, this kind of **Resurrection** does not allow the reanimated corpse to have any will or vitality.

### Strengthened Abilities

- **Spirit Channeling**: They can influence and are involved in the [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World"), able to recruit messengers on their own, as well as obtain help from certain [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World") creatures.
    - Compared to a **Spirit Medium** who needs to perform **Spirit Channeling** on site to achieve different effects, they can control and drive deceased and natural **Spirits** to fight in advance.
        - However, making these deceased and natural **Spirits** always follow their commands without being discovered and affected by other factors is a significant problem for a **Spirit Guide**.
                - In comparison with a **Spirit Medium's** capabilities, **Spirit Guides** are now capable of controlling groups of ownerless **Undead** that are of similar status to themselves.
            - The upper limit of **Undead** and **Spirits** they can control numbers in the thousands.
        - The strength of a **Spirit Guide** depends on the deceased or a natural **Spirits** they have found and controlled. This is the same with **[Spirit Warlocks](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Darkness_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_5:_Spirit_Warlock "Darkness Pathway/Abilities")**.
        - However, the inclination for **Spirit Guides** are deceased **Spirits**, while **[Spirit Warlocks](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Darkness_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_5:_Spirit_Warlock "Darkness Pathway/Abilities")** are better at controlling natural **Spirits**.
        - They can swap their own **Spirit** with the **Undead** under their command to be effectively immune to attacks that target the **Soul Body**.
        - To combat the **Spell of Harrumph**, [Burman](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Burman "Burman") used this technique to set a trap for [Lumian Lee](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee").
        - Some examples of the creatures that have been shown to be under their command are:
        - **Shadow-Swallowing Python**: A decaying, skeletal python oozing yellowish-green pus. An **Undead** creature specialized in consuming shadows and shadowy creatures.
            - Its fangless mouth resembles a vortex, emitting a hurried, piercing sound. It can emit a suction force that tugs at the surrounding shadows, drawing them in.
                        - Under their command, they can make the python target only their enemy instead of indiscriminately swallowing their allies or the **Spirit Guide** themself hidden in shadows.
                - **Death Knight**: A towering figure—a knight adorned in tattered black armor and a broadsword.
            - Pale flames flicker in its eye sockets and putrid liquid seeps from its armor’s crevices, with only sticky flesh clinging to its exposed skin.
                - **Living Shadow**: A tall, thin shadow that can emerge from the shadows in translucent form.
            - It can lunge at a target, resembling the possession of **Wraiths** and evil spirits, but lacks the speed to complete the process in a mere blink.
                - **Goddess of the Lake**: A powerful type of spirit, one that brings horror.
            - It appears as a foggy lake shimmering with light, producing a tranquil beauty. In the middle, concentric circles ripple out as beautiful and illusory figures float up.
                - Various **Undead** creatures can appear from the void and help them complete different tasks.
            - They can drag the **Spirit Guide** into the shadows, disappearing without a trace, even if their master is knocked unconscious.
                        - They can make the **Spirit Guide** swiftly soar, as if dragged by an unseen force.
                        - They can grab onto a target to restrain them.
                        - They can control a weak spirit from great distances for communication, reaching from [Backlund](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Backlund "Backlund") to [Tingen City](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tingen_City "Tingen City").
- **Knowledge** (**Spirit World**): They have considerable information about the [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World").
