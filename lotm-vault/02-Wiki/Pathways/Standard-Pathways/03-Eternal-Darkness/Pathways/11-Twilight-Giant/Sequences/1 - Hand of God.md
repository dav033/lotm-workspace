# Sequence 1 - Hand of God

## Explicit Powers

### Authorities

- **Proxy**: Like the **[Left Hand of Decay](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Second_Law_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_3:_Left_Hand_of_Decay)** in the same domain, "They" can act on behalf of the [Deity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Gods "Gods"), [Uniqueness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniqueness "Uniqueness"), or [Great Old One](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Old_One "Great Old One") "They" serve to manifest divine power and punishment.
    - Due to this authority, "They" cannot acquire new authorities aside from the **Proxy** authority and can only get enhancements to "Their" previous **Sequence** abilities.
        - If "They" don't harm "Themselves", "They" can for the short-term reach [Sasrir's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sasrir "Sasrir") level. If "They" harm "Themselves", "They" can be a consumable [Half Great Old One](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Half_Great_Old_One "Half Great Old One").
        - In a certain-death situation, "They" can theoretically kill a **[Red Priest](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Priest_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Red_Priest)** who had not **Sacrificed** to the [Sefirah](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirah "Sefirah"), but it's also difficult because the duration is very short.
            - The **[Red Priest](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Priest_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Red_Priest)** would most likely only be at a disadvantage for a short time, unless an opportunity is created in advance where "They" cannot dodge or block.
        - A **Hand of God** can only manifest such power briefly and with restraint. Otherwise, "They" will gradually lose "Their" sanity and become a true servant.
        - In addition, if the [True Deity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Gods "Gods") that the **Hand of God** had pledged "Their" allegiance too dies, the power that "They" can wield will decrease.
        - "Their" right hand will embody certain properties of the entity "They" serve.
        - Having ascended to this [Sequence](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sequence "Sequence"), the **Hand of God** [Dabomachie](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Dabomachie "Dabomachie") 's right hand seemed to be made entirely of glowing orange **Twilight**.
        - "They" can condense "Their" own power into a mark to lend to others.
- **Combat**: Some of "Their" corresponding abilities to the **Combat** authority have been enhanced.
    - **Protection**: "They" can summon **Cages** made out of dense light imbued with orange hues and another layer of unyielding radiance outside the targeted area that can prevent even an **[Observer](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sublunary_Eye_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_1:_Observer)** or **[Dimensional Shadow](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sublunary_Eye_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_2:_Dimensional_Shadow)** from crossing through.
        - "They" can then drive the **Twilight Sword** into the barrier of the **Cage** to repair it after withstanding attacks from multiple **Angels**.
                - Its range is enough to envelop and **Protect** all the [Protected Zones](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Protected_Zones "Protected Zones") created by [The Fool](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") and [Grisha Adam](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Adam "Adam").

## Authority → Power Mapping

## Potion

### Potion Properties

> A bluish-black liquid tinged with the colors of Dusk/Twilight, containing numerous transparent faces.

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> A translucent bluish-black giant hand with various divine patterns.

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- One of the Hands of Aurmir (content on the First Blasphemy Slate.)

Or
- One of the Hands of Badheil (content on the Second Blasphemy Slate).

Or
- A Hand of God Beyonder Characteristic.

Supplementary Ingredients

- 100 milliliters of a True God's Blood (the type that doesn't have Beyonder Characteristics).
- 9 drops of Water from the River Styx (or something similar).
- A religious text recording one's own legend and deeds of loyalty.
- A Flower blooming in a True God's Divine Kingdom.

### Advancement Ritual

Swear allegiance to a certain god or become a Servant of the River Styx .

Any tributary of the River of Eternal Darkness suffices to become a Servant.
