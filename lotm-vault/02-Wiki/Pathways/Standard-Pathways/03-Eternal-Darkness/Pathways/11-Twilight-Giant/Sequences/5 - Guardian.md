# Sequence 5 - Guardian

## Explicit Powers

### Traits

- **Strength of Giants**: Compared to a **Dawn Paladin**, a **Guardian** defense and strength is like an adult comparing their physique and strength to that of a child.
    - A **Guardian** would probably be able to withstand the bullets of a steam rifle, but in the wars of the steam era, they wouldn't die unless they were focused on by large-caliber cannons.

### New Abilities

- **Protection**: **Guardians** are rarely injured. By kneeling on one knee and planting the Sword in front of them, **Dawn** -like light will emerge, forming two indestructible invisible walls on the left and right. This allows them to take on damage for companions within a certain range, **Protecting** them.
    - Once fully committed to defense and abandoning offense, few can break through a **Guardian's** defense below that of the **High-Sequences**. This is regardless of the types of damage the enemy can inflict and their corresponding effects.
        - Both **Protection** and **Dawn Armor** can be used simultaneously.
                - When they attack, their defense will be reduced significantly; however, it will still be far superior to that of refined full-body armor.
                - **[Reapers](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Priest_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_5:_Reaper)** are one of the few non **High-Sequence Beyonders** that can penetrate through a **Guardian's** defenses.
- **Illusion Immunity**: They can't be deceived by **Illusions**.

### Strengthened Abilities

- They have gained improvements to other **Beyonder** powers from previous **Sequences**.
    - Their **Sword of Dawn**, **Hurricane of Light**, and other abilities can damage any creature.
        - **Supernatural Resistance**: This **Beyonder** power has been enhanced.
        - Within the same **Sequence** level, a **Guardian** can slow down the time of having their **Spirit Body Threads** controlled and thus being turned into a **Marionette**.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- **Protection**, introduced here for the first time, is directly reported at Sequence 0 as a named subordinate of [[Combat (Twilight Giant)]] ("Combat...contains subordinate authorities like Strength, Gigantism, Devastation, and Protection").
- Strengthened Sword of Dawn/Hurricane of Light/Supernatural Resistance continue toward [[Combat (Twilight Giant)]] and [[Twilight (Authority)]]. **Illusion Immunity** and **Strength of Giants** remain unattributed supporting traits.
