# Sequence 0 - Twilight Giant (Twilight, Giant)

## Explicit Powers

### Description

"They" hold partial authority over **Holiness**, **Twilight**, and **Combat** (which contains subordinate authorities like **Strength**, **Gigantism**, **Devastation**, and **Protection**) as well as a portion of the **Proxy** authority—where the symbol of **Enslavement** belonging to the **[River of Eternal Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/River_of_Eternal_Darkness)** manifests in the **Giant Pathway**.

### Authorities

- **Twilight**: This authority is derived from symbols of **Light**, **Space-Time**, and **Decay**.
    - "They" can cause **Matter**, will, and even the void to **Decay**, collapse, and vanish, radiating the aura of approaching **Twilight** and the **Decay** of all things.
        - **Twilight** and the **Passage of Time** counters the **Light**, **Holiness**, and **Sun** authorities of the [Sun Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sun_Pathway) as **Twilight** represents the end of all these.
                - A **Twilight Giant's** authority over the **Passage of Time** is able to even influence the will within **Beyonder Characteristics** to a limited extent.
                - "Their" **Twilight Sword** would cause even the void to shatter and **Decay** as the objects hit would wane uncontrollably and break down.
        - The **Twilight Giant's** mere existence causes matter and the void around "Them" to bend, making all things begin to uncontrollably **Decay**.
        - The [God of Combat](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/God_of_Combat "God of Combat") 's aura leaking from across the [Astral World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Astral_World "Astral World") into reality made the **Fog of War** that covered the battlefield around [Backlund](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Backlund "Backlund") vanish, and the [Saints](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sequence#Saints "Sequence") present found themselves abnormally weak, having difficulty in raising their arms.
        - "They" can solidify **Twilight** and slow the **Passage of Time**, and while solidification is only possible within **Twilight**, "They" can create a **Twilight** domain within a certain range.
        - By slashing towards "Their" target using the **Twilight Sword**, it will instantly envelop them in **Twilight's** glow, freezing them within as their thoughts and actions slow down, as if they existed in a different world.
            - A [Kings of Angels](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Kings_of_Angels "Kings of Angels") [Cheek](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Primordial_Demoness "Primordial Demoness") was affected by this from [Lumian's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee") **Angelic** -level **Twilight Giant Pathway** soldier, causing "Her" thoughts and actions to slow down and delaying "Her" attempt to destroy "Herself"
                - **Land of Twilight**: "Their" **Divine Kingdom** formed through **Twilight**, completely frozen within the **Passage of Time**. Those inside will find themselves weakened unless acknowledged.
            - It can persist for millennia even after "Their" death (e.g. [Giant King's Court](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Giant_King%27s_Court "Giant King's Court")).
        - "They" are able to cause targets to rapidly **Age**, **Decay**, and **Rot** —preventing the **Resurrection** methods of all beings with finite lifespans.
        - As [True Gods](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/True_Gods "True Gods") do not have the concept of lifespan, "They" won't die from this. However, "They" can still be affected by **Aging** and get weakened.
        - "They" can induce instability and **Loss of Control** in targets, with those targets' symptoms continuing to appear more and more severe.
        - "They" can slow the formation and opening of **Doors**.
        - "They" can weaken targets progressively.
        - The **Twilight** of the **Twilight Giant** and the **Decay** of the [Second Law Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Second_Law_Pathway) are both **Time** -related and have similar manifestations, but **Twilight** does not affect an organization's state.
        - **Dawn**:"They" also possess the authority of **Dawn**. This is because endings are inherently connected to beginnings.
        - **Dawn** can be used to directly **Purify** a **[Beyonder Characteristic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristic "Beyonder Characteristic")**, much like the [Sun Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sun_Pathway), though **Purification** from the is achieved through equal destruction and **Decay**.
- **Holiness**: "They" hold partial authority over **Holiness**.
    - **Death** is among the most solemn events of life and of the world—thus profoundly **Sacred**. Hence, the **Twilight Giant** also excels in **Purification** and discerning **Evil** and **Corruption**.
- **Combat**: "They" hold the authority of **Combat**.
    - The most terrifying **Strength**, the most **Gigantic** form, the most potent **Devastation**, the most unyielding **Protection** —together with **Skill** and experience, these compose individual **Combat**.
        - The one who commands all this is the [God of Combat](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/God_of_Combat "God of Combat"), who is able to strip "His" opponents of **Skill**, forcing them to fight by instinct alone.
        - **Devastation** is a subsidiary authority of **Combat** because it is **Devastation** caused by the action of **Combat**, making it a concept that is attached to **Combat's** actions.
        - In contrast, **Destruction** is not only the action but also a state of being for things. It is a state of being that can be achieved through **Combat** (which includes **Devastation**). It can be achieved by prematurely bringing forth the end of an event/being that is currently running its natural course. And last but not least, it is also just simply a final state of being, a state of finality.
                - In a vacuum, **Devastation** can counteract and neutralize **Humanity**, which is a subordinate authority; however, **Devastation** cannot erase **Humanity** from the root. It functions more like a continuous suppression through attrition. Hence it is not capable of erasing other authorities from existence, only being able to subject them to being damaged and depleted by attrition.
            - By contrast, the authority of **Destruction** is far more formidable, as it directly imposes the state of **Destruction** on a conceptual level.
        - The authority of **Combat** is a **Sequence 0** authority, making it the same level as **Sequence 0** authority **Destruction**, but it's lower than the symbolism of **Destruction**.
        - At the core of the lies **Decay** and **Decay** is a process. Thus, **Combat** itself is also a form of attrition, centering on using individual strength to "go against" another, which in turn generates "attrition".
- **Proxy**: "They" hold a portion of the **Proxy** authority.
    - Among the three [Pathways](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways") of the [River of Eternal Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/River_of_Eternal_Darkness), **[Death](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Death_\(Eternal_Sleeper\))** aligns most closely with the [Sefirah](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirot "Sefirot") 's essence, **[Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Darkness_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Darkness)** most resembles it outwardly, yet the **Twilight Giant** can partially employ it. This is one reason the [Giant King](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Aurmir "Aurmir") could contend with the [Dragon of Imagination](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ankewelt "Ankewelt").
        - [Sefirot](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirot "Sefirot") is power, but it is also danger. There is no way for "Them" to utilize the power of [Sefirot](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirot "Sefirot") without paying a price.
        - By using **Proxy**, the [God of Combat](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/God_of_Combat "God of Combat") can theoretically reach the [strongest tier of Half Great Old Ones](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Half_Great_Old_Ones#Tier_1 "Half Great Old Ones") for 2-3 seconds. This was how the [Giant King](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Aurmir "Aurmir") was able to escape from the [Ancient Sun God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ancient_Sun_God "Ancient Sun God") and passed on "His" characteristics to "His" successor.
        - However, in reality, it's impossible because "He" couldn't possibly completely pledge allegiance to the [River of Eternal Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/River_of_Eternal_Darkness "River of Eternal Darkness"), as this would mean never becoming a [Great Old One](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Old_One "Great Old One").
            - Thus, in practice, "His" self-detonation would only be a bit stronger than the [Evernight Goddess](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Evernight_Goddess "Evernight Goddess") before accommodating the [River of Eternal Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/River_of_Eternal_Darkness "River of Eternal Darkness").
                - There were also other factors preventing the true theoretical maximum from being achieved:
            - Firstly, the [Evernight Goddess](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Evernight_Goddess "Evernight Goddess") can also use items to call upon a bit of the [River of Eternal Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/River_of_Eternal_Darkness "River of Eternal Darkness") 's power to counteract, not be completely suppressed, and unable to **Resurrect**.
                        - Secondly, if the [God of Combat](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/God_of_Combat "God of Combat") truly self-detonates, it would threaten the [Astral Barrier](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Astral_Barrier "Astral Barrier") that protects and separates [Earth](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Map_of_the_World "Map of the World") from the [Outer Deities](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Outer_Deities "Outer Deities").
                - Someone like the [White Tower](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/White_Tower_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_White_Tower), who can influence [Astral World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Astral_World "Astral World") concepts, would use "Their" own methods to interfere with the **Proxy**.
                        - Thirdly, there are other interferences, like the [Underworld Daoist](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Underworld_Daoist "Underworld Daoist"), etc.
                - Even counting the strongest level, there's a possibility of counter-kill, but it's not certain.
            - If the [God of Combat](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/God_of_Combat "God of Combat") self-detonates at the highest level, "He" could obliterate everything on that battlefield, but that doesn't necessarily mean it could completely kill the [Earth Mother](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Earth_Mother "Earth Mother") and the [Evernight Goddess](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Evernight_Goddess "Evernight Goddess") because of "Their" respective authorities.
                - Furthermore, at least more than half of those 2-3 seconds would have to be used for fleeing, arranging final affairs, arranging for an heir, or activating a resurrection backup, just like the [Giant King](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Aurmir "Aurmir") back then.
