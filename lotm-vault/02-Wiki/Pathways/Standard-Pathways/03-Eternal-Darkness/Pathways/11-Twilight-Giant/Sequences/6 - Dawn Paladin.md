# Sequence 6 - Dawn Paladin

## Explicit Powers

**Traits**      ****

- **Strength of Giants**: **Dawn Paladins** possess **Giant** -like strength, powerful offensive capability, and relatively high defense.
    - By now, their bodies are muscular, well attuned to combat and their height is further increased.
        - **Dawn Paladins** can burst in speeds akin to a steam train accelerating at its highest speed.
        - With a low growl, [Father Utravsky](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Utravsky "Utravsky") charged through the flames created by [Klein Moretti](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") at the maximum speed of a steam train.
        - Even after experiencing a long drawn fight, a **Dawn Paladin's** stamina will seem to be limitless.
        - [Utravsky](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Utravsky "Utravsky") was able to attack multiple times in long sessions with his two-handed broadsword despite having been weakened as if experiencing a long drawn fight.
        - The force that a **Dawn Paladin** can strike with their weapons can create strong gusts.
        - The winds created by a weakened [Father Utravsky's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Utravsky "Utravsky") two-handed broadsword were so strong that it caused a **Sequence 7** [Klein Moretti](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") to almost lose his balance.
        - Aside from their natural height becoming taller, **Dawn Paladins** can further temporarily increase their height.
        - Descendants might be born with grayish-blue skin possessed by common **Giants**.

**New Abilities**      ****

- **Light of Dawn** **(Sunrise Gleam)**: A **Dawn Paladin** can fill an area of roughly 40 to 50 meters around them with pure, **Sacred Dawn Light**, which dispel **Illusions**, **Exorcise Wraiths** and **Shadows**, and even weaken [Evil Spirits](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Evil_Spirits "Evil Spirits").
    - It can dispel powers or energies leaning toward **Evil**, **Corruption**, and **Degeneration**, but in the **Domain** of the **Holy** and **Light**, the **Light of Dawn** is still inferior to a **[Light Suppliant's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sun_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_8:_Light_Suppliant)** powers.
        - The **Light of Dawn** can erase **Shadows** and nullify **Concealment** effects, such as **[Witches'](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Demoness_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_7:_Witch) Invisibility**. For **[Wraiths](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chained_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_5:_Wraith)** and **[Shadow Ascetics](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hanged_Man_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_7:_Shadow_Ascetic)**, their peculiarities are diminished.
        - A **Dawn Paladin's Light of Dawn** is also able to **Purify** mystical **Pathogens** in the environment, reduce radiation, and interfere with **Electromagnetic Fields**.
        - Except when dealing with **Wraiths** and **Shadows**, its **Mysticism** level is very low.
- **Dawn Armor****:** They can conjure a silver **Armor of Dawn** around their bodies which is equivalent to a refined full-body armor that doesn't have noticeable weight or inhibit their movement in any way.
    - It can recover from damage, but their stamina will automatically be drained during the process.
        - It will also take a certain amount of time to recover from that damage.
        - A **Dawn Paladin's Dawn Armor** includes gauntlets, breastplates, and a helmet.
- **Hurricane of Light**: Their most powerful attack. By inserting the **Sword of Dawn** into the ground and shattering it, they will form countless fragments of **Dawn** -like light that create a shimmering, violently sharp **Hurricane** filled with destructive power, cutting every **Shadow** and **Corrupted** entities.
    - **Evil** creatures like **[Devils](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Abyss_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_6:_Devil)** or **[Zombies](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chained_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_6:_Zombie)** would be subjected to the effects of **Purification** and the damage of a **Fragmentary Blade**. The combination of these aspects causes their **Hurricane of Light** to become excellent at purging **Evil**.
        - **Purification** weakens defense and inflicts harm on the **Evil** creature's spirit and flesh, while the **Fragmentary Blade** utilizes **Purification** to weaken defense and cut flesh. The more wounds and the deeper they were, the better the **Purification** effect.
            - **Hurricane of Light** would be even more effective on [Spirits](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Evil_Spirits "Evil Spirits") (such as a **[Wraiths](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chained_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_5:_Wraith)**) leaving the target close to death.
        - The **Hurricane of Light** can directly destroy a person's body, eliminate **[Wraiths](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chained_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_5:_Wraith)**, and even heavily wound [Evil Spirits](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Evil_Spirits "Evil Spirits"). It also possesses a unique strength that can more easily destroy creatures of the **Evil** and **Undead Domains**.
        - Their **Hurricane of Light** is stronger than a **Sword of Lava** (which is stronger than the **Light of Holiness**) when it comes to damaging non- **Evil** domain beings.
                - Although **Hurricane of Light** is an area-of-effect ability, a **Dawn Paladin** is able to control its direction to a certain extent.
        - After activation, the **Sword of Dawn** cannot be condensed for a short time. In addition, a **Dawn Paladin** would need at least more than *2 minutes* of recovery before they can form another **Hurricane of Light**.

### Strengthened Abilities

- **Dawn Paladins** experience a qualitative change.
    - Each of a **Dawn Paladin's Combat** related abilities, whether it be **Combat Mastery** or **Weapon Mastery**, will be enhanced massively.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- **Light of Dawn**, **Dawn Armor**, and **Hurricane of Light** are all Sword-of-Dawn-based powers; Sequence 0 places **Dawn** under the [[Twilight (Authority)]] heading ("endings are inherently connected to beginnings"), while Light of Dawn's dispelling of Evil, Corruption, and Degeneration also anticipates [[Holiness (Twilight Giant)]]. Hurricane of Light's destructive force additionally anticipates [[Combat (Twilight Giant)]]'s Devastation sub-authority.
- Strengthened **Combat Mastery**/**Weapon Mastery** → [[Combat (Twilight Giant)]]. **Strength of Giants** remains an unattributed supporting trait.
