# Sequence 2 - Glory

## Explicit Powers

### Description

The three authorities of the 's **Sequence 0** — **Holiness**, **Twilight**, and **Combat** —are all manifested at this stage. For [Angels](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Angels "Angels"), this is a kind of **Glory**.

### Traits

- **Strength of Giants**: "They" can grow to an enormous size.
    - A steel puppet of [Lumian Lee](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee"), possessing a temporary [Angel](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Angels "Angels") -level **Boon** from the [Evernight Goddess](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Evernight_Goddess "Evernight Goddess") grew to an enormous size, being comparable to [Lumian](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee") "Himself".

### Authorities

- **Twilight**: "They" hold the simplified authority of **Twilight**.
    - "They" can age "Themselves" rapidly, evaporate, and then be reborn in the [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World"), returning to normal before departing. Likewise, "They" can also delay one's aging process.
        - [Larrion](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Larrion "Larrion"), after accomplishing "His" arrangements, attempted to evade [Arianna](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Arianna "Arianna") and escape through the [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World") by rapidly aging "Himself" with the authority of **Twilight**.
        - The simplified **Twilight** authority can cause a single target or a group within an area to gradually age, have their lifespan reduced, and enter the countdown of life.
        - As dying of aging is something that the majority of **Resurrection** abilities cannot take effect against, one should never engage in a prolonged battle with a **Glory**.
            - A **[Beauty Goddess](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Moon_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_1:_Beauty_Goddess)** will be affected by this authority, but "They" will remain **Beautiful**.
                        - A **[Miracle Invoker's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fool_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_2:_Miracle_Invoker) Resurrection** is an example of one that can reset age.
                - "They" can also cause a single target or group within an area to gradually age and have their lifespan reduced with each strike.
- **Combat**: "They" hold the simplified **Combat** authority, thus being the top **Sequence 2** in frontal combat.
    - **Protection**: Its subordinate **Protection** can, at this stage, form a **Cage**, which in addition to defense, also gains a **Sealing** effect—a fusion with the decaying power of **Twilight**.
        - "Their" **Cages** are able to fully **Seal** off dangerous, corruptive [Sealed Artifacts](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sealed_Artifacts "Sealed Artifacts") (e.g., the [Gift of the Land](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Gift_of_the_Land "Gift of the Land")) and [Ritualistic Magic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ritualistic_Magic "Ritualistic Magic"), but requires "Their" presence to constantly fuel it, only being able to leave in short intervals during crises.
            - [Proof of Glory](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Proof_of_Glory "Proof of Glory") was used during an event to isolate and **Seal** the detrimental influences caused by deity-related [Ritualistic Magic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ritualistic_Magic "Ritualistic Magic") via the formation of a **Cage**.
                - "Their" **Cage** provides extremely powerful defensive and restraining capabilities, capable of **Protecting** companions and preventing "Their" enemies from escaping.
            - The [Tamara's Everchanging Card](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tamara%27s_Everchanging_Card "Tamara's Everchanging Card") 's effects couldn't break through the **Divine Barrier** created by the [Proof of Glory](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Proof_of_Glory "Proof of Glory"), nor could it allow the [Demoness of Orange](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Demoness_of_Orange "Demoness of Orange") to bypass it and **Blink** directly to her targets.
                - Its range is enough to envelop and **Protect** the [City of Silver](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/City_of_Silver "City of Silver").
        - **Twilight Sword**: An evolution of the **Silver Knight's Silver Rapier**. "They" can instantly condense a great sword composed of orange-red **Twilight** radiance that can pierce through the void.
        - It can use **Twilight's** relationship with **Space-Time** to manifest directly at the target's location—displaying **Devastation**, which is subordinate to the **Combat** authority.
                - "They" are able to wield this **Twilight Sword** directly as a hand-held weapon.
        - **Hurricane of Light**: The **Twilight Sword** will let "Them" transform the **Hurricane of Light** into an orange-red storm capable of annihilating a wasteland.
        - Countless shards of **Twilight** would tear apart everything around them, cutting all objects to pieces, causing them and the void to **Decay** until they collapse.
                - In the storm, affected targets will quickly break into tiny fragments, each piece dim and rusty, as if washed by the passage of endless time or having reached its **Twilight** years.
- **Holiness**: "They" hold the simplified authority of **Holiness**.
    - **Glory** is part of **Holiness**, and death is likewise one of the most sacred and solemn matters of life, therefore "Their" **Glory** is **Dawn**, yet also **Twilight**; it is **Devastation**, yet also **Purification**.
        - "Their" **Cages** bear a sense of **Holiness** and **Glory**.

### Mythical Creature Form

- "They" become a complete [Mythical Creature](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mythical_Creature_Form "Mythical Creature Form"). "Their" [Mythical Creature Form](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mythical_Creature_Form "Mythical Creature Form") is a dozen of meters tall **Cyclops** with elongated limbs, grayish-blue skin, and a single vertical eye on the forehead. Every inch of "Their" skin, every pore, and every piece of "Their" flesh seems to defy normal human senses, being combined in a unique way and containing an unimaginable sense of impact.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- The source directly names all three Sequence 0 Authorities manifesting here: **Twilight** (rapid aging/rebirth) → [[Twilight (Authority)]]; **Combat** (Protection/Cage, Twilight Sword/Devastation) → [[Combat (Twilight Giant)]]; **Holiness** ("Glory is part of Holiness") → [[Holiness (Twilight Giant)]].
- The complete Mythical Creature Form is a general trait, not an Authority.

## Potion

### Potion Properties

> A liquid formed from pure, holy, and profound light.

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> A pure square stone that radiates divine light.

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- The Crystallization of a Glorious Giant (e.g: The One Eye of Bladel).

Or
- The Crown of Aurmir.

Or
- A Glory Beyonder Characteristic.

Supplementary Ingredients

- 300 milliliters of blood from the Glorious Giant.
- One item corrupted by Dark Power.
- One Corpse buried for over a thousand years.
- One Poem praising itself (has to be sung for at least 10 years).

### Advancement Ritual

Fight an Angel or a Creature of equal power on your own (using beyonder items is allowed).
Or kill enough Enemies or Blasphemers on behalf of the God who blessed you.
