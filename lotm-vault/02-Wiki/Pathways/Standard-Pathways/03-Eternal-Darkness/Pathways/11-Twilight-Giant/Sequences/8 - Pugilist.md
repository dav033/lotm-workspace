# Sequence 8 - Pugilist (Gladiator)

## Explicit Powers

### Traits

- **Physical Enhancement**: Their physical abilities alone can weaken the effects of some **Beyonder** powers targeting them.

**New Abilities**    ****

- **Supernatural Resistance**: **Pugilists** are experts in **Combat** that are able to use their own bodies in order to mitigate the effects of certain supernatural forces.

### Strengthened Abilities

- **Combat Mastery** (**Close Combat**): **Pugilists** are more adept at close-quarters **Combat** than any other **Sequence**.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- **Supernatural Resistance** and strengthened **Combat Mastery** (Close Combat) continue to anticipate [[Combat (Twilight Giant)]].
