# Sequence 4 - Demon Hunter (Witcher)

## Explicit Powers

### Traits

- **Physical & Mental Enhancement**: **Demon Hunters** possess a terrifying **Physical** strength and a **Mind** that stands between that of a [God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Gods "Gods") and a human.
    - They are capable of running at such high speed that it whips up hurricane-force winds.
- **Spirituality**: A **Demon Hunter's Spirituality** will be enhanced significantly.
    - They will possess a strong **Spiritual Intuition**.

**New Abilities**    ****

- **Eye of Demon Hunting**: This is not a true visual ability but rather a general term for their capacity to identify a target's nature, weaknesses, condition, and whether they are **Corrupted** or **Possessed** by relying on their own senses, mental probing, and extensive experience.
    - They become sensitive to any traces of **Evil**, **Degeneration**, and **Corruption**. They can also rely on their sixth sense, **Spiritual Intuition**, and life experiences to judge the situation.
        - Veteran **Witchers** can use this to judge whether a target is lying.
                - Under their sixth sense, they can find the crux of the problem.
            - While not understanding how or the principles behind it, [Colin Iliad's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Colin_Iliad "Colin Iliad") rich experience in exploration and the intuition as a **Demon Hunter** made him believe that the statues were the reason for [Murskogan](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Murskogan "Murskogan") still being alive.
        - They can seemingly detect a **Silver Knight's** location when they use **Silver Rapier** within a short distance as well as be able to detect and block a **Silver Rapier**.
        - Using this will cause a complex, dark green symbol to appear in each of their eyes.
- **Knowledge** (**Hunting**, **Alchemy**): They are highly adept at dealing with all kinds of situations and possess knowledge of how different monster parts and herbs can be used. They are also skilled at discovering the weaknesses of various creatures and identifying the uses of various materials.
    - In a special **Cogitation** state, they can, based on the former and with the aid of the latter, concoct the corresponding *magical potions*, *holy ointments*, *essential oils* and *special marks.*
        - By ingesting, applying, or using these items, they can produce different extraordinary effects such as **Lightning Strikes**, **Freezing**, **Purification**, **Burning**, **Decay**, and **Exorcism**.
            - This allows them to counter targets in a highly targeted manner.
                - Orthodox **Demon Hunters** will typically wield dual swords so that different ointments can take effect simultaneously, enabling them to handle more complex situations.
- **Mind Concealment**: They can **Conceal** one's actions and intentions.
    - This effectively interferes with **Divination** and **Prophecy**, preventing even targets capable of foreseeing danger from detecting it—making them a nemesis of [Demons](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Abyss "Abyss").

### Strengthened Abilities

- All of a **Demon Hunter's Beyonder** powers from the **Dawn Paladin** and **Guardian** stages undergo a qualitative transformation.
    - **Protection**: They can create an invisible and indestructible wall of defense around them. Once it is anchored to the ground, the power of **Protection** becomes extremely difficult to shake.
        - The power of **Protection** can even block traversal through the [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World") as well as block or weaken **Curse** -type abilities.
        - **Light of Dawn**: Their **Light of Dawn** can be condensed into objects other than weapons.
        - **Witchers** can blanket their weapons with **Dawn's** glow.

### Mythical Creature Form

- As **Sequence 4 Demigods** they receive some level of **Godhood**, and with it comes an incomplete [Mythical Creature Form](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mythical_Creature_Form "Mythical Creature Form"). Disregarding all consequences, they can release their incomplete [Mythical Creature Form](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mythical_Creature_Form "Mythical Creature Form") to become an incomplete **Cyclops.** Due to their enhanced **Willpower**, the **Saints** of this Pathway have a higher probability of possessing a relatively routine [Mythical Creature Form](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mythical_Creature_Form "Mythical Creature Form").
    - Their incomplete [Mythical Creature Form](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mythical_Creature_Form "Mythical Creature Form") looks like a 4 meter tall **Cyclops**. Their skin turns bluish-black and muscular, and along with its pores and flesh, seemingly violating the normal confines of a human body. Their head swells up significantly, with the area from their forehead to nose cracking open to reveal a black vertical eye-like rift. On their body's surface contains symbols and the corresponding effects of *immense power*, *infinite mystery*, and a *strange psychic* *influence*.
        - If those under **Sequence 4** saw this form, they will suffer a strong backlash. Their brains might suffer an excruciating pain that would cause them to lose their minds, even their **Spirituality** would be corrupted. And if they couldn't withstand such an influence, going crazy, losing control, or even sudden death were certain outcomes.
                - On one hand, manifesting this incomplete [Mythical Creature Form](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mythical_Creature_Form "Mythical Creature Form") of a 4-meter tall one-eyed **Giant** will enhance a **Demon Hunter's** strength and level significantly, on the other hand, it would result in intense madness and provide strong inclinations towards the **Demon Hunter** losing control and becoming a [Rampager](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder#Rampager_\(Losing_Control\) "Beyonder").
            - While still at a disadvantage, a **Demon Hunter** in this state would be able to withstand the barrage of attacks from a **Silver Knight** who has not entered their **Mythical Creature Form**.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- Strengthened **Protection** → [[Combat (Twilight Giant)]]; strengthened **Light of Dawn** → [[Twilight (Authority)]] (Dawn) and [[Holiness (Twilight Giant)]].
- **Eye of Demon Hunting**, **Knowledge (Hunting, Alchemy)**, and **Mind Concealment** remain unattributed general abilities — Concealment in particular belongs to the Darkness Pathway's own [[Concealment (Darkness)]], not a Twilight Giant Authority. The incomplete Mythical Creature Form is a general Demigod trait, not an Authority.

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- The Eye of a Divine Giant.

Or
- The Heart of a Dragon-Eater.
- The vertical Eye of an Evil Spirit Hunter.

Or
- A Demon Hunter Beyonder Characteristic.

Supplementary Ingredients

- Choose one of:
    - 200 milliliters of blood from a Divine Giant,
Or
    - 200 milliliters of blood from a Dragon Eater.
- Choose one of:
    - 1 Skull of a Divine Giant.
Or
    - 1 Skull of an Evil Spirit Hunter.
- 49 different types of materials (different parts of Monsters or Herbs).
- 10 grams of Holy Ointment with purifying properties.

Resolve a problem or disaster caused by a powerful Demon or Evil Creature.

Note: Powerful is defined as possessing godhood.
