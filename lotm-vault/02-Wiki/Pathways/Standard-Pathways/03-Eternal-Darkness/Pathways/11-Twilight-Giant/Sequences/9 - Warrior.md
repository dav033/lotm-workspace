# Sequence 9 - Warrior (Divine Blood Warrior, Beyonder Warrior)

## Explicit Powers

### Traits

- **Physical Enhancement**: They possess supernatural strength and agility that greatly transcends those who are not **Beyonders**.

### New Abilities

- **Combat Mastery** (**Weapon**, **Armor**, **Martial Arts**): They have a mastery of all kinds of **Weapons** and **Armor**, with there being no **Weapon** they can't use.
    - **Warriors** also possess a mastery of all forms of **Combat** and **Martial Arts**, with there being no fighting style they can't learn.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- **Combat Mastery** (Weapon, Armor, Martial Arts) anticipates [[Combat (Twilight Giant)]] once the Pathway names its Authorities. **Physical Enhancement** remains an unattributed supporting trait.
