# Sequence 3 - Silver Knight

## Explicit Powers

### Individual Differences

- **Blessing**: **Silver Knights** are very good at frontal assaults. There will be small differences in the abilities between them due to different **Blessings** during their [Advancement Ritual](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder#The_Advancement_Ritual "Beyonder").
    - [Murskogan](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Murskogan "Murskogan") had received **Blessings** from the [Giant King Aurmir](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Giant_King_Aurmir "Giant King Aurmir"), which caused the various aspects of his **Silver Knight Beyonder** powers (**Mercury Liquefaction**, **Silver Rapier**, and **Light Concealment**) to be much stronger than normal.
        - [Colin Iliad](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Colin_Iliad "Colin Iliad"), who received [Klein's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") **Blessings** could occasionally make himself turn into a **Silver Rapier** and **Teleport** randomly, bringing along beneficial tendencies. In addition, his **Mercury Liquefaction** had gained some uniqueness.

**New Abilities**   ****

- **Mercury Liquefaction**: **Silver Knights** are able to melt into a malleable silvery metallic liquid that can restrict enemies and move rapidly in order to evade attacks.
    - The liquid volume of a **Silver Knight's Mercury Liquefaction** form is solely based on the **Silver Knight's** current mass upon ability activation.
        - They are also able to turn oneself into a **Silver Armor** for other targets (to defend an ally or to restrict and suffocate an opponent).
        - When getting suffocated, the enemy would appear as if they are wearing a full-body **Silver Armor** that has not a single gap.
- **Silver Rapier**: **Silver Knights** can condense silvery white beams of light (which take the appearance of swords)  out of thin air. These **Silver Rapiers** tear apart everything around it and are hard to detect.
    - These **Silver Rapiers** can **Teleport (Blink)** and bypass most barriers and obstacles, directly attacking their target. They can even erupt from within a target's body to deal heavy damage.
        - With a mere swipe of his finger after advancing into **Silver Knight**, [Colin Iliad](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Colin_Iliad "Colin Iliad") unleashed a single **Silver Rapier**, whose destructive power was enough to cleave a small hill in 2.
                - Enraged, [Murskogan](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Murskogan "Murskogan") utilized his **Silver Rapier**, forming a “long snake" that completely lifted the solid ground of the [King’s Court](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Giant_King%27s_Court "Giant King's Court"), tearing open a deep chasm.
        - Their **Silver Rapiers** are able to manifest almost without any indication, making it extremely difficult for an enemy to detect and predict where they will appear next.
- **Light Concealment**: Also known as **Light Occultation** or **Hide by Light**. A **Silver Knight** can utilize any light source as a way of dodging, evasion, and movement.
    - **Light Concealment** may pierce through objects depending on light-source connection.
        - It will take an **Ancient Bane** to prolongedly observe the area to discover the whereabouts of 2 **Silver Knight** statues (who lack life and intelligence) chasing an enemy out of instinct while **Concealing** their **Evil** intentions and being **Concealed** by the **Light**.
        - An **[Unshadowed's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sun_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_4:_Unshadowed) Unshadowed Domain** is able to fully nullify **Light Concealment**.
        - **Silver Knights** can also let other people enter their **Light Concealment**.
- **Silver Armor**: At this level, their **Protection** materializes as a full-body **Silver Armor** which is much stronger than a [**Giant Dragon**](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Visionary_Pathway "Visionary Pathway") in terms of withstanding blows.
    - It's defensive capabilities can shield a **Silver Knight** against a limited number of [Angels'](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Angels "Angels") attacks  while able to be permanently materialized as **Silver Armor**.
        - [Colin Iliad](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Colin_Iliad "Colin Iliad") had fully materialized his **Silver Armor** for days on end without experiencing any **Spirituality** expenditure upon advancing as a **Silver Knight**.

### Strengthened Abilities

- They have gained improvement of other **Beyonder** powers in the previous **Sequence**.
    - **Sword of Dawn**: At the **Sequence 3** level, a **Silver Knight** is now able to permanently materialize their **Sword of Dawn**, making it usable as a regular weapon.
        - A **Silver Knight's Sword of Dawn** possesses top sharpness and **Purification** attributes, while also being capable of tearing apart a small hill with a single strike.
        - **Hurricane of Light**: They can ignite their body with a transparent flame, emitting the bright light of **Dawn**, creating a terrifying **Hurricane of Light**.
        - [Colin Iliad](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Colin_Iliad "Colin Iliad"), upon using this ability, emanated beams of light that came from his **Soul**, body, **Beyonder Characteristics** as the two swords he struck out merged together, turning into a raging, violent **Hurricane of Light** that swept out and enveloped the [Dark Angel](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sasrir "Sasrir").
            - Once enveloped, the **Hurricane of Light** tore at the [Dark Angel's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sasrir "Sasrir") body, interrupting the powers "He" was going to use while making "Him" fade away into fragments of light due to losing the source of power that maintained "His" existence.
        - **Combat Mastery** (**Direct Combat**): They are extremely skilled in direct **Combat**.
        - **Physical Enhancement**: Their physical strength has been greatly enhanced.
        - When [Murskogan](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Murskogan "Murskogan") smashed his **Sword of Dawn** onto the ground, the resulting force caused the area to shake violently as the pillars snapped and fell into the orange cloud beneath and the ground between the two buildings exploded layer by layer, sending countless gravel flying.

### Mythical Creature Form

- Their [Mythical Creature Form](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mythical_Creature_Form "Mythical Creature Form") develops further, they are now a nearly 6 meter tall, one-eyed **Cyclops**. Their body is grayish-blue and their muscles are bluish-black. There is a dark crack on their forehead that attracts the [Souls](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul") around it. Their eyes are missing, replaced by smooth skin.
    - Every inch of their skin and flesh contains *immense power*, *infinite mystery,* and a *strange spiritual influence*. It makes everyone who witnesses them inevitably feel a sharp pain in their psyche, transforming them into a rampaging mess that wants to destroy everything.
        - These destructive tendencies also include themselves.
- As a **Sequence 3 Beyonder**, they can listen to prayers within a certain range.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- **Silver Armor** is this Sequence's materialization of **Protection** → [[Combat (Twilight Giant)]]. **Mercury Liquefaction**, **Silver Rapier**, and **Light Concealment** remain unattributed supporting powers.
- Strengthened **Sword of Dawn**/**Hurricane of Light**/**Combat Mastery** → [[Combat (Twilight Giant)]] and [[Twilight (Authority)]] (Dawn). The Mythical Creature Form's prayer-response is a general Demigod trait, not an Authority.

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- The essence of Aurmir's blood.

Or
- The Heart of a "King's Court Chaser" (a type of giant).

Or
- The heart of a "King's Court Watchman" (another type of giant).

Or
- A Silver Knight Beyonder Characteristic.

Supplementary Ingredients

- 300 milliliters of Water contaminated by the power of Death.
- One item worn or carried by a Sequence 3 or above from the Twilight Giant Pathway (for armor-type items, only one armor plate is required).
- One piece of Amber with a trapped ancient insect inside.
- One piece of paper recording 100 or more kills of the oneself.

### Advancement Ritual

Arrange a complex altar and place the remains of six powerful creatures that were hunted by oneself in the correct order and receive the blessing of a God (Angel-level at minimum).

The six creatures shouldn't necessarily be monsters, six Demigod-level Beyonders would also suffice.
