# Sequence 6 - Soul Assurer

## Explicit Powers

### Traits

- **High Spirituality**: A **Soul Assurer's** **Spirituality** will be enhanced slightly.
    - **Ritualistic Magic & Divination Arts**: They gain improvements in some spiritual abilities.
        - This gives them the ability to hold more complex **Ritualistic Magic**.
        - They can directly see spiritual lifeforms without the need to activate **Spirit Vision**.

### New Abilities

- **Requiem**: They can cause the [Spirit Body](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul") to sleep, suppressing and soothing their targets' desires, moods, and thoughts, this causes them to slow down or freeze on the spot.
    - This can also suppress spiritual abilities such as the **Spiritual** **Intuition** of the targeted entity.
        - They are able to directly sooth [Souls](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul"), ghosts, and the undead into silence using this ability.
        - Using **Requiem**, a **Soul Assurer** can also treat the emotional states of teammates, akin to a **[Psychiatrist](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Visionary_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_7:_Psychiatrist_\(Psyche_Analyst\))**.
        - Compared to a **[Psychiatrist's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Visionary_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_7:_Psychiatrist_\(Psyche_Analyst\))** **Placate**, a **Soul Assurer's** **Requiem** targets the [Soul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul").

### Strengthened Abilities

- A **Soul Assurer** has gained enhancements in previous **Sequence** abilities.
    - **Dream Invasion**: They can force targets in **Dreams** within a *150 meter range*.
        - **Midnight Poem**: They can use signing as a way to manifest effects.
        - **Agitate**: A **Soul Assurer** can agitate the [Spirit Body](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul") of a target, heightening the target(s) corresponding frustrations and destructive urges.
            - They can discern any latent problems in their target's [Soul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul") or mind through this.
- **Nocturnality**: They only need *two hours* of rest per day.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- **Requiem** (soothing Spirit Bodies into stillness) anticipates [[Spirit Body and Spirituality (Darkness)]], reported at Sequence 0 as being "the destination of Spirits and their Master."
- Strengthened **Dream Invasion** and **Midnight Poem** continue to anticipate the Dream sub-domain of [[Darkness (Authority)]].
