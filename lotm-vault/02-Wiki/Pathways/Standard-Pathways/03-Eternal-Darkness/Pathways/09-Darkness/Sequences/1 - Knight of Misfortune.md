# Sequence 1 - Knight of Misfortune

## Explicit Powers

### Translation Note

The "Misfortune" (厄难) authority of the **[Knight of Misfortune](#Sequence_1:_Knight_of_Misfortune)** uses slightly different characters compared to the "Misfortune" or "Bad Luck" (厄运) of the **[Wheel of Fortune](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Wheel_of_Fortune_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Wheel_of_Fortune)** or **[Darkness](#Sequence_0:_Darkness)**.
- **[Knight of Misfortune](#Sequence_1:_Knight_of_Misfortune)**: **Misfortune** (厄难, *Ènàn*):
    - 厄 (Adversity) + 难 (Disaster, Catastrophe); Can be translated as "Calamity", "Disaster", or their synonyms.
        - The second character, 难, is also present in the "Catastrophe" (灾难, *Zāinàn*) of a **[Demoness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Demoness_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_2:_Catastrophe_\(Demoness_of_Catastrophe\))**.
- **[Wheel of Fortune](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Wheel_of_Fortune_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Wheel_of_Fortune)** / **[Darkness](#Sequence_0:_Darkness)**: **Misfortune** (厄运, *Èyùn*):
    - 厄 (Adversity) + 运 (Fortune, Fate, Luck); Bad Luck

### Traits

- **Physical Enhancement**: "Their" Strength, agility, physique, and speed are true to the title of **Knight**, a **Knight** at the level of an [Archangel](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Angels "Angels").

### Authorities

- **Misfortune**: "They" can give **Misfortune** to enemies—the **Darkness'** authority over **Misfortune**.
    - While "Their" **Misfortune** leans more towards **Bad Luck**, national-scale **Misfortune** is possible.
        - This can manifest in the decline of a country's fortune accompanied by natural disasters, or as **Bad** **Luck** affecting every citizen of that country.
        - The **Misfortune** authority can bestow **Misfortune** upon [Wheel of Fortune](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Wheel_of_Fortune_Pathway), [Eternal Aeon](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Eternal_Aeon_Pathway), and [Eternal Edict](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Eternal_Edict_Pathway) [Beyonders](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonders "Beyonders") that are at the same level level as "Them".
- **Servant of Darkness**: In the **Dark Night**, a **Knight of Misfortune** is almost impossible to be truly killed, every time "They" die, "They" can return from the **Darkness** — a specific manifestation of the **Darkness** authority.
    - However, this doesn't mean immortality, since, eventually, one will integrate into the **Darkness**, becoming part of the **Darkness**.
        - Similar to the [Demoness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Demoness_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Demoness_\(Chaos_Demoness,_Primordial_Demoness\)) ' **Dark Magic** authority, there will be a significant degree of mitigation of **Darkness** -related attacks against "Them".

### Strengthened Abilities

- **Sword of Darkness**: The **Darkness** brings **Eternal Rest**, **Despair** and **Destruction**.
- **Commander of Spirits**: "They" can put **Natural** and **Undead Spirits** to sleep and command them.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- The source directly names both Authorities at this Sequence: **Misfortune** is "the Darkness' authority over Misfortune" → [[Misfortune and Catastrophe (Darkness)]]; **Servant of Darkness** (return from death via the Darkness) is "a specific manifestation of the Darkness authority" → [[Darkness (Authority)]].
- Strengthened **Sword of Darkness** → [[Darkness (Authority)]]; **Commander of Spirits** → [[Spirit Body and Spirituality (Darkness)]]. **Physical Enhancement** remains an unattributed supporting trait.

## Potion

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- A Knight of Misfortune Beyonder Characteristic

Supplementary Ingredients

- 1 "Fate's Blessing" documented on paper.
- 1 portion of the aura of the Underworld or Twilight Kingdom.
- 600 milliliters of one's own blood shed from a grievous injury.

### Advancement Ritual

- Bring Misfortune to either:
    - An Archangel
    - A civilization
    - A continent
    - A planet
- Note: In short, it is equivalent to "Your misfortune is me."
