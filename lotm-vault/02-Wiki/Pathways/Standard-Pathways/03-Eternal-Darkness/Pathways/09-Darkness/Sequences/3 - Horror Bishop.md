# Sequence 3 - Horror Bishop

## Explicit Powers

### New Abilities

- **Sword of Darkness**: It is a bone sword covered with strange patterns drawn from the depths of the **Darkness**, combining the power of **Repose** and **Horror**.
    - Swinging the sword can cause the **Darkness** to surge, making a tide of **Darkness** that symbolizes the danger in the **Darkness** as well as silent destruction and vanquishment.
        - When the target is submerged by the surging **Darkness**, it will be infected by the power of **Darkness** and directly melt, becoming part of the tide.
            - If not prevented in advance, it is unavoidable and cannot be escaped.
                - As this tide of **Darkness** created from waving this sword also symbolizes silent destruction and vanquishment, it is partially **Concealed**.
- **Nightmare World**: They can create a **Dream** world and pull everyone in range into it.
    - A **Horror Bishop's** **Nightmare World** range is around that of a small city*.*
        - It can protect a target's [Soul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul") from **Evil** or other kinds of **Corruption**.
        - They can move around while others are in the **Nightmare World**.
        - Examples of this in use are:
        - [Ilya](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ilya "Ilya") created a **Dream** that enveloped the entire Faoltec city in order to protect the soldiers and citizens from the aura of madness and evil emanated from [Suah](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Suah "Suah"), who was over two thousand meters away.

### Authorities

- **Horror Aura**: A lower manifestation of the authority of **Horror**.
    - When looking directly at them or being actively influenced by them, a target's body and mind will tremble involuntarily, which is difficult to contain and **Conceal**, and greatly affects their state.
        - **Beyonders** who are not strong enough may even be frightened to death by the **Horror Bishop**, or be scared out of control and become crazy.
                - As for "influence", a **Horror Bishop** merely entering a target's **Dream** can already cause that target to either lose control and/or die.
        - Newly promoted **Horror Bishops** cannot restrain their own terrifying aura, it is best that they don't appear in front of **Low-Mid Sequence** **Beyonders** and ordinary people.

### Strengthened Abilities

- Their previous abilities were drastically enhanced.
    - **Midnight Poem**: This ability of theirs have been greatly enhanced.
        - **Pacify**: Their chants and poems are able to put an entire city to sleep.
            - It makes the sunlight in a room suddenly dim, as if clouds had drifted in, causing the target to enter a deep **Slumber**.
        - **Spirit Commanding**: Their abilities with spirit have been enhanced.
        - **Intangible Spirit** : An intangible spirit that grows enormous and envelops the **Horror Bishop** entirely, carrying them into the [Mirror World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mirror_World "Mirror World").

### Mythical Creature Form

- As a **Sequence 3 Beyonder**, they can respond to prayers within a certain range.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- The source directly names the Authority at this Sequence: **Horror Aura** is "a lower manifestation of the authority of **Horror**" → [[Horror (Darkness)]].
- **Sword of Darkness** directly combines "the power of **Repose** and **Horror**" → [[Darkness (Authority)]] and [[Horror (Darkness)]] jointly, with its partial concealment touching [[Concealment (Darkness)]].
- **Nightmare World** continues to anticipate the Dream sub-domain of [[Darkness (Authority)]]; strengthened **Spirit Commanding** → [[Spirit Body and Spirituality (Darkness)]]. The Mythical Creature Form's prayer-response is a general Demigod trait, not an Authority.

Main Ingredients

- 10 grams of Underworld Supplicant's Residual Powder.
- 1 set of Residual spirits of nine creatures that died of fear.

### Advancement Ritual

- Face a situation that genuinely brings death, confronting the deepest biological fears.
- Survival isn't mandatory—what matters is overcoming or accepting the fear.
- Failure results in true death even after potion consumption.
