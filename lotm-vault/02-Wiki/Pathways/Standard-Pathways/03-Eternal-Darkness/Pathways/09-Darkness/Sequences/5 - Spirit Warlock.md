# Sequence 5 - Spirit Warlock

## Explicit Powers

### New Abilities

- **Spirit Commanding**: Their control over **Spirits** is greatly enhanced, they can **Seal** various **Spirits** within their teeth, then release them by tapping their teeth.
    - Their strength often depends on the **Deceased** and **Natural Spirits** they can find and control. They are better at controlling **Natural Spirits** compared to **[Spirit Guides](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_6:_Spirit_Guide)**, who are more suited toward **Deceased Spirits**.
        - The number of **Spirits** a **Spirit Warlock** can **Command** is limited by the strength of their own [Spirit Body](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul"). At this **Sequence**, they can only control *4-5 **Spirits*** at most.
                - The **Sealing** condition is defeating the **Spirits**. They can't **Seal** something exceeding their own level, otherwise it won't hold, unless other methods are used to complete the **Sealing**.
        - With the aid of these **Spirits**, they can effectively multitask, coordinating *2-3 tasks simultaneously*.
        - This is similar to the powers of a **[Gatekeeper](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_5:_Gatekeeper)**, though it is more focused on **Sealing** and **Sleeping** than an **Internal Underworld**.
        - Different types of **Spirits** have different strengths. Aside from **Wraiths**, **Shadows**, and **Evil Spirits**, the **Spirits** they have been shown to use are:
        - **Terror Banshee**: A powerful **Spirit World Creature** with a beautiful face and rotting body.
            - It has a pair of huge eagle wings and is good at draining the consciousness of others, injecting feelings of terror in them.
                - **Mud Spirit**: A rare **Natural Spirit** that resembles a series of illusory mud.
            - By enveloping the **Spirit Warlock**, they can sink to the ground and fuse into the earth. It allows them to quickly traverse the soil at a speed far faster than a steam metro.
                - **Shapeshifting Spirit**: A **Spirit** that can **Shapeshift** into a human, which can be used to fool attackers.
            - After being hit, the **Spirit** will rapidly turn transparent and dissipate.
                - **Severed Hands**: A pair of pale, translucent palms with bloody wrists.
            - It can be used to exert strength from afar, it can stop a **[Gatekeeper](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_5:_Gatekeeper)** from fully opening the **Door to the Underworld**.
                - Suitable **Spirits** can allow them to **Fly**  or **Float** in midair.

### Strengthened Abilities

- **Dream Invasion**: The range has increased to *200 meters*.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- **Spirit Commanding** anticipates [[Spirit Body and Spirituality (Darkness)]]. Its stated preference for controlling **Natural Spirits** over **Deceased Spirits** directly matches the Sequence 0 distinction that "Death leans towards the Undead while the Darkness leans towards Natural Spirits."
- Strengthened **Dream Invasion** continues to anticipate the Dream sub-domain of [[Darkness (Authority)]].
