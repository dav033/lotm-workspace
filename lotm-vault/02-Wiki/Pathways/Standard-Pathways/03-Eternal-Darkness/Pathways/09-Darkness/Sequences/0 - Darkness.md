# Sequence 0 - Darkness

## Explicit Powers

### Authorities

- **Concealment**: An authority that belongs to the **Singularity of All** symbolism. "They" can **Conceal** traces in all aspects, mislead related prying and [Divination](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Divination_Art "Divination Art"), make anything **Concealed**, undo such **Concealment**, and intuitively perceive and grasp many secrets.
    - "They" can directly drag "Their" targets into a **Concealed World**. Victims of this ability would seem to be wiped away from the real world, like a sketch being erased.
        - The **Concealed Worlds' Fate** isn't connected to the **Reality's** [Fate](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/River_of_Fate "River of Fate").
                - Victims are able to attempt escape; however, doing so will take some time.
                - "They" can transform into many symbols that symbolized **Concealment** and terror, enveloping "Their" enemies into a **Concealed** world.
                - "They" can turn hidden realms into **Concealed Worlds**.
            - These **Concealed Worlds** can then be used as **[Divine Kingdoms](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Divine_Kingdoms "Divine Kingdoms")** or as **Seals**.
        - "They" can silence others in various ways, ensuring that the secret "They" want **Concealed** will not be revealed by the target.
        - "They" are the most difficult [God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Gods "Gods") to be spied on or detected by others of the same rank.
- **Darkness**: "They" can induce **Dreams**, bring peace or **Eternal Rest**, and dissolve targets into **Darkness**, making them part of it. This can also be seen as partial authority over the **Dream** domain.
    - "They" can release the **Darkness** that is the **Destination of All Things**, which is extremely dangerous and may result in immediate death—even if "They" are trying to control it for an ally.
        - The remnants of "Their" own divine power can cause countless creatures within "Their" **Darkness** to vanish completely.
                - "They" can release a dense, profound, and silent **Darkness** that rapidly engulfs the target. This causes the projections of brilliant stars within the [Special Mirror World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Special_Mirror_Worlds "Special Mirror Worlds") to be submerged, extinguishing their radiance one after another.
        - As **Darkness** is one of the most fundamental symbols of the universe and an important motif, "They" cannot die in **Darkness** and can **Resurrect** with its help.
        - The **Tranquillity** and **Eternal Slumber** brought by the **Darkness** authority can resist **Corruption** and mental attacks by soothing the target.
        - When confronting a [Visionary](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Visionary_Pathway) **Beyonder** at the same level, "Their" **Dream** (**Darkness**) authority would lean towards **Nightmares**.
        - Even [True Deities](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Gods "Gods") can be pulled into a **Dream** through the **Darkness** authority via deep sleep inducement.
- **Spirit Body & Spirituality**: "They" are the destination of **Spirits** and their Master.
    - "Their" authority over the **Spirit Body** and **Spirituality** overlaps with the authority of the [Mother Goddess of Depravity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Goddess_of_Depravity) and [Death](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway).
        - **[Death](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Death_\(Eternal_Sleeper\))** leans towards the **Undead** while the **Darkness** leans towards **Natural Spirits**.
- **Misfortune & Catastrophe**: "They" can bring **Catastrophes** via **Misfortune**.
    - "Their" authority over **Misfortune** and **Catastrophe** overlaps with the authority of the [Demoness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Demoness_Pathway), [Red Priest](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Priest_Pathway), and [Wheel of Fortune](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Wheel_of_Fortune_Pathway) [Pathways](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways").
- **Horror**: "They" are the embodiment of the emotion of **Horror**.
    - "They" are able to make others feel tremendous fear and **Horror**, interrupting their actions.
        - [Evernight Goddess](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Evernight_Goddess "Evernight Goddess") ' eyes seemed to compress the starry night sky. It made one feel at peace, but it also made one feel an uncontrollable fear.
        - This authority overlaps with the authority of the [Mother Tree of Desire](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Tree_of_Desire).

## Authority → Power Mapping

- Sequence 0 is the capstone: the source directly confirms all five named Authorities of the Pathway.
- **Concealment** → [[Concealment (Darkness)]], directly "an authority that belongs to the **Singularity of All** symbolism" — matching this note's own symbolic derivation.
- **Darkness** (Dream/Nightmare domain, Destination of All Things) → [[Darkness (Authority)]].
- **Spirit Body & Spirituality** → [[Spirit Body and Spirituality (Darkness)]], directly stated to lean "towards **Natural Spirits**" versus Death's **Undead** — confirming the Sequence 5/6 inference.
- **Misfortune & Catastrophe** → [[Misfortune and Catastrophe (Darkness)]], confirming the Sequence 1/4 inference.
- **Horror** → [[Horror (Darkness)]], "the embodiment of the emotion of Horror" — confirming the Sequence 3 inference.

## Potion

### Potion Properties

> Soft, dreamlike and serene black gauze.

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- The Darkness Uniqueness.
- Three Knight of Misfortune Beyonder Characteristics.

Supplementary Ingredients

- 1 Moon or moon-like celestial body (mystically equivalent).
    - Note 1: The reason why the Moon is a supplementary ingredient for the Darkness is because of the "Light within Darkness" connection.
    - Note 2: "They" can either physically devour a real moon, or "They" can just use conceptual guidance to integrate it into the potion.

### Advancement Ritual

- Induce eternal slumber upon either:
    - A Uniqueness
    - A Divine Kingdom
    - A True Deity
- The higher the difficulty of the ritual, the better the effects.
