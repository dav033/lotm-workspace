# Sequence 1 - Illuminator (Civilization Enlightener, Light of Darkness)

## Explicit Powers

### Authorities

- **Civilization**: "They" have a certain degree of authority over **Civilization**.
    - "They" can allow **Chaos** to settle into a sliver of **Order**, allowing **Destruction** to give rise to a faint new **Life**, allowing **Darkness** to burst forth with the first **Light**.
        - "They" can rely on the weight of a **Civilization** in order to bind, impact, and crush a target.
        - "They" can reveal picture-like projections that record the vast and magnificent history of humanity and the development of **Civilization** to protect against attacks.
            - This is especially useful to contain the **Knowledge Injection** of a **[Knowledge Emperor](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hermit_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_1:_Knowledge_Emperor)** and can block them from passing through even in the state of **Informatization**.
        - "They" are able to prevent targets from correctly using **Civilization** technology.
        - **Illuminators** can even use **Civilization** to realize certain **Civilization** effects.
        - This is almost similar to **Miracles**, because if something is a core element of **Civilization**, "They" can do the corresponding **Civilization** effects such as:
            - Clearing certain sources of **Corruption**, which embodies the influence of **Civilization** in public health and sanitation, such as sewer systems.
                        - Rebuilding a damaged city at extremely fast speed, because **Civilization** is embodied in the field of infrastructure construction.
                        - Rapidly transport materials or personnel, or impede it because **Civilization** is also part of the transportation industry.
- **Manufacturing**: "Their" **Manufacturing** authority has been enhanced.
    - Since artistic and cultural expressions—like music, painting, literature, and other art forms—are components of **Civilization**, "They" can manifest "Them" with **Mechanical Creations**, while non- **Mechanical** **Creations** can exhibit certain **Beyonder** properties.
        - **Illuminators** can use this to compose music that directly influences the audience's emotions, or create paintings that can psychologically affect, inspire, attract, or even draw the viewer into the artwork.
- **Education**: "They" have a certain degree of authority over **Education**.
    - "They" can enhance the intelligence of a target, provide enlightenment and inspiration, trap the enemy in a mathematical maze, or achieve similar effects.

### Strengthened Abilities

- "Their" previous **Sequence** capability have been improved.

### Mythical Creature Form

- "Their" **Mythical Creature** now is capable of manifesting a complete depiction of **Civilization**.
