# Sequence 5 - Astronomer (Astromancer)

## Explicit Powers

### New Abilities

- **Astronomy**: If **Astronomers** have a preliminary understanding of the nature of different **Astronomical** phenomena, they can make use of them to a certain extent.
    - When there is a **Solar Storm**, they can guide the energy and matter that has arrived within the [Astral Barrier](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Original_Creator "Original Creator") and is within their own capability to concentrate to a certain extent.
        - This can be used to interfere with or attack the target.
        - When there is a **Meteor Shower**, they can guide the fire rain with a certain weight.
        - The strength of the capabilities is within the scope of a **Sequence 5 Beyonder**.
        - Of course, the universe is dangerous, and thus this power is dangerous.
        - If they are not careful, they will encounter all sorts of terrifying and bizarre things.
            - For example, when studying the **Blood Moon** phenomenon and trying to utilize it, they may find that they are inexplicably pregnant or are no longer themselves.
- **False Constellations**: **Astronomers** can create a fake **Starry** **Sky** in order to confuse or interfere with the astrological predictions made by the affected targets.
    - It can also be used to simulate astronomical phenomena, such as solar storms or meteor showers, provided the they mastered the essence of the corresponding phenomenon.
        - By utilizing **False Constellations**, they can reproduce similar effects to **Astronomy**, though these are weaker than those generated by genuine astronomical events.
        - The changes in constellations caused by this can also cause corresponding phenomena within a *100 meter range*, such as tides or electromagnetic disturbances.
- **Star of Curses**: They are able to fire rays formed from condensed **Starlight** that can cause various effects such as weakness, mutation, and trauma.
- **Blessing of Stars**: **Astronomers** are able to attract **Starlight**, altering the **Gravity** acting upon a targeted area or corresponding target.

## Authority → Power Mapping

This maps conceptual origin; it does not mean a Sequence 5 Astronomer owns an Authority.

- [[Reality Knowledge (Paragon)]] → **Astronomy**, **False Constellations**, **Star of Curses**, **Blessing of Stars** — the Paragon's expression of stellar phenomena leans toward the real, discovered astronomical side of the Knowledge symbolism, mirroring the Hermit's mystical/astrological expression of the same Stars.
