# Sequence 2 - Knowledge Magister (Master)

## Explicit Powers

### Translation Note

In the original Chinese text, 规律 (guīlǜ) carries multiple meanings and in this context, can be translated as " **Laws** ", as is done in the English WebNovel translation. However, as the term " **Law** " is already used for a similar word, 律法 (lǜfǎ), and both are treated as distinct authorities, this page will consistently use a single English term (**Principles**) to represent it for clarity and distinction.
- **Laws** (律法, *lǜfǎ*):
    - Refers to codified, enforceable laws—rules established by authority, often carrying judicial or binding force. It aligns with legal systems, decrees, and formal structures of governance.
- **Principles** (规律 *guīlǜ*):
    - Refers to inherent patterns or natural laws—the underlying order of reality that exists independently of authority. It is closer to universal principles or the fundamental workings of the world.
        - In other contexts this term can also denote simpler patterns or regularity, not a strict law, but rather an observable consistency or recurring behavior over time such as everyday routines or behavioral patterns.

### Authorities

- **Manufacturing**: "They" possess a weakened authority over **Manufacturing**.
    - "They" are able to create most of the items "They" imagine, even those related to the supernatural.
        - "They" can create futuristic wonders beyond the scope of current world-era technologies such as nuclear fusion-based technology, giant mechanical robots (such as the ones in [Gundam](https://pt.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gundam)), and interstellar travel starships with just a simple gesture.
        - "They" can also can have temporary substitutes in the absence of materials.
- **Principles**: "They" wield a certain degree of authority over **Principles**.
    - "They" can alter the physical **Principles** to a certain extent, making combustion not necessarily produce flames, frost bringing warmth, and even the earth no longer bearing weight.
        - "They" can weaken **Gravity**, causing nearby items to float even in the absence of **Wind**.
- **Analysis**: "They" have an enhanced **Analysis** authority.
    - "They" can see the essence of [Angels](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Angels "Angels") and formulate tactics accordingly.
        - [Bornova Gustav](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Bornova_Gustav "Bornova Gustav") saw an invisible vortex wearing a silk top hat and a black trench coat in essence. Inside the vortex, there were transparent and distorted maggots squirming about in the cluster when [Gehrman Sparrow](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") appeared before "Him".
- **Mentor**: This is derived from the authority of **Education**.
    - "They" possess the ability to **Test** others and are highly skilled at teaching others.
- **Learning**: "They" have a weakened **Learning** authority.
    - **Learning** something after just one look is no longer a myth.

### Strengthened Abilities

- "Their" previous **Sequence** capabilities have been improved.

### Mythical Creature Form

- "They" have become a full **Mythical Creature**. "Their" full **Mythical creature Form** is an **Informational** being that is composed of numerous depictions of civilizations, though still unable to manifest a complete depiction of **Civilization**.
    - "They" are able to transform into a substantial amount of pale-white fog to move around, which can contort and writhe later to rebuild "Their" body.
        - This is essentially a form of **Information** reconstruction; however, since the **Paragon** is [Steam](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/God_of_Steam_and_Machinery "God of Steam and Machinery"), this manifests as transforming into steam.
