# Sequence 4 - Alchemist

## Explicit Powers

### Description

**Mechanical Heaven** is not only a description of the **Divine Kingdom** of a **Paragon** but also an acknowledgment of the **Alchemists'** usual performance.

### New Abilities

- **Alchemy**: The core ability of an **Alchemist**.
    - An **Alchemist** can infuse the items they create with [Souls](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul"), giving them a certain **Life**, but its **Alchemical** life is not as good as that of the **[Ancient Alchemist](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_4:_Ancient_Alchemist_\(Classical_Alchemist,_Human_Alchemist\))**.
        - The [Souls](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul") and **Life** force are not created out of thin air. They come from fragments of remnant souls in the [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World") and the extraction of life from the surrounding environment.
                - They can create a machine that looks indistinguishable from humans via injecting it with a [Soul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul"), giving it the ability to move, talk, and behave just like a human.
                - The specialty of an **[Ancient Alchemist](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_4:_Ancient_Alchemist_\(Classical_Alchemist,_Human_Alchemist\))** can just barely be emulated to create different golems and human beings using incomplete spirits and various metals.
                - They can create spirituality gems out of humans to power mechanical prosthetics.
        - They can quickly create various temporary dolls and objects using readily available materials, achieving different effects which depends on the environment and materials.
        - They can also prepare long-term magical dolls with more powerful abilities in advance.
                - The dolls created by an **Alchemist** can disguise themselves as humans.
                - This is a tribute/reference to [Fullmetal Alchemist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fullmetal_Alchemist).
        - If they have high **Sequence** materials, they can even create things that can fight against [Angels](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Angels "Angels").

### Strengthened Abilities

- **Manufacturing**: Beyond combat, **Alchemists** play a wider role in daily life.
    - They can make a mechanical body that has powerful mechanical tools installed inside their bodies such as a cannon, a device that allows **Flight**, and functional cybernetic eyes.
        - [Claude](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Claude "Claude") modified his body, becoming a mechanical being with height of over three meters. One of his cybernetic eyes resembled an emerald, while the other resembled a ruby, supported by numerous components. His temples were encased in a transparent special material, revealing the squirming grayish-white brain within.
            - This made him able to perform any task just like a normal person, including combat. However, since his Sequence isn't high enough, he wasn't able to replace the human brain.
                        - He was also able to create a **Spirituality Gems** using human lives as power sources.
        - They are able to create an unusual circular disk covered in complex patterns with a mirror-like luster that is able to track down entities related to the items placed on it.
        - This can even apply on items that are actually **Historical Projections**.
            - Using the disk was something he had made just two days ago, [Jack Walton](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Jack_Walton "Jack Walton") put the Gustav Family Crest (that was a **Historical Projection**) on this device which served as a medium to point to [Louis Gustav](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Louis_Gustav "Louis Gustav").

Mythical Creature Form

- As **Demigods** at **Sequence 4** who have obtained partial **Godhood**, they are able to manifest their incomplete **Mythical Creature** **Form** if they disregard any of the consequences.
