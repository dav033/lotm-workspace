# Sequence 0 - Paragon

## Explicit Powers

### Symbolism

- **Knowledge** (**Information**): Derived from this symbolism, "They" hold the portion of authority that leans toward **Reality**.
    - This symbolism allows the [Gods](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Gods "Gods") of the two subordinate [Pathways](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways"), or even the **Sequence 1** or **Sequence 2**, to transform into a vast and complex torrent of **Information**, an illusory, overwhelming, and terrifying flood of **Information**.

### Authorities

- **[[Reality Knowledge (Paragon)|Reality Knowledge]]**: A **Paragon** possesses all the **Knowledge** that has been discovered in the **Real** world and is able to modify it to a certain extent.
    - This authority takes effect immediately at the continental level, rapidly at the planetary level and gradually at the star system level, but is ineffective outside of these levels.
        - The **Paragon** is able to cause a target to temporarily lose the corresponding **Knowledge** and be rejected by **Knowledge** or have **Knowledge** controlled by a target turn into a lurking agent or **Corrupter** beneficial to "Him".
                - "They" can make videos, photos, novels, or messages collapse into pure snow-like static, effectively destroying the **Information** structure it holds, which will also rapidly spread to form a huge **Information Vortex**.
            - Not only is this an effective way to interfere with general **Information**, it also can be used to destroy the origin of those **Information** or deal with the **Information** abilities of the [Hermit Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hermit_Pathway).
- **[[Civilization (Paragon Authority)|Civilization]]**: The rise and fall of **Civilizations**, the weight of **Civilizations**, and the vast **Knowledge** that has been accumulated by **Civilizations**.
    - "They" can designate a group, a country, a continent, or even a planet, keeping them in a state of barbarism and ignorance forever, or rapidly iterate and evolve the **Civilization**.
        - "They" can turn others into **Knowledge**, **Information**, and words, and imprint them into an illusory book, trapping those things which "They" can use for many different matters.
- **[[Principles (Paragon)|Principles]]**: "They" have partial authority over **Principles**, which leans toward **Reality**.
    - Compared to "Their" ability to change **Principles** of physics versus the **[Justiciar](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Justiciar_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Justiciar) '** s ability to change the underlying **Rules**, the **Justiciar** is stronger as it also effects mystical **Rules**.
        - **Principles** are a part of **Knowledge** and thus by mastering the **Principles**, a **Paragon** can make the world operate in a way that is more favorable to "Themselves".
        - "They" can release a blurry light that seeps out from the void and envelopes the surroundings, which has the effect of restoring the most basic structure of various objects.
- **[[Education (Demon of Knowledge)|Education]]**: "They" have partial authority to grant **Knowledge** to others.
    - Unlike the **[Hermit](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hermit_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Hermit)**, the **Paragon's** authority over **Education** places more emphasis on enlightenment, guidance, testing, and systematization.
- "They" have the subordinate authorities of **Learning**, **Manufacturing** and **Analysis**.
    - **Learning**: The authority of **Learning** can make the **Paragon** learn something easily, or make it impossible for others to **Learn** it no matter what.
        - **[[Manufacturing (Demon of Knowledge)|Manufacturing]]**: **Knowledge** combines in order to create miracles, almost divine things as well as things that are beyond the ordinary.
        - "They" can make advanced programs and effects that carry within it "Their" **Beyonder** powers and authorities that can then be bestowed onto everyday electronic devices like phones.
            - [Franca](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Franca_Roland "Franca Roland") used the **Information Shredder** to destroy all image of a painting created by [Luo Shan](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Luo_Shan "Luo Shan"). This in the end resulted in the destruction of the original painting.
                        - With various help and assistance, the **Information Shredder** created by the [God of Steam and Machinery](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/God_of_Steam_and_Machinery "God of Steam and Machinery") was used to kill off the [Hidden Sage](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hidden_Sage "Hidden Sage").
                - The [Mystical Items](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mystical_Item "Mystical Item") that "They" create without containing characteristics will still naturally fade over time—some last a few months, others a few years.
                - "They" are able to create an object that lets "Them" temporarily wield a [Uniqueness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniquenesses "Uniquenesses").
        - **Analysis**: The **Paragon** can quickly **Analyze** or even directly see the **Essence** of things, and then make use of them.
        - The **Analysis** authority heavily counters the **[Error](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Error)**, which relies on **Deceit**, or the **[Visionary](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Visionary_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Visionary)**, which relies on **Envisioning** false things.
- **[[Spirit World Mentor (Demon of Knowledge)|Spirit World Mentor]]**: The [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World") is a place where all kinds of **Information** converge, and **Information** is **Knowledge**.
    - It is naturally influenced by **Knowledge** [Pathways](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways"). Therefore, both the **[Hermit](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hermit_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Hermit)** and the **Paragon** have partial authority over the [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World").
- **Divine Kingdom**: **Mechanical Heaven** or **Civilization's Cemetery**, **Land of Perfection**.

## Authority → Power Mapping

The cited secondary inventory associates Sequence 0 **Paragon** with the listed Authorities. Their ownership and scope remain interpretations; the Pathway Hub separately identifies the Great Old One.

- [[Reality Knowledge (Paragon)]] → embodied directly as the "Reality Knowledge" authority listed above, encompassing all Knowledge discovered in the Real world.
- [[Civilization (Paragon Authority)]] → embodied directly as the "Civilization" authority listed above, governing the rise, fall, and accumulated Knowledge of Civilizations.
- [[Principles (Paragon)]] → embodied directly as the "Principles" authority listed above, the Reality-leaning counterpart to the Justiciar's Rules.
- [[Education (Demon of Knowledge)]] → embodied directly as the "Education" authority listed above, leaning toward **Enlightenment** rather than the Hermit's **Indoctrination**.
- [[Manufacturing (Demon of Knowledge)]] → embodied directly as the subordinate "Manufacturing" authority listed above, combining Knowledge to create miracles and beyond-ordinary things.
- [[Spirit World Mentor (Demon of Knowledge)]] → embodied directly as the "Spirit World Mentor" authority listed above, shared in partial form with the Hermit.

## Potion

### Potion Properties

> A flawless, ethereal humanoid form emitting a faint glow.

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- The Paragon Uniqueness.
- Three Illuminator Beyonder Characteristics.

Supplementary Ingredients

- Knowledge gleaned from the Aura or remnants of the Great Old Ones.

### Advancement Ritual

Nurture a Civilization, witnessing and recording its entire process from enlightenment, development and prosperity to decline, demise or replacement.

- Note: A regional civilization is sufficient. The main point is the long duration, and it cannot be interrupted.
