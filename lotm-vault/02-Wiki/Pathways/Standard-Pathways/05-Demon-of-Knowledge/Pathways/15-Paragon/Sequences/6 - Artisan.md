# Sequence 6 - Artisan (Machinery Specialist)

## Explicit Powers

### Traits

- **Spirituality**: Only the level of their [Ritualistic Magic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ritualistic_Magic "Ritualistic Magic") will be enhanced.

### New Abilities

- **Manufacturing**: Whether in reality or in the realm of the mystical, **Artisans** are undisputed masters of creation, being able to make **Beyonder** items and mechanical artifacts; however, those **Beyonder** items and mechanical artifacts will not be particularly powerful.
    - With **Manufacturing** as the "hammer" and **Spirituality** as the "fuel", **Artisans** can reshape raw [Beyonder Characteristics](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristics "Beyonder Characteristics") into completely different materials and shapes through the use of forging, assembling, inlaying, and other procedures.
        - There are still limitations in regards to the form of the [Beyonder Characteristics](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristics "Beyonder Characteristics") used as raw materials, which is based off the [Sequence](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sequence "Sequence") of the [Beyonder Characteristics](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristics "Beyonder Characteristics") itself.
        - Materials that are "symbolic" of a [Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways") can grant extra benefit from that symbolism, but this depends entirely on the specific material being used and not all materials that seem symbolic actually work this way or provide any benefit.
        - By using **Beyonder Characteristics** or the main ingredients for potions, **Artisans** are able to craft [Sealed Artifacts](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sealed_Artifacts "Sealed Artifacts") which have more powerful effects than normal while also possessing less negative and severe side effects than naturally formed or created [Sealed Artifacts](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sealed_Artifacts "Sealed Artifacts").
        - If a **Beyonder** item is made into a mechanical part, it takes effect on the entire construct, so multiple [Pathways](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways") **Beyonder** abilities can appear in a single mechanical creation.
            - This is so long as the balance is maintained without intense conflict.
                - The negative effects of the corresponding **Sealed Artifacts** depend on both the **Sequence** level of the **Artisan** and the [Sealed Artifacts'](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sealed_Artifacts "Sealed Artifacts") uniqueness themselves.
            - The abilities of their created **Sealed Artifacts** aren't necessarily weaker than a **Beyonder** of the same **Sequence**, and can even be stronger, but that is often a downside.
                - This means the stronger ability is the downside.
        - By imbuing items with **Spirituality**, they can produce enchanted **Beyonder** weapons such as axes, guns and bullets, cannons and cannonballs, bombs, etc. or worn [Mystical Items](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mystical_Item "Mystical Item") such as belts, brooches, rings, etc. with **Beyonder** effects but have a limited lifespan.
        - As such, they can directly fix the summoning rituals of [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World") creatures like **Messengers** into an item to make it more convenient.
        - Due to an **Artisan's** ability to know all the combinations of different **Beyonder** effects and negative effects while also not lacking [Mystical Items](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mystical_Item "Mystical Item"), their strength can reach the level of a **Sequence 5**.

## Authority → Power Mapping

This maps conceptual origin; it does not mean a Sequence 6 Artisan owns an Authority.

- [[Manufacturing (Demon of Knowledge)]] → **Manufacturing** — a direct, named expression of the authority: forging Beyonder Characteristics into Sealed Artifacts and mechanical items.
- **Spirituality** remains a generic trait (used only as ritual-magic "fuel" for Manufacturing) without a supported Authority assignment of its own.
