# Sequence 3 - Arcane Scholar (Demon of Arcana)

## Explicit Powers

### New Abilities

- **Knowledge Spirit**: They can transform into a certain amount of knowledge within a certain time limit (ranging from *5 minutes to* *days* depending on the knowledge they turn into).
    - In other words, when a person or a **Beyonder** opens a book and reads the text or learns knowledge, that **Information** may be the manifestation of a **Knowledge Spirit**.
        - The **Knowledge Spirit** uses this to enter the target's body of mind, subtly influencing their thinking and intelligence, completing the process of erosion and even **Corruption**.
                - It can also be active in the [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World"), giving certain misleading information or **Corruption** to those who obtain **Information** from the **Spirit World**.
- **Environment Controller**: An **Arcane Scholar** is able to accurately extract information about the surrounding environment and deduce what happened from it.
    - Though they cannot fully reconstruct the scene, they can confirm the battle that occurred in a certain area, the **Sequence** level involved, and the residual aura.
        - [Horamick Haydn](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Horamick_Haydn "Horamick Haydn") was able to instantly perceive upon his arrival that the battle that had taken place was at the [Saint](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Saints "Saints") Level, and also the residual aura created by the [Box of the Great Old Ones](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Box_of_the_Great_Old_Ones "Box of the Great Old Ones").
        - Regardless of the environment, an **Arcane Scholar** can effectively use the environment to enhance themselves and weaken the target.
- **Absorption**: They can extract **Life** force from organisms within a *five-kilometer radius*.

### Authorities

- **Dissociation**: They can emit a beam of light capable of destroying the structures of most objects. This application is derived from the **Analysis** and **Principles** authorities.
    - Targets that are affected will be decomposed into fragments that fall to the ground, with even their color evaporating and dissipating quickly.
        - [Horamick](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Horamick_Haydn "Horamick Haydn") pressed his extended palms against the wall. Without a sound, the majestic mural **Disintegrated**, turning into pieces of stone fragments that landed on the ground. Even the color quickly evaporated, disappearing without a trace.
                - Using the [Face of the Arcane](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Face_of_the_Arcane "Face of the Arcane"), [Franca](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Franca_Roland "Franca Roland") shot out from her eye a clear, misty light, which caused the invisible barrier to instantly reduce to its most basic, minute components and ceased to exist. Immediately after, the clear, misty light hit the bone- like figurine of the [Primordial Demoness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Primordial_Demoness "Primordial Demoness"), causing the figurine to begin to crumble.

### Strengthened Abilities

- Their previous **Sequence** capabilities have been improved.
    - **Manufacturing**: **Arcane Scholars** possess extraordinary **Manufacturing** abilities, being able to create mystical items, complex mechanical devices, or combine the two together.
        - They can **Manufacture** giant robots.
        - **Appraisal**: **Arcane Scholars** are able to quickly grasp the relevant information when using [Mystical Items](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mystical_Item "Mystical Item"), effectively avoiding its negative effects completely.

### Mythical Creature Form

- As a **Sequence 3 Beyonder**, they can respond to prayers within a certain range.

## Authority → Power Mapping

This maps conceptual origin; it does not mean a Sequence 3 Arcane Scholar owns an Authority.

- [[Principles (Paragon)]] → **Dissociation** — directly text-derived from the Analysis and Principles authorities, breaking down an object's basic structure.
- [[Manufacturing (Demon of Knowledge)]] → **Manufacturing** — creating mystical items, complex mechanical devices, or a combination of both.
- [[Reality Knowledge (Paragon)]] → **Appraisal**, **Environment Controller** — extracting and knowing the true, real nature and history of items and environments.
- **Knowledge Spirit** and **Absorption** read as distinctive Sequence-level traits (knowledge-manifestation, Life-draining) without a clean supported Authority assignment.

- Note: "Certain special Spirit World knowledge" refers to knowledge that can contaminate you, lock onto you, or knowledge that has come to life.
