# Sequence 5 - Constellations Master

## Explicit Powers

### Description

**Astrology**, **Constellations**, **Stars**, and **Starlight** are all concepts that possess dual meanings with both mystical and real astronomical significance. A **Constellations Master** has a basic grasp of the mystical aspects of these concepts, enabling them to utilize corresponding abilities.

### New Abilities

- **Starlight Cage**: Upon reciting the corresponding incantation, the brilliant **Starlight** swirling around the caster will begin to detach in successive streams, descending upon the target.
    - The **Starlight** instantly coalesces, forming a vast transparent amber that completely envelops the target, confining them to their original position.
- **Star Pillar**: A **Constellation Master** can scatter the **Stars** they create one after another and merge them into a magnificent pillar of **Starlight**, covering a certain area of space.
    - Any target within the affected area of space will be dissolved in this **Starlight**, instantly or extremely quickly having it's structure collapse and/or dissipate.
- **Stellar Self**: This ability makes the **Constellations Master's** form instantly transparent, as though transformed into a statue composed of countless **Starlight** particles
    - This "statue" can then shatter abruptly, as a brilliant **Starlight** surges forth in scattered points, allowing the **Constellation Master** to slip through the fissures as fragments of **Starlight** will coalesce anew within, revealing the caster's silhouette.
- **Starry Sea Shuttle**: They can make ships fly through the use of **Starlight**.
    - [Cattleya](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Cattleya "Cattleya") made bits of **Starlight** swirl around [The Future](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Stars_Pirates "Stars Pirates"), causing a squall to stir as the **Starlight** descended to form a swath of “floating ice”, dragging the ship into a “rapid flight".
- **Star Concealment**: **Constellation Masters** are able to use the **Stars** they have formed to **Conceal** the targeted area and nullify any outside interference within.
    - This could allow them to isolate certain **Beyonder** powers from affecting them.
- **Star Bridge**: They can manifest an illusory **Starry** bridge from their refined **Stars** to stand upon.
- **Star Illumination**: They can also utilize their **Stars** as a light source in dark areas.

### Strengthened Abilities

- **Eyes of Mystery Prying**: **Constellation Masters** will receive a great increase in their ability to sense hidden existences, making most things within a range of several tens if meters be visible to them in an unobstructed yet chaotic manner.
    - A **Constellation Master** can vaguely see layers of thick, shadowy curtains surrounding them, and behind these curtains, something seems to be watching them, watching all living beings.
        - They can also spy on the condition of an environment from a certain distance.
- **Spellcasting**: Their ability to cast **Spells** have been enhanced. Now, they do not necessarily need materials to cast some **Spells** and can instead cast them via simple gestures.
    - [Cattleya](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Cattleya "Cattleya"), through a few simple gestures and a whistle, was able to summon illusory ropes to bind two [Primordial Moon](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Primordial_Moon "Primordial Moon") believers.
        - **Flame**: They are able to summon a colorless **Flame** that is able to burn away things while selectively leaving other things unharmed.
        - [Cattleya](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Cattleya "Cattleya") summoned a colorless **Flame** to burn away the mutated vines affected by [Earth Mother](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lilith "Lilith") 's remnant aura while sparing the sailors.
        - **Myopia**: A special **Warlock** spell that weakens the effect of the **Eye of Mystery Prying** to avoid sudden death, but it couldn't be completely sealed.
        - [Nikila](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Nikila "Nikila") pulled out a brown leather scroll from an inner pocket, softly chanting a word in [Hermes](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Languages "Languages") -" *Myopia*." The scroll immediately ignited with black flames, quickly turning to ash. The mysterious deep purple in [Nikila](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Nikila "Nikila") 's eyes faded into the black depths of his pupils.
        - With the flick of her hand, [Cattleya](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Cattleya "Cattleya") was able to make the drops of blood of [Cielf June](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Cielf_June "Cielf June") to float and fly to her hands.

## Authority → Power Mapping

This maps conceptual origin; it does not mean a Sequence 5 Constellations Master owns an Authority.

- [[Mysticism Knowledge (Hermit)]] → **Starlight Cage**, **Star Pillar**, **Stellar Self**, **Starry Sea Shuttle**, **Star Bridge**, **Star Illumination**, **Spellcasting** — applied mystical knowledge of the dual (mystic/astronomical) nature of the Stars, channeled into Spells.
- [[Hiding (Hermit)]] → **Star Concealment** — using Stars to conceal an area and nullify outside interference, a direct echo of the Hiding authority.
- [[Mystery Prying (Hermit)]] → **Eyes of Mystery Prying** — strengthened here to sense hidden existences over a wide range.

- 90ml of pus from a Star Patterned Monster.
