# Sequence 4 - Mysticologist

## Explicit Powers

### Description

The power of a **Mysticologist** stems from their extensive knowledge of **Mysticism**, making them extremely dangerous. In addition, among all **Sequence 4 Beyonders**, a **Mysticologist** is the **Sequence** that encounters danger the easiest. This is because they often see things they shouldn't see, hear things they shouldn't hear, and know secrets they shouldn't be aware of. Thus, they must learn to restrain their curiosity if they wish to live a long life.

### Individual Differences

- **Mythical Creature Blood Spell**: Depending on the type of blood that was analyzed for their [**Sequence 4** Advancement Ritual](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hermit_Pathway/Advancement#Sequence_4:_Mysticologist "Hermit Pathway/Advancement"), a **Mysticologist's** strengths and abilities will vary.
    - For example, those who advance using the blood of a **[Snake of Mercury](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Wheel_of_Fortune_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_1:_Snake_of_Mercury_\(Giant_Serpent,_Snake_of_Fate,_Tail-Devouring_Serpent_of_Fate\) "Wheel of Fortune Pathway/Abilities")** will find it easier to become a **Clairvoyant** than others, and will also gain the **Spell Brief Luck**.
        - **Brief Luck**: This **Spell** gives the caster great amount of **Luck** for a short period of time.
            - Regardless of the terrifying **Lightning**, burning-white spear, or the slash of the **Black Knight**, none of them hit the target. [Cattleya](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Cattleya "Cattleya") seemed to be standing at the eye of the storm. No matter how dangerous the surroundings were, she was unaffected.
                - Those attacks and the attempts at control either narrowly passed her by or were canceled out by “friendly” forces. They were unable to achieve the desired effect, and they even helped her weaken the **Abyss** **Shackles** holding her down.

### New Abilities

- **Mystical Re-enactment**: The most core ability of a **Mysticologist**, a manifestation of their **Divinity** and knowledge. They are able to draw power from the different mysticism knowledge they grasped, and through that, create all sorts of magic or witchcraft.
    - The exclusivity of the mystical knowledge affects the strength of the **Spell**; the less that certain mysticism knowledge was known by others and the less that knowledge has spread, the more powerful **Spells** of this category will be and vice-versa.
        - There can be a chance of retaliation when using high level knowledge.
            - **Mysticologists** can draw power from the battle between [The Fool and Error](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/The_Fool%27s_Gambit "The Fool's Gambit") as well as the battle between [The God of Combat and Evernight Goddess](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/God_of_Combat#Divine_War's_Conclusion "God of Combat"); however, if they dare utilize it, they shouldn't be afraid of retaliation.
                        - If a **Mysticologist** used the **Mystical Re-enactment**; “ **The Feast of Betrayal** ”, once one of the three main leads of the “betrayal feast” sensed it, the **Mysticologist** would definitely die for obscure reasons. They would be unable to resist their death and die in an abnormally horrifying way, which is why [Cattelya](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Cattleya "Cattleya") only used this **Spell** with the blessing of [Mr. Fool](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti").
        - With their core ability of **Mystical Re-enactment**, **Beyonders** of the **Hermit** **Pathway** can potentially live longer and grow stronger through witnessing various mystical events, mastering much mystical knowledge, and creating powerful **Spells**.
        - Some examples of **Mystical Re-enactments** are as follows:
        - **The Feast of Betrayal**: it conjures up an image of the [Ancient Sun God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ancient_Sun_God "Ancient Sun God") getting devoured. It originates from the **Mysticism Knowledge** of the [Ancient Sun God's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ancient_Sun_God "Ancient Sun God") Death.
            - Its effect is to temporarily awaken or imbue items in the target's possession with sentience, causing them to betray the target.
                - **The Chess Game of Time**: This **Spell** slows down the actions of the target. It is like entering a region with a different flow of time.
        - **Mystical Re-enactments** that are created from the Fairy Tales of Earth's Original Civilizations are called **Fairy Tale Magic**. A few examples are:
        - **Avalon (Utopia)**: The **Utopia** is neither in reality nor in the [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World"). It is completely hidden somewhere, and only those who are invited or qualified can enter. Moreover, one must also master the corresponding sequence of numbers, 365 digits in total.
                - **Spear of Longinus**: A **Spell** that derives power from the knowledge of the [one true spear](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holy_Lange "wikipedia:Holy Lange") that pierced Jesus Christ in the Bible. It emits a strong sense of destruction and is able to lock against the targeted enemy, following them wherever they go.
            - To avoid the **Spear of Longinus**, a **[Secrets Sorcerer](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Door_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_4:_Secrets_Sorcerer "Door Pathway/Abilities")** would have to keep on "entering" into different **Concealed** spaces and hiding within illusory **Doors** over and over again as they were destroyed.
                - **The Ugly Duckling**: A **Spell** that derives power from the story of " [The Ugly Duckling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Ugly_Duckling "wikipedia:The Ugly Duckling") ". It allows the caster to temporarily use their **Mythical Creature Form** without having to worry about losing control but only for 10 seconds and once per day.
                - **Snow White**: A **Spell** made from the story " [Snow White](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snow_White "wikipedia:Snow White") ". It shares similar characteristics with **Divination** **Spells** and can counter **Divination** and clears the battlefield of the caster's tracks. It takes the form of a illusory, ancient **Mirror**.
            - It can also be used to increase the success rate and accuracy of **Prophetic** abilities.
                - **The Emperor's New Clothes**: A **Spell** derived from the story of *" [The Emperor's New Clothes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Emperor%27s_New_Clothes "wikipedia:The Emperor's New Clothes") "*. The caster becomes something that doesn't exist at all, allowing them to temporarily avoid any kind of attack once.
                - **Ariadne's Thread**: A **Spell** derived from the mythology of " *[Ariadne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ariadne "wikipedia:Ariadne")* ". This **Spell** creates an illusory ball of yarn that, when thrown into the void, leaves behind a bright colored thread which allows the caster to traverse the [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World") by following the thread.
            - It is also able to guide the user's direction.
                - **Jack and the Beanstalk**: A spell made from the story *" [Jack and the Beanstalk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_and_the_Beanstalk "wikipedia:Jack and the Beanstalk") "*. It can change the environment to a forest of long beanstalks and vines.
                - **The Little Mermaid**: A **Spell** invented from the story *" [The Little Mermaid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Little_Mermaid "wikipedia:The Little Mermaid") ".* With the **Spell**, the caster can turn **Invisible** and disappear into thin air.
                - **The Peach Blossoms' Source**: Inspired by " *[The Peach Blossom Spring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Peach_Blossom_Spring "wikipedia:The Peach Blossom Spring")* ", this **Spell** creates a barrier that is difficult to connect with the outside world.
                - **Sleeping Beauty**: A **Spell** derived from the story *" [Sleeping Beauty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sleeping_Beauty "wikipedia:Sleeping Beauty") "* that **Hypnotizes** the enemy and makes them tired and wanting to fall asleep.
                - **The Boy's Magic Horn**: From [The Boy's Magic Horn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Des_Knaben_Wunderhorn_\(Mahler\) "wikipedia:Des Knaben Wunderhorn (Mahler)"). This **Spell** condenses into a dark, ancient horn, carrying an extremely heavy and powerful connotation.
            - It's a horn of destruction and its low, resonant sound, combined with sonic waves, can shatter enemies and even **Space** itself.
                - **Hades' Invisible Helmet**: A **Spell** made from the myth of the " [Helm of Hades](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cap_of_invisibility "wikipedia:Cap of invisibility") ". It allows the caster to become **Invisible**.
                - **The Little Match Girl**: It conjures up a burning match, causing the enemy to hallucinate.
            - This **Spell** can cause a **[Secrets Sorcerer](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Door_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_4:_Secrets_Sorcerer "Door Pathway/Abilities")** to be influenced for a moment before breaking free.
                - **Cinderella**: It transforms the user's appearance, image, and temperament.

### Strengthened Abilities

- **Eyes of Mystery Prying**: The is the second core ability of a **Mysticologist**, their **Eyes of Mystery Prying** can now detect more **Concealed** objects and attributes.
    - They gain better control over their eyes, allowing them to deactivate the power of **Mystery Prying**.
        - A **Mysticologist's** mere gaze will be able to pierce into the [Cosmos](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Cosmos "Cosmos") and the world barrier.
        - [Cattleya](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Cattleya "Cattleya") upon advancing could see the various celestial objects and [Outer Deities](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Outer_Deities "Outer Deities") that were occupying the area as well as "Their" gaze on earth.
        - They can plant their **Eyes of Mystery Prying** onto someone else's body to spy on them.
        - Once it goes into a **Concealed** state it will be very difficult to detect it through other means.
- **Stellar Guidance**: Their previous capabilities involving **Stars** has been enhanced.
    - **Star Pillar**: The **Star Pillar** that they can make can envelop the surrounding area, preventing even a **[Secrets Sorcerer](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Door_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_4:_Secrets_Sorcerer "Door Pathway/Abilities")** from **Teleporting** and leaving the enveloped area.

Mythical Creature Form

- As **Demigods** at **Sequence 4** who have obtained partial **Godhood**, they are able to manifest their incomplete **Mythical Creature** **Form** if they disregard any of the consequences.
    - Their **Mythical Creature** **Form** looks like an abstract "black blob" appearing to be in a higher spatial dimension covered in cracks and eyes which have clear blacks and whites, coldly scanning their surroundings, as though they were formations of multifarious knowledge.
        - In this **Mythical Creature Form** a **Mysticologist** will be able to induce a knowledge storm in the minds of their targets, disrupting and distracting their thoughts.
                - Certain **Spells** (like **The Ugly Duckling**) that a **Mysticologist** possesses can allow them to temporarily reveal their **Mythical Creature Form** without danger.

## Authority → Power Mapping

This maps conceptual origin; it does not mean a Sequence 4 Mysticologist owns an Authority.

- [[Mysticism Knowledge (Hermit)]] → **Mystical Re-enactment**, **Mythical Creature Blood Spell**, **Stellar Guidance** — drawing power directly from grasped mysticism knowledge and mystical/historical events is the core expression of the authority at this Sequence.
- [[Mystery Prying (Hermit)]] → **Eyes of Mystery Prying** — the pathway's second core ability, now able to pierce the Cosmos and deactivate at will.

## Potion

### Potion Properties

> "A phantom, dark liquid from which purple sparks are constantly being ignited deep within"

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

- A vague, gem-like orb emanating a gloomy aura, as if a gaze peered out from within to scrutinize everything.
- An illusory gemstone shaped like an eyeball.
    - Its radiance subdues and exudes a murky aura, as though a gaze emanates from within to scrutinise all things.

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- 1 Scalp of an Arcane Spirit.
- 1 Eye of Evil God's Pupil.

Or
- A Mysticologist Beyonder Characteristic.

Supplementary Ingredients

- 13 drops of an Arcane Spirit's Tears.
- 80ml from an Eye of Evil God's Ash Liquid.
- 1 relic related to a mystical event.
- 1 Warlock potion created by the concoctor (any effect is acceptable).

### Advancement Ritual

Completely analyze a drop of blood from a Mythical Creature and obtain complicated and massive amounts of knowledge.

- Note 1: Due to the differing types of Mythical Creature blood employed, should they succeed, their specialisation within the 'Mysticologist' stage will also exhibit certain variations.
- Note 2: Although the advancement ritual of a Mysticologist doesn't have any requirements on the Mythical Creature's pathway, the best choice is still the Wheel of Fortune Pathway.
- Note 3: The blood of an Angel-level creature that is one of the main materials for a Sequence 2 angel would work, but the effect is not as good as a complete one.
- Note 4: The blood of a Boon-recceiving Angel would also work, as long as "Their" physical state has changed.
