# Sequence 2 - Sage

## Explicit Powers

### Authorities

- **Mysticism Knowledge**: "They" possess a certain degree of authority over **Mysticism Knowledge** as well as the corresponding, subordinate authorities over **Deletion** and **Revision**.
    - "Their" core ability is the control and utilization of **Information**, including but not limited to the **Sage's** own **Informatization**. By abstracting the body and breaking it down into streams of complex **Knowledge**, "They" will become a pure being of **Information**.
        - "They" can turn into a pure **Information** torrent and reform later to bypass any obstruction as well as transform into various symbols and words that can be either mystical or ordinary.
            - Even if some of "Their" **Information** were to be disrupted or destroyed, as long as the overall structure and balance remained as "They" reformed, "They" wouldn't die. At most, "They" would be missing a few limbs or **Spells** that can be added back later.
                        - This state makes "Them" impervious to most damage, but "They" can still be hurt by things like the [Tyrant Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tyrant_Pathway "Tyrant Pathway") 's **Information** -interfering abilities, the **[Paragon's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Paragon_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Paragon "Paragon Pathway/Abilities")** authority over **Reality Knowledge**, and the [Error Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway "Error Pathway") 's **Theft** and **Gifting**.
                - **Stealing** limbs isn't practical but **Stealing** abilities or data is easy, like using scissors to cut out a piece of **Information** and take it away. Even when turning into an **Information** torrent, a **Marauder** could **Gift** something else back to a **Hermit**, leaving them with some strange extra components.
                - In this state, "They" can detach "Themselves" from the state of being overwhelmed as a **Information** torrent, reassembling the body using vast and pure **Knowledge**.
        - In an **Informatized** -state, if "They" possess enough self-understanding, without disrupting the overall **Information** balance within "Them", "They" can add appropriate, non-conflicting, and fully mastered **Mysticism Knowledge** into a corresponding **Information** stream, while **Deleting** the unnecessary **Information**.
        - This will complete the transformation of the **Sage's** body as well as achieve the standardization and acceleration of certain **Spells**.
            - [Torriope's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Torriope "Torriope") modified **Information** flow let "Him" reform "Himself" into a combination of 5 things: a menacing wolf's head with black fur, a blazing throne of fire, a pale face with strange eyes, a writhing mass of maggots, and numerous asymmetrical symbols.
                - All of these are **Mystical Re-enactments** and this form allows "Him" to cast these instantly.
                        - [Retia Austin](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Retia_Austin "Retia Austin") was able to gradually transform "Herself" into a giant **Dragon** fully at the **Informational** level. Such a transformation granted "Her" a huge boost in physical strength and vitality.
        - "They" can see and filter out torrents of **Information** "They" want to know as well as be able to forge and manipulate **Information** itself.
        - "Their" ability to see **Information** allows "Them" the ability to see through realistic illusions as well as find weak points and exits in various **Seals**, such as spatial maze structures.
            - "They" are also able to correspondingly manipulate and redirect the flow of **Information** within such **Seals** or structures in order to bypass such obstructions. This even allow "Them" to make transport **Information** from and into the [Mirror World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mirror_World "Mirror World").
        - A **Sage** can create false **Information** that misleads others, as well as be able to create false **Information** in order to deceive laws.
        - [Torriope](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Torriope "Torriope") was able to prepare false **Information** in the form of a false **Informatized** self of "Him" while "His" **Concealed** true **Information** was rushing to escape [Madame Magician](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fors_Wall "Fors Wall").
                - "They" could make the sensation of coldness be experienced by the surrounding entities even if said coldness does not exist in the environment.
        - "They" can assimilate objects and **Beyonder** powers into a part of "Their" **Knowledge**.
        - This can allow "Them" to absorb attacks into "Their" **Informatized** body.
- **Education**: "They" have a small authority over **Education**.
    - "They" can impart **Knowledge** in the form of an invisible stream of **Information** carrying mystical power to targets they are looking at and can “materialize” **Knowledge** to pursue and attack.
        - This causes those affected to be stunned as they try to process the **Knowledge** given.
            - [Bernadette](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Bernadette_Gustav "Bernadette Gustav") used a pop-up message to incapacitate multiple people, so that [Lumian](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee") and team could be able to attack [Zaratul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Zaratul "Zaratul").
- **Mystery Prying**: "Their" **Prophecies** have been enhanced.
    - **Clairvoyance** (**Prophecy**): Once "Their" enemies take action, "They" can gain certain scenes or fragments of the future that can help aid "Them" in navigating and counterattacking such plans.
        - Any of "Their" enemies who are aware of this ability can only try to delay when such perceptions appeared or make the corresponding **Prophecies** difficult to interpret.

### Strengthened Abilities

- "They" have gained improvements to other abilities in the previous **Sequences**.
    - **Mystical Re-enactment**: This power of "Theirs" have been enhanced.
        - **Loss of Control Gaze**: A **Spell** derived from the strange death of one of the founders of the [Moses Ascetic Order](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Moses_Ascetic_Order "Moses Ascetic Order"). This **Spell** creates an illusory figure that, as soon as it solidifies, causes bloody chunks of flesh to fall continuously from its body. Each piece of flesh then turns into a cold, emotionless eye that emanates an aura of chaos, madness, and evil.
            - This **Spell** can cause even **Angels** to show obvious signs of losing control. The effect will be better the more unstable the targets mental state.
                - "They" can make a **Spell** that is related to **Concealment** and **Resurrection**, allowing "Them" to **Revive**.

### Mythical Creature Form

- "They" have become a full [Mythical Creature](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mythical_Creature_Form "Mythical Creature Form"). "Their" [Mythical Creature Form](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mythical_Creature_Form "Mythical Creature Form") is that of an **Informational** serpent or eyeball consisting of countless cold, eyelash-less eyes that are on the body.
