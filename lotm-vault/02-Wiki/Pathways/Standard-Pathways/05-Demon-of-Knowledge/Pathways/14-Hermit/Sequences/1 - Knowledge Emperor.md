# Sequence 1 - Knowledge Emperor

## Explicit Powers

### New Abilities

- **Knowledge Emperor**: "They" are able to utilize the **Information** gathered in the [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World") and influence the [Seven Lights](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Seven_Lights "Seven Lights").

### Authorities

- **Mysticism Knowledge**: "They" gained a deeper grasp over this authority.
    - **Knowledge** can bring power, and power can also bestow **Knowledge**. This is the authority of a **Knowledge Emperor**, which lets a **Knowledge Emperor** imbue abstract **Knowledge** with tangible power.
        - [Roselle Gustav](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Roselle_Gustav "Roselle Gustav") bestowed power to the **Knowledge** in the written potion formulas of the 22 [Pathways](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways"), transforming it into [Cards of Blasphemy](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Cards_of_Blasphemy "Cards of Blasphemy") that possessed the following effects:
            - Once assimilated into the user's **Spirit Body**, it will cause it to solidify and possess the full aura along with status of a **High Sequence Beyonder** of the represented [Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways").
                - If the user is of the same [Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways") as what the [Card of Blasphemy](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Cards_of_Blasphemy "Cards of Blasphemy") represents, they will obtain a boost in their abilities; however, it will not reach the **High Sequence** level like the aura it can bring out.
                        - Upon activation, a [Card of Blasphemy](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Cards_of_Blasphemy "Cards of Blasphemy") will create a convergence of **High Sequence Beyonders** of the particular [Card of Blasphemy's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Cards_of_Blasphemy "Cards of Blasphemy") represented [Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways").
                        - A [Card of Blasphemy](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Cards_of_Blasphemy "Cards of Blasphemy") possesses extremely strong **Anti-Divination** and **Anti-Prophecy** attributes that is able to **Conceal** it's location to even [True Deities](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Gods "Gods").
                        - **Note**: A reason the [Cards of Blasphemy](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Cards_of_Blasphemy "Cards of Blasphemy") carry such strong **Anti-Divination** powers is due to the [Mother Goddess of Depravity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Goddess_of_Depravity "Mother Goddess of Depravity") 's **Corruption** of "Him".
                - The [Mother Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Pathway "Mother Pathway") card is especially insidious due to the connection with "Her".
                - If "They" have sufficient **Knowledge** about a certain **Beyonder** power, "They" can theoretically imbue that **Knowledge** with power and incorporate it into "Their" own **Information Stream**, thereby gaining the **Beyonder** powers of other **Sequences**.
            - If "They" have enough **Knowledge** about a **Beyonder** power like **Disorder**, "They" can give power to that **Knowledge** and incorporate in "Their" **Information** flow.
                        - If "They" fully analyzed the knowledge of a **[White Angel](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sun_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_1:_White_Angel "Sun Pathway/Abilities")** and **[Conqueror](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Priest_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_1:_Conqueror "Red Priest Pathway/Abilities")**, "They" could endow that **Information** with power and incorporate it within "Them".
                - "They" could then combine **Pure White Light** and **Spear Of Destruction** to create an attack like **Pure White Spear of Destruction**; however, it won't possess the corresponding authority. An attack created this way can reach the power limit that doesn't contain Authority.
        - "They" can make others switch between a normal and **Information** stream state, helping them bypass obstructions. "They" can then help those affected reform later.
        - "They" can draw the surrounding **Information** into the palm of "Their" hand, condensing it into a shimmering ball of light containing the corresponding **Information**.
        - This not only allows "Them" to ascertain certain matters through **Information** but also allows "Them" to siphon certain **Information** which "They" can then replace.
        - **Digital Avatar**: When "They" transform into "Their" **Information** / **Knowledge** flow state, "They" are able to become a **Digital Avatar**.
        - This can allow "Them" to become a **WeChat** account, a character in a manga, or even an NPC or player in a video game.
        - "They" can ensure the element of surprise in a sudden attack.
        - [Zaratul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Zaratul "Zaratul") needed the help of a **Knowledge Emperor** or a **[Servant of Concealment](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Darkness_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_2:_Servant_of_Concealment "Darkness Pathway/Abilities")** in order to ensure the element of surprise in a sudden attack against [Klein Moretti](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti").
        - "They" can forge **Information** and spread it along **Spirit Body Threads**.
        - [Roselle Gustav](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Roselle_Gustav "Roselle Gustav") forged false **Information** about a targeted **Marionette** and sent it through the corresponding **Spirit Body Threads** to isolate [Klein Moretti's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") true body.
            - This worked by modifying **Information** of the owner of that particular **Marionette**, isolating the influence that [Suah](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Suah "Suah") and [Zaratul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Zaratul "Zaratul") had on [Klein Moretti](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") as a result.
- **Education**: "They" have more authority over **Education**.
    - "They" can conjure up complex and illusory **Symbols** in their eyes, pouring a massive amount of useless **Knowledge** into the target's mind, causing the target's head to explode.
        - In an instant, [Klein](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") 's head felt like it was exploding while his other thoughts were completely occupied, so much so that he couldn’t even lift a finger.
        - A **Knowledge Emperor** can protect other **Beyonders** of the from the **Knowledge Pursuit** of the [Hidden Sage](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hidden_Sage "Hidden Sage").
- **Mystery Prying**: "They" have a certain degree of **Mystery Prying** authority.
    - The **Mystery Prying** authority allows "Them" to directly **Pry** into many **Mysteries**, including those of the [True Deities](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Gods "Gods") and even [Great Old Ones](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Above_the_Sequence "Above the Sequence").
        - **Clairvoyance** (**Prophecy**): By momentarily receiving the strength of a **Knowledge Emperor** through a **Wish** from the [Magic Wishing Lamp](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Magic_Wishing_Lamp "Magic Wishing Lamp"), [Bernadette](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Bernadette_Gustav "Bernadette Gustav") managed to sense the death of [George III](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/George_Augustus_III "George Augustus III") via her **Clairvoyant** abilities.

## Authority → Power Mapping

This maps conceptual origin; it does not mean a Sequence 1 Knowledge Emperor owns an Authority.

- [[Mysticism Knowledge (Hermit)]] → **Knowledge Emperor** (core ability), **Digital Avatar** — deepened control over Information: imbuing Knowledge with power and shifting between physical and Information-stream states.
- [[Mystery Prying (Hermit)]] → **Clairvoyance** (**Prophecy**) — "a certain degree" of Mystery Prying authority extended even to sensing events involving True Deities and Great Old Ones.
- The page's own **Education** authority bullet (mass knowledge-injection) is already named directly in the text above.

## Potion

### Potion Properties

> A chaotic mass of intermingled bubbles in every colour imaginable

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> A transparent, illusory crown formed from the convergence of diverse information.

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- A Knowledge Emperor Beyonder Characteristic

Supplementary Ingredients

- 1 artifact engraved with knowledge related to the concoctor.
- 1 crown, in the truest sense.
- 9 types of knowledge closely related to Sequence 0.
- 1 tome compiling the knowledge discovered by concoctor.

### Advancement Ritual

Analyze oneself and master all knowledge related to oneself, then understand and find the most core, key part (in the Western Continent, this process is called "Illuminating the Mind and Perceiving the True Nature") and use it as a link to find a way to integrate the remaining parts and other knowledge they have to initially form a whole.

- The whole form is known as a Primordial Spirit/Nascent Soul.
