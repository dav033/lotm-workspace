# Sequence 8 - Melee Scholar

## Explicit Powers

### Traits

- **Physical Enhancement**: A **Melee Scholar** will gain an improved fitness.

### New Abilities

- **Combat Prying**: They seek the mysteries behind things, and fighting is one such thing.
    - This allows them to master many different styles of martial arts.

## Authority → Power Mapping

This maps conceptual origin; it does not mean a Sequence 8 Melee Scholar owns an Authority.

- [[Mystery Prying (Hermit)]] → **Combat Prying** — applying the pathway's prying instinct to the "mystery" behind combat technique.
- **Physical Enhancement** remains a generic potion trait without a supported Authority assignment.

## Potion

### Potion Properties

> A gray liquid that resembles a raging storm

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> A piece of bone with a dark gray color. It feels cold to the touch

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- The spine of an Iron-Armed Therianthrope (they may have been transformed from different races of wild animals, sharing the common feature of thick, iron-like forearms).
- The heart of a Four-Eyed Green Turtle.

Or
- A Melee Scholar Beyonder Characteristic.

Supplementary Ingredients

- 100ml of Iron-Armed Therianthrope blood.
- 20g of a Four-Eyed Green Turtle's shell powder.
- 9 sharp nails.
