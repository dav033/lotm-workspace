# Sequence 7 - Warlock

## Explicit Powers

### Traits### New Abilities

- **Spellcasting**: **Warlocks** use their knowledge of magic, gained through study, analysis, learning, and sometimes their connection to the [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World"), to create their **Spells** using their knowledge and mind.
    - This makes them very versatile, and they can be considered as a traditional fantasy mage.
        - The more knowledge they have the more comprehensive and powerful their **Spells** become.
        - They can't cast **Spells** quickly as even the simplest of **Spells** require 3 steps to finish:
        1. **Focus**: Concentrating one's own **Spirituality**.
                2. **Mental component**: Outlining the corresponding symbol of the **Spell** in their mind which can be replaced by reciting the **Spell's** corresponding incantation aloud.
            - While casting steps are required for a **Spell**, they can be omitted later.
                3. **Material component**: Using the materials to cast the **Spell**. The materials can either serve as a medium, or part of the **Spell**.
            - They can also use certain [Sealed Artifacts](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sealed_Artifact "Sealed Artifact") as a substitute for certain materials.
        - Some of the **Spells** they can cast include:
        - **Scene Depiction**: Using some powder to display the scenes they see from their **Eyes of Mystery Prying** onto mirrors or directly tossing powder to form a realistic picture.
                - **Soul Summoning**: A supplementary **Spell** designed to aid spirits in separating from the flesh or to help [Astral Projections](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul") find their spirits when adrift in the spirit realm.
                - **Hand of Force**: Using an iron-black powder to create a strong **Invisible Hand** which can be used as a way to exert force on their targets from afar.
                - **Wind Creation**: Using a slightly transparent powder to create strong **Winds**, this is useful to blow away airborne diseases or toxic gas.
                - **Exorcism**: Using pearl-like powder to clear weeds and vines, this is also useful to combat plant-based **Beyonder** powers.
                - **Flames**: Using powder that accurately lands on their targets, bursting into scarlet **Flames** that silently burn whatever they touch.
                - **Purification**: Using **Sun Flower** powder to create Area-of-Effect **Purification** effects, allowing them to **Purify** the undead.
                - **Lightning**: Using powder from a type of tree to create **Lightning** which can be used to stun enemies or clear areas.
                - **Plant Snare**: This uses specific leaves, petals and brambles that grow in size and join together to ensnare their targets.
                - **Sea Wave**: Using a light blue powder they can send out dark blue waves in all directions that can extinguish flames.
                - **Invisibility**: Using fluorescent powder, they can begin to fade, growing more transparent, vanishing in the blink of an eye.

### Strengthened Abilities

- **Knowledge** (**Witchcraft**): Having grasped the corresponding abilities in the mysticism domains, they can use their acquired mysticism knowledge to make potions and better [Mystical Items](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mystical_Item "Mystical Item").
    - Due to the influence of the [Mother Goddess of Depravity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Goddess_of_Depravity "Mother Goddess of Depravity"), **Warlocks** from the [Moses Ascetic Order](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Moses_Ascetic_Order "Moses Ascetic Order") used newborns not only as components in potions but also in witchcraft and better [Mystical Items](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mystical_Item "Mystical Item").
- **Eyes of Mystery Prying**: Their special eyes have been greatly enhanced.
    - As a **Warlock**, they can naturally **Pry** into the mysteries of various supernatural powers.
        - They can **Pry** into special blood, remnant auras, [Sealed Artifacts](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sealed_Artifact "Sealed Artifact") and **Beyonder** powers to gain the symbols and knowledge to make **Spells** from different domains like **Evil** or **Life**.
            - However, this relies on intellect and knowledge, and is quite dangerous.

## Authority → Power Mapping

This maps conceptual origin; it does not mean a Sequence 7 Warlock owns an Authority.

- [[Mysticism Knowledge (Hermit)]] → **Spellcasting**, **Knowledge** (**Witchcraft**) — the deliberate, study-driven construction of Spells is a direct channeling of mysticism knowledge into usable magic.
- [[Mystery Prying (Hermit)]] → **Eyes of Mystery Prying** — now able to actively pry into other Beyonders' powers and symbols, not merely perceive them passively.
