# Sequence 0 - Hermit

## Explicit Powers

### Symbolism

- **Knowledge** (**Information**): Derived from this symbolism, "They" hold the portion of authority that leans toward **Mysticism**.
    - This symbolism allows the [Gods](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Gods "Gods") of the two subordinate [Pathways](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways"), or even the **Sequence 1** or **Sequence 2**, to transform into a vast and complex torrent of **Information**, an illusory, overwhelming, and terrifying flood of **Information**.

### Authorities

- **[[Mysticism Knowledge (Hermit)|Mysticism Knowledge]]**: "They" are the embodiment of **Mysticism Knowledge**.
    - One of its core applications is to imbue **Digital Objects** with power, granting them genuine, distinct symbolic power in the true sense, though significantly weakened compared to the original.
        - **"0"** represents chaos, the unknown, what existed before the start. **"1"** represents the beginning, the [Oldest One](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Oldest_One "Oldest One") who made the world. **"2"** represents the world and various **Divinities** that were produced from the [Oldest One](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Oldest_One "Oldest One") 's body. **"3"** represents the contact between **Divinities** and material objects existing in the world to create all things.
            - By connecting **"1"** and **"0"**, end to end, "They" can form a digital world, which "They" can reduce to nothingness by changing **"1"** to **"0"**.
                - In terms of **Symbolism**, everything and anything is able to become **Numbers** with the corresponding occult implications and all of such **Numbers** possess **Spirituality**.
        - **Backup**: A subordinate authority of **Mysticism Knowledge**.
        - This allows for the **Revival** of **Information** after a **Backup**. For a specific **Backup** to receive the [Uniqueness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniquenesses "Uniquenesses"), it must be activated before its destruction. If not activated, [Uniqueness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniquenesses "Uniquenesses") will naturally transfer to the most recent **Backup**, following the proximity principle.
            - With repeated **Backups**, certain **Information** will undergo loss, degradation, **Errors**, and chaos in the process. The more times the overall **Information** is **Backed Up**, the higher the **Probability** of problems arising, and the greater the latent **Chaos**.
                - This is a fundamental rule of the entire world, a manifestation of **Error**, **Decay**, and increasing **Entropy**.
                    - Self-destruction leads to more significant **Information** decay and **Chaos** than being killed, because it starts from the inside, the denial of self-awareness.
                                        - More **Revivals** may cause some hidden **Loopholes** and **Errors** to appear, which is what those in the [Error Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway "Error Pathway") can capitalize on.
        - After the [First Blasphemy Slate](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/First_Blasphemy_Slate "First Blasphemy Slate") emerged, the **Hermit Uniqueness** would master the knowledge of all potion formulas even without having seen the Slate.
        - "They" have the corresponding subordinate partial authorities of **Copy,** **Deletion**, **Revision**, etc.
- **[[Hiding (Hermit)|Hiding]]**: "They" possess an authority of **Hiding**.
    - The **Hermit** can **Hide** the **Information** of others or "Themselves", making it impossible for other [True Deities](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Gods "Gods") to track or locate it.
        - Foreseeing danger, the [Hidden Sage](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hidden_Sage "Hidden Sage") **Concealed** "Itself" and the surrounding area.
- **[[Education (Demon of Knowledge)|Education]]**: This is the authority to grant **Knowledge** to others.
    - "They" are now able to automatically and directly infuse **Knowledge**, either positive or negative, into the minds of all **Beyonders** from the.
        - The [Hidden Sage](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hidden_Sage "Hidden Sage") routinely injects **Knowledge** into every **Beyonder** of the, causing those who fail to quickly digest and master it to experience 1 of 3 outcomes:
            1. Accept the modifications made by "Him".
                        2. Tenaciously resist "His" influence.
                        3. Lose control and/or die.
                - "Their" scream can be heard by every **Beyonder** from the when "They" die.
        - While the **Education** authority of the **[Paragon](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Paragon_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Paragon "Paragon Pathway/Abilities")** leans toward **Enlightenment**, the **Education** authority of the **Hermit** leans toward **Indoctrination**.
- **[[Mystery Prying (Hermit)|Mystery Prying]]**: No matter what the secret is, be it a kind of **Knowledge**, a part of " **Mysticism Knowledge** " or something else, it can be **Pried** into.
    - "They" are able to tell the specialties of "Their" enemies and the nature of their corresponding abilities and arrangements within the environment.
        - The [Hidden Sage](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hidden_Sage "Hidden Sage") was able to know the **Symbolisms** controlled by [The Fool](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") and the role the barrier **Sealing** the unknown realm played at a glance without further analysis.
        - Theoretically, "They" have a chance of piercing through the **Concealment** of the **[Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Darkness_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Darkness "Darkness Pathway/Abilities")**.
        - These two authorities mutually restrain each other. The outcome would depend on factors like each other's state, whether there are clues, etc.
- **[[Spirit World Mentor (Demon of Knowledge)|Spirit World Mentor]]**: The [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World") is a place where all kinds of **Information** converge, and **Information** is **Knowledge**.
    - It is naturally influenced by **Knowledge** [Pathways](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways"). Therefore, both the **[Hermit](#Sequence_0:_Hermit)** and the **[Paragon](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Paragon_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Paragon "Paragon Pathway/Abilities")** have partial authority of the [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World").
- **Divine Kingdom**: **The World in the Eyes** or **Digital Heaven**, **Land of Fairy Tales**.

## Authority → Power Mapping

The cited secondary inventory associates Sequence 0 **Hermit** with the listed Authorities. Their ownership and scope remain interpretations; the Pathway Hub separately identifies the Great Old One.

- [[Mysticism Knowledge (Hermit)]] → embodied directly as the "Mysticism Knowledge" authority listed above, including its subordinate **Backup**, **Copy**, **Deletion**, and **Revision** powers.
- [[Hiding (Hermit)]] → embodied directly as the "Hiding" authority listed above, concealing "Their" own or others' **Information** from every other True Deity.
- [[Education (Demon of Knowledge)]] → embodied directly as the "Education" authority listed above, leaning toward **Indoctrination** rather than the Paragon's **Enlightenment**.
- [[Mystery Prying (Hermit)]] → embodied directly as the "Mystery Prying" authority listed above, capable of piercing nearly any secret, symbolism, or arrangement.
- [[Spirit World Mentor (Demon of Knowledge)]] → embodied directly as the "Spirit World Mentor" authority listed above, shared in partial form with the Paragon.
