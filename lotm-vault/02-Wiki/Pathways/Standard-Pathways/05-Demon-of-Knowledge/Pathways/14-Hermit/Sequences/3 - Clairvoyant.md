# Sequence 3 - Clairvoyant

## Explicit Powers

### Authorities

- **Mystery Prying**: They have gained a partial authority over **Mystery Prying**.
    - Having truly come into contact with the authority of **Mystery Prying**, they can not only glimpse hidden secrets, knowledge, and existence, but also the secrets of **Destiny**.
        - **Clairvoyance** (**Prophecy**): **Prying** into the secrets of **Fate**, a **Clairvoyant** is able to foresee a choice's consequences through making a **Prophecy** of what would happen next, glimpse the best option for oneself, and even visualize past events.
            - They can gain inspiration for certain events and see vague images, but limited by one's own level, rank, and status, they will lack sufficient understanding of the specifics involving higher-ranking beings and cannot grasp the true nature of the matter.
                - The more serious the problem involved was, and the higher their status and level of those involved, the more damage **Prying** into those secrets would bring to them.
                        - **Clairvoyants** are able to foresee the coming of the [Apocalypse](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/The_Apocalypse "The Apocalypse"). After completing the corresponding [Ritual](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hermit_Pathway/Advancement "Hermit Pathway/Advancement"), they can clearly see the opportunity for [Advancement](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hermit_Pathway/Advancement "Hermit Pathway/Advancement").
                        - Examples of this in use are:
                - [Ma'am Hermit](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Cattleya "Cattleya") 's eyes, obscured by lenses, grew dark but remained unfocused, as if staring into a vast expanse. Countless brilliant specks of light appeared around her, as if she had shrunk the universe and brought it down to the ground in order to **Prophesy**.
                                - [Bernadette](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Bernadette_Gustav "Bernadette Gustav") tried to **Prophesy** the consequences of using the [Sage Frontlet](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sage_Frontlet "Sage Frontlet") abilities inside the [Black Emperor Mausoleum](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Primitive_Island_in_Fog_Sea "Primitive Island in Fog Sea"), and a corresponding scene of her disintegrating and transforming into several [Bernadettes](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Bernadette_Gustav "Bernadette Gustav") built on different tenets appeared before her eyes.

### Strengthened Abilities

- **Mystical Re-enactment**: This ability has undergone an enhancement.
    - The number of times they can use certain **Spells** and abilities derived from drawing power from mysticism knowledge and events has increased.
        - The number of uses of the **Ugly Duckling Spell** that [Cattleya](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Cattleya "Cattleya") was able to do had increased from *once* a *day* to *twice* per *day*.
        - In addition to that, a **Clairvoyant** is also able to draw power from mystical knowledge with a greater accuracy, thus increasing its power.
        - **Nine Suns**: It conjures up nine blazing **Suns** with intense heat that can cause pools of blood to quickly show signs of evaporation.
            - Seeing the pools of pus-like blood on the ground, eroding the soil in the extreme darkness, [Cattleya](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Cattleya "Cattleya") immediately conjured nine blazing **Suns**, with the intense heat causing the pools of blood to show signs of evaporation.
- **Stellar Guidance**: Their previous capabilities involving **Stars** has been enhanced.
- **Eyes of Mystery Prying**: They are capable of manifesting their **Eyes of Mystery Prying** on parts of their body, such as their scalp.

### Mythical Creature Form

- As **Sequence 3 Beyonders**, they can respond to prayers within a certain range.

## Authority → Power Mapping

This maps conceptual origin; it does not mean a Sequence 3 Clairvoyant owns an Authority.

- [[Mystery Prying (Hermit)]] → **Clairvoyance** (**Prophecy**), **Eyes of Mystery Prying** — the page's own text ties this directly to a partial claim on the Mystery Prying authority, prying into the secrets of Destiny.
- [[Mysticism Knowledge (Hermit)]] → **Mystical Re-enactment**, **Stellar Guidance** — continued channeling of grasped mystical knowledge into stronger, more frequent Spells.

## Potion

### Potion Properties

> A semi-solidified, translucent purple liquid streaked with fine, mercury-coloured 'veins'

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> A vertically shaped item with a mercury sheen.

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- Eyeballs of a Stargazer (outwardly, they are deformed fish with eyes protruding like pillars).
- Stardust Skin of a Treant High Priest.

Or
- A Clairvoyant Beyonder Characteristic.

Supplementary Ingredients

- 100 ml of the sap of a Treant High Priest.
- 10ml of brain fluid from a Stargazer.
- 1 book of prophecies with a higher accuracy rate than error rate.
- 9 hairs of people with abnormal fates (each one from a different source).

### Advancement Ritual

It involves gathering a vast amount of information, analyzing, deducing, and divining to 'Prophesy' a certain event that will occur three years later.(It must involve demigods.)

- Ultimately the performer has to witness its fulfillment. Throughout the process, they cannot rely on external forces or directly drive the event to happen; the ritual performer can only guide it indirectly.
