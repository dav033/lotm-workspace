# Sequence 9 - Mystery Pryer

## Explicit Powers

### Traits

- **Spirituality**: Their **Spirituality** will get enhanced upon drinking this potion.
    - **Magic & Divination Arts**: They have a comprehensive but rudimentary understanding and grasp of magic, Enchantment, witchcraft [Divination Arts](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Divination_Art "Divination Art"), [Ritualistic Magic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ritualistic_Magic "Ritualistic Magic"), and other mystical knowledge.
        - A **Mystery Pryer's** use of **Ritualistic Magic** allows them to summon [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World") creatures and form **Contracts** with them which enables them to eventually obtain messengers and helpers with unique powers as they progress forward in their studies.
            - As a **Mystery Pryer** progresses through the [Sequences](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sequence "Sequence"), they will be able to form **Contracts** with even more and more powerful and unique **Spirit World** creatures.
                - They can use many **Divination** methods, including astrology, cartomancy, spirit pendulums, spiritual numbers, and scrying in order to gain revelations from the [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World"), however, they are less effective in this matter than [Seers](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fool_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_9:_Seer "Fool Pathway/Abilities").
        - **Spirit Vision**: ([reference](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_Vision)) **Mystery Pryers** are able to utilize their **Spirit Vision** to deduce a person's health and emotions, as well as if an object or person contains some sort of magical aura.

### New Abilities

- **Eyes of Mystery Prying**: A **Mystery Pryer's** eyes are special and allow them to be able to see things that normally aren't visible.
    - They can **Pry** into things that shouldn't or can't be seen. This lets them peer through attempts to trap them in **Dreams** or **Illusions**, see truth and reality, discern the light of **Spirituality**, the state of a target's [Astral Body](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul#Astral_Projection "Soul"), [Ether Body](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul#Ether_Body "Soul"), and [Body of Heart and Mind](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul#Body_of_Heart_and_Mind "Soul"), as well as examine the various details of a target's [Astral Body](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul#Astral_Projection "Soul").
        - They can identify if their target is a **Beyonder** or not through the target's [Astral Projection](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul#Astral_Projection "Soul").
                - Utilizing the [Eye of Truth](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Eye_of_Truth "Eye of Truth"), [Lumian](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee") was able to view the concealed [Mirror Gardner Martin](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Gardner_Martin "Gardner Martin").
                - [Cattleya](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Cattleya "Cattleya") could view the light of **Spirituality** of the different items [Klein](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") was carrying.
        - **Mystery Pryers** have to be careful and show proper restraint with their powers as a **Beyonder** as they often see things they shouldn't see, leading to them being most exposed to **Corruption**.
        - This ability is instinctive and cannot be deactivated. **Mystery Pryers** can prevent it from affecting their daily lives through **Seals** or using special [Mystical Items](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mystical_Item "Mystical Item") of sufficient power.
- **Knowledge** (**Knowledge Pursuit**): **Mystery Pryers** will occasionally be chased by knowledge brought to life, imbuing them with knowledge in random intervals.
    - This can either lead to them losing control or gaining a benefit, though often it is the former due to its lack of predictability and interference from the [Hidden Sage](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hidden_Sage "Hidden Sage"), or other hidden entity such as the [Inextinguishable Ravings](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Inextinguishable_Ravings "Inextinguishable Ravings").
        - **Knowledge** **Pursuit** (and by extension, **Angel** -level **Education** abilities) cannot be treated with **Placate**. This is because **Knowledge Pursuit** is not mental confusion or an emotional outburst. The targets minds simply cannot withstand being flooded with so much knowledge in a short time, leaving them not even able to utilize self-protection mechanisms and pass out.
- **Quick Rituals**: They can quickly complete [Ritualistic Magic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ritualistic_Magic "Ritualistic Magic") without the need for most ritual items.
    - They can cast an invisible barrier with various sprays, powders, and an incantation.
        - They can cast a **Spell** that aids a **Mystery Pryer** in drawing someone's portrait.

## Authority → Power Mapping

This maps conceptual origin; it does not mean a Sequence 9 Mystery Pryer owns an Authority.

- [[Mystery Prying (Hermit)]] → **Eyes of Mystery Prying** — the instinctive, ever-active organ through which the pathway's core prying authority first manifests.
- [[Mysticism Knowledge (Hermit)]] → **Magic & Divination Arts**, **Quick Rituals** — a rudimentary, diluted grasp of the mystical knowledge that the Hermit authority fully embodies at Sequence 0.
- [[Education (Demon of Knowledge)]] → **Knowledge** (**Knowledge Pursuit**) — involuntary knowledge-infusion from the Hidden Sage, an early and uncontrolled brush with the Education authority's indoctrination bent.
- [[Spirit World Mentor (Demon of Knowledge)]] → **Spirit Vision** — perception of spiritual auras and traces, plausibly downstream of the pathway's partial claim on the Spirit World.
- **Spirituality** remains a generic potion trait without a supported Authority assignment.
