# Sequence 1 - Snake of Mercury (Giant Serpent, Snake of Fate, Tail-Devouring Serpent of Fate)

## Explicit Powers

### Authorities

- **Cycle**: "They" gain partial authority over **Cycle** and the corresponding **Save** and **Select** authorities.
    - **Cycle of Fate**: "They" can make people fall into a strange state, where **Time** maintains a certain degree of flow, but is fixed within a certain range, circulating over and over again.
        - Those affected by it wouldn't even realize they are trapped.
            - It can repeatedly trap other supernatural individuals of similar power level until the target realizes it, but each time consumes "Their" own **Luck**.
                - By breaking the medium, one can break free from it.
            - The medium for the **Cycle of Fate** that had placed the [City of Silver](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/City_of_Silver "City of Silver") members in a past [Rose Redemption](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Rose_Redemption "Rose Redemption") meeting was the hall itself, and destroying it would end the loop.
                        - When [Colin Iliad](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Colin_Iliad "Colin Iliad") destroyed the [Ouroboros](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ouroboros "Ouroboros") -like face that was inlaid on [Jack's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Jack "Jack") stomach, the **Cycle of Fate** that made the team repeat the exploration 5 times ended.
                - This balance will continue, that is, relative stillness.
            - Upon seeing a mural with the face of the [Angel of Fate Ouroboros](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ouroboros "Ouroboros"), [Derrick Berg](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Derrick_Berg "Derrick Berg"), [Lovia Tiffany](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lovia_Tiffany "Lovia Tiffany"), and [Colin Iliad](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Colin_Iliad "Colin Iliad") were trapped in a **Cycle of Fate**, with each one of those affected merging and being one of the members of the [Rose Redemption](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Rose_Redemption "Rose Redemption").
                - Each of the members of the [City of Silver](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/City_of_Silver "City of Silver") (who have now become these past [Rose Redemption](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Rose_Redemption "Rose Redemption") members) repeated a series of actions like puppets—approaching the table, sitting down, listening, and leaving.
                                - This **Cycle of Fate** made the figures that had once been in this secret meeting cross the borders of time, “revive” at their fixed seats, allowing their words that were from the past to reappear.
                        - [Derrick Berg](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Derrick_Berg "Derrick Berg") was assigned a mission to explore the [True Creator's Temple](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/True_Creator%27s_Temple "True Creator's Temple"), which resulted in him being trapped in a **Cycle of Fate** with his memories wiped each cycle, having repeated the exploration five times with slight differences each time.
                - In the first iteration of the exploration, all of the animal hide lanterns had been extinguished, nearly leading to a tragedy.
                                - In the second iteration of the exploration, someone lost control of themselves and almost swallowed a "mushroom".
                                - In the third iteration, [Jack](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Jack "Jack") told a story about him and his father trying to find the [True Creator's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/True_Creator "True Creator") holy residence.
                                - In the fourth iteration, Joshua was severely injured by [Jack](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Jack "Jack") who had mutated without any warning.
        - **Reboot (Reboot of Fate/Reboot of all things**): "They" can **Reboot** the state or events within a certain area, invalidating all events that took place before a **Reboot**.
        - However, the **Luck** they consumed or gained during the period **Rebooted** won't be affected.
                - The way it operates is different from a **[Worm of Time's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_1:_Worm_of_Time)** **Time** authority.
            - **Reboot** does not reverse the flow of **Time** like a **[Worm of Time](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_1:_Worm_of_Time)** and instead it overwrites the current manifested state of an environment with an older **Saved** state.
                - The following are just a few examples of **Reboot** in action.
            - [Will Auceptin](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Will_Auceptin "Will Auceptin") had utilized **Reboot** to **Reboot** [Amon's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Amon "Amon") **Avatar** state to when "He" had not yet **Parasitized** various people in Boklund Street.
        - When faced with an inescapable danger, "They" can **Reboot** the **Cycle** and revert to an embryo, but this doesn't reset "Their" accumulated and consumed **Luck**.
        - This will reset "Their" madness levels, allowing "Them" to reduce the need for an [Anchor](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Anchor "Anchor"). However, this reduction does not mean that "They" don't need one.
            - A certain level of anchor is still required. Otherwise, "They" can't sustain this state for long.
                - With each **Reboot**, "They" will strive to be slightly different in order to maintain a healthy mental state. A child's stage should be a child's, etc.
                - This kind of **Rebirth** is a form of **Death**
            - This is why [Ouroboros](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ouroboros "Ouroboros") 's [Honorific Name](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Honorific_Name "Honorific Name") included " *The Lingering Hero Spirit of The River"*
        - "They" are able to **Conceal** the peculiarity of "Their" own **Fate**.

### Mythical Creature Form

- "Their" full **Mythical Creature Form** is a **Snake of Mercury**; a giant scaleless snake with dense patterns and symbols on its surface, the symbols being various wheels that are connected to each other. Each wheel is different, and they represent different futures that might happen.

## Authority → Power Mapping

This maps conceptual origin; it does not mean a Sequence 1 Snake of Mercury owns an Authority.

- [[Cycle (Wheel of Fortune)]] → **Cycle**, **Cycle of Fate**, **Reboot (Reboot of Fate/Reboot of all things)** — directly stated as partial **Cycle** authority in the source text, encompassing the subordinate **Save** and **Select** authorities that underlie the looping and overwrite mechanics.

## Potion

### Potion Properties

> A head-to-tail mercury-colored small serpent (more like a flow of water) encased in a semi-transparent shell.

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> A mercury ring with countless cyclic symbols.

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- The Crystal Core of a Mercury Phoenix.

Or
- The Crystal Core of a Mercury Giant Serpent.
- Or
- A Snake of Mercury Beyonder Characteristic.

Supplementary Ingredients

- One's own umbilical cord from birth or eggshell remnants.
- 9 drops of "water" closely related to oneself extracted via a special method from the River of Fate (each drop is a segment of fate)
- One item contaminated by the Gray Fog of the Sefirah Castle (can be replaced by the blessing or bestowed item from Sequence 0 Fool).

### Advancement Ritual

- Find a way to extract all of your memories, then, after consuming the potion, infuse them back into yourself.
- Alternatively, with the aid of a Visionary during the ritual, regress to the state of a fetus, stripped of all memory, and awaken the full breadth of cognition buried deep within your soul and mind.
    - Note 1: Specific term from the Western Continent: Unveiling the Enigma of the Womb.
    - Note 2: Here memory mean ALL memory, conscious and unconscious
