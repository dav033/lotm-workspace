# Sequence 4 - Misfortune Mage

## Explicit Powers

### Traits

- **Physical Enhancement**: Their body has undergone a qualitative change and became a **Mercury Body** with passive **Anti-Divination** and **Anti-Prophecy** properties.
    - Their overall physical abilities have also been significantly improved.

### New Abilities

- **Misfortune Field**: Upon activation, all beings who enter within a *100-300 meter* range (depending on potion digestion and **Misfortune Mage's** state) of them will be **Cursed** with **Bad Luck**.
    - The branch that [Mr. Harry](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mr._Harry "Mr. Harry") was stepping on after landing on a tree immediately snapped after merely being within the **Misfortune Field** for a moment.
- **Group Blessing**: This is the same as **Blessing**, but can only be achieved by the **Misfortune Mage** consuming a significant amount of their own **Luck** reserves.
    - It can be given to any creature within a 50-meter radius of the **Misfortune Mage**.
        - The **Misfortune Mage** can choose who to give and who not to give **Luck** to.
- **Blessing**: By using their own **Luck** or pulling the target's future **Luck** into the present, they can **Bless** a chosen target, making them **Lucky.**
    - They can use **Blessing** to remove a prolonged period of **Misfortune**.
        - This is because **Luck** and **Misfortune** counteract each other.
        - Examples of the **Blessing** ability being used are:
        - [Ricciardo](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ricciardo "Ricciardo") **Blessed** [Darkwill](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Darkwill "Darkwill") with precisely enough **Luck** to win around 700 pounds.
                - [Ricciardo](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ricciardo "Ricciardo") removed the **Misfortune** from [Anderson](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Anderson_Hood "Anderson Hood") by utilizing a **Blessing**.
- **Absolute Perception**: They possess an **Absolute** **Perception** over the changes in **Fate**, the existence of **Beacons**, as well as those of higher mystical status.
    - [Ricciardo](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ricciardo "Ricciardo") knew the day he would meet [Gehrman Sparrow](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") he must not see anything him and thus wore an eye mask on that day.

### Strengthened Abilities

- **Curse of Misfortune**: A **Misfortune Mage** can **Curse** the target with **Bad Luck**, making them extremely **Unlucky.** This kind of **Misfortune** is one that can threaten one's life.
    - Due to the **Dream** having ended before [Anderson](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Anderson_Hood "Anderson Hood") could ask for [Klein Moretti's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") help, causing [Klein](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") to think he was already mutated which made him attack [Anderson](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Anderson_Hood "Anderson Hood") with several **Wind Blades**.
        - The axe that [Anderson Hood](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Anderson_Hood "Anderson Hood") used in the **Dream** broke in two, and as the crack was towards the back, the fragment shot at him, making his right chest and abdomen bleed like a fountain.
        - Due to the streak of bad **Luck**, the treasure hunting team [Anderson](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Anderson_Hood "Anderson Hood") was a part of failed to reach the island on time before the storm arrived, which sank their ship and treasures.
        - The rest of the treasure hunting team either drowned, got struck by **Lightning**, or got eaten by the underwater monsters, while [Anderson](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Anderson_Hood "Anderson Hood"), being relatively stronger and **Luckier** than them, was sent flying by the waves and successfully swam to the shore of an island.
        - Soon after [Anderson](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Anderson_Hood "Anderson Hood") stated that he may attract monsters due to his bad **Misfortune**, the island they were on quaked as a **Stone Giant** arose from the primitive forest.
        - The [Future](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Stars_Pirates "Stars Pirates") (with [Anderson](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Anderson_Hood "Anderson Hood") in it) happened to cross an area that has the remnant auras of the [Earth Mother](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lilith "Lilith"), rapidly **Mutating** [Frank Lee's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Frank_Lee "Frank Lee") **Crossbreeds**.
        - It caused the mushrooms that [Frank](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Frank_Lee "Frank Lee") was working on to enter the [Future](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Stars_Pirates "Stars Pirates") and merge with it, making it spurt out milk all across the ship.
                - It also made a pirate **Beyonder** grow a watermelon on his head, which resulted in him dying as he tried to open it with brute force.
                - A grayish black hand also appeared from the sea hitting the [Future's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Stars_Pirates "Stars Pirates") side, grabbing it, and attempting to hoist the ship up.
- **Psyche Storm**: Compared to a **Sequence 6 Calamity Priest's** **Psyche Storm**, a **Misfortune Mage's** **Psyche Storm** has undergone a qualitative change.
    - Their **Psyche Storm** not only affects the opponents' **Spirit** **Bodies** through their extremely high **Spirituality**, causing dizziness and disorientation, it also causes confusion within the mind and thoughts of the affected targets, bringing a bit of madness.

### Mythical Creature Form

- As **Demigods** at [Sequence 4](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sequence#High-Sequence_\(Demigod\) "Sequence") who have obtained partial **Godhood**, they are able to manifest their incomplete [Mythical Creature Form](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mythical_Creature_Form "Mythical Creature Form") if they disregard any of the consequences.

## Authority → Power Mapping

This maps conceptual origin; it does not mean a Sequence 4 Misfortune Mage owns an Authority.

- [[Fate (Wheel of Fortune)]] → **Misfortune Field**, **Group Blessing**, **Blessing**, **Curse of Misfortune** — probable expressions of the **Good Luck**/**Bad Luck** sub-authorities under **Fate**.
- [[Fate (Wheel of Fortune)]] → **Absolute Perception** — probable expression of the **Foresight** sub-authority, given its listed tie to sensing changes in **Fate** and **Beacons**.
- **Physical Enhancement** and **Psyche Storm** remain traits/abilities without a supported Authority assignment from this pathway's set.
