# Sequence 2 - Soothsayer

## Explicit Powers

### Traits

- **High Spirituality**: "Their" **Spirituality** can utilize the [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World") to influence **Dreams** and **Mirrors**.
    - "They" can locate one's [Astral Projection](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul#Astral_Projection "Soul") in the [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World") and inject information in one's **Dreams**, creating a **Revelation** that evolved into a specific **Dream**.

### New Abilities

- "They" are able to access and utilize the **Connection** that things have with others' **Fate**.
    - "They" can access the interlayers of **Destiny** formed by things and arrange certain things.
        - Since [Penglai](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Penglai "Penglai") happened to be near the boundary of the seal, some fishermen went out to sea to fish or adventure, and these people happened to remain outside the **Seal** of the [Western Continent](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Western_Continent "Western Continent"). However, they can still be considered connected by **Fate** with the [Western Continent](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Western_Continent "Western Continent") itself, which could create some interlayers of **Destiny**. This allowed [Cohinem](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Cohinem "Cohinem") to enter these interlayers and hide some arrangements for **Resurrection** there.
        - "They" can use the **Connection** "They" have with an object to store and channel "Their" abilities through it, even if "They" are not in the vicinity.
        - [Klein Moretti](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") summoned [Will Auceptin](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Will_Auceptin "Will Auceptin") 's ice cream from the past to establish a subtle **Connection** with "Him" in order to be blessed with good **Luck**.
                - [Ouroboros](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ouroboros "Ouroboros") imbued "His" murals with many of "His" powers, be it giving people extreme **Misfortune** or trapping them in a **Cycle of Fate**.
                - [Will Auceptin](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Will_Auceptin "Will Auceptin") utilized **Reboots** through the medium of "His" folded paper cranes to help [Klein Moretti](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") in battle.
                - [Cohinem](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Cohinem "Cohinem") used her golden wine cup to cause her power to affect [Alger](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Alger_Wilson "Alger Wilson").

### Authorities

- **Fate**: "They" possess partial control over the **Fate** authority, which encompasses the subordinate authorities of **Good Luck**, **Bad Luck**, and **Foresight**.
    - **Prophecy**: "They" are able to **Prophesy** how different results will occur between certain people and events due to good or bad **Luck**.
        - "Their" spoken **Prophecies** will have a high probability of becoming a reality and even if the final outcome does not match that of a **Soothsayer's** **Prophecy**, it will be similar.
            - The **Prophecy** " [Lumian Lee](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee") will ultimately emerge victorious in this battle, thanks to his exceptional good fortune." had become a reality during his fight against the sentient **[Weather Warlock](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Priest_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_2:_Weather_Warlock) Beyonder Characteristic**, causing it to be incapacitated.
                - This was not any change that was caused by [Lumian Lee](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee") attacks. It was instead caused by the attacks in the [Bansy Harbor](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Bansy_Harbor "Bansy Harbor") telegraph ruins, causing a leakage point within the [City of Calamity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/City_of_Calamity "City of Calamity") that could trigger anomalies and cause the sentient **Beyonder Characteristic** to be subjected to unseen influences.
                        - During the [Battle with Demoness Sect](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_of_Primordial_Demoness "Death of Primordial Demoness"), [Franca](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Franca_Roland "Franca Roland") used [Post-Apocalyptic Scripture](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Post-Apocalyptic_Scripture "Post-Apocalyptic Scripture") to make **Prophecy:** “All **Demigods** attacking me will suffer the backlash of their own [Sealed Artifacts](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sealed_Artifact "Sealed Artifact") due to **Bad Luck**.” This directly led to [Demoness of Silver](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Demoness_of_Silver "Demoness of Silver") ’s death through her [Sealed Artifact](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sealed_Artifact "Sealed Artifact") ’s side effect, [Demoness of Green](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Demoness_of_Green "Demoness of Green") ’s [Sealed Artifact](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sealed_Artifact "Sealed Artifact") leaving her defenseless against [Franca](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Franca_Roland "Franca Roland") 's **Curse** and [Demoness of Brown](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Demoness_of_Brown "Demoness of Brown") getting lost while **Wandering** and became corrupted by [Mother Goddess of Depravity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Goddess_of_Depravity "Mother Goddess of Depravity").
        - **Revelation of Fate**: "They" are able to gaze upon the long **River of Fate** in order to receive desired **Revelations** and **Prophecies**.
        - In **Chaotic** situations, with many choices and only a few paths leading to a good ending, **Prophecies** can be used to gain **Revelation**, which requires interpretation.
            - If it is correctly interpreted it will guide the inquirer to the best possible outcome.
                        - If the **Revelation** is not correctly interpreted, the inquirer will suffer immensely.
                - **Prophecies** of the **Future** can be true or false. If someone believes a false **Prophecy** and prepares to avoid or embrace it, it will become true.
            - When [Lumian](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee") opened [Post-Apocalyptic Scripture](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Post-Apocalyptic_Scripture "Post-Apocalyptic Scripture") for the first time it showed **Prophecy**: “In the Year 1368, as July fades to its final days, crimson shall rain from the heavens.” which then changed to: “In the Year 1360, as July fades to its final days, crimson shall rain from the heavens.”. This **Prophecy** was true predicting coming of [Apcocalypse](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/The_Apocalypse "The Apocalypse") earlier than **Prophecy** of [God of Kowledge and Wisdom](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/God_of_Knowledge_and_Wisdom "God of Knowledge and Wisdom") foretold before.
        - **Words of Fortune & Misfortune**: "They" are able to bring **Good** or **Bad Luck** upon any living beings within a *30 kilometer radius*.
        - When "They" give **Good Luck** onto targets, "They" are able to choose whether to consume "Their" own **Luck** or the someone else's **Good Luck**.
                - The **Misfortune** "They" can bring can make any [Beyonder](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder "Beyonder") under the **Demigod** level die miserably within an extremely short period of time.

### Mythical Creature Form

- "Their" **Mythical Creature** form is now complete, it's a **Snake of Mercury**.
    - **Body of Mercury**: "Their" body and the [River of Fate](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/River_of_Fate "River of Fate") have developed a marvelous connection and an indescribable fusion, becoming the darling of **Fate**; once subjected to attack, "Their" body possesses a passive ability to not be hit and not be influenced within ten seconds, followed by continuous **Luck**.

## Authority → Power Mapping

This maps conceptual origin; it does not mean a Sequence 2 Soothsayer owns an Authority.

- [[Fate (Wheel of Fortune)]] → **Fate**, **Prophecy**, **Revelation of Fate**, **Words of Fortune & Misfortune** — directly named as partial **Fate** authority, drawing on its **Good Luck**, **Bad Luck**, and **Foresight** sub-authorities.
- [[Fate (Wheel of Fortune)]] → **Connection** — probable interpretive extension of the Fate authority, since the ability directly channels one's link to others' **Fate**.
- **High Spirituality** and **Body of Mercury** remain traits describing the Soothsayer's fusion with the River of Fate without a supported standalone Authority assignment.

## Potion

### Potion Properties

> A pure, viscous mass of mercury covered in countless tiny symbols and patterns.

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> Either a scale-covered horn, a wood-grained heart, or a cracked turtle shell, all in a mercury silver color.

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- Horn of a Fate Grey Tiger.

Or
- The Heart of a Scale-covered Treant Elder.

Or
- A Soothsayer Beyonder Characteristic.

Supplementary Ingredients

- 100 milliliters of Fate Grey Tiger blood or 100 milliliters of Scale-covered Treant Elder sap or 100 milliliters of a corresponding Angel's blood (choose one).
- One important prophecy that has been fulfilled (must have been recorded on stone or paper before it came true).
- The blessing of one of the Seven Lights of the Spirit World.
- Luck accumulated over more than a hundred years.

### Advancement Ritual

Peer into the River of Fate, uncover an important revelation concerning at least one nation, and ensure that this revelation ultimately manifests exactly as you "saw" it.
