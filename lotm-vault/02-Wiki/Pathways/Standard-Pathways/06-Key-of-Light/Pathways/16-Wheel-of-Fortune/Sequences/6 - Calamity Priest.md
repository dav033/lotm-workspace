# Sequence 6 - Calamity Priest

## Explicit Powers

### Traits

- **High Spirituality**: Their **Spirituality** surpasses that of other [Pathways](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways").

### New Abilities

- **Calamity Attraction **: Their body will now attract **Calamities**, mainly in two ways.
    - **Passive Attraction**: They will passively suffer all kinds of **Calamities**, but can see them in advance via their **Foresight** and make the preparations needed to eliminate or mitigate their effects.
        - Because of their **Foresight**, **Calamity Priests** can use the dangers of their own passive encounters and preparation to attack their enemies.
        - **Active Attraction**: They can attract **Calamities** to varying degrees, affecting targets and enemies as a result. Then through their **Luck**, they can avoid most of the danger from it.
        - In essence, it allows them to pull their enemies into a situation where they can take advantage of their strengths the best.
- **Psyche Storm**: Using their **Spirituality** that surpassed that of other [Pathways](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways"), they can directly affect their opponent's [Spirit Body](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul#Soul_Body "Soul"), causing them to feel dizzy and lost.
    - If a **Calamity Priest's** enemy entered such a state, they would be extremely prone to making errors, thus causing the **Calamity** to snowball and eventually devour them.

## Authority → Power Mapping

This maps conceptual origin; it does not mean a Sequence 6 Calamity Priest owns an Authority.

- [[Fate (Wheel of Fortune)]] → **Calamity Attraction** — probable expression of the **Bad Luck** sub-authority for magnetizing misfortune, supported by the **Foresight** sub-authority for the passive variant's advance warning.
- **High Spirituality** and **Psyche Storm** remain traits/abilities without a supported Authority assignment from this pathway's set.
