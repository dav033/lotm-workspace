# Sequence 0 - Wheel of Fortune

## Explicit Powers

### Description

To an extent, "They" are equivalent to the embodiment of **Fate**, capable of roaming freely in the [River of Fate](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/River_of_Fate "River of Fate").

### Authorities

- [[Fate (Wheel of Fortune)|Fate]]: This is a core authority, the **Fate** authority derived from the **Fate** symbolism. It is not a complete authority, as other [Pathways](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways") also have partial **Fate** authority. "They" are the embodiment of **Fate**, both **Good Luck** and **Bad Luck**, with a particular aptitude for **Foresight**.
    - **Foresight**: "They" gain partial authority of **Foresight,** able to grasp the [River of Fate](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/River_of_Fate "River of Fate") to obtain visions of the future as well as **Destined** **Beacons**.
        - "They" can profoundly influence short-term tributaries of the [River of Fate](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/River_of_Fate "River of Fate") in a short-period of time, ensuring that "Their" **Prophecies** will be precisely fulfilled.
            - This is akin to a grand **Prophecy** technique.
                - The **Wheel of Fortune** has the strongest **Prophetic** ability.
        - As the embodiment of " **Fate** ", **Fooling** "Them" is also equivalent to **Fooling Fate**.
        - "They" can perform the [Tunnel Effect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_tunnelling), but it would consume "Their" own **Luck**.
        - "They" can make the atoms in "Their" body perfectly align to let a blade pass through "Them", which has a **Probability** of around 10^{-10^{21}} of happening.
        - As the embodiment of " **Fate** ", "They" can roam freely in the [River of Fate](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/River_of_Fate "River of Fate").
- [[Probability (Wheel of Fortune)|Probability]]: Another core authority derived from the symbol of **Fate**, it lets "Them" manipulate the **Probability** of events and the behavior of peoples and objects from all around the world.
    - Everything can be **Probabilistic**. This is one of the manifestations of the **Disordered** nature of **Fate**. If one masters **Probability**, they master the **Fate** of all things.
        - The **Wheel of Fortune** can amplify the **Probability** of adversely affecting a target to the limit, causing them to fail in everything they do while also having rare unlucky events become extremely likely and common for the target. Likewise, "They" can do the opposite.
            - Due to the [Die of Probability's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Die_of_Probability "Die of Probability") pip being 1, [Darkwill](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Darkwill "Darkwill") had almost died in his **Dream** as his head head swelled with a sharp pain and he struggled to breathe.
                - Soon after, if [Klein](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") hadn't properly controlled his strength as the door slammed into [Darkwill's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Darkwill "Darkwill") head, his skull would have probably cracked open.
                                - It had also made it so that even though [Darkwill](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Darkwill "Darkwill") explained his situation and the [Die of Probability](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Die_of_Probability "Die of Probability") with lot's of logic, [Klein](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") would still not believe him.
                    - This was due to the fact that [Darkwill](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Darkwill "Darkwill") 's earlier explanation happened to match certain theories of his, making him instinctively believe that weak lie over the recent explanation as being the true reason behind his predicament.
                        - With the [Die of Probability's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Die_of_Probability "Die of Probability") pip at 2, the sky suddenly turned dark as the wind howled with increasing strength and the air's humidity clearly climbed.
                - As [Darkwill](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Darkwill "Darkwill") looked out the window to check on the exact situation within the ship a silver bolt of **Lightning** struck him before [Klein](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") could even react.
- [[Cycle (Wheel of Fortune)|Cycle]]: Derived from the **Return** symbolism, it means **Returning** to the mother body, **Returning** to the primordial source, **Returning** to the oldest origin. This is the instinct of the universe.
    - This authority includes the domain of **Reincarnation**, which is shared with the **[Mother](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Mother)**.
        - Under the **Cycle** authority are the lesser authorities of **Save** and **Select**.
        - The **Save** authority can reproduce a past segment of **Fate** no more than three minutes away from the current segment that can replace the current segment.
                - The **Select** authority can choose one path among the many **Fate Tributaries** in the **River of Fate**. For example, choosing the one leading to **Reboot**.
- [[Disorder (Wheel of Fortune)|Disorder]]: The **Disorder** authority is one of the manifestations of the **Disorder** symbolism. The essence of **Fate** is **Disorder**, which embodies madness. The **Wheel of Fortune** both keeps watch over them and uses them.
    - This **Chaos** is the **Disorder** of [Fate](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/River_of_Fate "River of Fate"), rooted in the [Original Creator's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Original_Creator "Original Creator") madness.
- **Light Cone World**: "Their" **Divine Kingdom**, a completely **Probabilistic** realm where every move corresponds to a dice with different numbers.
    - The **Wheel of Fortune** can manipulate these illusory dice.

## Authority → Power Mapping

The cited secondary inventory associates Sequence 0 **Wheel of Fortune** with the listed Authorities. Their ownership and scope remain interpretations; the Pathway Hub separately identifies the Great Old One.

- [[Fate (Wheel of Fortune)]] → embodied directly as the "Fate" authority listed above, including its **Foresight** sub-authority.
- [[Probability (Wheel of Fortune)]] → embodied directly as the "Probability" authority listed above.
- [[Cycle (Wheel of Fortune)]] → embodied directly as the "Cycle" authority listed above, including its **Save** and **Select** sub-authorities.
- [[Disorder (Wheel of Fortune)]] → embodied directly as the "Disorder" authority listed above.
- **Light Cone World** is the Divine Kingdom trait tied to this Sequence and remains without a standalone Authority page in this pathway's set.

## Potion

### Potion Properties

> A slowly rotating, semi-illusory, semi-substantial wheel filled with mysterious meaning and symbol.

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- The Wheel of Fortune Uniqueness.
- Three Snake of Mercury Beyonder Characteristics.

Supplementary Ingredients

- Nine beacons from the River of Fate (from the past, present, or future).

### Advancement Ritual

- One must find the right opportunity in the flow of destiny, then take the potion.
- The right opportunity cannot be divined and cannot be predicted. It can't be locked in any way. One can only rule out certain obstacles and must patiently look for it.
