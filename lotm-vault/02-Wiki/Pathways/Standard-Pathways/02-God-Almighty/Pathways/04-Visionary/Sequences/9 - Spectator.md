# Sequence 9 - Spectator

## Explicit Powers

- **Enhanced Mental Attributes** — improves inferential, analytical, observational, and identification ability along with memory of the Body of Heart and Mind; if drunk by an animal, it gains human-level intelligence and speech within days.
- **Enhanced Vision** — sharpens eyesight enough to analyze a target's body language even from a distance.
- **Body Language Analysis** — reads a target's true thoughts from their expressions, manners, and subconscious actions to build an accurate mental model of them.

## Authority → Power Mapping

This maps conceptual origin; it does not mean a Sequence 9 Spectator owns an Authority.

- [[Discernment (Visionary)]] → **Body Language Analysis** — low-Sequence Power on the conceptual line that later becomes Authority.
- **Enhanced Mental Attributes** and **Enhanced Vision** remain traits without independent Authority status.

## Potion

- Properties: sticky silver liquid filled with countless bubbles, creating a serene atmosphere.
- Beyonder Characteristic appearance: a silver-white vertical eye.
- Main ingredient: a Spectator Beyonder Characteristic.
- Supplementary ingredients: Purified Water (80 ml), Autumn Crocus Essence (5 drops), Cow Teeth Paeonol Powder (13 g), elf flower petals (7).
- Acting method: a Spectator is always just a spectator — they watch openly and honestly from their position rather than spying or taking the stage themselves.
