# Sequence 6 - Hypnotist

## Traits

- **Physical Enhancement** — strength and agility exceed an ordinary person's.
- **Dragon Scales** — a layer of scales can form over the skin, largely resisting and reducing physical damage.

## Explicit Powers

- **Psychological Invisibility** — by controlling the target's Body of Heart and Mind, the Hypnotist remains in the blind spot of their consciousness; unaffected by Spirit Vision and even a Hunter's Danger Intuition, and can persist even standing before a target who has already seen them. Maintaining it while actively interacting with the surroundings takes full focus and breaks once that interaction becomes too strong to ignore.

## Strengthened Abilities

- **Hypnosis** — a qualitative evolution of Psychological Cue, split into Non-Combat Hypnosis (getting a target to concentrate on something in order to directly alter their consciousness/subconscious, resistible if it threatens their life or deeply held values, or if their Soul and mind are especially firm) and Battle Hypnotism (forcefully hypnotizing an enemy into abnormal actions such as attacking allies; cannot end a target's life or force suicide, and only lasts briefly before the target snaps awake).

## Authority → Power Mapping

- [[Discernment (Visionary)]] → **Psychological Invisibility**, **Hypnosis**.
- **Physical Enhancement** and **Dragon Scales** remain traits without independent Authority status.

## Potion

- Properties: translucent, colorless, sticky liquid that resembles a still mirror with tiny whirlpools when disturbed; may cause a brief chill on consumption.
- Beyonder Characteristic appearance: a sticky grayish-white colloid resembling an unfixed mirror, containing minuscule light-refracting bubbles.
- Main ingredients: the complete pituitary gland of an adolescent Mind Dragon, or 60 ml of a Black-hunting Giant Lizard's spinal fluid plus the fruit of an Illusory Chime Tree, or a Hypnotist Beyonder Characteristic.
- Supplementary ingredients: blood from an adolescent Mind Dragon or black-hunting giant lizard (80 ml), scales/bark from the same source (3), a belladonna fruit (1), a mirror kept in one's own room for over three months (1).
- Consumption backlash: after a brief chill, the Soul Body, Astral Projection, and Ether Body are absorbed into the Body of Heart and Mind, briefly exposing the drinker to the boundless Sea of Collective Subconscious; retaining a sliver of lucidity and resisting the urge to fuse into it, the drinker's consciousness contracts back before scales and hair mutations recede, leaving clear eyes with a strange bottomless whirlpool underneath as a sign of dispersing spirituality.
