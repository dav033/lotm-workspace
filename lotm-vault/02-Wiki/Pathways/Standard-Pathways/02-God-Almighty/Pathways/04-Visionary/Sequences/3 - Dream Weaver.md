# Sequence 3 - Dream Weaver

## Explicit Powers

- **Dream Weaving** — weaves Dreams indistinguishable from reality, drawing a target in and creating near-realistic projections that directly attack mental weaknesses and gradually destroy psychological defenses; anything the target believes is real becomes real to them and can cause death from fright or other mental causes, even though the images cause no direct physical manifestation. Even bystanders not directly targeted can be influenced or corrupted if caught inside the Dream.

## Strengthened Abilities

- **Plague Storm** — releases a storm of thoughts, desires, and wills through Mind Deprivation and Mental Plague, causing Corruption and madness in everyone caught within it; especially dangerous to Angels given their precarious mental balance.
- **Enhanced Mental Attributes** — psychic/spiritual control is strong enough that an enemy's intuition (e.g. Danger Intuition) cannot form a clear predictive image of the Dream Weaver's actions.
- **Psychological Invisibility** — creates a mental mirage realistic enough to fool even Demigods specializing in Spiritual Intuition and illusion discernment.
- **Consciousness Stroll** — its limitations lessen: the physical body (the user's and others') can now enter the Sea of Collective Subconscious directly, achieving a form of Teleportation; Mind Islands no longer need to be entered individually, since the Dream Weaver can observe the Sea of Collective Subconscious itself for insight into others' truest thoughts.
- **Hypnosis** — Battle Hypnotism now interferes with thoughts passed through Spirit Body Threads, closer to interference with will than mental Hypnosis; this Psyche Interference is weaker than the original effect and requires deep mastery to use.
- **Virtual Persona** — virtual personalities can become real, living people who can consume potions to advance (equivalent to the main body consuming another low-Sequence potion) and interact independently with the environment; even their deaths do not harm the true body. Previous identities of others can also be created this way, if the target opens their Body of Heart and Mind. This ability is a prerequisite for eventually becoming a Sequence 0 Visionary.

## Mythical Creature Form

- The incomplete form develops further: the head becomes closer to a lizard than a human, the pupils turn golden and vertical, and the Dragon Scales become intertwined with cold, warped mysterious patterns.

## Potion

- Properties: dark, constantly shifting, mist-like liquid.
- Beyonder Characteristic appearance: an unreal, grayish-white, dream-formed brain wrapped in thin mist.
- Main ingredients: the crystalline heart of the Treant King, or the complete brain of a Dream Dragon, or a Dream Weaver Beyonder Characteristic.
- Supplementary ingredients: Treant King sap or Dream Dragon blood (100 ml), 9 strands of hair each from 13 intelligent beings who died or went insane due to Nightmares, a Sapling (1), Nightmare Grass juice (10 drops).
- Advancement ritual: cause at least 100,000 people to have the same dream on the same night.
