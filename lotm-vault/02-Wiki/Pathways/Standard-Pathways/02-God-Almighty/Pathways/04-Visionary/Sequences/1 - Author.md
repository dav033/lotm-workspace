# Sequence 1 - Author (Fairy Tale Author)

## Explicit Powers

- **Dream of Reality** — intertwines Dreams with reality, letting illusion and substance coexist so the two become difficult to tell apart.
- **Coincidence** — preliminary control over the Sea of Collective Subconscious lets seemingly accidental, wondrous phenomena be turned into echoes of the mind that appear on command.

### Authorities

- **Envisioning (Fantasy into Reality)** — a partial authority touching the Psyche, the Sea of Collective Subconscious, and the Astral World, temporarily pulling concepts into reality as truly existing entities capable of real damage or tangible change. Written or recited stories become reality; a story that lacks internal logic increases both Spirituality expenditure and detection risk. Its range of influence is limited to roughly the size of a big city like Backlund.
- **Discernment** — preliminary control over the Sea of Collective Subconscious grants the trait that any mention of the Author becomes known to them, and the more someone knows about the Author the more the Author learns about that person and their contacts, making the person more likely to appear in the Author's stories; blockable by beings of comparable Concealment ability.
- **Psyche (Mind)** — partial authority over Mind, letting the Author steer the minds of living creatures according to their will while those creatures find it hard to sense the influence and believe the resulting actions are their own.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- [[Envisioning (Visionary)]] → **Envisioning (Fantasy into Reality)**, **Dream of Reality**, **Coincidence** — initial mastery of the Pathway Authority.
- [[Discernment (Visionary)]] → **Discernment**.
- [[Mind (Visionary)]] → **Psyche (Mind)**.

## Potion

- Properties: transparent liquid precipitating countless images of fate and projections of concepts, appearing as tiny points of starlight.
- Beyonder Characteristic appearance: a crystal-clear, pen-like finger bone.
- Main ingredient: the Quill of Alzuhod.
- Supplementary ingredients: Muddy Water from the underground Nightmare Swamp (100 ml, dangerously close to the Chaos Sea and thus unsafe for High Sequence powerhouses to retrieve personally), a fragment of a "Meteor" fallen from the Astral World (1), the 9 most popular novels or fairy tale books of the current era, one's own depraved nature.
- Advancement ritual: using real people as characters, write a medium- to long-length novel and let their actual fates follow the story's plot to its conclusion, leaving imprints in both the Spirit World and the River of Fate.
- Acting method: an Author decides how the story unfolds, where characters go, the ending, and whether they live happily ever after; the story cannot be illogical, so a "reasonable development" always unfolds, with each person merely a word written under the Author's pen.
