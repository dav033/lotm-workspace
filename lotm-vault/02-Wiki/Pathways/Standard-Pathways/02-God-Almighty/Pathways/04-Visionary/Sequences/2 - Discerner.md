# Sequence 2 - Discerner

## Description

Discernment is described as the foundation of description, interference, arrangement, and Imagination for this Pathway; knowing oneself and the enemy ensures victory.

## Explicit Powers

- **Dream Maze** — creates a labyrinth made of many people's Mind Islands, with doors leading to the deepest, secret-holding parts of each mind; the maze subjects everyone inside to mental/spiritual interrogation, confusion, and disturbance, and Corruption inside it can spread to all its constituent minds and cause loss of control.

### Authorities

- **Discernment** — discerns a target's hidden psychological vulnerabilities and deepest fears, and preliminarily perceives a target's past Fate and resulting causal connections. Can sometimes penetrate or be blocked by Concealment, Deceit, Fooling, and similar abilities in a mutually restraining relationship; normal Anti-Divination and Anti-Prophecy methods cannot protect against it.
- **Psychological Cue** — elevated to authority level: as long as the Discerner truly believes in their own strength, they gain real power without being disadvantaged by appearance or physical condition (a "Pure Idealism" effect). With sufficient understanding of an Outer Deity's symbolism, the Discerner can briefly enter that level of power but will inevitably be assimilated into the corresponding entity afterward; fully understanding a Sequence 1 Angel or True Deity can let a Discerner temporarily elevate to that rank, again at the risk of assimilation. A Visionary's existence blocks a Discerner from ever reaching True Deity level this way, since it would conflict with the Visionary's own claim on that level.

## Strengthened Abilities

- **Dream Weaving** — effects now reach further into reality itself (turning a target crazy, monstrous, or changing their personality) and can exit the Mind World to be woven directly into reality as a flawless illusion.
- **Consciousness Stroll** — perception of the Sea of Collective Subconscious becomes deeper and more meticulous, sensing the exchange between a person's heart and the Sea itself.
- **Virtual Persona** — can use Virtual Personas to elevate a target's Mind Island, suppressing their consciousness's ability to fight back and consider multiple things at once.

## Mythical Creature Form

- Becomes a complete Giant Mind Dragon, though for this Pathway the Dragon form is only a conception of the highest authority and can shift with a single thought. It can morph into any Mythical Creature Form and wield the corresponding Beyonder powers; if an enemy believes the assumed form is real, it becomes real to them, but it cannot manifest as reality without a believing enemy present, and its powers only work against those who believe they are real.

## Potion

- Properties: ghastly pale liquid with illusory vertical eyes repeatedly opening and closing.
- Beyonder Characteristic appearance: a pale, hole-riddled heart inlaid with numerous vertical eyes.
- Main ingredients: the pale heart of Ariehogg, or the pale heart of another Ancient Mind Dragon, or a Discerner Beyonder Characteristic.
- Supplementary ingredients: blood from an ancient Mind Dragon or Discerner (90 ml), 3 self-woven true identities, 9 Black Flowers representing the deepest and most terrifying parts of the human heart, 1 non-tail feather of the Solar Divine Bird.
- Advancement ritual: delve into the subconscious of at least 10,000 humans, uncover their deepest fears, most primal desires, and psychological roots, then leave a mark of oneself.
