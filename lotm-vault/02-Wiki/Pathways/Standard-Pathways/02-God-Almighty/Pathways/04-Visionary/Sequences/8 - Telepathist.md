# Sequence 8 - Telepathist

## Explicit Powers

- **Enhanced Vision** — eyesight is further sharpened beyond a Spectator's.
- **Mind Reading** — reads the superficial thoughts of others and can simulate their likely trajectory to a degree.

## Strengthened Abilities

- **Body Language Analysis** — observation extends past superficial detail into a target's aura, Ether Body, or other mysterious domains, including recognizing what emotional reaction and body language would be appropriate to show in a given situation.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- [[Discernment (Visionary)]] → **Mind Reading**, strengthened **Body Language Analysis**.
- **Enhanced Vision** remains a trait without independent Authority status.

## Potion

- Properties: a liquid with constantly changing lusters, said to shine into the depths of everyone's hearts.
- Beyonder Characteristic appearance: transparent, dreamlike eyes.
- Main ingredient: a Telepathist Beyonder Characteristic.
- Supplementary ingredients: Chestnut Spore (5 g), Dragon Tooth Grass Powder (8 g), Pure White Elf Flowers (3 petals), Purified Water (100 ml).
- Consumption backlash: vision blanks into a blur or haze for longer than a Spectator experiences before clearing significantly.
- Acting method: do not act abruptly or against logic and reason.
