# Sequence 5 - Dreamwalker

## Explicit Powers

- **Alteration** — modifies a target's Dreams to influence them over a long period without their realizing it, using the Dream as an entry point and Astral Projection as leverage to slowly contaminate the Spirit Body before reaching the Body of Heart and Mind; milder and more furtive than Hypnosis, and usable on Demigods with sufficient preparation.
- **Guidance** — while perceiving a target's Dreams, causes changes within them to subtly draw out the target's deepest secrets; unlike a Nightmare, cannot forcefully pull someone into a Dream unless combined with Hypnosis.
- **Dream Traversal** — turns the physical body and Spirit Body incorporeal, allowing the Dreamwalker to hide in or jump between others' dreams, limited to a maximum of 500 meters between two dreams belonging to intelligent creatures.

## Strengthened Abilities

- **Placate (Psychoanalysis)** — given enough time, can return a Demigod who has not truly lost control (only entered an Incomplete Mythical Creature Form) back to normal; more effective if the Demigod opens their Body of Heart and Mind, allowing the Dreamwalker to split Placate into multiple treatments and plant reassuring Psychological Cues. Attempting to treat the Body of Heart and Mind directly before using Placate a few times risks infecting the Dreamwalker with the target's own inclination to lose control.

## Potion

- Properties: liquid dotted with darkened points of grayish-white light; tastes sour, sweet, bitter, surreal, and stimulating, like a wild dream.
- Beyonder Characteristic appearance: a large amount of purged grayish-white matter forming a child-fist-sized, heart-shaped object with wrinkles, eye-like cracks, and patterns nearly identical to a Manipulator's Characteristic.
- Main ingredients: an adult Mind Dragon's complete brain, or a Dream Catcher's Heart plus a Mind Illusion Crystal, or a Dreamwalker Beyonder Characteristic.
- Supplementary ingredients: Blood of an Adult Mind Dragon (100 ml, or 90 ml Dream Catcher's Blood), Adult Mind Dragon scales or Mind Illusion Remnant Dust (3), an Off-White Dragon's Breath Grass (1), Treeman leaves (3).
- Advancement ritual: seek out a Spirit World harpy, sign a contract with it, then consume the potion amid intense joy or anger while holding one of its tail feathers; the harpy (or an equivalent nightmare-giving substitute) exists to snap the drinker awake if they become unwilling to leave the dream.
- Consumption backlash/ritual effect: the drinker is startled into a vision of a perfect, wish-fulfilled world before their Astral Projection rises and breaks free, revealing the dream as a huge bubble grown from their island of consciousness, enclosing the Sea of Collective Subconscious; they then forcefully escape.
- Acting method: come, see, record, but do not interfere — be a Spectator; if interference is necessary, remain an unseen mastermind and conductor.
