# Sequence 7 - Psychiatrist (Psyche Analyst)

## Traits

- **Physical Enhancement** — strength, speed, dark-vision, and overall health improve; smell becomes sensitive enough to help grasp a target's truest emotions and thoughts.

## Explicit Powers

- **Frenzy** — triggers a target's emotions and mental state, inflicting intense psychological damage and occasionally a direct loss of control.
- **Telepathy** — through mediums like candlelight or extracts, partially hypnotizes a target to communicate directly with their Body of Heart and Mind, or to enter the deepest part of their Soul (the bottom of their dreamland) where a corresponding scene materializes.
- **Psychological Cue** — through specific methods, words, and a medium, cues a target to abide by an arrangement without realizing it; used on oneself, it can keep the user lucid inside a dream, and can make a target strongly abide by a promise from the bottom of their heart.
- **Awe (Dragon's Might, Mass Chaos)** — instantly panics, freezes, or turns chaotic those within range as though facing a dragon; resistible with strong mental resistance or advance warning.
- **Placate (Psychoanalysis)** — helps a Beyonder or mortal on the verge of losing control regain reason and escape danger; success improves the higher the Psychiatrist's Sequence is above the target's. Also usable to calm psychological instability for communication, or to awaken a feeling/memory that Placates an unstable target.

## Strengthened Abilities

- **Body Language Analysis** — can now be done indirectly, such as analyzing a person's body language from a picture to form an effective mental model of them.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- [[Discernment (Visionary)]] → **Telepathy**, **Psychological Cue**, strengthened **Body Language Analysis**.
- [[Loss of Control (Visionary)]] → **Frenzy**, **Awe**, and **Placate** as counter-applications targeting or reversing loss of control.
- **Physical Enhancement** remains a trait without independent Authority status.

## Potion

- Properties: slightly golden liquid that ripples like a deformed, gigantic pupil; tastes cool like ice cream.
- Beyonder Characteristic appearance: a huge golden iris that could expose everyone's inner thoughts.
- Main ingredient: a Psychiatrist Beyonder Characteristic.
- Consumption backlash: hearing sharpens dramatically, vision smooths into a mirror-like reflection, scales grow over exposed skin, and the irises turn vertical and faintly golden as the drinker nears losing control; recovery requires entering a Spectator-like calm state before the scales recede and eyes return to normal.
- Acting method: help others solve their mental problems; people wear different personality masks for different situations.
