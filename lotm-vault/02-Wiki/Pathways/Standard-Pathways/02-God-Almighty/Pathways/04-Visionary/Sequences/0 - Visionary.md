# Sequence 0 - Visionary

## Explicit Powers

- **Envisioning (Fantasy into Reality)** — what the Visionary envisions materializes; their Divine Kingdom can descend upon the physical world and proclaimed futures unfold as reality. Touches the Psyche/cognition and the Astral World, letting the Visionary separate two Sequence 1 Beyonder Characteristics and replace them with Imagined counterparts while keeping their own rank and power; the separated true Characteristics can be recombined with another identity or an Imagined life form to create a fully independent Author, which can be transformed back into the Visionary's true form as a Resurrection plan in dire need. With sufficient understanding and mastery of relevant concepts, the Visionary can be Omnipotent in a sense, though this Omnipotence cannot resist authorities that erase Illusions or pierce Fantasies. Every Visionary's Divine Kingdom is unique to their own Imagination. A special, non-Sefirah-dependent trait lets them Envision a Half Great Old One if they have accommodated the White Tower Pathway, hold Chaos Sea clearance, or have been Corrupted by another Sefirah, combined with a second Uniqueness and its Sequence 1 Characteristic.
- **Psyche (Mind)** — as master of the whole Mind World (individual Mind Islands and the Sea of Collective Subconscious), the Visionary can regress a being's mind to a fetal state (stripping even subconscious memories) while awakening the full depth of buried cognition, inject negative emotion into an Advancement Ritual at a critical moment to guarantee loss of control, and manufacture or naturally recognize Humanity (a quality that is both good and evil, rational and mad) in a target.
- **Discernment** — as Ruler of the Mind World, the Visionary is Omniscient in a sense within it, extending into the Fate, Reality, and Illusion Domains; facing a Visionary, one has no secrets, and awareness of the Visionary's existence can cover the whole world.
- **Dream** — partial authority over the concept of Dreams itself; against a Darkness Pathway Beyonder of the same level, this authority leans toward weaving Dreams that target the Mind.
- **Loss of Control** — partial authority over Loss of Control; for Beyonders, the collapse of the Psyche often marks its beginning.

## Potion

- Properties: a golden potion that seems to contain a fantasy world within.
- Supplementary ingredients: a Realm self-created from Fantasy or Dreams, which later evolves into a Divine Kingdom upon becoming a God.
- Advancement ritual: make the development of the times align with one's own expectations, then consume the potion at a suitable node in that trend.

## Notes

- The rituals for this Pathway are comparatively simple because Visionaries can heal and analyze themselves (via Placate/Psychoanalysis-derived self-treatment), letting them counter the madness and loss of control the potion causes; other adjacent Pathways lack a self-Placating ability and may remain conscious while spiraling into loss of control even after a perfectly completed ritual.
