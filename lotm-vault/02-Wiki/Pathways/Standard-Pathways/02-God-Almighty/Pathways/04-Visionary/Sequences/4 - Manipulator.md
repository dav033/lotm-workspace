# Sequence 4 - Manipulator

## Traits

- **Physical Enhancement** — as a Demigod, strength and defense (via Dragon Scales) reach the level of breaking steel with a punch and outrunning a steam train.

## Explicit Powers

- **Manipulation** — the Body of Heart and Mind passes through the Sea of Collective Subconscious into another's Mind Island, directly changing subconscious and reading thoughts to silently Manipulate a target or a group; buried psychological hints are carried out accurately, and most victims never realize they were manipulated even after recovering.
- **Mental Plague** — secretly implants a Mental Plague seed in a target's Mind Island that quietly infects the surrounding Sea of Collective Subconscious, causing widespread mental illness and madness; because it originates within the target, an Angel's blessing can at best weaken and delay it, not isolate it, and it is difficult to offset with a Virtual Persona since it is erosion/infection rather than a direct attack.
- **Mind Storm** — sweeps a designated thought outward to disturb the surrounding Sea of Collective Subconscious, causing corrupting "voices" and repeated blows to enemies' Mind Islands that disturb Spirituality and can break their mind barrier.
- **Virtual Persona** — creates up to 13 Virtual Personas (at the start) with a corresponding Body of Heart and Mind, defending against Mind Domain influence and allowing undetected invasion of another's Mind Island.
- **Mind Dragon Breath** — an area-of-effect breath attack directly harming the Body of Heart and Mind and Soul Body; an upgraded form of Psychic Piercing, stemming from the Mind Dragon Mythical Creature Form.

## Strengthened Abilities

- **Mind Deprivation** — a qualitative change to Awe: all creatures within range inevitably suffer abnormal conditions ranging from buckling to the ground and fervent worship-like crowding to blank standing, fleeing, or fainting.
- **Psychological Invisibility** — potent enough to evade most Demigods so long as the user avoids their direct field of view.
- **Consciousness Stroll** — the evolved form of Dream Traversal: consciousness and Spirit Body (not the physical body) can freely swim the Sea of Collective Subconscious, though Dreams are still needed to traverse specific areas.
- **Mind Reading** — as Demigods, Manipulators can easily distinguish a Marionette from its true body, greatly mitigating a Bizarro Sorcerer's strongest ability.

## Mythical Creature Form

- Incomplete Godhood grants an incomplete Giant Mind Dragon form: a human-headed, gray-winged, thick-limbed dragon body engraved with complex three-dimensional symbols. Anyone who looks upon it suffers mental confusion and thought-warping, even at the Spirit Body level. The form grants dragon-level strength, defense, and flight but lacks strong offensive Beyonder powers of its own; using Psychoanalysis and Hypnosis, a newly advanced Manipulator can maintain full control over it for about 1 minute before suffering negative effects.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- [[Mind (Visionary)]] → **Manipulation**, **Mental Plague**, **Mind Storm**, **Virtual Persona**, **Consciousness Stroll**.
- [[Loss of Control (Visionary)]] → **Mind Deprivation** as an inflicted-loss-of-control application.
- [[Discernment (Visionary)]] → strengthened **Mind Reading**, **Psychological Invisibility**.
- **Physical Enhancement** and **Mind Dragon Breath** remain traits/Powers without independent Authority status.

## Potion

- Properties: countless points of light float within, resembling the Sea of Collective Subconscious; the advancer cannot process the taste before the backlash hits.
- Beyonder Characteristic appearance: a fist-sized, heart-and-brain-like object with grayish-white wrinkles and intricate symbols connecting to an unseen realm.
- Main ingredients: a complete brain of an Elderly Mind Dragon, or the crystalline heart of a Tree Mentor, or a Manipulator Beyonder Characteristic.
- Supplementary ingredients: Elderly Mind Dragon blood (80 ml), Tree Mentor's Golden Leaf (3 pieces), seven drops of tears shed by different humans or non-human creatures due to intense emotion.
- Advancement ritual: in the middle of a grand event with at least 10,000 people, resonate with their emotions, then consume the potion.
- Consumption backlash/ritual effect: the drinker's sense of body dissolves as they merge into the Sea of Collective Subconscious for the first time without a dream or Mind Island as an intermediary, confronting fear, madness, and mental corruption; the surrounding "sea" resonates with the crowd's sorrow and pain, forcing the drinker to Placate themselves while splitting into many transparent bodies that step onto different people's Mind Islands and directly witness their emotions and the scenes that caused them.
