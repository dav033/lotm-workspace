# Sequence 5 - Shepherd

## Explicit Powers

### Description

**Shepherds** are ranked first below [High Sequences.](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sequence "Sequence") They are so all-rounded that if they combined and matched their Beyonder powers well enough, they would almost have zero shortcomings.

### New Abilities

- **Grazing**: **Shepherds** can swallow a target's [Soul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul") and **Beyonder Characteristics** or **Boon** powers into their body, including **Wraiths** and [Evil Spirits](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Evil_Spirits "Evil Spirits"), and use those **Beyonder** powers for themselves.
    - They can **Graze** [Saint-level](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Saint "Category:Saint") [Souls](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul"), however, doing so will greatly increase their risk of losing control.
        - If a **Grazed** [Soul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul") is at the level of a **Demigod**, it can be directly released into the real world to fight. This is due to the corporeal **Spirit Bodies** that **Demigods** possess.
            - Using the **Grazed** [Soul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul") powers will only consume that [Soul's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul") **Spirituality**
                        - Releasing a [Soul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul") won't allow the **Shepherd** to use another [Soul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul").
                        - Only one of such [Soul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul") can exist in the real world at a time.
        - Losing **Grazing** will make them lose control because of the [Souls'](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul") interaction in their body.
        - Making them unable to use **Grazed** [Souls](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul") will not have much of an effect on them. It is only when they lose the ability fully would they begin to lose control.
            - A **[Depriver's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tail-Devourer_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_5:_Depriver)** **Deprivation** as well as a **[Prometheus'](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_6:_Prometheus)** **Theft** are examples of **Beyonder** powers that can cause a **Shepherd** to lose control.
        - **Shepherds** can't activate **Grazing** continuously, which restricts passive abilities.
        - As such **Beyonder** powers that are more passive in nature (like a **[Devil's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Abyss_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_6:_Devil) Danger Premonition**) are effective only when actively **Grazing** that particular [Soul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul").
        - A **Shepherd**, upon full potion digestion, will be able to **Graze** up to *7 [Souls](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul")*.
        - They can only use one [Soul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul") at a time, but they can instantly switch between them.
        - They can only use up to *3* **Beyonder** powers from a particular [Soul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul").
        - As the **Sequence** increases, they will be able to use more abilities from **Grazed** [Souls](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul").
                - When **Grazing** a new [Soul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul"), a **Shepherd** will be able to choose which powers to acquire.
                - They can still use their own **Beyonder** powers when using a [Soul's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul") **Beyonder** powers.
            - **Beyonder** powers that normally don't allow its user to move freely like a **[Nightmare's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Darkness_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_7:_Nightmare) Nightmare State** can be used without the **Shepherd** needing to stay still.
        - A **Shepherd's** **Grazed** target has to include a [Soul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul").
        - Such a condition won't be changed even at higher **Sequences**. Even the **Hanged Man** needs a [Soul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul") to **Graze** a [Uniqueness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniquenesses "Uniquenesses") or **Beyonder Characteristic** and cannot simply target it alone.
                - **Grazing** entails a certain reduction in the potency **Beyonder** powers of the **Grazed** [Soul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul"). This is because the [Soul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul") must be damaged before it can be **Grazed** by a **Shepherd**.
            - Even if the [Soul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul") of a target stays **Grazed**, it will gradually wear away afterwards, meaning it can't be permanently kept by them.
                        - If a [Soul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul") does not fall in **Depravity**, and instead is forcibly **Grazed**, it won't completely cooperate with the **Shepherd**.
                        - Depending on the state of the [Soul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul"), its power might be halved.

### Strengthened Abilities

- **Flesh and Blood Magic**: They have further mastered abilities under **Flesh and Blood Magic**.
    - **Devouring**: **Shepherds** can directly **Devour** a target's **Flesh** and **Blood**, along with their [Soul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul"), leaving no traces behind.
- **Controlling Shadows**: An advanced version of **Manipulating Shadows**, which allows the direct **Manipulation** of deep **Shadows** not belonging to living creatures.
    - They can give these **Shadows** physical substance, make them bind targets, crush or compress objects, or **Corrode** and melt entities.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- **Grazing**, introduced here for the first time, is directly reported at Sequence 0 as "the authority of Grazing, derived from the Omnipotence symbolism" → [[Grazing (Hanged Man)]].
- Strengthened **Flesh and Blood Magic** → [[Mutation (Hanged Man)]]; strengthened **Controlling Shadows** → [[Shadows (Hanged Man)]].

## Potion

### Potion Properties

> A gray-white liquid with fleeting, bloated visages; tears the soul upon consumption.

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> A translucent gray-white hand.

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- Crystallized Bloody Scarecrow.
- Brain of a Herd Keeper (monkey-like in appearance).

Or
- A Shepherd Beyonder Characteristic.

Supplementary Ingredients

- 80 milliliters of Herd Keeper's Blood.
- 7 strands of Bloody Scarecrow Straw.
- 1 Whip that has Flogged at least 100 People.
- 10 grams of Marigold Balm.

### Advancement Ritual

- Extract one's soul from the body.
- Influence the soulless body to consume the potion.
- Reintegrate the soul to complete the ascension.
