# Sequence 0 - Hanged Man

## Explicit Powers

### Description

The **True God** presiding over the domain of **Degeneration** / **Depravity**, an amalgamation of **Black Shadows** and **Writhing Flesh**, possessing five heads and the corresponding **Shadows**. "He" holds partial authority over the domains of **Shadow**, **Darkness**, **Degeneration** / **Depravity**, **Filth**, and **Mutation**, while also embodying the concepts and partial **Symbolism** of **Sin-Bearing** and **Sacrifice**.

### Symbolism

- **Sin-Bearing** (**Scapegoat**): "They" can take the damage and death intended for others, redirecting the enemy’s primary attacks toward "Themselves".
    - The [True Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/True_Creator "True Creator") bears the world's **Sins**, making "His" believers innocent in the eyes of the world. As a result, they cannot be found guilty or punished by **Beyonder** powers similar to an **[Overseer's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chaos_Mist_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_4:_Overseer)**.
- **Sacrifice** (**Hanged Man**): By **Sacrificing** "Themselves", "They" can secure absolute hope.

### Authorities

- **Darkness**: Compared to the **[Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Darkness_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Darkness)**, the **Hanged Man’s** **Darkness** leans toward an all-consuming, devouring **Darkness** and a **Darkness** that corrupts the heart.
    - Those who walk into "Their" **Shadows** and **Darkness** without sufficient protection or status will quickly fall into **Depravity**, turning into **Corrupted** monsters.
        - Every year, many individuals within the [Forsaken Land of the Gods](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Forsaken_Land_of_the_Gods "Forsaken Land of the Gods") fall under the **Shadows** and **Darkness**, quickly turning into **Corrupted** monsters.
- **Mutations**: "They" hold authority over **Mutations** which is the authority that the **Flesh and** **Blood** authority is derived from.
    - "They" can create countless **Corrupted** monsters from nothing in the **Darkness** that possess the particularity of **Resurrection** —a qualitative change of **Flesh and Blood Immortality**.
        - The [Forsaken Land of the Gods](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Forsaken_Land_of_the_Gods "Forsaken Land of the Gods") is cursed with the influence of the [Ancient Sun God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ancient_Sun_God "Ancient Sun God"), spawning countless **Corrupted** monsters in the **Darkness**.
        - The **Hanged Man's** authority of **Mutation** is also capable of influencing the **Change** of all things to some extent, which partially overlaps with the authority of the **[Fool](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fool_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Fool)**.
- **Shadows** (**Lord of Shadows**): "They" have now become the absolute ruler of the **Shadow** **World**.
    - "Their" **Divine Kingdom** is that of the entire **Shadow World**, or something similar.
        - "Their" **Shadows** inherently possess some **Concealment** abilities.
- **Degeneration** / **Depravity** (**Degenerate Nature**): "They" are the **Degenerate Nature** of all living things.
    - "They" can make a **Shadow** rise and adhere to "Their" target's back, while other similar **Shadows** will continually emerge from the depths of the target's psyche, merging within and out and reshaping their form, being the inherent **Degenerate Nature** in all living things.
        - Apart from certain **High Sequence** **Beyonders** (such as an **[Angel of Redemption](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Eternal_Aeon_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_1:_Angel_of_Redemption)**), no one is able to separate from their own **Depraved Nature**. At most, they will only be able to suppress it's effects on them.
        - While the **[Chaos Primogenitor's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chaos_Primogenitor_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Chaos_Primogenitor) Degeneration** authority focuses on a comprehensive **Corruption** of body, mind, and **Spirit**, the **Hanged Man's** authority over **Degeneration** is self- **Corruption**.
        - Violating one's three outlooks, values, and morals is **Degeneration**. Violating the **Rules** established by the [Trunsoest Brass Book](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Trunsoest_Brass_Book "Trunsoest Brass Book") is also **Degeneration**.
        - If the **Hanged Man** embodies the **Degenerate Nature** of every living being, then the **[Abyss](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Abyss_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Abyss)** is the environment that inevitably leads all those within it to **Degenerate**, not only inducing **Degeneration** but also creating it.
- **Filth** (**Incarnation of Filth**): Being tainted by the **[Chaos Sea](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chaos_Sea)**, "They" can **Defile** all things.
    - When combined with the **Darkness** and **Degeneration**, "They" are capable of projecting traces of the **[Chaos Sea](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chaos_Sea)** 's influence.
        - "He" can plant a "seed" that melts into the **Spirit Body** of "Their" target. Part of the target's body would be merged with the "seed", making it extremely hard to **Purify**. It would influence the target mystically, but would also influence their **Mind** and **Fate**.
        - The [True Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/True_Creator "True Creator") planted a "seed" in Klein that forced him to meet [Sasrir](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sasrir "Sasrir") in the [Giant King's Court](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Giant_King%27s_Court "Giant King's Court"). This was partly due to mystic means but was also because of alterations in the mind as well as in **Fate**.
- **Grazing**: "They" have the authority of **Grazing**, derived from the **Omnipotence** symbolism.
- **Profane Language**: A manifestation of the authorities of **Degeneration** and **Filth**.
    - Those who heard "Their" **Depraved Language** would feel extreme pain as their bodies began to convulse and tremble uncontrollably, quickly cracking open to reveal flesh and blood. Inside, black worms and white moths squirmed, eventually forming all manner of indescribable eyes.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- Sequence 0 is the capstone: the source directly confirms all named Authorities of the Pathway.
- **Darkness** → [[Darkness (Hanged Man)]], including the "Corrupted monsters" recurring across this Sequence and [[1 - Dark Angel|Dark Angel]].
- **Mutations** → [[Mutation (Hanged Man)]], directly "the authority that the Flesh and Blood authority is derived from," confirming the Sequence 3/6 inference.
- **Shadows** (**Lord of Shadows**) → [[Shadows (Hanged Man)]].
- **Degeneration**/**Depravity** (**Degenerate Nature**) → [[Degeneration (Hanged Man)]].
- **Filth** (**Incarnation of Filth**) → [[Filth (Hanged Man)]], matching Sequence 2's derivation of Profane Language.
- **Grazing** → [[Grazing (Hanged Man)]], directly "derived from the Omnipotence symbolism."
- **Profane Language** is reconfirmed as a manifestation of Degeneration and Filth, not an independent Authority.
- **Sin-Bearing** (Scapegoat) and **Sacrifice** (Hanged Man) are directly labeled **Symbolism**, not Authority, by the source's own heading, and are left unmapped here accordingly.
