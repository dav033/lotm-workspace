# Sequence 9 - Secrets Suppliant

## Explicit Powers

### Traits

- **High Spirituality**: **Secrets Suppliants** have a high **Spiritual Perception** which they can use it to detect some mysterious and horrible existences.
    - **Ritualistic Magic & Divination Arts**: They can use their strong **Spirituality** to perform [Divination](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Divination_Art "Divination Art") and [Ritualistic Magic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ritualistic_Magic "Ritualistic Magic"), but it's not as accurate as [Pathways](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways") that specialize in this field.
        - Their **High Spirituality** also allows them to detect auras of relatively powerful **Beyonders** (provided that those **Beyonders** did not conceal their aura beforehand).
        - [Osta Trul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Osta_Trul "Osta Trul") was able to feel that the real world was wrapped in a [thick veil](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Astral_Barrier "Astral Barrier"), and beyond that veil, [emotionless eyes](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Outer_Deities "Outer Deities") were watching them.

### New Abilities

- **Knowledge (Mysticism)**: **Secrets Suppliants** are bestowed with a decent amount of knowledge regarding sacrifices and some knowledge on [Ritualistic Magic](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ritualistic_Magic "Ritualistic Magic").
    - There are some [Honorific Names](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Honorific_Name "Honorific Name") included in the potion's knowledge, but they're all secret entities with sinister names that includes elements like "depravity", "true kin", or the "gaze of fate".
        - It is known that the information attached to the potion contains the name of the [True Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/True_Creator "True Creator") and **Angel** of **Fate** [Ouroboros](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ouroboros "Ouroboros"); however, the three parts of the name "gaze of fate" does not fully describe the latter's **Honorific** **Name**. [Aurora Order](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Aurora_Order "Aurora Order") members who gave this name have other purposes, so they have made some processing on the name.
        - The knowledge attached to the potion given will more or less cause distortions in a **Secrets Suppliants'** cognitive perspectives and thus make it easy for them to lose control.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- No Authority is named at Sequence 9. **High Spirituality**, contact with secret entities, and **Knowledge (Mysticism)** are early precursors of the domains the Pathway later names as [[Shadows (Hanged Man)]] and [[Darkness (Hanged Man)]], but current evidence does not establish either at this early stage.
- **Ritualistic Magic & Divination Arts** remain general Beyonder techniques, not Pathway-specific Authorities.
