# Sequence 4 - Black Knight

## Explicit Powers

### New Abilities

- **Cull of Spiritual Flesh**: This is the core **Beyonder** power of a **Black Knight**, stemming from the **Degeneration** domain. It manifests as a **Black** **Greatsword** and has the following effects:
    - It can render any living creature with **Degenerate** thoughts to lose their defenses.
        - It **Corrodes** both **Flesh** and **Blood** while also obliterating [Souls](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul").
        - It can cleave through powerful physical barriers.
- **Black Armor**: They can transform into a small giant *2-3 meters tall*, covered in a **Black**, cold **Armor**, with only two balls of deep red light flashing at the position of their eyes.
    - This **Black Armor** is formed from the powers of **Degeneration**, protecting them from various attacks and it can also fracture to redirect damage, protecting the **Black Knight**.
- **Shadow of Depravation**: This peels off one's **Depraved** thoughts into a **Shadow**, forming an uncontrollable, independent creature.
    - This can be used to purge a foreign consciousness out of their mind.
        - For instance, they can eject a **[Manipulator's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Visionary_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_4:_Manipulator) Virtual Persona** from their mind and turn it into a **Shadow**. If the **[Manipulator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Visionary_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_4:_Manipulator)** cannot quickly resolve this, their fractured thoughts would gradually become independent, turning them half-mad and even causing them to lose control.
        - This can disintegrate into a void of **Darkness**, causing objects it touches to be stained with a layer of thick, sticky blackness—either instantly corroding and breaking apart, or **Degenerating** and turning to attack the **Black Knight's** enemies.
        - The **Corrupted** [Enuni](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Enzo "Enzo") made his illusory black wings disintegrate to defend against [Derrick](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Derrick_Berg "Derrick Berg") 's barrage of **Unshadowed Spears**; some instantly corroded and broke, others left a curved arc in the air as it spun around and tore at [Derrick](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Derrick_Berg "Derrick Berg") himself.
        - This can also transform the **Shadow** into armor for defense, separate from the **Black Armor**.
        - The dark wings that enveloped [Enuni's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Enzo "Enzo") body blocked influences and attacks such as the **Unshadowed Domain**  and **Hurricane of Light**.
- **Commandeering Shadows**: This makes the target’s own **Shadow** come alive and surge like rising water, tightly binding them.
    - This **Shadow** is equivalent to a fraction of the target, therefore severing their **Shadow** will make their spirit lose integrity.

### Strengthened Abilities

- Other **Beyonder** powers from previous **Sequences** are enhanced.
    - **Flesh and Blood Magic**: Their grasp on abilities of this domain have been strengthened.
        - **Flesh Rug**: They can transform themselves into a thin, **Bloody**, and **Flesh** -dripping **Rug** that covers an area, effectively dividing the battlefield and preventing grouping among enemies.
        - **Grazing**: Their ability to **Graze** [Souls](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul") have been enhanced.
        - They can use more abilities from **Grazed** [Souls](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul").
                - They can externally release any **Grazed** [Soul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul"), which can use all their **Beyonder** powers in this form.
                - They can hold up to *9 **Grazed** [Souls](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul")*.
        - **Controlling Shadows**: Their ability to control **Shadows** have been enhanced.
        - They can make the surrounding streets quickly fill with a pitch-black and strange liquid that then surge over and quickly coagulated to form a strong cage that surrounds an area.

### Mythical Creature Form

- As **Sequence 4 Demigods** they receive some level of **Godhood**, and with it comes an incomplete [Mythical Creature Form](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mythical_Creature_Form "Mythical Creature Form"). Disregarding all consequences, they can release their incomplete [Mythical Creature Form](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mythical_Creature_Form "Mythical Creature Form") to become a black **Shadow** and writhing **Flesh**.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- **Cull of Spiritual Flesh** and **Black Armor** are both directly "stemming from"/"formed from the powers of" **Degeneration** → [[Degeneration (Hanged Man)]].
- **Shadow of Depravation** and **Commandeering Shadows** → [[Shadows (Hanged Man)]]; the former's ability to disintegrate into "a void of **Darkness**" also touches [[Darkness (Hanged Man)]].
- Strengthened Flesh and Blood Magic/Grazing → [[Mutation (Hanged Man)]] and [[Grazing (Hanged Man)]]; strengthened Controlling Shadows → [[Shadows (Hanged Man)]]. The incomplete Mythical Creature Form is a general Demigod trait, not an Authority.

Or

Supplementary Ingredients

- Black Liquid of a Shadow Knight.
- 1 drop of Blood from Each Ritual Participant.
- 10 drops of Lemon balm Essential Oil.

### Advancement Ritual

- Induce the complete degeneration of over 10,000 individuals without using violence.
    - The higher the sequence of the degenerate individuals, the better the ritual effect.
- The more concentrated the transformation from normalcy to degeneracy in time, the more effective the ritual becomes.
