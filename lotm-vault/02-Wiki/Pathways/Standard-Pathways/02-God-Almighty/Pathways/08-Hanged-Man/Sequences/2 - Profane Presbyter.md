# Sequence 2 - Profane Presbyter

## Explicit Powers

### Authorities

- **Profane Language**: "They" have mastered the **Profane Language**, a manifestation of the authorities of **Degeneration** and **Filth**.
    - "They" can weaponize a target's responses and words- extracting phrases or magnifying ambiguities to impose a word-spirit like effect such as imprisonment, **Corruption** or separation.
        - Any actions that have the meaning of “exchange” or “communication” can be affected by **Profane Language**. For example, sign language can be distorted or misinterpreted.
            - When [Klein](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") answered who he was, it ended up separating "a lonely traveler" from him, splitting his [Gehrman Sparrow](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") persona that has the same thoughts and abilities, effectively canceling [Klein's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") **Historical Projection Summoning**.
                - The separation here was when Klein's **Degenerate** thoughts combined with his **Shadow**, which then cancelled out his **Historical Void Summoning**.
                        - When [Klein](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") said that he didn’t know where the owner of the city had gone, Angel of the Holy Word [Steph](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Steph "Steph") extracted the words “I don’t know where I went,” trapping him and isolating him from the outside world.
        - "They" can achieve enhancements on "Themselves", making it so that when "They" reveal "Their" identity, each word that references "Them" enhances "Their" level and strength.
        - When [Steph](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Steph "Steph") announced "Himself" to [Klein](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti"), "He" used this aspect of the **Profane Language** authority, effectively enhancing "His" level and strength with each and every word.
        - "They" can induce a target to **Degenerate** via a "spiritual" **Cull of Spiritual Flesh**. Targets who possess **Degenerate** thoughts are not able to resist this influence.
        - "They" can bring out the **Degenerate** thoughts of "Their" opponents as their [Soul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul") plummets, sinking into the viscous black depths of their inner psyche, restraining their actions.
            - [Alger Wilson](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Alger_Wilson "Alger Wilson") experienced this from the middle head of [The Setting Sun Rooster](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/The_Setting_Sun_Rooster "The Setting Sun Rooster"), causing his [Soul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul") to plummet, sinking into the viscous black depths of his inner psyche. This caused **Malice**, dissatisfaction, and jealousy surged within him as he felt an overwhelming urge to conserve his strength and avoid unnecessary risks.
                - "They" can bring an invisible force pulling a target's consciousness and spirit downward, as though they were an irreversible sunset, making their expression twitch uncontrollably as they begin to succumb to **Depravity**.
            - However, **Depravity** is a process, not an instantaneous event.
        - Through "Their" authority of **Profane Language**, "They" can create and direct **Curses** onto targets that inflict **Degeneration** and **Corruption**.
- **Flesh & Blood:** Their previous abilities were strengthened.
    - **Trinity Body**: Each of "Their" created heads can now use more than one **Grazed Soul**.
        - [Steph](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Steph "Steph") created a head covered in blood on “His” left shoulder. Around that head were two skinless arms that had mangled flesh. At the same time, another bloody head grew on [Steph](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Steph "Steph") ’s right shoulder, one that looked to be in “His” twenties. Amongst the three heads, on used **Grazed** [Souls](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul") that had powers that can **Steal** [Klein](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") ’s thoughts, while the other was staring coldly at the target, deepening the self-awareness of the lonely traveler.

### Strengthened Abilities

- "Their" capabilities in the area of **Flesh and Blood Magic** as well **Shadows** have been strengthened.
    - **Grazing**: "They" can **Graze** up to *18 [Souls](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul")*.

### Mythical Creature Form

- "Their" [Mythical Creature Form](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mythical_Creature_Form "Mythical Creature Form") has become complete. "Their" [Mythical Creature Form](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mythical_Creature_Form "Mythical Creature Form") is that of a black **Shadow** and writhing **Flesh**.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- The source directly names **Profane Language** as "a manifestation of the authorities of **Degeneration** and **Filth**" → [[Degeneration (Hanged Man)]] and [[Filth (Hanged Man)]] jointly.
- **Flesh & Blood** (Trinity Body) → [[Mutation (Hanged Man)]]. Strengthened **Grazing** → [[Grazing (Hanged Man)]]; strengthened Shadow control → [[Shadows (Hanged Man)]]. The complete Mythical Creature Form is a general trait, not an Authority.
