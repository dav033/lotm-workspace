# Sequence 8 - Listener (Whispered)

## Explicit Powers

### Description

[Nighthawk](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Nighthawks "Nighthawks") files state that every **Listener** is a lunatic - even if they look normal on the surface, they are always crazy on the inside. The files also state that it was difficult for **Listeners** to survive the next five years after taking the potion if they were unable to advance, because they frequently came into contact with powerful, distorted entities.

### Traits

- **High Spirituality**: Their spiritual perception is further enhanced.

### New Abilities

- **Listen**: Every **Listener** can **Listen** directly into the whispers of the secret entities; thus, they frequently come into contact with information from powerful, distorted, and unique entities.
    - While they can obtain powerful, twisted, and unique abilities, if they cannot advance in **Sequence**, it would be difficult for them to survive for more than 5 years.
        - Every **Listener** is a lunatic - even if they look normal on the surface and behave normally at ordinary times, they are always crazy on the inside.
            - **Listeners** will often hear the ravings of the [True Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/True_Creator "True Creator").
                - For Oracles of the [Aurora Order](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Aurora_Order "Aurora Order"), the ability to directly hear the ravings of the [True Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/True_Creator "True Creator") and "His" instructions grants them strong autonomy.
        - Even if they don't actively use **Listening**, one will hear more than ordinary people and most of the **Low-Mid Sequence** **Beyonders** within a normal area.
        - When the source of the sound is close enough to them, a **Listener** will be more affected and will receive more dangerous information.
        - At this stage, this **Beyonder** power is always active (in a passive state). At later **Sequences**, they can choose whether or not to proactively use it.
        - Entities will always be able to speak to a **Listener**, even if a **Listener** removes their ears.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- No Authority is named at this Sequence. **Listen** (contact with the whispers of secret, distorted entities) anticipates the Pathway's eventual [[Shadows (Hanged Man)]] and [[Darkness (Hanged Man)]] domains without confirming either.
- **High Spirituality** remains an unattributed supporting trait.
