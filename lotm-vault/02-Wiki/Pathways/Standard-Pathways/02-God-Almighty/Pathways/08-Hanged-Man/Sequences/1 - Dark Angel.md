# Sequence 1 - Dark Angel

## Explicit Powers

### Authorities

- **Shadows** (**Shadow Ruler**): A **Dark Angel** is able to control and use the **Shadow World**; however, "They" are not yet the **Shadow World's** full master.
    - Similar to the [Demoness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Demoness_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Demoness_\(Chaos_Demoness,_Primordial_Demoness\)) ' **Dark Magic** authority, "They" are effectively immune to any **Spells** or abilities that utilize **Shadows** or the **Shadow World**, and can alter those powers freely.
        - This also makes "Them" **Omnipotent** against creatures that reside within the **Shadow World**.
        - After completing the [Advancement Ritual](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonders#The_Advancement_Ritual "Beyonders") and advancing to **Dark Angel**, "They" will be the **Shadow** of the **Sun**, increasing "Their" resistance towards the **Sun's Purification**, making "Them" no longer be severely countered by the [Sun Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sun_Pathway) as before.
- **Degeneration** / **Depravity**: "They" have mastered the authorities of **Degeneration** domain.
    - A **Dark Angel** is able to make *people*, *objects*, *projections* and *abilities* **Corrode** and **Betray** their owner. Even a target's own body is no exception.
        - [The Evil Spirit Sasrir](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sasrir "Sasrir") **Degenerated** [Lovia](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lovia_Tiffany "Lovia Tiffany") 's body, making it **Betray** her.
        - **Profane Language**: The **Dark Angel's** voice, being both evil and sinister, carries a strong sense of **Degeneration**.
        - With "Their" use of **Profane Language**, "They" can cause accelerated life drain on targets as well as intensify the madness that is within an incomplete [Mythical Creature Form](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mythical_Creature_Form "Mythical Creature Form").
            - Using words like "Blasphemer, die!", "They" could make a **Demigod's** consciousness turn blurry, their energy evaporate, flesh wither inch by inch, and mind losing grasp over sanity.
        - **Depraved Dependents**: "They" can make released **Grazed** [Souls](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul") into temporary followers, up to 22 [Souls](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul"), but at the same time, they are affected by the **Degeneration** authority and will obey the **Dark Angel's** orders wholeheartedly as well as retain their full original abilities.
- **Darkness**: A **Dark Angel** is able to manifest an embodiment of the **Darkness** authority.
    - "They" can conjure a pitch-black, viscous "ocean" filled with an aura of **Degeneration**, swallowing enemy attacks and nullifying them. Alternatively, it can coat the enemy's surface with a layer of viscous black liquid to disrupt cognition and movement.
        - It can swallow the remaining silvery-white light of the **Silver Rapier** from a **[Silver Knight](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Twilight_Giant_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_3:_Silver_Knight)** and the **Flaring Sun** from an **[Unshadowed](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sun_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_4:_Unshadowed)**, reducing it to nothing.

### Strengthened Abilities

- **Grazing**: "They" can **Graze** up to *22 [Souls](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul")*.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- The source directly names three Authorities at this Sequence: **Shadows** (**Shadow Ruler**) → [[Shadows (Hanged Man)]]; **Degeneration**/**Depravity** (including **Profane Language** and **Depraved Dependents**) → [[Degeneration (Hanged Man)]]; **Darkness** → [[Darkness (Hanged Man)]].
- Strengthened **Grazing** → [[Grazing (Hanged Man)]].
