# Sequence 3 - Trinity Templar

## Explicit Powers

### New Abilities

- **Shadow Substitute**: It allows one's **Shadow** to take damage in place of the main body.
    - The amount of times they are able to use **Shadow Substitute** depends on whether the **Shadow** has been destroyed and whether it has recovered.

### Authorities

- **Flesh & Blood:** They gain certain manifestations of the **Flesh** and **Blood** authorities—which are subordinate to the **Mutation** authority.
    - **Trinity**: They can sprout *2 **Shadowed*** *heads* from their shoulders— *one young and one old*.
        - Each head can take away a third of the body and, through **Flesh and Blood Magic**, instantly become whole again.
        - **Flesh and Blood Immortality**: So long as there is a single cell left and so long as there is other **Flesh** and **Blood** around to be absorbed, a **Trinity Templar** can rebuild their body.
        - The premise is of course that their spirit is not destroyed.

### Strengthened Abilities

- They gain improvements to **Beyonder** powers such as **Cull of the Spirit Flesh** and prior abilities.
    - **Grazing**: The **Grazing** of a **Shepherd** reaches the level of a **Sequence 3**.
        - They can simultaneously release up to *3 [Souls](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul")*, one from each of their three heads.
                - They can **Graze** up to *13 [Souls](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul")* in total.

### Mythical Creature Form

- As a **Sequence 3 Beyonder**, they can listen and respond to prayers within a certain range.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- The source directly confirms **Flesh & Blood** as "subordinate to the **Mutation** authority" → [[Mutation (Hanged Man)]], covering **Trinity** and **Flesh and Blood Immortality**.
- **Shadow Substitute** → [[Shadows (Hanged Man)]]. Strengthened **Grazing** → [[Grazing (Hanged Man)]]. The Mythical Creature Form's prayer-response is a general Demigod trait, not an Authority.
