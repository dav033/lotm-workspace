# Sequence 7 - Shadow Ascetic

## Explicit Powers

### New Abilities

- **Summon Shadows**: **Shadow Ascetics** can summon **Shadow** creatures to influence, restrict or attack the target; however, **Summoning** a **Shadow** creature is a risky action.
    - The **Summoned Shadow** creature may be something terrifying and out of control, or it may merely be a monster that only feeds on the **Shadow Ascetic**.
- **Shadow Curse**: Using the target's **Flesh** and **Blood**, the cooperation of a [Ritual](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ritualistic_Magic "Ritualistic Magic"), or part of the target's **Shadow** as a medium, they can inflict a **Curse** on them.
- **Manipulating Shadows**: After **Lurking** in the **Shadows**, **Shadow Ascetics** can influence the target's **Shadow** in the **Shadow** **World**, and even try to capture it.
    - A **Shadow Ascetic** can envelop a target in huge black chrysalis made from **Shadows** that can contain a **[Pugilist](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Twilight_Giant_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_8:_Pugilist_\(Gladiator\))** **Rampager**.
- **Shadow Shaping**: **Shadow Ascetics** are able to directly utilize the power of **Shadows** and **Shape** those **Shadows** into weapons or animals.
    - Such **Shadow** items can cause damage that makes the target cold and **Degenerate**.

### Strengthened Abilities

- **Listen**: It is at this **Sequence** where a **Shadow Ascetic** can turn off this **Beyonder** power.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- **Summon Shadows**, **Shadow Curse**, **Manipulating Shadows**, and **Shadow Shaping** are the Pathway's first named Shadow-domain powers, directly anticipating [[Shadows (Hanged Man)]], reported as a named Authority at Sequence 1 and Sequence 0.
- Shadow Shaping's ability to make a target "**Degenerate**" anticipates [[Degeneration (Hanged Man)]].
- Strengthened **Listen** remains unattributed.
