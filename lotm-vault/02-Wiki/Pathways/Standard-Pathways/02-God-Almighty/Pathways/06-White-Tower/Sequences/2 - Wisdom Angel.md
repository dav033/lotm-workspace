# Sequence 2 - Wisdom Angel

## Explicit Powers

### Authorities

- **Wisdom**: When "They" have to make a decision, "They" will always make most correct choices at the right time due to "Their" ability to accurately foresee shifts in situations.
    - Combined with their abilities as **Prophets** and **Cognizers,** after **Cognizing** all of "Their" enemies' abilities and secrets and **Prophesying** the future, "They" are very hard to kill or to deal with.
        - The [Demoness of Gold](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Demoness_of_Gold "Demoness of Gold") did not return to the [Mirror World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mirror_World "Mirror World") or face [Xio](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Xio_Derecha "Xio Derecha") or [Derrick](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Derrick_Berg "Derrick Berg"). As the wielder of the [Eye of God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Eye_of_God "Eye of God"), she was able to always foresee shifts within a situation and make right choices at the right time, thus allowing her to evade many disasters that fell in others' way.
        - **Wisdom** also brings enhancements to abilities such as **Learning** and **Analysis**, allowing "Them" to utilize these authorities to a limited extent.
        - "They" are able to see the target's strengths and weaknesses, as well as unravel the mysteries, weaknesses, or flaws of the observed object.
        - "They" gain abilities related to the [Body of Heart and Mind](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul#Body_of_Heart_and_Mind "Soul").
        - The **Seal** on [Ludwig Phil](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ludwig_Phil "Ludwig Phil") damaged his mind, rendering him foolish.

### Strengthened Abilities

- "Their" previous **Sequence** capabilities have been improved.
    - **Imitation**: **Imitated** abilities and **Spell** effects can reach *90%* of the original's potency.

### Mythical Creature Form

- "Their" [Mythical Creature Form](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mythical_Creature_Form "Mythical Creature Form") is now complete; it is that of a phantom tower, each level composed of thick books, each book bearing a brass-colored eye. The higher the level, the darker it becomes, filled with an aura of **Madness**, **Destruction**, **Misfortune**, and **Disaster**.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- The source directly names the Authority at this Sequence: **Wisdom** → [[Wisdom (White Tower)]], which it states brings enhancements to **Learning** and **Analysis**, both later reported at Sequence 0 as partial authorities derived jointly from [[Omniscience (White Tower)]] and [[Wisdom (White Tower)]].
- Strengthened **Imitation** continues to draw on [[Omniscience (White Tower)]]; the completed Mythical Creature Form is a general trait, not an Authority.

## Potion

### Potion Properties

> A viscous, grayish-white liquid with wrinkles everywhere; multiple brass eyes are concealed within the wrinkles.

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> A grayish-white brain with densely packed brass-colored eyes and a blood-red tinge.

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- The Beyonder Characteristic of the "Dragon of Wisdom".

Or
- The Beyonder Characteristic of the Knowledge Dragon (a certain being from the Second Epoch).

Or
- A Wisdom Angel Beyonder Characteristic.

Supplementary Ingredients

- 150ml of blood from either the Dragon of Wisdom, the Knowledge Dragon, or a Wisdom Angel.
- 10,000 souls of creatures that helped enlighten the concoctor's wisdom after their natural death.
- 1,000 heartfelt titles of "teacher" or "mentor" from diverse intelligent beings (voice collected and documented in a record).
- A complete grasp of a certain period of history (has to cover at least 100 years, incorporated into the potion in the form of data).

### Advancement Ritual

Use intellect rather than strength to bring down an angel.
