# Sequence 4 - Prophet

## Explicit Powers

### Description

They begin to master higher levels of **Mystical knowledge** in the realm of **Fate**, thereby gaining the ability of “ **Prophecy" **

- **Physical Enhancement**: They gain the physical power of a **Demigod**.

### New Abilities

- **Prophecy**: They can get a relatively clear view of the most likely scenarios to happen in the future, or a vague view of the possibility that is most likely to become reality.
    - [Lucca](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lucca_Brewster "Lucca Brewster") **Prophesied** that he will be in a difficult circumstance in the future, and that one of the people he meets in the next few days ([Gehrman Sparrow](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti")) will help him deal with that situation.
        - **Demigod** -level Officials of the [Church of the God of Knowledge and Wisdom](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Church_of_the_God_of_Knowledge_and_Wisdom "Church of the God of Knowledge and Wisdom") **Prophesied** that [Lumian](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee") would appear in [Azshara](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Azshara "Azshara"), thus allowing them to make arrests in advance.
- **Astral World Mystic Arts**: **Prophets** can acquire some non-dangerous [Astral World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Astral_World "Astral World") **Knowledge**, and thus rely on this knowledge to learn or create some **Mystic Arts** that utilize [Astral World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Astral_World "Astral World") power.
    - **[Herabergen](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/God_of_Knowledge_and_Wisdom "God of Knowledge and Wisdom") 's** **Extermination of Worms Art**: Because the concept of a "Worm" is targeted, this mysticism can effectively kill various individual "Worms" within its range, such as so-and-so, so-and-so, so-and-so (sic), but does not include a collection of worms.

### Strengthened Abilities

- Their previous Sequences’ abilities have been enhanced.
    - **Imitation**: Their **Imitation** of **Spells** is at *80%* potency of the original effects now.
        - **Analyze**: They can discern **Beyonders** from ordinary people with **Analysis**.
        - They can also decipher secrets hidden within a **Seal** and invent **Spells** on the spot for their situation.
        - **Mystic Arts**: Their ability to create various **Mystic Arts** has been enhanced.
        - [Lucca Brewster](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lucca_Brewster "Lucca Brewster") **Sealed** himself to a certain extent at the critical moment so as not to instantly lose control when using his incomplete [Mythical Creature Form](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mythical_Creature_Form "Mythical Creature Form"). This **Seal** can allow them to survive for at least *7 minutes*.
                - [Lucca Brewster](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lucca_Brewster "Lucca Brewster") who was trapped underground, focused his attention on deciphering the secrets hidden within a **Seal** and used those secrets to invent **Spells** for his situation.
                - **Stiano’s Golden Armor Art**: This **Spell** shapes gold into a thin, golden armor that possesses defensive capabilities equivalent to that of a **[Guardian](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Twilight_Giant_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_5:_Guardian)**.
                - **Harps Rain of Sterility:** It renders the target infertile but can be reversed with the corresponding mystic technique or by a professional doctor.

### Mythical Creature Form

- As **Sequence 4 Demigods** they receive some level of **Godhood**, which they can use to transform into an incomplete [Mythical Creature Form](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mythical_Creature_Form "Mythical Creature Form") that consists of **Illusory Bookshelves**.
    - They look like **Illusory Bookshelves** that don't appear real. There are pools of black shadows hidden in different spots on the bookshelf as they curl and stretch like eyes from time to time.

### Notes

## Authority → Power Mapping

- **Prophecy** (foresight of future information) anticipates the past/present/future scope Sequence 0/1 confirm for [[Omniscience (White Tower)]].
- **Astral World Mystic Arts** anticipates [[Astral World Ladder]], the Astral World-scoped Authority reported at Sequence 0.
- Strengthened **Imitation**, **Analyze**, and **Mystic Arts** continue to draw on [[Omniscience (White Tower)]] and [[Wisdom (White Tower)]]. The incomplete Mythical Creature Form is a general Demigod trait, not an Authority.

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- The heart scales of an adult Brass Dragon.
- The brass vertical eye of a three-eyed Naga.

Or
- A Prophet Beyonder Characteristic.

Supplementary Ingredients

- 100 ml of blood from an adult Brass Dragon.
- 1 tooth from a three-eyed Naga.
- 10 drops of water corroded by the River of Fate or a high sequence of the Fate Pathway.
- 1 book of prophecy that has been partially fulfilled.

### Advancement Ritual

Find fragments of information about one's own future in the chaotic, overlapping spacetime of the Spirit World and make them a reality.
