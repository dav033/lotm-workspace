# Sequence 1 - Omniscient Eye

## Explicit Powers

### Authorities

- **Omniscience**: "They" are able to see the information about anyone and anything, regardless of whether it pertains to knowledge about a target's past, present, or future. The respective scope of this **Omniscience** is the monitoring of **Information**.
    - **Omniscience** can be activated on targets and situations that are nowhere near "Them", enabling "Them" to still be able peer into a target's past, present, or future information.
        - [Arrodes](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Arrodes "Arrodes"), despite being nowhere near the [Astral World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Astral_World "Astral World"), was able to hear a voice coming from deep within [Tenebrous Heaven](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Divine_Kingdoms "Divine Kingdoms") that shouted the [Sanguine Ancestor's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lilith "Lilith") name.
        - "They" are able to instantly see all the information about the state, weaknesses, and the exploitable vulnerabilities of any target.
        - When "They" detect an exploitable problem, "They" could see how to exploit it with the most appropriate way, and see how exploiting it would be manifested.
            - [Cheek](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Primordial_Demoness "Primordial Demoness") used **Omniscience** authority to know every information about [Amon's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Amon "Amon") state, and weaknesses, searching for vulnerabilities to exploit, which she found quickly.
                        - [Lumian Lee](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee"), using **Omniscience** authority, was able to instantly detect the exploitable problems and ways to worsen it within the [Malevolent Dragon](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Malevolent_Dragon "Malevolent Dragon").
        - An **Omniscient Eye's Imitated** abilities and **Spell** potency are now 100% identical to the original, but it does not involve authority.
        - As [Sasrir](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sasrir "Sasrir") ’s eyes were dyed with a brass sheen, an illusory [River](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/River_of_Fate "River of Fate") shimmering with waves of light materialized. The [River](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/River_of_Fate "River of Fate") flowed upstream as the illusory “light” that had evaporated and severed appeared once again and connected to it. The damage "He" had suffered had quickly returned to normal. It was the **Cycle of Fate**, the **Rebooting** of all things.
- **Astral World Mentor**: "They" can, in the short-term and with limited-scope, manipulate [Astral World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Astral_World "Astral World") concepts to render certain actions of a target ineffective, cause an error or amplify their impact.
    - For example, "They" can redefine the concept of **Water** in the [Sea of Collective Subconscious](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mind_World "Mind World") or **Water** in the [Chaos Sea](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chaos_Sea), making them unusable or better utilized by a **[Tyrant](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tyrant_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Tyrant)**.
        - This temporary modification of concepts can only affect one aspect of the original concept and must not involve the subject, it also can't exceed more than one aspect.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- The source directly names both Authorities at this Sequence: **Omniscience** (monitoring of Information, past/present/future) → [[Omniscience (White Tower)]]; **Astral World Mentor** is a limited-scope precursor of [[Astral World Ladder]], the fuller Astral World Authority Sequence 0 confirms.

## Potion

### Potion Properties

> A brass-colored liquid that swirls wildly, forming a vortex that resembles a giant eye.

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> A giant brass eye reflecting the illusory White Tower, which seemed to be built from countless books.

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- A Omniscient Eye Beyonder Characteristic

Supplementary Ingredients

- 100 ml of a Dark Angel's blood.
- 100 ml of a Thunder God's blood.
- 100 ml of an Author's blood.
- 100 ml of a White Angel's blood.
- (Note: All of the ingredients above can be replaced with ones of higher quality)

### Advancement Ritual

Briefly enter a semi-chaotic state, but retain basic consciousness and rationality, and take the potion in that state.

- The methods include, but are not limited to, the Chaos influence from Demoness, the corruption of the Chaos Sea, and so on.
