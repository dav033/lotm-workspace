# Sequence 5 - Mysticism Magister

## Explicit Powers

### New Abilities

- **Mystic Arts**: A **Mysticism Magister** masters various magical, diverse, unique, and rare **Mystic Arts**, and can even invent their own strange yet effective **Spells**. **Mystic Arts** are formed from the **Analysis** of **Principles**.
    - Depending on the chosen [Advancement Ritual](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/White_Tower_Pathway/Advancement#Sequence_5:_Mysticism_Magister), the area of the **Mystic Arts** created and mastered by the **Mysticism** **Magister** will vary:
        - "Teaching at least five **Mid-Sequence** **Beyonders** who have independently comprehended [Acting Method](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Acting_Method "Acting Method") " means being more adept at creating and using **Mystic Arts** in the corresponding **Mid-Sequence Beyonder's** field.
                - "Teaching at least fifty Erudites" means being well rounded in the **Mystic Arts** field, with no weaknesses but also no strengths.
        - When a **Mysticism Magister** first advances, they can naturally obtain *6 to* *13 **Mystic Arts*** from the current **Omniscience** authority holder.
        - After that, they can acquire **Mystic Arts** through learning and self-creation.
        - **Mysticism Magisters** can achieve the effect of being well-rounded with this ability.
        - So long as they have the required knowledge and materials at hand, they can make any **Spell** from healing to defense, offense, and support as **Omniscience** implies **Omnipotence**.
        - Their **Spells** include definitions such as **Uncommon**, **Unique to Oneself**, and **Distinctive**, and at the same time, their **Spells** may or may not have casting steps.
        - Unlike the **Spells** created by **[Warlocks](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hermit_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_7:_Warlock)**, **Mystic Arts** can often omit the casting materials, incantations, and most casting steps, making them faster and more secretive.
            - Of course, not all **Mystic Arts** can omit those. **Mystic Arts** with prior preparations generally either have very special effects or are very powerful.
                - By obtaining a weakened version of an ability and strengthening it through continuous **Analysis**, they can develop unique new skills through independent refinement, fusion, and deduction.
        - Some of the **Spells** and techniques they can use include but are not limited to:
        - **Mirror Curse**: [Edwina Edwards](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Edwina_Edwards "Edwina Edwards") used a bizarre but powerful spell that uses a brass mirror to reflect [Ulyssan](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ulyssan "Ulyssan") 's body parts, pinching the reflection at the mirror’s surface before pulling outward, causing **Ulyssan'** s wound to instantly worsen.
                - Theoretically, if a **Mysticism Magister** has mastered sufficient knowledge, they can reproduce the **Spells Evans Freezing Ray** and **Elemental Order** (these **Spells** are from [Cuttlefish's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Cuttlefish_That_Loves_Diving "Cuttlefish That Loves Diving") other novel, [Throne of the Magical Arcana](https://www.novelupdates.com/series/throne-of-magical-arcana/)).
            - **Evans Freezing Ray**: This is a high-precision **Freezing Spell** that emits a concentrated, colorless dim ray that **Freezes** specific targets, turning their skin into a layer of crystal **Ice** and also **Freezing** the target's surroundings.
                        - **Elemental Order**: This is a **Spell** based on the periodic law of basic elements, capable of precisely arranging electrons, allowing the them to *control and combine* elements for attack, defense or recycling.
                - **Mysticism Magisters** can use **Mystic Arts** to delay the loss of control of **Beyonders**, or to permanently or temporarily enhance their own physical abilities.

### Strengthened Abilities

- The previous **Sequences** ’ abilities have been enhanced.
    - **Imitation**: Their **Imitated** **Spells** can now have *60%* of the original effect.
        - It is possible to **Analyse** the **Principle** of an **Imitated** abilities and use the new **Knowledge** and **Principles** to create new **Mystic Arts **

## Authority → Power Mapping

- **Mystic Arts** is directly "formed from the Analysis of **Principles**" and initially granted "from the current **Omniscience** authority holder," directly evidencing [[Principles (White Tower)]] and [[Omniscience (White Tower)]] as its joint source.
- Strengthened **Imitation** continues to draw on [[Omniscience (White Tower)]] (see Sequence 6).

## Potion

### Potion Properties

> A dark red liquid with brass colored scales rising and sinking.

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> A translucent, curved "blood sausage".

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- The brain fluid of a young Brass Dragon or Brass Giant Lizard.
- The small intestine of a Shadow Naga.

Or
- A Mysticism Magister Beyonder Characteristic.

Supplementary Ingredients

- 3 scales of a young Brass Dragon or Brass Giant Lizard.
- 120 ml of blood from a Shadow Naga.
- 1 Mysticism Spell that has been conceived and verified but not yet created.
- 1 piece of a spell's notes recorded by another person (can be a copy and come from Warlock, Witch, or other Polymath or Mysticism Magister of the White Tower Pathway, etc.).

### Advancement Ritual

Train at least

- 50 Erudites or
- 5 Mid Sequence Beyonders who have independently comprehended the Acting Method.
    - The former is the all-rounder polymath direction. The latter is about which pathways of knowledge one is more proficient in depending on which pathways the Beyonders you taught are from.
