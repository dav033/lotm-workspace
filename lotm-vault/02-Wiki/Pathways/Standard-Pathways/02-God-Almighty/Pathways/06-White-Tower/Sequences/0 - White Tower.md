# Sequence 0 - White Tower

## Explicit Powers

### Symbolism

- "They" symbolize the underlying symbolism of **Knowledge leads to Calamity**.

### Authorities

- **Omniscience**: The **White Tower** has **Omniscience** over all things, which is unaffected by the [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World"). At this level, the scope of "Their" **Omniscience** is **Knowledge**.
    - As **Omniscience** implies **Omnipotence**, "They" can **Imitate** any ability, achieving the original's power, or even optimize and surpass it; however, this does not include authorities and symbols.
        - Even if the **White Tower** fully grasped the knowledge to the corresponding authorities and symbolisms, "They" cannot acquire them, only imitating their external manifestations.
            - This is because authorities and symbolisms are more like the embodiment of permissions and concepts. Without obtaining them, they cannot be used.
        - Even if the **White Tower** can see the future information, this doesn't mean "They" can see it entirely if it concerns a high level matter. For example, "They" might gain vague foresight that one would gain something unique, however, "They" might not know what it's.
        - The [God of Knowledge and Wisdom](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/God_of_Knowledge_and_Wisdom "God of Knowledge and Wisdom") had seen via **Omniscience** that [Lumian Lee](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee") would gain something rather unique, but "He" didn't know what that 'thing' would be.
            - That 'thing' was **Chaotic Vortex Face** from [God Almighty](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/God_Almighty "God Almighty") that can separate **Yin** and **Yang**.
        - **Omniscience** can be influenced by [Symbolisms](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Symbolism "Symbolism") like **Foolishness** and **Inconceivable**.
        - **Principles**: Knowing the **Principles** allows one to grasp and utilize it.
        - At this level, "They" can "open source" "Their" **Spells** to be used by ordinary people, albeit it won't be very strong and has some requirements.
            - These "open source" **Spells** require materials, incantations, [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World") resonance, **Spirituality** absorption, etc.
                - This partial authority is subordinate to the **Omniscience** authority.
                - Grasping and utilizing the **Principles** allows one to do things like:
            - The [God of Knowledge and Wisdom](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/God_of_Knowledge_and_Wisdom "God of Knowledge and Wisdom") placed [Morora](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Morora "Morora"), along with the surrounding mountains, on his stomach, with the path of exile leading to the [Astral World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Astral_World "Astral World").
                - Since [Morora](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Morora "Morora") is located within the [Astral World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Astral_World "Astral World"), the truth is **Morora** itself is also a conceptual existence, merely perceived by its inhabitants as a real, physical city.
                                - Within, abilities like **Teleportation** will only let the user **Teleport** within the mountainous area, and summoning messengers will also fail.
                                - The entrance to [Morora](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Morora "Morora") is located within the underground of the Holy Temple of Knowledge, behind a large double brass door.
- **Astral World Ladder**: "They" can influence the concept of [Astral World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Astral_World "Astral World") convergence to a certain extent.
    - "They" can influence [Astral World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Astral_World "Astral World") concepts, and while "They" can't yet completely block **Proxy**, **Sacrifice**, compliance, etc., "They" can effectively interfere with it.
        - For instance, it can cause the concept of " **Allegiance** " to briefly increase in extension or intension, causing the **[Hand of God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Twilight_Giant_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_1:_Hand_of_God) '** s allegiance to suddenly fail to meet all conditions, allowing only proxying of a portion of the originally intended power, or even fail.
            - When it comes to the **[Twilight Giant](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Twilight_Giant_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Twilight_Giant_\(Twilight,_Giant\))**, "They" can use their own methods to interfere with "Their" **Proxy**, reducing the power of the self-detonation.
        - **Astral World Ladder** also symbolizes the layers of **Knowledge** that lead to the most essential aspects of the universe as well as the most fundamental dangers.
- **Creation**: A partial authority over **Creation**, originating from the [Symbolisms](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Symbolism "Symbolism") of **Maker** and **The Omnipotent and Omniscient**.
    - The **Omniscient** **White** **Tower** can naturally **Create** anything, but it cannot escape the symbolisms and authorities that do not belong to "Them".
- **Calamity**: "They" have a partial authority over **Calamity**.
    - They are capable of inflicting **Madness** and **Destruction** onto others as certain **Knowledge** is the embodiment of those symbols.
- **Wisdom**: "They" have partial authority over **Wisdom**.
    - **Omniscience** means being able to provide the **Wisest** guidance and make the **Wisest** choices.
- **Learning**: "They" have partial authority over **Learning**, which is derived from **Omniscience** and **Wisdom**.
- **Analysis**: "They" have partial authority over **Analysis**, which is derived from **Omniscience** and **Wisdom**.
    - The **Analysis** of **Principles** is the basis of **Mystic Arts**
- **Tower of Omniscience** (**Tower of Madness**): This is "Their" **Divine Kingdom**.

### Mythical Creature Form

- An illusory **Tower**, every level was made up of thick books. Each book has a brass eye on it. The higher one looks, the darker it becomes. It is filled with the aura of insanity, destruction, inauspiciousness, and disaster. The tower extends into the sky, as though it encompasses the entire world within itself.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- Sequence 0 is the capstone: the source directly confirms all named Authorities of the Pathway.
- **Omniscience** (scope: Knowledge) → [[Omniscience (White Tower)]].
- **Astral World Ladder** → [[Astral World Ladder]].
- **Creation** → [[Creation (White Tower)]], "originating from the Symbolisms of Maker and The Omnipotent and Omniscient" — matching this note's existing symbolic derivation.
- **Calamity** → [[Calamity (White Tower)]].
- **Wisdom** → [[Wisdom (White Tower)]].
- **Learning** and **Analysis** are directly stated as partial authorities "derived from Omniscience and Wisdom" — sub-authorities of [[Omniscience (White Tower)]] and [[Wisdom (White Tower)]], not independent Authority nodes.
- **Principles** is described here as "subordinate to the Omniscience authority," while Sequence 3 ([[3 - Cognizer|Cognizer]]) describes it as its own partial authority ("gain partial authority over Principles"). Both readings are preserved from the source without resolving the nesting; [[Principles (White Tower)]] remains its own Authority note pending clarification.
- **Tower of Omniscience** (**Tower of Madness**) is directly "Their Divine Kingdom" — a domain manifestation fusing the Pathway's Authorities, not an independent Authority.

## Potion

### Potion Properties

> Glimmering white light points that spiral into a giant tower reaching to infinity, each light point seems to contain a book.

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- The White Tower Uniqueness.
- Three Omniscient Eye Beyonder Characteristics.

Supplementary Ingredients

- A tall tower filled with all the knowledge the concoctor possesses, containing relics of a Great Old Ones (relics of a Half Great Old One also count).

### Advancement Ritual

Participate in a calamity brought by an existence beyond Sequence 0 and survive (the conditions are also met by a Sefirah if the ritual performer has the knowledge of the corresponding Sefirah's Great Old One).

- The principle behind the ritual is the symbolism of "Knowledge leading to Calamity".
