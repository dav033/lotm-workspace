# Sequence 6 - Polymath

## Explicit Powers

### Description

This **Sequence** allows them to learn and understand a great deal. Because of their extensive knowledge, they become Jack-of-all-trades, able to identify, see clearly, remember, and **Imitate** abilities they've encountered.

### New Abilities

- **Analysis**: With prior knowledge, a **Polymath** can **Analyze** the **Beyonder** powers used by an opponent, quickly identify the specific situation, and understand strengths as well as weaknesses. Of course, **Analysis** can also be wrong and fail.
    - The more comprehensive their knowledge and the deeper their understanding of the target's [Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathway "Pathway") and **Beyonder** powers, the more effective their **Analysis** becomes.
        - If the **Polymath** makes errors or an incomplete **Analysis** while dissecting an ability, the ability will mutate strangely or just fail entirely.
        - The prerequisite for a **Polymath's** **Analysis** to work successfully is to have enough preexisting knowledge of the target's [Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathway "Pathway") and subsequent **Beyonder** powers.
        - **Analysis** let's them rapidly identify a target's **Beyonder** powers, allowing them to know of the target's strengths and weaknesses as well as **Imitate** more quickly.
        - Despite having only directly confronted [Jason Beria](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Jason_Beria "Jason Beria") once, [Isengard Stanton](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Isengard_Stanton "Isengard Stanton") was able to use [2-081](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/2-081 "2-081") to **Imitate** and utilize the **Beyonder** power **Danger Premonition**.
            - This case of analyzing a passive ability is attributed to the properties of [2-081](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/2-081 "2-081"), whose formation method enhanced the power of **Analysis**.
        - They can **Analyze** **Demigod** -level **Beyonder** powers.
        - They can **Analyze** **Fate** -related **Beyonder** powers.
- **Imitation**: Once sufficiently **Analyzed**, There is a chance for the **Polymath** to **Imitate** an **Analyzed** ability to a certain extent.
    - The more effective their **Analysis** is, the higher the probability that their **Imitation** succeeds. However, the higher the [Sequence](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sequence "Sequence") of the **Beyonder** power, the higher the chances of failure.
        - Even if a **Polymath** manages to **Imitate** a **Beyonder** power of the same [Sequence](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sequence "Sequence") level as them, the power they **Imitate** will always be very different from the original.
            - [Edwina Edwards](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Edwina_Edwards "Edwina Edwards") failed in her search for messengers partly because the **Imitation** of undead powers weren't enough to sign a **Contract**.
        - They can **Imitate** passive **Beyonder** powers such as a **[Devil](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Abyss_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_6:_Devil) 's** **Danger Premonition**. However, such abilities cannot be copied through observation alone and requires anatomical research.
        - They can **Imitate** even powers like **Recording**, but it requires a lot of knowledge.
                - **Polymaths** are also able to **Imitate** **Fate** -related **Beyonder** powers.
        - At the stage of a **Polymath**, as long as the corresponding knowledge is **Analyzed** and mastered, there is no limit to the number of **Spells** and abilities that can be **Imitated**.
        - However, the effect of **Imitation** will vary depending on the degree of **Analysis**, the degree of knowledge mastery in addition to the degree of potion digestion.
            - It may be *20%* at the worst, and can reach to *50%* of the original at the best.
        - They can **Imitate** powers at the **Demigod** -level, and if the knowledge is fully grasped, its effects are not much different from a **[Scribe](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Door_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_6:_Scribe) 's** **Recording**.
        - The chances of successfully **Imitating** a **Demigod** -level power is very low, and they must fully master the knowledge of the corresponding power to reach its maximum level.
                - Such **Imitations** can harm an **[Overseer](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chaos_Mist_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_4:_Overseer)** who is immune to attacks that lack godhood.
        - This is different compared to **Replication/Recording** of the [Door Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Door_Pathway).
        - Using a computer analogy, **Imitation** is equivalent to reverse-engineering the code yourself and writing a similar one, whereas **Recording** and **Replication** are equivalent to a "Copy" operation.
        - **Imitation** can't **Imitate** authorities, even when one reaches **Sequence 0**.
        - The abilities within an authority can still be **Imitated**, but they are inferior to the permission itself, meaning they will be suppressed by the permission.

### Notes

## Authority → Power Mapping

- Sequence 0 directly confirms **Analysis** as "partial authority... derived from **Omniscience** and **Wisdom**," so it maps to [[Omniscience (White Tower)]] and [[Wisdom (White Tower)]] jointly, though neither is yet named this early.
- **Imitation** is enabled by the same Omniscience-implies-Omnipotence principle Sequence 0 states directly ("As Omniscience implies Omnipotence, 'They' can Imitate any ability"); it is a manifestation of [[Omniscience (White Tower)]], not an independent Authority, and Sequence 0 directly states Imitation "can't Imitate authorities."
