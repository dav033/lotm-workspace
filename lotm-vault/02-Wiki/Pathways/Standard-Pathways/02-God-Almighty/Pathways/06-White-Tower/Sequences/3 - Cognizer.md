# Sequence 3 - Cognizer

## Explicit Powers

### Translation Note

In the original Chinese text, 规律 (guīlǜ) carries multiple meanings and in this context, can be translated as " **Laws** ", as is done in the English WebNovel translation. However, as the term " **Law** " is already used for a similar word, 律法 (lǜfǎ), and both are treated as distinct authorities, this page will consistently use a single English term (**Principles**) to represent it for clarity and distinction.
- **Laws** (律法, *lǜfǎ*):
    - Refers to codified, enforceable laws—rules established by authority, often carrying judicial or binding force. It aligns with legal systems, decrees, and formal structures of governance.
- **Principles** (规律 *guīlǜ*):
    - Refers to inherent patterns or natural laws—the underlying order of reality that exists independently of authority. It is closer to universal principles or the fundamental workings of the world.
        - In other contexts this term can also denote simpler patterns or regularity, not a strict law, but rather an observable consistency or recurring behavior over time such as everyday routines or behavioral patterns.

### Authorities

- **Principles**: "They" gain partial authority over **Principles**.
    - **Cognizing**: It is the ability to understand the rules of the world and grasp many profound mysteries, enemies standing in front of a **Cognizer** can rarely hide any secret. Everything, be it their strengths, weakness, or anything else is all laid bare for the **Cognizer** to see.
        - They **Cognize** the essence without any senses or specific forms of cognition, effectively viewing the true essence of things without relying on their senses.
            - They can use their deep insight to see through and **Analyze** certain symbols and use it to create **Spells** through a process of **Imitation**.
                - For example, **Cognizers** can look and peer into the symbols of [Lumian's Dream](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian%27s_Dream "Lumian's Dream") and derive certain **Spells** based off the symbols they have comprehended.
                - Because a **Cognizer** possesses the leverage to move the world and achieve their goals, they are often able to solve problems in a very easy way.
            - For instance, a **Cognizer** can cause a mountain to collapse, perhaps even without the ability to cause calamity or destruction, just by simply prying away a rock.
                - A **Cognizer** does not directly manipulate the **Principles**, but rather, after understanding them, employs corresponding methods to leverage these **Principles**.
            - Compared to a **[Mentor of Deceit](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_3:_Mentor_of_Deceit)** who directly deceives **Principles** and **Rules**, a **Cognizer** first engages in targeted, misleading actions before leveraging the **Principles** to accomplish **Deception** in order to achieve a similar effect.

### Mythical Creature Form

- As **Sequence 3 Beyonders**, they can now respond to prayers within a certain range.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- The source directly names the Authority at this Sequence: **Principles** ("'They' gain partial authority over Principles") → [[Principles (White Tower)]]; **Cognizing** is the direct exercise of that Authority.
- The Mythical Creature Form's prayer-response is a general Demigod trait, not an Authority.
