# Sequence 8 - Light Suppliant

## Explicit Powers

- **Knowledge (Mysticism)** — knowledge of Ritualistic Magic within the Sun Domain, including feats normally reserved for later Sequences: summoning a pure beam of Fiery Light and making Holy Water through Ritual.
- **Evil Detection** — senses the presence of undead creatures and evil entities.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- [[Light (Sun)]] → the Fiery Light summoning component of **Knowledge (Mysticism)**.
- [[Holiness (Sun)]] → **Holy Water** creation, **Evil Detection**.

## Potion

- Properties: blazing white magma.
- Beyonder Characteristic appearance: semi-solidified magma radiating a blazing white light.
- Main ingredients: a piece of Brilliance Rock or the Powder of Dazzling Soul, plus Blood of a Mirror Hedgehog or the Heart of a Magma Titan; or a Light Suppliant Beyonder Characteristic.
