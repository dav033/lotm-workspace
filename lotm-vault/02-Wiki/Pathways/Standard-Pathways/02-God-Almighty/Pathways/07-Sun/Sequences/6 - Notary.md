# Sequence 6 - Notary

## Description

Notaries indirectly benefit business through the enforceability of their Contracts, earning them wealth and status the Church of the Fool enviously calls "Fortune."

## Traits

- **Physical Enhancement** — a large boost to already-strong physical attributes, though Notaries remain primarily a support role.

## Explicit Powers

- **Notarization** — using a language that stirs the powers of nature, a Notary can Authenticate, Amplify, or Nullify:
  - **Authentication**: determines the authenticity of things such as potion formulas and enforces Contracts; once signed, a Contract cannot be violated by a Sequence 5 Beyonder and is very difficult even for a Sequence 4 Demigod to break (forceful violation carries a steep price, and liars/breakers are engulfed in burning golden flames). Unlike an Imperative Mage, the binding force comes from the witness of Gods or higher beings rather than rule-based Order.
  - **Amplification**: a valid Notarization ("God says it's effective!") temporarily enhances a targeted Beyonder power, including a Notary's own Purification/Exorcism.
  - **Nullification**: an invalid Notarization ("God says it's ineffective!") weakens or forcefully disperses a targeted Beyonder power; can affect verbal-command abilities, authorities (e.g. Mutation, Wishes), and traits (e.g. Sovereign of Spirits).
