# Sequence 3 - Justice Mentor

## Traits

- Holding a religious position (e.g. church Pope) causes prayers and praise to visibly manifest as points of holy light around the altar's Sacred Emblem, each representing an Anchor of the prayer's target of Faith.
- Can radiate pure, brilliant, holy golden light like a Sun descending on the dark regions of the Mirror World.

## Explicit Powers

- **Contract & Order** — the Justice Mentor forms a personal, idealistic set of Justice principles that defines their own Order: a Contract with themselves, the deities, and the world. Their powers only reach full potential once they comprehend their own core Justice; violating their own standard risks self-Purification or full loss of control. The Distortion authority of the Black Emperor Pathway can influence or even suppress a Justice Mentor's principles.
  - **Justice Halo**: within a 10-kilometer radius, actions aligned with the Mentor's Justice (including their own Beyonder powers and Sealed Artifacts) are greatly enhanced, while violating actions are greatly weakened or nullified; can be deactivated so allied Wraiths/Evil Spirits are not suppressed.
  - **Judgment of Justice**: single-target version, drastically weakening or nullifying any power (even an Angel's) that violates the Mentor's Justice, at twice the strength of the Justice Halo.
  - Notary and Unshadowed abilities gain new manifestations under this authority, amplifying Justice-aligned actions and weakening violations.
- **Holy Contract** — establishes Contracts so binding that even an Angel will not dare break them; enforcement is automatic even without a Sequence 0 Sun present, as an instinct of the authority itself.

## Strengthened Abilities

- **Unshadowed Domain** — fills the area with pure, warm, sacred golden light that pushes Desolation and Death auras to the edges; within it, most Demoness Pathway mystical Pathogens quickly perish, though a few ordinary disease agents may survive.
- **Holy Equipment** — no longer limited to the caster; Holy Armor can now be formed on allies remotely, temporarily suppressing the wearer's malicious thoughts and withstanding (though eventually succumbing to) a Profane Presbyter's Cull of Spiritual Flesh.

## Mythical Creature Form

- As a Sequence 3 Beyonder, can listen to prayers within a certain range.

## Potion

- Properties: intertwined yet distinct strands of dark golden light.
- Beyonder Characteristic appearance: a heart or brain condensed from pure radiance.
- Main ingredients: the head of a Radiant Giant, or a deep black tail feather of the Sun Divine Bird King plus the stamen of the All-Praiser (a giant-flower-shaped monster), or a Justice Mentor Beyonder Characteristic.
- Supplementary ingredients: blood of a Radiant Giant (100 ml), All-Praiser petals (9), a notebook recording one's own justice, one strand of sunlight each from dawn, noon, and dusk.
- Advancement ritual: clearly define one's own justice and uphold it without the slightest violation for three years.
