# Sequence 7 - Solar High Priest

## Traits

- **Physical Enhancement** — the body strengthens and gains considerable resistance to ailments and treacherous environments.

## Explicit Powers

- **Sun Halo** — a golden halo around the head enhances allied courage and Purifies Evil energies within a 20-meter distance.
- **Sun Holy Water** — imbues ordinary water so it can Exorcise Evil Spirits, drive away cold, and Purify wraiths.
- **Holy Oath** — silently reciting ancient words temporarily strengthens one's own strength, agility, fire damage, and holy damage.
- **Holy Light Summoning** — summons a holy beam of Light from the sky to Purify the undead and damage other normal targets.
- **Cleave of Purification** — imbues physical attacks, weapons, and bullets with a Purification effect especially potent against wraiths.
- **Horror Immunity** — instantly fills the body with warmth that removes Fear, working against both passive influence and active mental attacks meant to break the target's psyche.
- **Fire of Light** — instantly produces dense, golden, holy Flames at a target location, forming an ocean of light due to their density.

## Strengthened Abilities

- All Spell and sacrifice abilities within the Sun Domain are greatly enhanced.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- [[Holiness (Sun)]] → **Sun Halo**, **Sun Holy Water**, **Cleave of Purification**, **Horror Immunity** — Purification/Exorcism is directly noted to work on the category of Evil (including depravity) and, less potently but still effectively, on the undead since they are incompatible with reality rather than inherently Evil.
- [[Light (Sun)]] → **Holy Light Summoning**, **Fire of Light**.
- [[Order (Sun)]] → the oath-based enforcement underlying **Holy Oath**.

## Potion

- Properties: golden in color and scorching hot.
- Beyonder Characteristic appearance: a golden sphere with faint red streaks.
- Main ingredient: a Solar High Priest Beyonder Characteristic.
- Supplementary ingredients: a Dawn Rooster's Blood (100 ml), "Sun" essential oil (10 drops), Fingered Citron Powder (8 g), Solidified Magma (5 g).
- Consumption backlash: a burning throat, eyes lighting up like miniature suns, concentric ripples of pure light, visible black skin patterns, and feathering hair as the drinker nears losing control, before returning to normal with breath mixed with the sun's warmth and considerable resistance to ailments and harsh environments.
