# Sequence 0 - Sun

## Symbolism

- **Inextinguishable Light** — the Sun is exceedingly difficult to fully destroy; even if annihilated, revival remains possible. The Omnipotent and Omniscient (God Almighty) can also more easily return via this Pathway.

## Explicit Powers

- **Light** — wherever Light exists, the Sun exists, illuminating and destroying everything; described as the essence of Energy, the cornerstone of God Almighty's Energy symbolism.
- **Order** — a more positive, self-centered, exclusive version of Order than the Black Emperor's, deriving from the holder's own prior standard of Justice, with some overlap/intersection between the two Pathways' Order.
  - **Contracts**: a lesser authority derived from Order, able to affect verbal-command abilities, other authorities, and even traits; once signed, even a Great Old One lacking the corresponding Symbolism is severely restricted. The Sun Pathway must use language (spoken or written) to establish Order. At Sequence 0, Amplification no longer requires saying "God Says."
- **Holiness** — under this authority, all dissonance is Purified and dispelled while acts of Holiness are enhanced to their highest potential.
  - **Faith**: a lesser authority stemming from Holiness, covering Purification of body and mind and the worship of others or oneself; sufficiently fanatical belief can resist (though not fully block) a Demoness's Charm, and the Sun heavily restrains Death (the Eternal Sleeper).
- **Sun** — a lesser authority fused from Holiness and Light: can turn a childlike Sun in the Astral World into a terrifyingly blazing golden fireball able to illuminate the real and Astral Worlds together, replace Night with Day in a region and disperse Shadows, project immense Energy and ultra-high-temperature flames that Purify filth and evil, create countless miniature Suns that intertwine into an almost-real golden Sun capable of illuminating or slowly destroying an entire Solar System, and create an Eternal Daytime that Purifies filth/evil, awakens sleepers, and reveals Concealed things.

## Potion

- Properties: a star appearing to erupt in a storm.
- Main ingredients: three White Angel Beyonder Characteristics.
- Supplementary ingredients: the concept of the Sun from the Astral World.
- Advancement ritual: consume the potion in either the darkness that is the destination of all things, or in the all-encompassing color of the Chaos Sea. The former is extremely dangerous and can cause immediate death, even to an ally releasing the corresponding power; the Ancient Sun God is noted to have emerged via the latter condition, leveraging the Evernight Goddess's power during a historical divine war.
