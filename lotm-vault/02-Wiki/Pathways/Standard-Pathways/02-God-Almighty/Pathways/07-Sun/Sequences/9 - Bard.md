# Sequence 9 - Bard

## Traits

- **Physical Enhancement** — physical condition strengthens upon advancement.

## Explicit Powers

- **Singing** — imbues effects on the Bard and allies through Singing, a devotion-and-submission-based "job": courage, dispelled Fear, enhanced strength, and a degree of spiritual recovery, agility, and speed; for believers of the Eternal Blazing Sun specifically, Singing also deepens piety and improves obedience. Singing cannot produce effects beyond these.

## Authority → Power Mapping

This maps conceptual origin; it does not mean a Sequence 9 Bard owns an Authority.

- [[Holiness (Sun)]] → the faith/piety-deepening component of **Singing** for Eternal Blazing Sun believers.
- **Singing**'s physical/courage effects remain a Power without independent Authority status.
- **Physical Enhancement** remains a trait without independent Authority status.

## Potion

- Properties: a constantly churning crimson liquid.
- Beyonder Characteristic appearance: a crimson crystal that sings.
- Main ingredients: a Crystal Sunflower, an Adult Flint Bird's Tail Feather, or a Fire Bird's Tail Feather; plus a piece of Siren Rock or a Singing Sunflower; or a Bard Beyonder Characteristic.
- Supplementary ingredients: Red Wine (80 ml), a blade of Midsummer Grass (1), July Wine Juice (5 drops), a blade of Elf Dark Leaf (1).
