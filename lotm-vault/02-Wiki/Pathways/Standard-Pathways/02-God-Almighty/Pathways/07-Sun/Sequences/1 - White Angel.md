# Sequence 1 - White Angel

## Explicit Powers

- **Holiness, Order, & Faith** — partial authority over all three.
  - **Incarnation of Order**: as long as commercial activity and overall Order tied to the White Angel remain intact, they can draw power from it to alleviate injuries and maintain their condition.
  - **Holy Kingdom**: creates an illusory domain vast enough to cover a city the size of Backlund, filled with the Sun's radiance and Purification; neither Darkness nor Undead (including friendly Wraiths and Spirits) can exist within it, effectively countering and potentially nullifying the Darkness Pathway's Revival method.
  - **Servant of Faith**: Holiness leads to Faith as a subordinate authority, letting the White Angel grant themselves or followers temporary immunity to many forms of damage (Darkness, Filth, Death) through religious fervor, or gradually convert a target or group into believers of the Eternal Blazing Sun.
  - **Pure White Light**: destruction is a form of Purification and vice versa; everything except Pure White and the Sun itself will fall apart and be liberated under this Light.
- **Sun** — can theoretically serve as a Sun to illuminate the world.
  - **Solar Envoy**: emits infinite pure Light, as though suddenly becoming a Sun descended into reality; its illumination can melt an Unshadowed Spear, calm a Grazed Silver Knight's Hurricane of Light, and dim a Silver Rapier so it can only damage the Solar Envoy's aura rather than their body; the sight of it can make an Unshadowed or Black Knight bow in worship, and can quickly melt and evaporate a Grazed Silver Knight Evil Spirit.

## Potion

- Properties: a pure white, flawless, scorching liquid.
- Beyonder Characteristic appearance: a pure white human silhouette or a holy rib.
- Main ingredients: a rib of Aucuses, or a White Angel Beyonder Characteristic.
- Supplementary ingredients: 300 ml of one's own blood, one unstable miniature Sun, a symbolic artifact representing oneself as a target of faith (e.g. a Sun Sacred Emblem worshiped for over ten years above the cathedral where one serves as guardian angel), Angel blood from the Visionary, Tyrant, White Tower, or Hanged Man Pathways.
- Advancement ritual: receive sincere, exclusive praise and worship from 1,000,000 humans including oneself — higher status lowers the required number, but self-inclusion and retention of one's sense of self are mandatory.
