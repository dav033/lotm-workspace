# Sequence 5 - Priest of Light

## Explicit Powers

A Priest of Light masters divine Spells in the Sun Domain and can Purify the undead and filthy creatures within a range, acting as the nemesis of filth and the undead.

## Strengthened Abilities

- **Light of Holiness** — a stronger version of Holy Light Summoning: a pure, blazing pillar of Light descends from the sky, especially effective against the undead, vaporizing the target's body; the caster's eyes gain a Night Vision effect (two miniature suns), and the Spell is activated by leaning back with open arms as if embracing the Sun.
- **Purification Halo** — a stronger version of Sun Halo: Purifies all undead and foul creatures within a controllable range, dyeing the area golden, warming and emboldening allies, and driving away Evil.

## Potion

- Properties: a hot, golden, light-emitting liquid.
- Beyonder Characteristic appearance: a glowing, translucent crystal, a pure and bright manifestation of holiness.
- Main ingredient: a Priest of Light Beyonder Characteristic.
- Supplementary ingredients: Rosemary (5 g), Fingered Citron Juice (7 drops), Rock Water (7 drops), a King of Dawn Roosters' Blood (60 ml).
- Advancement ritual: in pure darkness, bury the entire body in ice that usually doesn't melt, then consume the potion.
- Consumption backlash: outsiders see a radiant light emerge from the ice, scattering pure light with a burning intensity, warmth, and vitality distinct from the surrounding environment.
- Acting method: dispel the darkness and praise the Sun.
