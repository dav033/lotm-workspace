# Sequence 2 - Lightseeker (Sun Chaser)

## Explicit Powers

- **Sun & Light** — partial authority over the Sun and Light.
  - **Solar Envoy**: transforms into a miniature Sun, radiating terrifying Heat and Light while unleashing devastating Purification; can instantly evaporate a 2-kilometer-radius Fog of War, naturally dissipates harm from Darkness/Cold/Filth/Corruption/Death/Decay, and lets the user reassemble their body after taking damage as Light.
  - **Praise the Sun**: brings about absolute Purification, qualitatively enhancing all of the user's own aspects.
  - Can transform into pure, sacred, massless condensed Light to fly and move at light speed, and can anchor to the Sun itself to resist disorientation and prolonged Space-Time Maze effects.

## Strengthened Abilities

- **Spear of Light** — a blazing pure white spear tipped with illusory pure white angelic feathers, which also block surrounding Space to prevent enemy escape via methods like Mirror Blink.
- **Justice Trial** — a Lightseeker who has fully defined and understood their core Justice principle can inflict 3 to 5 times the Justice Halo's damage on a target who violates that specific principle.

## Mythical Creature Form

- Becomes a complete Sunbird wreathed in golden flaming feathers, appearing as a ball of light.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- [[Sun (Authority)]] → **Solar Envoy**, **Praise the Sun**.
- [[Light (Sun)]] → light-speed/massless travel, **Spear of Light**.
- [[Order (Sun)]] → **Justice Trial**.

## Potion

- Properties: a liquid composed of converging streams of pure light.
- Beyonder Characteristic appearance: a cluster of darkness whose core contains light that cannot escape.
- Main ingredients: the heart of the Radiant Giant Leader, or a Lightseeker Beyonder Characteristic.
- Supplementary ingredients: a pair of the Radiant Giant Leader's eyes (or a Lightseeker's corresponding part), 300 ml of the Radiant Giant Leader's blood (or a Lightseeker's corresponding part), a self-transcribed holy book praising the Sun, nine types of blood from fallen clergy.
- Advancement ritual: find an item of at least Angel-rank authority with a deep mystical connection to the Sun, bind one's most important memory (closely tied to one's core justice) to it, and place it before oneself before drinking; the item acts as a beacon of direction and clarity through a melting, near-death consumption backlash, letting the drinker eventually reunite their spirit, soul, and body at a symbolic set of doors revealing the item's true nature.
