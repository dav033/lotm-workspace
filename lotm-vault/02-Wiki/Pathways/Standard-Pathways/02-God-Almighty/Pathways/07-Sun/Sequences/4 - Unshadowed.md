# Sequence 4 - Unshadowed

## Traits

- **Physical Enhancement** — evolves from an auxiliary clergyman into a comprehensive Sun warrior with qualitative gains in physique, strength, speed, and agility.
- **Intuition** — a sharp Demigod-level Intuition warns when something has gone wrong.

## Explicit Powers

- **Purification** — the Unshadowed's core power: directly Purifies Degeneration, Corruption, Corrosion, Darkness, Evil, Ailments, and related Domains without needing separate theurgical abilities. Extremely effective against wraiths, the fallen, and the unclean; makes an Unshadowed very hard to Parasitize and can melt a Silver Knight's Silver Rapier. Can Purify Beyonder Characteristics directly (including separating the highest-Sequence Characteristic out of a whole one) and rid a Sealed Artifact or Characteristic of mental pollution; can lower a Beyonder's Sequence by 1 per hour of Purification (6 hours can reduce even a Saint to a normal person), including recovering the Characteristics of a failed-but-not-dead-or-lost-control advancer so they can leave the mystical world. Can also Purify mental madness/spiritual imprints, though this carries the hidden risk of making the target more extreme.
- **Unshadowed Domain** — floods a region with bright blazing Light, removing darkness and revealing all Concealed objects, while weakening Corruption/Degeneration/Darkness powers within it; reveals Light Concealment and defends against Darkness/Evil Domain attacks. The domain has no physical barrier, so anyone can leave it or exert outside influence.
- **Flaring Sun** — instantly calls forth a huge ball of pure Light from countless sacred flames at a target location, dealing great damage to Corruption/Degeneration/Undying targets (including melting a Silver Knight's Silver Armor, though not decisively); has a large area of effect that risks friendly fire, activated by opening one's arms as if embracing a divine gift.
- **Holy Eye** — infuses the eyes with power to seek out shadows of cold, crimson, or fallen Evil through physical obstructions, shoot a Purifying burning Light, or see surrounding scenes through sunlight.
- **Holy Equipment** — condenses a golden glow into a full set of Holy Armor made of pure Light, plus a chosen Holy Weapon such as a mace of Light.
- **Unshadowed Spear** — condenses a spear of pure Sunlight for ranged strikes that explodes into a miniature Sun of brilliant Light on contact or after traveling a set distance, bright enough to illuminate a city and especially devastating against Purification-weak targets.
- **Solar Oath** — applies enhancements to oneself and allies within a certain range.
- **Pure White Ray** — condenses Light into a scorching, dazzling ray attack.

## Strengthened Abilities

- **Notarization** — massively enhanced and highly compatible with other powers, letting the Unshadowed Amplify their already strong Purification; specific proclaimed lines can now avoid ambiguity in what is being Amplified or Nullified. This particular enhancement stems from Demigod status itself rather than from an Unshadowed Beyonder Characteristic.

## Mythical Creature Form

- Incomplete Godhood grants an incomplete Sunbird form wreathed in golden flames.

## Potion

- Properties: a golden, hot, violent, impurity-free liquid.
- Beyonder Characteristic appearance: a drop of golden blood emitting the Sun's own light and heat.
- Main ingredient: an Unshadowed Beyonder Characteristic.
- Supplementary ingredients: a Sun Divine Bird's Blood (100 ml), Holy Brilliance Rock Liquid (30 ml), Mutated Fingered Citron Juice (7 drops), Magma Heart Powder (10 g).
- Advancement ritual: extract one's strongest, most treasured emotions before drinking, then have them injected back into the drinker during the ritual.
- Consumption backlash/ritual effect: the potion purifies the drinker's remaining corruption and heaviness into a purer, holier form as self-awareness and emotion are stripped away, leaving only the instinct to praise the Sun, until the extracted emotions are guided back to form a sturdy foundation that lets the drinker withstand the potion's final cleansing waves; the drinker finally opens pure white eyes.
