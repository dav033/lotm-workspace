# Sequence 5 - Ocean Songster

## Explicit Powers

### Traits

- **Aquatic Affinity**: They have more comprehensive and longer-lasting talents for underwater areas.
    - They are now able to move freely underwater for even longer periods of time.
        - Even many [Saints](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sequence "Sequence") couldn't dive into the depths to fight them.
        - They are now able to understand and communicate with aquatic animals.
        - They are now able to freely extract oxygen from the water.
        - Underwater, they fear no [Beyonder](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonders "Beyonders") below [Demigod](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sequence "Sequence") level.
- **Physical Enhancement**: When they sense an abnormality of intense danger, their heart will contract and expand like the source of a **Storm** as their blood will surge through their veins and arteries like a tidal wave.

### New Abilities

- **Lightning Manipulation**: **Ocean Songsters** have superficial control over **Lightning**, and at this level, other than **Lightning Strike**, they must use other mediums to employ **Lightning**, such as arrows.
    - **Lightning Bolt Arrow**: With the aid of a bow and arrow, an **Ocean Songster** can condense a solid arrow from **Lightning** that deals massive damage to enemies.
        - It moves at an unavoidable speed, as if it were from a god of thunder.
                - The steps involved in a **Lightning Bolt Arrow** are as follows.
            - Their hair unfurls, defying nature, each strand distinct and swirling with **Lightning**. Upon drawing a bow, the sky darkens, clouds gather, and **Lightning** dances.
                        - They then release the silver-white **Lightning** from their hair, entwining it with the arrow before releasing the string and shooting the arrow out.
                        - Dark clouds lump the sky and a thick **Lightning Bolt** descends superimposing itself on the arrow, making it completely silver white.
        - **Lightning Strike**: An **Ocean Songster** can produce silver-white electric light capable of paralysis, generating a **Lightning Strike** to smite their targets.
        - **Lightning Strikes** contain **Purification** attributes that can damage **Undead** and [Evil Spirits](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Evil_Spirits "Evil Spirits") and can be infused into weapons such as bullets, and daggers to augment them.
            - The power of an **Ocean Songster's Lightning Strike** will cause the target's body to tremble and convulse as if performing a grotesque dance as their skin will quickly char.
                - It can't be dodged—only preemptively avoided. Even if it does not hit, it will spread to nearby conductive materials in the form of countless small "electric snakes".
            - **Lightning** makes even non-humanoid individuals (such as **Shadow** creatures) be paralyzed.
                - They can cast down a **Lightning Strike** with just sight as well as from their back.
        - They are capable of transmitting sound over long distances in the form of **Lightning**, easily passing through various obstacles.
        - Upon reaching the target, the bolt of silver **Lightning** will turn back to the **Ocean Songster's** voice, relaying the message contained within.
- **Singing**: The ability to influence targets with one's voice, it varies based on the user's individual traits.
    - This ability will be different because of every **Ocean Songster's** uniqueness, producing different branching paths, which includes:
        1. Using beautiful **Singing** to interfere with the enemy's [Spirit Body](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul"), causing them to turn adrift and fall into a daze, or to enhance one's own explosive power.
                2. An **Ocean Songster** using chaotic and unpleasant **Singing** to leave the enemy frustrated, causing them to lose their rationality.
            - With [Alger Wilson's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Alger_Wilson "Alger Wilson") **Singing**, [Gehrman](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") who already stuffed his ears and converged his **Spirituality** suddenly felt his forehead’s blood vessels throb, instantly having the urge to kill the singer and destroy everything before him.
                - In addition, his mind had the feeling of being ripped apart with his muscles and vessels squirming as a result.
                3. Simulating a **Thunderous** boom to leave others in awe.
        - Blocking one's ears and converging one's **Spirituality** cannot fully eliminate the negative effects of this ability. Even a deaf person can hear it. This is because this includes an "exchange" at the spiritual level, making it difficult to defend against.
        - In addition, it is very difficult to avoid an **Ocean Songster's** **Singing** through **Paper Figurine Substitutes** within the affected area.
- **Pressure Resistance**: A **Spell** -like effect that makes them not fearful of deep sea pressure.

### Strengthened Abilities

- **Water Control**: A **Ocean Songster's** ability to control water has been greatly improved.
    - **Water Curtain**: They can create a **Water Curtain** for covering or defense.
        - They can now create enormous water spheres and corrosive rainwater.
        - They can create sticky liquid that has lubrication effects.
        - This can be used to get rid of friction , and combined with the **Wind-blessed's** ability to create an air cushion, it can make the opening of a stone door not create any sound at all.
- **Wrath**: By drawing in a gasp, their eyes can burn with rage as their muscles swell.
- **Wind Control**: Their ability as a **Wind-blessed** has been moderately enhanced.
    - **Wind Binding**: They are able to create spiraling **Winds** to **Bind** a target's actions.
        - **Flight**: An **Ocean Songster** can now **Fly** for longer periods of time.
        - They can wrap their body in a whirlwind and **Fly** through the air, avoiding a gun shot that has just been sent as well as the **Black Flame** from a **[Demoness of Pleasure](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Demoness_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_6:_Pleasure_\(Demoness_of_Pleasure\) "Demoness Pathway/Abilities")**.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- **Lightning Manipulation** (Lightning Bolt Arrow, Lightning Strike) is the precursor named ability of [[Thunder (Tyrant)]] (Electricity & Magnetism).
- **Singing** has direct textual confirmation: Sequence 0 states "the ability of Singing is also derived from this authority," identifying [[Tyranny (Tyrant)]] as its source.
- Strengthened **Water Control** → [[Water (Tyrant)]]; strengthened **Wind Control** → [[Wind (Tyrant)]]; strengthened **Wrath** → [[Tyranny (Tyrant)]].
- **Pressure Resistance** remains an unattributed supporting trait.
