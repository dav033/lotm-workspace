# Sequence 0 - Tyrant

## Explicit Powers

### Authorities

- **Matter**: This authority is partially derived from the **Maker** symbolism and intersects with the **Thunder** authority. **Matter** can be converted into **Energy**, and **Energy** can be condensed back into **Matter**, encompassing the **Electromagnetic**, **Strong**, and **Weak Forces**.
    - "They" are able to approach the speed of **Light** while retaining mass, unlike the **[Sun](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sun_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Sun "Sun Pathway/Abilities")**, who moves at the speed of **Light** without mass.
        - "They" can transform into layers of entangled ball **Lightning**, shooting down from the [Astral World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Astral_World "Astral World") at near- **Light** speed and illuminating the skies and the world below.
            - This mass of **Lightning** can tear through the void. When its explosion and shockwave unfurl, it erupts in a deafening roar that can shatter and dissipate the natural light of the [Crimson Moon](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Goddess_of_Depravity "Mother Goddess of Depravity"), leaving behind a breach.
                - "They" can accelerate a portion of **Matter** toward the upper limit of speed, creating a violent "wave" that could destroy a planet.
            - This manifests as a points of **Light** that carry a **Storm**.
        - "They" can transform into a current that briefly reaches the speed of **Light**.
- **Thunder** (**Electricity** & **Magnetism**): This authority is partially derived from the **Maker** symbolism. "They" command the forces of **Electricity** and **Magnetism**, allowing "Them" to unleash powerful **Electromagnetic Storms** capable of destroying all **Information** structures.
    - The remnants of "Their" divine power can create bolts of silver **Lightning** capable of covering the entirety of [Nois City](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Nois_City "Nois City") and fully collapsing it. However, their power is only enough to injure an [Angel](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Angels "Angels").
        - These bolts of silver **Lightning** can tear through the **Darkness** that envelops the area, as well as all the **Corruption**, **Concealment**, **Darkness**, vileness, and **Evil**. Those within the eye of the **Lightning** storm will dissipate, while terrifying, silvery-white, twisted bolts of **Lightning** snake toward the surroundings.
                - It can also summon thousands of **Lightning** bolts as thick as an anaconda, causing them to descend from the sky, smite the surrounding area, and transform it into a **Lightning** forest emitting a powerful destructive aura.
            - This **Lightning** can rapidly break down the barrier created by the black velvet 'Curtain' (**Realm of Mysteries**). Targets within it burst into ephemeral illusions under the piercing strike, becoming instantly charred black and reduced to ashes.
        - "They" can send down a house-sized ball of **Lightning**, dragging a silvery-white stream of light behind it, capable of striking and damaging a target that has transformed into a figment of **Imagination**.
        - A [Kings of Angels](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Kings_of_Angels "Kings of Angels") of the [Error Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway "Error Pathway"), possessing the [Error Uniqueness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniquenesses#Error_Pathway "Uniquenesses") and *two* **Sequence 1 Beyonder Characteristics**, can endure this by distributing the damage among "Their" **Avatars** after transforming into a figment of **Imagination** to escape the surroundings. Even then, "They" will still suffer damage that takes time to recover from. In addition, "Their" [Error Uniqueness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniquenesses#Error_Pathway "Uniquenesses") would appear to crack.
        - "They" can instantly turn the sky gloomy, as though it is filled with the indescribable wrath of nature. The night sky would instantly erupt with **Lightning** of different colors, baring fangs and brandishing claws as it expanded wantonly. It seems to envelop the entire sky in a sea of **Lightning**, with tempestuous bolts striking down like **Torrential Rain**.
        - The **Lightning** can illuminate all of [Utopia](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Utopia "Utopia") and more than half of the [Berserk Sea](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Berserk_Sea "Berserk Sea").
                - The might of the **Storm** can stun an **[Attendant of Mysteries](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fool_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_1:_Attendant_of_Mysteries "Fool Pathway/Abilities")**.
        - "They" can create countless silver-white electric snakes that intertwine into huge, flamboyant **Lightning**, capable of submerging the surroundings in a dazzling and intense **Ocean of Thunder**.
- **Tyrant**: The King of Kings. This authority encompasses **Intimidation**, **Wrath**, and a portion of **Horror**. The ability of **Singing** is also derived from this authority.
    - "They" can exude a domineering, savage, **Tyrannous**, and terrifying aura. "Their" **Storms** contain immense **Horror**.
        - The [Lord of Storms](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lord_of_Storms "Lord of Storms") generated **Thunderclaps** that awed all living beings, including [Angels](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Angels "Angels") (except [0-17](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Angel_of_Concealment "Angel of Concealment")), interrupting them and rendering them stiff.
                - "Their" **Tyrannical** aura can stop a [Chained Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chained_Pathway "Chained Pathway") **Beyonder** from **Possessing** someone, repel them, and force them to lose their **Invisibility**.
- **Water**: This authority originates from the intersection of the **Maker** and **Omnipotence** symbols. It encompasses the authority of the **Seas** and a portion of **Aquatic Life**.
    - This allows the **Tyrant** to influence any source related to the concept of **Water**, such as the [Sea of Collective Subconscious](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mind_World#Sea_of_Collective_Subconscious "Mind World"), [Chaos Sea](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chaos_Sea "Chaos Sea"), and [River of Fate](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/River_of_Fate "River of Fate").
        - Some of these, like the [River of Eternal Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/River_of_Eternal_Darkness "River of Eternal Darkness"), can only be influenced indirectly to a relatively low degree.
                - **Fog** does not fall under this authority as it can be smoke, dust, or a collection of conceptually illusory things.
        - **Seas**: The **Seas** fall under the broader **Water** authority.
        - "They" can cause the **Seas** to surge upward toward the clouds, intersecting with the **Sky**.
        - **Aquatic Life**: This is another aspect of the **Water** authority.
        - This authority can alter certain forms of **Aquatic Life**, making them more octopus-like.
- **Divine Kingdom**: "Their" **Divine Kingdom** is formed from the fusion of the **Tyrant**, **Thunder**, and **Calamity** authorities.
    - **Chasm of Storms**: This **Divine Kingdom** focuses on the **Thunder** and **Calamity** authorities.
        - **Dreadful Heaven** (**Sole Obedience to the Storm**): This emphasizes the **Tyrant** authority.
- **Calamity**: "They" possess partial authority over **Calamity**, which is subordinate to the broader authority of **Catastrophe**.
    - "They" can banish others into a never-ending **Storm**, using it to **Seal** them.
        - "They" can trigger **Earthquakes** powerful enough to destroy the [Earth](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Map_of_the_World "Map of the World").
- **Wind**: The **Wind** authority intersects with the authority of **Calamities**.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- Sequence 0 is the capstone: the source directly confirms all six named Authorities of the Pathway and their symbolic derivations.
- **Matter** → [[Matter (Tyrant)]], "partially derived from the Maker symbolism and intersects with the Thunder authority."
- **Thunder** (Electricity & Magnetism) → [[Thunder (Tyrant)]], "partially derived from the Maker symbolism."
- **Tyrant** (Intimidation, Wrath, portion of Horror, source of Singing) → [[Tyranny (Tyrant)]].
- **Water** (Seas, portion of Aquatic Life) → [[Water (Tyrant)]], "originates from the intersection of the Maker and Omnipotence symbols."
- **Calamity** → [[Calamity (Tyrant)]], "subordinate to the broader authority of Catastrophe."
- **Wind** → [[Wind (Tyrant)]], which "intersects with the authority of Calamities."
- **Divine Kingdom** (Chasm of Storms, Dreadful Heaven) is an listed fusion construct of the Tyrant, Thunder, and Calamity Authorities, not an independent Authority in its own right.

## Potion

### Potion Properties

> A massive humanoid form condensed from lightning and storms.

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- The Tyrant Uniqueness.
- Three Thunder God Beyonder Characteristics.

Supplementary Ingredients

- Blood of a God sufficient to drench the whole body.

### Advancement Ritual

- Gather over 100,000 followers. They must submit and believe in the person out of fear.
- Challenge a Sequence 0 True God and survive.
- After surviving the challenge, drink the potion under the watch of the fearful followers.
