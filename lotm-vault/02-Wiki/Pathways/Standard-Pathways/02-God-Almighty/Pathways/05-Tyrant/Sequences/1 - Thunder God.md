# Sequence 1 - Thunder God

## Explicit Powers

### New Abilities

- **Incarnation of Light**: "They" can transform "Themselves" into an energy body, capable of flying or attacking at speeds approaching, but not quite reaching, that of light.

### Authorities

- **Thunder**, **Wind**, **& Calamity**: "They" hold authority over **Thunder**, **Wind**, and **Calamities**.
    - **Heavenly Punishment**: "They" can manifest a massive bolt of **Lightning** to descend from the sky, imbued with the powers of **Disintegration**, **Purification**, and **Destruction**.
        - It is very potent against targets from the **Darkness**, **Death**, **Corruption**, and **Filth** domains.
                - It can attack the weaknesses of targets that attempt to transcend nature or matter.
            - Targets that attempts to take on a non-natural form such as by becoming a torrent of **Information** or through **Symbolization** would still be harmed. It will also prevent them from evading the **Lightning** strike by entering other domains.
                - **Heavenly Punishment** is derived from a portion of the **Thunder** authority.
        - **Storm Hell**: "They" can designate a **Sea** area, a large island, or a city cluster to be engulfed in a destructive **Storm Hell**, with the effect potentially lasting indefinitely.
        - This is derived from part of the **Calamities** authority combined with the **Wind** authority.
        - **Dance of Thunder**: A **Thunder God** is able to use invisible **Thunder** that disrupts, interferes with, and destroys **Information** and electromagnetic structures.
        - This makes it the nemesis of **Information** -based systems.
        - **Ocean of Lightning**: "They" can make a **Sea** composed of terrifying **Lightning** with intense electromagnetic effects.
        - It can be paused to a halt by a **Replicated** **Time** authority of the **[Worm of Time](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_1:_Worm_of_Time "Error Pathway/Abilities")**, but only for a moment.
                - A **Thunder God's** **Ocean of Lightning** is capable of covering an entire large island.
        - "They" can reach or even surpass other [Sequence 0](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Gods "Gods") s in the domain of **Thunder** depending on "Their" mastery over the corresponding authority.
        - Compared to a [Sequence 0](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Gods "Gods") without a similar authority, "They" are naturally stronger. Compared to a [Sequence 0](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Gods "Gods") with a similar authority, it depends on how much authority permissions the [Sequence 0](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Gods "Gods") itself has delegated.

### Strengthened Abilities

- "Their" previous **Beyonder** powers have been enhanced.
    - **Source of Wrath**: "They" can reach close to a [True Deity's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Gods "Gods") power through "Their" anger.
        - **Singing**: "Their" ability to **Roar** has been greatly enhanced.
        - **Roar of the Thunder God**: This is a sonic attack that spreads at the speed of **Lightning**, inducing **Horror**, **Intimidation**, and other effects. It is particularly effective against targets from the **Darkness**, **Death**, **Corruption**, and **Filth** domains.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- The source directly names all three Authorities held at this Sequence: **Thunder**, **Wind**, and **Calamity** → [[Thunder (Tyrant)]], [[Wind (Tyrant)]], [[Calamity (Tyrant)]].
- **Heavenly Punishment** and **Dance of Thunder** are "derived from a portion of the Thunder authority" → [[Thunder (Tyrant)]]; **Storm Hell** is "derived from part of the Calamities authority combined with the Wind authority" → [[Calamity (Tyrant)]] and [[Wind (Tyrant)]] jointly; **Ocean of Lightning** → [[Thunder (Tyrant)]].
- **Incarnation of Light** foreshadows the near-light-speed travel that Sequence 0 attributes to [[Matter (Tyrant)]], but is not itself reported as that Authority here.
- Strengthened **Source of Wrath** and **Roar of the Thunder God** → [[Tyranny (Tyrant)]].

## Potion

### Potion Properties

> Water of thunder formed by intertwining flashes of multicolored lightning.

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> A "scepter" woven of lightning.

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- A Thunder God Beyonder Characteristic

Supplementary Ingredients

- A fragment of lightning from the Astral World.
- One Depraved nature of a Hanged Man Pathway Angel.
- One stone from the underground Nightmare Swamp (Born under the influence of the "Chaos Sea", widely present underground—yet approaching it means coming dangerously close to the Chaos Sea, so High Sequence powerhouses cannot personally retrieve it).

### Advancement Ritual

Find and harness lightning to disrupt at least three Sequence 2 Angels' advancement rituals to Sequence 1 (optimal if the angels belong to the Hanged Man, White Tower, Visionary, Sun, or Tyrant Pathways).

- Interrupting a ritual to Sequence 0 will count as completing it in one go.
- Interrupting a ritual isn't limited to drinking the potion but any part of the ritual, such as blowing up the marionette town of a Miracle Invoker that want to advance to Attendant of Mysteries.
