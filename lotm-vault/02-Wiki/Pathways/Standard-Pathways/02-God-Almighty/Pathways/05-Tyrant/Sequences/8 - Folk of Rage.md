# Sequence 8 - Folk of Rage (Guardian of the Storm)

## Explicit Powers

### Traits

- **Physical Enhancement**: A **Folk of Rage's** strength, speed, and resistance are all greatly enhanced—facing them is like facing a storm.

### New Abilities

- **Wrath**: When enraged, they can release several strikes that surpass ordinary limits.
    - This greatly enhances both a **Folk of Rage's** strength and speed.
- **Raging Blow**: This is a terrifying blow delivered through accumulated anger.
    - A **Folk of Rage** must first accumulate "hits" to activate this ability.
        - Being intoxicated can aid the process of accumulating "hits".

## Authority → Power Mapping

- **Wrath** and **Raging Blow** anticipate the anger-driven Wrath component that Sequence 0 directly folds into [[Tyranny (Tyrant)]] ("This authority encompasses Intimidation, Wrath, and a portion of Horror"); no independent Authority is evidenced this early.
- **Physical Enhancement** remains an unattributed supporting trait.
