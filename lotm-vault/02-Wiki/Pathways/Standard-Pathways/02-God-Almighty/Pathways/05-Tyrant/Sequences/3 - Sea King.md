# Sequence 3 - Sea King

## Explicit Powers

### Description

Even with range limitations, within the waters they control, **Sea Kings** are undoubtedly powerful rulers, able to walk freely on the seabed, wantonly stir up **Tsunamis**, cause **Sea** levels to rise, and dominate marine life. In their home turf, they are absolutely capable of overwhelming all **Demigods** of the same level.

### Traits

- **Enhanced Mental Attributes**: They possess a potent psyche that is terrifying.
- **Aquatic Affinity**: They are able to freely walk on the **Ocean** seafloor.
- **Physical Enhancement**: They have **Sea** -monster like strength.
- **Balance**: They possess unimaginable levels of **Balance**.

### Authorities

- **Wrath of the Sea**: Part of the **Sea** authority, a **Sea King** can cause the entire sea area to repels the corresponding target, blocking and attacking them through various methods.
    - One such method are **Tsunamis**.
- **Sea Master**: Formed from the parts of the **Aquatic Life** authority and **Tyrant** authority, **Sea Kings** are equivalent to rulers of an entire expanse of the **Sea**.
    - **Sea Kings** can command many **Sea** creatures to do their bidding as well as transform themselves into any **Sea** creature, making the seabed be their home turf.
        - Their psyche is so potent that it is terrifying, and with that psyche, they are able to search for any possible abnormal spots within an area of the **Sea**.
        - This will cause external prying to be affected, turning everything indistinct.
        - A **Sea King** is able to turn the surrounding waters into a calm, windless lake, which can reflect the scenes of the past within the area.
        - When fighting in the **Ocean**, they can unleash their fullest potential and absolutely overwhelm any and all same level **Demigods**.

### Strengthened Abilities

- **Hurricane**: They can shatter **Hurricanes** into countless streaks of silvery-white **Lightning**, bursting with a destructive, violent aura that engulfs the area, forming a **Lightning Storm**.
- **Torrential Downpour**: A **Torrential Downpour** could spread a **Sea King's** **Spirituality**, making every dark cloud and every drop of water from that **Calamity** become their eyes.
    - They can quickly cover a very large area of [Calderón City's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Calder%C3%B3n_City "Calderón City") periphery with dark clouds that release bean size *raindrops* that form a catastrophic storm. Such weather heavily restricts the use of **Flame** based abilities like **Flaming Jump**.
- **Tsunamis**: With a rise in **Sequence** level, a **Sea King** is now able to set off *40* to *50 meter high* **Tsunamis** wantonly as well as raise the **Sea** level of an entire region.
    - In the port of [Bayam](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Bayam "Bayam"), the blue **Water** rose slowly but firmly, gradually approached the dyke as the city was being flooded with **Water** in many places, making the environment was greatly suppressed. Eventually the dyke collapsed, and the rising seawater poured into [Bayam City](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Bayam "Bayam").
- **Water Manipulation**: A **Sea King** can create **Destructive Droplets** through **Water**.
    - **Destructive Droplet**: By disintegrating or reorganizing **Water** droplets, a **Sea King** can release terrifying destructive power, which works by disintegrating hydrogen atoms (E = MC²).
        - This creates **Fusion** of a certain yield.
        - Even when about to die, they can cause **Water** to gush in from all directions, collapsing into a whirlpool that emits a terrifying suction force that can swallow a cargo ship.
        - This can cause many objects within a warehouse, along with the soil to be dragged into the whirlpool as even people will find themselves being flown up and dragged in.
                - Even if one uses a **Paper Figurine Substitute**, they will still soar into the air and be unable to extricate themselves from the terrifying suction force.
                - A **[Zombie](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chained_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_6:_Zombie "Chained Pathway/Abilities")** can avoid being flown around, but can't avoid being sucked in by the suction force.
                - The combination of this whirlpool and a **Hurricane** can interfere with the ignition of **Flames**.
                - The **Ice Wall** of a **[Zombie](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chained_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_6:_Zombie "Chained Pathway/Abilities")** would only last a *second* before being shredded into pieces.
                - Beyond the range of a *warehouse*, the suction force of this vortex can't affect anyone.
        - A **Sea King** would be able to rapidly make the flesh and blood of ordinary people lose moisture, to the point if becoming desiccated corpses.
- **Lightning Manipulation**: **Sea Kings** are able to create **Lightning Storms**.
    - **Lightning Storm**: They can make silver-white "electric snakes" burst forth, slashing densely at the surroundings as "branches" that envelop an area of *hundreds of meters* in a **Lightning** "forest".
        - Any creatures caught within the **Lightning Storm** will be shattered and vaporized. This is a ranged attack that doesn't distinguish between friend and foe.
                - A **Sea King's** **Lightning Storm** carries a destructive aura so intense it seems tangible, stunning enemies in place and interfering with their thinking.
            - When [Xio](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Xio_Derecha "Xio Derecha") and [Fors](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fors_Wall "Fors Wall") were searching for **Ghosts** in East Borough, they were alarmed by the sky that was lit up by a **Recorded Lightning Storm** whose twisted form and sense of horror left them trembling and not daring to look straight at it despite the distance.
                - They can generate **Lightning Storms** from the **Sky** or from thin air.
        - Even when about to die, they can release a silver ball of **Lightning** that transforms into streaks of stunning **Lightning** bolts that quickly expand outwards, occupying an entire warehouse.
        - Those outside the area will still suffer effects, their body convulsing and hair standing up.
        - **Thunderclap**: A **Sea King** can create a sonorous **Thunderclap**, one that strikes awe into one's mind and spirit. This **Thunderclap** is one that can also awe **Spirit Bodies**.
- **Wind Manipulation**: The power of a **Sea King's** **Winds** have been enhanced.
    - The speed of the **Winds** a **Sea King** can now conjure is able to break the sound barrier, creating **Sonic Booms** which could make a pirate ship fly out.
        - **Flight**: This also allows a **Sea King** to **Fly** at speeds faster than the speed of sound.
                - **Sonic Boom**: A **Sea King** can now create **Sonic Booms** at a distance.
        - They can make clouds to gather in midair, weaving into a massive sword, which can descend to stand before an ally.
- **Navigation**: A **Sea King** can never get lost.
    - This is assuming there is no superposition of other powerful factors.

### Mythical Creature Form

- As a **Sequence 3 Beyonder**, they can listen and respond to prayers within a certain range. A **Sea King's** range for responding to prayers is relatively far-reaching compared to other **Sequence 3 Beyonders**.
    - While commanding them, they can bestow **Sea** creatures (such as fishes) with remnant **Beyonder** **Spirituality** to a degree, allowing them to transmit the scenes they saw by praying to achieve a form of surveillance.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- The source directly names both Authorities at this Sequence: **Wrath of the Sea** is "part of the Sea authority" → [[Water (Tyrant)]]; **Sea Master** is "formed from the parts of the Aquatic Life authority and Tyrant authority" → [[Water (Tyrant)]] and [[Tyranny (Tyrant)]] jointly.
- Strengthened Hurricane/Torrential Downpour/Tsunamis → [[Calamity (Tyrant)]] and [[Water (Tyrant)]].
- **Destructive Droplet** (Water disintegration/fusion) foreshadows [[Matter (Tyrant)]]'s Matter-Energy conversion, formally reported only at Sequence 0; here it remains an extension of [[Water (Tyrant)]].
- Strengthened **Lightning Manipulation** (Lightning Storm) → [[Thunder (Tyrant)]]; strengthened **Wind Manipulation** (Sonic Boom, Flight) → [[Wind (Tyrant)]].
- **Navigation** remains an unattributed supporting ability; the Mythical Creature Form's prayer-response is a general Demigod trait, not an Authority.
