# Sequence 4 - Cataclysmic Interrer

## Explicit Powers

### Traits

- **Underwater Vision**: They no longer have any reading troubles caused by insufficient light.
    - Even in the dark deep sea, they can see the surrounding objects clearly.
- **Scale Armor**: Their **Illusory Scales** have strong defensive ability.
- **Spiritual Intuition**: They are sensitive to the level of a high-ranking person.

### New Abilities

- **Hurricane**: They can make violent **Winds** collide with the air, creating an explosive sound, while a terrifying **Hurricane** visible to the naked eye appears, swirling and winding.
    - The airflow of the **Hurricane** swirls in circles. If unchecked, it will suddenly rise from the ground, sweeping everything on the ground into the air while soaring into the sky is like a terrifying giant. Wherever it passes, the ground is clean and not a single thing is left behind.
        - The visible gales and howling **Winds** that a **Hurricane** causes sight-based mystical connections to be severed and obstructed.
                - The strength of a **Hurricane** depends on current potion digestion of the **Cataclysmic Interrer** and the environmental factors.\[?\]
                - A **Cataclysmic Interrer** is able to choose to control the directions of their **Hurricane** or simply let it spiral out of control.
- **Tsunami**: They are able to call forth huge **Water** waves approaching *10 meters high* to crash down on their targets, washing everything away in a powerful torrent.
    - [Alger Wilson](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Alger_Wilson "Alger Wilson") let the tidal waves he has made via a **Tsunami** envelop the **Cathedral of Waves** in a thick barrier with silver bolts of **Lightning** flashing within it.
- **Earthquake**: They can easily create an **Earthquake** on any land, and in doing so, make the land split into two and have lava erupt from within the fault line.
    - Relying on their own power to cause ground subsidence and the eruption of magma, an **Earthquake's** power will be limited to level 6 and can be concentrated in one area.
        - If the earth itself is accumulating power and there are signs of a terrifying **Earthquake**, the upper limit depends on the magnitude of the **Earthquake** brewing in the area.
        - The lava formed from an **Earthquake** is enough to cause the **Darkness** from the underground of the **Cathedral of Waves** to shrink back a little and pause for a second.
- **Torrential Downpour**: They can quickly form a **Torrential Downpour** across an extremely large area, obstructing any perception within that area.
- In addition, they can create other **Disasters** like **Snowstorms**, **Rainstorms** and **Floods**, situated in the **Domain** of **Calamities**.

### Strengthened Abilities

- **Water Manipulation**: Their ability to control **Water** has undergone a qualitative change.
    - **Water Wall**: For protection, they can make a wall formed out of blue seawater besides them.
        - This blue seawater wall can effectively slow down the **Petrification** coming from the underground chamber within the **Cathedral of Waves**.
        - They can form a silent stream of **Water** that can surge out, eroding the area.
        - **Cataclysmic Interrers** also have the ability of **Wave Manipulation**.
- **Lightning Manipulation**: They possess a stronger control and power over **Lightning**.
    - **Thunder Strike**: A **Lightning Strike** hitting their target will have more intensity and accuracy, instantly scorching them and dealing heavy **Purification** damage.
        - **Chain Lightning**: A **Cataclysmic Interrer** can create several **Lightning Bolts** at the same time, and those **Lightning Bolts** can **Chain** between targets.
        - A **Cataclysmic Interrer** can create **Lightning Calamities**.
- **Singing**: Their capabilities when singing has been enhanced qualitatively.
    - Through singing a melody of death, a **Cataclysmic Interrer** is able to influence their surroundings and disrupt the operations of [Sealed Artifacts](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sealed_Artifact "Sealed Artifact") within the vicinity.
        - [Alger Wilson](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Alger_Wilson "Alger Wilson") sang a melody of death, influencing his surroundings and disrupting the operations of the other 2 [Grade-1 Sealed Artifacts](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sealed_Artifact "Sealed Artifact") that weren't under his attention before.
        - **Roar**: A **Cataclysmic Interrer's** vocal cords have enough strength to produce a **Roar** with enough intensity to create a sound wave that can topple a ship.
- **Wind Control**: Their ability to control the **Wind** has been enhanced.
    - **Windblade Legion**: They can manifest *hundreds* to *thousands* sharp and tiny **Windblades** that move towards the target from all directions.
        - **Flight**: A **Cataclysmic Interrer** possesses real **Flight** capability now and can move at speeds of up to *500 kilometers* per hour.
        - Besides **Windblade Legion** and **Flight**, a **Cataclysmic Interrer** also possesses the **Beyonder** power **Son of the Wind**.

### Mythical Creature Form

- As **Demigods** at **Sequence 4** who have obtained partial **Godhood**, they are able to manifest their incomplete **Mythical Creature** **Form** if they disregard any of the consequences.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- **Hurricane**, **Tsunami**, **Earthquake**, **Torrential Downpour**, and the other named Disasters are directly "situated in the Domain of Calamities," confirming [[Calamity (Tyrant)]] as their source.
- Strengthened **Water Manipulation** (Water Wall, Wave Manipulation) → [[Water (Tyrant)]].
- Strengthened **Lightning Manipulation** (Thunder Strike, Chain Lightning, Lightning Calamities) → [[Thunder (Tyrant)]], with Lightning Calamities overlapping [[Calamity (Tyrant)]].
- Strengthened **Singing** (melody of death, Roar) → [[Tyranny (Tyrant)]].
- Strengthened **Wind Control** (Windblade Legion, Flight, Son of the Wind) → [[Wind (Tyrant)]].
- **Underwater Vision**, **Scale Armor**, and **Spiritual Intuition** remain unattributed supporting traits; the incomplete **Mythical Creature Form** is a general Demigod trait, not an Authority.

Supplementary Ingredients

- 500 milliliters of the whale's gastric fluid.
- One tentacle of the vortex terror-beas.
- 50 milliliters of water from the "Spring of the Elves".
- 10 drops of mucus from a deep-diver leader.
