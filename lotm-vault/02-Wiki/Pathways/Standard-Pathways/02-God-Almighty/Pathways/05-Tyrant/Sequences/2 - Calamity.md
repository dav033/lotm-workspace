# Sequence 2 - Calamity

## Explicit Powers

### New Abilities

- **Sound of Horror**: Through sound, "They" can instill extreme **Horror** in living beings and even [Sealed Artifacts](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sealed_Artifact "Sealed Artifact") within a radius of *hundreds* of *kilometers*, forcing them to obey.
    - The arrival of [Gaard II](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Gaard_II "Gaard II") spread extreme fear, as if the sound of a booming musical instrument had exploded in the hearts of every [Beyonder](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonders "Beyonders") present.

### Authorities

- **Calamity**: "They" hold partial authority over **Calamities**.
    - **Descent of Calamity**: Includes **Thunder Storms**, **Snow Storms**, **Tsunamis**, etc., which can be freely summoned, each capable of destroying a port or even an entire island.
        - "They" can summon a **Calamity** that creates an area of extreme coldness that can **Freeze** everything within the surroundings in seconds.
        - **Storm Incarnation**: "They" are able to unleash **Hurricanes** and **Storms** capable of destroying a large city. This is derived from part of the **Calamities** authority.
        - "They" can cause dark clouds within an area to quickly converge together in the sky, forming brows and a mouth, as though a face was hidden within.
            - This can be done from "Their" position in [Bayam](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Bayam "Bayam") to a mountain outside the city.

### Strengthened Abilities

- "Their" previous **Beyonder** powers have been enhanced.
    - **Source of Wrath**: **Wrath** can temporarily enhance one's power to the next **Sequence**.
        - The price of **Source of Wrath** is the abandonment of rational thought.
                - **Source of Wrath** is not able to enhance one's status.
                - It can be used on oneself or to influence a target.
        - **Lightning Manipulation**: "Their" ability to use **Lightning** has been enhanced.
        - **Magnetic Pole Pull**: "They" can create or manipulate **Magnetic Fields** to push, pull, or rotate targets. The rotation of a **Magnetic Field** itself can accumulate power.
            - "They" can cast down a thick bolt of **Lightning** out of nowhere which can illuminate the surroundings and form a **Lightning** cage that can enclose around the arm of an **Abomination** that is transferring "Their" powers away across a wide distance.
                - **Electromagnetic Disruption**: "They" can cause severe **Information** interference in a designated city cluster, large island, or corresponding **Sea** area.
            - This will render communication methods such as telegraphs ineffective.
                - "They" can send **Lightning** from "Their" position in [Bayam](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Bayam "Bayam") to a mountain outside the city.
        - **Wind Manipulation**: "Their" **Flight** speed has reached a qualitative change.
        - **Flight**: "They" can **Fly** at the speeds of a ballistic missile.
        - **Singing**: "Their" **Singing** has been greatly enhanced.
        - "They" can **Sing** an ethereal **Song**, causing targets to involuntarily recall their past, causing Indescribable grief to surge from the bottom of one's heart.
            - Within the **Dream**, [Cohinem](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Cohinem "Cohinem") used this to affect [Alger Wilson](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Alger_Wilson "Alger Wilson"), causing him to involuntarily recall the past of his mother who had long passed away, and the days when he was a bullied child, which prevented him from waking up immediately.
                - "They" can **Sing** a **Song** that spells out an extreme sense of sadness and melancholy, causing one's [Spirit Body](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul") to produce pale, non-existent arms that constantly tore at them.
            - This will cause the affected target to begin showing signs of losing control as well as have the thoughts that originally existed in their mind disrupted by the **Song** and were cut off by the pain, unable to take form any further.
                - This was also done by [Cohinem](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Cohinem "Cohinem") within [Alger Wilson's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Alger_Wilson "Alger Wilson") **Dream**.

### Mythical Creature Form

- "Their" [Mythical Creature Form](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mythical_Creature_Form "Mythical Creature Form") has become complete. "Their" [Mythical Creature Form](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mythical_Creature_Form "Mythical Creature Form") is that of a giant octopus-like creature with thick, terrifying tentacles surrounded by silver-white **Lightning**.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- The source directly names **Calamity** ("They hold partial authority over Calamities") → [[Calamity (Tyrant)]], including its Descent of Calamity and Storm Incarnation expressions.
- **Sound of Horror** anticipates the "portion of Horror" that Sequence 0 folds into [[Tyranny (Tyrant)]].
- Strengthened **Source of Wrath** and **Singing** → [[Tyranny (Tyrant)]].
- Strengthened **Lightning Manipulation**, **Magnetic Pole Pull**, and **Electromagnetic Disruption** → [[Thunder (Tyrant)]], whose Sequence 0 definition directly covers Electricity and Magnetism.
- Strengthened **Wind Manipulation**/Flight → [[Wind (Tyrant)]]; the complete Mythical Creature Form is a general Demigod trait, not an Authority.

## Potion

### Potion Properties

> Like a tornado compressed and sealed inside a bottle, accompanied by silver-white lightning.

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> A dark, dangerous cloud mass brewing with terror.

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- The Beyonder Characteristic of Queen of Calamity Cohinem (Note: Cohinem is not a pure Sequence 2 Angel of the Tyrant Pathway).

Or
- The Beyonder Characteristic of the "King of the Deep Sea".

Or
- A Calamity Beyonder Characteristic.

Supplementary Ingredients

- An item from the remains of a historic disaster.
- 600 ml of the King of the Deep Sea's or another Calamity's blood.
- 3 leaves of the Elven Ancestor Tree.
- One hair each from a thousand people who died of fear.

### Advancement Ritual

With only your own strength, trigger a natural disaster affecting at least one million people. At the peak of their fear and despair, reveal yourself and halt the disaster—this stage must also rely solely on your own power.
