# Sequence 5 - Affliction (Demoness of Affliction, Lady of Diseases)

## Explicit Powers

### Traits

- **Physical Enhancement**: A **Demoness of Affliction** can freely manipulate their hair, turning it into an effective close-combat weapon and a precise tool.
    - Their hair can pierce skin, inject **Black Flames**, and cause stiffness to the contacted area.
        - To a **Demoness**, scars aren’t a thing.
        - Their hair can be used defensively to intercept and alter the trajectory of incoming attacks.
        - [Tracy](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tracy "Tracy") used her hair to deflect [Klein](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") ’s **Air Bullets**, changing their trajectory before they could hit her.

### New Abilities

- **Disease**: A **Demoness of Affliction** can create multiple airborne pathogens that have their infection speed and the onset of diseases supernaturally accelerated.
    - The initial range is *30 meters*, going up to *50 meters* upon full digestion of the potion.
        - They can spread **Diseases** at a larger range if they utilize the wind correctly.
        - The infection rate and onset of **Disease** vary based on the constitution of their targets.
        - Once infected, the initial *10 seconds* are the incubation period, followed by mild symptoms such as allergies, fever, cough, and sneezing.
                - After *20-30 seconds*, the target will begin to experience symptoms of more dangerous illnesses like pneumonia and bronchitis.
                - After *2-3 minutes*, the disease will become critical as the target begins to experience cardiac arrest or a brain aneurysm.
        - **Flames** created by a **[Pyromaniac](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Priest_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_7:_Pyromaniac_\(Fire_Mage\))** (and other similar abilities) are able to kill most of the pathogens created by a **Demoness of Affliction**, reducing the pathogens' impact on them.
        - Undead creatures won't be infected by their **Diseases**.

### Strengthened Abilities

- **Threads**: A **Demoness of Affliction's** **Threads** have been significantly enhanced.
    - Now their **Threads** are like extensions of a **Demoness's** arms, capable of more precise and detailed actions such as wrapping around small objects and moving them.
        - Their enhanced **Threads** can precisely stimulate different parts of the target’s body, causing intense physical reactions and disrupting their ability to fight effectively.
        - Binded layer by layer on his body by invisible **Threads**, [Nikila](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Nikila "Nikila") felt the invisible **Threads** lightly caressing him, from his ears and face to his chest and inner thighs, teasing and scratching with seductive touches. This instantly sent his blood surging, his thoughts sinking deep to his nether regions.
- **Charm**: A **Demoness of Affliction** can now use their **Charm** as an active ability. A simple glance, gesture, or word from a **Demoness of Affliction** can now completely **Charm** a person.
    - When combined with showcasing their appearance, figure, and aura, an active **Charm** becomes more effective. This will most likely lead to 1 of 2 scenarios:
        1. **Charmed** enemies will hesitate to harm them and be unable to use their full strength.
                2. Some **Charmed** enemies might even give up resisting just to gain favor from them.
        - Their own appearance, aura, figure, and passive charm have all been further enhanced.
        - Repeated daily interactions and use will make a target truly fall in love with them.
- **Dark Magic**: Its capabilities have also been enhanced.
    - They can now further **Curse** their targets by reflecting a part of the target's body in a mirror and burning that mirrored region with **Black Fire**.
        - They can release a **Blue Illusory Halo** from their body, which spreads **Frost** in its wake and rapidly freezes the surrounding area, creating a frozen world covered in thick **Ice** and **Frost**.
        - Their **Invisibility** is powerful enough to temporarily evade detection from enhanced senses, such as a [Clown](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fool_Pathway/Abilities "Fool Pathway/Abilities") ’s **Danger Intuition** and spiritual perception, making it difficult for even a [Sequence 6](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sequence "Sequence") **Faceless** to locate them within a short period of time.
        - However, strong emotional fluctuations such as rage or hatred can still reveal their location through spiritual perception.
- **Mirror Magic**: They can now use the [Mirror World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mirror_World "Mirror World") to create scene illusions with pre-set **Mirrors** and **Mirror Mazes** to ensure that their enemies don't escape.
    - Their illusions made from **Mirrors** can also deceive various recording objects.
