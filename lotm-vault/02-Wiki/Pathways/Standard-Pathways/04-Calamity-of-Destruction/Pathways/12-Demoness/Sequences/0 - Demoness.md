# Sequence 0 - Demoness (Chaos Demoness, Primordial Demoness)

## Explicit Powers

**Description** "She" is the Source of **Catastrophes**, the Symbol of the **Apocalypse**, the **Demoness** wielding **Chaos**, and the Ruler of the [Mirror World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mirror_World "Mirror World"). "She" corresponds to the feminine aspect of the [Original Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Original_Creator "Original Creator") and possesses the authorities of **Primordium**, **Chaos**, **Charm**, the **Mirror World**, **Femininity**, **Dark** **Magic**, and **Disease**.

### Authorities

- **Chaos**: The core authority of the **Demoness,** governing the return of all things to **Chaos** and the **Primordial** state.
    - **Catastrophe** led to the collapse of an entire system. **Apocalypse** deepened that collapse into a total explosion of contradictions, entangling all into one. **Chaos** represented the singularity in a convergence, the conclusion after the total explosion.
        - Thus, **Chaos** could not be applied directly to a stable and balanced target. A **Catastrophic** collapse must first be created in the designated area, followed by the total explosion of contradictions to form a substantial vortex of **Chaos**. Only then could it spread outward to affect the target.
        - The **Chaos** vortex "She" creates is composed of liquid encompassing all colors, embracing all possibilities; anything drawn into it is shredded into fragments, and dissolved, becoming part of the myriad unrealized possibilities.
        - The **Demoness'** all-consuming **Chaos** vortex would be capable of consuming voices that contain or represent power, swallowing them completely in a short time; however, this is not the case in every scenario.
            - When [Amon](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Amon "Amon") used his **Stolen Wish** from [Klein](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") to briefly erase all **Envisioned** things, the **Chaos** vortex was only able to weaken the voice representing the **Wish**, unable to swallow it completely in a short time.
                        - With clench of one of "Her" arm, "She" can make the void before "Her" collapse entirely, meeting its **Apocalypse**, forming a vortex of **Chaos** that contains all colors and possibilities.
                        - "She" is able to flicker through the [Mirror World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mirror_World "Mirror World") to reappear around "Her" target in various positions, each leaving behind a **Mirror Projection**, then, by clenching "Her" right fist, all the **Mirror Projections** surrounding the target will collapse into vortices composed of an illusory liquid encompassing all colors and possibilities.
                - These dozens of chaotic vortices would rapidly expand and merge into a single mass, swallowing the target. It heralded an **Apocalypse** -a return to the **Primordial** state.
                - The **Chaos** vortex created by "Her" can also be used defensively.
            - [Primordial Demoness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Primordial_Demoness "Primordial Demoness") formed a **Chaos** vortex to shield "Herself" from **Darkness** brought by the [Evernight Goddess](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Evernight_Goddess "Evernight Goddess").
        - **Chaos** is a branch of the symbolism of destruction and endings, distinct from **Death**, **Eternal Darkness**, **Decay**, **Ruin**, **Slaughter**, or **Fragmentation**, which represent other forms of finality.
        - **Disease & Plague**: These are subordinate authorities of **Chaos** and components of **Catastrophe**.
        - "She" embodies the concept of **Disease** itself, and this concept can be attached to anything.
                - **Plague** can create various mystical pathogens that can erode [True Gods](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Gods "Gods"), and can also directly attach the concept of **Plague** to a designated area.
                - At the level of a **Demoness of Affliction**, the range of **Plague** is fixed by the [Beyonder Characteristics](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristics "Beyonder Characteristics"). However, as the [True God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Gods "Gods") of the, "She" could modify the scope of **Plague**.
        - Normally, a **Chaos Demoness** that has completed the [Advancement Ritual](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Demoness_Pathway/Advancement#Sequence_0:_Demoness_\(Chaos_Demoness,_Primordial_Demoness\)) via the [Chaos Sea](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chaos_Sea) can use that portion of the [Chaos Sea](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chaos_Sea) 's **Corruption** to slightly influence and interfere with things related to those five [Pathways](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways"), but otherwise cannot use the abilities from those five [Pathways](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways").
- **Primordium**: **Chaos** represents the end, while **Primordium** represents the beginning. As the feminine aspect of the [Original Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Original_Creator "Original Creator"), "She" holds authority over both beginning and end, which are identical to a certain extent. At the level of the [Original Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Original_Creator "Original Creator"), **Chaos** and **Primordium** merge into one.
    - At "Her" current level, the **Primordium** authority of **Sequence 0** represents **Stillness**, **Stagnation**, and **Omnipotence**, with subordinate authorities such as **Petrification**, etc.
        - The **Stillness** and **Stagnation** of **Primordium** tend toward a state in which **Time** ceases to exist. **Omnipotence** refers to various forms of **Dark Magic**, contact with **Chaos**, and the potential to invoke the [Chaos Sea](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chaos_Sea "Chaos Sea").
            - "Her" gaze is capable of instantly **Petrifying** the entire body, including the target's **Spirituality**.
        - **Dark Magic**: "She" is the Sovereign of all **Dark Magic**, which is a subordinate authority of **Primordium**. Any **Spell** that falls within the domains of **Darkness**, **Curse**, **Depravity**, or **Evil** will either become ineffective against "Her" or be enhanced and altered to "Her" advantage. This does not apply to **Spell** -like abilities derived from **Beyonder Characteristics**.
        - The **Shadow Magic** and **Flesh and Blood Magic** of the [Hanged Man Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hanged_Man_Pathway) won't be affected as they are abilities derived from **Beyonder Characteristics**.
                - A **[Warlock's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hermit_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_7:_Warlock)** own witchcraft that falls under the domains of **Darkness**, **Curse**, **Depravity**, or **Evil** counts as **Dark Magic**, as do **Imitated Spells**.
                - "She" can "open-source" "Her" **Dark Magic** for use by ordinary people, although it is not very powerful and comes with specific requirements.
            - Such forms of **Dark Magic** require materials, incantations, [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World") resonance, **Spirituality** absorption, etc.
- **Charm**: "She" possesses **Charm** at the conceptual level, exerting an extreme effect on all things by inducing infatuation, reluctance to part, and possessiveness.
    - All things become infatuated and entranced by even "Her" slightest expression and remain in perpetual ecstasy. Even rules and concepts are reluctant to constrain or oppose "Her".
        - [Cheek](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Cheek "Cheek") 's voice alone was enough to make [Lumian Lee's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee") entire body submit to "Her". "Her" smile could halt a [King of Angels'](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Kings_of_Angels "Kings of Angels") rage, and "Her" appearance could even **Charm** [The Fool](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti").
                - Just a slight look at "Her" hands invoked a full **Petrification** of [Lumian Lee](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee"), along with an outflow of "His" most primitive desires, rendering "His" mind blank.
        - "Her" **Charm** works on even [Great Old Ones](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Old_Ones "Great Old Ones"); however, there are dangers.
        - If those [Great Old Ones](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Old_Ones "Great Old Ones") desire "Her" [Uniqueness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniquenesses "Uniquenesses") or **Sequence 1 Beyonder Characteristics**, "Their" possessiveness will be amplified to madness, compelling "Them" to devour and fuse with "Her", becoming one permanently.
                - If a [Great Old One](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Old_One "Great Old One") symbolizing **Impotence** existed, the **Demoness** would be able to make "Them" stand up again.
        - If "She" were to go mad, this authority would not reach its full potential.
        - This was the case for [Primordial Demoness Cheek](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Primordial_Demoness "Primordial Demoness").
        - In comparison, the **Beauty** of the [Moon Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Moon_Pathway) is pure and sublime, something even more **Divine** than the **Charm** of the, which centers on **Femininity** and **Allure**.
- **Mirror World**: "She" has complete control over the **Mirror World** and all its derivative abilities, including **Immortality** and **Resurrection**. "She" is the master of the **Mirror World** —"She" is the **Mirror World**.
    - At this stage, "Her" **Mirror Person** merges with the self. From then on, one exists both in reality and in the **Mirror**, embodying both states.
        - Destroying all [Mirror Worlds](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mirror_World "Mirror World") would be the only way to truly kill the **Demoness**.
        - "Her" **Divine Kingdom** is tied to the [Mirror World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mirror_World "Mirror World") and has the following effects:
        - Only **Females** can exist there—even [Gods](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Gods "Gods") would acquire distinctly **Female** characteristics.
                - Without sufficient protection, one would often meet disasters intrinsically tied to oneself.
                - Gazing upon what should not be seen would immediately **Petrify** one into stone.
                - Extreme **Pleasure** sporadically manifests, making one reluctant to leave.
        - This authority counts partially as a **Space Domain** authority.
        - "She" can prevent the [Mirror World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mirror_World "Mirror World") from collapsing.
        - [Primordial Demoness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Primordial_Demoness "Primordial Demoness") stopped the collapse that was induced by Dual-Pathway God [Amon's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Amon "Amon") **[Sufferer](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Eternal_Aeon_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_3:_Sufferer)** 's re-enactment of [Mr. Fool's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") supernova.
        - **Mirror World** creatures are subject to "Her" command.
        - "She" can fuse with the [Mirror World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mirror_World "Mirror World") at any time.
        - This lets "Her" appear in any corner as "Her" voice echoes from all directions.
- **Femininity**: As a feminine aspect of the [Original Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Original_Creator "Original Creator") and representation of **Yin Energy**, "She" possesses some authority over female forces and concepts.
    - If this authority were to malfunction, all females in the universe would suffer anomalies.
        - "She" can transform living beings and inanimate objects "She" perceives into females.
        - The [Primordial Demoness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Primordial_Demoness "Primordial Demoness") altered various infants, "Her" hair, and rebellious mystical pathogens to make them real females to control and manipulate.
        - For children born from the union of the [True Gods](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/True_Gods "True Gods") of the [Demoness](#Sequence_0:_Demoness_\(Chaos_Demoness,_Primordial_Demoness\)) and [Red Priest](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Priest_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Red_Priest), it's normal for twins to be a boy and a girl. Any other outcome would be considered unusual.

### Mythical Creature Form

- "She" appears enormous, clad in a white dress made of bone. "Her" visage grows increasingly beautiful, yet from different angles exudes distinct temperaments: pure, elegant, majestic, delicate, pristine, and decadent.
    - "Her" black hair, as thick as pythons, exudes an eerie slickness. Some strands rise and rotate, their black- and-white eyes spinning; others extend forward, sprouting serpent-like heads that flick their tongues. Others coil at "Her" feet, forming a vortex of snakes. Whether one gazed directly at the **Primordial Demoness** or not, everything would react instinctively to "Her" presence.
        - Anything directly touched by these tentacles is transformed into beautiful women of varying sizes, who then run rampant and destroy all matter.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- Sequence 0 is the capstone: the source directly confirms all six named Authorities of the Pathway.
- **Chaos** → [[Chaos (Demoness)]], the "core authority," with **Catastrophe**, **Apocalypse**, and **Disease & Plague** reported as its subordinate stages/components.
- **Primordium** → [[Primordium (Demoness)]], with **Petrification** reported as a named subordinate authority.
- **Charm** → [[Charm (Demoness)]], held "at the conceptual level."
- **Mirror World** → [[Mirror World (Demoness)]], of which "She is the master... She is the Mirror World."
- **Femininity** → [[Femininity (Demoness)]].
- **Dark Magic** is directly described here as "a subordinate authority of **Primordium**," in tension with the vault's separate top-level [[Dark Magic (Demoness)]] note established from Sequence 7 onward; both readings are preserved without forcing a resolution.

## Potion

### Potion Properties

> An exquisitely charming female human skin.

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- The Demoness Uniqueness.
- Three Apocalypse Beyonder Characteristics.

Supplementary Ingredients

- One portion of genuine love originating from oneself (This is illusory, it must be transformed into something between real and illusory before being used).
- Half of the Mirror World.

### Advancement Ritual

- Altered Ritual: Descend into the depths of the earth, find the Chaos Sea, endure a degree of its corruption while maintaining oneself—neither falling nor transforming.
    - This ritual shown in the Second Blasphemy Slate was influenced by the Primordial God Almighty to be beneficial to "Him" as a resurrection plan.
- Alternative Ritual: Find a way to borrow the special traits from the Sequence 1s of five Pathways related to the Chaos Sea, combine them into a power close to the Chaos Sea, and control it.
    - This ritual was not stated in the Second Blasphemy Slate.
