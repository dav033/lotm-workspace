# Sequence 3 - Unaging (Demoness of Unaging)

## Explicit Powers

### Description

Every **Demoness of Unaging** is forever youthful, skilled in **Resurrection**, bizarrely difficult to kill, a darling of the [Mirror World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mirror_World "Mirror World"), and a true **Gorgon**.

### New Abilities

- **Mirror Self**: Upon advancing, the consciousnesses of their **Mirror Person** and real-world counterpart will fuse together, allowing them to exist both outside and inside the mirror.
    - The simplest application of their **Mirror Self** is to fix their state within one of their countless **Mirror** **Persons**, allowing them to maintain their youth forever.
        - Because of this, a **Demoness of Unaging’s** lifespan exceeds that of other **Saints** and even **Angels** from many pathways, allowing them to live for *at least 2,000 years*.
                - This can also be used on other individuals, temporarily fixing their age to match that of a chosen **Mirror Trace**, however it can only last for *no more than 30 minutes*, since the mystical connection is only established with an illusory trace in the mirror—not real nor stable.
                - This doesn't refer only to facial **Agelessness** but also encompasses one's entire state—physical, spiritual, and all other aspects.
            - This will maintain their peak state until the end of their long lifespan.
        - When their body dies, a **Mirror Person** can split off from it again and revive them. With the characteristics of a **Mirror Person**, they can now revive through that **Mirror Person**.
        - A **Demoness of Unaging** can use environments with multiple **Mirrors** to temporarily multiply their **Mirror Persons**. As long as one survives, they cannot truly die.
                - The death of either the **Demoness of Unaging's** body or the **Mirror Person** alone is no longer true death for them—it is more like a partial injury that can be healed.
                - **Concealed Resurrection**: They can use their own blood, hair, skin, or other media to create a **Mirror Person** sleeping within a **Mirror**, hiding that mirror in a secret place under **Anti-Divination** conditions, and cutting off corresponding mystical connections to prevent curses and other harm from affecting the sleeping **Mirror Person**.
            - Initially, they can only separate one **Mirror Person**. With full potion digestion, the number of **Concealed** **Mirror Persons** that can be created rises to two.
                        - The revival process through a **Dormant Mirror** would take considerable time—dozens of minutes or even hours—preventing further participation in a given battle.
                        - Through this, they can survive attacks or situations that would cause most **Sequence 3 Saints** from other [Pathways](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways") to die irreversibly.
                - All living beings that entered the [Underworld](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Underworld "Underworld") would die on the spot, however, this type of death does not affect the isolated **Dormant Mirrors**.
                                - Although a **Demoness of Unaging** and their normal **Mirror Person** would still die due to a **[Left Hand of Decay](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Second_Law_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_3:_Left_Hand_of_Decay) 's** left hand, they could revive through a **Dormant Mirror**.
                - Certain powers or situations can exploit the mystical connection between them and their **Mirror Self**, as well as their shared, connected psyche.
            - Losing control would affect both their **Mirror Self** and **Dormant Mirrors**.
                        - Through the manipulation of **Principles** and mystical connections, a **[Mentor of Deceit](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_3:_Mentor_of_Deceit)** can ensure that their destruction extends to their **Mirror Selves** and **Dormant Mirrors** alike.
                        - The **Transfiguration Curse** of a **[Disciple of Silence](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chained_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_3:_Disciple_of_Silence_\(Cursed_Artifact\))** is symmetrical, it targets both them and their **Mirror Self**. Moreover, as a defenseless animal, they would be captured, and all their hidden **Dormant Mirrors** could be uncovered through the connection between their body and their revival arrangements.
                        - The seeds of a **Mental Plague** would continue to affect them post revival, which may cause them to suffer issues or even lead to their demise.
                        - **Seeds of Malice** planted deep within their mind would also erode their **Mirror Self's** psyche, this allows a **[Bloody Archduke](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Abyss_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_2:_Bloody_Archduke)** to use these seeds to revive within the **Demoness of Unaging**, igniting their desires and burning their blood.
                        - Deeply understanding the names and information of [Great Existences](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Existences "Great Existences") would severely corrupt oneself, this is something that **Mirror Projections** can't block, and even hidden, sleeping mirrors would be affected.
        - At the **Demoness of Unaging** stage, their **Mirror Selves** cannot directly engage when battling in the real world. However, if the battlefield is in the [Mirror World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mirror_World "Mirror World") and the main body enters it, **Demonesses** can attack the enemy together with their **Mirror Selves**, essentially creating a two-against-one situation.
        - Using **Mirror Substitution**, [Clarice](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Clarice "Clarice") 's body devolved into a mirror, split apart completely, and floated down as microscopic fragments. The [Demoness of Black](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Clarice "Clarice") 's **Mirror Self** walked out and was then intercepted by [Franca](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Franca_Roland "Franca Roland") 's **Mirror Self**.

### Strengthened Abilities

- **Petrification**: A **Demoness of Unaging's** **Petrification** ability is no longer limited to their hair, but it still cannot directly **Petrify** targets from a distance.
    - They can slowly **Petrify** their surroundings, creating a plague-like area that **Petrifies** anyone and anything that touches the infested zone.
        - Alternatively, a **Demoness of Unaging** can use their spider silk as a medium for **Petrification**.
- **Charm**: Their **Charm** from the **Demoness of Pleasure** stage has been greatly strengthened.
    - They can perfectly present the **Charm** of every stage of womanhood, with the corresponding skin condition and temperament. People caught in their **Charm** find it difficult to resist.
        - When a **Demoness of Unaging's** **Charm** is used actively, it is capable of halting someone’s thoughts even if they possess considerable mental resistance and strength.
        - They can take advantage of an opponent’s emotional fluctuations to increase the effectiveness of their **Charm**, even against another **Demoness of Unaging**. This can be countered by having one’s **Mirror Person** use **Charm** on its original body, restoring clarity and resisting the opposing **Charm**.
        - Over the course of daily interactions, a **Demoness of Unaging's** **Charm** can make [Angels](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Angels "Angels") fall in love with the Demoness without "Them" realizing it.
        - Their **Charm** is effective against newly promoted **Demoness of Unaging**, since the digestion of the **Unaging** potion usually took a century. Therefore, these newly advanced might still be in an emotionally and lustfully unstable state for several years—perhaps even a decade or more—making them vulnerable to the allure of **Demonesses** of the same tier.
- **Dark Magic**: Their abilities related to **Mirrors** and **Curses** have been greatly strengthened.
    - Their **Curse** -related capabilities have been enhanced in scope and power.
        - With large-scale, special **Dark** **Magic**, necessary mediums, and proper arrangements, some of the **Curses** created by a **Demoness of Unaging** can harm [Angels](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Angels "Angels").
                - As long as there's a physical medium from the victim, a **Demoness of Unaging's** **Curses** can affect not only the target themselves but also their direct relatives.
                - As a **Demoness of Unaging's** eyes have become **Mirrors**, whatever they see can become a potential target for the **Curses** they may cast.
                - They can summon silent **Black Flames** that spread across a large area, covering every corner while selectively avoiding designated targets. The flames can be used to cleanse an area without harming those they intend to spare.
                - Given sufficient time, a **Demoness of Unaging** can freeze an area the size of Trier. Without external assistance, however, this feat is difficult to accomplish, as it requires time to spread their **Frost** throughout the entire region and can be interrupted by outside interference.
- Their **Mirror Magic** has been massively enhanced.
    - They are able to use **Mirror World Traversal** with their eyes.
        - They can directly transmit power through the [Mirror World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mirror_World "Mirror World").
        - Combined with **Mirror Projection**, **Demonesses of Unaging** can manifest their power throughout an area the size of [Trier](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Trier "Trier") using placed **Mirrors**.
            - **Mirror Projections** cannot leave their corresponding **Mirror's** reflection range.
                        - The power attached to a **Mirror Projection** cannot exceed [Sequence 4](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sequence#High-Sequence_\(Demigod\) "Sequence").
        - They can forcibly pull people into the [Mirror World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mirror_World "Mirror World").
        - [Beyonders](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonders "Beyonders") at a lower level than them in terms of [Sequence](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sequence "Sequence") will not even notice that they are being pulled into the [Mirror World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mirror_World "Mirror World").
                - When it comes to [Saints](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Saints "Saints") and [Angels](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Angels "Angels") of an equal or higher [Sequence](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sequence "Sequence") level, the chances of success and detection depend on the [Saint](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Saint "Saint") or [Angel's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Angels "Angels") current state.
                - A **Demoness of Unaging** can pull multiple houses or streets into the [Mirror World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mirror_World "Mirror World"). What gets pulled is up to them, as it can be done selectively.
            - [1-63](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/1-63 "1-63") was used by the [Nighthawks](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Nighthawks "Nighthawks") to selectively pull [Beyonders](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonders "Beyonders") into the [Mirror World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mirror_World "Mirror World") in order to deal with [Lanevus](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lanevus "Lanevus") and the [Devil Dog](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Devil_Dog "Devil Dog").
                - They can forcibly transfer a typhoon's weak points and structural flaws into the [Mirror World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mirror_World "Mirror World"), and it could be repeated multiple times quickly.
            - However, the mirror may be unable to withstand the strain, causing it almost to shatter. As such, they would need to unhesitatingly pour in a large amount of **Spirituality**, forcibly dragging that cloud wall and hurricane area into the [Mirror World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mirror_World "Mirror World"), but even then, cracks would appear on the surface of the mirror used.
        - They can make better use of **Mirrors** for surveillance.
        - They can detect everything that enters or leaves an area, even **Information** or mystical connections, and can use [Magic Mirror Divination](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Divination_Art#Magic_Mirror_Divination "Divination Art") to trace those who have left.
                - They can use **Mirrors** to transmit **Information**, such as their voice through nearby **Mirrors**, or distant scenes that can only be viewed through other **Mirrors**.
        - A **Demoness of Unaging** can control **Mirrors** within a *15-kilometer* range upon full digestion, as well as the **Mirrors** they have visited before. This can cover an entire small city.
        - **Mirror Substitution**: A **Demoness of Unaging** can now control the timing of their return to reality after activating their **Mirror Substitution**.
        - [Clarice](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Clarice "Clarice") delayed her return to reality after using **Mirror Substitution** to avoid being burned by **Fire of Destruction**.
- **Plague**: Upon full digestion, a **Demoness of Unaging's** **Plagues** can reach a range of up to *10 kilometers*.
    - They can temporarily cure some functional disorders through their control of the **Plague**.
- **Threads**: Their own control over the spider silk they produce has been enhanced slightly.

### Mythical Creature Form

- Their [Mythical Creature Form](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mythical_Creature_Form "Mythical Creature Form") develops further, with their form as a **Gorgon** becoming more complete, as their hair is now fully made of serpents, each with a clear white-and-black eye on its head.
    - In this form, a **Demoness of Unaging's** **Petrification** ability will be greatly enhanced, causing anything their serpents see to be **Petrified** instantly, as if from *thin air*.
- As a **Sequence 3 Beyonder**, they can respond to prayers within a certain range. As a result of their unified consciousness, they can manage interactions with their believers without hampering their combat effectiveness or disrupting their daily-life interactions.
    - They can respond with revelations and give blessings through **Threads**, **Dark Magic**, **Plagues**, **Petrification**, **Mirror Self**, and their other **Beyonder** abilities.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- **Mirror Self** and expanded **Mirror Magic** (Mirror World Traversal, Mirror Projection) strongly anticipate [[Mirror World (Demoness)]]; Sequence 0 directly confirms the Demoness has "complete control over the Mirror World and all its derivative abilities, including Immortality and Resurrection" — matching this Sequence's core theme.
- Strengthened **Petrification** → [[Primordium (Demoness)]]; strengthened **Charm** → [[Charm (Demoness)]]; strengthened **Dark Magic** (Curses, Black Flames, Frost) → [[Dark Magic (Demoness)]] and [[Chaos (Demoness)]]; strengthened **Plague** → [[Chaos (Demoness)]]. The Mythical Creature Form's prayer-response is a general Demigod trait, not an Authority.

## Potion

### Potion Properties

- A grayish-white liquid clearly reflecting the surrounding Mirror World.
- It has the taste of soil and stone.

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

- An exquisite, beautiful human skin.
- A fist-sized spring-like crystal.

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- 1 Heart of a Mirror God
- A pair of Gorgon Eyes

Or
- A Unaging Beyonder Characteristic.

Supplementary Ingredients

- Nine Mirror God Fragments
- 80 milliliters of Gorgon Blood
- An antique mirror over 500 years old with a corresponding mirror realm
- 22 drops of water from a drowned person's lungs

### Advancement Ritual

Find your mirror self, enslave them solely with your own power, or make them truly infatuated with you, or reconcile with them.
