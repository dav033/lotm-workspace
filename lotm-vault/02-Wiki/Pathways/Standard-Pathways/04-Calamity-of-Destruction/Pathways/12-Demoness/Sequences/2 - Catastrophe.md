# Sequence 2 - Catastrophe (Demoness of Catastrophe)

## Explicit Powers

### Description

Everyone who mentions "Their" name feels fear from the depths of their heart.

### Authorities

- **Catastrophe**: "They" possess the **Catastrophe** authority, which is subordinate to **Chaos**.**** It encompasses both natural and man-made calamities. This extends to certain authorities and abstract concepts.
    - Natural disasters like blizzards, hurricanes, tsunamis, earthquakes, meteor impacts, floods, volcanic eruptions, or even the collapse of the [Mirror World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mirror_World "Mirror World") can be brought about through **Catastrophe**.
        - [Krismona](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Krismona "Krismona") 's remnant projection instantly turned the area around the [Samaritan Women's Spring](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Underground_Trier#Samaritan_Women's_Spring "Underground Trier") into a world ruled by ice and snow, capable of freezing [Sequence 5 Beyonders](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder "Beyonder") on the spot and making the environment nearly unbearable for [Sequence 3 Beyonders](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sequence#Saints "Sequence").
                - The "Drought Demon" stopped the winds, calmed the waves, and rapidly evaporated water from the azure sea, causing the [Blue Avenger](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Blue_Avenger "Blue Avenger"), which had been sailing through fierce winds and huge waves, to suddenly plunge.
                - [Judith](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Judith "Judith") brought about the complete collapse of the surrounding [Mirror World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mirror_World "Mirror World"), causing [Lumian](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee") 's team to be swallowed by **Space-Time** turbulence. This turbulence weakened the connections between Beyonders, made perception of external coordinates imprecise, blurry, and chaotic.
                - They can evade attacks by allowing the surrounding **Mirror World** to collapse before the strike lands, becoming engulfed by the resulting **Space-Time** turbulence and re-emerging through another mirror passage elsewhere.
                - The [Calamity of Crimson](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Calamity_of_Crimson "Calamity of Crimson") manifested **Catastrophe** as the gradual collapse and disintegration of the surrounding [Mirror World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mirror_World "Mirror World"). This not only restricted the opponent’s ability to evade, but also dragged every living being within the affected area toward annihilation and oblivion.
        - Similarly, events involving societal order and mechanical constructs fall within "Their" purview.
        - These include train accidents, building collapses, horse stampedes, or accidental gun discharges.
        - When extended to authorities and abstract concepts, **Catastrophe** can manifest by inducing or exacerbating issues that destabilize a previously stable system.
        - In such cases, if a **Beyonder's** state is inherently unstable, a **Demoness of Catastrophe** can induce them to lose balance and lose control on the spot. For the target, this would undoubtedly be a **Catastrophe**.
        - This authority can manifest as **Curses of Destiny**.
        - "They" can create objects or leave behind words that, without directly contacting the target, cause those who touch the object or hear the words to encounter one **Catastrophe** after another in their subsequent lives.
        - This can be seen as an extension of the [Red Priest Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Priest_Pathway) 's **Weakness Investigation** ability, reflected in a neighboring pathway.
        - A **[Reaper](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Priest_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_5:_Reaper)** understands the structural integrity of a building and identifies the key points needed to demolish it. A **Demoness of Catastrophe**, on the other hand, can treat Beyonders, areas, or natural environments as if they were such structures or systems, detonating their hidden issues and triggering catastrophic collapse.
                - A **[Reaper](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Priest_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_5:_Reaper)** targets weaknesses from the outside in, while a **Demoness of Catastrophe** works primarily from the inside out—or combines both internal and external forces.

### Strengthened Abilities

- **Plague**: "Their" mystical pathogens can now erode and influence the bodies of complete [Mythical Creatures](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mythical_Creature_Form "Mythical Creature Form").
- **Mirror Self**: The number of **Mirror Persons** "They" can initially separate is *5*.
    - Even the black great sword burning with the **Fire of Destruction**, wielded by [Lumian](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee") after sacrificing to the [City of Calamity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/City_of_Calamity "City of Calamity") to obtain the power of **Conquest**, with a hint of sefirah-quality power, simultaneously affected both the **Mirror Person** and the real body through **Cull** and the **Sword of Destruction**. However, the isolated **Sleeping Mirrors**, having the mystical connections to them severed, remained unaffected.

### Mythical Creature Form

- "They" become a complete [Mythical Creature](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mythical_Creature_Form "Mythical Creature Form"). "Their" [Mythical Creature Form](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mythical_Creature_Form "Mythical Creature Form") is that of a **Gorgon**.
    - While in "Their" full [Mythical Creature Form](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mythical_Creature_Form "Mythical Creature Form"), "They" can instantly summon a forest of poisonous black snakes that obscures the earth, pierces the sky, and completely surrounds "Their" target.
        - As an **Angel-level Demoness**, "They" can sustain a **Divine Descent** for a minute or two without dying.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- The source directly names **Catastrophe** as an authority "subordinate to **Chaos**" → [[Chaos (Demoness)]]. No separate Authority note is created for Catastrophe since the source frames it as a Chaos sub-stage, consistent with how Twilight Giant's Protection/Devastation were treated as Combat sub-authorities.
- Strengthened **Plague** → [[Chaos (Demoness)]]; strengthened **Mirror Self** → [[Mirror World (Demoness)]]. The complete Mythical Creature Form is a general trait, not an Authority.
