# Sequence 1 - Apocalypse (Demoness of Apocalypse)

## Explicit Powers

### New Abilities

- **Mirror World**: They" are able to directly utilize the power and special traits of the [Mirror World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mirror_World "Mirror World"), and can rapidly swap reality with its **Mirror** image.
    - "They" can, for a limited time, allow a **Mirror Entity** to enter reality.
        - Even if these **Mirror Entities** were to reach the level of a [King of Angels](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/King_of_Angels "King of Angels"), a **Demoness of Apocalypse** would still be capable of controlling "Them".

### Authorities

- **Apocalypse**: The ascendant form of the **Catastrophe**,**** which is itself subordinate to **Chaos**.
    - It brings complete **Apocalypse** within a designated area, causing all things, including **Fate** itself, to become entangled and reach **Termination** in a total explosion of contradictions—something that **Catastrophe** cannot match.
        - The focus of **Catastrophe** lies in the various manifestations of disaster, while the focus of **Apocalypse** is the arrival of the final result— **Chaos** represents that ultimate outcome.
            - **Black Flames** are a manifestation of the **Chaos** authority.
                        - ⁠ **Frost** is a manifestation of the **Catastrophe** authority.
                - A **Demoness of Apocalypse** can casually trigger natural disasters on a national scale.
        - Examples of the **Apocalypse** authority in use include:
        - [Lumian Lee](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee") caused a **[Pallbearer](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_3:_Pallbearer)**, who had already been teetering on the brink of losing control due to corruption, to completely lose control by erupting his inner conflict under the influence of fate.
                - The localized **Apocalypse** initiated by [Lumian Lee](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee") caused the area around "Him" to collapse into a void, erasing all remnants except for a lone wooden coffin.
- **Pleasure**: The supreme **Pleasure** that drives people to indulge is part of the ultimate **Charm**, while the extreme emptiness and pain that follow make that **Charm** even more irresistible. Crying in **Pleasure**, sublimating in **Pleasure**, and dying in **Pleasure** —is this not a kind of **Apocalypse**?
    - **Pleasure** is subordinate to the **Charm** authority.
- **Disease**: "They" gain a portion of the **Disease** authority. No longer limited to controlling mystical pathogens, "They" can now govern all causes of **Disease**.
    - "They" cannot yet manipulate the **Mental Plague** of the [Visionary Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Visionary_Pathway), only after the authority evolves into the symbol of **Disease** does it become possible.

### Strengthened Abilities

- **Dark Magic**: "Their" mastery of **Dark Magic** reaches the level of an **Archangel** -ranked **Witch**.
    - **Curses**: "Their" **Curses** can exploit even the faintest connections.
- With the help of a [Great Old One](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Old_One "Great Old One"), a **Demoness of Apocalypse** can possess a lifespan measured in *hundreds of thousands of years*.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- The source directly names all three Authorities at this Sequence: **Apocalypse** ("the ascendant form of the Catastrophe, which is itself subordinate to Chaos") → [[Chaos (Demoness)]]; **Pleasure** ("subordinate to the Charm authority") → [[Charm (Demoness)]]; **Disease** ("a portion of the Disease authority") → [[Chaos (Demoness)]].
- **Mirror World**, named directly as a new ability, anticipates [[Mirror World (Demoness)]]. Strengthened **Dark Magic** → [[Dark Magic (Demoness)]].

### Advancement Ritual

Any one of the three rituals is effective:

  1. Advance at the universally acknowledged end of an epoch or the beginning of the next.
  2. Advance during the signs and process of the apocalypse's arrival.
  3. Advance when one's own involvement leads to the fall of a deity.
