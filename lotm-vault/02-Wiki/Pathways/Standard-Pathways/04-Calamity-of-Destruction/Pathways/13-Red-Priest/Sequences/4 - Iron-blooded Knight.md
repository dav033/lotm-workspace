# Sequence 4 - Iron-blooded Knight

## Explicit Powers

### Gender Transition

- Advancing to a **Iron-blooded Knight** results in becoming a male regardless of the original gender.

### Traits

- **Courage**: **Iron-blooded Knights** have **Courage** comparable to steel, giving them high resistance against mental attacks and ensures they are not knocked down by fear.
    - However, such **Courage** balances fear without recklessness, ensuring that they don't suffer from falling into any traps due to their enhanced confidence.
- **Physical Enhancement**: They have gained enhancements to their physical attributes that allow them to be able to fight against [Twilight Giant Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Twilight_Giant_Pathway "Twilight Giant Pathway") **Giants** or **Dragons**.

### New Abilities

- **Weaponizing**: **Iron-blooded Knights** can turn any ordinary object into a terrifying murderous weapon.
    - An ordinary fountain pen could become as dangerous as a fired cannonball. Such "weapons" will also have their corresponding durability/strength match their offensive ability.
        - Their **Augmented** objects can serve as carriers for their **Fireballs,** allowing for them to divert some power to constantly accelerate, bombing a target area from great distances.
        - An **Iron-Blooded Knight** can perform such remote bombardments from a *5 to 10 kilometer range*, presumably according to potion digestion.
            - Across such great distances, the time elapsed to its destination is enough that even **Mid-Sequence Beyonders** with strong **Spiritual** **Intuition** could detect it and escape the covered area. As such, its greatest use is to destroy fixed targets.
- **Chain of Command**: **Iron-blooded Knights** have a hidden connection with those under their command.
    - The need of a ritual for this connection depends on several things.
        - If the mental resonance is strong enough and lack of subconscious resistance is especially high, merely declaring that a target is part of their team can form a connection.
            - Otherwise, they must conduct a ritual for such a connection to be made.
        - The leader of a **Hunter** team must be a **Hunter**. If the team has multiple **Hunters**, only the highest **Sequence** leads; if the team has multiple **Hunters** at the highest **Sequence**, they determine superiority via combat.
        - There are 3 ways they can use **Chain of Command**:
        1. **Communication**: Using the connection, they can share senses between their troops.
            - While they can't exactly pass down messages to their troops, an **Iron-blooded Knight** can use the hidden connection to their troops to inform them of certain matters.
                - [Egor Einhorn](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Egor_Einhorn "Egor Einhorn") had, in the midst of his thoughts, planned on using the hidden connection between his troops to inform his adjutant to pay him a visit.
                                - [Egor Einhorn](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Egor_Einhorn "Egor Einhorn"), from hearing a sudden noise, immediately woke up every sailor and soldier in the Nepos to prepare for a sudden battle.
                        - They can share power, damage, vision, hearing, and feelings with their team.
                2. **Strength Concentration**: They can focus the power of their army onto themselves.
            - At this level, they can only **Concentrate** the **Spirituality** and overall power of their army to themselves, and not the **Beyonder** powers that their teammates may possess.
                        - When it comes to **Concentrating** the power of an army to themselves, it will cause each and every one of their **Beyonder** powers to be enhanced in terms of potency.
                3. **Alleviation**: They can spread the damage on themselves to their troops.
            - This does not only refer to spreading damage that is of a physical nature onto their troops but is also something that can be used on mental influences.
                - Through the [Sword of Courage](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sword_of_Courage "Sword of Courage"), the negative side effect of being overwhelmed by **Pleasure** was instead turned into a brief daze for [Lumian Lee](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee").
                                - [Egor Einhorn](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Egor_Einhorn "Egor Einhorn") had spread the illusory hearing damage he experienced from [Klein Moretti](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") across his troops, causing them to hear a faint buzz.
- **Galvanization**: **Iron-blooded Knights** can transform their body and their own flames into steel at will.
    - This increases the their defensive capabilities, but not to the point where their **Galvanised** form can defend from attacks that will melt or destroy steel.

### Strengthened Abilities

- **Pyrokinesis**: The heat from their flames increases exponentially, the flames taking on a bluish-white color. Such an enhancement also increases their flames' explosive strength by many factors.
    - By coating an **Augmented** item in flames to function as a **Fireball**, an **Iron-blooded Knight** can unleash a violent explosion that can destroy everything within a typical public square.
        - [Lumian Lee](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee") had used the [Sword of Courage](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sword_of_Courage "Sword of Courage") as a **Fireball** within the [Special Mirror World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Special_Mirror_Worlds "Special Mirror Worlds") the size of a plaza, causing a massive explosion that had flames burn everything within.
        - They can **Conjure** a flame sword and have it inserted in the ground beneath them, causing blazing white-blue **Fireballs** to spread layer upon layer, igniting every corner in a large area.
        - In an effort to kill [Voisin Sanson](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Voisin_Sanson "Voisin Sanson"), [Lumian Lee](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee") brought the [Sword of Courage](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sword_of_Courage "Sword of Courage") and inserted it into the ground, destroying and igniting every corner of the ruins.
        - Each strike an **Iron-blooded Knight** does that is imbued with their high-temperature bluish-white flames can cause a massive explosion and spread to a target's body.
        - [Lumian Lee](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee") demonstrated this through the strikes caused by his [Sword of Courage](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sword_of_Courage "Sword of Courage") causing massive explosions and spreading onto [Higdon](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Higdon "Higdon").
        - Their normal flames and power they possess are akin to that of high-class cannons, being capable of resisting its impact and mutually destroying each other.
        - [Egor Einhorn](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Egor_Einhorn "Egor Einhorn") had shot out various flames and **Fireballs** that intersected the trajectory of those fired cannonballs, causing them to explode prematurely mid-flight.
- **Flame Transformation**: They can now flame their body at will, allowing them to disperse into multiple flames which they can control precisely to move and then coalesce back into their body.
    - Even if most of the flames are extinguished by the enemy, it will not affect the integrity of their physical form, but instead weaken their vitality and **Spirituality** proportionally.
        - In this state, so long as the attack does not harm their spiritual consciousness, they can mostly heal physical-level injuries by devouring natural **Flames**.
- **Cull**: Their **Cull** ability has reached the **Demigod** level in terms of potency, allowing them to heavily damage **Demigods** through a well hit strike or the use of their **Beyonder** abilities.

### Mythical Creature Form

- As **Demigods** at **Sequence 4** who have obtained partial **Godhood**, they are able to manifest their incomplete **Mythical Creature** **Form** if they disregard any of the consequences and become an incomplete **Calamity Giant**.

## Potion

### Potion Properties

> Like molten iron, blazing red, high temperature, and vivid.

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> Usually fuses with an arm bone, forming an iron-black sword-shaped object, heavy, solid, and burning in its own flames.

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- A Magma Giant's core.
- A Stone of Catastrophe.

Or
- A Iron-blooded Knight Beyonder Characteristic.

Supplementary Ingredients

- 80 milliliters of boiling magma.
- 20 grams of flora powder corrupted by a Stone of Catastrophe.
- 20 grams of soil soaked in soldier's blood from a large battlefield.
- 1 acorn.

### Advancement Ritual

Form a team of at least thirty people, cultivate a deep friendship with them, make them strong one after another. The higher the strength and tacit understanding of the team, the better the effect of the ritual.
