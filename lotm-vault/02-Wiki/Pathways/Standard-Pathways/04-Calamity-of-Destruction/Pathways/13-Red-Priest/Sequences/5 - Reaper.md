# Sequence 5 - Reaper

## Explicit Powers

### Description

The **Reaper** has the highest damage output between all **Sequence 5** **Beyonders**, and from this point onward the **Red Priest Pathway** will have this advantage in every **Sequence**, however, some [Pathways](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways") have abilities with instant death effect, which the **Red Priest Pathway** doesn't have.

### Traits

- **Physical Enhancement**: Their strength, speed, and endurance have improved.
    - However, they are still not resilient enough to withstand a bullet.
- **Spirituality**: Their **Spirituality** has been enhanced to a significant degree.
- **Enhanced Mental Attributes:** A **Reaper's** thinking becomes clearer.

### New Abilities

- **Weakness Investigation**: Through supernatural means, a **Reaper** is able to discern a target’s vulnerabilities and weak points in their defenses.
    - They can also find weaknesses in certain physical structures or supernatural barriers (like the walls from a **[Guardian's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Twilight_Giant_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_5:_Guardian "Twilight Giant Pathway/Abilities") Protection**).
        - Their **Weakness Investigation** discernment also applies to non-physical bodies and weather phenomena as well.
        - This is seen with how [Lumian Lee](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee") attacked [Hisoka's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hisoka "Hisoka") **Dream Projection**.
- **Cull**: They can infuse any of their attacks with this **Beyonder** power to harvest their target's life, making each attack akin to striking an enemy's vital points and weaknesses.
    - To a certain extent, they could even inflict real damage on **Demigod** -level creatures, provided they didn’t block or successfully evade, and refrained from using mystic defenses.
        - They can damage non-physical bodies, physical structures, and phenomena with **Cull**.
            - In an attempt to complete the ritual needed to become a **Weather Warlock**, [Lumian Lee](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee") had discerned the weakness of the weather in [Berserk Sea](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Berserk_Sea "Berserk Sea") before attacking it with **Cull**.
        - If they hit a genuine weakness or vital point, it could deliver a fatal blow, making it challenging for the target to withstand three consecutive attacks.
        - This rule applies to even high defense targets like **[Guardians](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Twilight_Giant_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_5:_Guardian "Twilight Giant Pathway/Abilities")**.
            - [Anderson Hood](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Anderson_Hood "Anderson Hood") hit a weakened **Stone Golem** in the heart with an uppercut infused with **Cull**, causing it to freeze on the spot before crumbling into rubble seconds later.
- **Precision**: A **Reaper** is able to precisely target a predetermined location and carefully manipulate **Fireballs**, **Fire Ravens**, and all sorts of **Fire Spells**.
    - Through this, they can split a colossal **Fireball** into hundreds, striking different targets with accuracy, achieving effective area-of-effect damage.
- Both **Cull** and **Precision** require a substantial amount of **Spirituality**, which makes them hard to repeatedly use when in prolonged fights.
    - The exception to this rule is using the combination of **Precision** and **Fire Raven**, as **Fire Raven** could allocate a bit of **Spirituality** and is easily manipulated, making the overall consumption of a **Reaper's** **Spirituality** low.

### Strengthened Abilities

- **Pyrokinesis**: Their ability to manipulate and control flames has been enhanced.
    - **Compression**: They can **Compress** flames into a blazing white state in an incredibly short time.
        - **Conjure Flames**: They appear to be better able to **Conjure** and manipulate flames.
        - A **Reaper** is now capable of **Conjuring** their flames into a burning figure that can move and attack in a simple motion. This serves as a method of attack and deception.
- **Flame Transformation**: Ignoring the high **Spirituality** consumption, they can now merge with their flames and swiftly cover dozens to hundreds of meters.
