# Sequence 3 - War Bishop

## Explicit Powers

### New Abilities

- **War Song & War Cry**: **War Bishops** can yell out a **War Cry** or sing a **War Song** to enhance oneself and their allies and also weaken any enemies that are near them.
    - These abilities can boost the team's morale, temporarily enhance of strength, influence the enemy's mentality, and suppress the target's performance.
- **Fog of War**: A **War Bishop** can form a **Fog of War** that disrupts the senses of anyone within it and also isolates the connection of things in the inside.
    - It reduces visual range, hearing, and smell of all the living beings within, while also enveloping the corresponding [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World"), which suppresses the interaction between spirit entities and the Spirit World, preventing most escape attempts using **Spirit World Traversal** and dulling spiritual warnings and intuitions.
        - It also disrupts most forms of mystical connections from accurately targeting those within a **Fog of War**, preventing most forms of **Curses** and **Divination** from working.
        - Within the **Fog of War**, they are unaffected by anything.
- **Sacrifice**: After offering sufficient **Sacrifices**, each **War Bishop** can petition the or the **[Symbolism](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Symbolism "Symbolism") of War** for assistance once per war.
    - Through aid from the **Essence of War**, they can manifest a crimson, dripping banner emblem on their forehead that eases the conditions of **Chain of Command**.
        - This crimson, dripping banner emblem on their forehead will increase the range of connecting to a target along with lowering the difficulty of forming a mental resonance.
        - A **War Bishop** will be able to temporarily obtain the **Beyonder** powers of a **Weather Warlock**.
        - They can gain an increase in lifespan through **Sacrificial** [Rituals](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ritualistic_Magic "Ritualistic Magic").

### Authorities

- **Gathering**: The power of the high-level **Red Priest Pathway** belonged to **War**, and **War** was the art of **Gathering the Masses**, this is the authority of a **War Bishop**.
    - They could gather power from their army and impart their own power to one of their troops, effectively increasing that member's strength for a period of time.
        - The maximum range they can **Gather Strength** from others power to use for themselves or **Gather** power for their allies is *3 kilometers*.
                - Not only can they use the **Beyonder** powers of team members, but they can also share their own abilities and power with them.
            - **War Bishops** can only fuse the abilities they get from a teammate of the [Demoness Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Demoness_Pathway "Demoness Pathway"). This is a limitation that won't be broken even for the **Higher Sequences**.
                - [Franca](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Franca_Roland "Franca Roland") had shared "Her" ability of **Black Flames** with [Medici](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Medici "Medici"), allowing the latter to utilize the **Fire of Destruction** and **Sword of Destruction**.
                        - They can use the **Beyonder** powers of their team members without their consent, but the team members need the **War Bishop's** permission to use theirs.
                - When they borrow an ally's ability, the ally will temporarily lose it.
                        - If one ally borrows the **Beyonder** powers of the **War Bishop**, other allies cannot borrow other **Beyonder** powers at the same time.
                - The total amount of **Spirituality** and strength of the team remains unchanged.

### Strengthened Abilities

- **Chain of Command**: **War Bishops** are able to unleash the true strength of an army.
    - **Crowd Control**: They can fortify and connect members together, allowing them to strongly resist outside interference. This manifests as blood and rust-like beams of light that connect the group.
        - Even while [Tyrant Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tyrant_Pathway "Tyrant Pathway") **Beyonders** in the [Holy Wind Cathedral](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Church_of_the_Lord_of_Storms#List_of_Buildings "Church of the Lord of Storms") sent several giant bluish-black **Wind** **Blades** akin to surface-to-air missiles, the **War Bishop's** airships remained fine.
            - When those **Air** **Blades** were about to hit the airbags of the airship, an invisible layer of defense appeared and shielded it from all the attacks, though the transparent "wall" formed from **Crowd Control** did shake from the assault.
                        - While a **Hurricane** was formed with the help of [Tyrant Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tyrant_Pathway "Tyrant Pathway") **Beyonders** in the [Holy Wind Cathedral](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Church_of_the_Lord_of_Storms#List_of_Buildings "Church of the Lord of Storms"), the fleet of airships supported by a **War Bishop** were able to move on.
        - **Communication**: Their previous ability as an **Iron-blooded Knight** has now been enhanced, allowing a **War Bishop** to pass down messages to their own troops.
        - In addition to that, a **War Bishop** can choose to selectively shield and modify certain information within the **Communication**, which can be used in many **Conspiracies**.
- **Pyrokinesis**: Their flames are now pure azure, and the corresponding destructive power and the heat that their flames emit have been enhanced significantly.

### Mythical Creature Form

- As a **Sequence 3 Beyonder**, they can respond to prayers within a certain range.
    - The more anchors there are within a certain range from a **War Bishop**, the stronger they will be.
        - These anchors must be for the **War Bishop** for this effect to work.
                - Each of their soldiers count as anchors for the **War Bishop**.

- Note 1: The imminent atmosphere of war will attract comets symbolizing war through the spirit world. They will streak across the night sky bringing revelations, but among such comets, those with corporeal substance that can enter the real world are limited in number.
