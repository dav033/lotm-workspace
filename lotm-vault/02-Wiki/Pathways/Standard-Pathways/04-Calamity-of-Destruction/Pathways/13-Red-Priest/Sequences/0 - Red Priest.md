# Sequence 0 - Red Priest

## Explicit Powers

### Description

" **Red** " represents the color of **War**, and " **Priest** " can be understood as a ritual of strength.

### Symbolism

- **Lord of War**: A symbolic extension of the **War** authority that lets "Him" and "His" teammates share "Their" power, rank, and the power of the [Uniqueness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniqueness "Uniqueness") across an entire planet simultaneously.
    - Only one team member can use the [Uniqueness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniqueness "Uniqueness") at any given moment. If "His" subordinate uses it, their insufficient foundation prevents them from unleashing its full might.
        - A single team member can share their **Beyonder** powers with everyone else.
        - Through the use of [0-01](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/0-01 "0-01"), [Lumian Lee](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee") shared the **Blink** ability from a **Boon** [Angel](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Angel "Angel") soldier of the [Door Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Door_Pathway "Door Pathway") with everyone within "His" entire army.
        - "He" can freely exchange positions with "His" team at any time.

### Authorities

- **Conquest**: This authority can extend to territories and even certain abstract concepts.
    - **Conquest** can only make others soldiers and then utilize their abilities; it cannot directly seize the abilities of others. In theory, it could lead to omniscience and omnipotence.
        - If "He" were to **Conquer** a [True God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/True_God "True God"), "He" would be able to utilize all of "Their" authorities, concepts, and symbols.
            - When **Conquering** lower-status beings, even if they resist **Conquest,** their **Beyonder Characteristics** may still rebel against them, causing them to lose those **Beyonder** **Characteristics**.
                - Due to [Alista Tudor's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Alista_Tudor "Alista Tudor") **Conquest,** [Azik Eggers](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Azik_Eggers "Azik Eggers") lost an entire set of **Characteristics**; only the extra parts remained.
                        - **Pathways** that possess a strong dominance over both others and themselves, such as the [Death Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway "Death Pathway"), can resist **Conquest** to a certain degree.
                - "They" are able to a make [Sealed Artifact](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sealed_Artifact "Sealed Artifact") enter a **Conquered** state, causing its negative effects to be temporarily cancelled.
        - "His" **Divine Kingdom** is formed through completely **Conquering** a land, battlefield, or special area. This is a deeper conceptualization of **Conquest**.
        - Any living being that enters the **Divine Kingdom** without adequate protection or rank will immediately become a soldier of the **Red Priest**.
        - The aura of **Conquest** the **Red Priest** exudes is so overwhelming that even [True Deities](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Gods "Gods") may bow "Their" heads down to "Him".
        - The emergence of [Lumian Lee's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee") hands from the **Door of Summoning** caused a group of [Sanguines](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sanguines "Sanguines") to submit and bow their heads involuntarily.
- **War**: Even without personally starting a **War**, "He" can cause **War** to break out in a target area through a mere gaze or presence, controlling its scale, intensity, and nature.
    - **Courage**: A subsidiary authority of **War** —the **Courage** of the mind means no fear, and the **Courage** of the body is being extremely difficult to destroy.
        - **Fog of War**: At the [True Deity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Gods "Gods") level, the **Red Priest's Fog of War** should now be able to affect even the [Astral World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Astral_World "Astral World").
- **Destruction**: At its highest level, **Destruction** can shatter an entire planet. At its lowest level, it can kill a [True God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/True_God "True God"). It also includes the ability to detect weaknesses and lock onto targets.
    - Unlike **Devastation** that is achieved through the actions of combat and is supplementary to **Combat**, **Destruction** is both an action and a state of being on its own.
        - While **Combat** and **Destruction** are equal in terms of [Sequence 0](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sequence_0 "Sequence 0") authorities, **Combat** is inferior to the symbolism of **Destruction**.
        - **Destruction** can be achieved through combat or quickening the natural course of an event/being unto its end, or even as a form of the state of finality.
        - Those killed by the authority of **Destruction** cannot be **Resurrected** without special, advanced arrangements, and will otherwise face their **Termination**.
                - The authority of **Destruction** is far more formidable than **Devastation**, as it directly imposes the state of **Destruction** on a conceptual level.
                - The attack of an [activated](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Activation "Activation") [Uniqueness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniquenesses "Uniquenesses") of the **Red Priest Pathway** is able to shatter a planet and break the defenses of a **[Hand of God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Twilight_Giant_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_1:_Hand_of_God "Twilight Giant Pathway/Abilities")**.
        - **Flames**: A lesser authority of **Destruction** —the formless and colorless **Flame** that burns all things, reducing everything to ash.
        - Using the authority of **Sacrifices** to gain additional power from the [City of Calamity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/City_of_Calamity "City of Calamity") will cause "His" **Flames** to turn blood-red.
        - **Disaster**: A lesser authority of **Destruction** —the **Red Priest** is the master of various calamities.
- **Sacrifices**: "He" can influence and interfere with any [**Sacrificial Ritual**](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ritualistic_Magic#Sacrifice "Ritualistic Magic") within a star system and can appeal to the sources of power ([Sefirot](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirot "Sefirot")) to petition for strength or projections.
    - "He" is able to petition to other [Sefirot](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirot "Sefirot"), as long as they are fragments of the [Original Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Original_Creator "Original Creator") —but this also means constantly walking the edge of **Corruption**.
        - [Sefirot](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirot "Sefirot") is power, but it is also danger. There is no way for the **Red Priest** to utilize the power of [Sefirot](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirot "Sefirot") without paying a price.
                - The **Red Priest** is able to hold a sacrificial ritual towards a [Great Old One](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Old_One "Great Old One"), if "He" decides to surrender and become "Theirs".
        - As the **Priest** of the [Original Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Original_Creator "Original Creator"), "He" was once able to petition "Their" power; however, "He" can no longer do so because the [Original Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Original_Creator "Original Creator") is dead.
        - By offering sufficient **Sacrifices** to the [City of Calamity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/City_of_Calamity "City of Calamity"), "He" can gain the powers and authorities of the [Demoness Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Demoness_Pathway "Demoness Pathway") temporarily.
- **Charm**: Like the **[Demoness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Demoness_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Demoness_\(Chaos_Demoness,_Primordial_Demoness\) "Demoness Pathway/Abilities")**, "He" also possesses a certain degree of **Charm** authority that is symbiotic with the **Conquest** authority. The **Red Priest** is the most charismatic of males.
    - This includes the **Charm** that makes one desire to submit and be loyal to "Him", as well as the **Charm** that **Provokes** hatred and the urge to kill "Him" immediately.
        - [Azik](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Azik_Eggers "Azik Eggers") had experienced these effects before.
        - Any life form that beholds the **Red Priest** will be struck by "His" overwhelming beauty. Those of lower status will instantly lose their sanity.
        - Female [Great Old Ones](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Old_Ones "Great Old Ones") will feel reluctance to harm or desire to possess the **Red Priest**, even briefly pausing to admire "Him", while male [Great Old Ones](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Great_Old_Ones "Great Old Ones") would react with jealousy and deep loathing, wanting to kill "Him" at once.
- **Masculinity**: As a **Masculine** aspect of the [Original Creator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Original_Creator "Original Creator") and representation of **Yang** **Energy**, "He" possesses some authority over **Male** forces and concepts.
    - "He" is able to transform living beings and inanimate objects into **Males**, bestowing varying degrees of reproductive ability.
        - The authority of **Masculinity** makes the **Red Priest** the strongest, most **Masculine**, and most fertile **Male**.
        - For children born from the union of the [True Gods](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/True_Gods "True Gods") of the [Demoness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Demoness_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Demoness_\(Chaos_Demoness,_Primordial_Demoness\) "Demoness Pathway/Abilities") and [Red Priest](#Sequence_0:_Red_Priest), it's normal for twins to be a boy and a girl. Anything else would be unusual.
