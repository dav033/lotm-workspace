# Sequence 2 - Weather Warlock

## Explicit Powers

### Traits

- **Charm**: "They" possess a **Charm** that makes people want to follow and be loyal to "Them", and may cause women to develop feelings of admiration, but there is no active **Charm**.
- **Information-Gathering**: "Their" ability to observe the environment and thus extraction and restoration of environmental information has significantly enhanced.
- **Physical Enhancement**: "Their" strength and stamina are enhanced.
    - During battle, even without revealing "Their" **Mythical Creature form,** "They" can expand to 2.7-2.8 meters, like a small giant.
- **Spirituality**: "Their" amount of **Spirituality** gets enhanced as well.
- **War Song & War Cry**: "They" can let out a soul-shaking, chilling cry, causing those affected to feel their bodies and minds tremble, as if hearing the incessant drumbeats of an ancient battlefield.

### Authorities

- **Weather** (**Weather Manipulation**): "They" possess a partial authority over **Weather**, allowing "Them" to change regional **Weather** and create extreme **Weather** conditions, such as thunderstorms, hurricanes, heavy rain, hail, blizzards, frost, and dense fog.
    - "They" are able to guide the **Weather** to strike with colossal and magnificent lightning bolts, forming a silver-white divine tree that connects the sky and earth as if carrying horrific kinetic energy or releasing stored power, causing an impact.
        - Rather than **Concentrate** power to "Themselves" to enhance the lightning bolts, it is better to share "Their" **Weather** authority with "Their" team members as a way to hopefully trigger chain reactions or chaotic changes, exacerbating their power.
            - This is **Weather**, where a butterfly's wing flap can eventually evolve into a hurricane.
                - If this is successful, it will make the thunderstorm even more pronounced and exaggerated than if "They" had enhanced it by **Concentrating** the strength of "Their" troops onto "Themselves".
                                - If a **Weather Warlock** were to share "Their" **Weather** authority with thousands of soldiers, "They" can compete against a **[Demoness of Catastrophe's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Demoness_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_2:_Catastrophe_\(Demoness_of_Catastrophe\) "Demoness Pathway/Abilities")** destructive power.
        - "They" can make the weather in the sky form torrential rain that can be guided to aim to a single area, which in a span of *2-3 hours*, can submerge the entire [Bansy Harbor](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Bansy_Harbor "Bansy Harbor").
        - Then, by sending a long, lashing bolt of lighting, the countless electric snakes produced would plung into the pooled water of the surroundings, rapidly darting to form a vast silver web covering the entire ruined area, making the subsequent lightning strikes more destructive and far-reaching.
                - In the realm of angelic combat, this is utterly useless. No [Angel](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Angels "Angels") would drown in this and there's ample time to escape the scene. However, this is excellent for slaughtering ordinary people and Low-Sequence [Beyonders](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonders "Beyonders").
        - Just as "They" can create extreme weather conditions, "They" are also able to disperse and calm down various weather phenomena, both extreme and normal.
        - While "They" cannot directly form meteors or earthquakes, "They" are able to create some indirectly through the precise trigger of other weather phenomena.
        - "They" can bring forth a horrifying hurricane that "They" can form over the sea which can sweep in towering azure waves over *10 meters high* to effectively create a small tsunami.
        - Through this authority, "They" can fly by the wind and levitate in the air.
- **Gathering**: "They" can concentrate and **Gather** strength within a *10 kilometer range*, counted from the center of the team leader.
    - "They" are still limited to sharing only one ability with their team members at a time, but now "They" can allow every team member to use the shared **Beyonder** power simultaneously while lending them the corresponding **Spirituality** for the ability. The team's overall **Spirituality** however does not increase.
        - The complete **Mythical Creature** form and self-modified traits due to advancement, e.g. gender transformation, can't be shared.
        - Like with **War Bishops**, the ally will temporarily lose the ability borrowed by a **Weather Warlock**.

### Strengthened Abilities

- **Weaponizing**: "They" can make materials like wood and paper as strong as the [Sword of Courage](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sword_of_Courage "Sword of Courage"), which "They" can launch at high speeds over distances at several times the sound barrier.
    - Their remote bombardment range using **Weaponized** objects as carriers has been enhanced to vary between *several hundreds to thousands of kilometers*.
        - However, this was not very useful in actual combat. Even if the weapon carrying the **Fireball** flew quickly, adding an extremely long distance would still require a certain time to reach the target area.
            - Not to mention **Angels** and **Saints**, even **Mid-Sequence Beyonders** with strong **Spiritual Intuition** could detect it in advance and escape the potentially covered area in time. The current level of the **Fog of War** could not be attached and follow along.
- **Pyrokinesis**: "Their" flames now glow a violet-tinged azure to an azure-tinged violet depending on the potion's digestion, and "Their" control over flames has been massively improved.
    - "They" can cover a battlefield of *tens of kilometers*, directly raining down **Meteor Shower**, or summoning flocks of **Fire Ravens**.
        - **Compression**: Their a **Compressed** fireball's explosive power of now far surpasses that of a fleet's main guns by quite a margin.
        - "They" can malke Blue-violet flames rolled out from within "Their" body, quickly condensing into pieces of iron-black armor, transforming into a knight clad in full armor.
- **Flame Transformation**: "They" can now turn into a stream of flame, a huge flaming spear, and a terrifying flaming meteor to attack enemies or travel quickly.
    - Combined with hurricane-like winds, "They" can reach speeds up to *4000-5000 kilometers per hour*, which can be further enhanced with the potion's digestion.
        - "They" can further enhance "Their" speed through the explosive force of flames to continually accelerate, reaching a terrifying speed at the cost of damaging "Their" body which means "They" shouldn't do it for too long.
- **Weakness Investigation**: The scope of this ability has been enhanced, allowing targets "They" see be in the overall sense, including **Substitutes**, **Avatars**, and manifestations.
    - "They" can then find the weak points in this whole. if combined with **Cull** and the target doesn't have enough **Substitutes** or **Avatars**, "They" can wipe them all out at once.
- **Cull**: Its power has been greatly enhanced, which, when in combination of **Weakness Investigation**, make it so that even those in the [Demoness Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Demoness_Pathway "Demoness Pathway") can't take a few of such blows.
    - "Their" weapons also become extensions of "Their" bodies.
- **Galvanization**: "Their" ability to turn into steel no longer turns "Them" into ordinary steel, but closer to the symbolism of "steel", able to withstand attacks that would normally destroy or melt real steel.
    - "They" can transform "Their" flames into actual weapons or iron-black armor with this level of symbolic properties, though "They" could only maintain this for about *10 minutes.*
- **Chain of Command**: This hidden line of command "They" had since "They" became an **Iron-blooded Knight** have been massively enhanced.
    - "They" are able to use borrowed ability of "Their" army, along one of "Their" abilities from the **Chain of Command** to achieve new and unique effects.
        - By borrowing powers of **[Manipulator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Visionary_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_4:_Manipulator "Visionary Pathway/Abilities")**, and attaching it to "Their" **Communication**, teammates would transmit all inner thoughts or fleeting ideas to their companions.
        - A **Weather Warlock** is able to directly descend to the location of "Their" team member through "Their" team members' prayers.
- **Fog of War**: The **War Bishop's** ability to produce a **Fog of War** has been greatly enhanced at "Their" level. At even higher Levels "Their" **Fog of War** may even affect the [Astral World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Astral_World "Astral World").
    - The range that "Their" **Fog of War** can initially expand to is *20 kilometers*, which will continue increase as "Their" potion is further digested.
        - The **War Bishop's** trait of being unaffected by anything while within the **Fog of War** can now be shared with "Their" own team members.
        - At "Their" current level, the **Fog of War** "They" form now has the ability to interfere with outside **Divination** and **Prophecy**.
- **Sacrifice**: "They" can now petition the or the **[Symbolism](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Symbolism "Symbolism") of War** for assistance once every week, borrowing its strength to help achieve certain victories.
    - In special environments or circumstances, this allows "Them" to turn "Their" flames into a dark-red, nearly purple flame, and temporarily gain the powers of **Conquest**.
        - [Diest](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Diest "Diest") used the special nature of [Fourth Epoch Trier](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fourth_Epoch_Trier "Fourth Epoch Trier") to use the **Puppets into Soldiers** ability of a **Conqueror** and strengthen his **Flames**.
                - As a Priest of the [City of Calamity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/City_of_Calamity "City of Calamity"), [Lumian](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee") obtained the power of **Conquest**, supplemented with a hint of [Sefirah](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirah "Sefirah") -quality **Conquest** power.
        - By conducting a sacrifice exceeding standard to the **Essence of War** during a large-scale war, "They" can approach or even briefly reach the **Sequence 1** level.
- **Tracking**: "Their" **Tracking** skills are of an advanced level.

### Mythical Creature Form

- "They" become a complete **Mythical Creature**, "Their" **Mythical Creature** form is a *5-6 meter tall* **Calamity Giant**, an iron-black metal skeleton engulfed in majestic purple flames, along with various symbolic elements representing real phenomena.
    - A **Calamity Giant** would ignite those who witness and approach "Them", while the unfathomable knowledge emanating from "Them" would materialize various weather phenomena.
        - If "They" don't suppress and restrain "Their" psyche, the weather will gradually become more volatile and extreme within the area "They" reside in, and the probability of wars and massacre events occurring will continually increase.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- [[War (Red Priest)]] → Gathering, War Song, War Cry, Chain of Command, and Fog of War.
- [[Sacrifices (Red Priest)]] → the weekly petition for assistance through War symbolism.
- [[Charm (Red Priest)]] → passive follower-oriented Charm.
- [[Destruction (Red Priest)]] → destructive flame, Cull, and weakness-targeting applications as limited expressions.
- Weather Manipulation remains a named Power at this Sequence; the source inventory does not list Weather as an independent Red Priest Authority.

## Potion

### Potion Properties

> A formed, purplish liquid that burns.

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> A palm-sized, crystalline and porous pillar. The various holes in the pillar contained mists, hall, rainwater, lightning, and other meteorological phenomena, sometimes diffusing outwards. sometimes receding back inside.

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- Skull of a Weather Giant
- Core of a Mist Jellyfish

Or
- A Weather Warlock Beyonder Characteristic.

Supplementary Ingredients

- 30 milliliters of blood each from three deceased individuals who died in different extreme weather conditions.
- 500 strands of a Weather Giant's hair.
- Five Mist Jellyfish Crystals.
    - Note: After the Fourth Epoch, Weather Giants and Mist Jellyfish no longer exist. Supplementary materials can substituted with the corresponding parts from a Weather Warlock.
- A segment of a tree branch struck by lightning.
- 20 milliliters of the first snow melt of a region each year.

### Advancement Ritual

Forcefully change the weather of a region without external aid in a very short time.
