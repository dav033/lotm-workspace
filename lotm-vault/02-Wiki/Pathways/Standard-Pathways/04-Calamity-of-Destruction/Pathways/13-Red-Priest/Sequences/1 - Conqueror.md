# Sequence 1 - Conqueror

## Explicit Powers

### Authorities

- **Conquest** & **War**: "They" obtain part of the **Conquest** and **War** authorities.
    - **Conquest**: "They" can make the target's body, mind and spirit want to submit, and choose to submit. Once the target is completely **Conquered**, they will be loyal to "Them" forever.
        - It can also be used to make the target lose consciousness and control the bodies of the affected creatures to act according to "Their" will regardless of whether the creature is conscious or not.
            - [Sauron-Einhorn-Medici](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sauron-Einhorn-Medici "Sauron-Einhorn-Medici") channeled "His" will of subjugation into the unstable [Vermonda Sauron](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Vermonda_Sauron "Vermonda Sauron"), **Conquering** "His" will and allowing "Him" to plunge "His" sword into "His" skull.
                - It can cause 2 **Weather Warlocks** and "Their" allies, as well as a crazed **Conqueror** to feel a sense of awe overwhelm "Their" bodies and minds, dissuading any inclination to resist.
                - There is no limit for the numbers of targets "They" are able to **Conquer**.
        - **Puppets into Soldiers** (**Casting Beans into Soldiers**): A combination of the **War** and **Conquest** authorities, allowing "Them" to **Conquer** inanimate objects to turn into soldiers.
        - If soldier dolls or “beans” have been prepared in advance using **Beyonder Characteristics** or **Boons** from other [Pathways](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways"), those soldiers will likewise possess those abilities.
            - [Sauron-Einhorn-Medici](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sauron-Einhorn-Medici "Sauron-Einhorn-Medici"), through "His" **Conquering** authority, deployed life-like metallic figurines infused with [Beyonder Characteristics](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristics "Beyonder Characteristics"), instantly showing the formidable strength of "His" legion while implementing "His" plans.
                - These soldiers are able to use the **Conqueror's** powers.
            - [Lumian Lee's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee") **Sword of Destruction** tore the surrounding [Mirror World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mirror_World "Mirror World") into fragments, each fragment was **Conquered** to be turned into a soldier that shot **Black Flame** composed short arrows towards [Judith](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Judith "Judith").
                        - Through the soldiers made from “His" **Conjured** flames, [Lumian Lee](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee") possessed a ready made army that can use "His" own **Spirituality** and activate "His" own **Beyonder** powers and **Contract** abilities.
                - Other examples of this usage are:
            - [Vermonda Sauron](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Vermonda_Sauron "Vermonda Sauron") let out a scream, scorching the ground with purple flames as earth puppets crawled out. These *3 to 4 meter tall* puppets charred with an iron hue and speckled in dark-red blood became "His" soldiers in battle.
                        - [Diest's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Diest "Diest") soybeans transformed into giants with blurred faces and yellowish skin while "His" various palm-sized iron soldiers sprang into life and had expanded in size, joining the battle against [Vermonda Sauron](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Vermonda_Sauron "Vermonda Sauron").
                        - [Vermonda Sauron](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Vermonda_Sauron "Vermonda Sauron") granted "His" Sequence 6 [Conspirer](#Sequence_6:_Conspirer) descendant an equivalent of a [charm](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mystical_Item#Charms "Mystical Item") that allowed the latter to create animated wax soldiers from human corpses.
        - **Gathering**: **Gathered** power can undergo a qualitative transformation when reaching sufficient numbers, capable of rivaling an attack from an [activated](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Activation "Activation") [Uniqueness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniquenesses "Uniquenesses").
        - "They" can enable "Their" soldiers to share "Their" rank by simultaneously sharing all of "Their" abilities and **Spirituality**; its only drawback is the mass expenditure of **Spirituality**.
        - **War Commander**: A **Conqueror** can establish a temporary team of *unlimited size* that enjoys almost all the benefits of a true team within a *50 kilometer range*.

### Strengthened Abilities

- "They" gain further enhancements and strengthening to "Their" other **Beyonder** powers.
    - **Sacrifices**: If a **Conqueror** were to **Sacrifice** "Themselves", "They" can achieve a power burst that reaches similar levels to a **[Hand of God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Twilight_Giant_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_1:_Hand_of_God "Twilight Giant Pathway/Abilities")** and **[Hand of Order](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Justiciar_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_1:_Hand_of_Order "Justiciar Pathway/Abilities")** doing the same.
        - This is the level of the [weakest tier of Half Great Old One](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Half_Great_Old_Ones#Tier_3 "Half Great Old Ones").
        - **Spear of Destruction**: The **Cull** ability at this level has undergone a qualitative transformation.
        - It can be used on a single target or to annihilate an area. Even if it doesn’t hit a weak point, it still inflicts massive destruction. If it strikes a clone, projection, or **Substitute**, it will transmit the destruction to the true body. Striking a true weak point is equivalent to certain death.
        - **Galvanization**: "Their" ability to turn "Their" flames and body into steel has been enhanced.
        - "They" are able to create a massive, bloodstained, iron-black chair or a throne pieced together from iron-black weapons just by **Conjuring** flames and **Galvanizing** it into steel.
                - "They" can materialize an iron-black blood-stained armor whose mystical and physical defenses reach a level not much inferior to a **[Hand of God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Twilight_Giant_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_1:_Hand_of_God "Twilight Giant Pathway/Abilities")** through **Galvanization**.
        - **Pyrokinesis**: "Their" flames now burn in a pure violet color.
        - [Vermonda Sauron](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Vermonda_Sauron "Vermonda Sauron") let out a frenzied and terrifying roar where the weather had dramatically shifted. The formless flames that illuminated the surroundings and shrouded the entire "sky" seemed to be influenced, coalescing into a massive vortex. Within the vortex, shapeless and translucent flames descended from above, striking the wilderness like a colossal sword that pierced through heaven and earth.
        - **Weaponizing**: "They" can coalesce fog into a spear-like form, a weapon capable of shattering mountain peaks.
        - In an instant, the spear, crafted from the gray fog, erupted into violent flames, taking on a violet hue. It exuded an aura of supremacy, as if it aimed to **Conquer** all in its path. Witnessing this surreal phenomenon, whether it was [Snarner Einhorn](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Snarner_Einhorn "Snarner Einhorn"), [Diest](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Diest "Diest"), [Vermonda Sauron](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Vermonda_Sauron "Vermonda Sauron"), or "Their" allies, it was as though they beheld a city enshrouded in fog. A sense of awe overwhelmed "Their" bodies and minds, dissuading any inclination to resist.

## Potion

### Potion Properties

> A dazzling, blazing purple sphere of light in the shape of a crown.

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> A strange crown covered in rust and blood. A blood-stained iron crown, formed by the broken skull in hand.

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- A Conqueror Beyonder Characteristic

Supplementary Ingredients

- 90 milliliters of blood from a Conqueror or contaminated liquid from a Conqueror Beyonder Characteristic.
- A genuine crown.
- A drop of fingertip blood from each member of one's own team.
- Nine heads of powerful enemies (at least Sequence 4 in status).

### Advancement Ritual

Conquer a nation with a population of more than 30 million inhabitants, making all beings there fear, obey, and revere you.
