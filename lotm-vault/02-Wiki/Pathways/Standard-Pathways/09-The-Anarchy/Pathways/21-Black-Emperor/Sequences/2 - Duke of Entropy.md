# Sequence 2 - Duke of Entropy (Entropy)

## Explicit Powers

### Authorities

- **Disorder**: "They" gain partial authority over **Disorder**, which gives rise to the **Entropy** ability.
    - **Entropy**: The longer one fights a **Duke of Entropy**, the more random, **Disordered** and uncontrollable one's own abilities, thoughts, and state become, eventually turning into a mess, falling into a strange state of annihilation; the target of **Entropy** can be an enemy, or it can be the environment, rules, etc.
- **Exploit**: "They" gain partial authority over **Exploitation**.
    - "Their" **Exploitation** of the rules have been strengthened to a degree where a **Duke of Entropy** can **Exploit** "Their" airborne state when jumping once to travel from the earth to the crimson moon.
- **Strata**: "Their" **Superior** ability has been enhanced.
    - **Superior**: Using [Solomon's Mask](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Solomon%27s_Mask "Solomon's Mask"), [Miss Judgement](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Xio_Derecha "Xio Derecha") grew significantly over *6 feet*.

### Strengthened Abilities

- "They" gain enhancements of previous **Sequences'** abilities.
    - **Distortion**: "Their" **Distortion** ability has reached the [Angel](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Angels "Angels") level, allowing "Them" to **Distort** the rules created by authorities to a certain degree, twisting them to suit their will.
        - [Xio Derecha](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Xio_Derecha "Xio Derecha") **Distorted** the rule created by the [Pillar of Severity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pillar_of_Severity "Pillar of Severity"), "The battlefield shall be divided," by intensifying its core principle - **Balance** - to an even more extreme degree.
            - This caused the **Balance** to focus on [Sealed Artifacts](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sealed_Artifacts "Sealed Artifacts") as well, causing [Xio Derecha](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Xio_Derecha "Xio Derecha") and the [Demoness of Gold](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Demoness_of_Gold "Demoness of Gold") to join the battle between [Derrick Berg](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Derrick_Berg "Derrick Berg") and [Demoness of Orange](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Demoness_of_Orange "Demoness of Orange").
                - With "Their" **Distortion** power, "They" can redirect the change of the battlefield made by a **[Planeswalker](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Door_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_2:_Planeswalker "Door Pathway/Abilities")** to somewhere else.

### Mythical Creature Form

- "They" have become a full [Mythical Creature](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mythical_Creature_Form "Mythical Creature Form"); "Their" full [Mythical Creature Form](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mythical_Creature_Form "Mythical Creature Form") takes the appearance of a **Giant Black Baboon** with nine arms and a dark crown on its head.

## Authority → Power Mapping

This maps conceptual origin; it does not mean a Sequence 2 Duke of Entropy owns an Authority.

- [[Disorder (Black Emperor)]] → **Disorder**, **Entropy** — direct partial-authority expression, with Entropy as the named effect it gives rise to.
- [[Autocrat (Black Emperor)]] → **Exploit**, **Strata**, **Superior** — Exploit and Strata are both named subordinate authorities under Autocrat (per the Sequence 0 authority tree), so this reads as a probable partial expression rather than a standalone Authority.
- [[Distortion (Black Emperor)]] → **Distortion** — enhanced expression of the same Distortion authority carried from earlier Sequences.
