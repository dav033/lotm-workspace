# Sequence 0 - Black Emperor

## Explicit Powers

### Authorities

- **[[Autocrat (Black Emperor)|Autocrat]]**: A core authority of the **Black Emperor**, and an authority under the **Shadow of Order** symbolism. Under it are the subordinate authorities like **Distortion**, **Exploit**, **Definition** and **Strata**.
    - Except for the **Strata** authority, all the other subordinate authorities are based on **Order**, by **Exploiting Loopholes** within **Order** or **Distorting** the original intent of it, the **Black Emperor** can make **Order** serve "Their" own purposes.
        - In barren and dead places, both the **[Justiciar](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Justiciar_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Justiciar "Justiciar Pathway/Abilities")** and the **Black Emperor** can rebuild a complete set of **Order** suitable for human survival. The **[Justiciar](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Justiciar_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Justiciar "Justiciar Pathway/Abilities")** relies on the original fundamental **Rules** to recreate an **Order** suitable for human survival, while the **Black Emperor** **Distorts** the original **Rules** to make them suitable for humans. The effect is the same, but the methods of realization differ.
                - In comparison, the **[Sun](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sun_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Sun "Sun Pathway/Abilities") '** s **Order** is more positive, self-centered, and exclusive, and must derive from "Their" previous standard of justice. There is an overlap and intersection in the authority of **Order** between the and the [Sun Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sun_Pathway "Sun Pathway").
        - **[[Distortion (Black Emperor)|Distortion]]**: "They" can **Distort** certain underlying rules of the world itself. Also capable of **Distorting** processes and actions, turning them into complete opposites.
        - The **Black Emperor** can **Distort** **Order** into **Disorder**, making light become shadow, noise shift to silence, mouths transform into nostrils, a lack of people becoming a crowd, and death reverse to life.
            - "They" can **Distort** the extension direction of **Spirit Body Threads**, making those who can see it unable to detect the target in advance.
                        - "They" are able to disrupt **Teleportation** by **Distorting** the destination of the **Teleportation**, turning everything extremely chaotic.
                        - "They" can delay the consequence of an effect by constantly **Distorting** their state back to when the effect's activity is only gradual.
                        - "They" can **Distort** the spatial position between a used **Substitute**, swapping the positions of the **Substitute** and the main body.
                - Using this on a [Fool Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fool_Pathway "Fool Pathway") **Beyonder** will result in them forcibly appearing in the desired position once they exit the [Historical Void](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Historical_Void "Historical Void").
                        - The rules and process to use [Sealed Artifacts](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sealed_Artifacts "Sealed Artifacts") can be disrupted and **Distorted**, stopping the user from activating their power.
                        - "They" are able to **Distort** the **True Vision** of onlookers, causing them to see scene of the past from one or two seconds ago.
                        - "They" can **Distort** the rules of **Concealed** spaces, making its entrance appear directly before "Them".
                - When "They" advance to become a **Black Emperor**, "Their" ascension will cause many **Distortions** to manifest in reality, disrupting all sorts of **Order** from across the world.
            - **Spirit World Traversals** will be **Distorted**, causing the user to discover that their intended destination is far away, or that their **Wandering** has ended abruptly.
                        - **High Sequence** [Error Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway "Error Pathway") **Beyonders** will have the connection to "Their" external **Worms of Time** be **Distorted**, losing the ability to command "Them".
                        - The rules of the **[Circle Inhabitant](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Eternal_Aeon_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_4:_Circle_Inhabitant "Eternal Aeon Pathway/Abilities")** will be **Distorted**, allowing leaving the area of the **Circle Inhabitant** without it triggering the **Loop**.
                - After fulfilling certain conditions, the **Black Emperor** will be able to use the **Distortion** authority to **Distort** authorities, mystical connections, and even faith.
            - [Roselle](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Roselle_Gustav "Roselle Gustav") forcefully **Distorted** the faith of the [Uncertain Mist](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uncertain_Mist "Uncertain Mist") 's followers to "Himself" using "His" authority, the 's connection with the [Uncertain Mist](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uncertain_Mist "Uncertain Mist"), and the corruption of the [Uncertain Mist](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uncertain_Mist "Uncertain Mist") while relying on the [Astral Barrier](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Astral_Barrier "Astral Barrier").
                - This granted "Him" absolute authority over the [Uncertain Mist](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uncertain_Mist "Uncertain Mist") followers but also deepened "His" connection to the [Uncertain Mist](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uncertain_Mist "Uncertain Mist").
                        - [Roselle Gustav](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Roselle_Gustav "Roselle Gustav") **Distorted** the relationship between the main body and the external items, briefly making "Himself" an appendage of the [Genie](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Genie "Genie").
        - **Resurrection**: Even if "They" were to be truly annihilated and killed, **Death** is a part of the most underlying and core part of the **Order** of reality, thus, the **Black Emperor** can be resurrected. "They" will still be able to awaken and return from one of the mausoleums "They" made for "Their" apotheosis.
        - There are three stages in the process of "Their" **Resurrection**:
            1. The [Uniqueness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniqueness "Uniqueness") leaves the person possessing it, turning into an abstract concept.
                        2. The subjects ruled by the **Black Emperor** will hear "Their" mighty voice again.
                        3. "They" will fuse with the [Uniqueness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniqueness "Uniqueness") and would reappear in the [Astral World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Astral_World "Astral World").
                - The **Sequence 1** characteristics would be returned to "Their" hands.
                    - Whether the **Sequence 1 characteristics** come with the corresponding **Sequences 9-2 characteristics** depends on the state of the Sequence 1 characteristic itself.
                - "Their" **Resurrection** is an **Order** that other [True Gods](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/True_Gods "True Gods") are unable to **Distort** or prevent. It can be interfered with in the following known scenarios:
            1. The intervention of a [God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/God "God") formed from the + 2 [Black Emperor Sequence 1](#Sequence_1:_Prince_of_Abolition_\(Abolition\)) + 1 [Justiciar Sequence 1](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Justiciar_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_1:_Hand_of_Order "Justiciar Pathway/Abilities").
                        2. The birth of [The Anarchy](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/The_Anarchy "The Anarchy").
                - With the [Lord of Mysteries](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lord_of_Mysteries "Lord of Mysteries") ' help, "They" could still **Resurrect** through the **Rules**, but "They" would only be able to reclaim two portions of the [Prince of Abolition](#Sequence_1:_Prince_of_Abolition_\(Abolition\)) Beyonder characteristics.
                        3. Deliberately terminating the **Resurrection** process "Themselves".
                - This would leave "Them" as an incomplete Sequence 0. "They" would then live in a special state inside "Their" mausoleum, without the [Uniqueness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniqueness "Uniqueness") and [Beyonder Characteristics](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristics "Beyonder Characteristics").
                - How long it takes for the retrieval of the [Prince of Abolition](#Sequence_1:_Prince_of_Abolition_\(Abolition\)) [Beyonder Characteristics](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristics "Beyonder Characteristics") and the total duration of the **Resurrection** still depends on related arrangements.
            - Unlike [Solomon](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Solomon "Solomon"), [Roselle](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Roselle_Gustav "Roselle Gustav") 's **Resurrection** was fast since [Adam](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Adam "Adam") took the initiative to give the characteristics and the to him.
                - "Their" **Resurrection** can cleanse the **Corruption** and madness brought by multiple different pathway [Beyonder Characteristics](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonder_Characteristics "Beyonder Characteristics"), but can't cleanse the **Corruption** and madness that's intrinsic to itself. For the latter, it includes the following known scenarios:
            1. Failing the [Apotheosis Ritual](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Black_Emperor_Pathway/Advancement#Sequence_0:_Black_Emperor "Black Emperor Pathway/Advancement").
                        2. The **Corruption** and madness brought along by the [Mother Goddess of Depravity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Goddess_of_Depravity "Mother Goddess of Depravity").
                - Even if "They" were killed and had all nine mausoleums destroyed, if a certain amount of the **Order** set up by "Them" remained, it is still possible for "Them" to mysteriously **Revive**. This appears to be a **Loophole** against the natural **Order** of death.
                - [Ancient Gods](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ancient_Gods "Ancient Gods") with the like the [Mutated King Kvastir](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mutated_King_Kvastir "Mutated King Kvastir") could also **Resurrect** with this mechanism, provided that "They" had prepared thoroughly.
- **[[Disorder (Black Emperor)|Disorder]]**: A core authority of the **Black Emperor**. "They" wield the majority of this authority.
    - "They" are no longer merely a parasite, interpreter, or **Distorter** of **Order**; "They" have broken free from **Order**, plunging the very concepts of **Order** and **Rules** themselves into total **Disorder**.
        - "They" can cause a person's thoughts to descend into **Disorder**, making it become active and uncontrollable. This seemed to be a trend that couldn’t be changed — one that led to chaos.
- **Divine Kingdom**: **Order Hell**, or **False Law Heaven**.

## Authority → Power Mapping

The cited secondary inventory associates Sequence 0 **Black Emperor** with the listed Authorities. Their ownership and scope remain interpretations; the Pathway Hub separately identifies the Great Old One.

- [[Autocrat (Black Emperor)]] → embodied directly as the "Autocrat" authority listed above, encompassing the subordinate **Distortion**, **Exploit**, **Definition**, and **Strata** authorities.
- [[Distortion (Black Emperor)]] → embodied directly as the "Distortion" authority listed above, and underlies the **Resurrection** mechanism as a **Loophole** against the natural **Order** of death.
- [[Disorder (Black Emperor)]] → embodied directly as the "Disorder" authority listed above.

## Potion

### Potion Properties

> The reflection of a collapsing world.

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- The Black Emperor Uniqueness.
- Three Prince of Abolition Beyonder Characteristics.

Supplementary Ingredients
An alternate world where order has completely collapsed.

### Advancement Ritual

- Possess your own country, linking your name to the title of Emperor and making it common knowledge among the populace.
- Establish a set of strict and complicated rules that defy normality, including architectural styles.
- Spur your citizens to secretly establish nine mausoleums in the style of pyramids.
- Enter one of the mausoleums.
- Drink the Sequence 0 potion while a large majority of the citizens are scattered across different cities in the corresponding sacrificial rituals involved.
