# Sequence 7 - Briber

## Explicit Powers

### New Abilities

- **Bribe**: The core ability of a **Briber**. By giving their target an actual or symbolic **Bribe**, they would be able to achieve several domineering effects over their target.
    - A **Bribe** can be anywhere between giving a target money to throwing an object near the direction of the target; even if the target does not "accept" the object, it still counts as a **Bribe**.
        - There are *4 types* of **Bribes**: **Weaken**, **Charm**, **Arrogance**, and **Connection**.
        - **Weaken**: For a certain amount of time, this significantly weakens the target's attacks, defenses, and control over the **Briber**.
                - **Arrogance**: The target will become proud and **Arrogant**, lowering their intelligence and leading them to make erroneous judgments.
                - **Charm**: The target will feel a great sense of fondness, making it difficult to have any thoughts of animosity or even wish to fight the **Briber**.
            - If the conditions are suitable, the person who received the **Bribe** has an extremely small chance of attacking their companions.
                - **Connection**: This establishes a connection between the target and the **Briber**.

## Authority → Power Mapping

This maps conceptual origin; it does not mean a Sequence 7 Briber owns an Authority.

- **Bribe** (and its **Weaken**, **Charm**, **Arrogance**, and **Connection** effects) remains a named Beyonder ability without a clearly supported Authority assignment among [[Autocrat (Black Emperor)]], [[Disorder (Black Emperor)]], and [[Distortion (Black Emperor)]] — it reads as foundational technique rather than a jurisdiction expression.
