# Sequence 3 - Frenzied Mage (Frenzy)

## Explicit Powers

### Authorities

- **Strata**: They possess partial authority over **Strata**, from which the **Superior** ability originates.
    - **Superior**: Their **Commanding Presence** comes from a "rightful role," from social class to their strength and resources, this dignified aura can now be materialized into a weapon.
        - Even their voice can make **Spiritual Bodies** tremble and make subordinates feel a powerful pressure, forcing them to follow orders and being unable to lift their heads.
        - A **Frenzied Mage** can gigantify their body, increasing their height in the process.
- **Disorder**: They gain a certain degree of authority over **Disorder**, from which the **Frenzy** ability originates.
    - **Frenzy**: In the midst of **Frenzied** fluctuations, their abilities will undergo some random variations, which are often beneficial, while the abilities of their opponents will also undergo random changes, which are often detrimental.
        - Of course, whether beneficial or detrimental, the **Frenzied Mage** themselves are not able to predict the specific effect.
            - [Concerto of Light and Shadow](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Concerto_of_Light_and_Shadow "Concerto of Light and Shadow") turned a volley of **Air Cannons** into illusory doves that don’t pose any threat.
                        - [Concerto of Light and Shadow](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Concerto_of_Light_and_Shadow "Concerto of Light and Shadow") changed the **Distortion** effects' of [Qonas Kilgor](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Qonas_Kilgor "Qonas Kilgor") to summoning a flower from the garden below.
                        - [Concerto of Light and Shadow](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Concerto_of_Light_and_Shadow "Concerto of Light and Shadow") made the **Air Cannons** and the **Deprivation Bullet** explode, turning into red, purple, yellow, or green fireworks.

### Strengthened Abilities

- They gain enhancement of previous **Sequences** ' other abilities and traits.
    - **Distortion**: Their **Distortion** can now can directly **Distort** the trajectory of projectiles as well as **Distort** a target's intention to move forward, making them unable to stop.
        - [Nast Solomon](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Nast_Solomon "Nast Solomon") was able to **Distort** [Klein's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") intent of "proceeding forward", making that intent into an action that was impossible for [Klein Moretti](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") to stop.

### Mythical Creature Form

- As **Sequence 3 Beyonders**, they can respond to prayers within a certain range.

## Authority → Power Mapping

This maps conceptual origin; it does not mean a Sequence 3 Frenzied Mage owns an Authority.

- [[Autocrat (Black Emperor)]] → **Strata**, **Superior** — Strata is a named subordinate authority under Autocrat (per the Sequence 0 authority tree), so this reads as a probable partial expression rather than a standalone Authority.
- [[Disorder (Black Emperor)]] → **Disorder**, **Frenzy** — direct partial-authority expression, with Frenzy as the named effect it gives rise to.
- [[Distortion (Black Emperor)]] → **Distortion** — enhanced expression of the same Distortion authority carried from earlier Sequences.
