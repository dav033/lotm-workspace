# Sequence 9 - Lawyer

## Explicit Powers

### New Abilities

- **Eloquence**: **Lawyers** are masters of eloquence and reasoning, able to influence judgment, thoughts, and conclusions through their words, actions, and established processes.
    - They can, to some extent, distort or guide the target's thinking, making them seem approachable and trustworthy.
        - Instead of negotiating further, [Klein Moretti](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") readily agreed to a major financial transaction without any hesitation.
        - This influence is difficult to detect as **Spiritual Intuition** alone might not be enough to notice their deception.
        - [Klein Moretti](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") was unable to realize that his thoughts had been distorted until [Jurgen Cooper](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Jurgen_Cooper "Jurgen Cooper") mentioned the abnormality, and even then, he wasn’t certain.
        - This power can be passively triggered without the listed intent of the **Lawyer**.

## Authority → Power Mapping

This maps conceptual origin; it does not mean a Sequence 9 Lawyer owns an Authority.

- [[Distortion (Black Emperor)]] → **Eloquence** — the described ability to "distort or guide the target's thinking" is a low-Sequence echo of the Distortion authority applied to perception and judgment rather than physical rules.
