# Sequence 4 - Earl of the Fallen (Fallen)

## Explicit Powers

### Description

There are rules to everything, and everything follows certain rules. As **Demigods** of the, they are good at finding whatever loopholes there are in an **Order** to **Exploit** for themselves. Through **Magnify**, **Exploit** and **Distortion** they can achieve certain things that only **Beyonders** of the [Justiciar Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Justiciar_Pathway "Justiciar Pathway") can accomplish.

### New Abilities

- **Exploit**: They can directly **Exploit** the **Laws**, extending certain states for longer periods of time or ending it ahead of time. This makes the rules more beneficial to themselves.
    - For example, after jumping in mid air, an **Earl of the Fallen** can **Exploit** the state of being away from the ground, extending it and achieving the effect of floating.
        - By **Exploiting** the jumping process of their jump itself while also **Exploiting** the state of being away from the ground, an **Earl of the Fallen** can achieve a form of flight.
        - They can achieve effect similar to the Manipulation of Laws of nature from **Deceit** of the **[Mentor of Deceit](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_3:_Mentor_of_Deceit "Error Pathway/Abilities")** when paired with **Distortion**.
- **Bestowment**: They can directly **Bestow** a negative effect onto a target, which can cause them to:
    - Turn sluggish;
        - Become greedily impatient;
        - Lose their fighting spirit; or
        - Be only capable of focusing attention on money.
- **Magnify**: They can boost the effects or influence of their own actions.
    - The interactions and effects an object are able to bring out can also be **Magnified**, causing said object to possess a stronger influence or effect than normal.
        - They are able to **Magnify** the effects and side-effects of [Sealed Artifacts](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sealed_Artifacts "Sealed Artifacts"), allowing them to make certain lower rank [Sealed Artifacts](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sealed_Artifacts "Sealed Artifacts") affect even **Demigods**.
        - They can **Magnify** the potency of natural laws and phenomena, turning the smallest of weather conditions into a disaster.
        - An **Earl of the Fallen** can **Magnify** the strong winds near them, enhancing their speed and transforming them into a small **Hurricane**.
        - Even the state of a target as well as their actions can be **Magnified**. For some actions, a **Magnification** of them will bring certain effects.
        - **Earls of the Fallen** are able to **Magnify** the act of "grabbing", allowing them to grab someone from nearly *100 meters away.*
                - They are able to **Magnify** a hug and turn it into a restriction. Similar to grabbing someone from nearly *100 meters away*, they can do this across large distances.
                - The injuries they have dealt to their opponents or that are just present can be **Magnified**, making them in some cases simply die on the spot.
                - An **Earl of the Fallen** is able to **Magnify** the potency of their ordinary attacks to the point where those attacks will become an execution.
        - Under normal circumstances, **Magnify** could only amplify the effects and influence that strengthened one’s body or actions without helping others.
        - **Magnify** cannot be used consecutively in a quick succession. It is limited to a one-time use each time.
        - **Magnify** is effective on abilities above the **Sequence 4** level.

### Strengthened Abilities

- They gain enhancements of their abilities from previous **Sequences**
    - **Disorder**: It influences the structure of objects, the standards of measurement, and the accuracy of attacks.
        - It will make a seemingly massive building collapse, causing what seems like a huge distance to be shortened to that of a few paces, as well as allowing attacks that are aimed at them to miss their mark.
            - [Qonas Kilgor](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Qonas_Kilgor "Qonas Kilgor") unleashed **Disorder** to grasp the patterns of the **Concealed** world that enveloped Maygur Manor, using the feedback to determine the location of an exit.
        - **Bribe**: Their **Bribes** are stronger and can be used in tandem with **Magnify**.
        - A simple rose being thrown can be **Magnified** to an extent that it causes a violent quake when impacting the ground while at the same time imparting the effects of **Bribe—Weaken**.
        - **Distortion**: They can better **Distort** a target’s language, actions, intentions, as well as can **Distort** the effects of attacks, turning fatal wounds into serious injuries, strikes into pushes, advances into retreats, and escapes into charges.
        - They can **Distort** the rules of the **Marionette Interchange** power of a **[Bizarro Sorcerer](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fool_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_4:_Bizarro_Sorcerer "Fool Pathway/Abilities")** so that they could only swap between 2 **Marionettes**.
                - [Klein's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") **Air Cannons** had it's direction and trajectory **Distorted** to the right by [Qonas Kilgor](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Qonas_Kilgor "Qonas Kilgor"), significantly missing him in the process.
                - They are able to directly create a **Seal** in thin air, effectively creating an invisible wall, through just **Distorting** an act of "closing a door".
                - [Qonas Kilgor](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Qonas_Kilgor "Qonas Kilgor") was able to fully **Distort** his moving trajectory to a large degree, causing him to change directions instantly.
                - They are able to distort the level of a mental disease, such as a **Mental Plague** from a **[Manipulator](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Visionary_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_4:_Manipulator "Visionary Pathway/Abilities")**, weakening it and slowing down the deterioration.
                - **Distortion** can cause a bullet's trajectory to be ridiculously off its mark and simultaneously cause its power to be extremely lacking.

### Mythical Creature Form

- As **Demigods** at **Sequence 4** who have obtained partial **Godhood**, they are able to manifest their incomplete **Mythical Creature** **Form** if they disregard any of the consequences and become an incomplete **Curly Haired Baboon**.

## Authority → Power Mapping

This maps conceptual origin; it does not mean a Sequence 4 Earl of the Fallen owns an Authority.

- [[Autocrat (Black Emperor)]] → **Exploit** — named subordinate authority under Autocrat, per the Sequence 0 authority tree.
- [[Disorder (Black Emperor)]] → **Disorder**, **Bestowment** — Bestowment's forced negative states (sluggishness, impaired judgment, lost focus) parallel Disorder's effect of making a target's thoughts and actions uncontrolled.
- [[Distortion (Black Emperor)]] → **Distortion** — direct partial-authority expression, enhanced from earlier Sequences.
- **Magnify** and **Bribe** remain named Beyonder abilities without a clearly supported Authority assignment among the three listed here.
