# Sequence 6 - Baron of Corruption (Corruption)

## Explicit Powers

### Description

They appear to be the compliant to the rules, but in fact **Distorts** them, constantly drawing on the power of **Order** to serve themself.

### Traits

- **Physical Enhancement**: A **Baron of Corruption's** physique is further enhanced.
    - Their punches resemble that of a cannonball, and can cause an entire building to shake.

### New Abilities

- **Distortion**: They can use the loopholes found in **Order**. By **Distorting** the target’s words, actions, and intent, they could formulate a certain **Order** that provided themself with an advantage, so as to achieve the goal of restraining and influencing their opponent.
    - They can **Distort** the direction and target of attacks.
        - [Mithor King](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mithor_King "Mithor King") **Distorted** the trajectory of a bullet upwards slightly, causing it to miss.
                - [Klein](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") used [Creeping Hunger](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Creeping_Hunger "Creeping Hunger") to **Distort** the target of [Ludwell](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ludwell "Ludwell") 's **Door to the Underworld**, using the skeletons and zombies to substitute himself.
                - Using [Creeping Hunger](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Creeping_Hunger "Creeping Hunger"), [Klein](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") **Distorted** [Kircheis](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Kircheis "Kircheis") ' **Sulfur Fireballs**, they lost their trajectories and acted chaotically as though in random motion, some even flew backwards at Kircheis himself.
        - They can **Seal** ordinary rooms or **Unseal** confined spaces.
        - The action of "closing the door" can be **Distorted** into the rule " **Seal** this place, isolate the outside from the inside", one that can withstand the bombardment of several **Sulfur Fireballs** from a **[Desire Apostle](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Abyss_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_5:_Desire_Apostle "Abyss Pathway/Abilities")** afflicted with **Bribe-Weaken**.
                - [Mr. A](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mr._A "Mr. A") **Distorted** [Bakerland](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Bakerland "Bakerland") 's order to stay away from his room and to not be disturbed, which made it as though the room became a completely isolated world.
                - [Lumian](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee") **Distorted** the action of "opening the door" with the concept of " **Unsealing** this confined space" to open the **Bottle of Fiction**.
        - Other uses of this ability include:
        - [Klein](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") used [Creeping Hunger](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Creeping_Hunger "Creeping Hunger") and **Distorted** the goal of "separate and intercept" into "separate and take action alone", making [Kircheis](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Kircheis "Kircheis") and his companions separate.
- **Corrosion**: It can turn the hearts of people within *10 meters* dark and greedy, making them make irrational choices.

## Authority → Power Mapping

This maps conceptual origin; it does not mean a Sequence 6 Baron of Corruption owns an Authority.

- [[Distortion (Black Emperor)]] → **Distortion** — direct partial-authority expression, exploiting loopholes in **Order** to formulate an advantageous **Order**.
- [[Disorder (Black Emperor)]] → **Corrosion** — the forced descent into irrational, uncontrolled thought parallels Disorder's effect on a target's thoughts and state.
- **Physical Enhancement** remains a generic Beyonder trait without a supported Authority assignment.
