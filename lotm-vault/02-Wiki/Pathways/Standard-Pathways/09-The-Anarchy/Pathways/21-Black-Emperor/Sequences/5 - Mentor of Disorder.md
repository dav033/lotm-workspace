# Sequence 5 - Mentor of Disorder (Disorder)

## Explicit Powers

### Traits

- **Physical Enhancement**: A **Mentor of Disorders** physique is further enhanced.

### New Abilities

- **Disorder**: They can produce a **Disorder** effect on a target or the surrounding area.
    - A **Mentor of Disorder** can use **Disorder** to make it difficult for attacks to land on them.
        - They can cause the **Wind Blades** of an **[Ocean Songster](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tyrant_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_5:_Ocean_Songster "Tyrant Pathway/Abilities")** to move in every direction at random to perfectly avoid hitting the target.
        - They can make enemies easily "choose" to make the wrong judgment.
        - The negative effects of the [Hand of Horror](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hand_of_Horror "Hand of Horror") caused [Audrey](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Audrey_Hall "Audrey Hall") to unknowingly make a mistake, making her take a bath in an abnormal procedure.
        - They can **Disorder** the standards of distance measurement.
        - [Bird Mustang](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Bird_Mustang "Bird Mustang") covered quite a sizable distance in about eight steps to arrive in front of [Nast Solomon](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Nast_Solomon "Nast Solomon") 's cabin.
                - They can make it so walking to the left would cause one to walk forward. This can cause an **[Ocean Songster](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tyrant_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_5:_Ocean_Songster "Tyrant Pathway/Abilities")**, a **Beyonder** with exceptional **Balance**, to hobble forward.
        - In conjunction with **Distortion**, they can extricate themselves from unfavorable situations.
        - One such situation is a **[Wraith](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chained_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_5:_Wraith "Chained Pathway/Abilities") 's Possession**.
- **Commanding Presence:** A **Mentor of Disorder** is able to display a sense of great royalty, a majesty that causes others to want to lower their bodies and obey their every command.

### Strengthened Abilities

- **Distortion**: The **Baron of Corruption** 's power is elevated to the Sequence 5 level.
    - The **Distortion** of a target's language, actions, and intentions gains enhancements.
        - The **Seal** created by **Distortion** can barely withstand several **Sulfur Fireballs** from a **[Desire Apostle](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Abyss_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_5:_Desire_Apostle "Abyss Pathway/Abilities")** even without the support of **Bribe-Weaken**.
                - [Lockhart Siakam](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lockhart_Siakam "Lockhart Siakam") was able to **Distort** the stairs to the attic, causing them to rise as if giving him a hand and thus allowing him to get closer to the attic.
                - [Lockhart Siakam](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lockhart_Siakam "Lockhart Siakam") had **Distorted** the surrounding vases and other objects in his way, causing them to avoid him as if they had a life of their own.
        - They can now **Distort** the concepts of oneself and the target, causing both parties to equally share the damage from **Curses**, **Diseases**, etc., they receive.
        - [Lockhart Siakam](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lockhart_Siakam "Lockhart Siakam") **Distorted** the concept of himself and [Jason Beria](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Jason_Beria "Jason Beria") such that half of the **Language of Foulness** 's effects were borne by the latter.
- They gain enhancements of other abilities from previous **Sequences**.

### Notes

## Authority → Power Mapping

This maps conceptual origin; it does not mean a Sequence 5 Mentor of Disorder owns an Authority.

- [[Disorder (Black Emperor)]] → **Disorder** — direct partial-authority expression.
- [[Distortion (Black Emperor)]] → **Distortion** — direct partial-authority expression, enhanced from earlier Sequences.
- [[Autocrat (Black Emperor)]] → **Commanding Presence** — echoes the **Strata**/**Superior** subordinate authority (per the Sequence 0 authority tree) seen as "Commanding Presence" in the Frenzied Mage Sequence.
- **Physical Enhancement** remains a generic Beyonder trait without a supported Authority assignment.

## Potion

### Potion Properties

> A jet-black liquid that generates its own vortexes and waves, sometimes expanding and sometimes contracting.

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> A tightly clenched, semi-transparent, jet-black fist. The fist gives a direct sense of power and unnerving strangeness. The patterns, light spots and nails on it seem to follow normal rules, yet they are full of incongruous meaning, as if they are hiding a lot of madness and disorder.

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- 1 Fairy Spark (possesses self-awareness).
- The head of "Child of the Forest" (it's an almost black dark green baboon with deformed horns resembling a crown).

Or
- A Mentor of Disorder Beyonder Characteristic.

Supplementary Ingredients

- 100 ml of a "Child of the Forest's" blood
- 7 drops of cedarwood hydrolat,
- An ear of a serial traitor (has betrayed at least twice),
- 1 frangipani flower.

### Advancement Ritual

Become the dominant force in a city's underground order, causing the city's crime rate to increase more than tenfold; this state must last for at least three months.
