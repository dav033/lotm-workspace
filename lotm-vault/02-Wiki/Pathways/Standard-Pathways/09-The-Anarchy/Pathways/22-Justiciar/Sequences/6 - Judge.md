# Sequence 6 - Judge

## Explicit Powers

### Strengthened Abilities

- **Authority**: A **Judge's** **Authority** have undergone a qualitative change, not only making them have an indescribable aura that makes people around them subconsciously avoid their gazes but also being able to influence the environment.
    - They can use **Mystical Languages** such as **Ancient** **Hermes** to establish new **Rules** or make a corresponding **Verdict** for a certain area. Everything within that area must abide by the **Rules** or the **Verdict's** outcome and violations will result in being bound by the **Rules**.
        - Both the **Judge's Spirituality** and **Rules** limit **Verdict** to a degree.
        - Examples of common **Verdicts** are:
        - **Prohibition**: Pushing forward with an open palm, a **Judge** can use **Ancient Hermes** to establish a rule forbidding certain actions or **Beyonder** powers, forcing everyone within a certain range to comply with the **Prohibition**.
            - **Prohibitions** generally follow this format: "'X' is **Prohibited** here".
                - Some of the examples of the **Beyonder** powers and actions a **Judge** can **Prohibit** include the following: **Flight**, floating, **Teleportation**, **Invisibility**, etc.
                    - When it comes to the **Prohibition** of **Invisibility**, those who are hidden in shadows won't be compelled to reveal themselves, as they weren't invisible to begin with. They had merely shape-shifted into a shadow being, blending with the shadows.
                        - At its current level, **Prohibition** targeting specific races or individuals cannot directly kill the target; it can only cause continuous suppression or repel them from the **Prohibition's** area of effect.
                - Such **Prohibitions** that are directly aimed at a particular existence will be quickly suppressed if there is a level difference.
                                - Examples of this are **Prohibiting** the existence of beings such as **Specters** and **[Wraiths](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chained_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_5:_Wraith)** within an area.
                        - With preparation and only providing assistance, it can influence even **Saints** to a certain extent, though not necessarily effectively, but it can cause some disruption.
                - [Frunziar](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Frunziar_Edward "Frunziar Edward") 's **Prohibition** of flight made [Ulyssan](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ulyssan "Ulyssan") 's wings seemingly weighed down by invisible objects weighing a hundred times its own body weight, though it eventually managed to fly with great difficulty.
                                - [Xio](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Xio_Derecha "Xio Derecha") caused [Botis](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Botis "Botis") 's normally instantaneous **Teleportation** to be blocked by a rusty, abnormally heavy door—something that couldn't keep him from leaving after making adjustments.
                - **Imprison**: This **Verdict** creates layers upon layers of transparent walls as well as a flowing, extremely viscous, transparent liquid that freezes the target in place, making it difficult for even **Spirits** to pass through.
            - **Imprison** can root **Spirit Body** type creatures in place  but can't prevent **Spirit Bodies** from escaping via abilities like **Substitution**.
                - **Release**: The effect can be ended early using the word " **Release** ".
                        - It will not trigger abilities such as **Mirror Substitution** unless they have been used beforehand.
                - **Confinement**: This is **Imprison** applied to a certain area, creating an immaterial wall—a transparent cell—with their target at its center, impenetrable by **Spirit Bodies**.
            - This can be used to circumvent **Substitution** abilities by **Confining** a small area.
                - This is because targets would reappear within the **Confinement's** boundaries after using **Substitution** abilities.
                        - This can be used to **Seal** a room to prevent entry or exit.
                - **Confinement** caused the dining room of [Capim](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Capim "Capim") to "freeze", as if an additional layer of invisible wall surrounded the area.
                - **Maintain Secrecy**: By making eye contact with the target, their eyes flash with a strange light, making the other person feel that they must do as instructed and not reveal secrets.
                - **Death**: After declaring the target as **Dead**, their body merges with some strange force, dragging a blur at speeds that create afterimages as they crash into the enemy.
            - Their fist strikes the target's head with an unavoidable force, causing devastating damage and it can also be aimed at a specific spot on the target, inflicting a severe injury.
                - **Flog**: Their hand is swung so that the target appears to be struck by an invisible soft whip, which can tear clothes, lacerate flesh, and expose bone.
                - **Exile**: By saying the word " **Exile** ", they can send an immense and invisible power that forces targets out at high speeds.
            - This affects **Spirit Bodies** as well, causing them to be blown away.
- **Jurisdiction**: A **Judge's** area of **Jurisdiction** is enhanced.
    - The upper limit of its maximum range approaches the size of [Backlund](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Backlund "Backlund").

## Authority → Power Mapping

This maps conceptual origin; it does not mean a Sequence 6 Judge owns an Authority.

- [[Judgement (Justiciar)]] → **Authority**, **Prohibition**, **Imprison**, **Release**, **Confinement**, **Maintain Secrecy**, **Death**, **Flog**, **Exile** — direct partial-authority expression, the suite of Verdicts a Judge can impose on those within their established Rules.
- [[Order (Justiciar)]] → **Jurisdiction** — echoes Order's domain-of-effect, the area within which new Rules and Verdicts hold.
