# Sequence 1 - Hand of Order

## Explicit Powers

### Authorities

- **Order**: "They" gain partial authority over **Order**.
    - **Order Proxy**: "They" are able to establish many new **Rules** (not at the underlying level) for an entire battlefield, all "Their" enemies, an entire city, or a small nation.
        - "They" are able to modify or even temporarily remove existing **Rules** (not at the underlying level), but these rules cannot be completely fixed and remain in effect permanently.
                - Due to the characteristics of the, if the **[Nation of Disorder](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Nation_of_Disorder)** gives permission, "They" can act as a **Proxy**. Otherwise, not. It must have a compliant procedure.
            - If the **Hand of Order** has authorization from the [Celestial Thearch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Celestial_Thearch "Celestial Thearch"), then without harming "Themselves", "They" can reach [King of Kings of Angels](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Kings_of_Angels#Kings_of_Kings_of_Angels "Kings of Angels") level of strength for *3 seconds*.
                - At the cost of certain death, "They" can reach the level of the [weakest tier of Half Great Old One](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Half_Great_Old_Ones#Tier_3 "Half Great Old Ones").
                        - When the [Sefirah](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirah "Sefirah") has no consciousness and operates via [Symbolism](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Symbolism "Symbolism"), it must use specific methods to possibly succeed. Those specific methods need to be figured out.
        - "They" can sense the changes in **Order**.
        - "They" can observe the tug-of-war between **Disorder** and **Balance**, the evolving **Order** of the battlefield, and the enemy forces' formation, carefully calculating the **Rules,** and what kind of **Order** should be established.
                - [William Augustus I](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/William_Augustus_I "William Augustus I") managed to sense the death of [George III](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/George_Augustus_III "George Augustus III") through the changes in **Order**.
        - "They" can quell **Disorder** and **Anomalies**, exist in an independent position, and control the effects.
        - After dividing the battlefield into different sections, [William Augustus I](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/William_Augustus_I "William Augustus I") ’s **Historical Projection** stood at an isolated spot, ensuring that none of the aftershocks attacked the mausoleum below.

### Strengthened Abilities

- "They" gain an enhancement of abilities from previous **Sequences**.

This maps conceptual origin; it does not mean a Sequence 1 Hand of Order owns an Authority.

## Potion

### Potion Properties

> A pure, transparent and unblemished brass colored liquid.

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> A brass 'fist' with clearly defined knuckles that clenches tightly.

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- A Hand of Order Beyonder Characteristic

Supplementary Ingredients

- 1 code of law that has remained unchanged for over 300 years
- 9,000 portions of blood from lawbreakers (each portion must be at least 5 ml; the higher the level of the lawbreaker, the fewer portions are required)
- 9 abolished laws that violated normal circumstances
- 100 grams of contaminants caused by a fallen True God

### Advancement Ritual

Equivalent Rituals:

- Establish a nation with a population of at least 10 million, making one's will the order and a strict system of rules within that nation. or
- Control a nation with a population of at least 50 million, making one's will the order and a strict system of rules within that nation.
    - In both rituals, the more Beyonders and the higher their level among the citizens, the fewer people are required, but the minimum population cannot be less than 1 million.
