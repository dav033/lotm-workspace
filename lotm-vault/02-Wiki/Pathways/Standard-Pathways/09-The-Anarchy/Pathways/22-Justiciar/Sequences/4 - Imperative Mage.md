# Sequence 4 - Imperative Mage

## Explicit Powers

### Traits

- **Physical Enhancement**: They have gained enhancement to their physical fitness.

### New Abilities

- **Imperatives**: The core ability of an **Imperative Mage**; It allows them to set broad restrictions and maintain them for a period of time.
    - The broader the restrictions set by an **Imperative**, the shorter the duration.
        - Generally speaking, the shortest duration is *30 seconds* and the longest is *15 minutes*.
        - Examples of the **Imperatives** they can employ are:
        - " *Weaken Mysticism, Enhance Reality* " will greatly weaken all **Beyonders** and mysterious effects within an area (including their side), while strengthening **Reality's** influence.
            - This is a powerful tool for the military in **Reality** to combat **Beyonders**.
                - " *Weaken Reality, Enhance Mysticism* " is the opposite **Imperative** of the former, which is able to enhance **Beyonder** effects while weakening the influence of **Reality**.
                - Appropriate **Imperatives** are also able to help the them accomplish many things/effects that are normally impossible.
            - For example, levitation, or being able to slow down bullets and cannonballs as though they sunk into a quagmire formed of air.
- **Contracts**: **Imperative** **Mages** can also create **Contracts** that conform to the **Rules** of the world and internalize these contracts into various restrictions on the physical and **Spirit** **Bodies**.
    - Unlike a **[Notary](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sun_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_6:_Notary)**, the binding force of an **Imperative Mage's** **Contracts** comes from the rule-based **Order**, rather than from the witness of [Gods](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Gods "Gods") or higher beings.

### Strengthened Abilities

- The abilities of all the previous **Sequences** have been massively strengthened.
    - **Supernatural Intuition**: As practitioners of **Order**, **Imperative Mages** can distinguish between ordinary people and **Beyonders** based on their "positions" and can even determine the level of a **Beyonder**, that is, their "position".
        - **Beyonders** who have **Concealment** powers to some degree, such as **[Bizarro Sorcerers](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fool_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_4:_Bizarro_Sorcerer)**, are able to hide their **Sequence** level from them but not their status as a **Beyonder**.
                - They can also perceive the intertwining **Beyonder** powers of their targets, manifesting in different colors and states. This allows them to use **Deprivation** effectively.
        - **Authority**: Their **Authority** has been amplified to the point that it can intimidate the target and leave them rooted to the spot, also their **Verdicts** have been enhanced as well.
        - **Prohibition**: They can now **Prohibit** more actions and **Beyonder** powers.
            - They can **Prohibit** an area from having any interaction from the outside world.
                        - They can **Prohibit** stand-ins, preventing any usage of **Substitution** abilities.
                        - They are able to **Prohibit Marionette Interchange**.
                - **Deprivation**: **Deprives** a target of a certain **Beyonder** power.
            - Stripped **Beyonder** powers are made unusable for a certain period of time.
                - **Deprivation** is unavoidable and could only be resisted or weakened through one's level or **Sequence's** characteristics.
                        - Different from a **[Depriver](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Tail-Devourer_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_5:_Depriver) 's** **Deprivation** which takes away the corresponding Beyonder Characteristics or the [Error Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Error_Pathway) 's **Theft** which is within the **Inconceivable** symbolism, the **Deprivation** of an **Imperative Mage** only **Deprives** the target from using that power and does not completely take it away from them.
                - **Exile**: Saying the word " **Exile** ", they can **Exile** their targets into a void, the destination unknown. This allows them to deal with enemies that cannot be resolved quickly.
                - **Execution**: An **Imperative Mage** can make a fist with their right hand and swing it down hard, which can directly **Execute** the target and kill them from afar.
                - **Confinement**: By making the left hand loosely clenched, they can **Confine** the target.
                - **Intimidation**: This **Verdict** **Intimidates** the target and keeps them in place.

### Mythical Creature Form

- As **Demigods** at **Sequence 4** who have obtained partial **Godhood**, they are able to manifest their incomplete **Mythical Creature** **Form** if they disregard any of the consequences and become an incomplete giant **Brass Pillar**.

## Authority → Power Mapping

This maps conceptual origin; it does not mean a Sequence 4 Imperative Mage owns an Authority.

- [[Order (Justiciar)]] → **Imperatives**, **Contracts**, **Supernatural Intuition** — direct partial-authority expression; the text directly frames Supernatural Intuition as a trait of "practitioners of Order," and Contracts derive their binding force from rule-based Order rather than a witness.
- [[Judgement (Justiciar)]] → **Authority**, **Prohibition**, **Deprivation**, **Exile**, **Execution**, **Confinement**, **Intimidation** — direct partial-authority expression, the suite of Verdicts imposed on those who fall under judgment.
- **Physical Enhancement** remains a generic Beyonder trait without a supported Authority assignment.

Supplementary Ingredients

- 100 ml of blood from a Mask Elf
- 10 g of rust sediment from a Copper Faced Evil Spirit
- 1 copy of the current Kingdom's main legal code
- 10 drops of Mandrake hydrolat

- Create a law that differs from, but does not exclude, existing laws, and have it promulgated and implemented through the current national legislative process
    - Once ten thousand people have violated this law and have been punished accordingly, consume the potion.
