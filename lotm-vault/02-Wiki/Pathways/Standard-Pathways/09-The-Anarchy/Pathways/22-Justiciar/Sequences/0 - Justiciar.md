# Sequence 0 - Justiciar

## Explicit Powers

### Translation Note

In the original Chinese text, 律法 (lǜfǎ) and 规律 (guīlǜ) are distinct terms with different nuances, though both can be translated as “ **Laws** ”, as is done in the English WebNovel translation. Since these terms are both distinct authorities, this page will consistently use the same English words to refer to the same Chinese words throughout for clarity and distinction.
- **Laws** (律法, *lǜfǎ*):
    - Refers to codified, enforceable laws—rules established by authority, often carrying judicial or binding force. It aligns with legal systems, decrees, and formal structures of governance.
        - Officially translated as "laws and decrees"  and "set of laws"  on *some* relevant chapters.
- **Principles** (规律 *guīlǜ*):
    - Refers to inherent patterns or natural laws—the underlying order of reality that exists independently of authority. It is closer to universal principles or the fundamental workings of the world.
        - In other contexts this term can also denote simpler patterns or regularity, not a strict law, but rather an observable consistency or recurring behavior over time such as everyday routines or behavioral patterns.

On the other hand, the symbolism of **Laws** also uses different Chinese characters, 定律 (dìnglǜ), which are also the same characters used for the "Laws" in the Laws of Beyonder Characteristics.

- **Laws** (定律, *dìnglǜ*)
    - Refers to fixed laws or established principles—a stable, universally applicable rule that does not change.

### Authorities

- **[[Order (Justiciar)|Order]]**: The **Justiciar** possesses the **Order** authority and its corresponding subordinate authorities over **[[Rules (Justiciar)|Rules]]**, **[[Laws (Justiciar)|Laws]]**, **[[Principles (Justiciar)|Principles]]**, etc.
    - "They" can modify the underlying **Rules** of the world to some extent.
        - Compared to the **[Paragon's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Paragon_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Paragon)** ability to change **Laws of Physics** versus the **Justiciar's** ability to change the underlying **Rules**, the **Justiciar** is stronger as it also effects mystical **Rules**.
                - The [Trunsoest Brass Book](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Trunsoest_Brass_Book "Trunsoest Brass Book") has **Rules** presented in grayish-black that, if it were able to awaken further, it could change and do the following modifications:
            - " **[Scholar of Yore](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fool_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_3:_Scholar_of_Yore)** has the ability to summon **Historical Void Projections**. The success rate of **Summoning** depends on the familiarity and friendliness of the target..."
                - It might have reduced the success rate of summoning **Historical Void** **Projections**.
                        - "When one’s rationality drops to 20% of its original value, there would be signs of losing control..."
                - It may cause [Beyonders](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Beyonders "Beyonders") to show signs of losing control if their rationality falls a little.
                - "They" can make certain modifications to the **Rules** of the **[Red Priest's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Priest_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Red_Priest)** **Sacrifices**.
        - "They" are able to remove existing, non-underlying **Rules**.
        - "They" are able to establish various new **Rules** for the world.
        - These **Rules** can't be fundamental but they will be permanent, with an effect ranging from small to large, from a single nation to a planet or a star system.
            - The **[Son of Chaos](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Son_of_Chaos)** can even influence the **Laws of Beyonder** **Characteristics** such as **Indestructibility**, **Convergence**, and **Separation** to a certain extent, making limited, low-level, and small-scale modifications.
                - **Rules** have to be publicized or announced before it could truly be effective. Thus, **Concealing** the announcement of those **Laws** will make them ineffective.
                - The **Rules** can be made stricter over time with each additional clause. It can reach a level where it will be impossible for a person to abide by "Their" **Laws**.
            - The **Rules** that had been created will restrict "Them" as well. However, "They" could get rid of those **Rules** to break the restriction.
                - "They" can theoretically create a method for **Resurrection**; however, the **Rules** involved must be mutually reinforcing and can't directly stipulate "Their" **Revival**.
            - For instance, "They" can establish that the **Souls** of the deceased in this area remain preserved and do not enter the [Spirit World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_World "Spirit World"). Then "They" need to specify the duration of **Soul** preservation before stipulating that **Ghosts** can retain their memories. Only then, through step-by-step regulations, can **Resurrection** be achieved.
                - However, this approach is overly complex and easily disrupted.
                - The [Trunsoest Brass Book](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Trunsoest_Brass_Book "Trunsoest Brass Book") set several different **Rules**, each with its own corresponding **Judgment** when broken.
            - *"Lost items are regarded as abandonment."*
                        - *"All living beings are equal before the **Law**. Even [Angels](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Angel "Angel") can be killed by ordinary people"*.
                        - *“Resurrection of the deceased is prohibited here!”*
                - This weakened the will of the [Celestial Worthy](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Celestial_Worthy "Celestial Worthy") awakening inside the body of [Klein](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti").
                        - *"Without [my](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Trunsoest_Brass_Book "Trunsoest Brass Book") permission, no living being can leave [this place](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Belltaine_City "Belltaine City")."*
                - This prevented [Klein Moretti](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Klein_Moretti "Klein Moretti") 's **Spirit Body** from leaving and entering **[Sefirah Castle](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sefirah_Castle)**.
        - In barren and dead places, both the **Justiciar** and the **[Black Emperor](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Black_Emperor_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Black_Emperor)** can rebuild a complete set of **Order** suitable for human survival.
        - The **Justiciar** relies on the original fundamental **Rules** to recreate an **Order** suitable for human survival, while the **[Black Emperor](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Black_Emperor_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Black_Emperor)** will **Distort** the original **Rules** to make them suitable for humans. The effect is the same, but the methods of realization differ.
        - The **Justiciar** can grant **Immunity** to specific individuals.
        - This will enable those individuals to disregard the effects of indiscriminate **Rules**.
        - **Order Proxy**: "They" can reach the [strongest tier of Half Great Old Ones](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Half_Great_Old_Ones#Tier_1 "Half Great Old Ones").
        - "They" can completely obtain all the abilities and symbolisms on the level of the [Celestial Thearch](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Celestial_Thearch "Celestial Thearch") in [The Apocalypse](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/The_Apocalypse "The Apocalypse") war, but "They" would die and could only maintain this state for *less than 3 seconds*.
- **[[Judgement (Justiciar)|Judgment]]**: Anyone who violates the **Order** can be **Judged**.
    - Each **Judgment** will be supported by the will and power of the entire world, and this can be performed either remotely or in person.
        - The [Trunsoest Brass Book](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Trunsoest_Brass_Book "Trunsoest Brass Book") could **Punish** targets each time its **Laws** had been broken, which would also increase the corresponding level of **Punishment** until it reaches **Death**.
- **[[Disorder (Justiciar)|Disorder]]**: "They" have partial authority over **Disorder**.
    - The **Justiciar** is able to restore **Disorder**, quell **Disorder**, and extinguish **Disorder**. **Order** can also create **Disorder**.
- **[[Balance (Justiciar)|Balance]]**: "They" are able to force **Balance**.
    - In addition to the previously manifested **Balances**, it can also **Balance** many abstract things, such as restoring the **Balance** between reason and madness for a certain period of time, but only *3 minutes* at most.
- **Divine Kingdom**: **Ancient Court of Judgment** or **Heaven of Laws**.

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- The Justiciar Uniqueness.
- Three Hand of Order Beyonder Characteristics.

Supplementary Ingredients

- An alternate world whose original rules have been completely replaced by one's own order.

### Advancement Ritual

Hold a sufficiently just trial concerning a serious matter involving a True God, in accordance with the rules one has previously established and ensure that the judgment is truly carried out, then take the potion.
