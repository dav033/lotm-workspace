# Sequence 5 - Disciplinary Paladin

## Explicit Powers

### Traits

- **Physical Enhancements**: Their body is sufficiently strong enough and sufficiently robust.
- **Enhanced Mental Attributes**: They have a mystical sense of self-restraint.
    - This makes them experts at resisting interrogation and prying.
        - They can resist truth serum and **Hypnosis**.
- Their intuition and **Spiritual** **Perception** have been enhanced.
    - This makes them able to pinpoint the source of the threads of a **[Demoness of Pleasure](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Demoness_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_6:_Pleasure_\(Demoness_of_Pleasure\))**.
        - They would be able to perceive any surreptitious modification of the terms the moment they sign a contract with an **[Overseer](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chaos_Mist_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_4:_Overseer)**.

### New Abilities

- **Punishment**: They can select a target for **Punishment**, after setting it, they would emanate a dawn-like glow, enhancing their influence and offensive power against the target.
    - Targets for **Punishment** include but are not limited to:
        - Those who attack the **Disciplinary Paladin** or affect their actions.
                - Those who are not part of the surrounding environment.
                - Those who violate a certain law or morality.
                - Those who should not affect **Reality**.
                - Those who violate their own rules.
        - They can choose non-living objects as targets for **Punishment**.
        - [Stratford](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Stratford "Stratford") chose the "bindings" restricting him to be a target of punishment, tearing the **Abyss Chains** apart with immense strength.
        - They can **Punish** the offender in different ways, for instance:
        - A **Disciplinary Paladin** can restrict the offender, seemingly immobilizing their target's feet with invisible shackles, making their movements rigid.
        - As the target is selected for **Punishment**, the **Disciplinary Paladin** will the seem to grow taller in the target's eyes, striking fear into their heart. The target will sense that the **Disciplinary Paladin** is a formidable figure who could end their existence with a mere thought.

### Strengthened Abilities

- **Authority**: The aura of their **Authority** causes surrounding beings to feel inexplicable horror. The affected target's movements become slow, and they unconsciously wish to lower their heads, prostrate themselves, listen to every word of the **Disciplinary Paladin** and obey every order given by them.
    - Some of the words they can utilize have undergone a qualitative change.
        - **Prohibition**: This is the **Judge** ability at **Sequence 5**, it can be stacked to further impose restrictions on targets that can resist the rules to some extent.
            - If an enemy violates the rules set by the user, the **Disciplinary Paladin** will be enhanced by an unknown force, attacking the enemy with a guaranteed hit.
                - **Death**: It has undergone a qualitative change. At this level, this **Verdict** can be used by them to target a specific location on the target, inflicting severe damage.
            - For example, shortly after [Siatas's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Siatas "Siatas") successful attack, [Frunziar Edward](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Frunziar_Edward "Frunziar Edward") used “ **Death** ” with the [North King’s](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ulyssan "Ulyssan") wound as the target, causing all the flesh and blood in the surrounding area to wither, making the wound open all the way to the bones.
- They have gained improvements to other abilities and traits from previous **Sequences**.

## Authority → Power Mapping

This maps conceptual origin; it does not mean a Sequence 5 Disciplinary Paladin owns an Authority.

- [[Judgement (Justiciar)]] → **Punishment**, **Authority**, **Prohibition**, **Death** — direct partial-authority expression, selecting and enforcing consequences on targets who violate a law, morality, or rule.
- **Physical Enhancements** and **Enhanced Mental Attributes** remain generic Beyonder traits without a supported Authority assignment.
