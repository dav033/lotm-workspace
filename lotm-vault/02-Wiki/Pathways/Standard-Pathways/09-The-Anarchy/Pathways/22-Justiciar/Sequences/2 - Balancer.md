# Sequence 2 - Balancer

## Explicit Powers

### Authorities

- **Balance**: "They" gain partial authority over **Balance**.
    - "They" can divide the battlefield, separating the various sides into different areas while roughly ensuring a **Balance** of power in each battlefield.
        - Before ending their own battle and making the corresponding battlefield cease to exist, neither [Angels](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Angels "Angels") nor [Saints](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Saints "Saints") can interfere in other battles.
            - Even a [Planeswalker](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Door_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_2:_Planeswalker) will be locked in their respective area, preventing them from joining other battlefields.
                - If one side becomes too strong, members who exceed the **Balance** limit will be excluded from the battlefield within a short period of time.
            - This will last anywhere between *10 seconds to 2 minutes*, within that time, those affected will be unable to participate in the battle.
                        - Alternatively, these members could freely choose their own battlefield if the **Balancer** does not prevent them from doing so.
                - The underlying **Rule** is to end **Disorder**, restore **Order**, and enforce **Balance**, however, this may not necessarily mean perfect **Balance**. Consequently, this **Balance** can **Distorted** by a **[Duke of Entropy](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Black_Emperor_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_2:_Duke_of_Entropy_\(Entropy\))**.
            - The **Rule** created by the **Pillar of Severity** created a disparity in strength between the two sides when including [Sealed Artifacts](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sealed_Artifacts "Sealed Artifacts"). The [Sun](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Derrick_Berg "Derrick Berg") could not use the [Proof of Glory](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Proof_of_Glory "Proof of Glory") due to its characteristics, while the [Demoness of Gold](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Demoness_of_Gold "Demoness of Gold") could freely use the [Eye of God](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Eye_of_God "Eye of God").
                        - Using [Solomon's Mask](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Solomon%27s_Mask "Solomon's Mask"), [Judgment](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Xio_Derecha "Xio Derecha") **Distorted** the **Balance** to become more extreme, allowing her and the [Demoness of Orange](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Demoness_of_Orange "Demoness of Orange") to join the [Sun](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Derrick_Berg "Derrick Berg") and the [Demoness of Gold](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Demoness_of_Gold "Demoness of Gold"), while forcing [Mirror Roselle Gustav](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mirror_Roselle_Gustav "Mirror Roselle Gustav") and the [Demoness of Black](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Clarice "Clarice") to face [Temperance](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sharron "Sharron") who wielded the [Box of the Great Old Ones](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Box_of_the_Great_Old_Ones "Box of the Great Old Ones").
        - In a one-on-one situation, a **Balancer** can forcibly reduce or increase the enemy's strength to a level roughly equivalent to their own within *1 minute*.
        - The stronger the enemy, the shorter the duration of this **Balance** with *1 minute* being the upper limit. In other words, this meant that the **Balancer** can be evenly matched with anyone, even a [True Deity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Gods "Gods"), though this will only last for *3 seconds*.
        - "They" can sense imbalances and **Disorder** in the surrounding area, allowing them to detect targets in a **Concealed** state.

### Strengthened Abilities

- **Laws**: They gain the ability to enact **Laws and Decrees** which allow multiple **Prohibitions** or **Enhancements** to take effect simultaneously by defining the environment.
    - [0-02](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/0-02 "0-02") declared *"This battlefield is a Land of Light, a domain of righteousness. All depravity and evil shall find no footing."*
        - This caused the [Mirror World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mirror_World "Mirror World") to be illuminated by an unknown source of light and the head of [The Setting Sun Rooster](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/The_Setting_Sun_Rooster "The Setting Sun Rooster") to droop lifelessly with a snap, unable to utter corrupted words or emanate **Depravity**. It also made the [Demoness of Cyan](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Yalenna "Yalenna") unable to cast **Curses** or other **Dark Magic**.
- "They" gain an enhancement of abilities from previous **Sequences**.

### Mythical Creature Form

- "They" have become a full **Mythical Creature**; "Their" **Mythical Creature Form** is that of a giant **Brass Pillar** with rules written on it.

## Authority → Power Mapping

This maps conceptual origin; it does not mean a Sequence 2 Balancer owns an Authority.

- [[Balance (Justiciar)]] → **Balance** — direct partial-authority expression.
- [[Laws (Justiciar)]] → **Laws** (the Laws and Decrees ability) — direct partial-authority expression, defining an environment to enact multiple Prohibitions or Enhancements simultaneously.

- 1 item that only praises the Sun
- 1 wisp of the temptation to deprave from the Abyss of Mind/Heart
- 150 grams of contaminants originating from a "Duke of Entropy" or similar beings

### Advancement Ritual

- End the chaos on a continent and bring about a balance among the various forces which can be maintained for at least a hundred years.
    - The ritual does not require for one to be in a dominant position, but they must play a crucial role.
