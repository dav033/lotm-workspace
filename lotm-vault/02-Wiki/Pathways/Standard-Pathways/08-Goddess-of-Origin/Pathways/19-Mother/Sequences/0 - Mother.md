# Sequence 0 - Mother

## Explicit Powers

### Authorities

- [[Mother (Authority)]]: This authority of the **Mother** is derived from the **Mother of All** symbolism.
    - The **Mother** gives birth to all things, including [True Deities](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Gods "Gods"), concepts, and worlds.
        - "She" can help catalyze the [activation](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Activation "Activation") of an inanimate [Uniqueness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniqueness "Uniqueness") to make it come to life.
            - The [Brood Hive](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Brood_Hive "Brood Hive") 's influence over the [Knowledge Moor](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Knowledge_Moor "Knowledge Moor") [activated](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Activation "Activation") the [Hermit Uniqueness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniquenesses#Hermit_Pathway) and made the [Hidden Sage](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hidden_Sage "Hidden Sage") come to life.
                        - [Lilith](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lilith "Lilith") used "Her" **Mother** Authority to catalyze the [activation](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Activation "Activation") of the [Error Uniqueness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Uniquenesses#Error_Pathway), helping [Amon](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Amon "Amon") come to life.
- [[Earth (Mother)]]: The authority of **Earth** is derived from the two symbols, **Mother of All** and **Brood Hive of Filth**.
    - Once a portion of **Nature**, **Earth** was given the meanings of **Origin of Life**, **Mother of All Things**, and the destination of which all things **Return**. Through this, the **Earth** had completed the **Cycle** of **Reality** and became independent from **Nature**.
        - "She" is able to make the **Earth** and surrounding land quake violently, tearing open a huge hole and rift across an entire planet that leads directly to the [Chaos Sea](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Chaos_Sea "Chaos Sea") at its innermost core.
            - This is a symbolic **Return** to the **Origin**.
- [[Nature (Mother)]]: This authority is derived from the **Goddess of Origin** symbolism.
    - The **Mother** is a part of **Reality**, and is difficult to completely destroy or truly **Seal** unless "She" willingly yields, **Returns** to the true [Mother Goddess](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Goddess_of_Depravity "Mother Goddess of Depravity"), or is replaced.
        - "She" possesses immense vitality and regenerative prowess, able to survive direct attacks from [True Deities](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Gods "Gods") without offering any resistance.
            - Under the **Fire of Destruction** created by a [Dual Pathway](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Pathways "Pathways") [True Deity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Gods "Gods"), [Lumian Lee](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee"), [Earth Mother’s](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lilith "Lilith") immense vitality tried to reconstitute "Her" and bring her back to **Life**, even though "She" offered no resistance.
        - "She" is able to cause plants to grow wildly, forming vast **Natural** barriers that are capable of withstanding the attacks of even [True Gods](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/True_Gods "True Gods").
        - "She" is able to create a barrier made of *clusters of wheat, flowers, mushrooms, newborns*, *and trees* that intertwine layer by layer, **Sealing** what is inside.
            - Each damaged layer- whether it's the wheat, flowers, mushrooms, trees, or newborns- would quickly return to "Her" embrace and grow again and be reborn, maintaining the barrier "She" has made.
- [[Death (Mother)]]: "She" possesses a partial authority over **Death**, which is derived from the symbols of **Goddess of Origin** and **Brood Hive of Filth**.
    - " *From the **Mother** you came, and to the **Mother** you return* " - such is the **Origin** of all things, and **Returning** signifies **Death**.
        - "She" can frantically drain the **Life** of even [True Deities](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Gods "Gods") should "She" pierce "Them" in the heart, dragging "Them" down in an act of **Returning** it to the **Earth**, back to a **Mother's** embrace.
            - Such an ability was demonstrated when [Earth Mother](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lilith "Lilith") stabbed the [God of Combat](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/God_of_Combat "God of Combat") in the heart, an act that eventually led to "His" death as "His" vitality began to perish.
        - As the **Brood Hive** also has a part of the **Death** symbolism, the authority of **Death** also points to **Corruption**, **Loss of Control**, and **Bodily Mutation**.
- [[Life (Mother)]]: "She" also possesses a partial authority over **Life**, derived from the symbols **Mother of All** and **Goddess of Origin**.
    - "Her" presence alone would make all kinds of plants mutate and grow uncontrollably, wildly absorbing nutrients from unknown sources; even withered plants would grow once more.
        - In just *7 or 8 seconds*, many places in [Backlund](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Backlund "Backlund") became a primeval forests. It was as if the city had been abandoned for more than a century, with towering trees connected to one another and covering half the surrounding *sky*.
        - The **Mother** is able to make other creatures **Mutate** uncontrollably, turning them into blooming plants or making them bear fruit, returning them to the land.
        - [Amon's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Amon "Amon") **Avatars** experienced this effect from "Her" authority, with some turning into plants that bloomed or bore fruit, **Returning** to the land.
        - The ability to make someone instantly **Pregnant** is the result of a qualitative change of the ability to grant **Life**.
        - [Omebella's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Omebella "Omebella") [corpse](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Gift_of_the_Land "Gift of the Land") could make people pregnant because "She" had once accommodated the.
- **Bountiful Earth**: The **Divine Kingdom** of the **Mother** is a bountiful and joyous land filled with the **Mother** ’s own definition of **Nature**, **Life**, and **Death**.
    - However, its **Cycle** remains incomplete.
- **Return**: This is a lower authority derived from the **Earth** and **Death** authorities.
    - Through cooperation with the **[Death](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Death_\(Eternal_Sleeper\))**, "They" could make "Their" own **Cycle of Reincarnation**.
        - This will be similar to the **Cycle of Reincarnation** formed by the **[Eternal Darkness](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Eternal_Darkness)** and **[Mother Goddess of Depravity](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Goddess_of_Depravity)**, albeit limited to a single star system.
        - The authority of **Reincarnation** is shared with the **[Wheel of Fortune](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Wheel_of_Fortune_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_0:_Wheel_of_Fortune)**.
- **Healing (Treatment)**: A lower authority originating from the **Mother** and **Life** authorities.
    - [Earth Mother](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lilith "Lilith"), alongside [Demigods](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Saints "Saints") and [Angels](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Angels "Angels") from the [Church of the Earth Mother](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Church_of_the_Earth_Mother "Church of the Earth Mother") were able to protect [Naboredisley](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Naboredisley "Naboredisley") 's true body residing on [Hanth Island](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hanth_Island "Hanth Island") from the full **Corruption** from the [Mother Tree of Desire](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mother_Tree_of_Desire "Mother Tree of Desire").
        - This happened by severing [Soul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul") fragments of the [blood-colored Demon](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Naboredisley "Naboredisley"), and giving them **New Life**, enabling them to utilize the **Cycle** of **Rebirth**, growth, aging, **Death**, and **Rebirth** again to resist the **Corruption** spreading from their true bodies. Aging [Soul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul") fragments are inherently more vulnerable to **Corruption** from the main body, and newborns are the healthiest.
- **Desolation**: A lower authority born from the intersection of the **Nature** and **Death** authorities.
- **Transmutation**: A lower authority originating from the **Mother** and **Life** authorities.
- **Harvest**: This is a lesser manifestation of the **Nature** and **Life** authorities.

## Authority → Power Mapping

The cited secondary inventory associates Sequence 0 **Mother** with the Authorities catalogued above. Their ownership and scope remain interpretations; the Pathway Hub separately identifies the Great Old One.

- [[Mother (Authority)]] → embodied directly as the "Mother" authority listed above.
- [[Earth (Mother)]] → embodied directly as the "Earth" authority listed above.
- [[Nature (Mother)]] → embodied directly as the "Nature" authority listed above.
- [[Death (Mother)]] → embodied directly as the "Death" authority listed above.
- [[Life (Mother)]] → embodied directly as the "Life" authority listed above.
- **Bountiful Earth**, **Return**, **Healing (Treatment)**, **Desolation**, **Transmutation**, and **Harvest** are lower authorities compounded from the five above (as their own entries describe) and have no standalone Authority page.
