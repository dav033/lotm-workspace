# Sequence 2 - Desolate Matriarch

## Explicit Powers

### Gender Transition

- Advancing to a **Desolate Matriarch** results in becoming a female regardless of the original gender.

### Authorities

- **Earth**, **Desolation**, **Harvest, & Life**: A **Desolate Matriarch** holds a partial authority over **Earth**, **Desolation**, **Harvest**, and **Life**.
    - "They" can induce land within *300 kilometers* to bloom with **Life**, making soil fertile, water flow abundant, boosting the tendency of **Life** to reproduce, and preventing factors that hinder it.
        - Even the most unfavorable environmental conditions won't stop **Life** within this area from bearing offspring, only reducing the quantity and frequency of **Life** produced.
        - "They" can cause the land within a *300-kilometer range* to become barren, causing crop failure, and plants to wither. Unless the effect ends, the affected land won't become fertile again.
        - [Angel](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Angels "Angels") -level abilities similar to **Purification** are able to suppress but not fully dispel this, limiting the effect it causes to a very small area and making it unable to spread.
        - **Desolation**: "They" can cause all **Life** within a *30-kilometer range* to rapidly drain away until **Death**. Similar abilities outside this domain will also gradually wither and be weakened.
        - Lifeless **Undead** who enter this domain will quickly return to the **Earth**.
        - Within a *50-kilometer range*, the **Desolate Matriarch** can rapidly form various toxins within the environment and increase the tendency of every **Beyonder** to lose control.
        - "They" can also grow all kinds of extraordinary plants that are beneficial to "Them" as well as tame any animals within range that does not possess higher intelligence.
        - The **Desolate Matriarch** can also create edible plants in any environment and promote their growth, reproduction as well as **Harvest** within a *50-kilometer radius*.
        - In the [Forsaken Land of the Gods](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Forsaken_Land_of_the_Gods "Forsaken Land of the Gods"), the powers of [Gift of the Land](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Gift_of_the_Land "Gift of the Land") had created [Black Faced Grass](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Creatures_and_Plants#Black_Faced_Grass "Creatures and Plants") that inhabitants in the [City of Silver](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/City_of_Silver "City of Silver") survived on.

Strengthened Abilities ****

- **Evil Spirit Transformation**: "They" are able to turn all dead humans within a *50 kilometer range* into [Evil Spirits](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Evil_Spirits "Evil Spirits"), with the weakest being at **Sequence 5** with the strongest as **Demigods**.
    - This is equivalent to mass-producing **Sequence 5 Beyonders** and creating a number of **Demigod** -level [Evil Spirits](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Evil_Spirits "Evil Spirits") which can cause an area to be inhabitable for years.
        - Those who have eaten food created by the **Desolate Matriarch** or have strong connection to "Her" within a *300 kilometers* radius will also become [Evil Spirits](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Evil_Spirits "Evil Spirits").
- **Artificial Life Creation**: "They" are able to create dolls and **Golems** from **Soil**, **Plants**, **Lava**, **Ores**, and **Metals**, and give them some corresponding level of **Life** and power.
    - The strongest of the dolls and golems "They" can create can only be at the level of a [Sequence 3](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sequence "Sequence"), and "They" can only make a maximum of nine [Demigod-level](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sequence "Sequence") constructs in total.
- **Mythical Creature Transformation**: The improved version of **Animal Transformation**. "They" are able to briefly **Transform** "Themselves" into a **Mythical Creature**.
    - However, "They" must be able to withstand the mysticism knowledge and maddening will brought about by the corresponding **Mythical Creature** **Form**.
- **Life Deprivation**: Each strike of the **Desolation Matriarch** will now subject the target to a trial of **Losing Control** and a trial of **Death**.
    - If the target is unable to pass, they will either **Lose Control** on the spot or **Die** instantly.
        - "They" can make the cracked soil erupt with brown tree roots that shoot out from all directions, covering the sky and earth. Being struck by these roots means **Death**, or worse, **Losing Control**.

Mythical Creature Form

- "They" have become a complete [Mythical Creature](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mythical_Creature_Form "Mythical Creature Form"). "Their" [Mythical Creature Form](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mythical_Creature_Form "Mythical Creature Form") is a mass of flesh and blood covered with various reproductive organs, golden ears of wheat, mushrooms, vines, and soil.

## Authority → Power Mapping

This maps conceptual origin; it does not mean a Sequence 2 Desolate Matriarch owns an Authority.

- The **Earth, Desolation, Harvest, & Life** partial authority named above already self-identifies its sources; of these, only [[Earth (Mother)]] and [[Life (Mother)]] have standalone Authority pages — **Desolation** and **Harvest** remain lower/derived authorities without one (see [[0 - Mother]]).
- [[Death (Mother)]] → **Evil Spirit Transformation**, **Life Deprivation** — both effects turn the dead (or the dying) into instruments or trigger a fatal trial, despite the Sequence's named jurisdiction being framed as Desolation rather than Death directly.
- [[Life (Mother)]] → **Artificial Life Creation** — animating golems out of soil, plants, lava, ores, and metals is a direct expression of granting Life.
- **Mythical Creature Transformation** has no clearly supported Authority beyond general Nature/Life symbolism and is left unmapped.

## Potion

### Potion Properties

> A brownish-yellow liquid resembling the residue of rotting flesh, causing abnormal nausea.

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> A clump of brown soil covered with grass, grains, and vines.

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- Reproductive organs of a Child of Harvest.

Or
- Heart of a Daughter of Desolation.

Or
- A Desolate Matriarch Beyonder Characteristic.

Supplementary Ingredients

- 300 milliliters of blood from a Child of Harvest or Daughter of Desolation (other equivalents are also acceptable).
- 10,000 souls of creatures that died of starvation.
- One type of a desertified oasis remnant.
- A section of a creature's limb with vigorous vitality and extremely strong self-healing ability (such as Sanguines).

### Advancement Ritual

Conceive, reproduce, and nurture an entire race.
