# Sequence 6 - Biologist

## Explicit Powers

### New Abilities

- **Cross-breeding**: **Biologists** possess the ability to make a chimera or **Cross-breed** between various animals, plants, and even an object, directly leading to them creating a new species.
    - **Biologists** are able to create **Cross-breeds** with different ordinary materials, allowing them to create unique species that possess abilities that can be directed to a certain purpose.
        - [Frank Lee](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Frank_Lee "Frank Lee") created an apple **Cross-breed** that is not only much softer and juicier then a normal one but could also heal the illness caused by the [Biological Poison Bottle](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Biological_Poison_Bottle "Biological Poison Bottle").
                - [Frank Lee](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Frank_Lee "Frank Lee") created special types of fish **Cross-breeds** that have alcohol instead of blood. These ultimately failed to become a species because of their short lifespan.
                - [Frank Lee](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Frank_Lee "Frank Lee") was able to create a special type of fungus that is able to make any bull, regardless of their gender, be able to produce milk for a period of time.
        - While they can make **Cross-breeds** using normal materials, ingredients from **Beyonders** would allow them to create more magical **Cross-breeds** with more unique abilities.
        - [Frank Lee](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Frank_Lee "Frank Lee"), through combining a **[Rose Bishop's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Hanged_Man_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_6:_Rose_Bishop)** flesh with mushrooms, was able to give the fungi the ability to grow through the consumption of flesh.
            - Later on, [Frank Lee](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Frank_Lee "Frank Lee") had modified this mushroom **Cross-breed** and created different variations of such mushrooms that had unique properties from the original.
                - Prototype 2 had the following **Cross-breed** variations:
                    - The first was a **Cross-breed** with wheat and can be grounded to make bread.
                                        - The second could be mixed with milk and have milk be drawn from it.
                                        - The last was **Cross-breed** with fish and thus had a meaty flavor.
                                - Prototype 3 had the following **Cross-breed** variations:
                    - The first type of **Cross-breed** from Prototype 3 of the mushroom **Cross-breed** cannot be eaten as it instead accumulates various kinds of poisons.
                                        - The second type of **Cross-breed** can be used for food only after being cooked at high temperatures through boiling, stewing, and frying.
        - Before reaching the **Sequence 4** level, their **Cross-breeds** will lack a [Soul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul").****
- **Poison Creation**: A **Biologist's** own body can produce highly potent toxin, which can then be accumulated and released through their hair or other means.

### Strengthened Abilities

- **Knowledge (Life)**: They have a deep understanding of the characteristics of living organisms.

## Authority → Power Mapping

This maps conceptual origin; it does not mean a Sequence 6 Biologist owns an Authority.

- [[Life (Mother)]] → **Cross-breeding**, **Knowledge (Life)** — creating new hybrid species and possessing deep insight into living organisms both draw on the Life jurisdiction.
- **Poison Creation** has no clearly supported Authority among the five available and is left unmapped.
