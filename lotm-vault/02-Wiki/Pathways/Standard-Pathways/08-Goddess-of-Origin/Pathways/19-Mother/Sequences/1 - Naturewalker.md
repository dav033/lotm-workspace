# Sequence 1 - Naturewalker

## Explicit Powers

### Authorities

- **Nature** & **Mother**: "They" hold a partial authority over **Nature** and **Mother**.
    - **Child of Nature**: A **Naturewalker** is the **Child of Nature**.
        - "They" can reshape the environment, making even the [Abyss](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Abyss "Abyss") suitable for human habitation.
                - "They" are able to truly become one with the **Natural** elements within range - earth, forests, flowing water, mountains, ores, etc — and traverse through them at varying speeds.
            - In the [Western Continent](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Western_Continent "Western Continent"), this is known as "Various Escapes Techniques".
                        - "They" can also directly draw upon or utilize their power.
                - As the **Mother of All Things** is also the **Mother** of **Concepts**, things that belong to the broad concept of **Nature** can be compatible with "Them" and thus heed to "Their" will.
            - This includes **Fire**, **Lightning**, the **Sun**, the **Moon**, etc.
        - **World Creation**: "They" can nurture and conceive a realm embodying the concept of **Nature**, which is akin to the **Painting World**, the [Mirror World](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Mirror_World "Mirror World"), or a more complete alternate **Dimension**.
        - These realms are easier to break than an **[Attendant of Mysteries'](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Fool_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_1:_Attendant_of_Mysteries) Realm of Mysteries**.

### Strengthened Abilities

- All "Their" previous abilities have been enhanced.

## Authority → Power Mapping

This maps conceptual origin; it does not mean a Sequence 1 Naturewalker owns an Authority.

- [[Nature (Mother)]] → **Child of Nature**, **World Creation** — the source text directly names Nature as one of the two authorities this Sequence holds partial jurisdiction over.
- [[Mother (Authority)]] → **Child of Nature**, **World Creation** — named alongside Nature as the other partial-jurisdiction source for the same environmental-reshaping and realm-conceiving powers.

## Potion

### Potion Properties

> A rich, grassy liquid brimming with vitality.

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> Crystal clusters composed of five colors: red, green, gold, black, and brown.

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- A Naturewalker Beyonder Characteristic

Supplementary Ingredients

- 100 grams of soil blessed by one's own mother (note: either a real mother or a mystical mother is acceptable).
- One spark of fire from the earth's core.
- One branch of a thousand-year-old oak tree.
- 10 drops of Ichor.
- 100 grams of newly born gold.

### Advancement Ritual

Find a way to create a world that exists only as a concept, without nature or elements, and completely encompass it, then use it to enter the Astral World and consume the potion.
