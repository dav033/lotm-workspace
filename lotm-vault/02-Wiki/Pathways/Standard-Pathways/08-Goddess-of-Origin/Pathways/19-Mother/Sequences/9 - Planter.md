# Sequence 9 - Planter

## Explicit Powers

### Traits

- **Physical Enhancement**: **Planters** possess several physical enhancements upon drinking the potion, showcased by their high physical strength.

### New Abilities

- **Farming Proficiency**: **Planters** possess a high proficiency with farming tools, distinguishing among different seeds, and nurturing them.
- **Weather Forecasting**: They can predict the weather to a certain extent by observing clouds, wind, and other natural phenomenon.

## Authority → Power Mapping

This maps conceptual origin; it does not mean a Sequence 9 Planter owns an Authority.

- **Physical Enhancement**, **Farming Proficiency**, and **Weather Forecasting** are baseline potion traits with no clear connection to any of the Mother pathway's core Authorities ([[Mother (Authority)]], [[Earth (Mother)]], [[Nature (Mother)]], [[Death (Mother)]], [[Life (Mother)]]) at this early Sequence; they remain unmapped, paralleling how Sequence 9 Seer's baseline traits are handled in the Fool pathway.
