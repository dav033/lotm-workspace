# Sequence 8 - Doctor (Healing Pastor)

## Explicit Powers

### Traits

- **Spirituality**: Their **Spirituality** has become enhanced, allowing them to use **Spirit Vision**.
    - **Spirit Vision**: Through [Spirit Vision](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Spirit_Vision "Spirit Vision"), they can discern a patient’s health condition with precision.
        - Even without it, they can discern hidden illnesses from the target's external appearance.

### New Abilities

- **Healing**: Their principle power. They have a variety of abilities regarding the process of **Healing**, be it in the physical and [Soul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul") fields, allowing them to be able to **Heal** and **Treat** most forms of injuries.
    - **Surgical Mastery**: They are experts at surgery, possessing the corresponding skills in suturing severed limbs and transplanting internal organs, and having a fine control over medical tools.
        - It allows them to help a patient's condition without utilizing other **Beyonder** powers. When combined with their **Treatments**, there are few physical things they can't fix.
        - **Treatments**: **Doctors** possess a variety of **Treatments** and thus are rather good at healing a variety of different injuries/illnesses such **Treatments** as the baseline method.
        - **Evil Ailment Treatment**: They are able to relieve certain aliments and diseases associated with the domains of **Evil** and **Depravity**.
                - **Disease Treatments**: Even if the disease is not associated with the domains of **Evil** and **Depravity**, they can still heal it.
                - **Trauma Treatment**: They are able to effectively **Treat** and cure most forms of physical trauma through a **Treatment**.
            - For wounds that can be accessed without barriers, they can cure those directly. For example, they can let their palm shimmer with a clear light and press it on the wound.
                - This will make the wound will contract and wiggle, before **Healing** quickly.
                        - For wounds that cannot be accessed without barriers, they must open the cavity and touch the injured area, which is equivalent to performing a surgical operation.
        - **Soul Suture** (**Soul Sewing**): By having the patient let go of their bodies and mind, **Doctors** can carry out a [Soul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul") level surgery to **Sewing** up the corresponding wound.
        - If a person were to completely open up their body and mind, leaving their [Soul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul") unprotected, a **Doctor** could be able to sever the person’s [Soul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul").
            - **Soul Suture** could be used by them as both a method of attack on the [Soul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul"), as well as a healing method to remove **Corruption** and **Curses**.
                        - With enough practice, it is not impossible for a **Doctor** to artificially remove certain emotions and desires from a patient's [Soul](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Soul "Soul").
                        - In terms of healing soul injuries, **Soul Suturing** is much more effective then simply doing an **Evil Ailment Treatment**.
        - **Giving Life**: **Doctors** are also able to gradually restore the energy of a person in a weakened state, restoring that person's strength and vigor.
- **Time Distinguishment**: A **Doctor** is able to easily distinguish the time.

## Authority → Power Mapping

This maps conceptual origin; it does not mean a Sequence 8 Doctor owns an Authority.

- [[Mother (Authority)]] → **Healing**, **Surgical Mastery**, **Treatments**, **Evil Ailment Treatment**, **Soul Suture** — the Doctor's healing kit corresponds to the "Healing (Treatment)" lower authority, itself derived from Mother and Life (see [[0 - Mother]]).
- [[Life (Mother)]] → **Giving Life** — restoring a weakened person's strength and vigor directly echoes the Life authority's vitality-granting effect.
- **Spirituality**, **Spirit Vision**, and **Time Distinguishment** are potion/sensory traits without a supported Authority assignment (compare how Sequence 9 Seer's Enhanced Memory and Spirituality are handled in the Fool pathway).
