# Sequence 2 - Life-Giver

## Explicit Powers

- The cited secondary source reports a Creation-derived expression of Life, partial authority over Spirituality, Moonlight Transformation, multiple Doors of Summoning, Bat Wings, and Crimson Moonlight.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- [[Life (Moon)]] → the Creation-derived Life expression.

## Potion

- Formula: a Sanguine Duke Beyonder Characteristic or matching Artificial Vampire legacy; the source lists a mother's amulet, hair from self-created blood offspring, corresponding blood, and an Oak Seed.
- Ritual: divide oneself into five parts, destroy and seal them separately, then take the potion with the heart-containing part before complete death.
