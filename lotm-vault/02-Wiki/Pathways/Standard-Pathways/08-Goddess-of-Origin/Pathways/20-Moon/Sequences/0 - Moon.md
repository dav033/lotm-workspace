# Sequence 0 - Moon

## Explicit Powers

- The cited secondary source treats Moon primarily as a Symbolism rather than a single Authority.
- It associates this Sequence with Summoning, Beauty, partial Life, Spirituality, partial Darkness, partial Bizarreness, and subordinate authority over plants, animals, humans, and supernatural creatures.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- [[Summoning (Moon)]] → the reported Summoning scope and subordinate governance of supernatural creatures.
- [[Beauty (Moon)]] → Sequence 0 Beauty.
- [[Life (Moon)]] → the reported partial Life Authority.
- [[Spirituality (Moon)]] → authority over Spirituality.
- [[Darkness (Moon)]] → the reported partial Darkness Authority.
- [[Bizarreness (Moon)]] → the reported partial Bizarreness Authority.
- Governance of plants, animals, and humans remains subordinate scope in the source; no additional Authority note is created from those object classes.

## Potion

- The supplied advancement capture leaves its first main ingredient blank; this note does not infer it.
- It lists three Beauty Goddess Beyonder Characteristics and blood from a Great Old One's or Sefirah's offspring.
- Ritual: enter the Astral World and occupy the Moon concept or engrave one's true name within it. The supplied source says its stated condition prevented a true Sequence 0 Moon from appearing in history.
