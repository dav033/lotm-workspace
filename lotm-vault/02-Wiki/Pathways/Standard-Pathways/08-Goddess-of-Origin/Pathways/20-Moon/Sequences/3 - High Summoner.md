# Sequence 3 - High Summoner

## Explicit Powers

- The cited secondary source reports a Door of Summoning for Spirit World creatures and partial authority over Summoning.
- It also reports greatly improved scent tracking for blood.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- [[Summoning (Moon)]] → the reported partial Authority and Door of Summoning for Spirit World creatures.
- Blood tracking remains a strengthened trait rather than an independent Authority.

## Potion

- Formula: a Sanguine Marquis Beyonder Characteristic or matching Artificial Vampire legacy; the source lists corresponding blood and nails, a Gray Fog-corrupted item or qualifying Fool substitute, and a Moon Paper Substitute.
- Ritual: find Moon-linked Spirit World information tied to one's past, present, and future, then define it as a True Name through ceremony. The source warns that an incorrect choice enslaves the aspirant to the Moon.
