# Sequence 5 - Scarlet Scholar

## Explicit Powers

- The cited secondary source reports terrifying speed and unusually strong regeneration.

## Authority → Power Mapping

## Potion

- Formula: legacy of a Sanguine Viscount or matching Artificial Vampire; alternatives are Moon-worshipping Monster blood crystals and Elderly Bat wings.
- Ritual: gather materials representing moon phases and drink the potion under a Full Moon.
- Acting method: sincerity toward, worship of, and study of the Moon.
