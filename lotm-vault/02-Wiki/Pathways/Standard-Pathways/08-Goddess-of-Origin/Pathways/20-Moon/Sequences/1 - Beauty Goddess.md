# Sequence 1 - Beauty Goddess

## Explicit Powers

- The cited secondary source reports a lower expression of Beauty authority and a degree of the Moon concept.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- [[Beauty (Moon)]] → the reported lower expression of Beauty Authority.
- Moon remains a Pathway Symbolism in this classification, not a separate Moon Authority.

## Potion

- Formula: a Beauty Goddess Beyonder Characteristic; supplementary ingredients are conception amniotic fluid or a permitted substitute, an incomparable artwork, a God's Child's blood, and newly sprouted Mistletoe.
- Ritual: use a Moon Paper Substitute remembered by many creatures while the original body remains buried in a coffin for at least three hundred years; then the original body drinks the potion.
