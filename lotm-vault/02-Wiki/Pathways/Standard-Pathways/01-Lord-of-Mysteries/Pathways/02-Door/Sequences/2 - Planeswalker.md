# Sequence 2 - Planeswalker

## Explicit Powers

- **Symbolization** — transforms into symbolic starlight to travel and resist ordinary physical effects.
- **Dimension Sight** — perceives higher-dimensional or spatial structures, including projecting distant scenes into a viewable veil, and can miniaturize people/objects/scenes into a toy-like stored state covering a city the size of Backlund. Affected beings become stiff and jerky like wind-up toys; Sequence 5 or lower Beyonders will not notice, Sequence 3 or higher will. They must be released within 24 hours or become permanently converted, and only one miniaturized space can be held at a time (swappable for a new one).
- **Realm Doors** — opens Doors to compatible realms such as the Mirror World, Underworld, or Dreams, either directly or by Replicating another Pathway's travel method (e.g. a Demoness Pathway Mirror traveler, or a Gatekeeper for a hidden Underworld); the Historical Void cannot be entered without a Replicated power.
- **Group Wandering** — transports multiple targets toward a chosen destination.
- **Replication** — recreates powers, instances of a power's use, or entire scenes through sufficient understanding without first Recording them; carries the Inconceivable symbolism, letting it replicate some authorities (though not Uniquenesses), while abilities that alter a state without direct use are especially hard to replicate.
- **Space Tearing** — destroys or separates space at Angel level.
- **Starlight and Conceptual Sealing** — seals entities, areas, powers, or concepts within current limits.
- **Advanced Astrology, Secret Keeping, and Space Concealment** — strengthens earlier powers, including a Hostel-like concealed space similar to the Sublunary Eye Pathway's Angels.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- [[Replication]] → **Replication** — direct initial Authority mastery.
- [[Door (Door Pathway)]] and [[Alternate Worlds (Door)]] → **Realm Doors**, **Group Wandering**.
- [[Space (Door)]] → **Symbolization**, **Dimension Sight**, **Space Tearing**, **Space Concealment**.
- [[Sealing (Door)]] → **Starlight and Conceptual Sealing**.
- [[Concealment (Door)]] → **Secret Keeping**, **Space Concealment**.
- [[Position (Door)]] → destination selection and advanced Astrology.

## Potion

- Properties: colorless liquid composed of countless illusory doors.
- Beyonder Characteristic appearance: a resplendent, slightly burning torch.
- Main ingredients: a Worm of Star, a Worm of Time, and a Worm of Spirit.
- Advancement ritual: leave legends about oneself at nine different locations outside the home planet.
