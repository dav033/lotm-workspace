# Sequence 4 - Secrets Sorcerer

## Explicit Powers

- **Secret Keeping** — blocks sensing and tracking or turns the user into a mystical secret; can prevent conversations from leaking.
- **Space Concealment** — splits and conceals part of a spatial area; every concealed space needs a Door as its exit or observation point, and removing the concealment is usually faster than exiting it normally.
- **Space Pocket** — creates a concealed space accessible through a designated Door, including a special Mystical Item housing a small concealed space (roughly apartment-sized) that must be periodically recharged or lose its contents to the Spirit World.
- **Door Transformation** — transforms into layered illusory Doors while the real body waits behind them in a different world, gazing from afar; the illusory Doors vary in appearance.
- **Exile** — throws a partially-controlled target into chaotic space-time turbulence for about 20 seconds at this Sequence before they automatically return, contingent on the target surviving the turbulence.
- **Enhanced Blink** — rapidly alternates positions and can cast Beyonder powers faster than normal for a limited time via its numerous Worms of Star, including dodging some mental attacks.
- **Qualitative Record** — records Angel-level powers, even from Kings of Angels, though notably weaker than the original; can also record certain "states" of other beings for combat use.
- **Restriction Opening** — uses Door Opening against some teleportation prohibitions, including illusory Doors placed by a Judge.

## Potion

- Properties: rippling with starlight as if containing a miniature galaxy, alternately described as painfully icy water.
- Beyonder Characteristic appearance: a crystal refracting countless illusory, resplendent doors, formed from insects.
- Main ingredients: Siskun Demon Worm's Nest, or Ankh Gate's Ashes, or Golden Phoenix's Feather.
- Supplementary ingredients: a Walnut (1), Lake Water Freshly Reflecting the Cosmos (100 ml).
- Advancement ritual: seal a hostile demigod-level creature; using less outside help produces a better result.
- Consumption backlash/ritual effect: all senses vanish along a hallucinated night sky, and insect-like creatures crawl from the body toward an embedded-diamond "Milky Way," carrying part of the drinker's flesh and consciousness as uncontrollable would-be Avatars; the drinker's own completed seal then appears as an abstract mark, merges partially, and resolves into a layered "door" concept that isolates the drinker from the surrounding cosmos while the sealed creature senses and corrodes at the barrier. Drinkers affected by Bethel Abraham specifically may also hear an unfamiliar but instantly comprehensible calling-for-help voice.
