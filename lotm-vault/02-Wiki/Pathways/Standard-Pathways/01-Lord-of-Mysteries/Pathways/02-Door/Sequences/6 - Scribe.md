# Sequence 6 - Scribe

## Explicit Powers

- **Record** — records an observed active Beyonder power (not passive ones) and stores it for one later use, functioning like "copy-paste" rather than the White Tower Pathway's reverse-engineered Imitation.
- **Power-Slot Management** — capacity and success depend on the recorded power's level, familiarity, and potion digestion: initially 1 demigod-level slot, 8 slots for Sequence 5-6 powers, and 20 slots for Sequence 7-9 powers, expanding to 2 demigod-level slots after full digestion; recorded effect strength scales down more for higher-Sequence originals (demigod-level halved at best, Sequence 5-6 keeps over half strength, Sequence 7 and lower is nearly full strength).
- **Strengthened Previous Abilities** — improves Astrology, Door Opening, and Trickmaster spells.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- [[Replication]] → **Record** — directly established lower and restricted form of the later Replication Authority.
- [[Door (Door Pathway)]], [[Space (Door)]], and [[Position (Door)]] → strengthened inherited Door and Astrology powers.
- Slot limits are mechanics of **Record**, not a separate Authority.

## Potion

- Properties: transparent, nearly solidified liquid.
- Beyonder Characteristic appearance: a shimmering eye embedded with gray-white patterns and flecks of starlight.
- Main ingredient: a Scribe Beyonder Characteristic.
- Supplementary ingredients: Diary Pages over 22 Years Old (3), Mercury (10 ml), Honeysuckle Essential Oil (10 drops), Seawater/Lake Water/Glacier Water (30 ml each).
- Consumption backlash: the brain rapidly expands with vitalized cells forming a haloed "door" around it while the drinker separates into individual cells and hears ravings (a specific full-moon loss-of-control risk applies to Abraham family lineage members); control eventually returns along with resonant knowledge of how to use the Record power, summarized as "I came, I saw, I record."
