# Sequence 1 - Key of Stars

## Explicit Powers

- **Position Authority** — locates self, targets, or objects and can hide or seal a Position; once a target is located and unobstructed, this authority can be used to exert further influence on it, provided a corresponding mystical connection is created and exploited before the target severs it.
- **Space Fragmentation** — splits, collapses, or degrades spatial regions.
- **Gravity Manipulation** — alters gravity through advanced control of space, bending Space, time, and light.
- **Advanced Sealing** — seals physical entities, concepts, rules, possibilities, convergence, or selected capabilities within limits, including Space-Time Labyrinths that turn a short journey into a winding maze with the exit fixed at the farthest point.
- **Higher-Dimensional Teleportation** — opens Doors to higher dimensions.
- **Enhanced Dimension Sight** — reads complex spatial and dimensional relationships, including tracking movement between dimensions.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- [[Position (Door)]] → **Position Authority**, target location, and hidden Position.
- [[Space (Door)]] → **Space Fragmentation**, **Gravity Manipulation**, **Higher-Dimensional Teleportation**, **Dimension Sight**.
- [[Sealing (Door)]] → **Advanced Sealing** and sealing of Position.
- [[Door (Door Pathway)]] → higher-dimensional access and travel.
- [[Concealment (Door)]] → hiding a Position — supporting Power, not an independent Authority.

## Potion

- Properties: deep, lightless liquid that nonetheless attracts abundant starlight.
- Beyonder Characteristic appearance: a simple, unadorned key seemingly forged from pure starlight.
- Main ingredient: a Key of Stars Beyonder Characteristic.
- Supplementary ingredients: a Compass (or similar object) linked to the Astral World (1), the Core of a Wandering Asteroid (1), a portion of one's favorite food or drink (1).
- Advancement ritual: find a continuously rotating, signal-emitting heavy planet, approach and land on it, then establish a sufficient mystical connection with it.
