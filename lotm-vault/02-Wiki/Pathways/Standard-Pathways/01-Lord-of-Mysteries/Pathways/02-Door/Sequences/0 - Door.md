# Sequence 0 - Door

## Explicit Powers

- **Door** — opens, closes, creates, or controls Doors in physical, spiritual, astral, dimensional, and conceptual contexts; can temporarily shut off everything related to the Door concept to strengthen Seals, or open/weaken them across the world for a limited time.
- **Space** — fragments, folds, joins, collapses, or replaces spatial relationships; alternate worlds built from Space authority cannot be made fully independent and must be founded on Reality, the Spirit World, or the Astral World.
- **Sealing** — strengthens, weakens, creates, or removes seals, including conceptual seals over rules, authorities, or a target's Convergence Instinct; sealing a King of Angels with multiple Sequence 1 Characteristics has required two True Gods acting together.
- **Alternate Worlds** — creates or accesses alternate worlds and special spatial realms; an Alternate World can itself serve as this Pathway's Divine Kingdom.
- **Replication** — recreates understood powers, scenes, or supernatural effects; at this Sequence it can replicate a True God's authorities without replicating their underlying symbolism, though not the symbolism of other Gods or Great Old Ones.
- **Position** — locates, hides, fixes, or influences a target through its Position; the Position authority is described as the cornerstone of the group's Beacon of Destiny symbolism.
- **Conceptualization** — becomes or forces a cluster of Door-related concepts and symbols.
- **Rules-Based Divine Kingdom** — creates a Divine Kingdom structured through Door and spatial rules.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- [[Door (Door Pathway)]] → **Door**, universal passage, access, and closure.
- [[Space (Door)]] → spatial destruction, travel, folding, fragmentation, and conceptual space control.
- [[Sealing (Door)]] → physical and conceptual **Sealing**.
- [[Alternate Worlds (Door)]] → creation and access of **Alternate Worlds**.
- [[Replication]] → **Replication**.
- [[Position (Door)]] → location, hidden Position, and influence after locating a target.
- [[Concealment (Door)]] → secret and unspeakable aspects of the Divine Kingdom.
- Rapid **Blink** can create an omnipresent combat state; omnipresence is an effect, not a separate Authority.

## Potion

- Properties: a spherical liquid seemingly containing entire worlds.
- Main ingredients: three Key of Stars Beyonder Characteristics.
- Supplementary ingredients: mastery of astronomical, geographical, and folkloric knowledge of at least three galactic systems.
- Advancement ritual: break out of the seal or banishment of a True God.
