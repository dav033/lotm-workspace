# Sequence 8 - Trickmaster

## Explicit Powers

- **Flash** — produces a burst several times more dazzling than an old-fashioned camera flash, meant to impair vision rather than illuminate.
- **Black Screen** — creates a lightless curtain for concealment.
- **Gas Transfer** — moves toxic or harmful gas away or to a chosen destination, limited to about 15 meters.
- **Loud Noise** — creates a deafening, explosion-like sound to fake explosions or disrupt attention/senses.
- **Freezing** — chills by touch or a short-range ray (about 10 meters, arrow-comparable speed), lowering temperature or forming frost/thin ice.
- **Electric Shock** — releases a limited electrical attack comparable to non-high-voltage shocks, requiring a conducting medium and unable to be projected away from the body.
- **Fog** (about 15-meter range), **Wind** (level 7-8 gale strength), and **Ignition** (within about 3 meters, by rubbing fingers together) — weak elemental or environmental effects.
- **Tumble** — either coats a roughly 5-6 meter radius area to cause slipping, or directly makes a designated target's feet slip.
- **Object Manipulation** — moves or bounces designated objects within about 5 meters, without fine or precise control.
- **Escape Trick** — after diverting attention (via props, Loud Noise, Black Screen, Flash, or other Beyonder powers), rapidly repositions; a lesser form of Blink.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- [[Door (Door Pathway)]] and [[Space (Door)]] → **Escape Trick** — lower spatial movement expression.
- [[Concealment (Door)]] → **Black Screen** — functional concealment, not proof of Authority mastery.
- Remaining tricks are listed spells without supported independent Authority assignments.

## Potion

- Properties: vibrant, colorful liquid resembling poisonous mushrooms.
- Beyonder Characteristic appearance: a transparent, rainbow-refracting crystal.
- Main ingredient: a Trickmaster Beyonder Characteristic.
- Supplementary ingredients: Hornbeam Essential Oils (5 ml), String Grass Powder (10 g), a blossoming Red Chestnut Flower (1), Purified Water (80 ml).
- Acting method: success depends on performance, fooling, and deception.
