# Sequence 7 - Astrologer

## Explicit Powers

- **Astrology** — divines information and future tendencies through stars and a crystal ball; the crystal ball can also recall the user's own past memories, similarly to Dream Divination. Astrology is directly not all-powerful.
- **Spiritual Intuition** — senses danger and some supernatural interference; greatly enhanced Spirituality lets the Astrologer consult it for premonitions.
- **Interference** — disrupts another person's divination or spiritual intuition.
- **Memory Recall and Identity Comparison** — recalls details and, by overlapping two people's silhouettes, can determine whether disguised individuals are the same person.
- **Strengthened Door Opening** — carries other people while phasing and can open a small Door in a wall to see inside a room.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- [[Position (Door)]] → locating and directional aspects of **Astrology** — strong inference.
- [[Concealment (Door)]] → **Interference** against divination — probable interpretation.
- [[Door (Door Pathway)]] and [[Space (Door)]] → **Strengthened Door Opening**.
- **Spiritual Intuition** and **Memory Recall** remain abilities without independent Authority status.

## Potion

- Properties: transparent, nearly solidified liquid.
- Beyonder Characteristic appearance: an eye-sized "diamond" that constantly refracts external light into a starlight-like radiance.
- Main ingredient: an Astrologer Beyonder Characteristic.
- Supplementary ingredients: Clematis Powder (10 g), a segment of Withered Grapevine (1), a pair of Octopus Eyeballs (1), Strong Liquor (80 ml).
- Acting method: Astrology is not all-powerful.
