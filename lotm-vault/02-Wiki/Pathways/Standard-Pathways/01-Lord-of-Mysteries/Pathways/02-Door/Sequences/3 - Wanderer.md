# Sequence 3 - Wanderer

## Explicit Powers

- **Wandering** — travels through the Astral World, Cosmos, and between planets without relying only on the Spirit World; the Spirit World can no longer trap a Wanderer.
- **Space Tearing** — through physical contact, tears space so the affected area is swallowed by the void and collapses in a space-time storm.
- **Space Integration** — joins spaces or spatial relationships, doubling as Concealment and as adaptation to varied planetary/interdimensional environments.
- **Spatial Cage** — develops Space Concealment into a temporary "spatial cage," a form of Sealing that can block all directions but one to make evasion difficult.
- **Advanced Door Opening** — breaks through nearly all ordinary and supernatural barriers except special counters.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- [[Space (Door)]] → **Wandering**, **Space Tearing**, **Space Integration**, **Spatial Cage**.
- [[Door (Door Pathway)]] → **Wandering**, **Advanced Door Opening**.
- [[Sealing (Door)]] → **Spatial Cage** — initial sealing expression.
- [[Spirit World Access (Door)]] no longer defines all travel at this Sequence; Astral and cosmic movement expands beyond it.

## Potion

- Properties: deep black liquid filled with countless glittering specks.
- Beyonder Characteristic appearance: a miniature door of starlight.
- Main ingredient: a Wanderer Beyonder Characteristic.
- Supplementary ingredients: Wandering Skin's Pus (10 ml), a Mist Watcher's Eyelid (1), White Mustard Seeds (9), Star Water From the Ritual Site (90 ml).
- Advancement ritual: find three pieces of Astral World information projected into the Spirit World, enter the dangerous scenarios they derive from, and record everything in full detail without forgetting it.
