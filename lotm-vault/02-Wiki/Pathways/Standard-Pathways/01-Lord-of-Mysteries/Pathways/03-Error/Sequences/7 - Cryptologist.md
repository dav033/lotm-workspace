# Sequence 7 - Cryptologist

## Explicit Powers

- **Decryption** — reconstructs mysticism-related truth or predicts developments from sufficient clues; requires enough information to deduce from and is not Divination or Prophecy, so it cannot produce wild guesses. Decryption efforts may draw attention from high-level beings.
- **Enhanced Observation, Association, and Deduction** — detects subtle evidence and connects it logically.
- **Enhanced Spiritual Intuition** — senses relevant supernatural information and danger.
- **Mysticism Knowledge** — gains knowledge needed to interpret supernatural evidence.
- **Secret and Incantation Decryption** — deciphers dreams, illusions, locations, charms, Mystical Items, and Sealed Artifact clues; can decrypt incantations that activate charms and Mystical Items.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- **Decryption** is an listed Power that uses information from the Spirit World; current official evidence does not establish Decryption as an independent Authority.
- [[Deceit (Error)]] → adversarial use of **Decryption** to detect deception — operational relationship, not ownership of a new Authority.
- Observation, deduction, intuition, and knowledge remain supporting abilities.

## Potion

- Properties: misty liquid with glimmering specks; induces scalp-piercing pain akin to skin peeling.
- Beyonder Characteristic appearance: a colorless, mist-emitting crystal.
- Main ingredient: a Cryptologist Beyonder Characteristic.
- Supplementary ingredients: Sphinx's Blood (80 ml), Mucus Left by a Soul-Bewitching Insect Colony (10 drops), Moonstone (1), Wild Rose (1), a Self-Designed Cipher written on paper (1 set).
- No published advancement ritual, ritual essence, consumption backlash, or acting method for this Sequence.
