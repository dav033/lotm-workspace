# Sequence 0 - Error

## Explicit Powers

- **Error / Bug / Loopholes** — creates, exploits, redirects, or repairs Errors in normal and supernatural rules; can turn falsehood into truth under specific preconditions (similarity, prior connection, logical contradiction, incomplete rules, or a constructed Erroneous environment) to steal authority from an imitated object, avoid attacks, or make Avatars, Historical Projections, or Substitutes equivalent to the true body for a given effect.
- **Deceit** — deceives cognition, rules, mystical mechanisms, the self, or higher-level interactions.
- **Theft** — steals physical or abstract targets, including powers, identity, Fate, Time, anchors, connections, and Authorities; when it targets a True God, Theft can take an entire authority including its subordinate authorities.
- **Fate Manipulation through Theft** — senses Fate's ripples and replaces or steals Destiny-related relationships.
- **Time Manipulation through Theft** — steals duration, lifespan, timing, or temporal opportunities.
- **Conceptual Parasitism and Avatars** — uses Spirit Body Threads, identity, and stolen connections at conceptual level; can create at least two Sequence 1-equivalent Avatars, with a theoretical single-Avatar ceiling near true Sequence 1 strength described as almost impossible and destabilizing to attempt, keeping the practical limit at no more than two such Avatars.
- **Rules-Based Divine Kingdom** — establishes an erroneous environment governed by exploitable rules.
- **Advanced Decryption** — extracts secrets and exploitable Loopholes from minimal information, including from an unfamiliar Divine Kingdom after only a couple of intrusion attempts.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- [[Error (Authority)|Error]] → **Error**, **Bug**, **Loopholes**, rule equivalence, redirection, and exploit creation.
- [[Deceit (Error)]] → **Deceit** against minds, rules, self-state, and mystical systems.
- [[Theft (Error)]] → all concrete and abstract **Theft** applications.
- [[Destiny (Error)|Fate (Error)]] → Fate sensing, Fate Theft, Destiny replacement.
- [[Time (Error)]] → Time Theft and temporal Loopholes.
- **Parasitism**, **Avatars**, and **Decryption** remain Powers or operating methods; they are not promoted to independent Authorities here.

## Potion

- Properties: liquid formed by overlapping layers of segmented worms.
- Main ingredients: three Worm of Time Beyonder Characteristics.
- Supplementary ingredients: three Beacons Extracted from the River of Fate.
- Advancement ritual: replace someone during their own Sequence 0 apotheosis ritual — this refers to the ritual itself rather than necessarily the exact moment of drinking the Sequence 0 potion, and does not obligate the advancer to become a Dual-Pathway God of Error and the target's Pathway.
