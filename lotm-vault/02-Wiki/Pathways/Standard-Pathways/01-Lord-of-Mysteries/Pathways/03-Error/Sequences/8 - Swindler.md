# Sequence 8 - Swindler

## Explicit Powers

- **Charm** — increases affinity and makes trust easier to establish.
- **Eloquence** — improves speech and adds a persuasive Beyonder effect that influences the Body of Heart and Mind through the Spirit World.
- **Thought Misdirection** — misleads thought through speech, posture, action, or arranged context; a skilled Swindler can mislead even without invoking a Beyonder effect.
- **Mental Disruption** — induces limited hallucinations or cognitive interference.
- **Enhanced Superior Observation** — reads a target's micro-expressions and small movements from a shorter distance, grasping mental state (though weaker than a Spectator's Superior Observation).
- **Strengthened Theft and Agility** — improves stealing technique (including stealing spiritual materials that interact with the Spirit World) and physical mobility.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- [[Deceit (Error)]] → **Eloquence**, **Thought Misdirection**, **Mental Disruption**, supernatural use of **Charm** — lower Powers related to Deceit.
- [[Theft (Error)]] → **Strengthened Theft**.
- **Superior Observation** and physical agility remain supporting traits, not Authorities.

## Potion

- Properties: crimson-foamed, dreamlike liquid; sweet yet agonizingly painful.
- Beyonder Characteristic appearance: a red, transparent lip-shaped crystal.
- Main ingredient: a Swindler Beyonder Characteristic.
- Supplementary ingredients: Purified Water (100 ml), Another's Tears (20 ml), Lapis Lazuli (1), White Chestnut Balm (10 g).
- No published advancement ritual, ritual essence, consumption backlash, or acting method for this Sequence.
