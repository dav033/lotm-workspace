# Sequence 5 - Dream Stealer

## Explicit Powers

- **Dream Theft** — steals, stores, transfers, or influences Dreams; can disrupt Dream Divination even without entering the Dream itself.
- **Thought and Intention Theft** — steals active thoughts, intentions, or decisions; the target often cannot tell a thought was stolen unless they think carefully about it, and cannot retain the stolen thought once taken.
- **Memory, Knowledge, and Ideal Theft** — steals mental information or convictions; stealing an ideal can leave the target without motivation.
- **General Ability Theft** — steals learned abilities such as walking, flying, speaking, or a specific language; each stolen general ability still occupies a Theft slot.
- **Attack Theft** — steals an incoming attack to nullify it; unless the attack itself carries a side effect on its user, the Dream Stealer suffers no side effect from absorbing it.
- **Enhanced Beyonder Power Theft** — steals stronger supernatural powers within roughly an 80-meter range; a stolen power is usable for about half an hour, or can be kept unused for about a week before it must be exercised.
- **Thought Usurpation** — replaces or redirects a target's intended thought or action.
- **Disguise / Theft of Heavenly Secrets** — hides information and steals prayer responses or related mystical opportunities; can also steal organs or a fetus from a womb.
- **Enhanced Decryption** — improves analysis of supernatural clues, including Bizarro Sorcerer-level illusions.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- [[Theft (Error)]] → all forms of **Dream**, **Thought**, **Memory**, **Knowledge**, **Ability**, **Attack**, and **Heavenly Secret Theft**.
- [[Deceit (Error)]] → **Thought Usurpation** and deceptive use of stolen information.
- Dreams are a target category of Theft here, not a separate native Authority.
- **Decryption** remains an listed Power using Spirit World information.

## Potion

- Properties: dark, murky liquid that binds all thought and induces lethal suffocation upon ingestion.
- Beyonder Characteristic appearance: a small, pale, shadow-tinted infant's hand.
- Main ingredient: a Dream Stealer Beyonder Characteristic.
- Supplementary ingredients: Dream-Eating Rat's Blood (30 ml), De-Spiritualized Depraved Breath (30 ml), Celestine (1), Lavender Hydrosol (10 drops), Another's Tears Shed Due to Shattered Ideals (50 ml).
- Advancement ritual: become a key antagonist or supporting figure in the unconscious dreams of at least 30 people; dream duration does not matter as long as it is occurring at the moment the potion is consumed.
