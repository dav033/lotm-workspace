# Sequence 4 - Parasite

## Explicit Powers

- **Parasitism** — enters a host in Initial or Deep-Level form to influence or control it; Initial Parasitism only perceives what the host senses, while Deep-Level Parasitism grants full control and can turn the host into an Avatar.
- **Worms of Time** — separates Mythical Creature components used for Parasitism and Avatars.
- **Avatars** — creates independent extensions that share identity and power with the main body.
- **Synchronization** — synchronizes information, state, or actions across Avatars.
- **Theft of Life** — steals lifespan or vital duration.
- **Expanded Conceptual Theft** — steals position, distance (at least 500 meters), powers, aspects of life (including organs), or links between a clone and its main body; up to three abilities can be held from the same target (only one usable at a time), and a stolen Beyonder power remains usable for about two hours regardless of the original holder's status or level.
- **Enhanced Decryption and Observation** — improves detection of clues and targets.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- [[Theft (Error)]] → **Theft of Life**, stolen position/distance/powers, and stolen mystical connections.
- [[Time (Error)]] → temporal aspect of **Theft of Life** and **Worms of Time** — early jurisdictional expression.
- [[Deceit (Error)]] → concealed replacement and host misdirection within **Parasitism**.
- **Parasitism**, **Avatars**, and **Synchronization** are Powers or operational domains, not independent Authorities in this map.

## Potion

- Properties: yellow-green liquid resembling squeezed insects with floating carcasses; triggers a sensation of the soul being possessed and skin peeled layer by layer. A failed promotion (without death or loss of control) turns the drinker into a carrier Parasitized by the potion.
- Beyonder Characteristic appearance: a diamond with translucent worms wriggling inside.
- Main ingredient: a Parasite Beyonder Characteristic.
- Supplementary ingredients: Soul-Snatcher's Blood (100 ml), Puppet Evil Insect Secretions (9 drops), Amethyst (1), Cherry Plum Juice (10 drops), an Imprisoned Soul (1).
- Advancement ritual: convince nine targets to willingly offer all they possess to sustain the drinker.
