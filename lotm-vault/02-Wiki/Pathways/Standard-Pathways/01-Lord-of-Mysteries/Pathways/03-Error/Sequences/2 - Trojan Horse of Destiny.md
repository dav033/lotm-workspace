# Sequence 2 - Trojan Horse of Destiny

## Explicit Powers

- **Loopholes** — detects and exploits gaps in rules, conditions, identities, or connections; no Beyonder power is described as uncrackable.
- **Fate Theft** — steals a target's Fate or Destiny-related position, including a full identity/Fate/self-awareness theft that leaves the target Fate-less, disconnected from reality, and slowly dying; Fate Siphoning can instead swap or transfer a period of Fate between two targets.
- **Identity and Self-Awareness Theft** — steals identity, consciousness-related ownership, or self-recognition; simultaneously stealing a high-level target's Fate, identity, and self-awareness can trigger an identity crisis from the resulting conflict of self-awareness.
- **Beyonder Characteristic Theft** — steals Characteristics under difficult conditions; stealing the highest-Sequence Characteristics takes significant time and carries a notable failure risk even with the target's full cooperation, and completing a prior Fate/identity theft on the same target sharply improves the odds.
- **Mystical Connection Theft** — severs and steals connections to external powers or anchors.
- **Spirit Body Threads Theft** — steals threads and redirects manipulation through Deceit.
- **Enhanced Parasitism** — parasitizes targets or living Sealed Artifacts more effectively.
- **Angel Avatars** — creates Avatars capable of Angel-level action.
- **Rule Deceit** — misdirects mysticism laws or rules, including ones Angels are bound to obey; Theft can be combined with Deceit.
- **Multi-Power Theft** — can steal up to six Beyonder powers from a single target in one attempt, each remaining usable for about a week.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- [[Error (Authority)|Error]] → **Loopholes** and exploitation of rules or conditions.
- [[Theft (Error)]] → **Fate**, **Identity**, **Self-Awareness**, **Characteristic**, **Connection**, and **Spirit Body Threads Theft**.
- [[Destiny (Error)|Fate (Error)]] → **Fate Theft** and Destiny-related replacement.
- [[Deceit (Error)]] → **Rule Deceit** and redirection of stolen Spirit Body Threads.
- **Parasitism** and **Avatars** remain Powers, not separate Authorities.

## Potion

- Properties: mercury-colored liquid with looping worm-shadow symbols, carrying various tastes and a sinking, drowning sensation reminiscent of the River of Fate.
- Beyonder Characteristic appearance: a multi-faceted crystal reflecting different identities.
- Main ingredients: a Postmortem Crystal of a Giant King's Court Priest, or a Soul-Snatcher King's Vertical Eye, or a Trojan Horse of Destiny Beyonder Characteristic.
- Supplementary ingredients: Ancient River Water (100 ml, from a river with a thousand years of history that has not changed course), a Worm of Spirit (1), a Worm of Star (1), False History Believed to be True recorded on paper (large amount).
- Advancement ritual: assume another's identity through non-Beyonder disguise/deception and earn their social circle's trust until that identity dies naturally, unless replacing someone already near death or with under three years left to live; higher-tier targets shorten the requirement to a minimum of six months without needing to reach natural death.
