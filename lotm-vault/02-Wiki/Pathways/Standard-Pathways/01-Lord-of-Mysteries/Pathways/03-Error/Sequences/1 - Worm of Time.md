# Sequence 1 - Worm of Time

## Explicit Powers

- **Time Theft** — steals Time, lifespan, temporal duration, or opportunities from a target. Aging steals only the duration of survival (not growth), so it cannot make a target's body mature faster; stealing a baby's lifespan whitens hair and forms wrinkles without producing adult growth. Stolen time can be "gifted" back to reverse the target's state.
- **Anchor Theft** — steals the anchors supporting another high-level existence; the sudden loss of an anchor is most disruptive during an advancement or apotheosis ritual.
- **Authority Theft** — temporarily steals an Authority or part of its usable effect under applicable limits; stealing an Authority takes it completely, including its subordinate abilities.
- **Symbolism Decryption** — decrypts higher-level symbols and mystical structures.
- **Avatar Succession** — an Avatar can replace the main body by stealing Fate, identity, anchors, Spirit Body, consciousness, and psyche.
- **Temporal Loopholes** — exploits gaps in time limits, duration, or sequence of events, including within Historical Projections and the Circle of Inevitability.
- **Time Manipulation** — a weakened Time authority allowing slowing, stopping, accelerating, or briefly reversing Time in a chosen area, with the ability to exempt selected targets.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- [[Theft (Error)]] → **Time Theft**, **Anchor Theft**, **Authority Theft**, and **Avatar Succession**.
- [[Time (Error)]] → temporal target and effect of **Time Theft**, **Temporal Loopholes**, and **Time Manipulation**.
- [[Destiny (Error)|Fate (Error)]] → Fate and identity components of **Avatar Succession**.
- [[Error (Authority)|Error]] → **Temporal Loopholes**.
- **Symbolism Decryption** remains a strengthened Power, not an independent Authority.

## Potion

- Properties: ethereal, shimmering, cyan-black, tasteless liquid; drinking it subjects the drinker to erosion and cutting by time.
- Beyonder Characteristic appearance: a crystalline pillar of light and shadow housing swiftly swimming 12-segmented Worms of Time, freezing everything nearby.
- Main ingredients: Aurmir's Right Eye, or a Worm of Time Beyonder Characteristic.
- Supplementary ingredients: an Ancient Clock (1), a Used Almanac (1), a Segment of Concealed Fate (1).
- Advancement ritual: plunge a city into temporal chaos for at least seven days.
