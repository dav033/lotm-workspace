# Sequence 3 - Mentor of Deceit

## Explicit Powers

- **Deceit** — deceives abstract concepts, supernatural perception, or rules within current limits; described as a lesser Deceit authority extending the earlier Swindler-level Eloquence to more abstract rules of reality.
- **Weakness and Rule Observation** — detects weaknesses and changes in Order or rules.
- **Enhanced Theft** — can steal three candidate Beyonder powers in a single attempt, prioritizing a target's three most recently used powers to improve accuracy.
- **Theft of Heavenly Secrets** — steals blessings, prayer responses, or concealed mystical opportunities under conditions; the lower a True Deity's intelligence, the higher the likelihood of success.
- **Mastered Avatars** — creates and controls Avatars with greater independence.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- [[Deceit (Error)]] → **Deceit**, supernatural misdirection, and exploitation of observed weaknesses.
- [[Theft (Error)]] → **Enhanced Theft**, **Theft of Heavenly Secrets**.
- [[Error (Authority)|Error]] → sensing rule changes and preparing exploitable Loopholes — precursor, not yet full Sequence 0 mastery.
- **Avatars** remain a Pathway Power, not an independent Authority.

## Potion

- Properties: emerald, crystalline liquid that appears deceptively ordinary; causes bodily and spiritual fragmentation.
- Beyonder Characteristic appearance: a colorless, fungal, curtain-like drape.
- Main ingredient: a Mentor of Deceit Beyonder Characteristic.
- Supplementary ingredients: Swamp Giant's Blood (80 ml), Blasphemous Priest's Fungal Shroud (20 g), Gold (30 g), Water Violet Hydrosol (10 drops), Spirit World Wraiths Who Died Due to the Advancer's Deceit (9).
- Advancement ritual: without violence, find and exploit loopholes in rules to temporarily collapse order in a region no smaller than a village — the smallest entity able to form a Spirit World projection.
