# Sequence 6 - Prometheus

## Explicit Powers

- **Beyonder Power Theft** — temporarily steals an active or available Beyonder power for later use.
- **Remote Object Theft** — steals physical objects across a distance.
- **Conceptualized Theft** — expands Theft beyond direct sleight of hand.
- **Physical Enhancement** — improves the body beyond earlier Sequences.
- **Mental Resistance** — increases resistance to mental influence and control.
- **Strengthened Previous Abilities** — improves observation, Decryption, and deception-related powers; Superior Observation now senses valuable items within a 50-meter range, along with their approximate value and possible type, resistible only by Beyonder Concealment powers.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- [[Theft (Error)]] → **Beyonder Power Theft**, **Remote Object Theft**, **Conceptualized Theft** — developing Power expression of the future Authority.
- [[Deceit (Error)]] → strengthened deception-related abilities.
- **Physical Enhancement** and **Mental Resistance** have no independent Authority assignment.

## Potion

- Properties: red-yellow, mesmerizing liquid with illusory swirling flames; drinking it feels like drinking fire.
- Beyonder Characteristic appearance: a miniature unlit crystal torch.
- Main ingredient: a Prometheus Beyonder Characteristic.
- Supplementary ingredients: Freshly Stolen Wine (100 ml), Cloaked Specter's Residual Powder (10 g), Citrine (1), Larch Essential Oil (10 drops), Tinder (1).
- No published advancement ritual, ritual essence, consumption backlash, or acting method for this Sequence.
