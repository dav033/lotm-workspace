# Sequence 5 - Marionettist

## Explicit Powers

- **Spirit Body Threads Perception** — perceives Spirit Body Threads at distances of up to 100 meters.
- **Spirit Body Threads Manipulation** — influences a target's Soul Body, Astral Projection, Body of Heart and Mind, and Ether Body, using the Ether Body as a bridge to control the physical body.
  - Active control has a range of 5-10 meters depending on potion digestion. Initial control takes about 5-20 seconds depending on digestion and the target's Spirit Body, progressively slowing thought and movement.
  - Sharp, abrupt physical or mental pain can interrupt initial control, immediately restore the target's faculties, and force the Marionettist to restart. If uninterrupted, complete conversion takes about 2.5-5 minutes, again depending on digestion and the target.
  - Forcing a target to consume a raw Beyonder characteristic can induce madness and destabilize its spirituality, making its Spirit Body Threads easier to control; this is an operational setup rather than a separate power.
  - The manipulation is difficult to notice, but divination or prophetic abilities can detect it.
- **Marionette Conversion and Control** — a converted target is effectively dead but retains a soul and remains under the Marionettist's control.
  - A Marionettist begins with a limit of one Marionette and can control up to three after fully digesting the potion. Other targets' threads can still be controlled at this limit, but those targets cannot be fully converted.
  - Control range begins at 100 meters and reaches at least 200 meters with digestion. The Marionettist can possess a Marionette's perspective, experiencing what it sees and feels, while the Marionette uses its original Beyonder powers and consumes its own spirituality.
  - Only superficial thoughts are immediately available. Detailed memories surface only when the Marionette encounters a familiar person or place. It can speak languages known in life, but this does not teach those languages to the Marionettist.
  - A Marionette's lifeless Mind Island makes it immune to Mind Domain powers and psychological effects. Conversely, its unnaturally still fate can expose it to observers of luck or fate, and powers that influence the dead can interfere with it.
  - Marionettes cannot advance by consuming potions. At the same Sequence level, resurrection methods do not free a converted target: resurrection leaves it a Marionette, and later death after control is lifted continues to consume those resurrection opportunities.
  - Marionettes can act as remote proxies and are comparatively effective at avoiding the negative effects of Sealed Artifacts.
- **Hidden-Entity Detection** — abnormal or traceable thread patterns reveal invisible targets, parasitism, other Marionettists' Marionettes, and similar concealed entities.
- **Strengthened Previous Abilities** — prior Beyonder powers increase by at least 50%; Air Bullet reaches force comparable to a steam-rifle bullet.

## Potion

### Potion Properties

- It is a completely black liquid containing countless tiny worms that squirm about unseen by the naked eye.
- A somewhat bitter potion with a mustiness when surging down their throat, gullet, and stomach.

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> A black, pupil-less eye.

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- Dust of Ancient Wraiths.
- Core crystal of a Six-Winged Gargoyle.

Or
- A Marionettist Beyonder Characteristic.

Supplementary Ingredients

- 80 milliliters of spring water from Sonia Island's Golden Spring.
- 10 grams of Drago Bark.
- Remnant Spirituality of Ancient Wraiths.
- 1 pair of eyes from a six-winged gargoyle.

### Advancement Ritual

Consume potion amidst the singing of mermaids.
