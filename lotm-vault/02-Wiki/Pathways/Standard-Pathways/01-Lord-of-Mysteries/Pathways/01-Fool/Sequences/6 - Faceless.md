# Sequence 6 - Faceless

## Traits

- **Enhanced Divination and Combat** — accumulated Pathway abilities improve both divination and fighting skill.

## Explicit Powers

- **Shapeshifting** — freely molds flesh and bodily features to imitate another person's face, proportions, voice, skin, hair, and scent to a limited degree. A Faceless can reposition features, remove all visible facial features, create false ones, and alter body odor through control of the pores; this control also supports highly convincing acting.
  - **Limits** — the ability cannot change the user's actual physical sex or the corresponding lower-body features. Height must remain within roughly one head of the original, mass and proportions cannot differ too greatly, and individual bones and limbs have finite enlargement and shrinkage limits.
- **Identity Recall and Recognition** — accurately recalls the appearance and temperament of known people and can recognize them despite a disguise.
- **Tracking and Anti-Tracking** — combines observation, acting, bodily control, and accumulated Pathway skills to follow targets or evade pursuit.
- **Strengthened Previous Abilities** — Flame Controlling, Flaming Jump, and other earlier powers become about 30% stronger. Air Bullet force and the Underwater Breathing pipe's length increase severalfold, while increased spirituality permits more uses of Paper Figurine Substitute and Damage Transfer.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- [[Change (Lord of Mysteries)]] → **Shapeshifting** — strong inference from the author's Transformation/Change classification at Sequence 0; this is a group-symbolism contribution, not native Pathway ownership.
- [[Fooling]] → deceptive use of **Shapeshifting** and constructed identities — operational application, not a separate Authority.
- **Identity Recall**, **Tracking**, and general strengthening remain abilities without independent Authority status.

## Potion

### Potion Properties

- Blackish-green in color.
- It occasionally produces eye-sized bubbles that emit a life-like burping sound every few seconds. When the bubbles reaches the surface, the bubbles burst, reflecting light in a myriad of colors that form random combinations resembling different faces.
- Upon drinking, slightly tingling sensation would swish in their mouth and oesophagus, and finally, it fermented into a numbing sensation.

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> A jelly-like translucent dark-green object. In the dark green background, from time to time, there were different faces emerging, like shadows hidden behind dark curtains.
