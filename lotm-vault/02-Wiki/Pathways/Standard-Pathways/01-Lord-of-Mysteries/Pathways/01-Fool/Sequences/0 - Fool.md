# Sequence 0 - Fool

## Traits

- A Sequence 0 Fool is a True Deity and an embodiment of the Pathway's Authorities.
- The Sequence is highly asymmetric: it can produce star-destroying effects but has a weaker body and defense than most True Deities. Direct engulfment by a real star can kill it.

## Symbolism

- [[Foolishness (Fool)|Blind Stupidity / Foolishness]] is the Fool Pathway's mind-domain symbolism and corresponds to [[Fooling]], but is not the Fooling Authority itself. It lowers intelligence, analytical ability, and attention, can induce unnoticed wrong judgments or forgetting, and can affect a True Deity. Reconstructing how a judgment was reached can expose the effect, while instinct-driven behavior resists it better.
- [[Change (Lord of Mysteries)]] and [[Inconceivable and Bizarreness (Lord of Mysteries)]] are Lord of Mysteries group symbolisms. Their interplay underlies Grafting/Reassembly, while their separate contributions also appear in Transformation, Miracles, History, Realm of Mysteries, and composite Marionette effects; this does not grant the Fool unrestricted ownership of either group symbolism.
- [[King of Space-Time]] is a group-level symbolism within Change. Together with Bizarreness it contributes to History and Realm of Mysteries; it is not the Fool's limited Time jurisdiction exercised through Fooling.
- [[Beacon of Destiny]] is a group-level symbolism expressed at this Pathway through Anchor of Destiny. It does not grant blanket control of Fate.
- [[Dominator of the Spirit World]] is a Lord of Mysteries symbolism. The Fool's Controller of the Spirit World is the Pathway's partial expression, not full native ownership of the Pillar-level symbolism or all group-level Spirit World Authority.

## Explicit Powers

- **Fooling**: Turns an existing falsehood into reality and encompasses reported partial jurisdictions over History, Time, Fate, Change, and Concealment. The false premise must exist before it can be made real.
  - **Fooling History** swaps the Fool's past, present, or future conditions, such as placing a past state in the present while the present self occupies the former state.
  - **Fooling Time** confuses event order, delays effects, or bypasses a frozen environment through real or conceptual gaps. It does not speed up or slow down Time as the Error Pathway does.
  - **Fooling Fate** interferes with expected development but does not confer unrestricted ownership of Fate.
  - **Conceptual Change** alters an environment according to an introduced concept.
- **Reassembly / Grafting**: Recombines unrelated physical objects, beings, the Fool, abstract concepts, and even Authorities into an inconceivable but real result. If the conceptual Reassembly is removed, its result ends immediately; Damage Transfer is a subordinate application.
- **Controller of the Spirit World**: Controls most Spirit World Authorities available to this Pathway, commands the Seven Lights and Angel-level Spirit World creatures to an extent, and gains a home-ground advantage there.
  - This does not make the Fool stronger overall than the Error or Door Pathways, and the Fool cannot seal the Spirit World without merging with the Door Pathway to some extent.
- **Miracles**: Produces incomprehensible, otherwise unexplainable results. The Fool can reset the Resurrection count once and has a higher upper limit than a Miracle Invoker.
- **History**: Manipulates established History and Historical Void phenomena to a limited extent, including by temporarily Fooling History; it does not rewrite the established past.
- **Realm of Mysteries**: Creates a complete Divine Kingdom from Bizarreness and King of Space-Time symbolism. Its Concealment makes the space incomprehensible, while Grafted rules and objects obstruct brute-force entry.
- **Transformation / Shapeshifting**: Performs high-level changes associated with Change symbolism.
- **Anchor of Destiny**: Makes the Fool's Fate exceedingly difficult to alter. Unless restrained, the Fool's words become nodes in the River of Fate, but their realization and final outcome can be distorted rather than matching the intended result.
- **Conceptual Marionettes**: Turns even conceptual beings into Marionettes. Sluggish thought during conversion is a low-level Fooling effect; Spirit Body Threads, Regeneration, Change, Bizarreness, and Spirit World control remain composite contributors.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- [[Foolishness (Fool)]] → cognition reduction, unnoticed misjudgment, and forgetting: the mind-domain symbolism corresponding to, but distinct from, the [[Fooling]] Authority.
- [[Fooling]] → **Fooling History**, **Fooling Time**, **Fooling Fate**, conceptual change, Concealment effects, and falsehood-to-reality effects within partial jurisdictions.
- [[Grafting and Reassembly]] → **Reassembly / Grafting**, including conceptual and Authority-level combinations. The Authority is reported as arising from the interplay of Change and Bizarreness; those group symbolisms are not thereby promoted to separate Fool Authorities.
- [[Spirit World Dominion]] → **Controller of the Spirit World**, Spirit Body Threads, and command of Spirit World entities as the Pathway's partial expression under [[Dominator of the Spirit World]], not full ownership of the group-level Authority.
- [[Miracles (Fool)]] → **Miracles**, Wish effects, and Resurrection-related restoration; the Authority is reported under [[Inconceivable and Bizarreness (Lord of Mysteries)]].
- [[History (Fool)]] → **History** and Historical Void applications; this Authority is reported as arising from [[King of Space-Time]] together with Bizarreness.
- Concealment (Fool, partial/derived) → Concealment within **Realm of Mysteries**, whose wider symbolic basis is Bizarreness plus King of Space-Time.
- [[Change (Lord of Mysteries)]] → **Transformation / Shapeshifting** and the conceptual-change component of Fooling: a group-symbolism contribution, not unrestricted Pathway ownership.
- [[Anchor of Destiny (Fool)]] → **Anchor of Destiny** under the group-level [[Beacon of Destiny]] symbolism.
- Puppetry → **Conceptual Marionettes**: a derived power-domain supported by Spirit World control, Change, Bizarreness, and Fooling, not an independent Authority.
- Time (Fool, limited jurisdiction) and Fate (Fool, partial jurisdiction) describe limited jurisdictions exercised through Fooling or Anchor of Destiny, not unrestricted standalone ownership.

## Potion

### Potion Properties

- A black, shapeless, dark liquid without a fixed form.
- This blob of liquid stretched out as though it was forming a strange, translucent hooded cloak or a person whose internal organs and flesh were emptied out.

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- The Fool Uniqueness.
- Three Attendant of Mysteries Beyonder Characteristics.

Supplementary Ingredients

- Control at least a quarter of the Fog of History.

### Advancement Ritual

Fool time, history, or fate once.
