# Sequence 9 - Seer

## Traits

- **Mental and Spiritual Aptitude** — the potion primarily improves spirituality, mental acuity, intuition, and interpretive ability, with very little direct physical or combat enhancement.
- **Enhanced Memory** — begins as a slight improvement and continues to strengthen with digestion, supporting the memorization of rituals and divination procedures.
- **Spirituality** — increases the capacity needed for Spirit Vision, divination, and ritual work.

## Explicit Powers

- **Spirit Vision** — perceives ghosts, specters, parts of a soul, and magical auras; it can also support judgments about a person's health and emotions. It provides pre-emptive awareness of obstructed objects and faint footsteps, but does not enhance ordinary sight, hearing, or touch.
- **Divination Arts** — grants aptitude for methods including dream divination, dowsing, astrology, spiritual numbers, and scrying.
- **Ritualistic Magic** — grants the talent to learn and perform a broad range of rituals, potentially reaching greater ritual expertise than a Sequence 9 Mystery Pryer. Advancement does not supply the procedures themselves; practical mastery still depends on study and practice.
- **Danger Intuition** — uses spirituality to sense threats without necessarily identifying their nature or source.

## Authority → Power Mapping

This maps conceptual origin; it does not mean a Sequence 9 Seer owns an Authority.

- Fate, as a partial jurisdiction candidate → **Divination Arts**, **Danger Intuition** — probable interpretation without a standalone Authority node.
- [[Spirit World Dominion]] → **Spirit Vision**, **Ritualistic Magic** — probable partial Pathway expression, not ownership of the group-level Authority.
- **Enhanced Memory** and **Spirituality** remain potion traits without a supported Authority assignment.

## Potion

### Potion Properties

- A dark-blue gelatinous liquid.
- The smell of the potion is faint and seemingly ethereal.
- A cool and smooth feeling on their oral cavity upon drinking it.

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> Blood as blue as the sky, creating illusory bubbles as if linked to the spiritual realm. A pure soft gelatinous crystal that reflects bits of lights seeming to contain a resplendent void of stars within.

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- 10 milliliters Lavos Squid's Blood.
- 50-gram Star Crystal.

Or
- A Seer Beyonder Characteristic.

Supplementary Ingredients

- 100 milliliters of Purified Water.
- 13 drops of Night Vanilla Liquids.
- 7 Gold Mint Leaves.
- 3 drops of Poison Hemlock.
- 9 grams of Dragon Blood Grass Powder.
