# Sequence 1 - Attendant of Mysteries

## Traits

- The boundary between physical body and Spirit Body disappears: the two remain separable yet unified and can be shifted, separated, or integrated at will. This state manifests Bizarreness and Spirit World symbolism.

## Explicit Powers

- **Spirit Body Threads Regeneration**: Restores the past Spirit Body Threads of material that once belonged to something with a Spirit Body, turning it into a Marionette.
  - It cannot create threads for something that never had them. Meat, fish, plants, and other former living material qualify; metals and minted currency generally do not.
  - A creature that eats regenerated food can also become a Marionette, resembling intense corruption.
- **Reassembly / Tampering / Grafting**: Combines unrelated physical objects, beings, locations, abstract concepts, definitions, directions, logic, or rules into an inconceivable but real result.
  - It can join beginnings to endpoints, redirect connections and targets, or alter the association of anchors and prayers.
  - Locations in reality or the Spirit World can be Grafted together for indirect teleportation. The user must locate the Spirit World destination; without access to Sefirah Castle, this generally requires preparation.
  - Grafting a remote area near the main body can keep travelling Marionettes inside their control range.
- **Realm of Mysteries**: Forms a curtain-like Concealed space around important thoughts, an embryonic Divine Kingdom that distorts Space-Time, separates its interior from outside, and prevents enemies or information from leaving.
  - It is more difficult to break into than certain comparable independent worlds, but that does not make it harder than all of them to destroy or seal.
- **Miracles**: Produces fantastical and bizarre effects directly, without reproducing them through lower- or same-level abilities.

## Strengthened Abilities

- **Spirit Body Threads Manipulation**: Fully controls and converts a target within about 3 to 4 seconds. A Marionette can use the Attendant's powers without containing a Worm of Spirit.
- **Resurrection**: The count resets to four regardless of uses spent as a Miracle Invoker.

## Mythical Creature Form

- The evolved Worms of Spirit distribute powers more robustly: theft of an ability from one worm-bearing Marionette does not remove that ability from the main body. Theft at a comparable level is also strengthened, so this is not absolute protection.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- [[Grafting and Reassembly]] → **Reassembly / Tampering / Grafting**: initial mastery of the Pathway Authority.
- [[Change (Lord of Mysteries)]] and [[Inconceivable and Bizarreness (Lord of Mysteries)]] → the reported symbolic basis of **Reassembly / Tampering / Grafting**. These remain group-level symbolisms rather than separate Authorities inherited by this Sequence.
- Concealment (Fool, partial/derived) → **Realm of Mysteries** and the hint of Concealment within Grafting.
- [[Miracles (Fool)]] → direct **Miracles** at Angel level.
- [[Spirit World Dominion]] → the body/Spirit Body state and **Spirit Body Threads Regeneration**: a partial, composite Pathway expression rather than ownership of the full group Authority.
- Puppetry remains the resulting derived power-domain for Marionette effects, not an independent Authority.

### Advancement Ritual

- Build a town consisting only of marionettes, and design a trajectory of fate for every marionette. By letting them interact with each other, they would act as a sufficiently real-life painting and create a corresponding area in the spirit world.
- The larger the town, the more the marionettes involved, the more detailed the daily lives are, and the more realistic and extensiveness the different fates are, the better the ritual's effects would be.
