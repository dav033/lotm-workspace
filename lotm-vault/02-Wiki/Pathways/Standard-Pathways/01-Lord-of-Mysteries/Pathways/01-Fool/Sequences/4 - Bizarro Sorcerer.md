# Sequence 4 - Bizarro Sorcerer

## Traits

- **Spirituality**: Spirituality is increased. Simple methods can produce more information, including instantaneous Dream Divination with a coin.
- **Spirit Vision**: Spirit Vision can be activated at will without a corresponding physical action.

## Explicit Powers

- **Worms of Spirit**: Separates components of the incomplete Mythical Creature Form. A separated worm remains part of the Bizarro Sorcerer, but does not give a Marionette thought or self-awareness.
- **Bestowal**: Places at least one Worm of Spirit in a Marionette so it can use the Bizarro Sorcerer's powers.
  - The initial limit is about 50 separated worms and rises with potion digestion. Exceeding the limit causes incurable schizophrenia-like symptoms and increases the risk of losing control.
  - Worms can be bestowed, retrieved, or switched through Spirit Body Threads within 1 kilometre. If the connection is cut, the Marionette dies shortly afterward; interruption of the characteristic's eventual return can permanently cost the worm.
  - A dead Marionette can be shaped into forms impossible for a living creature. A worm-bearing Marionette can convert another target and extend the initial conversion chain to 1,150 metres, but it must carry an additional worm to pass one onward.
- **Concealment**: Hides the user's Sequence from same-level or lower Justiciar Pathway Beyonders, but does not hide that the user is a Beyonder.

## Strengthened Abilities

- **Damage Transfer**: Transfers the user's or another person's wounds and diseases to a paper figurine.
- **Air Cannon**: Air Bullet is strengthened to cannon-level force.
- **Flaming Jump**: Flame-based phasing reaches 1 kilometre.
- **Illusion Creation**: Illusions receive a qualitative improvement, including extremely loud auditory illusions, illusory thoughts, and concealment of something as large as a cruise liner.
- **Shapeshifting**: Allows transformation into animals not too different in size; the size restriction is removed while in Spirit Body form.
- **Spirit Body Threads Manipulation**: Initial control takes about 3 seconds and full control about 15 seconds, beginning at a range of 150 metres and improving with digestion.
- **Marionette Control**: Initially controls up to 50 Marionettes within 1 kilometre. A Marionette outside that range remains controlled if it returns within 10 minutes.
  - The user can read a Marionette's superficial consciousness and approximate Spirit Channelling, with poorer results from higher-level targets.
- **Marionette Interchange**: Swaps bodies with a Marionette within 1 kilometre; the swap can include both bodies' external objects or exclude them.

## Mythical Creature Form

- The incomplete form consists of small transparent maggots inscribed with three-dimensional, tentacle-like patterns: the **Worms of Spirit**.
- The patterns express Bizarreness, Madness, Change, Strength, and Wisdom. Even two worms can saturate a bedroom with feelings of Madness and Bizarreness and make its lights fluctuate irregularly.
- A worm can be killed so its carcass receives bestowed power and serves as material for a high-level charm or special bullet. Doing so fractures the Spirit Body, causes considerable damage, and cannot be repeated frequently without risking death.
- Lost worms can be restored by absorbing Fool Pathway Beyonder Characteristics.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- [[Spirit World Dominion]] → **Worms of Spirit** and **Spirit Body Threads Manipulation**: a partial, composite Pathway expression, not ownership of the full group-level Authority.
- Puppetry → **Bestowal**, **Marionette Control**, and **Marionette Interchange**: a derived power-domain, not an independent Authority.
- Concealment (Fool, partial/derived) → **Concealment**: a lower expression of Realm of Mysteries.
- [[Change (Lord of Mysteries)]] and [[Inconceivable and Bizarreness (Lord of Mysteries)]] → shapeshifting, the Worms' divine patterns, and bizarre Marionette effects: group-level symbolic contributions, not native ownership of either symbolism by this Sequence.

## Potion

### Potion Properties

- Formed by intertwining strands of black mist resembling both human faces and various animals, these mists alternately vanish and reappear in an extremely bizarre manner.
- It is ice-cold.
- Upon drinking, it brings one numbness, one that reaches deep into their soul.

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> A ghostly blue single eye. A palm-sized human-shaped object. Filled with a transparent liquid, from time to time it bubbled, emanating a black glow. There appeared to be maggots circling the object.

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- A Bizarro Bane's Main Eye.
- The True Soul Body of a Spirit World Plunderer.

Or
- A Bizarro Sorcerer Beyonder Characteristic.

Supplementary Ingredients

- 200 milliliters of a Bizarro Bane's Blood.
- 30 grams of a Spirit World Plunderer's Dust.
- 10 grams of Red-hair Birch Bark.
- One segment of Golden Grapevines.
- Fingernail-Sized Self-made Rubber Mask.

### Advancement Ritual

Relying on one's strength and strategy, orchestrate a grand performance before many spectators to kill a Beyonder creature at the level of a demigod. Then, at the end of the performance, consume the potion.
