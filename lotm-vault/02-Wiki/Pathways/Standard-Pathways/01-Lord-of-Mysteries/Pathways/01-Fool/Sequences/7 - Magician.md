# Sequence 7 - Magician

## Traits

- **Hand Dexterity** — the hands become substantially more agile and nimble; with sufficient practice, this alone can support top-level mundane stage magic.
- **Strengthened Spirit Vision** — spiritual perception improves slightly, but still cannot see an Astral Projection deep within an Ether Body and therefore cannot by itself identify whether a target is a Beyonder.

## Explicit Powers

Magicians gain nine spell-like abilities that can be cast rapidly without chanting or separately injecting spirituality:

- **Damage Transfer** — moves a wound to another part of a body, such as an arm or leg, and can also be applied to another person's wounds. The Magician must still be alive and able to move a hand; each wound can be transferred only once, and damage cannot be transferred onto an object.
- **Flaming Jump** — phases through a previously placed spark or any flame within an initial range of 30 meters; the range grows with potion digestion. The Magician is immune to ordinary flames, a Pyromaniac's flames, and a Witch's black flames only while in transit, and must use Flame Controlling for protection after emerging. It can function where access to the Spirit World is restricted because the user does not directly enter it, although extreme bad luck or disturbances in the corresponding Spirit World region can still make the jump dangerous.
- **Air Bullet** — fires compressed-air projectiles by snapping the fingers or mimicking a gunshot. Initial speed and force are comparable to bullets from a custom revolver and improve with digestion and advancement.
- **Paper Figurine Substitute** — transforms a paper figurine into the Magician's likeness and swaps places with it, blocking a fatal blow or reducing curse damage while the Magician reappears nearby. A figurine can also serve as a crude body double.
- **Flame Controlling** — manipulates existing flames and ignites flammable substances within an initial range of 30 meters; the range grows with digestion and advancement. After fully digesting the potion, the Magician can create one swirling flame without an existing fire source.
- **Underwater Breathing** — creates a thin, invisible air pipe with an initial length of exactly 5 meters; its length increases with digestion.
- **Bone Softening** — temporarily softens the bones enough to escape restraints such as handcuffs, ropes, or confined containers.
- **Drawing Paper as Weapons** — expands Paper Daggers into the ability to harden paper into varied weapons or implements, including clubs, bricks, and canes.
- **Illusion Creation** — produces near-realistic combinations of color, sound, and smell. Sound illusions can also carry the Magician's voice secretly to another person.

They also gain two supporting capabilities:

- **Spiritual-Intuition Interference** — provides multiple ways to disrupt a target's spiritual intuition.
- **Minor Tricks** — performs limited stage-magic effects, such as exchanging an item in the hand with one in a pocket; these tricks have little direct combat value.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- [[Grafting and Reassembly]] → **Damage Transfer**, **Underwater Breathing** — identified by the author as lower or specific applications of Reassembly.
- [[Fooling]] → **Paper Figurine Substitute**, **Illusion Creation** — strong mechanical interpretation; the sources do not label these low-Sequence abilities as direct Authority use.
- **Flaming Jump**, **Air Bullet**, **Flame Controlling**, **Bone Softening**, **Drawing Paper as Weapons**, and **Minor Tricks** remain abilities without a supported Authority assignment.

## Potion

### Potion Properties

- A unique liquid resembling a constant display of fireworks, with colors of red, orange, yellow, green, and others continuously scattering, disappearing, and reappearing.
- It feels ice-cold.
- When drinking it, at every moment, it would feel like countless bubbles were bursting.

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> It was a grayish-white item about the size of a fist. Its surface was filled with gullies and it looked soft but ductile. It looked like a brain that had been extracted out of a living being. When touched, it becomes a gray-white sticky liquid.

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- 1 True Root of a Mist Treant.
- All the spinal fluid of a Dark Patterned Black Panther.

Or
- A Magician Beyonder Characteristic.

Supplementary Ingredients

- 60 milliliters of Purified Water.
- 30 milliliters of Mist Treant Juice.
- 3 grams of Droplet Gem Powder.
- 4 drops of Fantasy Grass Essential Oil.
