# Sequence 2 - Miracle Invoker

## Traits

- **Spirituality and Soul**: Spirituality, Spiritual Intuition, Danger Intuition, and divination receive a major increase.
- **Dream Divination**: Results can appear before the user's eyes without sleep. Combined intuition can show several possible futures and detailed distant scenes connected to the user.
- **Spirit Body Travel**: The Spirit Body can leave the physical body and enter the Spirit World.

## Explicit Powers

- **Wish Accumulation**: Fulfills the wishes of beings with Spirituality to build the collective capacity for larger Miracles. Early wishes must be small and accumulate step by step.
- **Wish Granting**: Fulfills another person's wish either with an available Beyonder power or artifact, or by spending accumulated wish power to create a true Miracle.
  - Available effects depend on what the individual Miracle Invoker has accumulated. Reported applications include teleportation, suppressing Godhood in an area, shrinking an area, restoring structures or bodies, extending lifespan, and temporarily raising power.
  - Larger wishes are more prone to distortion. listed limits on time, area, and scope reduce that risk; directly changing a high-level being has weaker or more distorted results.
  - Similar wishes cannot be granted repeatedly to the same person within a short period.
- **Resurrection**: On death, the Beyonder Characteristics return to the Historical Void and fuse with past Worms of Spirit, which re-enter reality and rebuild the body.
  - This default Miracle does not require accumulated wishes and can be used four times. The body must reform near the corpse, and the characteristics must be able to return to the Historical Void.
  - The physical body must be reborn within 3 days after the Spirit Body enters the Fog of History; otherwise the user truly dies or becomes an Evil Spirit. Natural death also consumes one use.
- **Jamming in Fate**: Raises or lowers the probability of a future outcome. It interferes with Fate but is distinct from the Wheel of Fortune Pathway's direct manipulation.

## Strengthened Abilities

- **Historical Void Summoning**: Extends summoning from people and objects to familiar scenes and historical events. Up to nine projections can exist, no more than three of them Angel-level.
- **Spirit Body Threads Manipulation**: Controls at least 5,000 Marionettes across a city-wide range.

## Mythical Creature Form

- This Sequence has a complete Mythical Creature Form: a vortex of layered Worms of Spirit with invisible tentacles growing from within.
- The worm-based body regenerates serious injuries rapidly, including an entire limb within seconds.
- At least 5,000 Worms of Spirit can be separated and implanted in Marionettes.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- [[Miracles (Fool)]] → **Wish Accumulation**, **Wish Granting**, and the default Miracle of **Resurrection**. This Authority is reported under [[Inconceivable and Bizarreness (Lord of Mysteries)]].
- [[History (Fool)]] → the Historical Void mechanism used by **Resurrection** and strengthened summoning.
- [[Anchor of Destiny (Fool)]] and Fate (Fool, partial jurisdiction) → **Jamming in Fate**: a probable lower expression associated by the secondary source with [[Beacon of Destiny]], not unrestricted Fate control or full Sequence 0 Anchor of Destiny.
