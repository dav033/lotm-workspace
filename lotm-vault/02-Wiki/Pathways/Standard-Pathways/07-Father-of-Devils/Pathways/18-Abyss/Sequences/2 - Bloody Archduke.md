# Sequence 2 - Bloody Archduke

## Explicit Powers

### Description

"They" wield portions of the authorities of **“Malice”** and **“Body”**. "Their" core abilities revolve around **Blood**, **Bodily Fluids**, **Fear**, and **Malice**.

### Authorities

- **Malice**: "They" have authority over the domain of **Malice**.
    - **Incarnation of Malice**: *" **Malice** never dies, neither do **Demons** ". "* They" can plant a **Seed of Malice** deep within a target's psyche. When "Their" main body is destroyed, "They" can **Resurrect** within the target's **Mind**, igniting the target's **Desires** and **Blood** and killing the target.
        - If "Their" target's main body has a connected psyche to other bodies (e.g. an **[Unaging's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Demoness_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_3:_Unaging_\(Demoness_of_Unaging\)) Mirror Self**), those psyches will also be eroded as well.
            - Using the [Robe of Divine Blood](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Robe_of_Divine_Blood "Robe of Divine Blood"), [Emlyn White](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Emlyn_White "Emlyn White") revived within [Katarina Pelle's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Katarina_Pelle "Katarina Pelle") **Mirror Self** after she herself resurrected, killing her in the process.
                - A **Bloody Archduke's** **Seed of Malice** is also able to catalyze the **Malice** of the target, making the affected target temporarily **Cold-Blooded**.
            - If the target commits a truly **Malicious** act during this period, they will fall further into **Depravity** until they fall into an [Abyss](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Abyss "Abyss").
                - This can both cause a target to fall into **Depravity** as well as be transported to the actual [Abyss](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Abyss "Abyss") because of the fact that the [Abyss](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Abyss "Abyss") is in everyone's heart.
                        - Affected by this, indescribable ravings echoed in [Katarina Pelle](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Katarina_Pelle "Katarina Pelle") 's ears, as if her innermost **Malicious** emotions and desires were singing in unison, causing her to show signs of losing control.
        - As an [Angel-level](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Angels "Angels") **Devil**, "Their" intense **Malice** and **Desires** can overpower other entities' mental control abilities over "Them", be it from active influence or passive effect.
        - Rather than succumbing to a **[Demoness of Catastrophe's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Demoness_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_2:_Catastrophe_\(Demoness_of_Catastrophe\)) Charm**, "They" would develop an intense possessiveness, enabling "Them" to act decisively instead of being frozen in place.
        - **Malice** brings forth **Fear** to the target. This is a pure and instinctive **Fear** that can cause the **Mind** to go blank, making difficult for those affected to think and act.
- **Body**: "They" also have authority over the domain of **Body**.
    - **Blood Sovereign**: "They" command the **Blood** and **Bodily Fluids** of "Themselves" and others.
        - As long as a single drop of **Blood** or **Bodily Fluid** has not been destroyed, a **Bloody Archduke** can quickly return and regenerate "Their" body.
                - A **Bloody Archduke** can use "Their" own **Blood** and **Bodily Fluids** in order to **Corrode** and **Pollute** the external world and targets.
            - Being corroded under "Their" influence or touch would make "Their" opponents bleed from all orifices of their body as both their **Blood** and **Desires** get ignited until death.

### Strengthened Abilities

- All abilities from "Their" previous **Sequences** have been further enhanced.

### Mythical Creature Form

- "They" have become a full **Mythical Creature**, with "Their" main characteristic being that they seem to be made of congealed **Blood**, capable of liquefying and coalescing.

## Authority → Power Mapping

This maps conceptual origin; it does not mean a Sequence 2 Bloody Archduke owns an Authority.

- [[Degeneration (Abyss)]], [[Desire (Abyss)]] → **Incarnation of Malice** — Malice is described elsewhere in this pathway as a subordinate authority born from Degeneration and Desires.
- [[Deviants (Abyss)]] → **Blood Sovereign** — Body is a subordinate authority under Deviants, matching command over blood, bodily fluids, and regeneration.
- **Strengthened Abilities** and **Mythical Creature Form** name no new power to map.

## Potion

### Potion Properties

> A vial of thick, disgusting, viscous blood.

### Beyonder Characteristic Appearance

> A deformed, enormous, black heart that exudes a strong bloody smell and drips blood constantly.

### Potion Formula

Main Ingredients

- The head of a "Demon Prince".
- The heart of a "Twin Serpent Lord".

Or
- A Bloody Archduke Beyonder Characteristic.

Supplementary Ingredients

- 1000ml of a Bloody Archduke's blood or (500 ml of a "Demon Prince's" acidic blood, 500 ml of a "Twin Serpent Lord's" venomous blood).
- 49 drops of blood from different races, ranks, and identities.
- 1 drop of a True God's blood.

### Advancement Ritual

- The ritual involves sacrificing 13 settlements, each containing at least 10.000 intelligent beings, to the Abyss.
    - Each sacrifice must be made within three months of each other.
    - Furthermore, the sacrifice must be performed using a "Blood Ritual," which involves killing each creature in the settlement one by one, then using their gushing blood to cleanse the altar and repaint the corresponding symbols and patterns.
