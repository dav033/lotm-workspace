# Sequence 7 - Serial Killer

## Explicit Powers

### Traits

- **Physical Enhancements**: After undergoing the initial stages of dehumanization, they possess a robust physique.
    - Even after having his chest caved, ribs cracked, face swelled, his left shoulder crumpled entirely, and a skull fractured, with charred marks covered his upper body after the brutal assault, [Bram Andariel](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Bram_Andariel "Bram Andariel") was far from unconsciousness. His mind still functioned, and thoughts raced before settling on [Lumian](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Lumian_Lee "Lumian Lee") 's smile.
- **Enhanced Mental Attributes**: Their intelligence and mental capabilities will be enhanced.

### New Abilities

- **Knowledge (Devil Worship)**: Upon advancement, a **Serial Killer** will gain a wealth of and master all kinds of knowledge related to **Devil** worship and its **Ritualistic Magic**.
    - By creating special or unique killing sprees, they would please and gain favors from **Devils**.
        - **Serial Killers** often tend to choose those who exhibit degraded behaviors, often linked to their personal traumas- as victims of their serial killing sprees.
                - Such **Serial Killers** enjoy creating unique serial killings but also commit their crimes not merely as a role, but also to please the **Devils** of the [Abyss](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Abyss "Abyss").
            - Once the serial killing cases are closed they would be able to perform the final step of this **Ritual**, letting them summon the corresponding **Devil** projection from the [Abyss](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Abyss "Abyss") and obtain it's assistance to achieve their desired outcome.
                - By saying foul words in the **Devil Language**, a mystical connection seemed to form with the **Serial Killer**, linking them based on their location, creating sinister symbols and patterns, marking the initiation of a ritual to summon the projection of an **Abyssal Devil**.
                    - Even if one's string of murders aren't entirely complete and fell short by a few targets, so long as they meet the minimum threshold, they can expedite the ritual and still summon the **Devil**; however, this shortcut wouldn’t yield the same effectiveness as the standard procedure.
                                - **Serial Killers** can summon these projections because the **Devils** respond to the 's unique abilities, not because those **Devils** themselves have attained the corresponding status.
                        - Theoretically, the summoned **Devil** projection will obey the [Ritual](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Ritualistic_Magic "Ritualistic Magic") performer; however, if it's rank is too high or it finds a loophole to bypass the ritual's protection, it will obey the corresponding true **Devil** in the [Abyss](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Abyss "Abyss").
- **Interference**: They can effectively interfere with [Divination](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Divination_Art "Divination Art") and **Spirit Channeling.**

### Strengthened Abilities

- **Demonic Attribute**: Their ability has been enhanced.
    - Releasing a dense black smoke, [Bram Andariel](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Bram_Andariel "Bram Andariel") morphed, adopting the color of a chameleon, seamlessly blending with the spreading smoke. Swift and silent, he approached the door, opened it with precision, and lunged outside.

## Authority → Power Mapping

This maps conceptual origin; it does not mean a Sequence 7 Serial Killer owns an Authority.

- **Knowledge (Devil Worship)** has no supported Authority assignment; it describes ritual technique for petitioning and summoning Devils rather than an expression of one of the pathway's own authorities.
- [[Curses (Abyss)]] → **Interference** — probable interpretation of its hexing effect on Divination and Spirit Channeling, without a standalone confirmation.
- [[Deviants (Abyss)]] → **Demonic Attribute** — the camouflage/mutation effect under Strengthened Abilities matches Deviants' authority over bodily mutation.
- **Physical Enhancements** and **Enhanced Mental Attributes** remain generic traits without a supported Authority assignment.
