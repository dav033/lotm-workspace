# Sequence 4 - Demon

## Explicit Powers

### Description

The " **Demons** of the **Body** " and " **Demons** of the **Mind** ".

### New Abilities

- **Demon of the Mind**: **Demons** are an advanced version of a **Serial Killer**. They are cold, sharp-minded, and sly. They are the textbook example of a standard high IQ creature.
    - They are cunning and enjoy misleading others.
        - In addition, they can effectively influence targets within a certain range, subtly and imperceptibly diminishing their thinking abilities.
            - This will make those affected careless and prone to errors.
- **Demon of the Body**: Being a " **Demon** of the **Body** ", they have physical attributes that are ranked pretty high on the total ranking list compared to other **Demigods**.
- As a **Demon**, they possess **Spells** and **Spell** -like abilities that are ranked pretty high on the total ranking list when compared to other **Sequence 4 Demigods**.
    - They have gained new **Spells** such as **Acid Swamp**, **Hellfire**, **Corrosive Fog**, **Filthy Illusion**, and **Projection** that does not rely on the [Abyss](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Abyss "Abyss").

### Strengthened Abilities

- **Desire Control**: They have undergone a qualitative change. Now, **Demons** no longer need to wait for an expression or increase in a target’s **Desires**. They can now directly manipulate it through their actions.
    - **Demons** are able to make their targets fall into an unavoidable illusion. Their sorrow, anger, pain, greed, vanity, arrogance, and other **Desires** would rapidly swell as illusions of those **Desires** will manifest around them in the form of themselves exhibiting those emotions.
        - Some individuals would have their mind explode from the illusion while others lose their sanity or experience a loss of control due to the illusion of themselves exhibiting that **Desire**.
        - They can use sound, eye contact, movements, gestures, and various other means to directly influence those within a certain radius, making them enter into a state of delusion while their emotions and **Desires** will automatically expand at flying speeds.
        - After which, one of three things can happen:
            1. The target(s) cognition or sanity will be completely destroyed.
                        2. The target(s) mind will be lit up like a fuse and explode.
                        3. The target(s) will start the process of losing control.
- **Danger Premonition (Malice Perception)**: Their ability to sense danger has been enhanced.
    - **Demons** are exceptionally sensitive to danger and sometimes, they can even predict the possibility of a threat to their lives *one* to *two days* early in advance.
- **Filthy Language**: A **Demon** will possess a more complete set of **Language of Foulness**.
    - Words like **Confusion**, **Sloth** and **Blasphemy** have been added to their repertoire.
        - The [Montsouris Ghost](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Montsouris_Ghost "Montsouris Ghost") said " *Human nature is evil!*", causing all [Saints](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sequence "Sequence"), but the three [Unshadowed](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sun_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_4:_Unshadowed), to suddenly feel an intense malice. It was resentment from being assigned to dangerous missions.

### Mythical Creature Form

- As **Demigods** at **Sequence 4** who have obtained partial **Godhood**, they are able to manifest their incomplete **Mythical Creature** **Form** if they disregard any of the consequences. Their **Mythical Creature Form** is mainly determined by race, individual personality, **Desires**, and will.
    - **Devil Transformation**: Even though manifesting the incomplete **Mythical Creature** **Form** can easily bring about losing control, **Demons** have a semi-solution around it. They can let a certain part or area of their body manifest the appearance and characteristics of their **Mythical Creature** **Form**. After which, they can maintain it that way for a prolonged period of time.
        - This way, they are often able to obtain unique, and bizarre effects and abilities related to the corresponding body part that has been **Demonized**.

## Authority → Power Mapping

This maps conceptual origin; it does not mean a Sequence 4 Demon owns an Authority.

- [[Corruption (Abyss)]] → **Demon of the Mind** — mental deterioration and loss of rational judgement mirror Corruption's role as "an inevitable outcome of the explosion of Desire and the loss of rational control."
- [[Deviants (Abyss)]] → **Demon of the Body** — Body is a subordinate authority under Deviants.
- [[Desire (Abyss)]] → **Desire Control** — direct manipulation of a target's Desires.
- [[Degeneration (Abyss)]], [[Desire (Abyss)]] → **Danger Premonition (Malice Perception)** — Malice is a subordinate authority born from Degeneration and Desires.
- [[Filth (Abyss)]], [[Corruption (Abyss)]] → **Filthy Language** — the Language of Foulness is elsewhere described as "filled with Filth and Corruption."
- [[Deviants (Abyss)]] → **Devil Transformation** — a partial, controlled manifestation of the Mythical Creature Form, matching Deviants' authority over mutation.

- 1 black heart of an Archdemon.

- 1 Sacred Symbol from the Church of a True Deity.

### Advancement Ritual

- Induce at least 10.000 intelligent beings to voluntarily sell their souls to the performer.
    - The performer must successfully acquire the corresponding souls.
    - The higher the target's rank, the fewer souls are required.
