# Sequence 6 - Devil

## Explicit Powers

### Description

**Devils** biggest weakness is their tendency to easily lose control. Even if they do not lose control, they will often exhibit a kind of insanity that is a culmination of both cruelty and cold-bloodedness. They are very easily possessed and controlled by their desire for blood, murder, and various other things.

### Individual Differences### Traits

- **Physical Enhancement**: The first stage of qualitative changes in the is the comprehensive enhancement of the **Devil's** strength, speed, and defense.
    - Their skin has been mutated, equivalent to wearing a layer of hard armor while their their blood and flesh can act as a retardant that can block objects and reduce damage.
        - A **Devil** also possesses strong resistance capabilities, being immune to most toxins/poisons and not fearing any **Curses** or **Flames** within a certain level.
        - A **Devil** is fast enough to create afterimages.

### New Abilities

- **Danger Premonition (Malice Perception)**: They can sense and grasp the place of origin of danger, as well as who this danger is from. In this way, the **Devil** can precisely devise countermeasures, take revenge, or nip the threat in the bud. Different **Devils** have different degrees of this intuition.
    - Unlike a **[Monster's](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Wheel_of_Fortune_Pathway/Abilities#Sequence_9:_Monster) Danger Premonition**, theirs doesn’t stem from **Fate** but from the [Abyss](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Abyss "Abyss").
        - Their **Danger Premonition** requires 3 conditions to be met before it can activate.
        1. If an existence is a fatal threat to the **Devil** and can cause life-threatening harm to them.
                2. Said existence is intending on doing such harm within a certain period and range.
                3. Said existence proceeds to actually act upon their intentions to harm the **Devil**.
        - Different variations of **Devils** would possess different degrees of **Danger Premonition**.
        - The "certain period" of sensing danger via **Danger Premonition** for some **Devils** can range from just a measly *few minutes*, to a whole entire *24 hours*.
                - The range of a **Devil’s** **Danger Premonition** may only span from a few miles- or in other cases- span the range of an entire metropolitan area.
        - Apart from this, they aren’t good at [Divination](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Divination_Art "Divination Art") or other methods of sensing danger.
- **Language of Foulness**: **Devils** can speak demonic words originating from the [Abyss](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Abyss "Abyss"), filled with **Filth** and **Corruption**, and unleash their corresponding abilities. At this stage, they can use the following:
    - **Slow**: This causes all targets within a *7-8 meter radius* to instantly turn numb and slow or even come to a halt, however, it can only be maintained for *2 seconds*.
        - **Death**: This **Spell** causes the target's blood to rust and clot. Victims will cough out rusted clumps of blood and have rusted red marks appear on their skin.
        - If the enemy fails to avoid or resist the effect of a **Devil's Death**, it can directly cause them to die or become very close to dying.
            - This will make the target's heart feel as if it is being squeezed tightly by an invisible hand as they die covered in mottled red rust.
        - **Corruption**: An area of effect attack that can cause the targeted area and individuals within to start showing signs and symptoms of **Abyss** **Corruption**.
        - This will cover the enemy's body with a slightly viscous black liquid, which then explodes into shattered pieces of paper and corrupted black fog.
- Upon advancing into this **Sequence**, **Devils** will gain access and mastery over a wide range of **Spells**, encompassing the fields of **Fire**, **Poison**, and **Filth/Foulness**.
    - **Sulfur Fireball**: They can launch multiple pale blue **Fireballs** that create a powerful explosion and poison anyone or anything that comes into contact with the **Flames**.
        - No more than *3 at a time* can be launched and conjured.
        - **Sword of Lava**: They can conjure up a massive blade shaped **Flaming** sword that looks like a scimitar in design and holds extremely high attack power.
        - A **Sword of Lava's** damage against non-undead and non-corrupted creatures is significantly higher than the [**Priest of Light's**](https://lordofthemysteries.fandom.com/wiki/Sun_Pathway/Abilities "Sun Pathway/Abilities") **Holy Light Summoning**.
                - One swing from a **Sword of Lava** is capable of slicing a thick stone pillar in two, with the impact surface looking like it has been seared and melted.
        - **Flame Cage**: Within a certain range, a **Devil** will be able to conjure up a large **Cage** of **Flames** that can be used to block targets from escaping.
        - A pale blue **Flame** can also be ignited in the palm of their hand, causing bullets to be trapped in the **Cage** and solidify in the pale blue **flame**.
        - In addition, **Devils** also possess **Spells** and abilities such as **Poisonous Flame Manipulation**, **Volcanic Eruption**, **Highly Toxic Vapor**, etc.
- **Devil Transformation**: **Devils** can transform into their true forms temporarily, gigantifying their bodies, giving them bat wings and goat horns, while also increasing their strength, speed, and defense as well. This is akin to breaking free of their bindings.
    - When they enter their **Devil Transformation** state, their skin will deeply mutates, appearing in a dull but actually deep, sinister black and their entire body seems to be covered in a thick, hard armor.
        - Their blood and flesh becoming extremely elastic and resilient.
        - While in their **Devil Transformation** state certain restrictions to **Spells** have been lessened. For example, they can now launch *10-20* **Sulfur Fireballs** simultaneously as opposed to just 3.
        - When creatures and people of different races become **Devils**, their outward appearance will differ; however, they will all share several common characteristics.
        - These details include gigantic bodies, deep black and sinister skin, curved and terrifying ram horns covered with countless mysterious patterns, and pale blue sulfur flames.

### Strengthened Abilities

- Their foundation as a **Serial Killer** and **Criminal** has been improved. They excel at using all kinds of items and objects to cause harm, hold strong hand-to-hand capabilities, and don't panic or experience fear.
    - This is effectively an enhancement to a **Serial Killer's Enhanced Mental Attributes** and a **Criminal's Criminal Proficiency** when it comes to the **Combat** field.

## Authority → Power Mapping

This maps conceptual origin; it does not mean a Sequence 6 Devil owns an Authority.

- [[Deviants (Abyss)]] → **Physical Enhancement** — the mutated skin, flesh, and resistance described here match Deviants' authority over bodily mutation.
- [[Degeneration (Abyss)]], [[Desire (Abyss)]] → **Danger Premonition (Malice Perception)** — directly said to stem "from the Abyss" rather than Fate, matching Malice's origin as a subordinate authority of Degeneration and Desire.
- [[Filth (Abyss)]], [[Corruption (Abyss)]] → **Language of Foulness** — directly described as demonic words "filled with Filth and Corruption."
- [[Deviants (Abyss)]] → **Devil Transformation** — a fuller manifestation of the same mutation-based authority as the Sequence 4 **Devil Transformation**.
- The broader **Spells** granted at this Sequence (Fire/Poison/Filth) are named examples rather than a single headline power, and **Strengthened Abilities** only references prior-Sequence proficiencies.
