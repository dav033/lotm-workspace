# Sequence 3 - Disciple of Silence

## Explicit Powers

- The cited secondary source reports brewing potent curses through prolonged silence.
- It reports possession of objects, an enhanced curse source, and Transfiguration Curse that temporarily turns a target into a harmless creature.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- [[Curses (Chained)]] → curses brewed through silence and Transfiguration Curse.
- [[Objects (Chained)]] → possession of objects as a limited early expression; this does not grant Sequence 0 control scope.

## Potion

- Formula: throat of a Cursed Artifact; supplementary ingredients include contaminated water, an item cursed by such an artifact, a complete skin, and a self-statue as anchor.
- Ritual: establish a cult whose followers understand, enact, and speak the aspirant's will; higher-level followers reduce the stated thousand-person threshold.
