# Sequence 5 - Wraith

## Explicit Powers

- The cited secondary source reports Wraith Transformation, Spirit World travel, invisibility, possession, Mirror Blink, spirit-directed attacks, and death-related spells.
- It retains most Zombie capabilities and reports stronger spirituality and anti-divination.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- [[Deviants (Chained)]] → Wraith Transformation as a Pathway-specific Deviant state.
- Possession, Mirror Blink, Spirit World travel, and death-related spells remain Powers; the supplied text does not assign each one to an independent Authority.

## Potion

- Formula: Wraith consciousness crystal; supplementary ingredients include Wraith blood, contaminated cold water, Wraith dust, a mirror, and nine souls.
- Ritual: enter the Spirit World, locate information corresponding to oneself, seal or imprison it for at least three minutes, then drink the potion before release.
