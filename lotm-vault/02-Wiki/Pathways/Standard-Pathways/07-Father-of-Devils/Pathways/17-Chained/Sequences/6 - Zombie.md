# Sequence 6 - Zombie

## Explicit Powers

- The cited secondary source reports a reinforced undead body, regeneration, ice and frost effects, decay effects, and control over undead and ghosts.
- It reports a replacement curse: urges for human blood and flesh worsen during a Full Moon.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- [[Deviants (Chained)]] → the transformed Zombie body and its regenerative undead state.
- [[Curses (Chained)]] → the Full Moon replacement curse.
- Ice, decay, and undead control remain sourced Powers without separate Authority promotion.

## Potion

- Formula: a Zombie heart; supplementary ingredients include living and Zombie flesh and blood, a Zombie eyeball, and Moonflowers.
- The supplied capture gives no acting method.
