# Sequence 4 - Puppet

## Explicit Powers

- The cited secondary source reports curse-making through mystical puppets, control of nearby lifeless objects, and a stronger Evil Spirit form.
- It also reports Objectification for restraining inner madness and a capacity to restrain Beyonder Characteristic convergence.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- [[Curses (Chained)]] → curse-making through mystical puppets.
- [[Objects (Chained)]] → control of lifeless objects and Objectification.
- [[Binding (Chained)]] → restraint of inner madness and Beyonder Characteristic convergence.
- [[Deviants (Chained)]] → the strengthened Evil Spirit form as a limited transformation expression.

## Potion

- Formula: Puppet eyes; supplementary ingredients include blood from a cursed death, Puppet hair, a frequently used item or clothing, and shackles or similar restraints.
- Ritual: possess a specially prepared, divine Mummy and consume the potion. The source describes a True Mummy as a demigod remnant prepared through a long burial process.
