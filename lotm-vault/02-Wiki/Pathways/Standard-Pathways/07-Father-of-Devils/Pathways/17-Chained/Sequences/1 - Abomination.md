# Sequence 1 - Abomination

## Explicit Powers

- The cited secondary source reports partial authority over Deviants and stronger curse effects.
- Evil Abomination spreads extreme evil and madness, dries nearby surroundings, and infects consciousness with madness.

## Authority → Power Mapping

- [[Deviants (Chained)]] → the reported partial Authority and Abomination state.
- [[Curses (Chained)]] → strengthened curse effects and the Mad Curse risk associated with advancement.

## Potion

- Formula: an Abomination Beyonder Characteristic; supplementary ingredients include nine curse or contamination types, a True God's child's corpse, supernatural blood, and an item from a broken or corrupted Divine Kingdom.
- Ritual: receive a qualifying True God's, activated Uniqueness's, or accommodated King of Angels' blessing. The source warns that the giver suffers a dangerous Mad Curse.
