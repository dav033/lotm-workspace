# Kotar

## Summary

Kotar is described by the supplied secondary capture as a Dark Demonic Wolf, a former subsidiary god of Flegrea, and a Sequence 2 Miracle Invoker of the Fool Pathway. The same capture records an earlier dual status as a Sequence 2 Miracle Invoker and Sequence 2 Servant of Concealment.

## Identity and Pathway

- God of Wishes under [[Flegrea]] during the Second Epoch.
- Sequence 2 Miracle Invoker of the [[Fool Pathway (disambiguation)]].
- Earlier dual pathway position: Miracle Invoker and Servant of Concealment, linking Fool and [[Darkness Pathway (disambiguation)]] evidence.
- Incomplete honorific name begins with “The Dark Lord that exists alongside History” and “The Embodiment of Countless Miracles”; the capture labels the remaining wording incomplete.

## History

- Survived from the Second Epoch into the Fifth Epoch by prioritizing safety and avoiding unworkable risks.
- After Flegrea’s death, Amon hunted him for centuries in the Forsaken Land of the Gods.
- On June 28, 1349 of the Fifth Epoch, he obtained a black velvet Curtain containing one Fool Pathway set from Sequence 9 to Sequence 1 after it was expelled from [[Sefirah Castle]].
- He used Nois City’s inhabitants as marionettes while preparing the Sequence 1 Attendant of Mysteries advancement ritual, then fled after sensing Amon and the True Creator.
- Klein later hunted him for the Beyonder characteristic required for advancement. Kotar committed suicide to escape, revived, and fled after leaving the Curtain behind.

## Relations

- Subsidiary god of [[Flegrea]].
- Dual Fool/Darkness Sequence 2 status is the key cross-pathway relation captured here.
- The Curtain, Nois City, Amon, the True Creator, and Klein are retained as source-reported names; this note does not create unverified character nodes for them.
