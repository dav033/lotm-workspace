# Flegrea

## Summary

Flegrea, the Annihilation Demonic Wolf, is described by the supplied secondary capture as an Ancient God of the Second Epoch associated with both the Darkness and Fool Pathways. The Darkness Pathway roster separately lists him under its Ancient Gods. This note remains an interpretation because the evidence is unofficial.

## Identity

- Ancient God of the Darkness Pathway and Fool Pathway.
- The source gives his Darkness composition as Uniqueness plus three Sequence 1 characteristics, and his Fool composition as Uniqueness plus one Sequence 1 characteristic.
- The capture characterizes him as crazy, cruel, bloodthirsty, and driven by a destructive instinct; this is source-reported characterization, not a general claim about the Pathways.

## History

The capture distinguishes a creation myth that forms demonic wolves from the Original Creator’s ears from its account of Flegrea as a surviving Pre-Epoch remnant wolf. It further reports that his descendants Antigonus and Mother of the Sky survived, crossed to the Northern Continent, and founded the concealed Nation of the Evernight.

The source places Flegrea’s death at the end of the Early Era of Fire, alongside Lilith and Kvastir, in a conspiracy involving the Ancient Sun God. It reports that the Darkness Uniqueness and a Sequence 1 Knight of Misfortune characteristic entered Amanises’s possession after Flegrea died; the later [[Evernight Goddess]] note records the corresponding holder relationship.

## Abilities and Pathway

- [[Darkness Pathway (disambiguation)]]: Uniqueness plus three Sequence 1 characteristics, per the supplied Flegrea capture.
- [[Fool Pathway (disambiguation)]]: Uniqueness plus one Sequence 1 characteristic, per the same capture.
- [[Sefirah Castle]]: the source says Flegrea knew of it under the name given by the King of Demonic Wolves but never entered it.

## Relationships

- Later Darkness holder: [[Evernight Goddess]].
- Descendants named by the source: Antigonus and Mother of the Sky; neither currently has an indexed character note in this vault.
- A separate Kotar capture identifies [[Kotar]] as God of Wishes, subsidiary god of Flegrea, and an early dual Fool/Darkness Sequence 2 figure.
