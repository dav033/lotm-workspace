# Zaratul

## Summary

Zaratul is described by the supplied secondary capture as the leader of the Secret Order and a high-Sequence Fool Pathway Beyonder. The capture states that he claimed Sequence 2, disappeared after a possible attempt to advance to Sequence 1, and later returned through resurrection-related events.

## Identity and Pathway

- Leader of the Secret Order.
- First introduced through Roselle’s Diary.
- Claimed Sequence 2 status; the source preserves uncertainty around the attempted Sequence 1 advancement.
- The Fool Pathway roster lists him among Book One and Book Two characters, with different vital-status entries across the two books.

## History

- During the First Solomon Empire, Medici observed Zaratul digesting a Sequence 4 potion.
- Zaratul exchanged Beyonder and Fourth Epoch knowledge with Roselle in return for the Antigonus Family Notebook.
- During the Sequence 2 to Sequence 1 advancement, his soul split into two. One half remained in Foggy Town; Klein helped the two halves reconnect and restore his sanity.
- He later operated in Backlund, warned the Rose School of Thought, assisted George III’s apotheosis trap against Gehrman Sparrow, cooperated with Suah, raided Utopia, and pursued a route involving Amon to remain a King of Angels.
- Klein killed him using the Law of Beyonder Characteristic Convergence. The capture records a possible resurrection through another Miracle Invoker characteristic and later describes his return in Circle of Inevitability through Loki’s body as a Celestial Worthy attendant.
- The later capture states that Amon’s parasitism killed him during the Primordial Demoness conflict.

## Evidence Limits

- This note preserves the supplied article’s Book One and Circle of Inevitability account as secondary evidence.
- The capture does not by itself establish a canon reconciliation of every resurrection, identity, and vital-status transition.
- The roster source is a status snapshot, not a complete biography.
