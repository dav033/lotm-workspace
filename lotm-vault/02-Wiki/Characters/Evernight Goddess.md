# Evernight Goddess

## Summary

The Evernight Goddess is the deity worshipped by the Church of the Evernight Goddess. The supplied secondary captures list her as the True God of the Darkness Pathway and describe her later accommodation of the River of Eternal Darkness, after which she becomes Eternal Darkness. These claims remain an interpretation because their direct evidence here is unofficial.

## Identity

- Also named Amanises and the Goddess of Misfortune in the supplied captures.
- The post-war honorific removes “Lady of Crimson” after she gives up the authority of the Moon; the remaining titles include Mother of Concealment, Empress of Misfortune and Horror, and Mistress of Repose and Silence.
- The source distinguishes the Moon as a widely associated symbol from the Star as a Tarot representation attributed to Mr. Door.

## History

The Darkness Pathway roster lists the Evernight Goddess as a Sequence 0 True God in both Book One and Book Two sections. The River of Eternal Darkness capture states that she first established a connection with the source Sefirah, later gained partial control, and finally fully accommodated it during the Apocalypse after the Western Continent seal was lifted.

## Abilities and Pathway

- Pathway: [[Darkness Pathway (disambiguation)]], Sequence 0 in the supplied roster.
- Sefirah relation: [[River of Eternal Darkness]]. The source describes this as accommodation of a Sefirah, not as proof that the Sefirah is a native Darkness Pathway Authority.
- Great Old One relation: [[Eternal Darkness]], through the supplied River capture’s account of her post-accommodation state.

## Relationships

- Church: Church of the Evernight Goddess; no organization note is currently indexed for this name.
- Earlier Darkness holder: [[Flegrea]], whose death is linked by the supplied Flegrea capture to the transfer of the Darkness Uniqueness and a Sequence 1 characteristic to Amanises.
- The supplied sources also connect her to the Eternal Darkness Group through the Darkness Pathway and River of Eternal Darkness.
