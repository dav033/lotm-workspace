# Fourth Pillar

The Fourth Pillar is a cross-group state associated with the convergence of [[Eternal Darkness]] and Calamity of Destruction. It is not part of a single Great Old One hierarchy, and full universal Termination must not be assigned to [[Eternal Darkness Group]] alone.
