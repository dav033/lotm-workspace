# River of Eternal Darkness

The River of Eternal Darkness is the Sefirah associated with [[Eternal Darkness Group]]. The supplied source describes conditional access by all three member Pathways, with different degrees and modes of affinity; this does not make the Sefirah a native Authority of any one Pathway.
