# River of Eternal Darkness

The River of Eternal Darkness is one of the Sefirot on Earth and the Sefirah associated with [[Eternal Darkness Group]]. The supplied capture connects it to the Above the Sequence state Eternal Darkness and describes distributaries such as the River Styx, Yellow Springs, and Samaritan Women’s Spring.

## Extracted Properties

- The river records Death Imprints of deaths and revivals. The capture says repeated deaths can draw a being’s true body toward the river; this remains a secondary claim.
- Its water is described as useful in rituals and abilities connected to the [[Darkness Pathway (disambiguation)]], [[Death Pathway (disambiguation)]], and [[Twilight Giant Pathway (disambiguation)]], with different compatibility and degrees of access. This does not make the Sefirah a native Authority of any one Pathway.
- The Oath of the River Styx is presented as a binding contract that even a Sequence 0 or a Great Old One cannot undo; retain as source-reported until official/high evidence is indexed.
- The capture describes the [[Evernight Goddess]] as gaining partial control and later fully accommodating the River to become [[Eternal Darkness]].
